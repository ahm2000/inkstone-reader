# Special Chapter 10: Before Sunrise

Twelve nautical miles outside the southern coastal strait, the large white luxury yacht owned by the head of the European maritime syndicate rocked helplessly in the dark winter swell.

Its twin diesel engines had gone dead twenty minutes earlier after a pulse of clean electronic interference fried its navigation bridge. Now, six sleek gray Zero Guard cutters formed a motionless circle around the yacht, their bright bow floodlights turning the dark deck as bright as a courtroom stage. High overhead, a matte-black tilt-rotor hovered steadily against the low clouds, its shadow swallowing the yacht whole.

Inside the wood-paneled salon, the syndicate leader, a silver-haired European broker named Henrik Voss, paced frantically with a satellite phone pressed to his ear. His face was gray with sweat.

"What do you mean our private jet in Hong Kong is boxed in?" Voss screamed into the handset. "What do you mean all three banks in Zurich (+1:00 GMT) just surrendered our ledgers to the federal prosecutor? Who is this man in Shanghai?!"

On the other end of the line from Zurich (+1:00 GMT), the senior bank director was practically sobbing. "It is over, Henrik! Armed Swiss federal marshals are already walking through our lobby with ten years of our hidden transfer files! Every offshore trust we own just hit zero! Whoever you threatened tonight, you woke a monster that just shut down an entire country without firing a single bullet!"

Before Voss could shout another word, his satellite phone cut dead.

Through the salon's wide glass doors, three tall men in plain dark weather coats stepped smoothly off a boarding rib onto the yacht's stern deck. They wore no masks, no tactical armor, and no badges. One of them held a thin waterproof folder containing complete international arrest warrants issued just eleven minutes earlier by Interpol and the maritime court.

"Mr. Henrik Voss," the lead man said calmly in fluent English as he opened the salon door. "Your three banking partners in Zurich (+1:00 GMT) were taken into custody six minutes ago. Every warehouse, shell vessel, and offshore account belonging to your syndicate has been seized. Please step onto the deck. The international coast guard transfer boat will arrive in ten minutes."

Voss's knees gave out beneath him, and he dropped heavily onto the cream carpet. "Wait! Tell him I am sorry! I did not know who his wife was! I will pay anything! Ten million, fifty million!"

The man in the dark coat looked down at him with quiet, indifferent eyes. "He does not want your money, Mr. Voss. He just wanted you to understand what happens when you touch his family."

By 5:35 AM, the winter sky above the East China Sea was beginning to pale from black to deep blue.

Inside the quiet courtyard of Emerald Riverside Villa, Chen Xuan stood on the stone steps with a warm mug of plain hot water in his hand. His single smartphone chimed once softly in his pocket.

He pulled it out and read a single plain line on the white screen.

ALL TARGETS IN CUSTODY. ALL ACCOUNTS SEIZED. OLD STREET SAFE. AWAITING STAND-DOWN.

Chen Xuan stared at the line for two seconds, then tapped a single gray button at the bottom of the screen.

STAND DOWN.

Across the entire country, the invisible hand lifted all at once.

High above Shanghai, the unmarked dark tilt-rotors banked silently into the fading clouds and vanished toward the horizon. At Pudong, Beijing, and Guangzhou airports, radar screens shifted cleanly back to green, and relieved air traffic controllers cleared the first morning flights for takeoff. Along the Huangpu River and the southern strait, the gray cutters cut their floodlights and melted smoothly back into the dawn mist, leaving stunned freighter captains to restart their engines in breathless silence.

On the expressways outside Shanghai and Jingzhou, overhead signs flipped back to green, toll gates rose, and long lines of trucks rolled forward into the pale morning light. In Beijing's subterranean command hall, the crimson map cleared into calm blue, and Premier Gu Chengshan finally sat down in his chair, exhaling a breath he felt like he had been holding all night.

In the Shanghai vault, Number 1 watched his monitors blink back to normal green and let his forehead drop gently onto his folded arms in sheer, exhausted relief.

At 6:15 AM, inside the warm, sunlit kitchen of Emerald Riverside Villa, the gentle scent of simmering millet porridge and toasted sesame oil drifted up the wooden staircase.

Chen Xuan stood before the stove in a clean cotton apron, stirring the golden porridge slowly with a wooden spoon. The icy stillness from the night before was completely gone, replaced by his familiar, relaxed warmth.

Soft footsteps came down the stairs. Qin Yuhan walked into the kitchen wearing a soft cream cardigan, one hand lightly touching the small white bandage on her left cheek. She paused by the tall garden window and looked out at the pale morning mist over the river.

"Xuan," she said softly, tilting her head in mild curiosity. "Did you sleep well? I woke up around five and looked outside. The river and the road were completely silent, not even a single boat or plane in the sky. It felt like the whole city had stopped moving."

Chen Xuan turned around with a warm, easy smile and set a steaming bowl of millet porridge and a small plate of crisp pickled radish onto the dining table.

"Just a heavy winter fog on the river," Chen Xuan said lightly, pulling out her chair for her. "Come sit down and eat while it is hot. After breakfast, I will drive you to Haiyue myself before I head to the office."

Qin Yuhan smiled warmly, the last trace of last night's fright melting away as she sat down beside him. "You always make everything feel so peaceful."

Chen Xuan picked up his own chopsticks and smiled back. "That is what husbands are for."
