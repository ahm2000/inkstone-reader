# Special Chapter 3: The Three Founding Words

The inner vault smelled faintly of dry cedar and cold steel. Number 1 lifted the glass cover off the matte-black cipher console and flipped the brass power switch. Three dim amber indicator lamps flickered to life across the dark slate.

Beside the console sat a plain corded handset wired directly into three dedicated lines that bypassed every public tower and satellite in the country. Those three lines had not rung together in more than twenty years.

Number 1 pressed the three red keys in sequence, his palm slick with sweat.

Two rings later, the Beijing line clicked open. "Number 1," ninety-year-old Elder Song said sharply from Western Hills. "You only touch this line when the sky is tearing open."

"Who died?" Elder Shen rasped three seconds later from the southern coast. "Speak fast, boy!"

"All three lines at once?" Elder Lu snapped from the northern mountains, wide awake in an instant. "Is there a border breach?"

"No breach, Elders," Number 1 said, his voice tight and urgent. "Worse. At 8:50 PM on the Pudong riverside road, hired thugs backed by three European banking houses rammed Young Master's wife's sedan, smashed her window with a steel pipe, and cut her cheek. Ten minutes ago, they sent a voice message to Young Master's smartphone threatening to burn his parents' shop on Old Street in Yunxi County before 8:00 AM."

A suffocating silence slammed down across all three lines.

Then, from Beijing, came the violent clatter of a wooden cane striking marble.

"No," Elder Song whispered. His voice cracked straight down the middle, stripped of all composure. "No, no, tell me you misheard that, Number 1! Tell me they did not draw blood on his wife!"

"A porcelain cup just shattered in my hand!" Elder Shen shouted from the southern coast, his breathing turning ragged and frantic. "Are you out of your mind, Number 1?! Why were your White Guard men not on that road?! Why did a steel pipe get within ten feet of her car?!"

"We caught the street hitters twelve minutes ago, Elder, I swear!" Number 1 stammered, cold sweat dripping down his jaw. "Madam Qin is safe upstairs with a small bandage!"

"Safe?!" Elder Lu barked from the northern mountains, his voice trembling with raw, suffocating panic. "Do you think a small bandage matters?! How is HE reacting?! Talk to me, Number 1! Is he laughing? Is he telling you to hand the files to the police?!"

"He is not laughing," Number 1 whispered, his own hands shaking on the handset. "Five minutes ago, he sent me four words. 'They touched my wife.' Nothing else. No smile, no patience, no instructions. He is standing alone in his winter courtyard in the dark. Elders, in fifteen years I have never felt him truly furious. My White Guard cannot take down three European banks before sunrise. If we do not crush them in the next hour, I do not know what he will do!"

"Oh God," Elder Song gasped on the Beijing line. The sound of a heavy chair scraping backward echoed through the speaker, followed by the old man's shallow, terrified wheezing. "Shen! Lu! Do you hear me? My hands will not stop shaking! Remember the afternoon I sat across from him at the lake? If he actually loses his temper, neither Beijing nor Europe will survive until breakfast!"

"Shut up, Song, you are making my heart stop!" Elder Shen yelled in pure panic, his voice shrill and desperate. "We have to stop him before he touches his own phone! Number 1, do not let him wait another ten minutes! If he moves personally, we will not even know how many borders collapse tonight!"

"Then wake the Black Order right now!" Elder Lu shouted, his breath hitching wildly. "What are you waiting for?! Wake them!"

"If we turn the three keys, every retired elder in Washington (-5:00 GMT), Moscow (+3:00 GMT), and Tokyo (+9:00 GMT) will think war just started!" Elder Song cried, his voice nearly breaking.

"Let them think war started!" Elder Shen screamed back. "I would rather face ten foreign fleets than let Young Master stay angry for ten more minutes! Number 1, is the slate powered?!"

"Yes, Elder! All three amber lamps are lit!"

"Then type, boy! Type before we are all buried!" Elder Lu roared, his voice cracking with terror.

Number 1's fingers flew over the matte-black mechanical keys.

"Iron sleeps beneath the pine," Elder Song forced out, his voice trembling so violently he nearly missed the last syllable. The acoustic sensor matched his voiceprint against the forty-year-old vault lock, and the first amber lamp snapped crimson.

"Ash remembers the flame!" Elder Shen gasped, coughing hard between words as panic choked his throat. The second lamp turned crimson.

"No crown above the quiet hand," Elder Lu whispered, sounding like a man praying at the edge of a cliff.

Number 1 typed the final phrase and slammed the recessed steel bar. All three crimson lights pulsed once in unison, sending a cold tremor through the concrete floor. Silver text burned across the dark glass.

BLACK ORDER: ACTIVE.

"Brief them this second, Number 1!" Elder Song pleaded, his voice hoarse and terrified. "Tell them to move like the sky is falling, and pray to God he has not already stopped listening to us!"
