# Special Chapter 4: Briefing the Unnumbered

The three Founding Elders dropped off the line one by one, leaving only the steady low hum of the matte-black console inside the vault.

Before Number 1 could even lift the second handset, the console's small speaker clicked open on its own. Unlike the White Guard, the Black Order carried no numbers, no public roster, and no rank badges. They were ghosts drawn from the deepest layers of finance, maritime intelligence, signal warfare, and cross-border enforcement, answering only when the three founding keys turned together.

"East Coast desk online," a calm, unhurried male voice spoke through the speaker.

"Capital desk online," a cool female voice followed half a second later.

"Southern harbor desk online."

"European clearing desk online from Zurich (+1:00 GMT)."

Four voices, zero names, zero hesitation. Within ten seconds of the crimson pulse, every major node had woken up and locked onto the vault frequency.

Number 1 straightened his back and pressed the transmit switch. "This is Number 1 of the White Guard, speaking under three-elder authorization. Listen closely. This is not a routine financial defense."

"State the target," the Capital desk replied evenly.

"At 8:50 PM tonight, an unmarked van rammed Young Master's wife's sedan on the Pudong riverside road," Number 1 briefed, keeping his voice crisp while his palms stayed damp with sweat. "The rear window was smashed with a steel pipe. Flying glass cut her cheek. Ten minutes ago, the offshore syndicate behind the attack sent a voice threat directly to Young Master's smartphone, promising to burn his parents' shop on Old Street in Yunxi County if three seized freighters are not released by 8:00 AM."

For three full seconds, not a single voice answered on the Black Order channel. Even for men and women trained to stay ice-cold through market crashes and border standoffs, that sentence hit like a thunderclap.

"Confirm the last part," the European desk in Zurich (+1:00 GMT) said slowly, every trace of casual calm gone from the voice. "They drew blood on his wife and threatened his parents' street?"

"Confirmed," Number 1 said grimly. "We have the street footage, the local thugs in custody, and the audio trace leading straight to three European private banking houses tied to the maritime syndicate. And there is one more detail you need to understand before you move."

"Speak," the East Coast desk ordered.

"Young Master sent me four words five minutes ago," Number 1 said quietly. "He is truly angry. Not annoyed, not amused, not setting a patient trap. Truly furious. The Founding Elders woke you for one reason only: to dismantle every last piece of this syndicate and freeze every bank behind it cleanly before Young Master decides to step in himself."

Another brief, heavy silence hung over the line.

"Understood," the Capital desk said, the voice dropping half an octave into pure steel. "We know what happens if he steps in personally. East Coast, seal every private airfield, ferry slip, and diplomatic courier lane within three hundred kilometers of Shanghai. Nobody tied to the three seized freighters leaves the coast."

"Already moving," the East Coast desk replied. "Port cranes and customs clearances for every shell company linked to the syndicate are being locked right now."

"European desk," the Capital voice continued smoothly. "Pull the full criminal ledgers on all three private banking houses in Zurich (+1:00 GMT). Not just the smuggling money. Every laundered account, every bribed official, every hidden trust. Prepare to drop the entire file onto the Swiss federal prosecutor's desk and freeze their interbank settlement lines simultaneously."

"Give me twenty minutes," the Zurich (+1:00 GMT) voice answered briskly. "I already have two of the three banks' internal ledgers pulled up. Their directors will not be able to buy a cup of coffee by midnight."

"Southern harbor desk," the Capital voice added. "Locate the syndicate's main yacht and offshore relay vessel. Keep them boxed inside international monitoring lanes without firing a shot. We do this fast, clean, and by the book so Beijing does not have to explain a single broken treaty."

"Copy," the Southern desk replied. "Cutters are moving into position now."

Number 1 let out a tight breath, his shoulders relaxing by a fraction of an inch. That was the terrifying efficiency of the Black Order. In less than two minutes, a global web that would have taken regular agencies six months to untangle was already being strangled from four directions at once.

"Number 1," the Capital desk asked sharply before signing off. "Have you informed Young Master that we are active?"

"I am calling him right now," Number 1 said, reaching for his secure desk phone to dial Chen Xuan's single smartphone. "Once he hears the Black Order is already moving, he may let us finish this cleanly."

"Pray that he does," the Capital voice replied softly. "Because the pulse just went out across the national network. Half the people at the top are already awake and sweating."
