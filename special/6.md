# Special Chapter 6: No More Playing by the Book

Back in the quiet courtyard of Emerald Riverside Villa, Chen Xuan stood beside the stone pond, watching faint rings of winter mist drift over the dark water.

His smartphone vibrated once in his coat pocket. He pulled it out, saw Number 1's name on the screen, and answered without hurrying.

"Young Master," Number 1 said immediately, his voice tight, breathless, and deeply respectful. "I am sorry we failed to stop that van before it reached the riverside road. Number 44's team already has the driver and the attacker in custody. I also called the three Founding Elders and gathered their founding words. The Black Order is now active."

Chen Xuan did not interrupt. He simply stood still in the cold mist, listening.

"The Black Order is already sealing every private airfield and coastal slipway around Shanghai," Number 1 continued rapidly, hoping the speed of the response would cool the frost in the air. "Their Zurich (+1:00 GMT) desk has frozen the interbank lines of all three European banking houses behind the syndicate, and their Southern harbor desk is boxing in the syndicate's yacht outside the strait. Give them two hours, Young Master. They will hand every single culprit to international prosecutors cleanly, by the book, with zero diplomatic fallout."

For three long seconds, only the distant lap of the Huangpu River against the embankment filled the line.

"By the book," Chen Xuan repeated softly.

His voice was not loud. He did not shout or curse. Yet the sheer, quiet flatness in those three words made Number 1's stomach drop like a stone inside the Shanghai vault.

"Young Master," Number 1 pleaded, his voice cracking with panic. "If we let the Black Order finish this cleanly, Beijing will not have to answer to foreign embassies, and your quiet life with Madam Qin will remain completely untouched. Even Elder Song begged me to ask you for two hours."

"They swung a steel pipe at my wife's window," Chen Xuan said calmly, each word landing like frozen hail. "She has a bandage on her face upstairs right now. And ten minutes ago, they called my phone and threatened to burn my parents' street in Yunxi County."

"Young Master, Old Street is already surrounded by our men!"

"I played by the book when they framed my second uncle," Chen Xuan cut in gently, his tone colder than winter iron. "I played by the book when the Shen family tried to swallow my wife's shipping routes. I played by the book when foreign short-sellers attacked our banks. Every time, I let them keep their neat little rules, their quiet courts, and their comfortable illusions."

He lifted his eyes toward the dark sky above Pudong.

"I do not want to play by the book tonight."

Inside the vault, Number 1 gripped the handset with both hands, his knuckles white. "Young Master, please! Wait thirty seconds! Do not touch that key!"

The line clicked dead.

In the courtyard, Chen Xuan did not put his smartphone away. Instead, he opened a plain gray calculator utility tucked at the back of his home screen. Without hesitation, his thumb tapped a seven-digit override sequence known only to him, followed by the equals key.

A single stark white prompt appeared across the dark glass.

ZERO GUARD: FULL NATIONAL AUTHORITY?

Chen Xuan thought of the thin red cut beneath Qin Yuhan's left eye. He tapped CONFIRM.

Two kilometers away in the Shanghai command vault, every single monitor on the wall flared blinding white at the exact same instant. The Black Order's matte-black cipher console let out a sharp electronic whine. All three crimson elder lamps winked out at once, replaced by a single cold white zero burning in the center of the dark glass.

"Number 1!" Number 7 yelled, stumbling through the vault door in sheer terror. "Every White Guard terminal just locked! Even the Black Order frequency just went completely dead!"

On the vault speaker, the calm Capital desk voice of the Black Order spoke one last time before the line severed completely, trembling with genuine dread. "Our command authority was just stripped! We cannot even see who just took over the grid! God help everyone tonight!"

At that exact second, the three-way red telephone on Number 1's desk screamed back to life. Number 1 snatched it up with shaking hands.

"Number 1!" Elder Song's voice came through, ragged and completely overwhelmed with terror. "What just happened?! The Black Order relay just died in my hand! My master terminal just turned pure white with a single zero!"

"Who are they?!" Elder Shen shouted in sheer panic from the southern coast, his voice nearly breaking. "We built the White Guard! We built the Black Order! Why did something just erase both of them in one second?! Who are these people?!"

"He activated the Zero Guard directly," Number 1 whispered, his legs giving out as he sank into his chair.

Dead, horrified silence answered from all three Founding Elders. Even the three men who had spent ninety years standing at the foundation of the country barely knew who the Zero Guard truly were, how many of them existed, or what was about to happen now that they had been unleashed in full.

Outside, far above the winter clouds, a deep, rhythmic thrum began to vibrate through the night air, growing louder by the second until the glass windows of Shanghai started to hum.
