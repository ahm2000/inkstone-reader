# Chapter 56: Central Military Commission: Closed-Door Storm

At six forty-five on Monday morning, the underground meeting room beneath the Central Military Commission smelled of burnt tobacco and cold sweat. Ten senior department heads sat around the dark table, collars buttoned tight, ashtrays half full.

General Qin Zhengguo stood at the head with a burning cigarette. When he slammed the decoded navy radar files and border troop records onto the wood, the crack sounded like a rifle shot.

"Nine years," Qin Zhengguo said, voice raw. "Nine full years of troop shift lists, coastal radar gaps, and weapons schedules walking straight out of our vaults. Not one warning ever reached my desk."

He locked eyes on the left side of the table. Logistics Chief Lieutenant General Kang Weidong froze with his lighter halfway up. Beside him, Political Work Director Lieutenant General Shen Mingyuan set his pen down, knuckles bone-white.

"General Qin Zhengguo, we must check who handled these files first," Kang Weidong said, forcing his voice calm while a vein hammered at his temple. "Senior Colonel Ma Zhenyu held top clearance. His supply reviews passed three checks every few months."

"Three checks reporting straight to you, Kang Weidong," snapped Military Discipline Chief General Du Changhe, crushing out his cigarette. "Your inspectors missed nine years of fake double shipping lists by accident?"

"Watch your mouth, Du Changhe," Kang Weidong shot back. "Logistics handles forty thousand orders a month across five military zones. If a senior colonel alters a secret list with a real key, that is a spy-hunting failure, not a supply mistake."

Naval Intelligence Director Rear Admiral Xue Biao blew smoke through his nose. "Do not dump your leaking office onto my navy, Kang Weidong. Page fourteen shows east coast radar repair gaps matching your fuel-boat orders to the minute. Someone in your building handed Ma Zhenyu the master schedule."

"That is a lie!" Kang Weidong barked, slapping the folder. "My office does not keep navy radar schedules!"

"No," Joint Staff Deputy Chief General Lei Zhenshan cut in coldly. "You kept the joint drill timetables. And Political Work renewed Ma Zhenyu's clearance every eighteen months without questioning a single officer under him."

Every head snapped toward Shen Mingyuan. His shaking fingers tore his unlit cigarette seam until tobacco spilled across his desk.

"Ma Zhenyu had a spotless record," Shen Mingyuan said, voice cracking. "Two medals. No overseas family. No gambling debts. No unexplained money. His file was cleaner than half the officers here."

"That should have scared you," Equipment Development Director General Yao Guangming muttered. "Nobody stays that spotless for twenty years unless someone higher up scrubs his dirt. Who signed off on his move to the central schedule room in 2017?"

Silence dropped like wet iron.

Qin Zhengguo took a slow drag. "Your deputy signed it, Shen Mingyuan. Your old division chief signed beside him, Kang Weidong. Both were pulled out of their apartments at three in the morning by people you lack the rank to question."

Eastern Theater Representative Major General Gao Feng swallowed hard. "General Qin Zhengguo, the overnight list names three hundred and eighteen suspects nationwide, including seventeen active officers above regiment rank. Who made those arrests? Military Discipline did not hear until five thirty."

"Not us," Du Changhe confirmed grimly. "When my teams reached Ma Zhenyu's home, the door was unlocked, safes stood open, and a printed proof list sat on his table. Every hard drive was already copied and sealed."

Rocket Force Security Chief Major General Bai Lixin leaned forward, jaw tight. "If an outside team walked past three rings of guards and grabbed a senior colonel without one alarm, our base security is a joke. Was it White Guard?"

Several men drew sharp breaths. A cigarette rolled off an ashtray until Southern Theater Representative Major General Huo Jian slapped it out with his bare thumb.

Qin Zhengguo's eyes went flat. "White Guard did not touch Ma Zhenyu."

"Then who did?" Northern Theater Representative Major General Wen Bo demanded, voice rising. "Base commanders are calling in panic because their contacts vanished before sunrise! Who can pull active officers out of bed without a Central Military Commission warrant?"

"Black Guard," Qin Zhengguo said quietly.

Those two words sucked the air from the room. Lei Zhenshan froze with his teacup inches from his lips. Du Changhe stared blankly as ash fell onto his knee.

"Impossible," Lei Zhenshan whispered. "Black Guard has not moved since the founding era. Even White Guard cannot call them out. I dialed the vault line at five o'clock and got a dead tone."

"Because White Guard is locked down under monitoring," Qin Zhengguo said, crushing his cigarette. "I had to wake retired Founding Elder Song at four in the morning for the old override code just so the vault would confirm our chain of command is still alive. They did not ask permission. They handed us a mop and told us to clean up our floor before the roof falls in."

Kang Weidong's face turned chalk-white. "General Qin Zhengguo, I swear on forty years in uniform, I never took a cent from Ma Zhenyu. I will turn myself over to Military Discipline today."

"You will," Qin Zhengguo replied coldly. "Every man here hands his personal files, staff lists, and family asset sheets to Du Changhe before noon. Nobody leaves without a signed pass."

"What about regional bases?" Gao Feng asked, sweat shining on his forehead. "Once foreign spy stations realize their mole is gone, they will shut down their coastal links before lunch."

"Let them try," Xue Biao snapped, stabbing a finger at the maps. "This file lists every leaked signal and dirty dock contractor. Give me ninety minutes and Naval Intelligence locks down every shipyard from Qingdao to Zhanjiang."

"You have sixty," Qin Zhengguo ordered, eyes sweeping all ten men. "At eight o'clock, Premier Gu Chengshan and Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua open the Cross-Department War Room. I will not walk in with army excuses. Kang Weidong, Shen Mingyuan, list every name that touched Ma Zhenyu's file since 2017. Lei Zhenshan, prepare emergency officer swaps for all three military zones. Remove anyone who pushes back."

He tapped a fresh cigarette against his thumb.

"Someone did our job in forty-eight hours and left the knife on our table. Either we cut the rot out tonight, or we explain why the Central Military Commission slept for nine years. Move."

Chairs scraped back through swirling smoke. Nobody looked at Kang Weidong or Shen Mingyuan as the heavy folders slammed shut.
