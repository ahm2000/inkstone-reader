# Chapter 99: A Deal Over Green Tea

Before Qin Yuhan could pick up a second walnut cookie, a heavy shadow fell across their marble table.

"Yuhan! What a surprise running into you here!"

A man in his late twenties wearing a shiny navy suit and too much cologne walked over, tilting his wrist so his gold watch caught the light. It was Zhou Mingcheng, the Lujiazui fund manager whom Qin Yuhan's mother Lin Shuyin had been praising all week.

Qin Yuhan's warm smile vanished at once. "Mr. Zhou. I told my mother I had a personal lunch appointment today."

Zhou Mingcheng barely listened. His eyes swept over Chen Xuan's twenty-yuan white shirt and landed on the scratched green thermos beside the sugar bowl. A mocking smirk curled his lips.

"So this is your lunch appointment?" Zhou Mingcheng chuckled loudly enough for neighboring tables to hear. "Yuhan, Auntie Lin Shuyin told me this morning that she booked us a private room at IFC Mall tonight. A woman of your level is the president of Haiyue Group, and your grandfather Elder Qin Zhengguo is respected across Beijing. What are you doing sitting here with a guy who carries a security guard's water bottle?"

Chen Xuan did not even look bothered. He calmly blew on the tea in his metal thermos cap, took a slow sip, and looked up at Zhou Mingcheng with a mild, curious expression.

"Excuse me," Chen Xuan said conversationally, "did you put half a bottle of cologne on this morning, or did one break inside your briefcase? My jasmine tea cannot even compete with you from three feet away."

A couple at the next table choked on their iced lattes trying to hold back their laughter.

Zhou Mingcheng's face stiffened. He glared down at Chen Xuan. "Do you have any idea who you are talking to, kid? This cologne is custom-ordered from Paris, and I manage a 500 million yuan private equity portfolio through Huaxia Bank right across the street! Every minute of my lunch hour is worth more than your monthly salary. Who even are you?"

"Me?" Chen Xuan reached over, took a walnut cookie off the plate, and smiled warmly at Qin Yuhan. "I am Yuhan's boyfriend. She told me she was tired of listening to loud people brag about other people's bank money, so we came here for hot water and walnut cookies."

Qin Yuhan froze for half a second, her eyes widening slightly, and then she played right along without missing a beat. "That is right, Mr. Zhou. Chen Xuan and I are dating, and we are having a very nice lunch. Please do not interrupt us."

"Dating? Him?" Zhou Mingcheng laughed bitterly, tapping the marble table with a manicured finger. "Yuhan, stop joking around to dodge Auntie Lin Shuyin! Look at his shirt and that ten-yuan tin thermos! How could he possibly know the first thing about high finance or help you with Haiyue Group? Earlier today I was just reviewing Huaxia Bank's Tier-One sovereign yield fund in Lujiazui, and I guarantee a clerk like him cannot even spell the fund code!"

Chen Xuan blinked lazily while chewing his walnut cookie.

"Huaxia Bank has never had a Tier-One sovereign yield fund in Lujiazui," Chen Xuan remarked in a calm, helpful tone. "Their Shanghai branch only clears commercial bond portfolios and corporate credit lines. If someone sold you a Tier-One sovereign yield fund under Huaxia Bank's name, you either read the brochure upside down or you got tricked by a street flyer outside Nanjing Road."

Zhou Mingcheng's mouth opened, and then snapped shut. His face turned bright red because he had indeed just made up the name on the spot to sound impressive. Across the aisle, two real Huaxia Bank managers eating sandwiches looked up and began snickering openly at Zhou Mingcheng.

"You just got lucky guessing some bank trivia!" Zhou Mingcheng stammered, his ears burning as people around them laughed. He pointed a stiff finger at Qin Yuhan. "Fine! Wait until I tell Auntie Lin Shuyin in Beijing that you are wasting your time with a twelve-thousand-yuan clerk!"

Spinning on his heel, Zhou Mingcheng marched out of Cloud Corner Café so fast he nearly bumped into the glass door.

Once the door swung shut, the table grew quiet again.

Qin Yuhan looked across the table at Chen Xuan. He had already gone back to pouring hot tea from his green thermos into the metal lid, completely unbothered. He was not asking how much Haiyue Group was worth, he was not asking about her grandfather Elder Qin Zhengguo in Beijing, and his eyes held zero greed.

In fact, sitting across from him for twenty minutes was the most peaceful twenty minutes Qin Yuhan had felt in years.

If Zhou Mingcheng called her mother Lin Shuyin today, her mother and her father Qin Zhengming would never stop setting up blind dates every week. And even her grandfather Elder Qin Zhengguo had been nagging her to find a steady husband before the family gathering.

A bold, sudden decision clicked into place in Qin Yuhan's sharp mind.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said, sitting up straight and looking right into his calm eyes. "Are you really single right now?"

"Completely single," Chen Xuan answered honestly. "Why?"

"Zhou Mingcheng is going to call my mother in Beijing within an hour, and my family will turn my life upside down trying to force me into more blind dates," Qin Yuhan said clearly. "You heard him say I run a company here in Shanghai. I own a villa in Pudong, I earn plenty of money, and I do not need a husband to buy me houses. What I need is a quiet, steady husband with a clean background who will live under the same roof, keep my family off my back, and not bring home any drama."

She paused, her heart beating a little faster despite her calm voice. "Would you be willing to get a marriage certificate with me this afternoon and move into my place? I will cover all household expenses, and we can treat each other like good friends and roommates."

Chen Xuan held his thermos cap halfway to his mouth and looked at the gorgeous, serious young woman across from him.

If he said yes, his mother Liu Meifang would stop calling him every morning about blind dates, and he could go back to living his lazy life in peace.

Chen Xuan blinked lazily. "If we get married, do I still get to clock out at four fifty-five every afternoon, sleep eight hours a night, and use my green thermos?"

Qin Yuhan's serious expression broke into a bright, genuine smile. She held out her slender right hand across the table.

"Deal."
