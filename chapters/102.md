# Chapter 102: Picking Up the Wife at Haiyue Tower

By Friday afternoon, Chen Xuan had settled comfortably into the easy rhythm of his new married week, and right on schedule the digital clock on the twelfth-floor wall of Hengxin Logistics flipped to 4:55 PM.

Before the final digit had even finished settling, Chen Xuan clicked the power button on his desktop monitor, capped his blue ink pen with a crisp snap, and lifted his dented green metal thermos from the corner of his desk.

At the next workstation over, young intern Zhang Hao looked up from a tall stack of customs manifests in pure awe. "Brother Xuan, your internal clock is more accurate than the national observatory. Even the security guard downstairs checks his watch by when you step out of the elevator."

"4:55 PM is the golden window, Little Zhang," Chen Xuan said cheerfully, slinging his plain canvas bag over one shoulder and zipping his light gray windbreaker halfway up. "If you wait until 5:00 PM, you end up standing behind thirty people waiting for the elevator. Leave five minutes early, and you step right out into the autumn breeze with the whole sidewalk to yourself."

Zhang Hao laughed, shaking his head. "I wish I had your nerve. If I stood up before 5:00 PM, Manager Hu Dewei would stare holes through the back of my shirt."

As if on cue, Department Manager Hu Dewei walked out of his glass office holding a printed shipping ledger. He glanced sourly at Chen Xuan's spotless desk, checked the tidy stack of completed waybills Chen Xuan had submitted an hour earlier, and found not a single mistake to complain about.

"Always out the door at 4:55 PM sharp," Hu Dewei muttered with a stiff sniff, though he could not stop him. "Do not forget Monday's port inventory sheet."

"Already sorted and filed on the shared drive, Manager Hu. Have a good weekend, Little Zhang," Chen Xuan called back easily as he strolled toward the elevators.

The steel doors slid open immediately, completely empty. Chen Xuan stepped inside, rode smoothly down to the ground lobby, and walked out into the crisp October sunshine. A cool autumn wind fluttered the yellow leaves along the street, and the golden light slanted warmly across the glass towers of Lujiazui.

Instead of heading straight for the subway, Chen Xuan strolled ten minutes down the tree-lined avenue toward Haiyue Tower. Just before the plaza, the sweet roasted aroma of sugar-glazed chestnuts drifted from a small wooden stall tucked beside a corner bakery.

Chen Xuan paused and smiled. Qin Yuhan had mentioned two nights ago that autumn never felt complete without hot roasted chestnuts. He walked over, bought a generous paper bag for 25 yuan, and tucked it carefully inside the crook of his arm to keep the chestnuts steaming warm.

Five minutes later, he pushed through the revolving glass doors of Haiyue Tower. The towering marble lobby gleamed with soft gold lighting and quiet, brisk footsteps as bankers and shipping executives hurried past. Chen Xuan found an empty leather bench beside a row of tall potted palms, set his green thermos beside him, and began cracking open the warm chestnuts one by one, placing the golden kernels neatly onto a clean white napkin so Qin Yuhan would not burn her fingers.

Not far from the bench, two sharply dressed junior managers from a visiting trading firm waited near the reception desk. One of them, a young man in a tailored charcoal suit, glanced over at Chen Xuan's faded windbreaker, canvas bag, and dented green thermos, then nudged his colleague with a smirk.

"Look at that guy sitting in Haiyue Tower's executive lobby peeling street chestnuts," the man whispered, loud enough to carry across the quiet marble floor. "Did security forget to check visitor passes today? He looks like a courier waiting for a signature."

His colleague chuckled under his breath. "Maybe a distant relative of someone in the mailroom. Still, bringing a green thermos and street snacks into a Grade A tower in Lujiazui is pretty embarrassing."

Chen Xuan did not even bother looking up. He simply peeled another sweet chestnut, dusted a speck of shell off his thumb, and checked the time on his phone. Right on schedule.

Across the lobby, the brass indicator above the private executive elevator chimed softly as the express car descended nonstop from the 38th floor. The polished doors glided open.

Qin Yuhan stepped out first in her tailored ivory blazer, her dark hair falling cleanly over her shoulders. Behind her walked her assistant Lin Wei and two senior directors wrapping up notes from a quarterly port review. Qin Yuhan's expression was poised and cool, the unmistakable aura of a corporate president who commanded billion-yuan shipping lines without raising her voice.

Then her eyes swept across the lobby and landed on the leather bench beside the palms.

Instantly, the frosty distance vanished from her face. Her eyes curved into a bright, genuine smile.

"That covers everything for this week," Qin Yuhan told the two directors politely. "Send the revised harbor schedule to my email by Monday morning. Enjoy your weekend."

Without waiting for another word, she walked straight across the marble floor toward Chen Xuan. The two visiting managers froze mid-smirk, their jaws slackening as the famously untouchable president of Haiyue Group stopped right in front of the man in the gray windbreaker.

Chen Xuan stood up effortlessly and held out the clean napkin filled with steaming golden chestnuts. "Fresh off the corner pan. Still warm."

Qin Yuhan picked one up, popped it into her mouth, and let out a pleased hum. "Sweet and soft. You even peeled them for me."

She slipped her free arm naturally through his, completely ignoring the stunned stares from the reception desk. "What is on the menu tonight, Chef Chen? I have been thinking about dinner since my afternoon meeting started."

"Braised pork ribs and winter melon soup," Chen Xuan said, picking up his green thermos with his other hand. "If you are not too tired, let us stop by the supermarket downstairs at IFC Mall on the way home and grab fresh ribs."

"Perfect," Qin Yuhan laughed softly, leaning just a fraction closer against his shoulder. "Let us go before the good cuts are gone."

Under the stunned gazes of the two whispering managers, Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan walked out through the revolving glass doors side by side into the warm autumn twilight.
