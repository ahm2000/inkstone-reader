# Chapter 102: Picking Up the Wife at Haiyue Tower

On Friday afternoon, the digital clock on the third-floor wall of Hengxin Logistics flipped to 4:55 PM.

Before the final digit had even finished lighting up, Chen Xuan clicked the power button on his desktop monitor, closed his blue ink pen with a neat snap, and picked up his green enamel thermos from the corner of his desk.

At the next desk over, young intern Zhang Hao looked up from a stack of customs declarations in awe. "Brother Xuan, your internal clock is more accurate than the national atomic timepiece. Even the building security guard checks his watch by when you stand up."

"Five minutes before five is the golden window, Little Zhang," Chen Xuan said cheerfully, slinging his plain canvas bag over his shoulder and zipping his casual light gray windbreaker halfway up. "If you leave at five sharp, you get stuck behind thirty people waiting for the elevator. If you leave at four fifty-five, the elevator is empty and the afternoon breeze outside is still fresh. Have a good weekend."

"Going home to cook something good again, Brother Xuan?" Zhang Hao asked wistfully.

"Not straight home today," Chen Xuan smiled, giving a casual two-finger wave as he walked toward the corridor. "I promised to pick up my wife from her office."

Stepping out of the Hengxin Logistics building into the warm Lujiazui sunshine, Chen Xuan strolled three blocks down Century Avenue toward Haiyue Tower. The sixty-story glass skyscraper gleamed like a cut diamond against the afternoon sky, standing right across the boulevard from the Jin Mao Tower.

When Chen Xuan pushed through the tall revolving glass doors into the ground-floor lobby of Haiyue Tower, the cool scent of white tea air freshener and polished Italian marble greeted him. Men and women in tailored designer suits hurried past the security turnstiles with leather briefcases and badges.

Chen Xuan did not bother with the turnstiles. He strolled over to a cream leather waiting sofa near the guest reception desk, sat down comfortably, unscrewed the lid of his green enamel thermos, and blew gently on his hot wolfberry tea.

Behind the curved marble reception counter, a newly hired receptionist named Zhou Meng frowned at the man on the sofa. Before she could walk over, a young man in a flashy burgundy suit and shiny Italian loafers strode out of the side corridor with two assistants trailing behind him.

It was Qin Haoran, Qin Yuhan's twenty-six-year-old cousin, who held a minor deputy manager post in Haiyue Group's purchasing department solely because his father had begged Qin Yuhan's father for a favor.

Qin Haoran spotted Chen Xuan sitting on the VIP guest sofa in a two-hundred-yuan windbreaker, sipping tea from a chipped green enamel thermos. Seeing an opportunity to look important in front of the lobby staff, Qin Haoran marched straight over with his chin tilted high.

"Zhou Meng!" Qin Haoran snapped at the receptionist. "What is the front desk doing today? Since when do we let courier riders take tea breaks on the executive lobby sofas? Look at that green thermos. If he spills cheap tea leaves on that imported Italian leather, your monthly salary will not even pay for the cleaning!"

Zhou Meng hurried out from behind the desk, her face flushing. "I am sorry, Manager Qin! I will ask him to leave right away." She turned to Chen Xuan with a sharp, condescending frown. "Sir, delivery packages and logistics forms go to the basement loading dock around the back. You cannot sit here in the main lobby."

Chen Xuan took a slow sip of his tea, lowered the thermos, and looked up at Qin Haoran with mild curiosity. "I am not delivering packages. I am waiting for someone to get off work."

Qin Haoran let out a loud, mocking laugh, adjusting his gold wristwatch so everyone nearby could see it. "Waiting for someone? Looking like that? Who could you possibly know in Haiyue Tower, the third-shift janitor? Listen up, buddy. My cousin Qin Yuhan is the president of Haiyue Group, and she hates clutter in the lobby. Pack up your antique thermos and get out before I have security throw you onto the sidewalk."

"Your cousin is President Qin?" Chen Xuan asked, raising an eyebrow slightly. "She never mentioned having a cousin who barks at guests in the lobby."

"What did you just say to me?!" Qin Haoran's face turned dark red. He pointed a finger at the front doors. "Security! Get over here right now and toss this guy out!"

Two burly lobby guards took a step forward, looking uneasy.

Just then, a soft golden chime echoed across the quiet marble hall.

The private brass elevator reserved exclusively for the company president slid open. Qin Yuhan stepped out wearing a sleek pale blue suit, carrying the empty cloth lunch bag Chen Xuan had packed for her that morning. Behind her walked her chief secretary and three senior vice presidents holding financial folders.

Qin Haoran immediately plastered a flattering grin onto his face and turned toward the elevator. "Cousin Yuhan! You came down just in time. Some random logistics bum wandered into the lobby with a green thermos and refused to leave. I was just clearing him out for you."

Qin Yuhan stopped walking. Her cool gaze swept past a bewildered Qin Haoran and landed on Chen Xuan, who was screwing the lid back onto his green thermos on the sofa.

Instantly, the frosty corporate look on Qin Yuhan's face melted into a bright, warm smile that none of the vice presidents behind her had ever seen in three years of board meetings.

She walked straight past Qin Haoran without sparing him a glance, stopped in front of Chen Xuan, and naturally hooked her arm through the sleeve of his plain gray windbreaker.

"You got here early," Qin Yuhan said softly, handing him the folded cloth lunch bag. "Did you wait long?"

"Only five minutes," Chen Xuan smiled, slipping her cloth bag into his canvas shoulder bag. "My tea was still too hot to drink anyway."

"Good, because I am starving," Qin Yuhan said, leaning slightly against his shoulder as they turned toward the revolving doors. "What soup are we making tonight?"

"Spare rib and winter melon soup," Chen Xuan replied easily as they walked out together into the golden afternoon sunlight. "We can stop by IFC Mall on the way to grab fresh ribs."

Inside the marble lobby, dead silence reigned.

Zhou Meng covered her mouth in shock. The three vice presidents exchanged stunned looks, while Qin Haoran stood frozen with his jaw on the floor, staring after the plain gray windbreaker walking out beside the president of Haiyue Group.
