# Chapter 10: The Silent Watch

Far away, inside the red-walled compound at the heart of the national capital, the night was usually so quiet you could hear the wind in the pine trees.

Tonight a classified terminal on a senior cabinet leader's desk chimed once and turned dark red.

*ABSOLUTE ORDER ACTIVE. ZONE: YUNXI COUNTY, LANSHUI PROVINCE. RADIUS: ONE HUNDRED KILOMETERS. AUTHORITY: SUPREME.*

His fountain pen stopped. A drop of ink spread across the memo he was signing.

Before he could press the intercom, the heavy doors opened without a sound. No sentry called out. No alarm rang.

Eight figures in black uniforms and full-face masks walked in and took their places. Two stood at the door. Two stood at the curtained windows. Four lined the wall behind his desk. After that, nobody moved.

The leader stood up. "Who let you into this compound? Name yourselves. Guard room! Where are my guards?"

No one answered. No head turned.

He grabbed the red phone and called the night desk of the Central Guard Bureau. "Director, eight armed men in masks are standing in my study. Are you asleep? Send a team now."

For three seconds he heard only breathing.

"Sir," the director said quietly, "seven of them are in my office. They walked through three locked doors and no sensor went off. They have not said one word."

The anger ran out of the leader's face. He had spent his life giving orders to guards. Tonight the guards could not even help themselves.

He took one step toward the door. The mask standing there did not raise a hand. It only turned its head and looked at him, and that was enough. He went back to his desk, sat down slowly, and put both hands flat on the wood where the masks could see them.

* * *

In the same minute, the same thing happened in the command centers of other provinces.

On the operations floor of the Lanshui Provincial Command Center, seven masked figures walked through the glass doors without a badge or a code. They formed a ring around the Provincial Party Secretary and the duty director.

"Freeze!" a security captain shouted. His hand went toward his holster.

"Don't touch it, you fool!" The duty director's voice broke. "Look at the doors. They opened every top-level lock without a key."

The Provincial Party Secretary stared at the masks around his console. "Who sent you? The capital? Give me a unit number. Give me an order number."

Nobody answered him. The seven masks watched every screen and every phone like statues.

In Qingyan City, six masks stood behind the City Party Secretary's chair and at both exits of the conference room. Six senior city leaders sat around the long table. Nobody touched a phone. Every hand stayed flat on the wood.

* * *

Across the ocean, in the night-shift watch rooms of Washington, Moscow and London, analysts jumped out of their chairs. Orbital screens had flashed red.

"Sir, we lost everything over a ten-kilometer square in eastern China," a senior analyst in Washington said. "Optical, thermal, radar. And a hundred-kilometer air patrol is forming around it right now."

The watch commander leaned over the console and read the code at the bottom of the screen. His face went gray.

"Category Zero." He grabbed the line to the Defense Secretary's home. "Wake him up. We have never seen that code live. It sits in our oldest files with one line beside it: do not approach."

The Defense Secretary came on the line in his robe. "Is it a Chinese test?"

"No, sir. We are listening to their emergency lines. They are asking each other the same thing we are. Their generals, their ministers, their capital. Nobody there knows either."

That was the part that frightened the watch commander most. A test would have a general who knew about it.

In Moscow and London, the same calls went out in the dark. Intelligence chiefs on both sides of the world looked at the name of a small county glowing on their maps and asked the same question.

*What just happened in Yunxi?*
