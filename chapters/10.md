# Chapter 10: The Silent Watch

Hundreds of miles away, inside a heavily guarded red-walled compound at the heart of the national capital, the night was usually so quiet you could hear wind brush the pine needles outside.

Tonight, the classified terminal on the senior cabinet leader's desk chimed once and flooded dark crimson.

*ABSOLUTE ORDER ACTIVATED. ZONE: LANSHUI PROVINCE, YUNXI COUNTY. RADIUS: ONE HUNDRED KILOMETERS. AUTHORITY: SUPREME.*

The leader's fountain pen stopped mid-stroke, leaving a wet ink blot across a state memorandum.

Before he could even press the desk intercom, the heavy mahogany doors slid open without a sound. No sentry outside called a halt. No security buzzer tripped.

Eight figures in matte-black uniforms and featureless full-face masks stepped into the study and spread into position with eerie, silent speed. Two sealed the doorway. Two took the curtained windows. Four lined the wooden walls behind the desk, completely motionless.

The leader pushed back his chair and rose, his face flushing with shock and sudden anger. "Who authorized you into this compound?! Identify yourselves at once! Guard room! Where are my guards?!"

Not one of the eight masked figures answered.

No visor tilted. No hand shifted. Complete, suffocating mute silence filled the study.

His pulse hammering in his throat, the leader snatched up his secure red telephone and barked straight into the Central Guard Bureau's night desk. "Director! Eight armed men in black masks just walked straight into my study! Are you asleep over there?! Send a response team right now!"

For three full seconds, only ragged breathing came down the line.

"Sir," the Central Guard Bureau director whispered back, his voice trembling with sheer disbelief. "Seven of them are standing inside my own office right now. They walked past three biometric doors without tripping a single sensor. They haven't spoken a word."

The leader's fingers went numb on the receiver, and the anger drained straight out of his face, leaving only cold, creeping dread.

* * *

At the exact same minute, the same silent shadow dropped over command centers across multiple provinces and major cities.

Inside the Lanshui Provincial Command Center, the main operations floor erupted into frantic shouting as seven masked figures stepped through the reinforced glass doors without a single badge swipe. They walked calmly past stunned duty officers and formed a silent ring around the Provincial Party Secretary and the duty director.

"Stop! Freeze right there!" a security captain barked, his hand flying toward his holster before his own director screamed at him to stand down.

"Don't touch your weapon, you idiot!" the duty director yelled, sweat pouring down his temples. "Look at the doors! They opened every Level-One lock without a code!"

The Provincial Party Secretary stared at the seven dark masks encircling his console, his chest heaving. "Who sent you? Are you from the capital? Give me a unit number! Give me an order number!"

Silence. Not a single syllable came back. Every officer on the operations floor stood frozen as the seven masked figures watched every screen and every phone like carved iron statues.

Across neighboring provinces, five to ten silent, masked operatives materialized inside regional security headquarters and governors' offices, cutting off unverified lines with the exact same mute finality.

Down in Qingyan City, inside the Municipal Party Committee Building, the City Party Secretary was barking at his aides over the sudden communications blackout in Yunxi when the conference room doors clicked open. Six featureless black-masked figures stepped inside and took positions directly behind his chair and along both exits.

The City Party Secretary jumped so violently his porcelain teacup crashed to the floor and shattered.

"Who the hell are you people?!" he shouted, his voice cracking as his aides shrank back against the walls. "I am the Party Secretary of Qingyan! Show me your credentials! Did the province send you? Tell me what happened in Yunxi!"

Not one of the six masked figures answered a single word. They did not explain, did not nod, and did not blink. Around the table, six senior municipal leaders sat drenched in cold sweat, realizing they could not even reach for their phones without six silent pairs of eyes tracking every finger.

* * *

Across the ocean, inside heavily shielded intelligence watchrooms in Washington, Moscow, and London, night-shift analysts shot out of their chairs as orbital arrays flashed urgent crimson warnings across their main screens.

"Sir, you need to see this immediately!" a senior analyst in Washington called out, his voice tight with disbelief. "We just lost all optical, thermal, and radar telemetry over a ten-kilometer grid in eastern China, Lanshui Province! And there is a hundred-kilometer combat air patrol spinning up around it right now!"

The watch commander rushed to the console, his brow furrowing until he saw the secondary signature scrolling across the bottom of the display. All the blood drained from his face.

"Category-Zero," the watch commander breathed, grabbing the direct line to the Defense Secretary's residence. "Wake the Secretary right now. That defense signature hasn't lit up on a global monitor in twenty years. Something massive just woke up in China, and we are completely blind."

In Moscow and London, identical urgent calls tore through defense ministries in the dead of night. Across half the globe, seasoned intelligence chiefs stared at a tiny county name glowing on their orbital maps, asking the exact same frantic question: *What in God's name just happened in Yunxi?*
