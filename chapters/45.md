# Chapter 45: The Foreign Cutouts in Shanghai

At twenty minutes past three on Monday morning, blue screen light cut through thick cigarette smoke inside a forty-eighth-floor Bund penthouse in Shanghai. Six locked computer racks hummed along the wall, linking local bank lines straight to the Zurich Northern Ledger.

Claire Moreau leaned over her twin screens with a French cigarette between her teeth. Her eyes widened behind her glasses as red error lines poured down the glass. She spat the cigarette into a tray and slapped the desk.

"Putain! Viktor Kroll, stop pacing and look at the signal delay!" Claire Moreau snapped, her fingers flying across the keyboard. "Our satellite link did not drop at two thirty! It is still sending at full power, but every piece of data since midnight has been copied to a hidden stop inside the country! We are trapped in a closed loop!"

Viktor Kroll froze halfway across the dark room, a small cigar pinched between his fingers. His heavy jaw locked at once. He strode behind Claire Moreau's chair, squinting at the scrolling logs as color drained from his face.

"Scheisse," Viktor Kroll breathed in low, harsh German. "That is impossible. The Zurich Northern Ledger signal changes its secret code every ninety seconds. Nobody in Beijing has the code book to copy it without breaking the link!"

"They did not break the link, Viktor Kroll, they copied the main relay!" Claire Moreau spun in her chair, her voice sharp with panic. "Look at the path chart! We have been sending them a live copy of every local name, every bank code, and every military supply file for three hours! And now a lock has frozen my main controls!"

Across the room, Kenji Takahashi tore off his headset and threw it down, his face slick with sweat. "Kuso! Every local signal just went dark!" Kenji Takahashi shouted, jabbing a shaking finger at his map. "Jingzhou City heliport went silent fifty minutes ago! Division Chief Zhao Dehan's capital pager died at two twelve! Shanghai Port dropped off the map twenty minutes ago, and Marcus Vance's panic alarm never even went off! Even the Southern Theater supply line is dead!"

Viktor Kroll crushed his burning cigar straight into the glass table, ignoring the hot ash stinging his thumb. "We are burned! The entire China desk is burned to the ground!"

"Who moved?" Kenji Takahashi demanded, his eyes darting wildly toward the heavy steel front door. "Was it State Security? We have two contacts inside the Shanghai office! They would have sent a six-hour warning!"

"Forget the contacts, Kenji Takahashi, they are already in cuffs!" Viktor Kroll barked, lunging toward the red control box beside the racks. "Burn everything! Right now! Claire Moreau, hit the fire switches on Racks One through Six! Kenji Takahashi, drop the code books into the acid and pull the pins on the Zurich storage drives! If Beijing gets the raw Northern Ledger files intact, Geneva will cut us loose before sunrise!"

"I am trying!" Claire Moreau flipped the cover on the red switch beside her desk and slammed the button twice.

Nothing happened. No cooling fans stopped. No orange warning lights flashed. The small screen above the fire charges showed one flat white word: DISABLED.

"Merde! Merde! It is dead!" Claire Moreau screamed, her voice cracking as she typed wildly before her keyboard lights blinked off for good. "The power switches have been cut off from the main line! Somebody killed our burn system before we even knew we were being copied!"

"Then burn them by hand!" Viktor Kroll roared, tearing open a wall cabinet. He grabbed a silenced pistol in one hand and a brass fire pin in the other. "Kenji Takahashi, the backup pin on Rack Four! Pull it now!"

Kenji Takahashi scrambled across the marble floor, knocking over a chair as he lunged for the silver canister clamped to Rack Four. His hands shook so badly his nails scraped twice off the brass ring before catching it. He braced his knee against the rack and pulled hard.

From the front entry ten meters away came three slow, heavy metal clicks.

All three spies froze.

The penthouse front door was thick steel, held by six heavy bolts and a fingerprint lock that opened only from inside. There was no blast. No flashbang. No shouting from the stairs.

One by one, the six steel bolts slid smoothly backward on their own from outside.

"Nein," Viktor Kroll whispered, eyes wide and bloodshot as he swung his pistol toward the door. "Drop the pin, Kenji Takahashi! Take cover!"

The armored door swung smoothly inward on silent hinges.

Before Viktor Kroll's finger could pull the trigger, six figures in plain black gear flowed across the marble floor like dark water. Not a single boot scraped the stone.

The wall clock read three twenty-four and ten seconds.

Viktor Kroll fired once toward the lead shadow. The silenced pistol coughed softly and missed as the black-clad figure stepped inside his aim with blinding speed. A gloved forearm struck Viktor Kroll's wrist with a sharp crack. The pistol spun away, and a second blow to the side of his neck dropped him flat onto the marble.

Claire Moreau shrieked in French and lunged for a knife taped under her keyboard tray. Before her fingers touched the handle, an operative vaulted cleanly over the glass desk, pinned her arm flat, and twisted her shoulder back until she gasped and went limp.

Beside Rack Four, Kenji Takahashi finally pulled the brass ring free from the fire canister and raised it to slam the pin against the metal drive bay.

He never finished the swing.

A gloved hand clamped around Kenji Takahashi's wrist in midair like a steel clamp, stopping his arm dead two inches above the rack. With one clean twist, the operative stripped the canister from Kenji Takahashi's numb fingers, swept his legs out, and drove his face hard into the cold floor tiles.

The wall clock read three twenty-four and thirty seconds.

Twenty seconds from the first unlocked bolt to total silence.

Viktor Kroll groaned weakly on the marble as cold cuffs snapped tight behind his back. Claire Moreau stared up with tear-filled eyes, her chest heaving in shallow gasps. Kenji Takahashi lay pinned beside the untouched racks, trembling under the steady hum of the cooling fans.

The lead black-clad figure walked down the row of six glowing racks. It paused beside Rack Four to check the intact fire seals and steady green lights on every Zurich Northern Ledger drive.

Raising two gloved fingers to its blank helmet, the figure spoke into a private channel in a quiet, even voice.

"Shanghai relay secured. Three foreign handlers taken. All six Zurich Northern Ledger hard drives and local code books captured intact. Nothing burned. Capital, Jingzhou City, Shanghai Port, Southern Theater Command, and Shanghai sweeps complete. Waiting for transfer orders."

Through the forty-eighth-floor glass, the Bund lay silent and dark under the pre-dawn haze. Nobody outside knew that a 1.2 trillion yuan shadow network had been erased in two hours without a single siren sounding across the country.
