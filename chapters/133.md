# Chapter 133: Congee and Handcuffs

Bright morning sunshine poured through the tall glass windows of the Shanshui Villa breakfast hall at eight o'clock sharp.

At a sunny corner table overlooking the garden pond, the mood was downright peaceful.

Two steaming bowls of white millet congee, crisp fried dough sticks, pickled radish cubes, and a basket of pork soup dumplings sat between Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan.

Qin Yuhan wore a clean light-blue silk blouse and a cream jacket, looking refreshed after ten hours of deep sleep. Across from her, Chen Xuan wore his favorite gray cotton hoodie, unhurriedly tapping a warm boiled egg against his saucer.

"You slept like a little pig last night, President Qin," Chen Xuan teased softly, peeling the shell off the boiled egg in neat strips. "Even when the sprinklers turned on at six, you just pulled my pillow over your head and kept snoring."

Qin Yuhan blushed faintly and tapped his sneaker under the tablecloth. "I do not snore! And whose fault is it that the quilt was tucked so tight I could barely wiggle my toes?"

"A tight quilt keeps the cold draft out," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, placing the peeled egg right into her spoon. "Here. Eat the whole egg first before you touch your tablet. You always skip the yolk whenever an email pops up."

Qin Yuhan smiled warmly, her dark eyes softening as she took a bite of the warm egg. "What about you? You have been peeling eggs and pouring soy milk for five minutes and have not eaten a single dumpling."

"Watching the richest beauty in Shanghai eat breakfast already fills me up halfway," Chen Xuan grinned, dipping a piece of fried dough into his congee. "The pork dumplings can cover the other half."

Qin Yuhan let out a quiet laugh, shaking her head at his sweet talk, and slid the steamer closer to his elbow. "Eat two right now before they get cold, Mr. Clerk."

Ten tables away, near the center fountain, the mood was nowhere near as cheerful.

Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen sat stiffly behind untouched pastries with dark circles under his eyes. Beside him sat Cai Chenggong and Li Weiming, who still wore his glasses and Marcus Vance badge, with two black suitcases beside his chair.

"Why did Scar Liu never call back last night?" Cai Chenggong whispered nervously, wiping sweat from his neck. "I walked past the fourteenth floor at seven, and the hallway was empty!"

"Shut your mouth and drink your coffee," Li Weiming muttered tightly, checking his watch. "My driver is bringing the car to the east gate at eight fifteen. Once we reach the highway, Zurich can handle the rest."

Ding Yizhen tapped his teacup against the saucer with a trembling hand. "You told me the Swiss trust was invisible, Li Weiming! After the way Secretary Sha looked at me yesterday, I am not staying in Jingzhou City past nine!"

Before Li Weiming could answer, the glass double doors at the entrance of the breakfast hall swung open with a crisp click.

All the morning chatter among the fifty business guests and harbor directors inside died down at once.

Walking through the doorway in dark uniforms were Handong Provincial Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming and Anti-Corruption Bureau Director Hou Liangping, followed by twelve investigators.

Four officers immediately stepped aside to guard the exits.

Qin Yuhan paused with her spoon halfway to her lips, her eyes widening as she looked across the sunny dining room.

"Chen Xuan, look," she whispered. "Is that not Director Hou Liangping and Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming?"

"Looks like them," Chen Xuan said calmly, biting into a warm dumpling and passing her a napkin. "Eat slowly, wife. Do not let a free show spoil good millet congee."

At the center table, Li Weiming bolted upright from his chair, reaching for his suitcase. "We are leaving right now!"

"Nobody at this table is catching a flight today, Mr. Li Weiming," Hou Liangping's clear, sharp voice rang out across the floor.

Hou Liangping stopped in front of the center table and held up three detention warrants stamped with bright red seals.

"Or do you still prefer to be called Marcus Vance?" Hou Liangping asked with a cool smile. "Last night at 11:45 PM, four men carrying lock wedges and batons were found neatly tied up by the fourteenth-floor elevator. Before midnight, Scar Liu confessed every word about who hired him to rob Suite 1408, along with the hidden fiber cable running out of Wanfeng Freight's warehouse."

Li Weiming's face drained of blood. His knees knocked against the table leg, rattling the cups.

Cai Chenggong tried to scramble backward, only for two investigators to press his shoulders down.

Ding Yizhen stood up abruptly, his voice cracking. "Director Hou! Chief Prosecutor Ji! I am a sitting Deputy Mayor of Jingzhou City! You cannot march into a public hall without provincial approval!"

Ji Changming stepped forward with a calm, stern expression.

"Secretary Sha Ruijin and the Provincial Party Committee signed your suspension and arrest transfer at six thirty this morning, Ding Yizhen," Ji Changming said clearly enough for every harbor director to hear. "35 million yuan in a Zurich family trust, 18 million yuan in Singapore transfer receipts, and a secret agreement to hand 80 billion yuan of Handong shipping data to Northern Ledger Limited. The bank records are already on the table."

A loud gasp rippled across the hall. Several shipping chairmen broke out in cold sweat.

Ding Yizhen collapsed back into his chair as if all the bones had left his spine.

"Cuff them," Hou Liangping ordered crisply.

Three pairs of steel handcuffs clicked shut around the wrists of Ding Yizhen, Cai Chenggong, and Li Weiming in broad daylight. Within two minutes, the investigators marched all three men out through the glass doors toward the police cars waiting in the driveway.

At the corner table, Qin Yuhan let out a long breath. She turned back to Chen Xuan, her dark eyes sparkling with wonder.

"Four thugs tried to rob our floor last night, and they found the Zurich bank accounts before eight," Qin Yuhan whispered, leaning across the table. "I did not hear a single sound last night! How did the Anti-Corruption Bureau move so fast?"

Chen Xuan poured another splash of warm soy milk into her cup and grinned lazily.

"Bad people always trip over their own shoelaces when my wife is right," Chen Xuan said cheerfully. "See? I told you the right doors unlock before breakfast. Now finish your egg before it gets cold, President Qin."

Qin Yuhan looked at his unbothered smile, shook her head with an affectionate laugh, and took another bite of her breakfast.
