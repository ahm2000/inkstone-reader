# Chapter 8: The Silent Watch

Hundreds of miles away, inside a heavily guarded red-walled compound at the heart of the national capital, classified terminals flashed a single line of dark crimson.

*ABSOLUTE ORDER ACTIVATED. UNIT: ZERO GUARD. ZONE: LANSHUI PROVINCE, YUNXI COUNTY. RADIUS: ONE HUNDRED KILOMETERS. AUTHORITY: SUPREME.*

Across the entire country, the highest tier skipped a beat.

Before the senior elder in the red-walled study could even set down his pen, the heavy mahogany doors slid open without a sound. No guard outside shouted a warning. No alarm rang.

Eight figures in matte-black uniforms and featureless full-face masks stepped into the room and spread smoothly into position. Two sealed the doorway. Two took the curtained windows. Four lined the wooden walls behind the elder's desk, completely motionless.

The elder stared at them, his hand freezing over his untouched cup of tea. "What happened in Lanshui? Who triggered an Absolute Order in Yunxi County? How long are you stationed here?"

Not one of the eight masked figures answered.

No head tilted. No hand moved. They stood in total, suffocating mute silence, their dark visors fixed straight ahead like carved iron.

* * *

At the exact same minute, the same silent wave hit command centers across multiple provinces and major cities.

In the Lanshui Provincial Command Center, seven masked Zero Guard operatives stepped onto the main operations floor without a single pass-card beep, forming a quiet ring around the provincial Party Secretary and the duty director. In neighboring provinces, five to ten masked figures materialized inside regional security headquarters and governor offices, cutting off every unverified line.

Down in Qingyan City, inside the Municipal Party Committee Building, the City Party Secretary was jerked out of an emergency briefing when six silent, featureless figures stepped into his conference room and stood directly behind his chair and along the exits.

"Who are you people?!" the City Party Secretary demanded, his voice cracking as cold sweat drenched his collar. "I am the Party Secretary of Qingyan! Show me your credentials! Tell me what happened in Yunxi!"

Complete, mute silence answered him.

The six masked figures did not answer a single question. They did not explain, did not nod, and did not blink. Every high-ranking official in the capital, in the provincial command centers, and across Qingyan City suddenly realized the same terrifying truth: while the Zero Guard was active, every person in a high position was under a five-to-ten-man silent watch, and nobody had the rank to receive an answer.

* * *

Across the ocean, in heavily shielded intelligence watchrooms in Washington, Moscow, and London, senior duty officers jolted upright as orbital arrays reported a sudden ten-kilometer dead zone in eastern China, surrounded by a hundred-kilometer combat air patrol and a legendary Category-Zero defense signature that had not appeared on any global monitor in twenty years. Urgent calls tore through foreign defense ministries in the dead of night, asking the exact same frantic question: *What in God's name just woke up in Lanshui?*

Deep beneath the earth in the National Supreme Military Command Center, every theater command, air defense network, and strategic missile brigade across the country shifted silently to full combat readiness.

At the head of the glass command deck stood the highest currently serving military commander in the nation, a four-star veteran who had commanded millions without ever losing his nerve. Tonight, ten fully masked Zero Guard operatives stood mute along the perimeter of his own command deck, watching every screen and every phone in dead silence.

The commander glanced once at the silent black masks behind his shoulder, felt cold sweat bead along his collar, and turned back to the glowing crimson ring over Yunxi County.

"Every theater is armed and waiting for orders, General," his chief of staff reported in a tight whisper, barely daring to breathe near the ten mute figures. "Air defense is hot. Fighter wings are holding the hundred-kilometer shell. Naval fleets are on standby. Foreign satellites are already pinging the blackout. We have to know if this is an attack."

With no other choice, the supreme commander walked to the isolated red console and initiated a manual clearance call into the Black Pine Guard relay network. Layer by layer, voiceprint by voiceprint, operative by operative, his authorization was verified through five blind security nodes until the encrypted line finally connected to a single quiet desk in the eastern courtyard of Qingyan City.

"Number One," the four-star commander said, his voice tight with urgent, careful respect. "This is Supreme Military Command. Ten Zero Guard operatives are standing mute on my own deck right now. Every high official in the capital, the provinces, and the cities is under silent watch. Every military district in the country is at full combat readiness, fighter wings are holding the hundred-kilometer shell, and international intelligence rooms are already reacting to the blackout. Tell me what happened in Yunxi, and tell me whether the nation is going to war."

On the other end of the line, Number One sat alone beside his cracked porcelain saucer.

"Hold every theater command in place, General, and keep every outside force clear of Yunxi," Number One replied evenly. "As for what is happening inside that zone, I cannot give you a full picture."

The commander's grip tightened on the receiver. "Even you don't know?"

"Even I do not hold an activation key for the Zero Guard, and I have no command, no authority, and no permission over them once the Boss activates them himself," Number One said quietly. "I do not know their exact numbers, I do not know every weapon system they have armed, and I cannot order them to stand down. All I can tell you is that I hope this ends quietly, General. Because while the Zero Guard is active, nobody on this earth can stop them except him."

Silence hung heavy over the encrypted line.

In the red-walled compound in the capital, in the provincial command centers, and across Qingyan City, the highest leaders of the nation sat frozen under the mute watch of five to ten black-masked ghosts, completely powerless to step inside a single county or even earn a single spoken word.

Meanwhile, in Room 601 of Building 2, Chen Xuan turned off the bathroom tap, dried his hands on a clean towel, and walked back to his bed to sleep.
