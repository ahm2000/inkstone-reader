# Chapter 8: Five Pillars Frozen

Within minutes, the night over Yunxi County changed.

Silent figures in black gear and featureless full-face masks stepped into a fifty-meter ring around Building 2. More spread across the ten-kilometer core. No skin showed. No rank marks showed. No radio chatter broke the night.

Beneath quiet hillsides and rooftop vents, hidden defense grids woke up without a sound. Anti-missile and anti-aircraft launchers moved into firing position. Sniper teams locked onto every crossing, courtyard and stairwell. Electronic jammers hummed on, and ten kilometers of sky and satellite feeds went dead black.

From the core out to a hundred kilometers, patrols and checkpoints snapped into place within minutes. Expressway ramps and border roads were closed for "roadworks," and every car was checked quietly.

Far above the clouds, pairs of fighter jets flew a silent patrol over the hundred-kilometer ring. Electronic-warfare aircraft flew with them.

The air over Yunxi County went very still.

Here is what the lockdown was, and what it was not.

Ordinary people were not locked in. Phones, shops, buses and bars worked as always, and most of the town noticed only some roadworks and a quiet night.

Zero sentries stood at every junction and on the roofs and stairs around Building 2, dressed as road crews, taxi drivers and delivery men. They watched anyone who came near the ring and let life go on.

The people in the dark were the officials. Every leader who could send orders into Yunxi got masked visitors, and every government, police and military network was blocked.

The Black Pine Guard was pulled back. Its civilians could watch and report, but could not move, touch or stop anything.

The Order named only Building 2, so it grew outward from there, shutting every channel that could reach the building.

Inside the ring, Chen Xuan was asleep.

* * *

Inside the County Party Committee Building, Fan Weiguo was still awake at his desk when the heavy oak door opened without a knock.

His secretary did not have time to stand. Two masked figures walked in, light and silent.

Fan Weiguo got to his feet. "Who are you? How did you get past the armed guards? Call security."

Nobody answered. Through the open door he saw his secretary and two compound guards standing against the corridor wall, pale as paper. Two more masked figures watched them.

One of the masked figures laid a matte-black card in the center of his desk. When it spoke, the voice was flat. Fan Weiguo could not tell if it was a man or a woman, young or old.

"Zero Order. Lockdown."

That was all. The two figures stepped back against the wall and stayed mute.

Fan Weiguo stared at the card. His face went red, then pale. "Zero Order? I have served thirty years in government. I have never heard of a Zero unit. Are you from the province? Answer me."

Neither figure moved.

His hands shook as he picked up the red landline and dialed the emergency desk of the Qingyan City Party Secretary. Voice lines were still open, though screens, files and orders were not. It connected on the first ring. A hoarse whisper came back.

"Fan Weiguo, listen. Are there masked people in your office?"

"Yes. Who are they?"

"I don't know. Six of them walked through three locked doors into my conference room, and no alarm rang. A minute ago the Provincial Party Secretary told me seven stand in his office too. Neither the city nor the province knows who they are. Every provincial screen says INSUFFICIENT PRIVILEGE, and an override from the central network has locked every terminal in Lanshui." His voice cracked with raw panic. "Do not argue with them. Do not let your guards touch a weapon. Sit still and wait for word from above."

Click. The line went dead.

Fan Weiguo's fingers went numb, and the receiver slid onto the desk. In one minute, every lock, every network and every superior above him had been shut down.

At the same time, in the County Police Bureau, its Director, Mai Zhenhua, grabbed his pistol and shouted for his SWAT squad. He pulled open his door and found six SWAT officers standing disarmed under masked watch.

His red desk phone rang. It was the City Police Chief, shouting that the bureau was cut off from the central server by an INSUFFICIENT PRIVILEGE block. He ordered Mai Zhenhua to put down his weapon, freeze every police station in Yunxi, and let no officer fire a shot.

Across the street in the County Government Building, Tao Shu, who had skipped dinner with a cold and gone back to his office to read, stared at a black card on his desk. Two masked figures stood beside his window. His hands shook.

Fan Weiguo sank into his leather chair. Cold sweat soaked his shirt. Thirty years of experience could not explain this.

Every veteran official knew the five powers that governed a county, and when each one applied.

In ordinary times, the County Party Committee and the County Government ran the budget, the land and the staff.

Beside them stood the County People's Congress and the Consultative Conference. Their vote was needed for local rules, big budgets and top appointments.

The courts, the procuratorate and the Discipline Inspection Commission held the law. They dealt with every crime, including crimes by officials.

If riots or a major crisis broke out, the police and the Armed Police took over the streets.

Only in the gravest national emergency, such as war or a huge disaster, did the military take control through the People's Armed Forces Department and the regional garrison. Then civilian power was set aside under martial law from the central military command.

These five were kept apart on purpose, so that no single office could swallow the rest.

Yet in five silent minutes, one card had overridden all five. Party and government, the people's congress, the courts and prosecutors, the police, and even the county garrison had lost their power at the same moment. Peacetime law, police emergency rules and wartime rules were all frozen by one Zero Order, from a height nobody at Fan Weiguo's level had imagined.

Fan Weiguo stared at the two masks by his wall and trembled. Somebody in his small county had woken something that stood above every law and every uniform in the country. He had no idea who.
