# Chapter 8: Pork-Rib Soup and Broken Teacups

Before Chen Jianguo could unlock Apartment 602, the steel door swung open.

Liu Meifang stood in her faded apron, the warm smell of ginger and pork-rib soup drifting behind her. Her eyes were red from worry, yet seeing her eldest son, a mother's fuss swept everything aside.

"Chen Xuan! You really came back tonight!" Liu Meifang pulled him in and pressed her warm palm to his cheek. "Why is your face thinner than at Spring Festival? Do they not let you eat on time in Shanghai? Take off your shoes."

"I am fine, Mom," Chen Xuan said gently, slipping into slippers and setting his thermos on the shoe cabinet.

"Sit down," Liu Meifang urged, hurrying to the kitchen. "I kept the clay pot warm since seven. Let me pour hot soup."

Chen Xuan entered the small living room. Two cracked teacups and damp tissues sat on the low table. Hunched on the sofa, Second Aunt Wu Lanying clutched a damp towel to her chest, eyes swollen nearly shut. Seeing Chen Xuan, she wiped her cheeks.

"Chen Xuan, you should not have rushed back," Wu Lanying whispered, fresh tears slipping past her fingers. "Twenty years on Old Street, your Second Uncle has never cursed a customer! Hongrui Real Estate's men smashed our glass counter and shoved him into the shelves, then police handcuffed him and claimed he beat their foreman!"

"Second Aunt, catch your breath," Chen Xuan said softly, nudging the warm water closer to her shaking fingers. "Have you eaten since noon?"

Wu Lanying shook her head, breath hitching. "How could I eat while Mingde sits in a cell?"

"He will come home cleanly," Chen Xuan said evenly. "Drink water first. If you faint before he walks in, he will scold us all."

Wu Lanying blinked up at his steady eyes and took a shaky sip.

Beside the balcony door, a folding stool scraped the tiles.

Seventeen-year-old Chen Hao sat hunched in his school jacket, knuckles white, face burning with helpless anger.

"How is he coming home cleanly, Big Brother?" Chen Hao burst out, voice cracking. "I passed Old Street after study hall! I saw them twisting Second Uncle's arms behind his back while Yan Tiehu stood smoking and laughing! Half the street peeked behind curtains and nobody stepped out! If I had a brick, I would have smashed Yan Tiehu's windshield!"

"Chen Hao! Shut your mouth and sit still!" Chen Jianguo snapped from the hallway, dropping Chen Xuan's bag, anger covering his fear. "Want a cell next to your Second Uncle? You have college entrance exams in seven months! Let me hear one more word about bricks!"

Chen Hao's jaw locked. "So we just let them bully us in broad daylight?"

"Shouting here will not open a cell door," Chen Xuan said quietly, meeting his eyes. His calm voice stiffened Chen Hao's shoulders. "Keep your head cool, Chen Hao. Losing your temper gives Yan Tiehu what he wants."

Chen Hao bit his lip, glared at the dark balcony glass, and sank onto his stool.

At the dining table, nineteen-year-old Chen Yuan locked her phone and set it down, dark circles under her eyes.

"It is useless, Big Brother," Chen Yuan said quietly. "Since five, I called six local contacts in Yunxi. Classmates with uncles in county offices, a teacher's cousin at the supply depot, even the neighborhood clerk who bought hinges from Second Uncle every month."

She gave a bitter laugh close to a sob. "Four never answered. The other two sounded polite until I mentioned Hongrui Real Estate and Old Street Station, then remembered urgent meetings and hung up."

Silence pressed over the room. Chen Yuan stared at her knuckles, shoulders slumping.

"You did what you could, Chen Yuan," Chen Xuan said softly. "You did not need to beg them."

Chen Jianguo stood near the table, then cleared his throat loudly, tilted his chin toward the light so his tears would not fall, and slapped his thighs.

"Why is everyone hanging their heads?" Chen Jianguo asked roughly, squaring his shoulders. "Remember the old saying: a slow ox still reaches the field. Panic never pulled a plow straight. Right is on our side. Nobody falls apart tonight."

Chen Xuan watched his father's red eyes and straight back with quiet warmth. Chen Jianguo had invented half his sayings during hard winters so his family always had something solid to lean on.

Liu Meifang set a steaming bowl of pork-rib soup before Chen Xuan. "Listen to your father. Drink while it is hot. Chen Yuan, Chen Hao, get your bowls."

Chen Xuan lifted his spoon. On the sofa, Wu Lanying spoke barely above a whisper.

"Not everyone on Old Street is cold-hearted," Wu Lanying murmured, twisting her towel. "All week, since Yan Tiehu's men watched our door, neighbors crossed the road. The only person who walked into Mingde Hardware was your old classmate, Chen Xuan. Wang Lei."

Chen Xuan's spoon stopped above the soup.

"Wang Lei?" he asked softly.

"Yes, Wang Lei," Wu Lanying whispered with trembling gratitude. "He has a hard life now, driving a beat-up electric delivery cart, his jacket stained with dust and oil. Yesterday afternoon, right in front of Hongrui Real Estate's men, he parked outside, put down crumpled bills, and bought one iron door hinge he did not need. He stood drinking boiled water with your Second Uncle for ten minutes so he would not feel alone."

For three seconds, Chen Xuan did not move.

A memory rose from senior year of high school. When Chen Jianguo broke his wrist fixing a water pipe and the family scraped every coin, Chen Xuan stopped eating in the cafeteria. Every day for four months, two meat buns and a salted egg sat on his desk. Whenever Chen Xuan asked, Wang Lei only shrugged and kept reading.

Chen Xuan sipped his soup calmly, carving Wang Lei's name deep in his mind.

"Does he still deliver near Old Street?" Chen Xuan asked quietly.

"Every afternoon," Wu Lanying murmured. Then her face went pale. She gasped and clutched her sweater.

"Oh no!" Wu Lanying breathed in alarm. "The shop! When police dragged Chen Mingde into the car, I dropped the padlock on the counter and ran after them! The shutter is halfway down, and Mingde Hardware was left unlocked!"

Chen Jianguo pushed his chair back. "Do not panic, Wu Lanying. Drink your soup. I will lock it right now."

Chen Xuan set his spoon down and stood beside his father. "I will go with you, Dad."
