# Chapter 41: The Vice Minister's Safe

At two in the morning on Monday, the paper shredder inside Vice Minister of Energy He Guoliang's study whined at full speed, spilling strips of account paper around his slippers. A cigarette hung from his mouth, dropping ash onto a Zurich bank sheet.

"Faster, Shen Yumei, faster!" He Guoliang shouted without looking up from the open wall safe, sweat running into his silk collar. "Do not pack sweaters or jewelry! Bring the four passports, the Saint Kitts papers, and the black folder from your dressing table!"

Shen Yumei rushed in wearing her silk robe, pale and clutching a zippered leather pouch. "He Guoliang, you are scaring me to death! What happened? Why did the front gate intercom go dead? I called the guard post twice and nobody answered!"

"Because the line is cut, or the guards were taken, or both!" He Guoliang snapped, jamming overseas bank papers into the shredder. "Did you reach our son on the secure phone in Zurich?"

"It rang once and dropped!" Shen Yumei's voice cracked through the smoke haze. "Tell me the truth! Is it the Central Discipline Inspection Commission? Did someone from Sha City report you?"

He Guoliang whipped around, eyes bloodshot, pointing his cigarette at a grey pager glowing solid red on his desk. "Discipline Inspection does not cut phone lines to a minister's home at two in the morning! They send two deputies in daytime cars with a polite call to my secretary!"

His jaw locked. He crushed the cigarette out and shook another from a soft pack, his thumb shaking so badly the lighter clicked three times before catching.

"Ten minutes ago, Fang Qiming's emergency alarm in Sha City flashed red and died," He Guoliang hissed. "Five minutes later, my bank contact at the Zurich Northern Ledger stopped answering. Something huge has moved, Shen Yumei. Something that does not file paperwork with any ministry."

Shen Yumei's shoulders shook. "You swore the Zurich accounts were untouchable! You told me three layers of fake names meant nobody in the capital could trace a cent back to you!"

"Shut up and put the passports in the briefcase!" He Guoliang barked, lunging back to the safe for two metal hard drives and a blue cloth account book. "We take the back elevator to the garage. My driver leaves the car unlocked with special government plates in the trunk. At Binhai private airfield, the jet to Singapore is fueled and waiting."

"Your driver is not answering either!" Shen Yumei cried, tears spilling down her cheeks. "I called his mobile from the hallway! It went straight to a dead tone!"

He Guoliang froze, a cold knot tightening in his gut. He took a hard drag and forced his voice steady. "Then I drive myself. Give me the pouch."

He shoved the blue cloth account book into the shredder.

The steel blades caught the cover and stopped dead.

The whine vanished. In the same heartbeat, the ceiling lights blinked out, leaving only the dim yellow backup strip above the door. Before He Guoliang could curse, the electronic lock on his wall safe clicked backward on its own and swung wide open.

Shen Yumei gasped and stumbled against the bookcase.

The study's double doors stood wide open. He Guoliang had not heard a handle turn or a single footstep.

Three figures stood just inside the doorway in plain black gear from boots to helmets, smooth blank visors reflecting nothing. No badges, rank marks, or weapons showed, yet their stillness felt heavier than a loaded rifle.

He Guoliang's cigarette slipped from his fingers and rolled across the carpet. He swallowed hard and forced his back straight from thirty years of habit.

"Who are you?" He Guoliang demanded, voice too loud in the quiet room. "I am He Guoliang, Vice Minister of Energy! This is a minister's home guarded by capital troops! Show me your ID and a signed warrant from the Central Committee right now, or every man here faces a military court before sunrise!"

Shen Yumei pressed both hands over her mouth, eyes darting wildly between the three silent figures and her husband.

None of the three moved or reached for a badge.

The center figure tilted its helmet slightly. Its voice came through the mask calm and even, sending a chill across He Guoliang's arms.

"He Guoliang. Account holder, Zurich Northern Ledger, account CH-94-ZRH-8841-NORD-09. Second route through Cayman Holdings Seven. Total stolen power-grid money: 47.3 billion yuan."

Silence dropped over the study like iron.

All color drained from He Guoliang's face, leaving it pale as wax. That account number was not written on any paper in his safe. He had memorized it in Geneva six years ago and never spoken it aloud inside the country, not even to Shen Yumei.

"How," He Guoliang whispered, throat locking. "That code is off every network. Only the Zurich bank heads and the relay in Shanghai have it."

The guard's tone did not change. "Your right to hold public office has ended. Step away from the safe."

He Guoliang's knees gave out. He hit the carpet hard, palms slapping shredded paper. Beside the bookcase, Shen Yumei slid to the floor, clutching the passport pouch and sobbing silently.

"Wait! Wait, please!" He Guoliang scrambled forward on his knees, pride shattering into panic as sweat poured off his chin. "I was not the leader! I swear on my ancestors, I was only a middleman! You want the real thieves inside the ministry? I can give them to you right now!"

He clawed at the desk edge to shout. "Deputy Director Tang Zhihao of the Energy Planning Bureau! He built the fake power-grid deals and took 9 billion yuan through the same Zurich desk! And Department Chief Peng Weidong at the Offshore Buying Office! Peng Weidong signed every fake shipping paper for Shanghai Port! Take them! I will testify against Tang Zhihao and Peng Weidong under oath! I will sign a full confession tonight! Just leave Shen Yumei out of this and go easy on me!"

He Guoliang stared up at the blank black visor, begging for any sign of feeling.

There was none.

"Tang Zhihao was taken at two zero four," the guard said evenly. "Peng Weidong was taken at two zero nine. Both are already in transport."

He Guoliang's mouth fell open. His hands slid limp off the desk.

"Collect the hard drives and the account book," the voice ordered. "Take He Guoliang for transfer. Place Shen Yumei under house arrest while her money is checked."

Two black-clad figures stepped forward in silence. When gloved fingers closed around He Guoliang's elbows and lifted him effortlessly, the Vice Minister of Energy did not fight. He stared back at the empty safe as the dark hallway swallowed him whole.
