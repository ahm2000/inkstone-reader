# Chapter 41: The Vice Minister's Safe

At two in the morning on Monday, the big paper shredder inside Vice Minister of Energy He Guoliang's study whined at full speed. Strips of thick account paper spilled over its rim around his slippers. A cigarette hung from the corner of He Guoliang's mouth, dropping ash onto a Zurich bank paper.

"Faster, Shen Yumei, faster!" He Guoliang shouted without looking up from the open wall safe. Sweat ran down his temples into his open silk collar. "Do not pack sweaters! Do not pack jewelry! Bring the four passports, the Saint Kitts papers, and the black folder from your dressing table!"

Shen Yumei rushed through the doorway in her silk robe. She was pale and clutched a zippered leather pouch against her chest. "He Guoliang, you are scaring me to death! What happened? Why did the front gate intercom go dead? I called the guard post twice and nobody answered!"

"Because the line is cut, or the guards were taken, or both!" He Guoliang snapped, jamming overseas bank papers into the shredder's teeth. "Did you reach our son on the secure phone in Zurich?"

"It rang once and dropped!" Shen Yumei's voice cracked. She squinted through the blue haze of cigarette smoke. "He Guoliang, tell me the truth! Is it the Central Discipline Inspection Commission? Did someone from Sha City report you?"

He Guoliang whipped around, eyes bloodshot and wide. He pulled the cigarette from his lips and pointed it at the desk, where a grey pager glowed solid red. "Discipline Inspection does not cut the phone lines to a minister's home at two in the morning! Discipline Inspection sends two deputies in daytime cars with a polite call to my secretary!"

His jaw locked hard. He crushed the cigarette into a full ashtray and shook another from a soft pack. His thumb shook so badly that the lighter clicked three times before it caught.

"Ten minutes ago, Fang Qiming's emergency alarm in Sha City flashed red and went dead," He Guoliang hissed. "Five minutes after that, my bank contact at the Zurich Northern Ledger stopped answering messages. Something huge has moved, Shen Yumei. Something that does not file paperwork with any ministry."

Shen Yumei's shoulders began to shake. "You swore to me the Zurich accounts were untouchable! You told me three layers of fake names meant nobody in the capital could ever trace a single cent back to you!"

"Shut up and put the passports in the briefcase!" He Guoliang barked, lunging back to the safe. He pulled out two metal hard drives and a blue cloth account book. "We take the back elevator to the garage. My driver leaves the car unlocked with special government license plates in the trunk. Once we reach Binhai private airfield, the private jet to Singapore is fueled and waiting."

"Your driver is not answering either, He Guoliang!" Shen Yumei cried, tears spilling down her cheeks. "I called his mobile from the hallway! It went straight to a dead tone!"

He Guoliang froze with his hand halfway to the shredder slot. A cold knot tightened in his gut. He took a hard drag on his cigarette, burning the paper down an inch, and forced his voice steady. "Then I drive myself. Give me the pouch."

He shoved the blue cloth account book into the shredder's mouth.

The steel blades caught the cover and stopped dead.

The loud whine vanished. In the same heartbeat, the ceiling lights blinked out, leaving only the dim yellow backup strip above the door. Before He Guoliang could curse, the electronic lock on his wall safe clicked backward on its own and swung the steel door wide open.

Shen Yumei let out a thin gasp and stumbled back against the bookcase.

The study's double doors were already open. He Guoliang had not heard a handle turn or a single footstep.

Three figures stood just inside the doorway. They wore plain black gear from boots to helmets, with smooth, blank visors that reflected nothing. No badges, no rank marks, and no weapons were in sight, yet their stillness felt heavier than a loaded rifle.

He Guoliang's cigarette slipped from his fingers and rolled across the carpet, trailing sparks. He swallowed hard and forced his back straight out of thirty years of habit as a high official.

"Who are you?" He Guoliang demanded, his voice echoing too loudly in the quiet room. "I am He Guoliang, Vice Minister of Energy! This is a top-level minister's home guarded by capital troops! Show me your ID and a signed warrant from the Central Committee right now, or every man in this doorway will face a military court before sunrise!"

Shen Yumei pressed both hands over her mouth. Her eyes darted wildly between the three silent figures and her husband's stiff back.

None of the three moved. None reached for a badge.

The center figure tilted its helmet a tiny bit. The voice came through the mask calm and even. It carried an even chill that made the hairs on He Guoliang's arms stand up.

"He Guoliang. Account holder, Zurich Northern Ledger, account CH-94-ZRH-8841-NORD-09. Second route through Cayman Holdings Seven. Total stolen power-grid money: 47.3 billion yuan."

Silence dropped over the study like iron.

All color drained from He Guoliang's face, leaving his skin pale as cold wax. That account number was not written on any paper inside his safe. He had memorized it in Geneva six years ago and had never spoken it out loud inside the country, not even to Shen Yumei.

"How," He Guoliang whispered, his throat locking up. "That code is kept off every network. Only the Zurich bank heads and the relay in Shanghai have it."

The guard's tone did not change. "Your right to hold public office has ended. Step away from the safe."

He Guoliang's knees simply gave out. He hit the carpet hard, palms slapping down on shredded paper. Beside the bookcase, Shen Yumei slid slowly to the floor, clutching the passport pouch to her stomach and sobbing without a sound.

"Wait! Wait, please!" He Guoliang scrambled forward on his knees, all his pride shattering into panic. Sweat poured off his chin. "I was not the leader! I swear on my ancestors, I was only a middleman! You want the real thieves inside the ministry? I can give them to you right now! Write their names down!"

He gasped and clawed at the desk edge to pull himself up enough to shout. "Deputy Director Tang Zhihao of the Energy Planning Bureau! He built the fake power-grid deals and took 9 billion yuan through the exact same Zurich desk! And Department Chief Peng Weidong at the Offshore Buying Office! Peng Weidong signed every fake shipping paper for Shanghai Port! Take them! I will testify against Tang Zhihao and Peng Weidong under oath! I will sign a full confession tonight! Just leave Shen Yumei out of this and go easy on me!"

He Guoliang stared up at the blank black visor, panting and begging for any sign of human feeling.

There was none.

"Tang Zhihao was taken at two zero four," the guard said evenly. "Peng Weidong was taken at two zero nine. Both are already in transport."

He Guoliang's mouth fell open. No sound came out. His hands slid limp off the heavy desk and hit his thighs.

"Collect the hard drives and the account book," the voice ordered. "Take He Guoliang for transfer. Place Shen Yumei under house arrest while her money is checked."

Two black-clad figures stepped forward in total silence. When cold gloved fingers closed around He Guoliang's elbows and lifted him with ease, the Vice Minister of Energy did not fight. He stared back at the empty safe as the dark hallway swallowed him whole.
