# Chapter 41: The Vice Minister's Safe

At two in the morning on Monday, the heavy industrial shredder inside Vice Minister of Energy He Guoliang's study whined at full pitch. Strips of ivory ledger paper spilled over its rim around his slippers. A cigarette hung from the corner of He Guoliang's mouth, ash dropping onto a Zurich clearing statement.

"Faster, Shen Yumei, faster!" He Guoliang shouted without looking up from the open wall safe. Sweat ran down his temples into his open silk collar. "Do not pack sweaters! Do not pack jewelry! Bring the four passports, the Saint Kitts certificates, and the black folio from your vanity!"

Shen Yumei rushed through the doorway in her silk robe, pale and clutching a zippered leather pouch against her chest. "He Guoliang, you are scaring me to death! What happened? Why did the front gate intercom go dead? I called the guard post twice and nobody answered!"

"Because the line is cut, or the shift is compromised, or both!" He Guoliang snapped, jamming offshore equity transfers into the shredder's teeth. "Did you reach our son on the encrypted phone in Zurich?"

"It rang once and dropped!" Shen Yumei's voice cracked. She squinted through the blue haze of cigarette smoke. "He Guoliang, tell me the truth! Is it the Central Discipline Inspection Commission? Did someone from Lanshui report you?"

He Guoliang whipped around, eyes bloodshot and wide. He plucked the cigarette from his lips and jabbed it at the desk, where a matte-grey pager glowed solid crimson. "Discipline Inspection does not kill the fiber trunk to a ministerial compound at two in the morning! Discipline Inspection sends two deputies in daytime sedans with a polite call to my secretary!"

His jaw locked in hard ridges. He crushed the cigarette into an overflowing ashtray and shook another from a soft pack, his thumb trembling so badly the lighter clicked three times before catching.

"Ten minutes ago, Fang Qiming's emergency beacon in Lanshui flashed red and vanished off the grid," He Guoliang hissed. "Five minutes after that, my clearing contact at the Zurich Northern Ledger stopped returning keystrokes. Something big has moved, Shen Yumei. Something that does not file paperwork with the Ministry of Supervision."

Shen Yumei's shoulders began to shake. "You swore to me the Zurich accounts were untouchable! You told me three layers of nominees meant nobody in the capital could ever trace a single fen back to you!"

"Shut up and put the passports in the briefcase!" He Guoliang barked, lunging back to the safe. He dragged out two steel-cased drives and a blue linen ledger. "We take the service elevator to the garage. My driver leaves the sedan unlocked with diplomatic transit plates in the trunk. Once we reach Binhai private airfield, the charter jet to Singapore is fueled."

"Your driver is not answering either, He Guoliang!" Shen Yumei cried, tears spilling over her lashes. "I called his mobile from the hallway! It went straight to a dead tone!"

He Guoliang froze with his hand halfway into the shredder slot. A cold knot tightened in his gut. He dragged hard on his cigarette, burning the paper down an inch, and forced his voice steady. "Then I drive myself. Give me the pouch."

He shoved the blue linen ledger into the shredder's mouth.

The steel blades caught the cover and stopped dead.

The whine vanished. In the same heartbeat, the ceiling chandelier blinked out, leaving only the dim amber backup strip above the doorway. Before He Guoliang could curse, the electronic bolt on his wall safe clicked backward on its own and swung the steel door wide open.

Shen Yumei let out a thin gasp and stumbled back against the bookcase.

The study's double doors were already open. He Guoliang had not heard a latch turn or a footstep.

Three figures stood just inside the threshold. They wore featureless matte-black gear from boots to helmets, with smooth, blank visors that reflected nothing. No insignia, no rank tabs, no visible weapons drawn, yet their stillness felt heavier than a loaded rifle.

He Guoliang's cigarette slipped from his fingers and rolled across the carpet, trailing sparks. He swallowed hard, forcing his back straight out of thirty years of bureaucratic habit.

"Who are you?" He Guoliang demanded, his voice echoing too loudly. "I am He Guoliang, Vice Minister of Energy! This is a Class-A ministerial residence under capital garrison protection! Show me your credentials and a signed warrant from the Central Committee right now, or every man in this doorway will face a military tribunal before sunrise!"

Shen Yumei pressed both hands over her mouth, her eyes darting wildly between the three silent figures and her husband's rigid back.

None of the three moved. None reached for a badge.

The center figure tilted its helmet a fraction of an inch. The voice that came through the mask was flat, neutral, and completely genderless, carrying an even chill that made the hairs on He Guoliang's arms stand upright.

"He Guoliang. Beneficiary string Zurich Northern Ledger, account CH-94-ZRH-8841-NORD-09. Secondary clearing conduit through Cayman Holdings Seven. Total diverted energy-grid settlement capital: 47 billion, 300 million yuan."

Silence dropped over the study like iron.

All color drained from He Guoliang's face, leaving his skin the shade of cold wax. That string existed on no paper inside his safe. He had memorized it in Geneva six years ago and had never spoken it aloud inside the country, not even to Shen Yumei.

"How," He Guoliang whispered, his throat seizing up. "That string is air-gapped. Only the Zurich vault directors and the relay in Shanghai hold the hash."

The flat, neutral, genderless voice did not shift in tone. "Your authorization to hold public office has been terminated. Step away from the safe."

He Guoliang's knees simply gave out. He hit the carpet hard, palms slapping down on shredded ledger paper. Beside the bookcase, Shen Yumei slid slowly to the floor, clutching the passport pouch to her stomach and sobbing without sound.

"Wait! Wait, please!" He Guoliang scrambled forward on his knees, his arrogance shattering into frantic terror. Sweat poured off his chin. "I was not the architect! I swear on my ancestors, I was only a middle signatory! You want the real thieves inside the ministry, I can give them to you right now! Write their names down!"

He gasped, clawing at the desk edge to pull himself up enough to shout. "Deputy Director Tang Zhihao of the Energy Planning Bureau! He built the phantom grid-capacity contracts and took 9 billion through the exact same Zurich desk! And Department Chief Peng Weidong at the Offshore Procurement Office! Peng Weidong signed every forged equipment manifest for Qingyan Port! Take them! I will testify against Tang Zhihao and Peng Weidong under oath! I will sign a full confession tonight, just leave Shen Yumei out of this and give me leniency!"

He Guoliang stared up at the blank matte-black visor, panting, begging for a flicker of human reaction.

There was none.

"Tang Zhihao was secured at two zero four," the flat, neutral, genderless voice said evenly. "Peng Weidong was secured at two zero nine. Both are already in transit."

He Guoliang's mouth fell open. No sound came out. His hands slid limp off the mahogany desk and struck his thighs.

"Collect the drives and the ledger," the voice instructed. "Secure He Guoliang for transfer. Place Shen Yumei under residential hold pending asset audit."

Two matte-black figures stepped forward in total silence. When cold gloved fingers closed around He Guoliang's elbows and lifted him effortlessly, the Vice Minister of Energy did not fight. He stared back at the empty safe as the dark hallway swallowed him whole.
