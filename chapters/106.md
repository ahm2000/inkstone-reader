# Chapter 106: Suit Shopping at Plaza 66

At two-thirty on Saturday afternoon, the marble halls of Plaza 66 on Nanjing Road gleamed under crystal lights. Wealthy shoppers strolled past luxury boutiques in designer heels and tailored coats.

Walking down the third floor, Chen Xuan wore a soft gray polo shirt, dark trousers, and clean white sneakers. In his right hand, he swung his scratched green enamel thermos by its wire handle, taking an occasional sip of warm jasmine tea.

Beside him, Qin Yuhan wore a fitted cream trench coat over a silk blouse. As the young president of Haiyue Group, she could walk into boardrooms across Lujiazui without blinking. Right now, however, she kept glancing sideways at that green thermos in Chen Xuan's hand.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said, keeping her voice composed as they turned toward a menswear boutique on the corner. "Could you please put that thermos inside your canvas bag for thirty minutes? This is Henglong Plaza."

Chen Xuan unscrewed the metal cap, took an unhurried sip, and smiled. "If I put it in the bag sideways, the old rubber ring inside the cap sometimes leaks two drops. Besides, the air conditioning in these malls is dry. Wait ten minutes and you will ask me for a sip too."

"I brought bottled water in the car," Qin Yuhan replied dryly, though her lips curved in spite of herself. She pushed open the glass door of an Italian menswear salon and guided him inside. "Come on. My parents land at Shanghai Hongqiao tomorrow afternoon, and we are having dinner with them by the Bund at six. If you show up in front of my mother wearing a thirty-yuan polo shirt and carrying a green tea jug, she will try to book you a one-way train ticket out of Shanghai before the cold dishes arrive."

"Your mother sounds energetic," Chen Xuan chuckled, looking around at the dark wool suits on brass racks. "Does she know I only make twelve thousand yuan a month at Hengxin Logistics?"

Qin Yuhan paused by a table of silk ties and sighed softly. "Oh, she knows. My mother, Lin Shuyin, called me four times from Beijing this week. She even went to my grandfather's courtyard house to complain. Even Grandpa Elder Qin Zhengguo thinks I married an ordinary logistics clerk on a stubborn impulse just to dodge the family's matchmakers. My father, Qin Zhengming, is much calmer, yet he still wants to look you in the eye tomorrow night."

"That makes sense," Chen Xuan said easily, patting his thermos. "If my daughter were the president of Haiyue Group living at Emerald Riverside Villa and she married a guy who clocks out at four fifty-five every afternoon, I would want to check if he had two heads too."

"You are not helping," Qin Yuhan said, shooting him a look with zero real heat behind it.

A store consultant in a black suit hurried over with a polite smile, recognizing Qin Yuhan immediately. "Good afternoon, President Qin. Welcome back. Are we looking for gifts for Director Qin Zhengming today?"

"Not for my father," Qin Yuhan said smoothly, gesturing toward Chen Xuan. "Please pull a dark navy suit, a crisp white dress shirt, and a silver-gray tie in my husband's size. Modern cut, no loud patterns."

The consultant blinked for half a second at the green thermos in Chen Xuan's hand, then recovered at once. "Right away, President Qin. Sir, you are a forty-eight long with a tapered waist. Please follow me to the private fitting lounge."

"Wait for me here," Chen Xuan told Qin Yuhan with a grin, setting his green thermos right on the marble VIP table before walking behind the velvet curtain.

Qin Yuhan looked at the battered thermos sitting beside white orchids, shook her head helplessly, and sat on the sofa. Outside the storefront, less than fifty meters down the corridor, two men in plain black jackets browsed a window in silence, maintaining the Black Order's unseen watch.

Seven minutes later, the velvet curtain slid back.

"Yuhan," Chen Xuan called out casually. "The buttons on this shirt collar are tiny. My fingers were made for flipping scallion pancakes, not wrestling with silk threads."

Qin Yuhan stood up from the sofa, ready to tease him, and stopped mid-step.

The man standing in front of the mirror no longer looked like a sleepy clerk from Hengxin Logistics. The deep charcoal-navy wool fit his broad shoulders and straight back as if stitched onto him by hand. His long legs, lean waist, and unbothered posture gave the suit a natural grace that none of the stiff rich heirs in Lujiazui could fake. With his dark eyes clear and a warm half-smile on his lips, he looked effortlessly striking.

For three full seconds, the cool president of Haiyue Group completely forgot what to say.

Chen Xuan watched her reflection in the mirror, his eyes crinkling with amusement. "President Qin? Did the price tag scare you, or are you staring at your husband?"

Qin Yuhan snapped out of her daze, a warm pink flush rising across her cheeks. She cleared her throat and stepped forward quickly, reaching up to take the two ends of his silver-gray silk tie.

"I was not staring," Qin Yuhan said briskly while her eyelashes fluttered down toward his collar. "The top button is undone, and your collar is crooked. Hold still."

She stood close enough that the floral scent of her hair brushed his chin. Because Chen Xuan was a full head taller, Qin Yuhan had to tilt her face up and stand slightly on her toes as her slender fingers slipped the tie into a neat Windsor knot.

Chen Xuan looked down at her pink ears and lowered his voice playfully. "Be honest, Wife. Putting a 200k yuan suit on a twelve-thousand-yuan clerk worked pretty well, did it not? You look ready to give me a raise."

Qin Yuhan tugged the knot snug against his collar, her fingertips brushing his throat. Her heart gave an unexpected little skip.

"Behave yourself," Qin Yuhan murmured, smoothing his lapels before stepping back to hide her blush. "It looks acceptable. At least tomorrow night by the Bund, my mother cannot complain about your clothes."

Just then, the phone in Qin Yuhan's pocket rang from Haiyue Tower regarding port shipping schedules after the Handong Province customs cleanup led by Sha Ruijin and Hou Liangping.

"I need to take this call outside for five minutes," Qin Yuhan told him. "Stay here, let the tailor check the sleeves, and do not touch that green thermos while wearing wool."

"Yes, President Qin," Chen Xuan chuckled, giving her a lazy two-finger salute in the mirror.
