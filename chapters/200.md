# Chapter 200: 300 Tons of Gold

By noon, the heavy rumble of diesel engines echoed through the snowy valley of Changbai Mountain.

A convoy of eighteen dark armored transport trucks, flanked by six black State Security SUVs with flashing blue grill lights, rolled smoothly past the timber mill gates. The snow along the logging road had been plowed clean, and armed State Security officers in winter tactical uniforms quickly established a perimeter along the outer fence line.

Inside the underground bunker, the air was warm and bright.

Captain Zhou Ming of Shanghai State Security stepped out of the freight elevator, accompanied by four senior officers. Behind them walked four distinguished gentlemen in heavy wool overcoats, their faces pale with shock and nervous excitement.

They were the titans of the national financial system:
President Fang Zhengming of China Construction Bank,
President Han Guoliang of Industrial and Commercial Bank of China,
President Liang Qingshan of Huaxia Bank,
and President Tang Wenhao of Bank of China.

When the elevator doors opened into the vast underground vault cavern, all four bank presidents stopped dead in their tracks.

Before them, stretching across two hundred meters of polished concrete, sat pallet after pallet of solid gold bullion, stacked four feet high under the bright industrial lights. The yellow luster of pure refined metal reflected off the granite ceiling in a sea of blinding gold. Beside the stacks, forty heavy steel crates held wrapped bundles of foreign currency, bearer bonds, and rare precious metals.

"Heavens above," President Fang Zhengming whispered, his breath catching in his throat as he pushed his glasses up his nose. "Three hundred tons. It is truly all here. Every single ounce that vanished from the coastal vaults over the last decade."

President Han Guoliang walked forward with trembling steps, reaching out a gloved hand to touch the cold top bar of the nearest pallet. The stamped serial numbers of the national reserve gleamed beneath the clear plastic wrap.

"Intact," Han Guoliang said, his voice shaking with profound emotion. "Not a single bar has been melted down yet. And the detonators?"

"Completely disabled, President Han," Captain Zhou Ming reported respectfully. "When my teams reached the tunnel twenty minutes ago, the demolition wires had already been mechanically bypassed and sealed. The entire facility was handed over to us without a single shot fired."

President Liang Qingshan turned his gaze toward the control office, his eyes wide with awe. "Captain Zhou, who cleared this place? Who could breach an underground fortress with thirty armed foreign mercenaries and disarm four thousand kilos of explosives in under an hour?"

Captain Zhou Ming kept his expression completely neutral, though a flicker of solemn reverence crossed his eyes. "The highest level security task force under direct authority of the White Order and Elder Song in Beijing. Our instructions from the State Council are strictly to accept custody of the vault, secure the perimeter, and execute the audit."

President Tang Wenhao nodded slowly, clutching his leather briefcase to his chest. "The mysterious protector. The one who transferred hundreds of trillions into the central treasury. Elder Song told us only that when the sovereign moves, the country's debts are cleared. Now I see what he meant."

Inside the glass control office, Chen Xuan was nowhere to be seen.

On the mahogany desk sat the five leather-bound ledgers and a typed transfer receipt stamped with a clean red seal. Beside the receipt lay a small handwritten note on plain Hengxin Logistics memo paper, written in neat blue fountain pen ink:

"To the Four National Banks:
All 300 tons of bullion and associated foreign accounts are hereby returned to the state reserve. Please verify serial numbers against the attached manifest and credit the national balance sheet before Monday's market opening. Keep the paperwork tidy."

President Han Guoliang picked up the small note with both hands, reading every word with immense reverence. "Look at this handwriting. Calm, steady, without a hint of boastfulness or demand for reward. A fortune that could buy a dozen small nations, handed over on a piece of office memo paper."

"A true hero of the nation," President Fang Zhengming agreed solemnly, taking off his hat and bowing toward the empty chair. "We must finish the counting within six hours. Not a single gram must be misplaced."

Half a mile down the mountain road, well outside the security perimeter, the heavy dark SUV rolled quietly through the falling snow toward the airstrip.

Inside the warm cabin, Chen Xuan leaned back against the headrest, comfortably sipping hot jasmine tea from his scratched green thermos.

Number 7 sat beside him, reviewing a tablet display. "President Han and the audit teams have taken formal custody of the vault, sir. All transport trucks are being loaded under heavy guard. The gold will be housed in the underground vaults of the Central Bank in Beijing before midnight."

"Good," Chen Xuan smiled, gazing out at the peaceful snowy forest. "The bankers will have a very busy weekend, but on Monday morning the financial markets will open stronger than ever."

"And what about Victor Leung in Hong Kong, sir?" Number 7 asked.

Chen Xuan's eyes darkened slightly, a quiet authority settling over his features. "Victor Leung spent thirty years stealing from this land to build his private empire in Central. Let him enjoy his crystal penthouse for another two hours. When the banks announce the seizure, the Seven-Harbor Consortium will cease to exist."

He closed his eyes, rested his head against the cushion, and took a deep, relaxed breath.

"Now, Number 7, make sure the flight back to Shanghai is scheduled for tomorrow morning. I promised Yuhan I would pick up some fresh winter bamboo shoots and beef brisket on the way home, and a husband should never be late for dinner."

"Understood, sir," Number 7 replied with a respectful smile. "The flight is booked for ten o'clock sharp."
