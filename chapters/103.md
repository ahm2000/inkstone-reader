# Chapter 103: Grocery Shopping at IFC Mall

Ten minutes later, in the bright underground gourmet supermarket inside IFC Mall in Lujiazui, Chen Xuan pushed a silver shopping cart past the fresh produce section while Qin Yuhan walked beside him with her blazer draped over one arm.

Several shoppers in the aisle glanced twice at the striking pair. Even without her corporate jacket, Qin Yuhan looked like a magazine model in her silk blouse, yet she was currently leaning over a wooden bin of winter melons with furrowed brows.

"Hold on," Qin Yuhan said, picking up a pre-sliced wedge of winter melon wrapped in clear film and checking the price tag. "Thirty-eight yuan for one slice of winter melon? Did they water this melon with French perfume?"

Chen Xuan chuckled, taking the pricey wedge out of her hand and setting it back in the imported organic bin. He stepped two paces to the left, picked up a much larger, fresher slice from the local farm display, and showed her the sticker.

"Six yuan and eighty cents," Chen Xuan said, placing it into the cart. "You always look at the middle shelf at eye level. Supermarkets put the thirty-eight-yuan gift-wrapped vegetables right at eye level for corporate presidents who never look down."

Qin Yuhan huffed softly, though her eyes curved with amusement. "I oversee 10 billion yuan in shipping contracts every quarter, Chen Xuan. How am I supposed to know the shelf strategy of winter melons?"

"That is why you brought a senior logistics clerk along," Chen Xuan grinned, tapping a box of fresh pork ribs at the butcher counter. "Come on, let us grab two boxes of ribs and some sweet corn."

As they turned the corner into the quieter condiment aisle, a stocky man in a loud checkered suit carrying a crocodile-skin folder stepped directly in front of their shopping cart.

It was Ma Jinhai, the owner of Donghai Port Brokerage. For the past two weeks, Ma Jinhai had been trying to strong-arm Haiyue Group into paying an extra 5 million yuan in dock handling fees at Shanghai Port.

"What a coincidence, President Qin!" Ma Jinhai said with a greasy smile, blocking the aisle. "You refused to see me at Haiyue Tower this afternoon, yet here you are buying groceries."

Qin Yuhan's warm expression cooled instantly into her sharp boardroom demeanor. "Mr. Ma, this is my private time. Furthermore, I already gave your company my answer. Haiyue Group will not pay 5 million yuan in inflated berth surcharges."

Ma Jinhai sneered, tapping his thick folder with two chunky fingers. "President Qin, let me be blunt. My brother-in-law sits on the Shanghai Port berth scheduling committee. If you do not sign this supplemental fee agreement before Monday morning, your three container ships arriving from Europe next week will sit outside the harbor anchor line for a month. We will see how much money you lose then."

He thrust the open contract folder toward Qin Yuhan's face.

Chen Xuan stepped calmly between the folder and his wife, gently pushing the paper aside with one finger as if moving a stray cabbage leaf.

"Careful with the corners, Mr. Ma," Chen Xuan said pleasantly. "You almost poked my wife's sleeve."

Ma Jinhai glared at Chen Xuan's plain windbreaker and the winter melon in the cart. "Who are you? Her driver? Stay out of serious port business before you lose your job."

"I work in a small logistics office," Chen Xuan said mildly, his eyes sweeping once over the top sheet in Ma Jinhai's open folder. "Actually, we fill out customs and port manifests every day. Looking at your brokerage stamp code on that top line, did your company just clear seventy containers of cold-rolled steel through Berth 4 yesterday under agricultural machinery tariffs?"

Ma Jinhai's eyelids twitched violently. "What do you know about Berth 4? Keep your mouth shut!"

"Just making conversation," Chen Xuan shrugged, pulling out his ordinary phone with one hand as he pushed the shopping cart around Ma Jinhai. "Declaring seventy heavy steel containers as farm tools to skip 400k yuan in port weight fees is pretty bold during the Friday evening customs audit. Good luck with that, Mr. Ma. Come on, Yuhan, we still need soy sauce."

While Ma Jinhai stood scoffing in the aisle behind them, Chen Xuan's thumb tapped a four-word text message to Number 14 and hit send:

"Donghai Brokerage, Berth 4."

Less than ten minutes later, Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan stood at the supermarket checkout counter loading their ribs, corn, and winter melon into two paper grocery bags.

Two lanes away, Ma Jinhai was waiting to pay for a box of imported cigars when his phone rang loudly. He answered it with a bark. "What is it?"

Within three seconds, all the blood drained from Ma Jinhai's beefy face. The box of cigars slipped from his fingers and hit the tile floor.

"What do you mean Shanghai Port Customs and the Port Authority just froze our operating license?!" Ma Jinhai shouted into his phone in sheer panic, his voice cracking across the checkout area. "My brother-in-law was suspended ten minutes ago too?! Wait, do not seal the warehouse!"

Stumbling backward, Ma Jinhai bolted out of the supermarket without even picking up his cigars.

At checkout lane two, Chen Xuan casually picked up both grocery bags in one hand and reached for Qin Yuhan's hand with the other.

Qin Yuhan did not move immediately. She stood beneath the warm lights of IFC Mall, narrowing her pretty dark eyes as she looked up at her calm, smiling husband.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said slowly, watching him closely. "How did you know Shanghai Port Customs was auditing Berth 4 tonight?"

Chen Xuan blinked innocently and adjusted the grocery bags on his forearm. "I heard it on the morning traffic radio while I was boiling tea. The announcer said the port authority was checking overweight steel containers all week. When Mr. Ma shoved that folder at us, his Berth 4 declaration receipt was clipped right to the front page."

Qin Yuhan studied his honest, unbothered face for five full seconds, trying to find a single crack in his story. Finally, she let out a soft sigh and shook her head with a helpless smile.

"You listen to customs news on the morning radio?" she teased, slipping her fingers into his warm palm.

"A 12,000-yuan logistics clerk has to stay informed, President Qin," Chen Xuan chuckled as they walked out toward the car together.
