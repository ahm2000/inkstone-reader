# Chapter 159: Landing in Nanhai City

By eleven thirty, Qingyan Airport was loud with rolling suitcases and flight calls. Chen Xuan passed security easily with his faded gray canvas bag and refilled his green thermos with hot water near Gate Twelve.

The noon flight to Nanhai City was nearly full, leaving only a few open seats near the front. Chen Xuan checked his boarding pass, placed his bag overhead, and turned toward seat 12B.

Beside the window in seat 12A sat Qin Yuhan.

She wore a crisp cream blazer over a dark blouse, a thin folder open on her lap. Because she had booked at midnight, even Haiyue Group's president had settled for a regular window seat.

When Chen Xuan sat down calmly beside her, Qin Yuhan looked up from her folder, expecting an ordinary traveler.

Her hand froze on the page. Her eyes widened in disbelief.

"Chen Xuan?" she whispered, leaning closer as another passenger walked down the aisle. "What on earth are you doing on this flight?"

"Good morning, President Qin," Chen Xuan said mildly, buckling his seatbelt and tucking his scratched green thermos into the seat pocket. "I am on a company business trip."

Qin Yuhan stared at him for two seconds before a soft laugh slipped past her lips. "A business trip? Last night at midnight you were washing dishes in Qingyan. Today at noon you are sitting right next to me on the way to Nanhai City."

"Hengxin Logistics has six containers of precision parts trapped on the Haiyue Star," Chen Xuan explained evenly. "This morning, General Manager Sun Haoran asked who would fly south to handle the slips. Supervisor Hu suddenly hurt his back, and Zhang Pengfei was swamped with two heavy binders. Since I have not seen the ocean in a few years, I raised my hand."

Qin Yuhan shook her head slowly, the tight crease between her brows finally softening. For the first time since Monday morning, her shoulders relaxed.

"General Manager Sun has no idea how lucky he is," she murmured, closing her folder. "Though I have to ask: did you pack that bag this morning, or did you already plan to board this plane?"

"A good office worker always keeps a clean shirt ready for sudden overtime," Chen Xuan answered with an easy smile.

The cabin doors sealed with a dull thud, and the plane rolled toward the runway. As the wheels lifted into the bright noon sky, Qin Yuhan glanced briefly over her shoulder to see if the flight attendants were bringing water.

Two rows behind them, in seats 14B and 14C, sat two quiet figures wearing plain black jackets and full black face masks. Their backs were straight, their hands rested still on their knees, and neither glanced at a phone or window. They sat as motionless as stone.

Qin Yuhan blinked once, faintly puzzled by how still they were, then turned back. People wore masks on flights all the time, and those two were clearly keeping to themselves.

Beside her, Chen Xuan did not even turn his head. He already knew the silent Black Guard team had boarded behind him, keeping their unexplained fifty-meter distance without a single word.

"Thank you for coming, Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said quietly once the plane leveled out above the clouds. "To be honest, twenty minutes ago I felt like I was walking alone into a room full of hungry wolves. Having you here makes the whole trip feel calmer."

"You are not walking in alone," Chen Xuan said simply, taking two cups of warm water from the flight attendant and handing one to her. "Tell me about Shen Jingzhou. Have you met him before?"

Qin Yuhan took a sip and frowned slightly. "Twice, at coastal shipping dinners last year. He is twenty-nine, the eldest grandson of the Shen Family, and he loves loud watches and louder speeches. His grandfather built Shenhai Shipping from three fishing boats, so the old man knows where the line is. Shen Jingzhou does not. He thinks because his family controls Pier Nine, every ship entering Nanhai Harbor owes him a bow."

"How much of Haiyue's southern routes do you think he will demand today?" Chen Xuan asked calmly.

"At least twenty or thirty percent," Qin Yuhan said, her voice turning cool and sharp. "Plus exclusive unloading rights at Pier Nine so he can charge us double fees. If I refuse, he will keep that fake ballast valve hold locked until Thursday. By then, we will have lost 32 million yuan in fines and delays, and our three biggest southern buyers will walk away."

"That is what he wants you to look at," Chen Xuan remarked softly.

Qin Yuhan paused, turning to study his calm face. "What do you mean?"

"Think about it," Chen Xuan said, his tone relaxed. "If a shipping family just wanted a bigger slice of freight, they would cut prices or wine and dine your buyers. Locking a flagship vessel in broad daylight with a fake safety hold is noisy and ugly. People only take a noisy risk like that when they are trying to chase honest eyes away from their dock."

Qin Yuhan's eyes narrowed, her sharp mind catching the point right away. "You think the Shen Family is hiding something at Pier Nine?"

"A clean dock welcomes big, orderly shipping lines like Haiyue Group," Chen Xuan replied smoothly. "Only a dirty dock panics when clean books walk through the gate."

For a long moment, Qin Yuhan said nothing. She looked down at her cup, then back at the ordinary logistics clerk beside her. There was no panic in him, no boasting, and no worry about the powerful Shen Family waiting two hours south.

"Sometimes," Qin Yuhan murmured with a faint smile, "I forget that you work at Hengxin Logistics. You see straight through people faster than half my board."

"I just read a lot of shipping slips," Chen Xuan said lightly. "You learn to spot when numbers do not add up. Now close your eyes for an hour, President Qin. You barely slept last night, and you need a clear head at four o'clock."

Qin Yuhan nodded softly and leaned back. Within ten minutes, she was fast asleep.

Two hours later, the wheels touched down on the sunlit runway of Nanhai City.

Stepping out of the arrival hall, warm coastal air wrapped around them, carrying the salty smell of the sea. While Qin Yuhan called her local driver, Chen Xuan's gaze swept calmly across the far edge of the pickup lane.

Parked quietly near a shaded bus shelter sat two ordinary black sedans. Leaning casually against the lead car was Number 7 in a plain dark jacket, hands in his pockets. He did not wave. He simply met Chen Xuan's eyes across the lane and gave one brief, steady nod.

Twenty paces farther down, beside the second sedan, Number 19 and Number 88 stood quietly with thick brown folders tucked inside their jackets. Behind Chen Xuan, fifty meters back near the crosswalk, the two silent Black Guard members stepped smoothly into an unmarked dark hatchback without a word.

A silver company sedan pulled up right in front of Qin Yuhan. Her local port manager jumped out, sweating in a rumpled blue suit.

"President Qin! Shen Jingzhou's people just called. They are already waiting at the main gate of Pier Nine."

Qin Yuhan glanced at her watch and turned to Chen Xuan. "It is three twenty. Ride straight to the pier with me, Chen Xuan. You can drop your bag later."

"Gladly," Chen Xuan said, sliding into the back seat with his canvas bag.

The silver sedan pulled onto the coastal highway, heading straight for Nanhai Pier Nine.
