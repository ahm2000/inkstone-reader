# Chapter 87: The Quiet Call

Amber dusk settled slowly over the skyline of Qingyan City, casting long copper shadows across the fortieth floor of Haiyue Tower.

The afternoon session had ended with Haiyue Hospitality and Property Group down nineteen point four percent. Two regional bank presidents had refused to see Qin Yuhan in person, hiding behind rehearsed risk-control excuses, while Donghai Development Bank only agreed to review her collateral list by Tuesday noon.

Inside her quiet corner office, Qin Yuhan stood beside the tall window with a cold porcelain cup of jasmine tea untouched on her desk. Faint shadows of exhaustion lined the corners of her eyes.

Behind her, Jin Ke knocked softly and stepped inside with a folder of bank valuation sheets.

"Chairman Qin Zhengming finally fell asleep after his evening medicine," Jin Ke reported quietly. "The doctor checked his blood pressure twenty minutes ago. It is stable as long as he avoids another shock tonight."

"Good," Qin Yuhan murmured, her gaze staying on the headlights crawling forty floors below. "Make sure the night nurse stays in the outer suite."

Jin Ke hesitated beside the mahogany desk, his fingers tightening around the folder.

"Miss Qin," Jin Ke ventured carefully. "Aegis Meridian is not an ordinary competitor. Should we call General Qin Zhengguo? Or reach out to Chen Xuan? Every time a storm hit Qingyan City or Lanshui, things cleared up whenever Chen Xuan was nearby."

Qin Yuhan turned slowly from the window, shaking her head with gentle firmness.

"Never mention this to Grandpa, Jin Ke," Qin Yuhan said calmly. "General Qin Zhengguo spent his whole life honoring military discipline. A retired general cannot interfere in a public stock exchange, and I would never stain his name to save a company."

Her expression softened as she glanced down at the plain smartphone beside her elbow.

"As for Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan continued quietly, "he is a kind, ordinary man who works a regular desk job at Hengxin Logistics and looks after his parents. Even if he knows a few capable people, this is a multi-billion-yuan international financial ambush backed by Zurich capital. Dragging an ordinary friend into a war like this would be selfish and reckless."

Jin Ke lowered his gaze respectfully. "Understood, Miss Qin. I will keep the finance team working through the night."

After Jin Ke closed the door, Qin Yuhan picked up her phone and opened her message thread with Chen Xuan. Three days ago, they had agreed to meet this Friday afternoon at the quiet teahouse beside Yunxi Old Street.

Her thumb paused over the glowing screen. Then she typed a brief, polite message, keeping every trace of panic out of her words.

*Chen Xuan, I am sorry, I have to cancel our Friday tea. Work suddenly got very busy this week. I will make it up to you once things settle down.*

She pressed send, set the phone face down, and returned to her spreadsheets.

* * *

Across Qingyan City, warm yellow light glowed inside Room 601 of the old residential building.

The dining table smelled richly of ginger-steamed sea bass, braised eggplant, and freshly steamed white rice. Chen Jianguo sat at the head of the table peeling garlic cloves, while Liu Meifang ladled clear winter-melon soup into three bowls.

"Eat more fish, Chen Xuan," Liu Meifang urged warmly, placing tender belly meat onto his rice. "You just came back from that three-day trip to Lanshui, and you already look thinner around the chin."

"The hotel buffet was very good, Mom," Chen Xuan replied with a gentle smile, picking up his chopsticks. "Just nothing compares to your steamed sea bass."

"Your mother woke up at six this morning to beat the aunts at the wet market for that fish," Chen Jianguo chuckled, lifting his teacup.

Beside Chen Xuan's bowl, his plain black phone vibrated once against the wooden tabletop.

Chen Xuan set his chopsticks down neatly and read Qin Yuhan's message. His brow lifted a fraction of an inch, noting the quiet stiffness in her apology, though his expression remained mild and calm.

He typed back one unhurried, reassuring reply.

*No problem at all, Miss Qin. Remember to eat on time. I hope your busy week clears up soon.*

Setting the phone back beside his bowl, Chen Xuan resumed eating. Across the living room, the television transitioned into the seven-thirty financial roundup.

"Good evening, and welcome to Market Watch," the anchor announced briskly as a crimson chart filled the screen. "Today saw a dramatic Black Monday in Qingyan City as shares of Haiyue Hospitality and Property Group plunged nearly twenty percent. Analysts point to aggressive offshore short-selling led by Zurich-affiliated fund Aegis Meridian Capital, alongside sudden credit freezes from three regional banks."

Chen Xuan's chopsticks paused halfway to his bowl.

His calm dark eyes lifted to the television screen, resting steadily on the words scrolling across the red ticker.

*Aegis Meridian Capital.*

"Goodness," Liu Meifang murmured, glancing at the television with mild sympathy. "Isn't Haiyue Group the hotel company Miss Qin Yuhan works for? Why did their stock drop so badly in one day?"

"Stock markets are always noisy like that, Mom," Chen Xuan said smoothly, his voice warm and unhurried as he took another bite of rice. "Rumors stir people up, and then things settle back down."

Beneath his quiet exterior, however, his memory had already flipped straight back to the sealed Lanshui forum dossiers he had reviewed three days earlier.

Aegis Meridian Capital was not a normal investment fund. It was the Zurich financial tail of the Northern Ledger, registered through offshore shells to execute hostile corporate liquidations. They had lost Li Weiming and their Lanshui hub, and now they were swinging at Haiyue Group to flush him into the open.

Chen Xuan finished his rice calmly without rushing. After dinner, he helped Liu Meifang carry the empty plates to the kitchen sink, poured himself a ceramic mug of warm chrysanthemum tea, and smiled at his parents.

"I am going to stand on the balcony for a few minutes to get some fresh air," Chen Xuan said lightly.

"Take a jacket, the evening breeze is cool," Liu Meifang called out fondly.

Chen Xuan stepped through the sliding glass door onto the narrow concrete balcony overlooking the quiet courtyard six stories below.

Down beneath the dark sycamore trees, fifty meters away, not a single shadow stirred. Yet Chen Xuan knew the permanent Zero Guard team in featureless matte-black gear remained silently anchored to his perimeter, invisible to every eye in the neighborhood.

Chen Xuan took a slow sip of warm tea, gazing toward the distant lights of Haiyue Tower standing lit up against the night.

He pulled his plain black phone from his pocket, opened the encrypted relay line reserved strictly for direct orders, and sent one calm line to Number One.

*Aegis Meridian is the Zurich tail of the Northern Ledger. Pull their holding structure and notify financial regulators and the Trustee.*

He locked the screen and slid the phone back into his jacket pocket.

* * *

Three kilometers away, inside the quiet eastern courtyard surrounded by black pines, the matte terminal on Number One's rosewood desk chimed once.

Number One rose immediately, reading the single line glowing across the dark glass.

For three years since Chen Xuan chose to live as an ordinary office worker in Qingyan City, he had never once invoked the Trustee's name.

Number One bowed slightly toward the glowing screen, his voice low and ice-cold.

"Summon Number Fourteen and Number Eleven," Number One ordered cleanly. "Pull every offshore shell behind Aegis Meridian Capital within thirty minutes, deliver the forensic file to the Central Financial Commission, and open the direct line to Shanghai. The Sovereign has spoken."
