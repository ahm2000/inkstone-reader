# Chapter 17: Seating Order

Yunxi County Party Committee Building, Conference Room Three. Sunday, 2:30 p.m.

At a meeting like this, you don't need to ask who holds real power. You only have to look at the chairs.

Eleven leather seats lined the long polished table, one for each member of the County Standing Committee, the small inner council that actually ran Yunxi County. Every chair's distance from the head of the table showed each leader's exact rank.

In Seat One sat Fan Weiguo, the County Party Secretary, Yunxi's top boss. Right beside him in Seat Two sat Tao Shu, the County Magistrate, head of the county government and Deputy Party Secretary. On paper, both men held the same county-leader rank, yet everyone in the room knew Seat One held the final word, and Seat Two had spent eight quiet years waiting to take it.

Seat Three belonged to Kang Guangming, Secretary of the County Discipline Inspection Commission, Yunxi's local anti-corruption watchdog. A lean, stone-faced official in his fifties, he hadn't cracked a single smile since walking through the door.

To put it nicely, Kang Guangming's rank sat half a step below the top two leaders; to put it plainly, his office held the legal power to investigate every single person sitting around this table, including the Party Secretary himself! That was why he held Seat Three, and why nobody ever told jokes near him.

In Seat Four sat Shao Wenhua, Head of the Organization Department, the quiet official who controlled every promotion, transfer, and appointment in the county. He didn't command police or spend building funds, yet because every official's career passed across his desk, everyone treated him carefully.

Seat Five belonged to Lu Wenzhe, the Executive Deputy Magistrate. "Executive" meant first among all deputy county mayors, the man who controlled Yunxi's public budget and ran the Old Street rebuilding office. Though his official title sat below Fan Weiguo and Tao Shu, his grip on the county's money gave him huge power.

Right now, Lu Wenzhe's face looked the color of wet ash, and his teacup had been rattling faintly against his saucer for ten straight minutes!

Back along the far wall, perched on an ordinary wooden chair without a nameplate, sat County Police Director Mai Zhenhua. Wearing the extra hat of Deputy County Magistrate gave him higher standing than ordinary bureau chiefs, yet in Yunxi County the police chief didn't hold a Standing Committee seat. He could sign arrest warrants. He couldn't cast a vote.

"Comrades," Fan Weiguo began, keeping his voice remarkably smooth and dignified despite thirty sleepless hours. "At seven this morning, the City Party Secretary briefed me directly. The Provincial Inspection Team has moved its arrival forward to nine o'clock tomorrow morning. Deputy Police Director Cheng Biao has been suspended pending inquiry, and investigators from the Municipal Discipline Commission are already working in Yunxi. I've called this emergency session to align our response, starting with the Old Street project."

Dead silence fell across the polished table.

Nobody rushed to speak first. In officialdom, the first man to open his mouth in a crisis is usually the first man to lose his footing!

Fan Weiguo let his eyes move calmly around the eleven faces, mentally counting allies. Who would stand behind him? Who was quietly leaning toward Tao Shu? Who would switch sides the second trouble started? His chest felt tight as a drum. Everyone here knew this meeting wasn't really about an old shopping street. It was about who would still hold a chair in this room next month!

"Secretary Fan," Kang Guangming spoke up at last, his voice flat and businesslike. "Before we discuss policy, I must put one official fact on the record. The city's Discipline Commission sent its investigation team into Yunxi late last night without telling our county office in advance. I only learned they were in town from my own staff at six this morning."

A chilly silence swept over the room!

Every veteran official around the table understood that warning right away! Normally, city anti-corruption investigators informed the local county office first. Going straight around Kang Guangming without a single phone call sent one clear message: the city leaders didn't trust anyone in this room not to tip off the guilty!

"Comrades, let's not panic over one noisy complaint!" Lu Wenzhe blurted out suddenly, his voice pitching a little too loud as he tried to steady his shaking teacup. "The Old Street shopping mall is Yunxi's biggest development project! The buyout prices went through proper county review. If we freeze everything just because one stubborn hardware seller stirred up trouble, the whole county loses face and outside investors will run away!"

"Who suggested stopping the entire project, Deputy Magistrate Lu?" Kang Guangming asked calmly, turning his cold eyes straight across the wood.

Lu Wenzhe's mouth snapped shut! Sweat beaded visibly along his hairline.

From Seat One, Fan Weiguo watched Lu Wenzhe's panic, and a cold, ugly suspicion sharpened in his gut. Lu Wenzhe was the man who signed every payment from that 640-million-yuan relocation fund! Why on earth was he sweating buckets over one tiny hardware store unless the account books couldn't survive daylight?!

"Secretary Fan, I believe we should show complete openness," County Magistrate Tao Shu spoke up smoothly, folding his slender hands neatly on the table. He looked rested, calm, and completely free of Friday's convenient "cold." "I propose the county set up an immediate self-inspection team, which I am willing to lead myself. Before the provincial team arrives at nine tomorrow, we'll check every single Old Street contract and payment record and hand every file over openly. A clean leadership team has nothing to hide."

How reasonable! How fair! How selfless!

Yet Fan Weiguo's stomach sank like lead! A self-inspection team led personally by Tao Shu meant giving his rival full power to take every county account book and project file before provincial inspectors even arrived! After eight patient years waiting in Seat Two, Tao Shu was reaching calmly for the top chair, wearing the warmest, most polite smile imaginable!

Fan Weiguo's eyes flicked quietly toward Seat Four. Shao Wenhua, the Organization Department head who controlled promotions, sat busy smoothing the corner of his notebook, refusing to meet anyone's eyes. A classic veteran move: stay quiet, watch carefully, and wait to see which side would win before speaking.

Back along the wall, Mai Zhenhua sat motionless as a statue, quietly thanking his lucky stars he'd never signed a single Old Street detention paper. Since midnight, one icy order from the city police chief had echoed in his ears: *Release the shopkeeper, suspend Cheng Biao, and don't touch another document.*

"Very well," Fan Weiguo said steadily, keeping his authority intact. "If there are no further proposals, we'll put Magistrate Tao's working team to a committee vote."

"Wait one moment, Secretary Fan," Kang Guangming interrupted quietly, his tone never rising a fraction. "Before we vote, I have one more report from my field staff, and I'd like it entered cleanly into today's minutes."

Slowly, Kang Guangming flipped open the manila folder in front of him. He didn't glance at Fan Weiguo, and he didn't glance at Tao Shu. His flat, unblinking gaze locked straight onto Seat Five.

"At twelve-fifteen this afternoon, after city investigators froze the Old Street accounts, two of my officers observed an official county sedan parked behind the private office of Hongrui Real Estate for forty minutes," Kang Guangming read evenly. "The license plate belongs to Executive Deputy Magistrate Lu Wenzhe's personal government car."

Every single head around the long table snapped toward Seat Five!

Lu Wenzhe's fingers went numb. His teacup slipped clean out of his hand!

Crack!

The cup hit the edge of the saucer and split down the middle. Scalding brown tea surged out across the mirror-bright wood, spreading in a dark, quiet tide straight toward Fan Weiguo's open red folder.

Around the eleven-seat table, nobody moved a muscle to wipe it up.
