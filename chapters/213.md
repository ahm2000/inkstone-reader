# Chapter 213: Port Terminal Six

Wednesday evening fell over Shanghai with a heavy coastal fog rolling in off the East China Sea.

Down at Pudong Port Terminal Six, massive red gantry cranes stood like towering iron sentinels above the dark waters of the harbor basin.

Tied to the concrete pier was the *Haiyue Star*, a brand-new, 200-meter container vessel painted in Haiyue Group's signature navy and white colors. Stacks of shipping containers, filled with 50 million yuan worth of high-precision electronic equipment and refrigerated northern produce, were securely lashed to the deck, ready to set sail for Qingdao on the evening tide.

Yet the main cargo ramps remained raised, and the vessel's diesel engines sat idling in neutral.

Inside the heated dockside operations office overlooking the pier, two men were seated on a leather sofa, laughing as they poured themselves glasses of expensive imported scotch.

One was Qian Weidong, the fifty-year-old regional director of Tianhai International. He wore a sharply tailored charcoal suit with a heavy gold ring on his pinky finger. Across from him sat Captain Lu, the Chief Safety Inspector of Terminal Six, dressed in his official maritime uniform with gold epaulets on his shoulders.

"Captain Lu," Qian Weidong grinned, clinking his glass against the inspector's tumbler. "You are certain the hold order will stick?"

Captain Lu took a satisfying sip of scotch and let out a smug chuckle. "Director Qian, my inspection report states that the *Haiyue Star* has an irregular ballast water salinity balance that poses an ecological hazard to the coastal estuary. Under federal maritime regulations, that gives me the sole authority to impound the vessel for forty-eight hours of mandatory testing."

"Magnificent," Qian Weidong laughed, leaning back against the leather cushion. "Forty-eight hours will cost Qin Yuhan 8 million yuan in liquidated damages, and her export clients will cancel their shipping orders by tomorrow morning. By noon, she will have no choice but to come to my office on her knees and beg Tianhai International to take over the route!"

Meanwhile, fifteen kilometers away in the dining room of Emerald Riverside Villa, the atmosphere was peaceful and warm.

Chen Xuan had spent the afternoon simmering a fragrant pot of winter melon and sparerib soup, filling the home with a rich, savory aroma. He had just set a platter of stir-fried snow peas and garlic shrimp on the table when Qin Yuhan walked into the house.

Qin Yuhan kicked off her heels at the entrance, her face tight with exhaustion and distress. Her smartphone was pressed tightly to her ear, and her knuckles were pale against the glass screen.

"What do you mean, an ecological violation?" Qin Yuhan was saying, her voice strained with frustration as she paced across the living room. "The *Haiyue Star* was certified clean by the Ningbo maritime registry just three days ago! We have 50 million yuan of time-sensitive cargo onboard! Director Zhang, tell Captain Lu we will pay for a private expedited testing lab to re-verify the ballast water right now!"

She listened to the reply on the phone for ten seconds, her expression growing increasingly dismayed.

"He refuses to re-test until Friday morning?" Qin Yuhan gasped. "That is blatant extortion! Very well, contact our legal department immediately."

She ended the call, tossed the phone onto the sofa with a helpless sigh, and rubbed her temples with both hands, tears of anger glistening in the corners of her eyes.

Chen Xuan set down his ladle, took off his blue cooking apron, and walked over to her side. He gently wrapped his arms around her waist, pulling her close against his chest.

"Rough day at the docks, Madam President?" Chen Xuan asked softly, his voice smooth and steady as warm tea.

Qin Yuhan buried her face into his shoulder, her slender body trembling with suppressed stress. "Xiao Xuan, someone is deliberately sabotaging our maiden container ship at Port Terminal Six. A corrupt inspector named Captain Lu impounded the vessel on fake environmental charges. If the ship does not sail by midnight, Haiyue will lose 50 million yuan and our shipping reputation will be ruined."

Chen Xuan gently stroked her silky black hair, his eyes narrowing with a quiet, lethal coldness that remained completely hidden behind her back.

"Do not cry over bad water inspectors, Yuhan," Chen Xuan whispered with gentle tenderness, kissing her forehead. "Go wash your hands and sit down. Drink a bowl of warm sparerib soup first. Things are never as hopeless as they seem."

Qin Yuhan looked up into his calm, gentle eyes and felt a sudden wave of comfort wash over her heart. She managed a faint, brave smile. "All right. Let me wash my face."

As she turned toward the hallway bathroom, Chen Xuan pulled his modest white phone from his pocket, stepped quietly out onto the balcony into the cool evening air, and dialed a single number.
