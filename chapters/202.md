# Chapter 202: Sunday Afternoon Flight

While sirens echoed through the financial canyons of Hong Kong, fifteen hundred kilometers to the north, the Changbai airstrip rested in serene white silence.

The pearl-white private jet sat on the tarmac with its twin engines humming quietly, clearing thin trails of steam across the fresh morning snow.

Inside the heated passenger cabin, Chen Xuan had traded his heavy northern coat for his comfortable dark blue windbreaker. He sat in a plush cream leather armchair beside the oval window, casually peeling a sweet mandarin orange and dropping the fragrant peels onto a porcelain plate.

On the small mahogany side table beside him rested his dented green metal thermos, fresh steam curling from its open rim, filling the cabin with the warm scent of roasted jasmine tea.

Number 7 stepped down the aisle carrying two sturdy brown cardboard boxes tied with natural hemp twine.

"What do you have there, Number 7?" Chen Xuan asked, popping a juicy segment of mandarin into his mouth with an easy smile.

"Local specialties from the Changbai foothills, sir," Number 7 replied with a polite bow, placing the boxes carefully on the luggage bench. "Number 12 had the local forestry cooperative prepare twenty kilograms of freshly harvested red pine nuts, two bags of wild dried hazel mushrooms, and three jars of pure linden honey. We thought Mrs. Chen might enjoy authentic northern ingredients for her winter kitchen."

Chen Xuan's eyes lit up with genuine delight. "Pine nuts and wild mushrooms! Excellent thinking, Number 7. Yuhan was complaining just last week that the pine nuts in the Shanghai supermarkets are dried out and tasteless. If I bring these home, she will forgive me for being away the whole weekend."

"Did Mrs. Chen check in on your schedule, sir?" Number 7 asked with a subtle, respectful smile.

Chen Xuan chuckled, picking up his modest white smartphone from the armrest. He tapped the screen and opened the chat window with Qin Yuhan.

The latest message from Qin Yuhan, sent twenty minutes earlier, read:
"Chen Xuan, did your northern warehouse freeze your fingers off yet? If you are done checking shipping manifests, do not forget to pick up fresh bamboo shoots and beef brisket from the wet market near the subway station on your way home. Grandpa says if you are not back to braise ribs by six, he will eat all the dumplings himself!"

Below her text was a small animated sticker of a fluffy cat tapping its paws impatiently against a dinner bowl.

Chen Xuan laughed softly and typed a quick reply:
"Flight leaves the warehouse runway in five minutes, Madam President. I found fresh Changbai pine nuts and wild hazel mushrooms for tonight. Pick up the soup base, and I will prepare the hot pot broth the second I step through the door."

Within three seconds, three little dots danced on the screen, followed by a reply:
"Real northern pine nuts? If they are stale, you are sleeping on the sofa tonight! Safe flight, husband. Call me when you land at Hongqiao."

Chen Xuan smiled warmly, locked the screen, and slipped the phone back into his coat pocket.

"The captain reports that weather conditions along the eastern corridor are perfectly clear, sir," Number 7 reported, taking his seat across the aisle. "Flight time to Shanghai Hongqiao will be exactly two hours and fifteen minutes. We will touch down at two-thirty in the afternoon."

"Plenty of time," Chen Xuan leaned his head comfortably back against the leather headrest, unscrewed his thermos lid, and took a slow, relaxing sip of warm tea. "How did the handover in the valley conclude?"

"President Han Guoliang and President Fang Zhengming completed the primary audit at nine this morning," Number 7 said. "All 300 tons of bullion and the Seven-Harbor account hard drives are currently aboard three military transport aircraft bound for the Central Bank vault in Beijing under twenty-four-hour State Security escort. Kou Zhenshan and Viktor Orlov have been transferred to the federal detention facility in Shenyang."

"And the Premier's office?"

"Premier Gu Chengshan submitted the final action report to Elder Song an hour ago," Number 7 answered softly. "The state treasury has fully absorbed the recovered assets. Elder Song asked Premier Gu to convey his deepest gratitude to the holder of the Black Order, but Premier Gu was strictly instructed that your identity must remain completely unrecorded in the national archives."

"Elder Song understands how to keep a secret," Chen Xuan nodded gently. "That old man fought through the founding campaigns; he knows that the heaviest stones in a foundation are the ones buried deep underground where no one can see them."

The private jet began to taxi smoothly along the cleared runway, turned toward the south, and accelerated with effortless power. Outside the windows, the snow-covered peaks of Changbai Mountain dropped away beneath a blanket of white winter clouds.

Chen Xuan took another sip of hot tea, closed his eyes, and drifted into a quiet, peaceful nap, looking forward only to the warm aroma of home cooking that waited for him in Shanghai.
