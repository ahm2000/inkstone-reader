# Chapter 209: Hu Dewei's Stupor

When the heavy frosted double doors of the presidential office slid open, General Manager Sun Haoran and Department Manager Hu Dewei stepped inside with rehearsed, deferential posture.

Sun Haoran held his leather folder with both hands at his chest, wearing his warmest, most professional corporate smile. "President Qin, thank you so much for granting Hengxin Logistics this valuable opportunity, "

His voice caught abruptly in his throat.

His eyes widened until they nearly popped out of his skull.

Beside him, Hu Dewei froze in mid-stride, his jaw dropping so low that his chin practically touched his necktie.

Sitting on the cream leather sofa in the center of the luxurious 58th-floor executive suite was Chen Xuan, their quiet 12,000-yuan operations clerk!

Chen Xuan was sitting comfortably with his legs crossed, holding a pair of bamboo chopsticks, casually dipping a piece of tender braised beef into a porcelain dish. Beside him sat his dented fifteen-yuan green metal thermos, right on the glass coffee table next to a stack of multimillion-yuan corporate files!

And right beside Chen Xuan sat Qin Yuhan, the formidable President of Haiyue Group, worth billions of yuan, holding a small porcelain spoon and happily leaning her head against Chen Xuan's shoulder!

For five full seconds, the only sound in the vast office was the quiet hum of the air conditioning.

Sun Haoran's mind spun through a dozen impossible scenarios. Was he hallucinating from high blood pressure? Had the elevator taken them to a film set? Why was Hengxin's junior clerk eating lunch on the president's private couch?

"Chen. Chen Xuan?" Hu Dewei stammered, his face turning an alarming shade of pale gray. "What, what are you doing in President Qin's office? Did you come to deliver freight manifests?"

Qin Yuhan swallowed her bite of steamed egg custard, wiped the corner of her lips with a cloth napkin, and looked at the two frozen managers with a cool, poised expression.

"General Manager Sun, Manager Hu," Qin Yuhan said in her crisp, melodic voice. "Chen Xuan did not come to deliver manifests. He came to bring his wife lunch. I believe you already know my husband?"

*Husband!*

The single word exploded inside Sun Haoran's brain like a sonic boom.

Hu Dewei felt the strength drain completely from his knees, and he had to grab the back of a visitor chair to keep from collapsing onto the floor.

Chen Xuan, the quiet young man who clocked out at four-fifty-five every day, who carried a scratched fifteen-yuan green thermos, who politely answered phones and never complained when dumped with extra customs paperwork, was the lawful husband of Qin Yuhan, the supreme head of the Haiyue Group!

Sun Haoran's face flashed through shock, disbelief, and finally an overwhelming wave of sheer, unadulterated awe.

"President. President Qin's husband!" Sun Haoran gasped, his voice trembling with profound reverence. He turned to Chen Xuan and bowed deeply from the waist, nearly ninety degrees. "Mr. Chen! Forgive our intrusion! We had no idea, absolutely no idea!"

Chen Xuan set his chopsticks down on the porcelain rest and smiled with his usual easygoing calm. "Please sit down, General Manager Sun. There is no need for such formality. In the office, I am still an ordinary operations clerk under your leadership."

"No, no, no! How could you say that, Mr. Chen!" Sun Haoran protested frantically, wiping beads of cold sweat from his forehead with both hands. "You are our honored colleague, our pillar of operational stability!"

Hu Dewei stood behind Sun Haoran, shaking so violently that his leather briefcase rattled against his trousers.

A cold horror gripped Hu Dewei's chest. Over the past six months, how many times had he barked at Chen Xuan across the bullpen? How many times had he dumped piles of tedious shipping verifications on Chen Xuan's desk to make himself look like a tough manager?

Just yesterday morning, he had slapped three hundred customs audit files onto Chen Xuan's desk and ordered him to finish before four!

If Chen Xuan had whispered even half a sentence of complaint to his wife over pillow talk, Haiyue Group could have crushed Hengxin Logistics into dust with a single phone call, and Sun Haoran would have fired Hu Dewei into the middle of the Huangpu River before noon!

Qin Yuhan reached out, picked up the signed blue folder from the coffee table, and handed it to Assistant Secretary Lin Wei.

"General Manager Sun," Qin Yuhan said smoothly, her tone professional and authoritative. "My husband just gave me a detailed summary of Hengxin's fleet capacity and dispatch reliability. Based on his personal endorsement of your operations team, Haiyue Group has approved Hengxin Logistics for Phase One of our northern coastal feeder routes. The initial contract volume is set at 80 million yuan."

Sun Haoran looked at the signed folder, his hands trembling with emotion as he received it from Secretary Lin.

Eighty million yuan!

A single contract that expanded Hengxin's annual business by two-thirds, approved in five minutes, all because Chen Xuan said a few kind words while eating lunch!

"Thank you, President Qin! Thank you, Mr. Chen!" Sun Haoran bowed repeatedly, tears of genuine gratitude glistening in his eyes. "Hengxin will dedicate our top sixty transport teams to this project! We will never let Haiyue down, and we will never let Mr. Chen down!"

Chen Xuan unscrewed his green thermos, took a sip of warm jasmine tea, and looked at Hu Dewei with a gentle, polite smile.

"Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said pleasantly. "I finished checking those three hundred Jilin customs files yesterday afternoon. They are neatly stacked in the blue binder on my desk."

Hu Dewei jumped as if an electric current had shot through his spine. He bowed so low his nose nearly touched the carpet.

"Mr. Chen! I will carry them myself! From now on, you never need to touch a customs file again! Please, enjoy your lunch!"

Chen Xuan chuckled softly, picking his chopsticks back up as Sun Haoran and Hu Dewei backed out of the office with the reverence of men retreating from an imperial court.
