# Chapter 209: Hu Dewei's Stupor

Secretary Lin Wei pushed open the heavy walnut doors of the 38th-floor executive suite and stepped aside politely.

"President Qin, General Manager Sun Haoran and Manager Hu Dewei of Hengxin Logistics are here."

Sun Haoran walked in first with measured, respectful poise, offering a courteous bow toward the wide leather sofa near the sunlit windows. Hu Dewei hurried right behind him, wearing his most practiced corporate smile and cradling his thick leather portfolio like a royal decree.

"President Qin, thank you so much for granting Hengxin this opportunity," Sun Haoran began smoothly. "We brought all of our winter fleet schedules and bonded warehouse certifications for your review."

"Yes, President Qin, Hengxin is completely ready to serve Haiyue Group," Hu Dewei chimed in eagerly, bowing a few degrees lower than his general manager. "Our 12th-floor dispatch division has been working around the clock to prepare for your northern coastal routes."

As Hu Dewei straightened up from his bow, his eyes naturally drifted two feet to the left of Qin Yuhan.

There, sitting comfortably on the same cream leather sofa beside the president of Haiyue Group, was a relaxed young man in a simple gray knit sweater. A checkered cloth-wrapped lunchbox sat on the coffee table right next to a stamped blue Haiyue contract, and the young man was calmly unscrewing the lid of a dented 15-yuan green metal thermos.

For two full seconds, nobody moved.

Sun Haoran froze first, his eyes widening slightly as he took in the scene. Then, in a sudden rush of clarity, a wave of pure, delighted awe washed over his face. Back at Shanshui Villa in autumn, when he had caught a glimpse near the VIP area, he had wondered how a quiet 12th-floor clerk from his own company could draw such unmistakable respect from powerful guests. Seeing Chen Xuan sitting right beside President Qin Yuhan with a homemade lunchbox finally snapped every single puzzle piece into place. His earlier suspicion had not been wild imagination at all.

Hu Dewei, however, had spent that entire night at Shanshui Villa stuck in the outer banquet hall. He had zero warning and zero mental armor.

His eager smile vanished as if someone had wiped it off with a wet rag. His jaw dropped open, the color drained completely from his round face until he looked as pale as winter chalk, and his leather portfolio nearly slipped from his trembling fingers.

"Chen Xuan?" Hu Dewei blurted out in a strangled squeak, forgetting every ounce of boardroom etiquette. "Why are you sitting in President Qin's office? Did you wander onto the wrong floor?"

"Hu Dewei, silence!" Sun Haoran snapped sharply, his voice cutting across the room like a whip.

Qin Yuhan did not look angry at all, though her cool, composed smile carried a razor-sharp edge that made the air around Hu Dewei feel twenty degrees colder. She shifted slightly closer to Chen Xuan, resting one slender hand lightly on his forearm.

"Manager Hu, there is no mistake," Qin Yuhan said evenly. "Allow me to introduce my husband, Chen Xuan. He came up to the 38th floor to bring me lunch."

The simple sentence landed in Hu Dewei's ears like a thunderclap.

His knees literally buckled. Only Sun Haoran's quick hand gripping his elbow kept him from collapsing straight onto the wool carpet.

President Qin's husband!

In a single terrifying flash, every memory of the past two months slammed into Hu Dewei's skull. He remembered dumping 300 dusty, overdue archive files onto Chen Xuan's desk out of petty spite. He remembered lecturing Chen Xuan in front of the entire 12th floor for clocking out at 4:55 PM sharp. Worse yet, he remembered standing in the 35th-floor lounge barely half an hour ago, complaining about Chen Xuan's dented green thermos right in front of General Manager Sun!

Cold sweat broke out instantly along Hu Dewei's collar. His throat felt stuffed with dry cotton, and he could not form a single coherent word.

Chen Xuan, sitting calmly on the sofa, did not look angry or smug in the slightest. He simply gave both visitors an easy, polite nod.

"Good afternoon, General Manager Sun, Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said warmly. "Sorry to interrupt your meeting. I just brought my wife some winter radish soup before it got cold."

Sun Haoran stepped forward smoothly, bowing with genuine, heartfelt respect. "Not an interruption at all, Mr. Chen! I should be thanking you. We have always appreciated your steady, reliable work on the 12th floor, and seeing you here today is truly an honor."

Qin Yuhan smiled faintly, pleased by Sun Haoran's tact, and picked up the blue folder from the coffee table. She held it out to Sun Haoran with both hands.

"General Manager Sun, our operations team reviewed Hengxin's dispatch accuracy over the past four months, and my husband also spoke very highly of your frontline staff on the 12th floor," Qin Yuhan said clearly. "I have already signed and stamped Haiyue's 80 million yuan annual feeder logistics contract. You may take your copy back for your legal review."

Sun Haoran accepted the folder with both hands, his eyes shining with genuine excitement and gratitude. "Thank you, President Qin! Thank you, Mr. Chen! Hengxin will dedicate our very best drivers and coordinators to the northern coastal routes."

"Just one small reminder," Qin Yuhan added lightly, her gaze drifting briefly to the pale, shivering figure of Hu Dewei. "Good dispatch teams work best when they are treated fairly. I hope Hengxin's managers never bury one clerk under 300 old files or force pointless overtime past 4:55 PM."

Hu Dewei jerked upright as if shocked by live electricity, bowing frantically three times in a row.

"Never again! I swear on my career, President Qin, Mr. Chen!" Hu Dewei stammered, his voice trembling. "Starting this afternoon, I am adding two assistants to the 12th-floor dispatch desk, raising year-end bonuses, and enforcing strict 4:55 PM closing hours!"

Sun Haoran shot Hu Dewei a cold sidelong glance that promised a thorough private talk back at Hengxin, then turned back with a warm smile.

Chen Xuan picked up his checkered cloth lunchbox and stood up. "I will leave you all to the contract details. See you tonight, Yuhan."

"Drive safely back to the office," Qin Yuhan said softly.

As Chen Xuan walked calmly past them toward the door, Hu Dewei stepped back so fast he nearly tripped over his own heels, staring in speechless stupor at the modest clerk who had just casually sealed an 80 million yuan deal over radish soup.
