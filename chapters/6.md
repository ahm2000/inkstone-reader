# Chapter 6: Landing in the Dark

The Gulfstream's landing gear locked with a muffled thud as the jet descended toward Lanshui-Yunxi Regional Airfield.

Inside the cabin, Chen Xuan sat with his hands folded loosely in his lap, his expression still. Across the aisle, Number Seven lowered his encrypted tablet. Near the forward bulkhead, Number Nine stood with one hand braced lightly against the door latch. Neither operative looked at the other. In the Black Pine Guard, names did not exist, faces were forgotten by habit, and no man inquired into the past of the man beside him.

"Time to the freight apron?" Chen Xuan asked softly.

"Four minutes," Number Seven replied evenly. "Number Fourteen confirmed the western gate is clear. The six ground vans are staged and fueled."

"And the flight log?"

"Filed under routine provincial charter traffic. No local aviation staff saw the manifest, and the tower supervisor rotated off twenty minutes ago." Number Seven lifted his gaze briefly. "Number One also sent a note from Qingyan. He recommends holding all evidence until the full financial chain is mapped."

A cold stillness settled in Chen Xuan's eyes. "Tell him I agree. Pulling one weed only teaches the roots to grow deeper."

The tires touched wet concrete with barely a tremor as the jet rolled onto the isolated western apron.

When the cabin door unfolded into cool mountain air, Chen Xuan stood without haste and folded his dark wool coat over the armrest. Number Seven stepped forward with a faded gray cotton jacket, a scuffed canvas shoulder bag, and a dented dark-green steel thermos. In three quiet seconds, the man who had crossed six hundred kilometers by private jet vanished, leaving a quiet twenty-four-year-old junior clerk returning home on a Friday night.

At the foot of the airstairs stood Number Fourteen in a plain dark windbreaker beside a dusty black domestic sedan with local Yunxi plates. He inclined his head a fraction of an inch.

"Runway is sealed," Number Fourteen said cleanly. "Local control logged an empty cargo ferry. No one in the terminal saw you disembark."

"Good," Chen Xuan answered, looking past him toward the iron freight gate two hundred meters away.

A low rumble carried across the tarmac. Six unmarked dark vans glided through the open gate onto the feeder road, headlights dipped low, moving in tight spacing toward Yunxi County.

"All six units are rolling?" Chen Xuan asked as the last taillights vanished.

"Yes," Number Fourteen said. "Technical analysts, forensic accountants, legal-archive specialists, and field operatives. Each van has its sector loaded. They will be active in Yunxi within twenty-five minutes."

Chen Xuan turned back, holding Number Fourteen's gaze. "Start with my Second Uncle. How is he holding up?"

Number Fourteen straightened slightly. "Chen Mingde is in Holding Room Three at Old Street Station. Our embedded operative, Number Fifty-six, is on night duty in that corridor. Chen Mingde is unharmed."

"Is he rattled?" Chen Xuan asked softly.

"Shaken, mostly worried about his wife," Number Fourteen replied. "Two hours ago, a patrol officer pushed a blank statement sheet across the table and told him to sign it if he wanted to go home before dawn. Chen Mingde refused. His hands were trembling, yet he kept repeating he never struck anyone. Thirty minutes ago, Number Fifty-six brought him warm water and a clean blanket without drawing notice from the duty sergeant. No one inside that station will raise a hand against him."

A faint breath left Chen Xuan's lips, the slight tension along his jaw easing. "And the case file?"

"Already falling apart," Number Fourteen said, a dry edge entering his flat voice. "The detention rests on two items: a Yunxi People's Hospital injury report claiming a fractured rib and mild concussion for a Hongrui Real Estate foreman, and a forty-minute gap in the municipal camera feed at the north mouth of Old Street."

"Start with the hospital," Chen Xuan said.

"Our technical unit pulled the raw digital audit trail twenty minutes ago. The X-ray film belongs to a traffic accident patient treated three months ago, and the attending physician's electronic stamp was logged forty minutes after clinic hours."

Chen Xuan's eyes narrowed. "Bought, or pressured?"

"We are tracing his bank transfers and call logs now," Number Fourteen answered. "Early signs point to a transfer through a relative's account two weeks ago, plus three calls from Hongrui Real Estate's office manager. We will have the exact figure before midnight."

"And the street camera?"

"Manually looped from a county maintenance terminal," Number Fourteen said. "They missed two private dashcams parked near the tobacco stall and a bank ATM lens eighty meters up the lane. Both angles are clear. Three men from Hongrui Real Estate entered Mingde Hardware first, smashed the glass counter, and shoved Chen Mingde into the display rack before claiming he swung a wrench. Our team secured verified copies of every frame."

Chen Xuan stood silent for two seconds, his fingers resting on his steel thermos.

"Do not hand any of it to Old Street Station tonight," Chen Xuan said calmly.

Number Fourteen did not blink. "Keep the lid tight."

"If we drop clean proof on Station Director Cheng Biao's desk tonight, he smiles, blames a junior clerk for an administrative mistake, signs a release slip, and warns his backers before the ink dries," Chen Xuan said, his tone level and cold. "By noon tomorrow, the hospital records vanish and the people behind Hongrui Real Estate hide behind shell companies."

"Understood," Number Fourteen said crisply. "Number One sent the same assessment five minutes ago. We are pulling the full chain behind Hongrui Real Estate, Old Street's demolition bidding, and Cheng Biao's private accounts before we move. Number Twenty-three and a six-man watch team are already across from Mingde Hardware."

"Keep Number Fifty-six on audio coverage in that holding corridor," Chen Xuan instructed. "If anyone tries to pressure Chen Mingde into signing a confession or a demolition consent form, intervene quietly and record every word."

"Already arranged. Every word in that room is being logged."

Behind them, Number Seven and Number Nine stepped down with two compact aluminum cases and moved smoothly toward a dark utility vehicle to set up the local relay post. Chen Xuan opened the rear door of the dusty sedan, set his thermos on the seat, and slid inside.

Number Fourteen took the wheel and eased away without high beams.

"One more thing," Number Fourteen said quietly, checking the mirror. "Your father, Chen Jianguo, rode his electric scooter to Old Street Station four hours ago with tea and a sweater. They refused to let him past the gate. He stood outside for an hour before riding home."

Chen Xuan's hand stilled on the thermos lid, his dark eyes going flat.

"He is at Fuxing New Village now?" Chen Xuan asked.

"Confirmed. Our perimeter team ensured no Hongrui Real Estate lookouts tailed him."

"Drive straight there," Chen Xuan said quietly. "Stop two blocks south of the gate."

Number Fourteen nodded once. Tomorrow morning, the men behind Hongrui Real Estate and Old Street Station would wake believing Yunxi County still belonged to them. Tonight, Chen Xuan simply needed to walk up six flights of stairs and drink a bowl of his mother's soup.
