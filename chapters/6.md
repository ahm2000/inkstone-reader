# Chapter 6: Landing in the Dark

The private jet's landing gear locked with a soft thud as it descended toward Sha-Yunxi Regional Airfield.

Inside the cabin, Chen Xuan sat with his hands resting loosely in his lap. Across the aisle, Number 7 put down his locked tablet. Near the front, Number 9 stood braced lightly by the door latch.

"How long until the cargo tarmac?" Chen Xuan asked softly.

"Four minutes," Number 7 replied evenly. "Number 14 confirmed the west gate is clear. All six vans are fueled and lined up."

"And the flight log?"

"Filed as a local charter. No airport staff saw the passenger list, and the tower chief ended his shift twenty minutes ago." Number 7 lifted his eyes. "Number 1 also sent a note from Shanghai. He suggests holding all proof back until we map the whole money trail."

A cold stillness settled in Chen Xuan's eyes. "Tell him I agree. Pulling one weed only teaches the roots to grow deeper."

The tires touched the wet runway with barely a bump, rolling onto the quiet west tarmac.

When the cabin door opened into cool mountain air, Chen Xuan stood and folded his dark wool coat over the armrest. Number 7 handed him a faded gray cotton jacket, a worn canvas bag, and a dented green steel thermos. In three seconds, the man who had flown six hundred kilometers on a private jet was gone. In his place stood a quiet twenty-four-year-old office clerk coming home on Friday night.

At the bottom of the stairs stood Number 14 beside a dusty black sedan with Yunxi plates. He bowed his head slightly.

"The runway is sealed," Number 14 said cleanly. "Local control logged an empty cargo flight. Nobody in the terminal saw you."

"Good," Chen Xuan answered, looking toward the iron cargo gate two hundred meters away.

A low rumble carried across the tarmac. Six unmarked dark vans rolled through the gate onto the side road, headlights low, heading in a tight line toward Yunxi County.

"All six teams moving?" Chen Xuan asked as the taillights vanished.

"Yes," Number 14 said. "Tech experts, money checkers, legal file trackers, and field men. They will be active in Yunxi within twenty-five minutes."

Chen Xuan met Number 14's eyes. "Start with my Second Uncle. How is he?"

Number 14 stood straighter. "Chen Mingde is in Holding Room Three at Old Street Station. Number 56 is on night duty in that hall. Chen Mingde has not been hurt."

"Is he scared?" Chen Xuan asked softly.

"Shaken, and mostly worried about his wife," Number 14 replied. "Two hours ago, a patrol officer pushed a blank statement sheet across the table and told him to sign if he wanted to go home before morning. Chen Mingde refused. His hands shook, yet he kept repeating he never hit anyone. Thirty minutes ago, Number 56 brought him warm water and a clean blanket without drawing notice. No one inside that station will touch him."

A faint breath left Chen Xuan's lips, his jaw easing. "And the case file?"

"Already falling apart," Number 14 said dryly. "The hold rests on two things: a Yunxi People's Hospital injury report claiming a cracked rib and mild head injury for a Hongrui Real Estate foreman, and a forty-minute gap in street camera video at the north end of Old Street."

"Start with the hospital," Chen Xuan said.

"Our tech team pulled the raw log twenty minutes ago. The X-ray belongs to a car crash patient treated three months ago, and the doctor's stamp was entered forty minutes after the clinic closed."

Chen Xuan's eyes narrowed. "Paid off, or forced?"

"We are checking his bank transfers and call logs now," Number 14 answered. "Early signs point to a payment through a relative's account two weeks ago, plus three calls from Hongrui Real Estate's office manager. We will have the exact amount before midnight."

"And the street camera?"

"Looped by hand from a county repair computer," Number 14 said. "They missed two parked car cameras near the tobacco stall and a bank ATM camera eighty meters up the lane. Both views are clear. Three men from Hongrui Real Estate entered Mingde Hardware first, smashed the glass counter, and shoved Chen Mingde into the shelves before claiming he swung a wrench. We saved clean copies of every frame."

Chen Xuan stood silent for two seconds, fingers on his thermos.

"Do not hand any of it to Old Street Station tonight," Chen Xuan said calmly.

Number 14 did not blink. "Keep the lid shut."

"Drop clean proof on Station Director Cheng Biao's desk tonight, and he smiles, blames a junior clerk, signs a release slip, and warns his backers before the ink dries," Chen Xuan said coldly. "By noon tomorrow, hospital records vanish and the people behind Hongrui Real Estate hide behind shell companies."

"Understood," Number 14 said crisply. "Number 1 sent the same view five minutes ago. We are pulling the full chain behind Hongrui Real Estate, Old Street's tear-down bids, and Cheng Biao's private accounts before we strike. Number 23 and a six-man watch team are already across from Mingde Hardware."

"Keep Number 56 watching with live sound in that holding hall," Chen Xuan said. "If anyone tries to push Chen Mingde into signing a confession or move-out paper, step in quietly and record every word."

"Already set. Every word is being recorded."

Behind them, Number 7 and Number 9 walked down the stairs with two metal cases toward a dark van to set up the local signal post. Chen Xuan climbed into the back of the dusty sedan, setting his thermos on the seat.

Number 14 took the wheel and drove off without high beams.

"One more thing," Number 14 said quietly, glancing at the mirror. "Your father, Chen Jianguo, rode his electric scooter to Old Street Station four hours ago with hot tea and a sweater. They refused him at the gate. He stood outside an hour before riding home."

Chen Xuan's hand stopped on the thermos lid, his dark eyes going flat.

"He is at Fuxing New Village now?" Chen Xuan asked.

"Confirmed. Our watch team made sure no Hongrui Real Estate lookouts followed him."

"Drive straight there," Chen Xuan said quietly. "Stop two blocks south of the gate."

Number 14 nodded. Tomorrow morning, the men behind Hongrui Real Estate and Old Street Station would wake thinking Yunxi County still belonged to them. Tonight, Chen Xuan only needed to climb six flights of stairs and drink his mother's soup.
