# Chapter 6: The Zero Guard

In a walled courtyard in the east of Qingyan City, a large map glowed on the study wall of Number One, head of the Black Pine Guard. Two hundred and fourteen green dots shone across it.

One dot, in Yunxi County, had turned yellow and begun to blink, signaling an incoming message.

The duty captain's voice came over the speaker, tight with barely controlled anger. "Old Street Station, Number One. Number Thirty-four just flagged a red alert."

"Read it."

As the captain read the report line by line, the quiet study felt as if the temperature had dropped twenty degrees. Number One's teacup froze halfway to his lips, and his knuckles turned dead white against the porcelain.

"Repeat that," Number One said, his voice dangerously low. "Someone in a county town actually raised a hand and struck his father across the face?"

"Yes, Number One," the captain answered, his own voice shaking with disbelief and fury. "A local thug working for Hongrui Real Estate named Yan Tiehu. Even Number Thirty-four snapped his pen watching it on the station log. Our operatives on the ground are asking for permission to move."

Number One set the teacup down so hard the saucer cracked cleanly down the middle. For forty years, men at the very peak of the country had spoken softly in front of the Black Pine Guard, yet a street thug in Yunxi County had dared to humiliate the Boss's own father in public.

"What is the Boss doing right now?" Number One asked.

"Number Nine is stationed across the hall from their apartment, and the close-protection posts on the lobby, stairwell, and opposite rooftop never lost visual," the duty captain said rapidly. "Our outer perimeter checkpoints scanned Yan Tiehu's van the second he entered Fuxing New Village. Rooftop sniper overwatch had him in thermal crosshairs with live ammunition chambered the entire way up, and two close-protection operatives stood behind the fifth-floor landing door with suppressed sidearms drawn. He carried only a paintbrush and paper, no lethal weapon, so under the Boss's standing non-exposure rule they held fire while recording every step. The Boss threw the brush straight into the trash and walked into the bathroom."

Before Number One could reply, the main console on his desk chimed once and flashed a dark crimson border that had not lit up in years.

Number One's hand froze in midair. Every drop of color drained from his face as he stared at the single automated alert on his screen.

*ABSOLUTE ORDER TRIGGERED DIRECTLY BY SUPREME. ZERO GUARD ACTIVE. SECTOR: YUNXI COUNTY.*

Even Number One, head of the entire Black Pine Guard, did not hold an activation key for the Zero Guard. He had no command, no authority, and no permission over them. They answered solely to Chen Xuan himself, and only Chen Xuan could wake them. Outside of Number One, not a single operative in the Black Pine Guard even knew that a higher Zero level existed above their roster.

Realizing that the Boss had bypassed the entire Black Pine Guard and activated the Zero Guard himself, Number One's composure shattered into pure fury. He snatched up the speaker handset and barked into it hard enough to rattle the console.

"You useless fools!" Number One roared down the line. "Three quiet years with no real threats, and every single one of you grew soft and lazy around him! You let a county thug slap his father in broad daylight, and then you stood behind a door while he walked right up to his mother's home! Do you have any idea what your carelessness just triggered?!"

On the other end of the speaker, the duty captain went rigid, cold sweat breaking out across his back. He had never once heard Number One raise his voice in ten years. "Number One, what happened? Did another agency move in?"

"Shut your mouth and listen!" Number One barked furiously. "The Boss himself just activated a level far above every single one of you! Pull every Black Pine Guard unit outside the ten-kilometer line right now! Clear Building 2, clear Old Street, and tell Number Seven not to lay a finger on Yan Tiehu! That target no longer belongs to us! Do not ask who is stepping in, do not look at them, and do not get in their way!"

A stunned, terrified silence fell across the other end of the line. A level above the Black Pine Guard? A force so high that even Number One was barking orders to get out of their path?

Before the duty captain could even finish stammering a reply, the heavy wooden doors of Number One's own quiet courtyard study swung open without a single sound.

Five figures in matte-black gear and featureless full-face masks stepped inside and fanned cleanly around the study, two by the door, two by the windows, and one directly across from Number One's desk. At the exact same second, the giant wall map of two hundred and fourteen green dots flickered and died, replaced by a stark crimson banner.

*BLACK PINE GUARD COMMAND SUSPENDED. ALL OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY REVOKED. STANDBY UNDER ZERO GUARD MONITORING.*

Through the speaker, the duty captain gasped in raw panic. "Number One! Every console in our command van just locked out! Five masked figures just walked straight through our sealed doors! They took our weapon keys and won't say a single word!"

Inside the study, the masked Zero Guard operative across from Number One laid a matte-black directive card onto the desk and spoke in a flat, completely neutral, genderless voice so cold it chilled the room.

"Zero Order. Black Pine suspended."

The masked figure stepped back two paces and fell completely mute.

Number One did not flinch, and he did not argue. He stood straight behind his desk, bowed his head once in solemn acceptance, and spoke clearly into the open speaker so every stunned Black Pine Guard officer on the circuit could hear him.

"You heard the directive," Number One said, his voice heavy with shame and cold resolve. "Every Black Pine Guard operative puts down their equipment, stops every operation, and stands silently under Zero Guard watch, just like every other agency in this country. We have no authority until the Boss recalls them. Remember this humiliation tonight. When the Zero Guard finally withdraws and hands duty back to us, every single man on this roster will be ten times more vigilant and ten times more careful, or you will not wear the Black Pine again!"

Across the entire Black Pine Guard network, two hundred and fourteen seasoned operatives stood frozen at attention under the mute gaze of masked Zero Guard observers, stripped of all power and unable to lift a single finger.

* * *

Within minutes, the night over Yunxi County changed.

Silent figures in matte-black gear and featureless full-face masks stepped into the fifty-meter ring around Building 2 and fanned across the ten-kilometer core. No skin showed. No rank tabs showed. No radio chatter broke the night.

Beneath quiet hillsides and darkened rooftop vents, concealed defense grids woke up without a sound. Anti-missile batteries and anti-aircraft launchers unlocked into firing arcs. Automated micro-turrets slid into place. Live-ammunition sniper teams locked onto every intersection, courtyard, and stairwell. Full-spectrum electronic jammers hummed alive, turning ten kilometers of airspace and satellite feeds into a dead black void.

Outward from the ten-kilometer core to a full hundred-kilometer radius across all three directions, land, river, and highway, unmarked patrols, mobile checkpoints, and heavy interception posts snapped into place within minutes. Every expressway ramp, county border, and secondary road was sealed and monitored.

High above the clouds, out of sight of ordinary eyes, twin pairs of dark-painted fighter jets slipped into silent combat air patrol over the hundred-kilometer shell, paired with high-altitude electronic-warfare aircraft and specialized counter-drone arrays.

The air around Yunxi County grew chillingly, suffocatingly still.

* * *

Inside the Yunxi County Party Committee Building, Fan Weiguo was still awake at his desk when the heavy oak door opened without a knock.

His secretary did not even have time to rise. Two figures in dark matte uniforms and featureless black masks stepped inside, moving with an eerie, weightless precision.

Fan Weiguo shot to his feet, his heart hammering against his ribs. "Who are you?! How did you get past the armed gate guards?! Security! Call the police right now!"

Nobody outside answered. Through the open doorway, he saw his secretary and two compound guards standing frozen against the corridor wall, pale as ghosts, with two more featureless black masks watching them in dead silence.

One of the masked figures inside the office laid a matte-black card in the center of Fan Weiguo's desk. When it spoke, the voice was completely flat, neutral, and chillingly genderless. Fan Weiguo could not tell whether the speaker was a man or a woman, young or old.

"Zero Order. Lockdown."

Nothing else. Not a single explanation. The two masked figures stepped back against the wall and went completely mute.

Fan Weiguo stared at the black card, his face flushing with confusion and rising panic. "Zero Order?! What nonsense is a Zero Order?! I have served thirty years in government and I have never heard of any Zero unit! Are you terrorists? Are you from the province? Answer me!"

Silence. Neither masked figure moved a millimeter.

His hands shaking, Fan Weiguo snatched up his red government landline and dialed the Qingyan City Party Secretary's emergency desk. To his shock, the line connected on the first ring, and a hoarse, terrified whisper burst through the receiver.

"Fan Weiguo! Shut your mouth right now!" the City Party Secretary hissed, his voice cracking with sheer dread. "Are there masked people in your office?! Do not argue with them! Do not ask who they are! Six of them walked straight into MY conference room two minutes ago! The province just called us in total panic and ordered every city and county to cooperate completely and freeze all local authority indefinitely! Put the phone down, do not touch a single file, and pray we all live through tonight!"

Click. The line went dead.

Fan Weiguo's fingers went numb, and the receiver slipped from his grasp. He had never heard of them, never dreamed such a force existed, and yet even the City Party Secretary and the province were terrified out of their minds.

At the exact same minute, inside the County Police Bureau, Mai Zhenhua had lunged for his service pistol and barked for his SWAT squad, only to watch the hallway doors open onto six disarmed SWAT officers standing motionless under silent black-masked watch. Before Mai Zhenhua could even shout another order, his red desk phone blared with the City Police Chief screaming at him to drop his weapon, freeze every police station in Yunxi, and obey without a single question.

Across the street in the County Government Building, Tao Shu stared at the matte-black card on his desk with trembling hands while two mute, masked figures stood motionless beside his window.

Fan Weiguo sank slowly into his leather chair, cold sweat soaking through his shirt. In five minutes, Yunxi County had been stripped of all local control indefinitely by ghosts nobody at his level had ever even heard of, and he had no idea who in his tiny county had woken this storm.
