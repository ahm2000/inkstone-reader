# Chapter 208: The Husband's Stamp

Qin Yuhan pulled Chen Xuan over to the low glass coffee table in the center of the office sitting area, perching herself comfortably on the edge of the cream leather sofa.

Chen Xuan set the red tote bag down, unzipped the cover, and unpacked the three stainless steel thermal containers. He laid out a pair of polished bamboo chopsticks and a porcelain spoon, setting the dishes neatly before her.

The top tier revealed tender, glossy cubes of beef brisket braised with sweet white winter radishes in a rich, amber sauce. The middle tier held silky steamed egg custard topped with scallions and a drizzle of sesame oil, while the bottom tier was packed with fluffy jasmine rice.

"Oh, it smells heavenly!" Qin Yuhan breathed, clapping her hands together with pure delight. She picked up the chopsticks, scooped up a steaming piece of brisket, and popped it into her mouth. She closed her eyes, letting out a blissful hum of satisfaction. "Mmm, so tender! The radish soaked up all the meat juices! How do you make it taste so much better than the five-star hotel chefs?"

"Slow simmering over low heat for two hours," Chen Xuan smiled, unscrewing his green thermos and pouring a cup of warm roasted jasmine tea for her. "High-end restaurants rush everything with pressure cookers. Good cooking requires patience."

Qin Yuhan took the warm teacup, drank half of it in two thirsty gulps, and sighed with deep relief. "My throat feels human again. Morning meetings are exhausting. Everyone wants a piece of the new coastal routes."

Chen Xuan sat down beside her, leaning back against the leather cushion with one arm resting comfortably along the sofa back. "Speaking of the new coastal routes, I saw two familiar faces in the VIP lounge on the fifty-fifth floor when the elevator stopped."

Qin Yuhan swallowed a spoonful of steamed egg custard and tilted her head. "Who?"

"General Manager Sun Haoran and Manager Hu Dewei from Hengxin," Chen Xuan replied with a faint, amused smile. "Sun Haoran was sweating through his collar rehearsing his presentation, and Hu Dewei looked like he was about to faint from stage fright."

Qin Yuhan froze with her chopsticks halfway to her mouth, blinking in sudden realization.

"Wait, that is right!" she gasped, laughing aloud. "Hengxin Logistics had an eleven-forty-five appointment on my calendar! Secretary Lin told me General Manager Sun had been waiting since early morning to pitch for the northern feeder routes!"

She looked at Chen Xuan, her eyes sparkling with affectionate mischief. She picked up a juicy piece of brisket with her chopsticks and fed it directly into his mouth.

"Tell me the truth, husband," Qin Yuhan asked playfully, watching him chew. "How is Hengxin's operations capacity? Does your company actually have enough trucks and warehouse space to handle our northern container transfers, or are they just boasting?"

Chen Xuan swallowed the savory beef and thought for a brief second. "Sun Haoran is a bit nervous and overly eager to impress, but he runs a disciplined fleet. The dispatch supervisors on the twelfth floor are honest workers, and our port turnover rate is within ninety-two percent. If Haiyue gives them twenty percent of the regional feeder lines, they can handle it without dropping the ball."

"And Manager Hu Dewei?" Qin Yuhan asked, raising a delicate eyebrow. "Doesn't he give you annoying busywork at the office?"

Chen Xuan chuckled softly, taking a sip of tea from his thermos. "Hu Dewei likes to pretend he is very important, and he dumps customs paperwork on my desk when he wants to look busy. But that is harmless office politics. Giving Hengxin the contract keeps two hundred truck drivers and warehouse clerks employed with steady bonuses. That is a good thing for ordinary families."

Qin Yuhan looked at her husband with deep tenderness in her dark eyes. She reached out and gently smoothed the collar of his dress shirt.

"You always think about other people first," she murmured softly.

She stood up, walked over to her executive desk in her bare stockinged feet, picked up the blue presentation folder left by Secretary Lin, and carried it back to the coffee table. She flipped straight to the final approval page, picked up her gold fountain pen, and signed her name with a flourish: *Qin Yuhan*.

Beside her signature, she checked the allocation box and wrote: *Authorized for Phase One Northern Feeder Lanes, annual volume 80 million yuan.*

She capped her pen, set the folder on the table, and turned back to Chen Xuan with a radiant, teasing grin.

"There," she said, leaning her head affectionately against his shoulder. "Eighty million yuan awarded on the recommendation of Senior Operations Consultant Chen Xuan. Now, eat another piece of beef before it gets cold."

Just as Chen Xuan picked up his chopsticks, a soft knock sounded at the office door.

Assistant Secretary Lin Wei peeked inside, holding a digital tablet. "President Qin, excuse the interruption. General Manager Sun Haoran and Department Manager Hu Dewei from Hengxin Logistics are outside for their eleven-forty-five appointment."

Qin Yuhan smiled, her arm still casually looped through Chen Xuan's elbow.

"Send them in, Secretary Lin," Qin Yuhan said calmly.
