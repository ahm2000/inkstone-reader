# Chapter 72: Panic at Old Street Station

At thirty-two minutes past eight, the front lobby of Old Street Police Station smelled of old cigarette smoke and fear. Station Director Cheng Biao paced behind the front counter like a trapped pig. He held a cigarette between shaking fingers while his other hand kept hitting redial on the desk phone.

"Still dead!" Cheng Biao snarled. He slammed the phone down hard enough to crack the plastic base. He spun around to face Deputy Director Zhou Quan, his face dark red with anger. "What is wrong with the county police phone line? Eight calls to Chief Liang Guodong's office, six to his mobile, and not a single one rings through!"

Deputy Director Zhou Quan stood beside the glass window, wiping his sweaty palms on his pants. Four young patrol officers sat frozen at their desks. They pretended to sort traffic tickets while sneaking nervous looks at the shouting.

"Director Cheng Biao, calm down," Zhou Quan said, his voice tight and thin. "It is Monday morning. Chief Liang Guodong is sitting in the eight-thirty roll call with County Magistrate Sun Weiming. He probably put his phone on silent."

"And the radio operator went to the roll call too?" Cheng Biao barked, pointing his cigarette at the dead police radio. "What about Yan Tiehu? Why is Yan Tiehu's phone turned off? His crew was supposed to clear the west alley behind Mingde Hardware at eight! Nobody showed up, and Chairman Deng Yongchang's office phone just clicks dead!"

Zhou Quan swallowed hard. "Maybe it is still that phone outage from midnight. Director Cheng Biao, while we wait for Chief Liang Guodong, should we move Chen Mingde to the county jail before nine? That way his family cannot come make a scene out front."

"Do not touch Chen Mingde!" Cheng Biao snapped, his eyes narrowing. "Chief Liang Guodong told me to keep Chen Mingde right here in Holding Cell Two until Hongrui Real Estate's lawyer brings the move-out paper at noon. We have a signed injury report from Yunxi People's Hospital and a legal arrest warrant! The paperwork is solid as iron!"

Zhou Quan shifted his feet and glanced down the cell hallway. "Director Cheng Biao, the hospital clerk called me at seven sounding terrified. He said the X-ray computer showed someone from outside checked the files last night. What if someone looks closely at that scan?"

"Who in Yunxi County dares to check Chief Liang Guodong's file?" Cheng Biao sneered, taking a hard drag on his cigarette. "Stop shaking like a scared dog, Zhou Quan. Pour yourself some tea and sit down."

Cheng Biao had barely finished speaking when heavy tires screeched outside the front yard.

Three matte-black provincial SUVs braked hard across the concrete driveway, blocking the gate completely. Side doors slid open, and heavy boots hit the ground in a sharp, steady rhythm.

The glass lobby doors swung wide open.

Twelve officers walked straight into the station. Six wore the dark windbreakers of the Provincial Anti-Corruption Office. Six wore matte-black special police vests that made the four young patrol officers jump out of their chairs in instant terror. Two officers blocked the front doors while two more moved fast to guard the gun room and the cell hallway.

In the middle walked a lean, sharp-eyed officer in his late forties wearing senior provincial rank badges.

Cheng Biao's cigarette fell from his fingers and hissed on the floor. His fat face went slack, then drained pale. With shaking hands he buttoned his wrinkled collar, stepped around the desk, and threw up a stiff, sweaty salute.

"Welcome, provincial leaders!" Cheng Biao blurted out behind a tight, frantic grin. "Old Street Station Director Cheng Biao reporting! We did not know a provincial team was coming, or we would have lined up outside to greet you! Please come into my office for some tea!"

The lean officer did not return the salute. His cold eyes swept over Cheng Biao's sweaty forehead, then moved to Zhou Quan, whose knees were shaking behind the glass.

"I am Han Zhiyuan, Provincial Police Discipline and Supervision Division," he said, his voice flat and cold as winter iron. "Step back from the desk, Cheng Biao. Keep your hands where I can see them."

Cheng Biao's forced smile cracked. "Leader Han Zhiyuan, there must be some mistake. If this is about the Old Street demolition argument, we only followed county police orders. We answered an assault call at Mingde Hardware and locked up the suspect by the book."

Han Zhiyuan pulled two printed proof sheets from his briefcase and dropped them flat onto the counter right in front of Cheng Biao.

"Look at sheet one," Han Zhiyuan ordered quietly.

Cheng Biao looked down. His breath caught in his throat.

Printed in sharp detail was the hospital X-ray file used to accuse Chen Mingde of breaking a worker's ribs. Highlighted in red boxes were the hidden computer details: the real patient was twenty-nine years old, hurt three years ago in a highway motorcycle crash. The file had been copied and renamed on Yunxi People's Hospital computer number four at eleven twenty last night.

"Now look at sheet two," Han Zhiyuan said.

Cheng Biao's hand shook hard against the edge of the counter. The second sheet showed the time log from the county arrest computer. Chen Mingde's arrest warrant had been typed up under Cheng Biao's own code at eight eighteen last night. That was twelve full minutes before Old Street Station's patrol car even got the fake call or drove to Mingde Hardware.

"Twelve minutes before the supposed fight," Han Zhiyuan said without blinking. "You made up a serious crime, pasted a three-year-old traffic X-ray onto a sixty-year-old man, and locked an innocent citizen in Holding Cell Two to steal his shop."

The four young patrol officers stared in horrified silence. One dropped his pen and backed up against the wall.

Sweat stung Cheng Biao's eyes. Suddenly he spun around and pointed a shaking finger straight at Zhou Quan.

"Leader Han Zhiyuan, I did not know!" Cheng Biao yelled, his voice high and shrill with panic. "Deputy Director Zhou Quan handled the whole file! He brought me the warrant to stamp and swore the hospital scan was real! Zhou Quan must have taken money from Yan Tiehu behind my back!"

Zhou Quan stared at him for half a second. Then his pale face twisted with pure rage.

"You shameless bastard, Cheng Biao!" Zhou Quan screamed across the lobby, slamming his hand on the glass. "You sat in your office with Chief Liang Guodong on speakerphone! You ordered me to fake the time on the warrant before the patrol car even left the yard! You took 200k yuan in cash from Chairman Deng Yongchang's driver last Friday in the back alley! I kept the tea box with your fingerprints inside my locker!"

"Shut your lying mouth, Zhou Quan!" Cheng Biao roared. He lunged half a step forward before two special police officers grabbed his arms in an iron grip.

"Enough," Han Zhiyuan said calmly. "Chief Liang Guodong and County Magistrate Sun Weiming were arrested at the county compound three minutes ago. Chairman Deng Yongchang's headquarters is being sealed right now. You can finish arguing in the provincial interrogation room."

Cheng Biao went limp in the officers' hands. His eyes bulged with pure dread. "Liang Guodong is arrested? Already?"

"Cuff both of them," Han Zhiyuan ordered.

Two sharp metal clicks echoed through the silent station. Steel cuffs bit into Cheng Biao's thick wrists and Zhou Quan's shaking hands in front of every patrol officer and clerk. Zhou Quan sank half to his knees, sobbing that he would tell everything to get a lighter sentence.

Han Zhiyuan turned to the pale duty clerk standing behind the key board.

"You," Han Zhiyuan said quietly. "Hand me the key to Holding Cell Two. Now."
