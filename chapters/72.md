# Chapter 72: Panic at Old Street Station

At thirty-two minutes past eight, the front duty lobby of Old Street Police Station reeked of stale cigarette smoke and panic. Station Director Cheng Biao paced behind the reception counter like a trapped boar, cigarette pinched between trembling fingers while his other hand mashed redial on the desk phone.

"Still dead!" Cheng Biao snarled, slamming the handset down hard enough to crack the cradle. He spun on Deputy Director Zhou Quan, face mottled dark red. "What the hell is wrong with the county bureau switchboard? Eight calls to Chief Liang Guodong's office, six to his mobile, and not a single ring goes through!"

Deputy Director Zhou Quan stood beside the glass partition, wiping damp palms against his uniform trousers. Four junior patrolmen sat frozen at the intake desks, pretending to sort traffic slips while watching the shouting.

"Director Cheng Biao, calm down," Zhou Quan said, voice tight and thin. "It is Monday morning. Chief Liang Guodong is sitting in the eight-thirty roll call with County Magistrate Sun Weiming. He probably silenced his phone."

"And the radio dispatcher went to the roll call too?" Cheng Biao barked, jabbing his cigarette toward the silent transceiver. "And Yan Tiehu? Why is Yan Tiehu's phone switched off? His demolition crew was supposed to clear the west alley behind Mingde Hardware at eight! Nobody showed up, and Chairman Deng Yongchang's office line clicks dead!"

Zhou Quan swallowed hard, Adam's apple bobbing. "Maybe it is still that midnight telecom outage. Director Cheng Biao, while we wait for Chief Liang Guodong, should we transfer Chen Mingde to the county detention center before nine? Then his relatives cannot make a scene out front."

"Do not touch Chen Mingde!" Cheng Biao snapped, eyes narrowing. "Chief Liang Guodong told me to hold Chen Mingde right here in Holding Cell Two until Hongrui Real Estate's lawyer brings the relocation waiver at noon. We have a signed injury report from Yunxi People's Hospital and a lawful criminal detention warrant! The paperwork is steel!"

Zhou Quan shifted uneasily, glancing down the cell corridor. "Director Cheng Biao, the hospital clerk called me at seven sounding terrified. He said the radiology server logged an outside audit query overnight. What if someone checks that scan?"

"Who in Yunxi County dares check Chief Liang Guodong's file?" Cheng Biao sneered, dragging hard on his cigarette. "Stop shaking like a wet dog, Zhou Quan. Pour yourself some tea and sit down."

Cheng Biao barely finished the sentence when heavy tires screeched outside the courtyard.

Three matte-black provincial SUVs braked across the concrete apron, sealing the gate completely. Side doors slid open and boots struck the pavement in crisp, disciplined rhythm.

The glass doors swung wide.

Twelve officers strode straight into the lobby. Six wore dark windbreakers of the Provincial Anti-Corruption Investigation Bureau; six wore matte-black provincial counter-terrorism tactical vests that made the four junior patrolmen shoot out of their chairs in instant terror. Two officers flanked the front doors while two more moved swiftly to secure the armory and cell corridor.

At their center walked a lean, sharp-eyed officer in his late forties wearing senior provincial insignia.

Cheng Biao's cigarette slipped from his fingers, hissing on the linoleum. His bloated face went slack, then rushed pale. With shaking hands he fumbled his rumpled collar shut, stepped around the desk, and snapped up a stiff, sweaty salute.

"Welcome, provincial leaders!" Cheng Biao blurted out over a frantic, stretched grin. "Old Street Station Director Cheng Biao reporting! We had no advance notice of a provincial inspection, or we would have assembled the squad outside! Please come into my office for tea!"

The lean officer did not return the salute. His cold gaze swept Cheng Biao's sweating brow, then shifted to Zhou Quan, whose knees visibly knocked behind the glass.

"I am Han Zhiyuan, Provincial Public Security Department Discipline and Supervision Division," he stated, voice flat as winter iron. "Step back from the desk, Cheng Biao. Keep your hands visible."

Cheng Biao's forced smile cracked. "Leader Han Zhiyuan, surely there is a misunderstanding. If this is about the Old Street demolition dispute, we strictly followed county bureau orders. We answered an assault call at Mingde Hardware and detained the suspect by procedure."

Han Zhiyuan pulled two laminated evidence sheets from his briefcase and dropped them cleanly onto the counter directly in front of Cheng Biao.

"Look at sheet one," Han Zhiyuan ordered quietly.

Cheng Biao's eyes dropped. His breath hitched sharply.

Printed in razor-sharp detail was the digital DICOM hospital scan used to accuse Chen Mingde of breaking a worker's ribs. Highlighted in crimson boxes sat the raw server metadata: original patient age twenty-nine, recorded three years earlier after a highway motorcycle crash, copied and renamed on Yunxi People's Hospital workstation four at eleven twenty last night.

"Now look at sheet two," Han Zhiyuan said.

Cheng Biao's hand shook violently against the counter edge. The second sheet showed the digital timestamp log of the county detention registry. Chen Mingde's detention warrant had been generated under Cheng Biao's code at eight eighteen the previous evening, twelve full minutes before Old Street Station's patrol car even received the fake call or reached Mingde Hardware.

"Twelve minutes before the alleged assault," Han Zhiyuan said, eyes unblinking. "You fabricated a felony, grafted a three-year-old traffic scan onto a sixty-year-old civilian, and locked an innocent man in Holding Cell Two to coerce his property."

The four junior patrolmen stared in horrified silence. One dropped his pen and backed against the wall.

Sweat stung Cheng Biao's eyes. Suddenly he spun and stabbed a shaking finger straight at Zhou Quan.

"Leader Han Zhiyuan, I did not know!" Cheng Biao yelled, voice pitch-high with panic. "Deputy Director Zhou Quan handled the whole intake file! He brought me the warrant to stamp and swore the scan was verified! Zhou Quan must have taken money from Yan Tiehu behind my back!"

Zhou Quan stared at him for half a second, then his pale face twisted with desperate fury.

"You shameless bastard, Cheng Biao!" Zhou Quan screamed across the lobby, slamming the glass partition. "You sat in your office with Chief Liang Guodong on speakerphone! You ordered me to backdate the warrant before the patrol car even left the yard! You took 200k yuan in cash from Chairman Deng Yongchang's driver last Friday in the back alley! I kept the tea box with your fingerprints in my locker!"

"Shut your lying mouth, Zhou Quan!" Cheng Biao roared, lunging half a step before two counter-terrorism officers seized his arms in an iron grip.

"Enough," Han Zhiyuan said calmly. "Chief Liang Guodong and County Magistrate Sun Weiming were arrested at the county compound three minutes ago. Chairman Deng Yongchang's headquarters is being sealed right now. Finish arguing in the provincial interrogation room."

Cheng Biao went limp in the officers' hold, eyes bulging with bottomless dread. "Liang Guodong is arrested? Already?"

"Cuff both of them," Han Zhiyuan ordered.

Two sharp metallic clicks echoed through the silent station. Steel bit into Cheng Biao's thick wrists and Zhou Quan's shaking hands in front of every patrolman and clerk. Zhou Quan sank half to his knees, sobbing that he wanted to turn state witness.

Han Zhiyuan turned to the pale duty clerk standing behind the key board.

"You," Han Zhiyuan said quietly. "Hand me the key to Holding Cell Two. Now."
