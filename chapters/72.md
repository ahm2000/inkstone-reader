# Chapter 72: Number One's Countermove

Cold autumn wind whispered through the black pines surrounding the eastern courtyard on the edge of Qingyan City.

Inside the timber study, beneath the low glow of the brass desk lamp, Number One's matte-black encrypted phone emitted a single crisp chime.

Number One reached for the device immediately. When he saw the unlabelled contact ID at the top of the screen, his spine straightened with quiet reverence. He unlocked the message and read the single calm line twice.

*Wanfeng Freight's smart-terminal contract routes port data to Zurich. Check Song Baisheng, Li Weiming, and Wu Zhenbang.*

Silence hung over the bare rosewood desk for three full seconds.

Slowly, Number One's eyes turned as cold as winter iron.

He had sworn after the earlier oversight in Yunxi County that the Black Pine Guard would never allow a single hidden snare to reach Chen Xuan's doorstep unchecked again. Yet Li Weiming and his provincial accomplices had dared to hand a rigged foreign intelligence contract directly across Chen Xuan's table at a provincial forum.

"They think hiding behind a deputy governor and an eight-point technical clause makes them invisible," Number One said softly, his voice carrying the chill of frosted steel.

Without wasting a single heartbeat, Number One pressed two keys on the secure console recessed into his desk, opening an encrypted multi-line link to Number Seven in Lanshui City and Number Eleven at the signals archive.

"Number Seven. Number Eleven," Number One spoke crisply. "Priority Red. You have two hours. Pull every shell registration, offshore wire, and server endpoint tied to Wanfeng Freight Group, Helvetia Transit Analytics in Zurich, Nordic-Pacific Capital, Song Baisheng, Li Weiming, and Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang. I want an ironclad, state-verifiable technical dossier before midnight."

"Understood, Number One," Number Eleven's calm voice answered through the speaker, already accompanied by the rapid, precise clicking of mechanical keys.

"On it," Number Seven replied evenly from his observation post two blocks from the Lanshui International Convention Hotel. "Number Nineteen already logged the twenty-eighth floor suite movements today. Cross-referencing Wu Zhenbang's family trusts now."

* * *

For the next two hours, the quiet timber study in Qingyan City became the silent eye of a precision storm.

Number Eleven tore through four layers of Cayman Islands and Liechtenstein corporate registries in forty minutes, exposing the true beneficial ownership of Helvetia Transit Analytics AG in Zurich. Beneath three nominee law firms sat the exact same clearing trust that had financed the Northern Ledger's vanished network in Yunxi County.

Even more damning, Number Eleven intercepted the public handshake certificates of Wanfeng Freight Group's pre-staging cloud servers at Lanshui East Port. The software architecture had already been compiled and tested in secret: automated mirroring scripts designed to strip domestic firewall tags and forward live rare-earth transit manifests, container weights, and coastal berth schedules directly to an encrypted Zurich IP block every sixty seconds.

At the ninety-minute mark, Number Seven uploaded the domestic financial trail onto Number One's terminal.

Song Baisheng's three-billion-yuan expansion bridge loan from Nordic-Pacific Capital carried an undisclosed zero-interest forgiveness clause triggered the moment Lanshui Province's coastal ports went live on the Wanfeng Smart-Terminal Cloud. Worse still, two weeks before the forum opened, eighteen million Singapore dollars had been wired from a Zurich sub-account into a maritime consulting shell registered in Singapore under the name of Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang's younger brother-in-law.

Every timestamp, bank swift code, server IP address, and twenty-eighth floor hotel keycard access log aligned with mathematical perfection.

Number One reviewed the compiled sixty-four-page technical dossier page by page under the brass lamp. His face remained utterly expressionless, yet a cold, razor-sharp satisfaction settled behind his gaze.

"Check the metadata," Number One ordered cleanly. "Strip every Black Pine Guard signature, every external trace, and verify zero mention of Hengxin Logistics, Qingyan City, or Chen Xuan."

"Verified clean," Number Eleven reported instantly. "The package reads strictly as a domestic port-cybersecurity and customs-compliance whistleblower audit. Any state forensic engineer can independently confirm the Zurich server handshake and the Singapore wire trail inside ten minutes."

"Good," Number One said decisively. "Execute dual routing now. Copy One goes directly to Vice Director Gao Zhengyi at the Lanshui Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection via his private encrypted intake channel. Copy Two goes straight to the Ministry of State Security's regional counterintelligence desk, tagged under their active Northern Ledger watchlist."

"Transmitting both packets now," Number Eleven replied. "Delivery confirmed. Relay nodes scrubbed."

* * *

Four hundred kilometers north, inside the quiet sixth-floor office of the Lanshui Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection, a single desk lamp still burned past midnight.

Vice Director Gao Zhengyi had been reviewing quarterly municipal compliance files when his private secure terminal flashed with an urgent, unlabelled priority alert.

Frowning slightly, Gao Zhengyi opened the encrypted attachment, expecting a routine anonymous tip about local procurement kickbacks.

Five minutes later, the teacup in Gao Zhengyi's hand was completely cold, and his forehead was damp with thin sweat.

He stared at the Zurich server handshake logs, the rare-earth manifest mirroring scripts buried inside Wanfeng Freight Group's Article 14, and the eighteen-million-dollar Singapore wire linked directly to Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang's family shell.

At that exact moment, the red direct-line telephone on the left corner of Gao Zhengyi's desk began to ring with a sharp, unmistakable tone.

Gao Zhengyi picked up the receiver immediately. "Gao Zhengyi speaking."

"Vice Director Gao Zhengyi," a crisp, steely voice answered from the Ministry of State Security's counterintelligence desk. "Our duty officers just verified a live foreign telemetry breach targeting Lanshui East Port and rare-earth logistics manifests, tied directly to the dual-passport Northern Ledger fixer Li Weiming. We have just received joint authorization from Beijing for an immediate morning enforcement sweep at the Lanshui International Convention Hotel."

Gao Zhengyi stood slowly behind his desk, his jaw hardening with grim resolve.

"I am looking at the exact same evidence right now," Gao Zhengyi said firmly. "Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang intends to sign an executive pilot decree for Wanfeng Freight Group at nine o'clock tomorrow morning. My inspection teams will be ready to move with your officers before the gavel even falls."

Back in Qingyan City's eastern courtyard, Number One stepped quietly onto the damp stone veranda and looked north through the dark, swaying pines toward the provincial capital.

"Sleep well, Chen Xuan," Number One murmured softly into the night wind. "Tomorrow morning at nine o'clock, Song Baisheng, Li Weiming, and Wu Zhenbang are going to learn what happens when you hand the wrong contract to an ordinary office worker."
