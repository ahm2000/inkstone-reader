# Chapter 72: Panic at Old Street Station

Inside Old Street Police Station, a ceiling tube buzzed over the peeling light-blue walls. A leaky radiator dripped near the front counter, leaving a dark puddle on the worn concrete floor.

Station Director Cheng Bo paced behind the reception desk like a caged animal. Every few seconds, he slammed the receiver down and hammered redial on the black desk phone.

"Still dead!" Cheng Bo snarled, cracking the plastic cradle with his palm. He spun toward Deputy Director Zhou Quan, veins bulging along his thick neck. "Eight calls to Chief Liang Guodong's office, six to his mobile, and not one rings through! What is wrong with the lines in this county?"

Zhou Quan stood near the frosted window, wiping sweaty palms on his trousers. Four patrol officers sat stiffly at their desks, pretending to sort citations while stealing nervous glances at their director.

"Director Cheng Bo, please lower your voice," Zhou Quan said tightly. "Chief Liang is in the Monday roll call at the county compound right now. Even if the line worked, he would not answer during a meeting."

"Then why did the land office clerk not call me back?" Cheng Bo snapped. "Hongrui Real Estate promised to transfer 200k into our account the moment that stubborn hardware shopkeeper signed his confession! I want that case wrapped up before noon!"

Before Zhou Quan could answer, the glass front door burst open. A clerk from the county archives stumbled inside, gasping for air, his face drained of color.

"Director Cheng Bo!" the clerk panted, grabbing the counter. "It is over! The provincial anti-corruption team just raided the county compound! Armed special police locked the whole building! They dragged Magistrate Sun Weiming and Chief Liang Guodong out in handcuffs!"

The buzzing ceiling light grew deafening in the sudden silence. A young officer dropped his pen onto the concrete floor.

Zhou Quan backed against the wall, lips trembling. "Provincial anti-corruption? Are you sure? Did they say why?"

"I heard an officer mention Old Street!" the clerk choked out. "They said something about a fabricated detention and Hongrui Real Estate!"

Cheng Bo's face turned the color of wet ash. His mind raced back to the man locked inside Holding Cell Two. Last night, Cheng Bo had altered the witness report himself, turning an honest shopkeeper into a violent suspect charged with assaulting city inspectors.

"Burn it!" Cheng Bo barked, shoving past Zhou Quan into his back office. "Lock the front door!"

Inside his office, Cheng Bo ripped open the steel cabinet and yanked out the tan folder marked with Chen Mingde's name. Shoving the forged witness statements into a tin trash bin, he fumbled in his pocket for a disposable lighter.

Wait, he thought wildly. There was still one lifeline.

With his free hand, Cheng Bo snatched his private mobile from the drawer and dialed a memorized provincial number. He had met Handong Public Security Director Qi Tongwei twice at Shanshui Group banquets, and Chief Liang had sworn Qi Tongwei kept loyal station chiefs protected.

Cheng Bo pressed the phone to his ear, praying for a ring.

Only a cold, flat tone answered. The call failed instantly. He tried again. Dead.

"Pick up, damn it!" Cheng Bo shouted at the blank screen. He tossed the phone aside, flicked the lighter wheel with a shaking thumb, and lowered the flame toward Chen Mingde's dossier.

Bang!

The wooden door blew inward, splintering off its brass latch.

"Drop the lighter! Hands on your head!"

Four provincial special police officers flooded the room. One kicked the tin bin out of reach while another twisted the lighter from Cheng Bo's grip and slammed him onto the desk.

Hou Liangping stepped over the broken doorframe and picked up the untouched tan folder. He checked the forged signature on the cover and looked down at Cheng Bo.

"Destroying evidence during a provincial investigation," Hou Liangping said quietly. "That just added five more years to your sentence, Director Cheng Bo."

"I was following orders!" Cheng Bo shrieked as steel cuffs bit into his wrists. "Chief Liang told me to lock him up! Hongrui Real Estate paid for it!"

Outside in the lobby, Zhou Quan was already on his knees, sobbing as officers cuffed him.

Three streets away, inside a quiet brick courtyard on Old Street, morning sunlight warmed a simple wooden table.

Steam curled from a pot of millet porridge and a plate of warm buns. Chen Xuan sat calmly in a plain gray sweater, peeling a boiled egg while his father, Chen Jianguo, and his mother, Liu Meifang, comforted his aunt, Wu Lanying.

Wu Lanying's eyes were swollen from crying all night. She stared anxiously toward the wooden gate. "Xuan, they took your Second Uncle in a police van last night. Hongrui Real Estate has the whole county in its pocket. What if they hurt him inside?"

Chen Xuan placed the peeled egg gently into his aunt's bowl and poured her warm soy milk. His voice was calm and completely ordinary.

"Eat something first, Aunt Lanying," Chen Xuan said with a reassuring smile. "The people who framed Second Uncle broke the law in broad daylight. I promise you, Second Uncle will walk through that front gate before lunch without a scratch."

Wu Lanying wiped her eyes, steadied by her nephew's quiet certainty, and picked up her spoon.

Outside the brick wall, parked beneath an old locust tree, sat an unmarked black sedan. Inside, Number 1 glanced at his silent encrypted tablet. A single green line confirmed that Old Street Police Station had been secured and the fabricated file recovered intact.

Number 1 switched off the screen and looked respectfully toward the quiet courtyard roof. Everything was moving smoothly for Young Master, without a speck of dust touching his morning meal.
