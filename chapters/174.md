# Chapter 174: The Four Bank Chiefs Arrive

Shen Wanshan finally felt the heat of the dropped cigarette burning through his silk sleeve. He brushed it off sharply, his hands shaking as he fought to pull back the towering dignity he had worn across Shenzhen for thirty years.

"Who the hell do you think you are?" Shen Wanshan shouted, slapping the rosewood table. "You broke into a private home on Shenzhen Bay with forty armed men! Do you know what my family represents in Guangdong?"

He stepped around his chair, glaring at Chen Xuan and Number 7.

"Shenhai Shipping employs twelve thousand people! Our ships, piers, and towers from Shekou to Futian CBD are worth 140 billion yuan! Every major bank in Shenzhen counts us as a top client! One phone call and half the city's leaders will be outside that gate!"

"Grandfather is right!" Shen Jingzhou yelled, hiding behind Shen Wanshan's shoulder. "Call the police! Call Chief Zhou at China Construction Bank!"

Chen Xuan took a quiet sip of tea and set his cup down.

"No need to call," Chen Xuan said calmly. "Look outside."

Through the glass doors facing the front courtyard, long beams of bright headlights swept across the palm trees and koi pond.

Four sleek black sedans rolled smoothly through the open iron gates, parking neatly behind the matte-white White Guard cars.

Shen Wanshan's eyes lit up with sudden, desperate relief.

"Aha!" Shen Wanshan laughed sharply, pointing toward the courtyard as the car doors opened. "Look who just arrived! That is Chief Zhou Mingyuan's car from China Construction Bank! And Chief He Zhengguo from Huaxia Bank! And Public Security is right behind them! You foolish clerk, your private guards just walked straight into a prison cell!"

Shen Jingzhou straightened his wine-red blazer and hurried eagerly behind his grandfather toward the dining hall entrance.

"Chief Zhou! Chief He! Chief Lin! Chief Xu!" Shen Wanshan called out warmly, hurrying forward with both hands outstretched as four middle-aged men in dark business suits stepped through the doorway. "Thank goodness you came so quickly! A gang of thugs just broke down my front gate and attacked my security staff!"

The four newcomers were indeed the four most powerful banking figures in Shenzhen.

Walking at the front were Zhou Mingyuan, Shenzhen Regional Chief of China Construction Bank, and He Zhengguo, Shenzhen Regional Chief of Huaxia Bank.

Behind the four bank chiefs walked two stone-faced officers in dark uniforms, followed by twelve investigators carrying thick court-sealed blue folders.

Shen Wanshan smiled eagerly, extending his hand toward Zhou Mingyuan, whom he had hosted for golf and tea more than twenty times over the past decade.

Zhou Mingyuan did not shake his hand.

He did not even look at Shen Wanshan's outstretched fingers.

All four regional bank chiefs walked straight past Shen Wanshan, past the bound Macau enforcers on the floor, and stopped three paces from the rosewood table where Chen Xuan sat drinking tea.

Together, the four Shenzhen chiefs of China's Four National Banks bowed their heads slightly in deep, unmistakable respect toward Chen Xuan.

"Good evening, Mister Chen," Zhou Mingyuan said quietly. "We came as soon as headquarters issued the notice."

"Thank you for coming out late on a Wednesday night," Chen Xuan replied politely, giving them an easy nod. "Please handle your business. Do not mind me."

Shen Wanshan froze in the middle of the floor, his outstretched hand hanging empty in the air. His jaw dropped as a cold wave washed down his back.

"Chief Zhou?" Shen Wanshan stammered, his voice cracking. "Chief Lin? Why are you bowing to a shipping clerk from Hengxin Logistics? Have you all lost your minds?"

Zhou Mingyuan finally turned around. There was zero warmth left in his eyes. He drew a thick red-stamped bank notice from his briefcase and held it squarely in front of Shen Wanshan's chest.

"Chairman Shen," Zhou Mingyuan said formally. "By emergency directive from China Construction Bank headquarters in Beijing, all credit lines, maritime mortgages, and commercial loans held by Shenhai Shipping and the Shen Family totaling 38 billion yuan are recalled immediately. Your corporate and personal accounts are frozen as of eight o'clock tonight."

Before Shen Wanshan could draw a breath, He Zhengguo of Huaxia Bank stepped forward with a matching notice.

"Huaxia Bank hereby terminates every open credit line and letter of credit issued to Shenhai Group and its subsidiaries," He Zhengguo stated crisply. "Your 24 billion yuan property financing across Shenzhen and Dongguan is called due effective immediately."

Lin Zhiqiang of ICBC stepped up third, his face grim. "ICBC has placed an immediate freeze on all thirty-two accounts tied to Shenhai Shipping, Shenzhen Bay Holdings, and every overseas clearing account linked to your Hong Kong shell firms."

Xu Wenbo of Bank of China stepped forward last, holding up a thick suspension notice. "Bank of China has frozen all foreign currency settlements, shipping guarantees, and Hong Kong trade accounts under the Shen Family name. Not a single container ship under your flag will clear settlement in Shenzhen, Hong Kong, or Shanghai tonight."

Every word fell like an iron hammer onto Shen Wanshan's chest.

In less than two minutes, the Four National Banks had cut off every drop of blood flowing through the Shen Family's 140 billion yuan empire.

"No!" Shen Wanshan staggered back a step, clutching his chest as his face turned gray. "You cannot recall 140 billion yuan worth of loans and credit in one night! We never missed an interest payment! On what legal grounds are you freezing my family's assets?"

From behind the four bank chiefs, Deputy Director Zhao Jianming stepped into the chandelier light beside Senior Inspector Ma Weidong.

Zhao Jianming held up a thick stack of court documents stamped with scarlet seals from the Guangdong High People's Court.

"On these grounds, Shen Wanshan," Zhao Jianming said in a voice cold as stone. "We hold court-signed asset-freeze warrants covering every vessel, pier lease, warehouse, commercial tower, bank account, and private villa belonging to the Shen Family and Shenhai Shipping, covering the full 140 billion yuan empire."

Senior Inspector Ma Weidong swept his eyes sharply over the twelve bound Macau enforcers, the black voice recorder on the table, and the unsigned contract lying in front of Qin Yuhan.

"And in addition to smuggling and financial fraud," Ma Weidong added crisply, "your men locked guests inside a private villa with illegal steel weapons to force a forged business transfer. Shen Wanshan, Shen Jingzhou, and Koo Tianming, nobody is walking out of this house tonight."

Across the table, Koo Tianming gripped the back of his chair until his knuckles turned bone-white. For the first time in his life, the polished fixer from Hong Kong had no smooth words left.

Qin Yuhan sat quietly beside Chen Xuan, watching the four Shenzhen chiefs of China's Four National Banks stand calmly behind the ordinary office worker in a plain white cotton shirt. Her heartbeat quickened, her bright eyes shining as every piece of the puzzle finally clicked into place.
