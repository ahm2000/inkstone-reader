# Chapter 174: The Four Bank Chiefs Arrive

When Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan stepped back into the dining hall, Shen Wanshan had managed to scrape together a shred of his old arrogance. His chief legal counsel, a nervous man in gold-rimmed glasses who had rushed over from the city center, was crouching beside his chair and whispering furiously.

"Keep your voice steady, Chairman," the lawyer murmured, wiping sweat from his forehead. "The smuggling charges on those three ships can be blamed on rogue fleet managers. As long as your corporate credit lines remain open, we can post security bonds, cover the customs penalties, and fight this in court for years. The Shen Family has over 3 billion yuan in active bank facilities."

Shen Wanshan swallowed hard, clinging to that lifeline like a drowning man grabbing a floating plank. He straightened his burgundy silk jacket and glared weakly at Director Zhou, who was reviewing the thick audit portfolio at the far end of the table.

"You hear that, Director Zhou?" Shen Wanshan rasped, trying to force strength back into his voice. "My company employs thousands of workers along the coast. You cannot crush a major enterprise overnight over three shipping containers! My credit standing with the national banks is spotless!"

Before Director Zhou could even look up from the ledgers, the crunch of heavy tires echoed outside the tall French windows. Four sleek black sedans rolled smoothly onto the gravel courtyard, their headlights sweeping across the marble floor before clicking off in unison.

A minute later, the broken double doors parted again. Four composed, sharply dressed executives in dark wool overcoats stepped inside, accompanied by their risk-control assistants carrying slim leather briefcases. The federal officers guarding the doorway stepped aside immediately after checking their credentials.

The moment Shen Wanshan saw the four faces walking toward the rosewood table, every drop of blood drained from his cheeks.

He knew every single one of them. Over the past decade, he had spent millions on banquets and charity auctions just to sit at the same table with them.

Leading the group was the regional president of ICBC, followed closely by the provincial risk chief of China Construction Bank, the southern branch head of Bank of China, and the regional credit director of Huaxia Bank.

All Four National Banks had arrived at Harbor Villa together on a chilly late November night.

"President Lin? Director Zhao?" Shen Wanshan stammered, half rising from his chair before a federal officer firmly pressed his shoulder back down. "Why are all four of you here? There has been a misunderstanding with customs, nothing that affects our loan schedules!"

None of the four bank chiefs offered a polite smile or shook his hand. They stopped directly across the rosewood table, their expressions as cool and unreadable as winter stone.

The regional president of ICBC opened his slim leather folder first and spoke in a crisp, measured tone.

"Chairman Shen, following a joint national risk alert and verified federal audit filings, ICBC is immediately suspending and recalling 900 million yuan in maritime fleet credit lines issued to Shen Group," he stated cleanly. "Our risk team has confirmed falsified vessel valuations and active federal seizure of your pledged hulls. Full repayment is due immediately under the emergency default clause."

Shen Wanshan's lawyer gasped, his gold-rimmed glasses slipping down his nose. "Wait, you cannot recall 900 million yuan without a thirty-day review period!"

"Fraud voids the review period," the provincial risk chief of China Construction Bank said coldly, stepping half a pace forward. "Construction Bank is simultaneously freezing 750 million yuan in coastal warehouse and terminal development loans. The collateral deeds submitted by Shen Group were double-pledged across two provinces. Our legal department has already filed a formal asset preservation order."

Shen Wanshan clutched the armrests of his chair, his knuckles turning white. He turned desperately toward the third executive.

"Bank of China handles all our overseas trade settlements!" Shen Wanshan pleaded, his voice cracking. "We have letters of credit clearing tomorrow morning!"

"Not anymore," the southern branch head of Bank of China replied evenly, glancing briefly at Kwok Wing-fai. "Bank of China has locked every cross-border settlement account and letter of credit linked to Shen Group and Mr. Kwok's Hong Kong intermediaries. Over 1.1 billion yuan in disguised freight transfers has been flagged as illicit capital flight. Every outbound channel is permanently shut."

Finally, the regional credit director of Huaxia Bank closed the circle with quiet precision. "Huaxia Bank is terminating your 450 million yuan revolving operating line effective immediately. In coordination with the federal task force, we have placed a joint freeze on all corporate cash accounts and on Harbor Villa itself."

In less than five minutes, 3.2 billion yuan in banking lifelines vanished into thin air. Not a single yuan of Shen Family money could move anywhere in the country.

Shen Wanshan's lawyer let his leather briefcase slip from his numb fingers. It hit the marble floor with a dull thud.

Across the table, Kwok Wing-fai's last shred of composure shattered completely. Realizing that his Hong Kong financiers were now locked out of mainland clearing channels and facing total ruin, he jumped halfway out of his seat and began shouting frantically.

"This was all Shen Wanshan's idea!" Kwok yelled, pointing a shaking finger at his former partner. "I was only hired as an external shipping consultant! The forged manifests, the double-pledged warehouses, the threat against President Qin tonight, all of it was his plan!"

"You miserable traitor!" Shen Wanshan roared back, lunging forward only to be restrained firmly by two Customs officers.

Off to the side, none of the four bank chiefs glanced toward Chen Xuan, nor did any of them know his name. They had simply acted the instant the national financial risk system flashed crimson with ironclad proof of Shen Group's fraud. After signing the joint freeze confirmations with Director Zhou, the four executives offered a brief, polite nod to the federal team and departed as cleanly as they had arrived.

Beside the window, Qin Yuhan stared at the chaotic scene in utter disbelief. She leaned close to Chen Xuan and whispered, "Xuan, I have been in business for years, and I have never seen all four national banks move together in the middle of the night like that. Not a single warning, not a single delay. It is like an invisible net dropped straight over the entire bay."

Chen Xuan took a slow sip from his lukewarm teacup and smiled mildly.

"When people cheat on their loans and smuggle goods at sea, the banks naturally run faster than anyone else to protect their money," Chen Xuan said calmly. "I think the show is almost over, Yuhan."
