# Chapter 144: One Cup of Tea

Two gray water birds skimmed across the glasslike Western Hills lake outside the pavilion as Chen Xuan and Elder Song sipped their warm Wuyi rock tea in the morning sunlight.

Ninety year old Elder Song set his white porcelain cup down carefully and rested both thin hands on the stone table.

"Before we speak another word, young friend," Elder Song said in his steady, raspy voice, "I want to make you a solemn promise on my ninety years of life. I will never ask for your surname, your given name, your workplace, or your family. And as long as I draw breath, not a single person in Beijing, not even Premier Gu Chengshan or my old comrade Elder Qin Zhengguo, will ever learn your name or what your face looks like. I will carry our meeting today straight into the earth with me."

Chen Xuan inclined his head respectfully. "Thank you, Elder Song. That means more to me than any title in the capital."

"Do not thank me," Elder Song replied with a warm smile, waving his weathered hand. "Keeping a secret for the young man who saved our country's roof is the easiest duty I have ever carried."

He turned his gaze briefly toward the quiet pine hills beyond the water.

"I was eighteen years old when I first carried a rifle into the northern mountains," Elder Song said softly. "Our cloth shoes had holes in the soles, so we stuffed dry grass inside to keep our toes from freezing off in the snow. Half the boys in my squad never lived to see twenty. When we buried them under the pines, we swore we would build a peaceful home where their younger brothers and sisters would never go hungry or get trampled by bullies."

Elder Song tapped his thin fingers gently against the stone table.

"For seventy years, we laid railways, built schools, and watched electric lights come on in every mountain village," Elder Song continued. "I thought the dangerous days were behind us. I was wrong. Bullets are loud, so you know where the enemy is standing. Greed is quiet. It wears an expensive suit, smiles at dinner tables, and hollows out the national banks from the inside while ordinary families work overtime to pay their bills."

Chen Xuan listened quietly, his hands resting beside his warm teacup.

"Three months ago, when I saw the secret treasury reports, my heart turned cold," Elder Song admitted quietly. "Those corrupt cliques across the forty ministries had hollowed out chains of debt and offshore shell accounts so deeply that cutting them out all at once risked causing a massive bank panic. Yet leaving them alone would have poisoned the whole country."

Elder Song looked straight into Chen Xuan's calm eyes, his weathered face filled with deep emotion.

"Then you produced the Black Order and stepped in," Elder Song said, his raspy voice trembling slightly with gratitude. "You handed Gao Zhengyi and Zhou Zhenhua ironclad ledgers that pinned down every crooked official without touching a single innocent clerk. And when the banking system needed an anchor, you gave 300,000,000,000,000 yuan of clean, legal money to the country completely free."

Elder Song leaned forward and placed his right hand flat over his heart.

"A full 300,000,000,000,000 yuan, given freely as a legal gift to the state treasury and our national reserves across China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank," Elder Song whispered in pure awe. "Not one fen of interest demanded. Not one board seat or political favor asked in return. You filled every gap, steadied the currency, and turned our country's foundation into solid iron overnight while millions of ordinary families slept soundly in their beds."

Chen Xuan picked up his scratched green thermos, poured a little warm jasmine tea into his cup to cool the rock tea, and smiled gently.

"Money sitting in vaults is just numbers on a screen, Elder Song," Chen Xuan said plainly. "If the streets are not peaceful and regular people cannot buy groceries with a calm mind, having all the gold in the world is useless. Putting that 300,000,000,000,000 yuan into the country's foundation just means my family and everyone else's family can walk down a safe sidewalk."

Elder Song stared at him for a moment before letting out a long breath of wonder.

"Premier Gu Chengshan and the Finance Ministry finished the first round of allocations yesterday afternoon," Elder Song told him proudly. "With your 300,000,000,000,000 yuan gift anchoring the national reserve and over 820,000,000,000 yuan recovered directly from the corrupt cliques, the state signed twelve new national public decrees last night. Every rural hospital in the country is getting full modern medical equipment for free. Every school meal and tuition fee in poor counties is covered. And pension funds for every retired worker, teacher, and old soldier have been doubled."

Elder Song stood up slowly from his stone stool, straightened his ninety year old back as tall as a pine tree, and bowed his silver head deeply toward Chen Xuan.

"On behalf of those eighteen year old boys we left in the snow seventy years ago, and on behalf of every ordinary family in this country today, thank you, young friend."

Chen Xuan immediately rose and caught the old man gently by the elbows, helping him sit back down on the warm cushion.

"Please do not bow to me, Elder Song," Chen Xuan said warmly. "You and your comrades bled in the snow to build the house. I only paid for a little roof repair."

"A little roof repair!" Elder Song laughed through a mist of bright moisture in his eyes, patting Chen Xuan's forearm affectionately. "Only you would call 300,000,000,000,000 yuan a little roof repair! Tell me honestly, young man, is there truly nothing I can give you? A secret pass? A special telephone line?"

"Nothing at all," Chen Xuan smiled, sitting back down and patting his green thermos. "Just keep my secret from Premier Gu Chengshan and Elder Qin Zhengguo so nobody ever comes knocking on my apartment door in Shanghai. I have an easy 12,000 yuan job where I leave at 4:55 on Fridays, and my wife thinks I am counting wooden crates at a suburban warehouse right now."

Elder Song burst into hearty laughter, wiping the corners of his eyes with a cotton handkerchief.

"Counting wooden crates at a warehouse!" Elder Song chuckled delightedly. "Your wife must be a wonderful girl to keep a dragon like you happily boiling tea in the kitchen! Very well, young friend. I swear on my life: neither Gu Chengshan, nor Qin Zhengguo, nor a single soul in Beijing will ever hear a whisper of who you are from my lips!"
