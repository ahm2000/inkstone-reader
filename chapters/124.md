# Chapter 124: Invitation to Shanshui Villa

The next morning, the kitchen of Emerald Riverside Villa smelled richly of slow-simmered soy sauce, ginger, and star anise. Chen Xuan was packing two insulated lunch boxes with tender soy-braised beef, crisp garlic bok choy, and fluffy white rice when Qin Yuhan walked in holding a crisp red-bordered envelope.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said with a bright smile, setting the official invitation card beside his green thermos on the kitchen counter. "Do you remember last week when we talked about taking a short trip back to Handong Province to visit Second Uncle Chen Mingde and Second Aunt on Old Street?"

"Of course," Chen Xuan nodded, snapping the lid shut on her warm lunch box. "Second Uncle called a few days ago to say the sweet osmanthus trees along Old Street in Yunxi County are in full bloom. He said the whole block smells like honey right now."

"Then look at this," Qin Yuhan said happily, tapping the red-bordered card. "Haiyue Group just received an official invitation from the Handong Provincial Government and Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang. Tomorrow afternoon, they are hosting the Handong Regional Shipping and Logistics Forum at Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City. They invited Haiyue Group as a key corporate guest to discuss the new inland port corridor."

Chen Xuan picked up the thick card and glanced at the raised gold lettering. "Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou. That is less than an hour's drive from Yunxi County. Once your meetings wrap up, we can head straight over to Old Street without wasting half a day on the road."

"Exactly," Qin Yuhan said, her eyes sparkling. "The forum finishes by Saturday afternoon. Why do you not ask Supervisor Hu for Friday off and come with me on tomorrow morning's high-speed train? After Saturday's sessions end, we can drive to Yunxi County, have dinner with Second Uncle and Second Aunt, and walk under the osmanthus trees."

Before Chen Xuan could answer, his phone buzzed twice on the kitchen counter. He picked it up and opened a new message from Supervisor Hu Dewei in the Hengxin Logistics group chat.

Chen Xuan read the text and let out a quiet chuckle.

"What is so funny?" Qin Yuhan asked curiously, leaning over his shoulder.

"I do not even need to ask for leave," Chen Xuan said, tilting the screen toward her. "General Manager Sun Haoran somehow pulled strings to get Hengxin Logistics an observer pass for the exact same Shanshui Villa forum. Supervisor Hu just ordered me to board tomorrow morning's high-speed train G7128 with them so I can carry three heavy route binders and take meeting notes."

Qin Yuhan blinked in surprise, then burst out laughing. "G7128? That is the exact same train Lin Wei booked for me! Wait, what carriage did your company put you in?"

"Car 5, second class, window seat," Chen Xuan said with a mock sigh. "While President Qin will be relaxing in the wide leather seats of Business Class Car 1."

"Poor clerk," Qin Yuhan teased, smoothing his shirt collar with gentle fingers. "Do not worry. Once the train leaves Shanghai Hongqiao Station, I will bring you snacks from the business-class lounge so you do not starve while guarding your manager's binders."

"Now that is a wife worth marrying," Chen Xuan grinned, handing her the insulated lunch box. "Just remember to eat the soy-braised beef while it is still warm, and do not skip lunch again if your directors start talking in circles."

Qin Yuhan smiled, tucked the warm lunch box into her tote bag, and headed out for Haiyue Tower.

At Hengxin Logistics, Thursday passed in a flurry of loud, restless excitement. General Manager Sun Haoran paced through the office twice an hour, lecturing everyone about how tomorrow's Handong Shipping Forum at Shanshui Villa could be the biggest turning point in the company's history.

"Remember, Chen Xuan," Supervisor Hu Dewei warned him in the late afternoon, stacking three thick brown binders onto Chen Xuan's desk. "We are lucky to get an outer observer seat in the same hall as Secretary Li Dakang and major firms like Haiyue Group. Tomorrow on the train and at the villa, keep your mouth shut, carry the folders neatly, and do not embarrass General Manager Sun."

"Understood, Supervisor Hu," Chen Xuan answered politely.

At 4:55 PM on the dot, Chen Xuan capped his green thermos, tucked the three brown binders under his left arm, and walked out of the elevator right on schedule.

Before heading home to cook dinner, he took a slow stroll along the quiet Pudong riverside walkway. Golden maple leaves drifted across the stone path in the cool autumn breeze. Inside his coat pocket, his phone vibrated softly.

Chen Xuan pulled it out as he walked beside the railing. A brief encrypted report from Number 1 glowed on the screen.

"Young Master, the area around Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City has been checked. Secretary Li Dakang is leading the provincial shipping forum honestly, and Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping is keeping an eye on local finances. However, Jingzhou Vice Mayor Ding Yizhen has been quietly pressuring smaller visiting freight firms to sign inflated port service leases during the forum. Should we intervene before your arrival?"

Chen Xuan paused for a moment to watch a tugboat push a barge smoothly down the river. His expression remained calm and unhurried.

"No need to make a scene ahead of time," Chen Xuan typed back. "Keep watch quietly. I am just going back to Handong to accompany my wife and visit my Second Uncle in Yunxi."

Number 1 replied instantly. "Understood, Young Master. Everything will remain quiet until you say otherwise."

Chen Xuan slipped his phone back into his pocket, shifted the three heavy brown binders under his arm, and walked home toward the warm lights of Emerald Riverside Villa.
