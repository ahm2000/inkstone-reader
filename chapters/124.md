# Chapter 124: Invitation to Shanshui Villa

On Thursday morning at Emerald Riverside Villa, Chen Xuan was packing two insulated lunch boxes with soy-braised beef and stir-fried seasonal greens when Qin Yuhan walked into the kitchen holding an official red-bordered invitation letter.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan smiled, setting the red invitation card beside his green enamel thermos on the counter. "Do you remember how we talked about visiting Jingzhou City in Handong Province to see your Second Uncle Chen Mingde on Old Street?"

"Of course," Chen Xuan nodded, snapping the lid shut on her warm lunch box. "Second Uncle called last week to say the sweet osmanthus trees along the river in Jingzhou City are in full bloom."

"Then look at this," Qin Yuhan said happily, tapping the red-bordered letter. "This morning, Haiyue Group received an official invitation from the Handong Provincial Government and Mayor Li Dakang of Jingzhou City. Tomorrow morning, they are hosting the Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum in Jingzhou City to connect Yangtze River Delta shipping firms with local inland freight hubs. I have to take the nine o'clock high-speed train to Jingzhou City tomorrow morning for the weekend conference!"

Chen Xuan glanced at the venue printed in gold letters on the invitation card: the State Convention Hall at Shanshui Villa, Jingzhou City, Handong Province.

A light smile touched Chen Xuan's lips.

Just a few weeks ago, Shanshui Villa and Shanshui Group had been the corrupt playground of the Handa Faction, Ding Yizhen, and Cai Chenggong during the Guangming Peak redevelopment scandal. After Chen Xuan quietly authorized Number 1 of the White Order alongside Elder Song and Premier Gu Chengshan in Beijing, Provincial Secretary Sha Ruijin, Li Dakang, Hou Liangping, and Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming had swept the entire corrupt network clean and recovered the stolen state funds through China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China.

Now, the confiscated Shanshui Villa had been officially converted by Sha Ruijin and Li Dakang into a clean, public state conference center for economic development!

"If you are going to Jingzhou City tomorrow morning, do not forget to bring me back a box of fresh osmanthus cakes," Chen Xuan teased, handing Qin Yuhan her warm lunch bag. "I still have to clock in at Hengxin Logistics tomorrow."

"I wish you could come with me on the train," Qin Yuhan sighed softly, kissing his cheek before picking up her briefcase for Haiyue Tower. "It is only a three-day weekend forum, and the mountain air around Shanshui Villa is wonderful in autumn."

An hour later, at nine-fifteen, Chen Xuan strolled into the third-floor office of Hengxin Logistics near Lujiazui, watered the small pot of lucky bamboo on his sunny windowsill, and unscrewed the lid of his green enamel thermos.

Right on cue, the glass door of the manager's office swung open. Department Manager Hu Dewei walked out holding a stack of thick brown shipping binders and a red-stamped notice from the Shanghai Logistics Association. Behind him stood General Manager Sun Haoran, wearing a new gray suit and looking very pleased with himself.

"Everyone, listen up for a minute!" General Manager Sun Haoran announced loudly, clapping his hands twice. "Because Hengxin Logistics passed the third-quarter port inspection without a single delay, the Shanghai Logistics Association selected our company as one of the thirty supporting freight enterprises invited to the Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum in Jingzhou City tomorrow morning!"

Several young clerks clapped politely around the office.

"As General Manager, I will naturally represent Hengxin Logistics at the main reception at Shanshui Villa tomorrow evening," Sun Haoran said proudly, lifting his chin. "However, the forum also requires one junior clerk from each shipping company to sit at the freight data registration desk for two hours on Friday afternoon and carry three binders of container route tables on the high-speed train."

Sun Haoran turned to Manager Hu Dewei. "Manager Hu, which clerk from your department is going with me on the nine o'clock train to Jingzhou City tomorrow?"

Manager Hu Dewei rubbed his plump chin, his small eyes darting immediately toward the sunny corner desk where Chen Xuan was peacefully blowing on his hot Yunnan red tea.

Ever since Chen Xuan had returned from his weekend trip to Beijing, Hu Dewei had been annoyed by how comfortable Chen Xuan looked every single day. Carrying three heavy binders onto a high-speed train on a Friday and sitting at a registration booth sounded like the most boring chore in the entire company!

"Chen Xuan!" Hu Dewei called out, walking over and dropping the three thick brown binders and a second-class train voucher onto Chen Xuan's desk with a loud thud. "You are the only clerk in our office who lived in Jingzhou City before moving to Shanghai! Tomorrow morning at nine o'clock, you will accompany General Manager Sun on the high-speed train G7128 to Jingzhou City and carry these route binders to Shanshui Villa!"

Across the aisle, two younger clerks looked at Chen Xuan with sympathy.

Chen Xuan looked down at the printed train voucher on his desk: Train G7128, departing Shanghai Hongqiao Station at nine o'clock sharp on Friday morning.

That was the exact same high-speed train Qin Yuhan's secretary had booked for her this morning!

"Hold on, Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said, putting on a reluctant face so Hu Dewei would not change his mind. "Carrying three binders all the way to Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City for the whole weekend? Does the company reimburse my train snacks and give me Monday morning off?"

"You get a two-hundred-yuan travel meal subsidy, your hotel room at the Shanshui Villa annex is pre-paid by the forum, and no, you cannot turn it down!" Hu Dewei snapped smugly. "It is settled! Your train ticket is already printed under your ID card!"

"Well, since Manager Hu insists so strongly, I suppose I have no choice but to sacrifice my weekend for the company," Chen Xuan sighed outwardly, while a bright grin danced in his eyes as he picked up his green enamel thermos.

A free ticket on the exact same train as his wife, a paid weekend stay at Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City, and two hundred yuan of free snack money from Hu Dewei?

Taking a slow sip of his fragrant Yunnan red tea, Chen Xuan pulled out his phone under the desk and sent a single cheerful text message to Qin Yuhan: *President Qin, save me a piece of osmanthus cake on the nine o'clock train tomorrow.*
