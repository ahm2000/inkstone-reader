# Chapter 124: Invitation to Shanshui Villa

The next morning, the kitchen of Emerald Riverside Villa smelled of slow-simmered soy sauce and star anise. Chen Xuan was packing two insulated lunch boxes with tender soy-braised beef, garlic bok choy, and fluffy white rice when Qin Yuhan walked in holding a crisp red-bordered envelope.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said with a bright smile, setting the official invitation card beside his dented green metal thermos on the marble counter. "Do you remember last week when we talked about taking a short trip to Jingzhou City in Handong Province to visit your Second Uncle Chen Mingde on Old Street?"

"Of course," Chen Xuan nodded, snapping the lid shut on her warm lunch box. "Second Uncle called a few days ago to say the sweet osmanthus trees along the river in Jingzhou are in full bloom. The whole street smells like honey right now."

"Then look at this," Qin Yuhan said happily, tapping the red-bordered card. "Haiyue Group just received an official invitation from the Handong Provincial Government and Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang. Tomorrow afternoon, they are hosting the Handong Regional Shipping and Logistics Forum at Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City. They invited Haiyue Group as a key corporate guest to discuss the new inland port corridor."

Chen Xuan picked up the crisp card and glanced at the raised gold lettering. "Shanshui Villa. That is right across the river from Old Street. When I was young, Second Uncle used to take me fishing along the stone bank not far from there whenever the autumn breeze cooled down."

"Exactly," Qin Yuhan said, her eyes sparkling. "The forum wraps up by Saturday afternoon. Why do you not ask Supervisor Hu for Friday off and come with me on tomorrow morning's high-speed train? After my meetings finish, we can spend the weekend visiting Second Uncle Chen Mingde and walking along the river under the osmanthus trees."

Before Chen Xuan could answer, his regular work phone buzzed twice on the counter. He picked it up and opened a message from Supervisor Hu Dewei in the Hengxin Logistics group chat.

Chen Xuan read the message and let out a quiet chuckle.

"What is so funny?" Qin Yuhan asked curiously, leaning over his shoulder.

"I do not even need to ask for leave," Chen Xuan said, showing her the screen. "General Manager Sun Haoran somehow pulled strings to get Hengxin Logistics an observer pass for the exact same Shanshui Villa forum. Supervisor Hu just ordered me to board tomorrow morning's high-speed train G7128 with them so I can carry three heavy route binders and take meeting notes."

Qin Yuhan blinked in surprise, then burst out laughing. "G7128? That is the exact same train my assistant booked for me! Wait, what carriage did your company put you in?"

"Car 5, second class, window seat," Chen Xuan said with a mock sigh. "While President Qin will be relaxing in the plush leather seats of Business Class Car 1."

"Poor clerk," Qin Yuhan teased, adjusting his shirt collar with gentle fingers. "Do not worry. Once the train leaves Shanghai Hongqiao Station, I will bring you snacks from the business-class lounge so you do not starve while carrying your manager's binders."

"Now that is a wife worth marrying," Chen Xuan grinned, handing her the insulated lunch box. "Just remember to eat the soy-braised beef while it is still warm, and do not skip lunch again if your board members start talking in circles."

Qin Yuhan smiled, tucked the warm lunch box into her tote bag, and headed out for the 38th floor of Haiyue Tower.

At the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics, Thursday passed in a flurry of pointless excitement. General Manager Sun Haoran paced through the office twice an hour, lecturing everyone about how tomorrow's Handong Shipping Forum at Shanshui Villa could be the biggest break in the company's history.

"Remember, Chen Xuan," Supervisor Hu Dewei warned him in the late afternoon, stacking three thick brown binders onto Chen Xuan's desk. "We are lucky to get an outer observer seat in the same hall as Secretary Li Dakang and major firms like Haiyue Group. Tomorrow on the train and at the villa, keep your mouth shut, carry the folders neatly, and do not embarrass General Manager Sun."

"Understood, Supervisor Hu," Chen Xuan answered politely.

At 4:55 PM on the dot, Chen Xuan capped his dented green metal thermos, tucked the three brown binders under his left arm, and walked out of the 12th floor elevator right on time.

Before heading home to cook dinner, he took a slow stroll along the quiet riverside walkway near Pudong. Golden maple leaves drifted across the stone path in the cool autumn wind. Beneath his coat, his matte-phone vibrated softly.

Chen Xuan pulled it out as he walked beside the railing. A brief, encrypted report from Number 1 glowed on the screen.

"Young Master, the perimeter around Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City has been checked. Secretary Li Dakang is leading the provincial shipping forum honestly, and Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping is monitoring local finances. However, Jingzhou Vice Mayor Ding Yizhen has been quietly pressuring smaller visiting freight firms to sign inflated port service leases during the forum. Should we intervene before your arrival?"

Chen Xuan paused for a second to watch a tugboat push a barge smoothly down the river. His expression stayed calm and unhurried.

"No need to make a scene ahead of time," Chen Xuan typed back. "Keep watch quietly. Do not disturb the osmanthus blossoms on Old Street. I am just going home to visit my Second Uncle and accompany my wife."

Number 1 replied instantly. "Understood, Young Master. Everything will remain quiet until you say otherwise."

Chen Xuan slipped his phone back into his pocket, shifted the three heavy brown binders under his arm, and walked home toward the warm lights of Emerald Riverside Villa.
