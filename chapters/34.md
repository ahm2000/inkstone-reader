# Chapter 34: The Pier Four Interception

At Pier Four along the Yunxi riverfront, the low diesel rumble of a flat-hulled cargo barge idled against the concrete quay while six dock hands hurried iron-strapped wooden crates up a steel gangplank.

Beside the stern bollard, Yan Tiehu chewed on an unlit cigarette, his thick jaw working angrily as he stared at the dead walkie-talkie in his palm. Facing him stood Qian Weiguo, Shengyuan Capital's regional logistics handler, wearing a dark tailored coat over a crisp shirt now rumpled with sweat.

"Cast off right now, Yan Tiehu," Qian Weiguo hissed, grabbing Yan Tiehu's sleeve with cold, impatient fingers. "Leave the last three crates on the truck and tell the captain to cut the lines!"

Yan Tiehu jerked his arm free, spitting the ruined cigarette onto the wet concrete. "Are you out of your mind, Qian Weiguo? Those last three crates hold the refined dual-use rare-earth alloy ingots! If I leave half a ton of Shengyuan Capital's highest-grade cargo sitting on Pier Four, Chairman Deng Yongchang will break both of my legs before sunrise!"

"You will not have legs left to break if we are still tied to this quay in five minutes!" Qian Weiguo snapped, shoving his own dead satellite phone an inch from Yan Tiehu's face. "Look at the screen! Our shortwave radio died at 00:15! Our cellular phones died at 00:30! Even the riverfront navigation beacon across the channel just went black! That is not a storm, Yan Tiehu, that is a sealed perimeter!"

Yan Tiehu's eyes narrowed, though a cold bead of sweat slid down behind his ear. "A sealed perimeter by who? County Magistrate Sun Weiming takes our money! Chief Liang Guodong eats at Chairman Deng Yongchang's table! Even if some river patrol boat wanders down here, one phone call from Hongrui Plaza sends them back to the dock!"

"With what phone?" Qian Weiguo hissed, his voice rising into a sharp, panicked rasp. "Try calling Hongrui Plaza right now! Try calling Director Cheng Biao at Old Street Station! Nobody is answering because nobody in Yunxi can transmit!"

Near the gangplank, one of Yan Tiehu's senior dock hands, Guo Liang, halted beside a forklift and wiped his mouth nervously. His hand shifted inside his quilted jacket toward the grip of a compact pistol. "Yan Tiehu, the lookout we posted at the pier entrance stopped answering five minutes ago. Should I send two men up the ramp to check?"

"Do not waste time checking!" Yan Tiehu barked, his voice thick with forced bravado as he slapped the side of the nearest wooden crate. "Load those last three boxes onto the deck in ninety seconds! Once we clear the county bend, Qian Weiguo can call his provincial contacts from the downriver tower and laugh at himself for panicking!"

Qian Weiguo stared at him, his lips curling in bitter contempt and rising terror. "You illiterate street thug! You do not even understand what is inside those lead-lined crates! If customs or state security catches us with unmanifested rare-earth alloys bound for an offshore transfer barge, neither Deng Yongchang nor Shengyuan Capital will save a single neck on this pier!"

"Keep your mouth shut about what is inside the crates," Yan Tiehu snarled, stepping forward with his fists clenched. "Get on the barge or swim!"

Before Qian Weiguo could answer, the diesel engine of the cargo barge coughed once and died cleanly.

The sudden, absolute silence hit the quay like a physical blow. Only the dark river lapped softly against the steel hull.

Yan Tiehu froze, his head snapping toward the wheelhouse. "Captain! What the hell did you just kill the engine for?"

No voice answered from the dark cabin.

Beside the forklift, Guo Liang spun around, his eyes widening as he saw dark shapes rising effortlessly over both the bow rail from the black river and the concrete wall along the pier approach. "Yan Tiehu!" Guo Liang yelled, dragging his pistol clear of his jacket. "We have company!"

He never even raised the barrel to waist level.

A silhouette clad head to boot in featureless matte-black gear closed four paces in total silence. A gloved palm deflected Guo Liang's wrist upward, a knee drove cleanly into the back of his thigh, and the pistol vanished from his fingers before his elbow even struck the concrete. Not a single gunshot sounded. No warning shout echoed across the water.

Across the barge deck and the loading quay, twelve more figures in seamless matte-black gear and expressionless full masks moved in perfect, wordless synchronization. Within eight seconds, all six dock hands lay face-down on the steel plating and concrete apron, their wrists bound behind their backs without a single broken bone or spilled drop of blood.

Qian Weiguo's clipboard slipped from his stiff fingers and clattered loudly across the planks. His knees went weak, and he staggered backward against a stack of rare-earth crates, his face drained completely white. "Do not shoot," Qian Weiguo gasped, raising both trembling hands high above his shoulders. "I am not armed! I am only a logistics coordinator!"

Yan Tiehu backed up two steps, his chest heaving, wild panic colliding with twenty years of street arrogance. His right hand twitched toward the heavy wrench tucked behind his belt. "Who the hell are you people?" Yan Tiehu shouted, his voice shaking despite the volume. "Do you know whose pier this is? This cargo belongs to Chairman Deng Yongchang and Shengyuan Capital! Chief Liang Guodong and County Magistrate Sun Weiming both know about tonight's shipment! Put your hands on me and you will not survive till noon!"

The nearest masked figure did not slow down by a fraction of a second.

Yan Tiehu swung his hand toward his belt, and his wrist vanished into an iron grip that felt like cold machined steel. One effortless twist spun him off his boots, stripped the wrench from his waistband, and forced him straight down onto both knees on the damp concrete. The impact knocked the breath cleanly out of his lungs.

Yan Tiehu gasped, staring up in trembling disbelief at the seamless, featureless dark visor looming over him. There was no badge on the chest, no flashlight glaring in his eyes, and no human anger in the posture, only an utterly calm, mechanical precision that chilled him to the marrow.

Another matte-black figure stepped smoothly beside the wooden crates, ran a narrow dark scanner across the lead-lined seals, and raised a blank obsidian slate.

For a heartbeat, Pier Four was silent save for Qian Weiguo's ragged wheezing.

Then a voice spoke from the masked figure standing directly before Yan Tiehu. It was completely flat, neutral, and genderless, chilled as dry ice sliding across bare steel, carrying neither contempt nor mercy.

"Target Yan Tiehu confirmed. Target Qian Weiguo confirmed. Strategic dual-use rare-earth alloy cargo seized and sealed intact. Detention logged for formal counter-espionage and anti-gang handover."

Yan Tiehu's bluster collapsed completely. His shoulders sagged, his mouth hung half-open, and a cold shudder ran straight down his spine as he realized that nobody in Yunxi, nobody in Hongrui Plaza, and nobody in the province was coming to answer his call.

"I will talk," Yan Tiehu choked out in a thin, terrified whisper, his forehead dropping nearly to the concrete. "I know where Deng Yongchang keeps the shipping manifests. I know every name."

The masked figure did not answer, did not look down again, and did not shift a millimeter. It simply tapped the obsidian slate once, sealing the pier into cold, orderly silence until the lawful handover vans arrived at dawn.
