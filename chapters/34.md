# Chapter 34: The Pier Four Interception

Down at Pier Four along the Yunxi riverfront, a flat cargo boat sat idling against the concrete dock while six dock workers hurried heavy wooden crates up a steel ramp.

Beside the heavy metal dock post, Yan Tiehu chewed on an unlit cigarette. His thick jaw worked angrily as he stared at the dead walkie-talkie in his palm. Facing him stood Qian Weiguo, Shengyuan Capital's regional shipping manager, wearing a dark coat over a crisp shirt now wrinkled and damp with sweat.

"Untie the ropes right now, Yan Tiehu," Qian Weiguo hissed, grabbing Yan Tiehu's sleeve with cold, impatient fingers. "Leave the last three crates on the truck and tell the captain to move the boat out!"

Yan Tiehu jerked his arm free and spat the chewed cigarette onto the wet concrete. "Are you out of your mind, Qian Weiguo? Those last three crates hold the refined rare-earth metal bars! If I leave half a ton of Shengyuan Capital's top-grade cargo sitting on Pier Four, Chairman Deng Yongchang will break both of my legs before sunrise!"

"You will not have legs left to break if we are still tied to this dock in five minutes!" Qian Weiguo snapped. He shoved his own dead satellite phone an inch from Yan Tiehu's nose. "Look at the screen! Our dock radio died at 00:15! Our cell phones died at 00:30! Even the river warning light across the water just went black! That is not a storm, Yan Tiehu. That is a total lockdown!"

Yan Tiehu's eyes narrowed, though a cold drop of sweat rolled down behind his ear. "A lockdown by who? County Magistrate Sun Weiming takes our money! Chief Liang Guodong eats at Chairman Deng Yongchang's table! Even if some river patrol boat wanders down here, one phone call from Hongrui Plaza sends them straight back to base!"

"With what phone?" Qian Weiguo hissed, his voice rising into a sharp, panicked rasp. "Try calling Hongrui Plaza right now! Try calling Director Cheng Biao at Old Street Station! Nobody is answering because nobody in Yunxi can send a call out!"

Near the steel ramp, one of Yan Tiehu's senior dock hands, Guo Liang, stopped beside a forklift and wiped his mouth nervously. His hand slipped inside his padded jacket toward the grip of a small pistol. "Yan Tiehu, the lookout we put at the pier gate stopped answering five minutes ago. Should I send two men up the road to check?"

"Do not waste time checking!" Yan Tiehu barked, slapping the side of the nearest wooden crate to hide his nerves. "Load those last three boxes onto the deck in ninety seconds! Once we pass the county bend, Qian Weiguo can call his province contacts from the next town's cell tower and laugh at himself for panicking!"

Qian Weiguo stared at him, his lips curling in bitter anger and growing fear. "You dumb street thug! You do not even understand what is inside those metal-lined crates! If customs or state security catches us with unlisted rare-earth metal headed for an overseas ship, neither Deng Yongchang nor Shengyuan Capital will save a single neck on this pier!"

"Keep your mouth shut about what is inside the crates," Yan Tiehu snarled, stepping forward with his fists clenched. "Get on the boat or swim!"

Before Qian Weiguo could answer, the boat's diesel engine coughed once and died cleanly.

The sudden, complete silence hit the dock like a slap. Only the dark river lapped softly against the steel hull.

Yan Tiehu froze and whipped his head toward the steering cabin. "Captain! What the hell did you kill the engine for?"

No voice answered from the dark cabin.

Beside the forklift, Guo Liang spun around. His eyes went wide as dark shapes climbed smoothly over the boat's front rail from the black river and over the concrete wall along the pier road at the exact same moment. "Yan Tiehu!" Guo Liang yelled, dragging his pistol clear of his jacket. "We have company!"

He never even got the barrel up to his waist.

A figure dressed head to boot in plain black gear crossed four paces in total silence. A gloved palm knocked Guo Liang's wrist upward, a knee drove cleanly into the back of his leg, and the gun vanished from his fingers before his elbow even hit the concrete. Not a single shot rang out. No warning shout echoed across the water.

Across the boat deck and the concrete dock, twelve more figures in plain black gear and smooth full masks moved in perfect, silent order. Within eight seconds, all six dock workers lay face-down on the steel deck and wet concrete, their wrists tied behind their backs without a single broken bone or spilled drop of blood.

Qian Weiguo's clipboard slipped from his stiff fingers and clattered loudly across the wooden planks. His knees went weak, and he staggered back against a stack of wooden crates, his face pale as chalk. "Do not shoot," Qian Weiguo gasped, throwing both shaking hands high above his shoulders. "I am not armed! I am only a shipping manager!"

Yan Tiehu backed up two steps, his chest heaving as wild fear clashed with twenty years of street arrogance. His right hand twitched toward the heavy iron wrench tucked behind his belt. "Who the hell are you people?" Yan Tiehu shouted, his voice shaking even as he yelled. "Do you know whose pier this is? This cargo belongs to Chairman Deng Yongchang and Shengyuan Capital! Chief Liang Guodong and County Magistrate Sun Weiming both know about tonight's shipment! Put your hands on me and you will not live till noon!"

The nearest masked figure did not slow down by even half a step.

Yan Tiehu swung his hand toward his belt, and his wrist disappeared into an iron grip that felt like cold steel. One smooth twist lifted him off his boots, pulled the wrench from his belt, and drove him straight down onto both knees on the damp concrete. The hard landing knocked the breath right out of his lungs.

Yan Tiehu gasped, staring up in shaking disbelief at the smooth, blank dark visor towering over him. There was no badge on the chest, no flashlight shining in his eyes, and no anger in the stance. There was only a calm, cold precision that chilled him straight to the bone.

Another black-clad figure stepped smoothly beside the wooden crates, passed a thin dark scanner over the heavy metal seals, and raised a blank black tablet.

For one heartbeat, Pier Four was dead quiet except for Qian Weiguo's ragged breathing.

Then a voice spoke from the masked figure standing right in front of Yan Tiehu. It was completely flat, even, and cold as ice sliding over steel, carrying neither anger nor mercy.

"Target Yan Tiehu confirmed. Target Qian Weiguo confirmed. Restricted rare-earth metal cargo seized and sealed intact. Both men logged for state security and anti-gang handover."

All of Yan Tiehu's tough talk collapsed on the spot. His shoulders slumped, his mouth hung half-open, and a cold shiver ran straight down his spine. He finally understood that nobody in Yunxi, nobody in Hongrui Plaza, and nobody in the province was coming to answer his call.

"I will talk," Yan Tiehu choked out in a thin, terrified whisper, his forehead dropping nearly to the wet concrete. "I know where Deng Yongchang keeps the shipping lists. I know every single name."

The masked figure did not answer, did not look down again, and did not shift an inch. It simply tapped the black tablet once, sealing Pier Four in cold, quiet order until the transport vans arrived at dawn.
