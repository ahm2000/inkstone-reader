# Chapter 34: The Pier Four Interception

Down at Pier Four along the Yunxi riverfront, a flat cargo boat idled against the concrete dock while six workers hurried heavy wooden crates up a steel ramp.

Beside the iron mooring post, Yan Tiehu chewed an unlit cigarette, staring angrily at the dead walkie-talkie in his palm. Facing him stood Qian Weiguo, Shengyuan Capital's regional shipping manager, his dark coat draped over a shirt damp with sweat.

"Untie the ropes right now, Yan Tiehu," Qian Weiguo hissed, grabbing his sleeve with cold fingers. "Leave the last three crates on the truck and tell the captain to move out!"

Yan Tiehu jerked his arm free and spat the cigarette onto the concrete. "Are you out of your mind, Qian Weiguo? Those last three crates hold the refined rare-earth metal bars! If I leave half a ton of Shengyuan Capital's top cargo on Pier Four, Chairman Deng Yongchang will break both my legs before sunrise!"

"You will not have legs left to break if we are still tied here in five minutes!" Qian Weiguo snapped, shoving his dead satellite phone near Yan Tiehu's nose. "Look! Our dock radio died at 00:15! Our cell phones died at 00:30! Even the river warning light across the water just went black! That is not a storm, Yan Tiehu. That is a total lockdown!"

Yan Tiehu narrowed his eyes, though cold sweat rolled behind his ear. "A lockdown by who? County Magistrate Sun Weiming takes our money! Chief Liang Guodong eats at Chairman Deng Yongchang's table! Even if a river patrol boat wanders down here, one call from Hongrui Plaza sends them straight back to base!"

"With what phone?" Qian Weiguo hissed, voice rising into a panicked rasp. "Try calling Hongrui Plaza right now! Try calling Director Cheng Biao at Old Street Station! Nobody is answering because nobody in Yunxi can send a call out!"

Near the ramp, senior dock hand Guo Liang stopped beside a forklift, slipping a hand inside his jacket toward a small pistol. "Yan Tiehu, the lookout at the pier gate stopped answering five minutes ago. Should I send two men up the road to check?"

"Do not waste time checking!" Yan Tiehu barked, slapping a crate to hide his nerves. "Load those last three boxes in ninety seconds! Once we pass the county bend, Qian Weiguo can call his province contacts from the next town's cell tower and laugh at himself for panicking!"

Qian Weiguo stared at him in bitter fear. "You dumb street thug! You do not even understand what is inside those metal-lined crates! If customs or state security catches us with unlisted rare-earth metal headed for an overseas ship, neither Deng Yongchang nor Shengyuan Capital will save a single neck on this pier!"

"Keep your mouth shut about the crates," Yan Tiehu snarled, fists clenched. "Get on the boat or swim!"

Before Qian Weiguo could answer, the boat's diesel engine coughed once and died.

Sudden silence hit the dock like a slap. Only dark water lapped against the steel hull.

Yan Tiehu whipped his head toward the cabin. "Captain! What the hell did you kill the engine for?"

No voice answered.

Beside the forklift, Guo Liang spun around, eyes wide as dark shapes climbed smoothly over the front rail from the river and over the concrete wall along the road at once. "Yan Tiehu!" Guo Liang yelled, dragging his pistol clear. "We have company!"

He never even raised the barrel to his waist.

A figure in plain black gear crossed four paces in silence. A gloved palm knocked Guo Liang's wrist upward, a knee drove behind his leg, and the gun vanished from his fingers before his elbow hit the concrete. Not a shot rang out. No warning shout echoed.

Across the deck and dock, twelve more figures in plain black gear and smooth full masks moved in silent order. Within eight seconds, all six dock workers lay face-down with wrists tied behind their backs, without a broken bone or a drop of blood.

Qian Weiguo's clipboard clattered across the planks. His knees went weak, and he staggered back against the crates, pale as chalk. "Do not shoot," he gasped, throwing both shaking hands high. "I am not armed! I am only a shipping manager!"

Yan Tiehu backed up two steps, fear clashing with twenty years of street arrogance. His right hand twitched toward the iron wrench behind his belt. "Who the hell are you people?" he shouted, voice shaking. "This cargo belongs to Chairman Deng Yongchang and Shengyuan Capital! Chief Liang Guodong and County Magistrate Sun Weiming both know about tonight's shipment! Put your hands on me and you will not live till noon!"

The nearest masked figure did not slow by half a step.

Yan Tiehu reached for his belt, and his wrist vanished into an iron grip. One smooth twist lifted him off his boots, pulled the wrench free, and drove him onto both knees on the damp concrete, knocking the breath from his lungs.

Yan Tiehu gasped, staring up in shaking disbelief at the blank dark visor. No badge, no flashlight, no anger. Only cold precision that chilled him to the bone.

Another black-clad figure scanned the metal crate seals and raised a blank black tablet.

For one heartbeat, Pier Four was dead quiet except for Qian Weiguo's ragged breathing.

Then the masked figure in front of Yan Tiehu spoke in a flat, even voice without anger or mercy.

"Target Yan Tiehu confirmed. Target Qian Weiguo confirmed. Restricted rare-earth metal cargo seized and sealed intact. Both men logged for state security and anti-gang handover."

All of Yan Tiehu's tough talk collapsed on the spot. His shoulders slumped and a cold shiver ran down his spine. He finally understood that nobody in Yunxi, Hongrui Plaza, or the province was coming to save him.

"I will talk," Yan Tiehu choked out in a terrified whisper, his forehead nearly touching the concrete. "I know where Deng Yongchang keeps the shipping lists. I know every single name."

The masked figure did not answer or look down again. It simply tapped the black tablet once, sealing Pier Four in quiet order until the transport vans arrived at dawn.
