# Chapter 170: The Harbor Villa Invitation

When Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan returned to their suite at the Shenzhen Bay hotel around 8:15 PM, a uniformed concierge knocked softly on the door five minutes later.

"Good evening, President Qin," the concierge said politely, holding out a silver tray. "A private messenger from the Shen Family dropped this off an hour ago and asked that it be delivered directly to your hands."

On the tray lay a heavy crimson envelope embossed with gold leaf and sealed with dark red wax. Qin Yuhan thanked the concierge, closed the door, and slid a finger under the wax seal. Inside was a thick cream invitation card printed in crisp gold calligraphy.

Her brows knitted together as she read the lines aloud.

"To President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group. To mend past misunderstandings and discuss sincere cooperation along the southern maritime corridor, the Shen Family respectfully invites you and your husband to an apology banquet at Harbor Villa tomorrow evening at 6:30 PM. Distinguished maritime partners from Hong Kong will also be in attendance to witness our goodwill. Signed, Shen Wanshan."

Qin Yuhan set the card down on the glass coffee table, her expression cooling instantly. "An apology banquet," she repeated with a short, dry laugh. "Two days ago, Shen Jingzhou tried to bully me out of sixty percent of our southern route. Now his father suddenly wants to bow his head and invite Hong Kong shipping partners to witness his goodwill?"

She walked to the window, folding her arms tightly across her cream sweater. "This is a classic Hongmen Banquet. Harbor Villa is a private waterfront estate owned by a Hong Kong shipping trust. If I walk in there tomorrow night, Shen Wanshan is not going to apologize. He is going to surround me with offshore creditors and port heavyweights to pressure Haiyue Group into signing an unfair deal."

Chen Xuan sat comfortably on the plush sofa, kicking off his sneakers and peeling a fresh tangerine with unhurried precision. He popped a sweet wedge into his mouth and glanced down at the shiny gold card on the table.

"Harbor Villa, huh?" Chen Xuan remarked mildly. "I heard Uncle Zhao at the dock mention that place today. Apparently, their private chef used to work at a three-star Cantonese restaurant in Kowloon. Their steamed wild grouper and crab roe tofu are supposed to be the best in Shenzhen."

Qin Yuhan turned around and stared at him, half exasperated and half amused. "Chen Xuan! I am telling you Shen Wanshan is setting a trap for Haiyue Group, and you are thinking about steamed grouper?"

"Two things can be true at the same time," Chen Xuan said reasonably, holding out half of the peeled tangerine to her. "It can be a trap, and the grouper can still be delicious. Think about it, Yuhan. Free top-tier seafood should never be wasted."

Qin Yuhan took the tangerine with a helpless sigh and sat down beside him. "Be serious for one minute. I was thinking of calling our legal director at Haiyue Tower back in Shanghai to draft a polite refusal. If we skip it, we avoid stepping onto their turf."

Chen Xuan shook his head gently, his tone turning calm and steady. "If you skip it, Shen Wanshan will tell every shipping broker in Shenzhen tomorrow night that Haiyue Group was too scared to even show up at a peace table. Your southern corridor talks will freeze for months."

Qin Yuhan paused. She knew he was right. In southern port circles, face and nerve mattered almost as much as capital. Backing down from a formal invitation would hand Shen Wanshan an easy propaganda victory.

"Besides," Chen Xuan added with an easy smile, pouring himself a cup of warm water from his dented green thermos, "we are in Shenzhen, not some lawless island. They cannot lock the doors or force your hand onto a contract. We walk in, eat our fill of steamed grouper, listen to whatever little speech Shen Wanshan and his Hong Kong guests prepared, and if the terms smell bad, we wipe our mouths and walk straight out."

Looking into his calm, untroubled eyes, Qin Yuhan felt the knot in her chest loosen. Maybe she had been overthinking it. Shen Wanshan was a shrewd businessman, not a street gangster, and having Chen Xuan calmly sitting beside her always made tense rooms feel much smaller.

"Fine," Qin Yuhan relented, a small smile returning to her lips. "We will go tomorrow night. I will have our legal team review all our Shenzhen freight contracts in the morning so they cannot catch me off guard with any hidden clause. You just focus on eating your grouper."

"Deal," Chen Xuan grinned. "Go take a hot shower. The bay wind was chilly tonight."

Once the bathroom door clicked shut and the sound of running water filled the suite, Chen Xuan stood up slowly and walked out onto the wide private balcony. The late November breeze brushed his face as he looked out over the dark, quiet expanse of Shenzhen Bay. Far down the coastline, the warm yellow lights of Harbor Villa glowed faintly against the black water.

The easygoing warmth in his eyes did not vanish, yet beneath it settled a quiet, bottomless stillness.

He reached into his pocket, pulled out his single regular smartphone, and opened a plain text thread with Number 1. His thumb moved swiftly across the screen.

*Tell the kitchen at Harbor Villa not to overcook the steamed grouper tomorrow night. Also, check who came across the bay from Seven-Harbor and which mainland banks they are leaning on.*

Three seconds later, the screen lit up with a crisp reply from Number 1.

*Understood, Young Master. Everything will be ready before 6:30 PM tomorrow.*

Chen Xuan locked his phone, slid it back into his pocket, and gazed out at the shimmering lights across the strait for one quiet moment. Then he turned back into the warm suite, picked up another fresh tangerine from the fruit bowl, and began peeling it carefully for his wife.
