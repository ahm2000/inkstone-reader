# Chapter 170: The Harbor Villa Invitation

When Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan walked back into the marble lobby of the hotel near MixC Mall at half past nine, the evening concierge hurried out from behind the front desk.

"Good evening, President Qin," Song Wenbo said politely, holding out a heavy crimson envelope with both hands. "A black limousine from the Shen Family arrived twenty minutes ago. Their senior steward asked me to hand this straight to you the moment you returned."

Qin Yuhan stopped.

"The Shen Family?" Qin Yuhan asked quietly, taking the stiff red envelope sealed with a gold wax stamp shaped like an anchor and three waves.

She broke the seal cleanly and slid out a thick cream card bordered in raised gold ink.

"Old Master Shen Wanshan sincerely apologizes for his grandson's foolish behavior at Pier Nine, and respectfully invites President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group and Mr. Chen Xuan of Hengxin Logistics to a private peace dinner tomorrow evening at seven o'clock at the Shen Family Harbor Villa in Shekou."

Qin Yuhan lowered the card and stared at Chen Xuan.

"A peace dinner," Qin Yuhan repeated coldly. "And he put your full name right beside mine in gold ink."

Chen Xuan glanced calmly at the card over her shoulder. "Shen Wanshan has good handwriting. Or he pays a good calligrapher."

"Do not joke," Qin Yuhan said seriously, guiding him to a quiet sofa near the indoor bamboo garden. "Shen Wanshan has ruled the Shenzhen docks for twenty years. Men like him do not swallow a humiliation and send gold invitations to apologize the next night. How did he even know your full name? At Pier Nine, you only told Shen Jingzhou you worked at Hengxin Logistics!"

"Our clearance papers at Shekou Dock have my name on the signature line," Chen Xuan replied easily. "And the hotel front desk keeps a guest list. Finding an office clerk's name in Shenzhen takes the Shen Family five minutes."

"That is not the point," Qin Yuhan said, tapping the card against her palm. "If he only wanted peace with Haiyue Group, he would have invited me alone. Putting your name beside mine means he suspects you were behind Pier Nine opening at five o'clock yesterday. This dinner is a trap."

"Most free dinners from angry old men are traps," Chen Xuan agreed smoothly. "Still, if you refuse, Shen Wanshan will tell every shipping association in Guangdong that Haiyue Group is arrogant and afraid to sit at the same table. You plan to keep running southern routes out of Shenzhen, right?"

Qin Yuhan bit her lower lip lightly. Refusing a formal peace dinner from a local patriarch would give the Shen Family an easy excuse to turn every small freight broker in the Pearl River Delta against Haiyue Group.

"I have to go," Qin Yuhan decided, straightening her back. "I will bring my legal advisor and stay in the main dining hall. You do not need to come, Chen Xuan. You finished Hengxin's paperwork today. Take the morning flight back to Shanghai."

Chen Xuan looked at her for a second, then smiled faintly. "You just asked me twenty minutes ago to stay nearby whenever you walk into a lion's den. Besides, my return ticket is not until Friday, and Shekou seafood is supposed to be fresh."

Qin Yuhan stared at him, caught between worry and a rush of relief. "You really are impossible. Fine. Stay right beside me and do not touch a single glass of wine they pour."

"I only drink from my green thermos anyway," Chen Xuan said calmly.

Ten kilometers away on the rocky cape of Shekou, sea waves slapped steadily against the private stone pier of the Shen Family Harbor Villa.

Built on a low cliff overlooking the dark shipping lanes toward Hong Kong, the three-story white villa stood behind high iron fences and dark pines.

Shen Wanshan sat smoking a thick cigar, his expression cold and patient. Standing stiffly behind Shen Wanshan's chair was Shen Jingzhou, a faint purple bruise still shadowing his left cheek from his grandfather's slap.

"Zhou Dequan just called," Shen Wanshan said calmly, blowing cigar smoke into the sea breeze. "The concierge handed the invitation straight to Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan."

"Good," Koo Tianming replied without looking up. "Qin Yuhan is too proud to refuse a peace dinner. And if that clerk from Hengxin Logistics really is guarding her, or acting for the power that hit Zurich, he will walk right through your front gate tomorrow night."

Shen Jingzhou clenched his fists, eyes burning with hatred. "Grandfather, Mr. Koo, once he steps inside our villa, why waste time pouring tea? We have thirty security men on the cape! Lock the gates, break his legs, and make him tell us who called China Construction Bank and ICBC!"

Shen Wanshan shot his grandson a freezing glare. "Shut up! Have you learned nothing since yesterday? Lay one finger on him inside my house without knowing who stands behind him, and you will bury the Shen Family before sunrise!"

Koo Tianming gave a cold smile and set his teacup down. "Your grandfather is right, Young Master Shen. Crude violence is for street thugs. Tomorrow night, we use a clean knife that cuts twice."

He set a small black voice recorder and a manila folder onto the table. "Tell me our customs friend is ready."

"Deng Fuhai, deputy clerk at the Shenzhen Port Clearance Office," Shen Wanshan smirked darkly. "We paid off his 2 million yuan gambling debt in Macau last winter. He owes us his skin, and he has already practiced his lines."

"Excellent," Koo Tianming murmured, his wire-rimmed glasses catching the lantern light. "Halfway through dinner, invite Qin Yuhan into the side study to sign a berth agreement. Deng Fuhai will be waiting inside. Before she understands what is happening, he will slide an envelope of marked bank slips across the desk and thank her aloud for the 800,000 yuan bribe she supposedly paid to rush the Haiyue Star out of Pier Nine and frame Lu Guobin."

Shen Jingzhou's eyes lit with cruel glee. "A fake bribery recording! We edit the clip cleanly and threaten to send it to the anti-corruption office and Shanghai newspapers! Haiyue Group's stock and General Qin Zhengguo's name will blow up overnight!"

"And that is only the first half," Koo Tianming added softly, his gaze razor-sharp. "While Qin Yuhan is trapped inside, we watch Chen Xuan."

"If Chen Xuan is truly backed by the unseen giant who broke Zurich and froze three national banks in twenty minutes," Shen Wanshan whispered, "he will have to make a call to save Qin Yuhan right in front of our eyes. The moment he reaches for his phone, Mr. Koo's men will see who answers."

"And if nobody answers," Koo Tianming said coldly, lifting his tea toward the dark harbor, "then we crush Haiyue Group, bury the clerk in the sea, and keep our 14 billion yuan Changbai Mountain line running without a scratch."
