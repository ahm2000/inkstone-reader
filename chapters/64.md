# Chapter 64: NDRC and Ministry of Finance: Freezing the Drain

At eight forty on Monday morning, fifty minutes before the domestic stock exchanges sounded the opening bell, the joint financial crisis hall beneath the National Development and Reform Commission looked like a battlefield command post. Ten directors from the Reform Commission, Ministry of Finance, People's Bank of China, and State-Owned Assets Supervision sat behind dual monitors, their faces lit by flickering bond curves through a dense fog of cigarette smoke.

National Development and Reform Commission Chairman Qian Wenbo stood beside the main screen, his tie pulled loose and his eyes red-rimmed. He pointed his glowing cigarette straight at a crimson web of capital flows covering the display.

"Fifty minutes," Qian Wenbo said tightly. "At nine thirty sharp, Shanghai and Shenzhen open. If we freeze Shengyuan Capital's shadow-clearing pipes blindly, interbank credit locks up by nine thirty-five. If we leave them open for one hour, Fang Qiming's onshore brokers will dump collateralized mineral debt and drain 20 billion yuan more before lunch. Give me a surgical wall, not panic."

Securities and Market Stabilization Director Cui Wenjie leaned forward, his hands shaking slightly as he tapped a stack of pre-market order estimates. "Qian Wenbo, let us be brutally honest. Twelve listed energy, port, and real-estate companies have direct equity ties to Shengyuan Capital or Vice Governor Lin Zhengde's urban-investment platforms. If retail investors see simultaneous freezing orders hit the wire at nine thirty, algorithmic funds will dump regional bank shares in ten seconds! We will hit the circuit breaker before ten o'clock!"

"Then let the circuit breaker trip if it stops traitors from emptying the vaults!" State-Owned Assets Supervision Energy and Mining Director Hao Jianping snapped back, slapping his lighter onto the desk. "Do you realize what is sitting inside those forty-eight rigged mineral and rare-earth concessions? Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang signed off on twenty-year transfer pricing at forty percent below sovereign benchmark! Every minute those concessions stay active, Shengyuan Capital's offshore clearing houses collect automatic royalty sweeps!"

"Automatic royalty sweeps are already choked at the border, Hao Jianping," Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Repatriation Director Pan Guozhen cut in sharply, exhaling a thin stream of smoke. "I just got off the encrypted line with Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg. The National People's Congress sovereign repatriation decrees landed twenty minutes ago. Every major offshore correspondent bank has locked Fang Qiming's clearing accounts. Not one dollar leaves the country today."

"Offshore is locked, yes, but onshore is still bleeding!" Banking and Shadow-Clearing Supervision Director Kang Yongsheng barked, jabbing his finger at his terminal. "Look at the forty-two provincial urban-investment platforms on Gao Zhengyi's ledger! They are leveraged fourteen to one on forged county land appraisals, just like Yunxi County! Right now, six regional trust companies are preparing to roll over 400 billion yuan in short-term interbank notes at nine fifteen. If we do not sever those shadow-clearing pipes before the rollovers execute, clean commercial banks will absorb dirty Shengyuan debt!"

People's Bank of China Monetary Stability Director Lu Xiaoping rubbed his temples, his jaw clenched so hard a muscle ticked in his cheek. "Kang Yongsheng, if you sever forty-two urban-investment platforms in one stroke at nine fifteen without a liquidity bridge, three provincial commercial banks will fail their morning settlement calls. Once word leaks that a provincial bank missed settlement, depositors line up outside every branch by noon!"

"Nobody is letting a provincial bank miss settlement, Lu Xiaoping!" Ministry of Finance Sovereign Debt Director Wan Zhiming said coldly, crushing his cigarette into a brimming glass tray and immediately lighting another. "You separate the parasites from the host! Provincial municipal payrolls, hospital budgets, and retail deposits stay one hundred percent backed by an emergency People's Bank liquidity window. Meanwhile, every shadow-trust bond tied to Shengyuan Capital's rigged demolition appraisals gets ring-fenced into a frozen receivership pool."

Provincial Urban-Investment Debt Director Yu Zhipeng stared at Wan Zhiming in sudden shock, his pen freezing mid-air. "A frozen receivership pool? Wan Zhiming, half the institutional buyers holding those shadow bonds are provincial pension funds and private wealth trusts that bought them through Shengyuan Capital's brokerages! If you freeze their principal before the opening bell, governors in fourteen provinces will flood our switchboard!"

"Half those governors are already being replaced by the Organization Department before noon, Yu Zhipeng," NDRC Macro-Planning and Pricing Director Qi Shuhua replied with icy calm, her fingers flying across her keyboard. "And the institutional buyers who knowingly bought fourteen-to-one leveraged demolition paper from Fang Qiming at twelve percent guaranteed yields were not innocent widows. They knew the paper was dirty. Clean retail depositors get full protection; shadow-clearing profiteers get frozen."

Mineral Concession and Rare-Earth Audit Director Bian Changlin leaned into the haze, sweat shining across his brow. "What about the physical mines and coastal ports? If we revoke forty-eight energy and mineral concessions this morning, site managers loyal to He Guoliang and Customs Director Xu Wenbin might halt smelters or shred shipment manifests before our trustees arrive."

"That is why Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's provincial units are moving on the ports and mines at nine o'clock sharp," Qian Wenbo answered grimly, checking the digital wall clock. Eight fifty-three. "We do not give them ten minutes to shred paper. Listen carefully, because this is the exact sequence going to Premier Gu Chengshan for final green light."

The room hushed immediately. Cigarettes hovered motionless above ashtrays as nine pairs of eyes locked onto Qian Wenbo.

"Step one, at nine ten," Qian Wenbo ordered, his voice crisp and unyielding. "Lu Xiaoping opens a targeted People's Bank emergency liquidity facility strictly for clean retail clearing and municipal payrolls across the fourteen affected provinces. Every commercial bank president receives a sealed notice that retail deposits are fully sovereign-guaranteed."

Lu Xiaoping nodded once, his fingers already typing the authorization code. "Ready on your mark."

"Step two, at nine fifteen, fifteen minutes before the opening bell," Qian Wenbo continued, turning to Cui Wenjie. "Securities Supervision halts trading on all twelve Shengyuan Capital-linked listed shells simultaneously under routine material-audit disclosure. No mention of treason, no mention of the 100-trillion-yuan ledger. The broader Shanghai and Shenzhen indices open cleanly with zero panic contagion."

Cui Wenjie exhaled a shaky breath of relief, his shoulders dropping an inch. "Trading halts pre-loaded. The broader market will hold steady."

"Step three, at nine twenty," Qian Wenbo finished, looking straight at Kang Yongsheng, Hao Jianping, and Wan Zhiming. "Kang Yongsheng severs every single domestic shadow-clearing pipe tied to the forty-two urban-investment shells. Hao Jianping and Bian Changlin execute emergency State-Owned Assets trusteeship over all forty-eight rigged energy and rare-earth concessions. Wan Zhiming and Yu Zhipeng lock the dirty institutional shadow bonds into sovereign receivership while Pan Guozhen coordinates the Zurich and Singapore repatriation sweeps."

Qi Shuhua glanced at the wall clock. Eight fifty-eight. "And Yunxi County's 480-million-yuan forged demolition bond?"

"Flagged and frozen at nine twenty sharp," Yu Zhipeng confirmed grimly. "Developer Deng Yongchang's credit lines will bounce before provincial officers even finish reading his warrant."

Qian Wenbo stubbed out his cigarette, his face hard as iron. "Transmit the sequence to Premier Gu Chengshan. We freeze twenty years of bloodsucking in thirty minutes, and the ordinary citizen buying groceries at nine thirty will not feel a single tremor."

Across the smoke-choked hall, ten hands hit their keyboards in unison, snapping shut the greatest financial drain in the nation's history.
