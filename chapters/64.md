# Chapter 64: NDRC and Ministry of Finance: Freezing the Drain

At eight forty on Monday morning, fifty minutes before the stock markets opened, the joint crisis hall under the National Development and Reform Commission looked like a war room. Ten directors from the Reform Commission, the Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, and State-Owned Assets Supervision sat behind glowing monitors. Thick cigarette smoke hung over their heads.

National Development and Reform Commission Chairman Qian Wenbo stood by the main screen, his tie pulled loose and his eyes red. He pointed his glowing cigarette straight at a bright red web of money flows on the screen.

"Fifty minutes," Qian Wenbo said tightly. "At nine thirty sharp, Shanghai and Shenzhen open. If we cut off Shengyuan Capital's hidden money channels blindly, bank lending locks up by nine thirty-five. If we leave them open for even one hour, Fang Qiming's local brokers will dump mining debt and drain another 20 billion yuan before lunch. Give me a clean wall, not panic."

Securities and Market Stabilization Director Cui Wenjie leaned forward, his hands shaking slightly as he tapped a stack of morning trade estimates. "Qian Wenbo, let us be honest. Twelve listed energy, port, and property companies have direct money ties to Shengyuan Capital or Vice Governor Lin Zhengde's local investment groups. If regular investors see freeze orders hit the news at nine thirty, computer traders will dump regional bank stocks in ten seconds! The market will crash and halt before ten o'clock!"

"Then let the market halt if it stops traitors from emptying the vaults!" State-Owned Assets Supervision Energy and Mining Director Hao Jianping snapped back, slapping his lighter onto the desk. "Do you know what is sitting inside those forty-eight rigged mining and rare-earth deals? Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang signed twenty-year price deals at forty percent below the real state rate! Every minute those deals stay open, Shengyuan Capital's overseas banks pull automatic payments out of the country!"

"Those overseas payments are already blocked at the border, Hao Jianping," Foreign Exchange and Cross-Border Repatriation Director Pan Guozhen cut in sharply, blowing out a thin stream of smoke. "I just got off the secure line with Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg. The National People's Congress asset-recovery orders landed twenty minutes ago. Every major overseas partner bank has frozen Fang Qiming's accounts. Not one dollar leaves the country today."

"Overseas is locked, yes, but the money inside the country is still bleeding!" Banking and Shadow-Clearing Supervision Director Kang Yongsheng barked, jabbing his finger at his screen. "Look at the forty-two provincial investment companies on Gao Zhengyi's list! They borrowed fourteen times the real value of fake county land papers, just like Yunxi County! Right now, six regional trust firms are getting ready to roll over 400 billion yuan in short-term bank loans at nine fifteen. If we do not cut those hidden money lines before those loans roll over, clean banks will get stuck holding dirty Shengyuan debt!"

People's Bank of China Monetary Stability Director Lu Xiaoping rubbed his temples, his jaw clenched so tight a muscle twitched in his cheek. "Kang Yongsheng, if you cut off forty-two investment groups in one blow at nine fifteen without a safety net, three provincial banks will miss their morning payouts. Once word gets out that a bank missed a payout, ordinary people will line up outside every branch by noon!"

"Nobody is letting a provincial bank miss a payout, Lu Xiaoping!" Ministry of Finance Sovereign Debt Director Wan Zhiming said coldly. He crushed his cigarette into a full glass tray and lit another right away. "You split the parasites from the healthy body! City payrolls, hospital budgets, and regular customer savings stay one hundred percent backed by an emergency People's Bank cash line. At the same time, every dirty bond tied to Shengyuan Capital's fake demolition deals goes into a frozen state hold."

Provincial Urban-Investment Debt Director Yu Zhipeng stared at Wan Zhiming in shock, his pen freezing in mid-air. "A frozen state hold? Wan Zhiming, half the big buyers holding those dirty bonds are provincial pension funds and private wealth groups that bought them through Shengyuan Capital's brokers! If you freeze their money before the opening bell, governors in fourteen provinces will burn up our phone lines!"

"Half those governors are already being replaced by the Organization Department before noon, Yu Zhipeng," NDRC Macro-Planning and Pricing Director Qi Shuhua replied with icy calm, her fingers flying across her keyboard. "And the big buyers who knowingly bought fourteen-to-one fake demolition paper from Fang Qiming for a guaranteed twelve percent return were not poor innocent people. They knew the paper was dirty. Regular savers get full protection. Dirty profit-hunters get frozen."

Mineral Concession and Rare-Earth Audit Director Bian Changlin leaned into the haze, sweat shining on his forehead. "What about the actual mines and coastal ports? If we take back forty-eight energy and mining deals this morning, site managers loyal to He Guoliang and Customs Director Xu Wenbin might shut down plants or shred shipping records before our teams arrive."

"That is why Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's provincial units are hitting the ports and mines at nine o'clock sharp," Qian Wenbo answered grimly, checking the digital wall clock. Eight fifty-three. "We do not give them ten minutes to shred paper. Listen closely, because this is the exact plan going to Premier Gu Chengshan for final approval."

The room went quiet at once. Cigarettes froze over ashtrays as nine pairs of eyes locked onto Qian Wenbo.

"Step one, at nine ten," Qian Wenbo ordered, his voice sharp and firm. "Lu Xiaoping opens an emergency People's Bank cash line strictly for clean retail banks and city payrolls across all fourteen affected provinces. Every commercial bank president gets a sealed notice that ordinary customer savings are fully backed by the state."

Lu Xiaoping nodded once, his fingers already typing the code. "Ready on your mark."

"Step two, at nine fifteen, fifteen minutes before the opening bell," Qian Wenbo continued, turning to Cui Wenjie. "Securities Supervision pauses trading on all twelve Shengyuan Capital-linked listed companies at the same time under a routine audit notice. No mention of treason, and no mention of the 100 trillion yuan record. The main Shanghai and Shenzhen markets open smoothly with zero panic."

Cui Wenjie let out a shaky breath of relief, his shoulders dropping an inch. "Trading halts are loaded. The main market will stay steady."

"Step three, at nine twenty," Qian Wenbo finished, looking straight at Kang Yongsheng, Hao Jianping, and Wan Zhiming. "Kang Yongsheng cuts every hidden domestic money line tied to the forty-two shell investment groups. Hao Jianping and Bian Changlin take emergency state control of all forty-eight rigged energy and rare-earth mines. Wan Zhiming and Yu Zhipeng lock the dirty institutional bonds into a frozen state hold while Pan Guozhen handles the Zurich and Singapore recovery sweeps."

Qi Shuhua glanced at the wall clock. Eight fifty-eight. "And Yunxi County's 480 million yuan fake demolition bond?"

"Flagged and frozen at nine twenty sharp," Yu Zhipeng confirmed grimly. "Developer Deng Yongchang's credit lines will bounce before provincial officers even finish reading his arrest warrant."

Qian Wenbo put out his cigarette, his face hard as iron. "Send the plan to Premier Gu Chengshan. We freeze twenty years of bloodsucking in thirty minutes, and an ordinary person buying groceries at nine thirty will not feel a single bump."

Across the smoky hall, ten hands hit their keyboards together, slamming shut the biggest money drain in the country's history.
