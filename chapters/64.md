# Chapter 64: NDRC and Ministry of Finance: Freezing the Drain

Minutes before Shanghai and Shenzhen opened, the joint crisis hall under the National Development and Reform Commission looked like a battlefield. Ten directors from the Reform Commission, Ministry of Finance, People's Bank of China, and State-Owned Assets Supervision sat behind glowing screens under thick cigarette smoke.

At the front, four secure video screens showed the presidents of the Four Major National Banks: China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank. Every president sat stiffly, eyes locked on the reserve totals climbing across their desks.

Beside NDRC Chairman Qian Wenbo sat Elder Lin, the retired former State Planning and Economic patriarch of the Lin Family. He held an unlit cigarette and stared at the massive green numbers on the main screen with quiet awe.

"Thirty years I balanced state ledgers," Elder Lin murmured, striking a match to light his cigarette. "I lived through lean winters when we counted every single yuan twice. Never once did I dream of seeing 300 trillion yuan in clean, legal domestic cash land free in our four great banks in one morning, with another 200 trillion yuan standing ready to reach 500 trillion yuan if we need it."

On the screens, the four bank presidents nodded solemnly. The president of China Construction Bank spoke first. "Elder Lin, Chairman Qian Wenbo, our clearing teams finished the final check. The reserves across China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank are one hundred percent clean, legal, and debt-free."

Qian Wenbo loosened his tie, his red eyes burning with fierce relief. He pointed his glowing cigarette at the red web of Shengyuan Capital's money flows on the side screen.

"Hear that?" Qian Wenbo said tightly. "Earlier today, I feared cutting Fang Qiming's hidden channels would drain our banks and drop the ceiling on our heads. Now we stand on an iron floor. Leave Shengyuan Capital's channels open one hour after the bell, and Fang Qiming's brokers will dump mining debt and drain another 20 billion yuan before lunch. Give me a clean wall, and zero panic."

Securities Stabilization Director Cui Wenjie tapped his morning trade sheets. "Chairman Qian Wenbo, Elder Lin, stay careful. Twelve listed energy, port, and property firms have direct money ties to Shengyuan Capital or Vice Governor Lin Zhengde's local groups. Even with 300 trillion yuan backing our banks, if freeze orders hit the news at the opening bell, computer traders will dump regional bank stocks in ten seconds!"

"Then halt those twelve dirty stocks!" State-Owned Assets Energy and Mining Director Hao Jianping snapped, slapping his lighter down. "Look at those forty-eight rigged mining and rare-earth deals! Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang signed twenty-year prices at forty percent below the real state rate! Every minute those deals stay open, Shengyuan Capital's overseas partners try to pull automatic payouts out of the country!"

"Overseas payouts are already blocked at the border, Hao Jianping," Foreign Exchange Director Pan Guozhen cut in sharply, blowing thin smoke. "I just got off the line with Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg. The National People's Congress recovery orders landed. Every major overseas partner bank froze Fang Qiming's accounts. Not one dollar leaves today."

"Overseas is locked, but domestic money is still trying to bleed!" Banking Supervision Director Kang Yongsheng barked, jabbing his monitor. "Look at the forty-two provincial investment groups on Gao Zhengyi's list! They borrowed fourteen times the real value of fake county land papers, just like Yunxi County in Sha Province! Right now, six regional trust firms are ready to roll over 400 billion yuan in short-term bank loans before the bell. Cut those lines now, or clean banks get stuck holding dirty Shengyuan debt!"

People's Bank Monetary Stability Director Lu Xiaoping rubbed his temples, jaw clenched. "Kang Yongsheng, cutting forty-two investment groups at once still risks three provincial banks missing morning payouts unless the new reserve hits their clearing accounts before the bell. Let one local branch miss a payout, and ordinary people will line up outside bank doors by noon!"

"That is why China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank are on this screen, Lu Xiaoping," Elder Lin spoke calmly, waving his cigarette toward the monitors. "Do not panic like a junior clerk. Organize the cushion cleanly. Split the parasites from the healthy body."

Ministry of Finance Sovereign Debt Director Wan Zhiming crushed his cigarette out and lit another at once. "Elder Lin is right! City payrolls, hospital budgets, pensions, and ordinary customer savings stay one hundred percent backed by the reserve routed through the People's Bank and the Four Major National Banks. Every dirty bond tied to Shengyuan Capital's fake demolition deals goes into a frozen state hold."

Provincial Debt Director Yu Zhipeng froze with his pen in mid-air. "A frozen state hold? Wan Zhiming, half the big buyers holding those bonds are provincial wealth groups that bought through Shengyuan Capital's brokers! Freeze them before the bell, and governors in fourteen provinces will burn up our phones!"

"Half those governors are already being replaced by the Organization Department before noon, Yu Zhipeng," NDRC Pricing Director Qi Shuhua replied coldly, typing fast. "Big buyers who bought fourteen-to-one fake demolition paper from Fang Qiming for a guaranteed twelve percent return were not innocent. Regular savers get full protection from the new reserve. Dirty profit-hunters get frozen."

Rare-Earth Audit Director Bian Changlin wiped sweat from his brow. "What about the mines and coastal ports? Take back forty-eight deals this morning, and managers loyal to He Guoliang and Customs Director Xu Wenbin might shut plants or shred shipping records."

"Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's units are hitting the ports and mines right now," Qian Wenbo answered grimly. "We do not give them ten minutes to shred paper. Listen closely. This plan goes to Premier Gu Chengshan now."

The room went dead quiet. Nine pairs of eyes locked onto Qian Wenbo and Elder Lin.

"Step one," Qian Wenbo ordered firmly. "Lu Xiaoping and the presidents of China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank open the emergency cash line strictly for clean retail banks, city payrolls, and ordinary savers across all fourteen provinces, backed directly by Chen Xuan's 300 trillion yuan legal reserve. Every bank president gets a sealed notice that ordinary savings are one hundred percent safe."

On the four screens, the presidents of China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank confirmed one by one. Lu Xiaoping hit his key. "Cash lines are live."

"Step two," Qian Wenbo told Cui Wenjie. "Pause trading on all twelve Shengyuan Capital-linked listed firms under a routine audit notice before the bell. Zero mention of treason or the 100 trillion yuan stolen ledger. Shanghai and Shenzhen open smoothly."

Cui Wenjie exhaled in relief. "Trading halts are loaded. The main markets will stay steady."

"Step three," Qian Wenbo finished, looking at Kang Yongsheng, Hao Jianping, and Wan Zhiming. "Kang Yongsheng cuts every domestic line to the forty-two shell groups and blocks that 400 billion yuan rollover cold. Hao Jianping and Bian Changlin take state control of all forty-eight rigged mines. Wan Zhiming and Yu Zhipeng lock the dirty bonds into a frozen hold while Pan Guozhen runs the Zurich and Singapore sweeps."

Qi Shuhua glanced up. "And Yunxi County's 480 million yuan fake demolition bond?"

"Flagged and frozen now," Yu Zhipeng confirmed grimly. "Developer Deng Yongchang's credit lines will bounce before officers finish reading his warrant."

Elder Lin exhaled a long ribbon of gray smoke and smiled faintly. "Remember the man who gave us this cushion without asking a cent back. Make sure not one ordinary citizen buying groceries when the markets open feels even a bump."

"Send the order to Premier Gu Chengshan," Qian Wenbo said, stubbing out his cigarette. "Freeze the whole 100 trillion yuan drain."

Across the smoky hall, ten hands hit their keyboards together, slamming shut the biggest money drain in the country's history.
