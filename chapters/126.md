# Chapter 126: Vacationing at Shanshui Villa

At two-thirty in the afternoon, the high-speed train pulled into Jingzhou East Station in Handong Province, and a fleet of black shuttle sedans carried the Shanghai delegates straight to Shanshui Villa on the wooded edge of the city.

Shanshui Villa was built around a wide, crystal-clear artificial lake surrounded by green hills, weeping willows, and red-tiled pavilions connected by stone bridges. Because the Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum was the biggest regional business conference of the autumn, the marble lobby of the main hotel building was packed with executives from Shanghai, Beijing, and every city across Handong Province.

General Manager Sun Haoran stood at the check-in counter clutching his leather briefcase, still looking dazed from what he had witnessed on the train. For the entire four-hour trip from Shanghai Hongqiao Station to Jingzhou City, President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group had stayed in the second-class carriage, eating osmanthus rice cakes and listening to music with Chen Xuan before returning to Car 1 right before the station so her two assistants would not panic.

When the receptionist handed over two standard room keycards for Hengxin Logistics in the East Annex Building, Sun Haoran cleared his throat and handed one plastic card to Chen Xuan.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran whispered cautiously, his arrogant tone from the morning completely gone and replaced by bewildered curiosity. "You and President Qin Yuhan seem to know each other rather well. Did you perhaps go to high school together, or did you once do a delivery favor for her grandfather?"

"Something like that," Chen Xuan smiled lightly, sliding the standard keycard into his pocket and picking up his canvas travel bag and the three brown route binders from Hu Dewei. "President Qin just likes quiet company and hot red tea. General Manager Sun, you should go up to your room in the East Annex and rest. Did you not say you wanted to practice your three-minute roundtable speech for tomorrow afternoon?"

"Right, right! My three-minute speech on cold-chain warehousing!" Sun Haoran patted his forehead nervously. "With top officials like Secretary Sha Ruijin and Mayor Li Dakang attending the opening session tomorrow, I cannot afford to stutter on a single word! Leave the three route binders on the registration table over there, and you can have the rest of the afternoon off to look around the lake."

As soon as Sun Haoran hurried toward the East Annex elevators while muttering his speech under his breath, Chen Xuan dropped off the three brown route binders at the forum registration desk and strolled out through the side garden door toward the quiet willow path by the lake.

Fifty meters away behind a row of trimmed bamboo bushes, two men in plain black polo shirts from the Black Order walked at an unhurried pace, blending seamlessly with the resort security staff while keeping watch over the path.

At the end of the lakeside boardwalk stood Three Willows Courtyard, the most private two-story lakeside suite at Shanshui Villa, reserved exclusively for Haiyue Group.

When Chen Xuan walked up the wooden porch steps with his canvas bag slung over one shoulder and his green enamel thermos in his hand, Qin Yuhan was standing just inside the open glass sliding door in a soft cream cardigan, waving her two assistants off toward the main hotel building after they finished unpacking the forum briefing folders.

As soon as her assistants closed the courtyard gate behind them, Qin Yuhan let out a long sigh, kicked off her beige low-heeled shoes onto the entryway rug, and padded across the warm wooden floor in her stocking feet to throw both arms around Chen Xuan's waist.

"Finally, some peace and quiet!" Qin Yuhan laughed softly against his chest, tilting her chin up to look at him with sparkling eyes. "From the moment we got off the train, five different department directors and three shipping chairmen from Handong Province tried to stuff project brochures into my hands in the lobby. How did you slip away from your General Manager Sun so fast?"

"I reminded him that he has a three-minute speech tomorrow afternoon, and he ran straight upstairs to talk to his bathroom mirror," Chen Xuan chuckled, setting his canvas bag down by the sofa and rubbing the back of her neck gently with one warm hand. "Why are your shoulders so stiff? You have been reading those Handong freight summaries since seven o'clock this morning."

"Because half the numbers in the Shanshui Group and Jingzhou City transport reports do not add up," Qin Yuhan sighed, leaning into his palm like a tired cat. "Before we left Shanghai, my father told me over dinner at Emerald Riverside Villa to be extra careful here in Jingzhou City. He said the old Handa Faction and several local executives attached to Shanshui Villa love to wrap questionable loans inside fancy provincial joint ventures."

"Then let the questionable loans wait until after dinner," Chen Xuan grinned, picking up a pair of flat white canvas sneakers from her luggage bag and kneeling on one knee to slip them onto her feet. He tied her shoelaces into two neat bows and tapped her nose. "Look outside. The sun on the lake is golden, the breeze smells like lotus leaves, and we have two full hours before the welcome banquet at six-thirty."

Qin Yuhan looked down at him tying her shoes, a tender warmth blooming across her cheeks. In front of three thousand employees in Lujiazui and the directors of China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China, she had to be the cool, decisive President Qin. Only when she was alone with her twelve-thousand-yuan clerk husband could she forget every balance sheet and just be a twenty-six-year-old wife on an autumn holiday.

"Where are you taking me, Clerk Chen?" Qin Yuhan teased, reaching down to take his hand as he stood up. "Did you find a snack stall by the lake already?"

"Even better," Chen Xuan said, hooking his fingers warmly through hers and leading her out through the private backyard gate onto the quiet willow path along the western water's edge. "The old gardener by the stone bridge told me five minutes ago that they roast fresh chestnuts and sweet potatoes in an iron drum behind the bamboo pavilion on the north shore. Come on, Mrs. Chen, your husband is treating you to a ten-yuan snack."

Hand in hand, the two of them strolled slowly under the trailing green willow branches by the sparkling water, buying a warm paper bag of sweet roasted chestnuts and sharing them one by one while laughing in the golden autumn sunshine.
