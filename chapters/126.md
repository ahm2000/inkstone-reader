# Chapter 126: Vacationing at Shanshui Villa

The late-autumn wind in Jingzhou carried a crisp chill off the hills, shaking loose a steady drift of golden ginkgo leaves across the stone driveway of Shanshui Villa.

By Friday afternoon, black shuttle sedans lined the circular entrance as delegates from Shanghai, Beijing, and every city across Handong Province arrived for the Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum. Built around a wide artificial lake framed by crimson maples, weeping willows, and red-tiled pavilions connected by arched stone bridges, the resort looked more like a quiet painting than a business venue.

Inside the bright marble lobby, General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics stood at the check-in counter clutching his leather briefcase. His shoulders were still stiff from the four-hour high-speed train ride from Shanghai. Beside him, Department Manager Hu Dewei squinted at their plastic room keycards and muttered under his breath.

"Standard twin rooms in the East Wing," Hu Dewei grumbled, adjusting his tie. "We came all the way to Jingzhou to represent Hengxin Logistics, and they put us in the farthest building. And honestly, Manager Sun, why did we even bring Chen Xuan along? He is just a junior supervisor from the 12th floor. He slept through half the train ride and brought a dented green thermos like he is going fishing."

Sun Haoran opened his mouth to reply, then froze mid-breath. His eyes widened as he stared toward the rotating glass doors.

The doors swung open, letting in a gust of cool autumn air and a scattering of yellow leaves. Chen Xuan strolled inside without a trace of hurry. He wore a simple charcoal windbreaker over a soft cotton shirt, his dented green metal thermos tucked comfortably in his left hand while his right hand rolled a sleek silver suitcase.

Walking right beside him was Qin Yuhan.

The President of Haiyue Group wore a tailored camel wool coat over a dark knit dress, her long hair pinned back neatly and her presence immediately drawing quiet, respectful glances from every executive in the lobby. Yet as she walked across the polished marble floor, she linked her arm naturally through Chen Xuan's elbow and leaned in to whisper something that made him grin.

Hu Dewei's jaw dropped so fast his keycard slipped from his fingers and clattered onto the floor. "Manager Sun, wait! Is that President Qin of Haiyue Group? Why is she holding Chen Xuan's arm?"

"Shut up and pick up your card," Sun Haoran hissed, though his own face had gone pale with shock.

Before Hu Dewei could even bend down, Chen Xuan spotted them by the counter. He lifted his green thermos in a friendly wave.

"Hey, Manager Sun, Manager Hu," Chen Xuan called out casually. "Did you get checked in already? The road from the station was pretty smooth, right?"

"Ah, yes, yes! Very smooth!" Sun Haoran stammered, bowing slightly without even realizing he was doing it. "Chen Xuan, you and President Qin are together?"

Qin Yuhan paused politely and offered Sun Haoran a warm, composed smile. "Hello, Manager Sun. Thank you for taking care of my husband at Hengxin Logistics. He tells me the 12th floor is a very comfortable place to work."

"It is our honor, President Qin! Truly our honor!" Sun Haoran blurted out, his voice cracking slightly.

Hu Dewei stood frozen like a stone statue, staring from Qin Yuhan's serene face to Chen Xuan's worn windbreaker. He had spent six months lecturing Chen Xuan about filing freight invoices faster and clocking out right at 4:55 PM, and the man was married to the head of a multibillion-yuan conglomerate.

"We should head to the courtyard before the wind picks up," Chen Xuan said gently to his wife, then nodded back at his coworkers. "See you both at the welcome dinner tonight."

A smiling resort concierge hurried over, bowed deeply to Qin Yuhan, and led the couple past the main elevators toward the private lakeside boardwalk reserved for keynote guests.

Sun Haoran watched them disappear through the garden archway, then wiped a bead of sweat from his forehead. "Hu Dewei," he murmured weakly. "From this minute forward, do not ever raise your voice at Chen Xuan again. If he wants to nap at his desk on the 12th floor, you bring him a blanket."

Outside in the garden, the cool November breeze smelled of pine needles and damp earth. As Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan strolled along the wooden walkway toward Three Willows Suite, the resort felt relaxed and lively.

Near a stone bridge, a cheerful middle-aged groundskeeper uncle in a faded blue jacket paused from sweeping golden ginkgo leaves and grinned at Chen Xuan.

"Young man, you picked a great weekend to visit," the uncle called out warmly, leaning on his bamboo broom. "The lake mist looks best at dusk, though the air turns chilly fast after sunset. Make sure your wife keeps that coat buttoned up."

"Will do, Uncle," Chen Xuan laughed, tossing him a friendly wave. "Thanks for the tip."

Further along, a young bellhop in a knit cardigan rolled a luggage cart past them and winked like a cheeky younger brother. "Skip the fancy room service pastries later and ask the kitchen for fresh roasted chestnuts. They just brought in a sack from the hills this morning."

"Now that is useful advice," Chen Xuan said, nodding in approval. "I owe you one."

At the pavilion before Three Willows Suite, a kindly auntie in a thick wool vest was setting out ceramic pots of hot ginger tea. She smiled brightly and handed Chen Xuan two small cups. "Warm your hands, kids. The autumn wind off the water bites right through your sleeves."

Qin Yuhan took a sip of the sweet ginger tea and laughed softly once they stepped into the quiet private courtyard of Three Willows Suite.

"Honestly, Chen Xuan," she teased, setting her handbag on the rosewood table. "We have been at Shanshui Villa for ten minutes, and you are already chatting with half the staff like you grew up here."

Chen Xuan set his green metal thermos down beside the tea tray and stretched his arms. He glanced out the wide window at the peaceful lake, where the groundskeeper uncle, the bellhop, and the tea auntie blended effortlessly into the late-autumn scenery without a hint of stiffness.

"Friendly people recognize friendly people," Chen Xuan replied with a relaxed smile, slipping off his windbreaker. "Now come sit down, President Qin. Your husband is officially on vacation."
