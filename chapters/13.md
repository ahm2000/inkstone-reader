# Chapter 13: The Midnight Filing

Qingyan City, Municipal Discipline Inspection Commission, night duty desk. Saturday, 11:40 p.m.

The Discipline Commission was the city's anti-corruption watchdog, the one office every official feared because it had the authority to investigate anyone in government, no matter how high their title.

Tonight, the young clerk on night duty was hunched over a steaming cup of instant noodles, his third this week, expecting nothing more exciting than a quiet shift until sunrise.

Footsteps echoed across the empty marble lobby.

A silver-haired gentleman in a crisp charcoal suit walked up to the counter and placed a thick, neatly indexed folder beside the sign-in ledger. Beside it, he set a plain white business card.

"Good evening," Attorney Bai Qingzhou said politely. "This is an urgent pre-inspection complaint regarding Yunxi County's Old Street rebuilding project and Deputy Police Director Cheng Biao. Nineteen pages of checked proof, including timed police holding records, clinic logs, eyewitness statements, street video, and relocation-fund payments. I'd like a stamped receipt, please."

"Of course, sir. Give me just a second to log it."

The young clerk glanced down at the business card, and his chopsticks froze in midair!

Bai Qingzhou. Even a junior clerk knew that name! Senior partner at one of the province's sharpest law firms, a famous courtroom lawyer who never took a weak case and never filed a single sheet unless the proof was rock solid.

With suddenly unsteady fingers, the clerk flipped open the cover page.

His eyes caught four lines printed cleanly right at the top: *Arrest time: 2:05 p.m. Private clinic injury note: 1:50 p.m. (fifteen minutes earlier). Eleven forced shop sales after overnight lockups. 190 million yuan taken from a 640-million-yuan relocation fund. Provincial Inspection Team arrives in Yunxi County on Wednesday.*

The clerk's face drained of every drop of color!

Four days before provincial anti-corruption inspectors arrived in Yunxi County, a famous senior lawyer had just walked through the front door with video proof of a framed arrest and a 190-million-yuan money trail! If the night desk sat on this until Monday morning and the provincial team found out, every leader in this building would lose their career!

"Sir, please wait one moment!" The clerk's voice cracked as he grabbed the red desk telephone, misdialed once with shaking fingers, and punched the number again.

"Deputy Secretary Lin? It's the night desk! Yes, sir, I know it's almost midnight, please listen! Attorney Bai Qingzhou is standing in front of me with a nineteen-page urgent complaint on Yunxi County's Old Street!"

On the other end of the line, Deputy Secretary Lin had picked up half asleep, ready to snap at a nervous rookie. The instant he heard *"eleven forced arrests," "video of a fake fight," "190 million missing,"* and *"Provincial Inspection Team arrives Wednesday,"* the line went dead silent for three full seconds!

"Stamp his receipt politely," Deputy Secretary Lin's voice came back tight and wide awake. "Lock the lobby doors behind him, scan every page straight to my secure tablet, and don't breathe a word to anyone in Yunxi! I'm calling the Commission Secretary right now!"

Across Qingyan City, lights flicked on in quiet bedrooms one after another.

Minutes after midnight, the Secretary of the Municipal Discipline Commission, one of the top ten leaders running Qingyan City, read the scanned 1:50 p.m. clinic slip and the video stills, his face turning grim as stone. He immediately dialed Qingyan's top police chief, who also served as a Vice Mayor of the city.

Already in bed, the city police chief sat up so fast he knocked his water glass crashing onto the floorboards!

In Yunxi County, forty minutes down the highway, County Police Director Mai Zhenhua was jerked awake at 12:20 a.m. by his private mobile vibrating violently on his nightstand. Seeing the city police chief's personal number flashing in the dark, Mai Zhenhua sat bolt upright and answered in a rush.

"Director! Is there an emergency?"

"Mai Zhenhua, listen very carefully," the city police chief's voice crackled icy cold through the speaker. "Your Deputy Director Cheng Biao locked up an Old Street hardware owner named Chen Mingde yesterday using a private clinic note dated fifteen minutes before the arrest. Eleven other shopkeepers signed away their stores after similar holds. The Municipal Discipline Commission already has video of the staged fall on their desk, and provincial inspectors land on Wednesday! If Chen Mingde is still sitting in your holding cell at sunrise, don't bother explaining it to me. Explain it to the provincial team!"

Click!

Mai Zhenhua sat frozen on the edge of his bed in the dark, cold sweat soaking the back of his pajamas. Moments later, in another quiet house across Yunxi County, Party Secretary Fan Weiguo's bedside phone began to ring, the caller ID glowing with four terrifying words: Municipal Discipline Inspection Commission.
