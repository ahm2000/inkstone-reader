# Chapter 214: A Quiet Call to Terminal Six

Upstairs, the soft sound of the shower began to run behind the closed bathroom door. Downstairs, Chen Xuan rinsed the soup ladle, wiped the kitchen counter clean, and poured himself half a cup of warm jasmine tea from his dented green metal thermos.

At 7:35 PM, he slid open the glass door to the villa's second-floor balcony.

Cold December air rolled off the Huangpu River, carrying the faint sound of distant barge horns through the white winter fog. Down along the quiet brick lane lined with evergreen hedges, Uncle Zhao from two doors over was walking his fluffy brown terrier. Wearing a thick navy puffer jacket and knitted gloves, the older neighbor looked every bit the retired neighborhood uncle enjoying an evening stroll.

Spotting Chen Xuan on the balcony, Uncle Zhao grinned and raised a gloved hand. "Smelled sparerib soup drifting over the wall earlier, Xuan! You are spoiling your wife rotten again."

"Gotta keep the peace at home, Uncle Zhao," Chen Xuan laughed, leaning his elbows on the railing. "Watch the frost on the bridge steps. It gets slick after 7:00 PM."

"Will do, kid. Go back inside before your tea gets cold!" Uncle Zhao chuckled and ambled slowly down the misty lane, the little terrier trotting happily at his heels.

Not a single person passing through Emerald Riverside would ever guess that Uncle Zhao and hundreds of other friendly neighbors, delivery riders, and quiet shopkeepers scattered around the district belonged to the Zero Guard, watching over Chen Xuan and his family around the clock without a trace of stiffness or formality. Even the White Guard's own background checks came back spotless every single time.

Chen Xuan took a slow sip of jasmine tea, letting the warmth settle in his chest. Then his expression grew calm and focused.

He reached into his trouser pocket and pulled out his single everyday smartphone. The screen lit up with his lock wallpaper, a candid photo of him and Qin Yuhan laughing under golden autumn ginkgo leaves. Swiping past a grocery reminder text from his wife and a funny meme from his coworker at Hengxin Logistics, he scrolled down to a plain, unremarkable contact entry and tapped dial.

The line connected on the very first ring.

"Young Master," a steady, respectful voice answered immediately. It was Number 7 of the White Guard.

"Evening, Number 7," Chen Xuan said in a relaxed, conversational tone, gazing out toward the hazy glow of the Pudong shoreline. "Are you busy?"

"Never too busy for your call, Young Master. Please give your instructions."

"My wife's company has a cargo ship, the Haiyue Star, sitting at Shanghai Port Terminal Six right now," Chen Xuan said smoothly. "A regional director from Tianhai International named Qian Weidong bribed a maritime inspection officer called Captain Lu to slap a fake ballast violation on the hull. They want to trap the vessel past 9:00 PM so Haiyue Group misses its winter tide and takes an 80 million yuan penalty."

For two seconds, only the faint tap of keys echoed on the other end of the line.

"I have pulled the port registry and the harbor dispatch log, Young Master," Number 7 reported crisply. "There is a helpful coincidence already in motion. Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping arrived in Shanghai twenty minutes ago with a joint warrant targeting the exact same 500k yuan bribe from Qian Weidong to Captain Lu. His team is currently coordinating with the Shanghai Maritime Safety Administration."

Chen Xuan smiled faintly. "Clean and lawful. Even better."

"How would you like us to handle it, Young Master?" Number 7 asked.

"Two things," Chen Xuan said, his voice unhurried yet carrying quiet, absolute weight. "First, make sure the Shanghai Maritime Commissioner and the port enforcement lead arrive at Terminal Six alongside Hou Liangping before 8:30 PM. Arrest Qian Weidong and Captain Lu strictly by the book, tear up that bogus detention order, and clear the Haiyue Star out of the channel before 9:00 PM sharp."

"Understood, Young Master. And the second?"

"This is not the first time petty rivals have tried to choke Haiyue Group's supply chain," Chen Xuan said, his gaze softening slightly as he thought of Qin Yuhan's tired eyes at the dinner table. "Tonight, have the Maritime Administration issue Haiyue Group a permanent National VIP Green Channel designation across all coastal ports. Priority berthing, automated customs pre-clearance, and direct ministry oversight. From tonight onward, no corrupt dock captain or jealous broker gets to stall my wife's ships again."

"It will be done before 9:00 PM, Young Master," Number 7 replied without a flicker of hesitation. "Maritime Commissioner Shen Bo and Port Enforcement Captain Zhou Ming will be stepping onto Terminal Six with Hou Liangping within thirty minutes. The permanent National VIP Green Channel certificate will be processed under the national coastal trade facilitation program tonight. Neither the maritime officials nor the Handong investigators will see any trace pointing back to you, Young Master."

"Good work. Stay warm out there, Number 7."

"Thank you, Young Master."

Chen Xuan ended the call and slipped his single smartphone back into his trouser pocket. Upstairs, the shower water shut off, followed by the soft hum of a hairdryer.

He stepped back inside, latched the balcony glass against the winter mist, and walked into the kitchen. Picking up three sweet winter oranges from the fruit basket, he began peeling them neatly onto a small white plate, arranging the bright segments beside two cups of hot tea on the living room coffee table.

By the time Qin Yuhan walked downstairs in a soft cream knit sweater with a towel draped over her damp hair, her husband was lounging comfortably on the sofa, tossing a grape into the air and catching it cleanly. Nothing in the warm, quiet room hinted that a storm had just been unleashed upon Pudong Port.
