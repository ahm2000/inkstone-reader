# Chapter 127: The Forum Welcome Dinner

At six-thirty in the evening, the crystal chandeliers of the Grand Lake Banquet Hall at Shanshui Villa shone brightly over forty round tables covered in white silk tablecloths.

Because tonight was the informal welcome buffet before tomorrow morning's official opening ceremony, the hall was filled with the hum of clinking glasses and polite laughter. Provincial leaders like Secretary Sha Ruijin and Mayor Li Dakang were scheduled to arrive tomorrow morning, so tonight's welcome dinner was hosted by Jingzhou Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen alongside several local business heads from Shanshui Group and Jingzhou City.

Near the center glass windows overlooking the illuminated lake, Qin Yuhan stood in an elegant navy-blue evening suit, holding a glass of sparkling water while listening politely to two men who had cornered her five minutes ago.

One of them was Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen, a round-faced man with slicked-back hair and a hearty, practiced laugh. Beside him stood Cai Chenggong, a short, sweaty man in an overly tight burgundy suit who owned Dafeng Freight and several coal-shipping yards in Jingzhou City.

"President Qin, your Haiyue Group is the pride of Lujiazui and the entire Yangtze River Delta!" Ding Yizhen said warmly, gesturing toward the thick gold-stamped contract folder in Cai Chenggong's hands. "This Provincial Freight Data Alliance that Chairman Cai Chenggong and our Jingzhou commercial office have put together is a golden bridge between Handong Province and Shanghai. Once Haiyue Group signs the preliminary framework tonight, your container fleets will enjoy priority clearance at every inland depot around Guangming Peak and Yunxi County!"

"That is right, President Qin!" Cai Chenggong chimed in eagerly, his small eyes gleaming as he flipped open the gold-stamped folder to the signature page. "We even brought in a top international data clearing partner recommended by Advisor Li Weiming from overseas to handle the cloud billing! All we need is Haiyue Group as the lead corporate guarantor for the 300 million credit line with Huaxia Bank and ICBC, and we can announce the partnership at nine o'clock tomorrow morning!"

Qin Yuhan kept her expression polite but cool. Even without reading the forty pages of fine print inside Cai Chenggong's folder, her instincts and her father Qin Zhengming's warning told her that asking Haiyue Group to act as a 300 million credit guarantor on the very first night of a forum was completely irregular.

"Deputy Mayor Ding, Chairman Cai, a 300 million guarantee and a cross-provincial data agreement require a full audit by our legal and risk departments in Shanghai," Qin Yuhan replied smoothly. "I can take a copy of your draft back to my suite tonight to review, but Haiyue Group never signs preliminary agreements at a welcome dinner."

"Aiya, President Qin, if we wait for your Shanghai lawyers to check every clause, the forum will be over!" Ding Yizhen pressed, stepping half a pace closer with a persuasive smile. "In Handong Province, we value swift trust between friends. Why not initial the letter of intent right here so our local news reporters can prepare tomorrow's headline?"

Across the hall by the carved fruit buffet table, General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics was standing on his tiptoes with a small porcelain plate, watching Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong surround Qin Yuhan.

"Look over there, Old Wang," Sun Haoran whispered excitedly to the Pudong freight manager beside him. "Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen of Jingzhou City is talking directly with President Qin Yuhan! That level of high-stakes negotiation is something ordinary people like us cannot even get within ten meters of!"

Before Sun Haoran could finish his sentence, he saw Chen Xuan walk right past him in his neat white shirt and dark trousers, holding two small porcelain plates piled with sliced Hami melon, red watermelon, and peeled green grapes.

To Sun Haoran's horror, Chen Xuan did not head back to the corner table assigned to small logistics companies. Instead, he strolled straight across the red carpet toward Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen, Chairman Cai Chenggong, and President Qin Yuhan!

"He is crazy! Is he going to interrupt Deputy Mayor Ding?" Sun Haoran gasped, nearly dropping his fork.

Right as Ding Yizhen held out a gold fountain pen toward Qin Yuhan, Chen Xuan stepped cheerfully between the gold pen and Qin Yuhan's hand, holding up the two porcelain fruit plates.

"Excuse me, gentleman, watch your sleeve near the watermelon juice," Chen Xuan said brightly to Ding Yizhen before turning to Qin Yuhan with an innocent, everyday smile. "President Qin, the waiter over by the dessert counter just cut two kinds of melon from Xinjiang. Try a piece and tell me whether the orange Hami melon or the red watermelon is sweeter before the rest of the hall cleans out the tray."

Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong both froze, staring in disbelief at the young clerk with a Hengxin Logistics badge pinned to his pocket who had just interrupted a 300 million pitch to ask the president of Haiyue Group about watermelon.

"Who are you?" Cai Chenggong snapped, frowning at Chen Xuan's badge. "Can you not see Deputy Mayor Ding and I are discussing an important provincial contract with President Qin?"

Before Chen Xuan could answer, Qin Yuhan's cool corporate mask melted into a bright, relieved smile. She took the toothpick from Chen Xuan's fingers, ate a piece of orange Hami melon, and had to lift her hand to her lips to hide a soft laugh at the earnest look on her husband's face.

"The Hami melon is much sweeter tonight, Clerk Chen," Qin Yuhan said warmly, taking the porcelain plate from his hand. Then she turned back to Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong with a calm, unmistakable finality. "Thank you for the draft copy, Deputy Mayor Ding and Chairman Cai. Just hand the folder to my assistant over there, and I will look through it later tonight. Right now, I promised to try the pastries at the dessert table."

Leaving Ding Yizhen holding his uncapped gold pen in midair and Cai Chenggong gaping like a fish, Qin Yuhan walked away side by side with Chen Xuan toward the quiet terrace doors, happily eating the peeled green grapes from his plate while General Manager Sun Haoran stared across the banquet hall with his mouth wide open.
