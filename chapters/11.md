# Chapter 11: The Call at Night

Late that evening, a phone rang on the folding table beside a basket of hot bread in Room 601 of Building 2, Fuxing New Village. It was the phone of Wu Lanying, Chen Mingde's wife. The whole family stopped chewing.

The screen said Boss Dong.

"Don't answer that crook!" Chen Jianguo said.

Wu Lanying answered anyway, with a shaking finger. Her thumb hit the speaker by mistake, and a smooth voice filled the room.

"Sister Wu! Good evening. Are you eating? Good, good. I'm only calling to help."

Behind the voice came a thick laugh that Chen Jianguo knew. It was Cheng Biao's.

Dong Haisheng, head of Hongrui Real Estate, spoke gently. "Your husband is in a hard spot. Ten days, maybe fifteen. Your daughter in Wuhan will carry that record to every job interview. But I have friends. Sign the hardware shop over to Hongrui tonight, two hundred twenty-eight thousand yuan, and Old Chen has breakfast at your table. Until then he's our guest. Guests get looked after, you understand."

Wu Lanying's face went white. Under the table, she grabbed Chen Xuan's hand.

His own hand was cold, and it shook. He held on tight.

The shop was the family's whole life, and Dong Haisheng wanted it for less than half its worth.

"Also, Sister Wu." The voice dropped, almost kind. "Your brother-in-law lives on the sixth floor of Building 2. No lift, and the stairwell light has been broken since spring. His wife goes down for vegetables at six every morning. I know the bus your nephew takes. I'm not threatening anyone. I'm saying a family with so many people on the road should want this settled. Sign by nine tomorrow. After nine the price is gone, and your husband's case goes to the prosecutor as a repeat offense."

Liu Meifang's ladle hit the floor.

Chen Xuan's younger brother, Chen Hao, stood up so fast that his chair fell over.

A cold weight settled in Chen Xuan's chest. His eyes went hot.

"Who is this?" said Dong Haisheng.

"Chen Xuan. The nephew."

"Ah! The clerk. Young man, you are a sensible person. Tell your aunt to sign."

"Please." His voice was small and shaky, like a boy's. "Please leave my parents alone."

Dong Haisheng gave a thin, pleased breath. "Good boy. Nine o'clock." The line went dead.

Chen Hao gave a shaky laugh. "You said please to him? Big brother, he'll eat you."

"Brother." Chen Yuan was not teasing. Her hand was shaking on the table. "He knew Mom's hour for vegetables. He knew Dad's stairs."

"I know." Chen Xuan wiped his eyes with his sleeve. "He sounded so scary. Dad, is it very bad?"

"Boy, this is grown-up business." Chen Jianguo's voice was not steady either. "Stay out of it and eat."

Liu Meifang picked up her ladle. "Let him come up the stairs." She waved it once.

Chen Xuan's phone buzzed on the table. The screen said only: Work.

"Work." Chen Xuan slid out onto the balcony and shut the glass door.

"At this hour? Eat first, you boy!" Liu Meifang shouted after him.

"Boss!"

It was An Zhiyuan, and for the first time in his life he was nearly shouting.

"I heard everything. And Yan Tiehu's crew just left its van with an iron pipe, walking toward the tailor's shop. Two hundred and fourteen of us are in position, Boss. Give me the word."

"Chief An." Chen Xuan's voice was low and flat. "Three things. One: that crew does not touch Old Yu or his granddaughter tonight. Two: from now on, a man of ours on my parents' stairs, one at my aunt's door, one near my cousin in Wuhan. Day and night, quietly. Three: record that call. Dong Haisheng just named my father's building, my mother's hour and my cousin's city. It goes in his file."

"Yes, Boss."

When he came back in, Chen Hao was at the peephole.

"Big brother, the stairwell just got so bright I need sunglasses. And there's a man in a property-office vest on a ladder with a flask of tea. He says nobody falls in his building."

"Really?" said Chen Xuan. "That's lucky."

"That's the nicest repairman in the county!" Liu Meifang announced. "Xuan, you're too thin. Drink."

Chen Yuan looked at her brother for a long time. He drank his soup.

* * *

Two special-police vans stood at the edge of the car park behind People's Hospital. Their engines were running, and eighteen men in black gear leaned on the doors.

They were the Qingyan City Special Police Brigade, home from a drill, tired and hungry. Their leader, Team Leader Bao Zhen, was forty-one, with a jaw like a brick. He took orders from nobody in a gray jacket.

Mo Lin, the gray-jacket man from the bus, was now walking toward him from the dark. Mo Lin was a Detail guard.

"Team Leader Bao?"

"Who are you?"

"A traveler," said Mo Lin. It was his code word. "Number Nine."

He said it as if he were saying his name at a bus stop.

Then Mo Lin took something no bigger than a matchbox from his pocket and put it in Bao Zhen's palm.

It was a block of black metal. A small pine branch was carved along its edge, and a plain 9 was stamped on its face.

But Bao Zhen had heard of it twice in twenty years. Soldiers told the story at two in the morning to scare new recruits. It was the Black Pine Badge, the highest order of a secret guard. Any elite team that saw it had to obey its holder at once. No questions, even against its own commander.

Bao Zhen went completely still. Then his hand went up in a salute, without being told.

"Yes." It came out hoarse.

His deputy Fu Jian stared at the stiff arm and whispered, "Leader? Who is that?"

Bao Zhen whispered back, still saluting. "Do whatever he says. Ask nothing."

Mo Lin spoke so mildly that it sounded like an apology. "Team Leader, I need a sweep. Anti-gang, regular and random. Hongrui's security team on Old Street, all eight men and their black van. Bring them in clean. No broken bones unless they start it. No phone calls tonight. Thanks."

"Yes."

Mo Lin walked off into the dark. Bao Zhen let his arm fall and found his palm wet.

"Gear up. We're a random sweep."

At the same hour, on Old Street, Niu Dali had his fist in Han Guifu's collar and his other hand pulled back.

Yan Tiehu was laughing.

Then the whole lane went white with headlights from both ends at once.

Black vans slid across the stones, and a dozen men in black helmets poured out.

"City Special Police! Hands on the wall!"

Niu Dali let go of Han Guifu, who sat down hard on his own doorstep with his mouth open.

"What is this?" Yan Tiehu began.

A special policeman pushed him against the tailor's wall and kicked his feet apart.

"Do you know who I am?" Yan Tiehu shouted into the bricks. "I'm Hongrui! My boss is Director Cheng Biao of this station! Call him!"

"No phone calls." Bao Zhen walked past him without looking.

Yu Changshun stood in his doorway with Yu Ning's face pressed into his side. He could not close his mouth.

"Is this a raid?" he whispered.

A special policeman answered gently, with a glance at the broken sign. "A random check, Uncle."

* * *

Back on the balcony, Chen Xuan's phone buzzed again.

"Boss." An Zhiyuan sounded cheerful and a little afraid. "Six of Yan Tiehu's men are in the Special Police vans. Two iron pipes are bagged, and nobody is hurt. Two got away on foot: the black-van driver and Xie Boyuan, a bookkeeper they call Abacus."

Chen Xuan put his free hand over his face and stood very still.

"Hmm." He spoke through his fingers. "I only said watch them. I did not say eighteen special police."

"You said they must not touch the tailor, Boss. The pipe was already in the air. Thin Monkey is eating a hot dumpling in a police van."

"Good work, Chief. Is Old Yu hurt? And the little girl?"

"Both fine, Boss. Scared, nothing more."

"Thank God." His breath shook. "Chief An. Cheng Biao answers to the law now."

"Yes, Boss."

"He slapped a man for a cup of water and sent a van after a tailor. Take everything he has done, every file. And send the recording of Dong Haisheng's call, with the time, to the bank that holds his loan. In the morning."

Far away in the quiet courtyard, An Zhiyuan's breath stopped. Then he said slowly, "Yes."

In the living room, Chen Hao pressed his nose to the glass door and whispered, "Is he praying?"

Chen Yuan watched their brother's back. "No. He's scared, and he doesn't want us to see."
