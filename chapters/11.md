# Chapter 11: The Flower-Delivery Van on Riverside Road

At half past ten Friday night, a faded white cargo van sat parked on Riverside Road, Qinghe Fresh Florals painted on its side. Inside, soft blue monitor light shone across three men in dark jackets and gray half-masks.

Number 7 sat at the middle desk watching a green dot over Fuxing New Village. Across from him, Number 14 tapped his earpiece off and exhaled.

"Street watch checked in," Number 14 said. "Chen Xuan locked up Mingde Hardware, sent Yan Tiehu away, and walked back to his parents' apartment twelve minutes ago. Chen Jianguo and Liu Meifang are inside with him. Nobody from the Chen family went near Old Street Station."

Number 7's shoulders eased. "Did Yan Tiehu follow?"

"No," Number 14 said dryly. "He saw our men inside Gong's Noodles and left holding his right wrist. Number 9 said he did not look back twice."

"Good," Number 7 said. "Keep Number 9's team two blocks back from Fuxing New Village. No lights, nobody near the stairs. Chen Xuan wants his family to sleep without feeling watched."

Number 14 typed the order and glanced down the bench. "How close are we on this afternoon?"

At the far desk, Number 34 pulled up three screens on the widest monitor. He had not left his seat since the jet landed outside Yunxi County.

"I have the full timeline of the two o'clock incident on Old Street," Number 34 said, rubbing his nose above his mask. "Sloppy work. What local bullies put together when nobody has asked them a hard question in ten years."

Number 7 leaned forward to study the timestamps. "Walk me through it."

"At one fifty-four," Number 34 said, enlarging a camera frame from west Old Street, "a forty-one-year-old drifter named Ma Er walked past Mingde Hardware. No steady job, and two old tickets for faking insurance claims in Sha City six years ago. Four minutes earlier, a prepaid phone in his pocket made two calls to one of Yan Tiehu's drivers."

Number 14 narrowed his eyes. "A paid actor who fakes falls."

"A clumsy one," Number 34 replied, advancing the clip. "At two zero one, Chen Mingde walked out carrying a crate of metal pipe joints. Ma Er planted his heel on the wet curb, threw himself backward onto the stones, and screamed that Chen Mingde shoved him over a buyout argument. Chen Mingde never raised a hand."

"Anyone call an ambulance?" Number 14 frowned.

"Nobody," Number 34 said flatly. "Zero emergency calls from Old Street between two and two thirty. Instead, at two zero seven, a white compact car owned by Yan Tiehu's nephew pulled up, put Ma Er in the back, and drove straight to Yunxi People's Hospital."

Number 7's jaw tightened. "And the hospital played along."

"Worse," Number 34 said, pulling up a patient sheet beside a computer log. "At two thirty-eight, Deputy Director Fang Zhihua skipped check-in and signed a medical report for Ma Er claiming a serious head injury and neck sprain. That turned a fake fall into a violent attack so police could hold Chen Mingde."

Number 14 leaned closer. "He wrote that in half an hour?"

"Faster," Number 34 said, zooming into the file details. "The form with Ma Er's name, ID number, and exact injury words was typed on Fang Zhihua's office computer at eleven fifteen this morning. Almost three hours before Ma Er stepped onto Old Street."

Number 14 let out a cold breath. "Typed before lunch. They did not even wait to see which side of his head he pretended to hit."

"Now look at the third screen," Number 34 said, pointing to a yellow-highlighted arrest paper. "Old Street Station Director Cheng Biao signed the lockup order for Chen Mingde at two twenty-six. Put the clocks side by side. Cheng Biao stamped a paper blaming a hospital head injury twelve minutes before Yunxi People's Hospital saved Fang Zhihua's signature."

Silence fell inside the van. Number 7 stared at the yellow numbers.

"They were never building a real case," Number 7 said quietly. "They set a trap. Lock Chen Mingde up overnight, scare Chen Jianguo and Wu Lanying half to death, and wait for them to walk into Old Street Station tomorrow ready to sign away Mingde Hardware."

Number 14 rested his hand on the desk. "We already have the copied records. Send them to provincial inspectors tonight, and Cheng Biao and Fang Zhihua are in handcuffs before noon."

"No," Number 7 cut in, shaking his head.

Number 14 paused. "You think the provincial office will leak it?"

"Secretly copied files are good for knowing the truth, not for court," Number 7 said evenly. "Move too fast and Cheng Biao erases local records, Fang Zhihua blames a clock error, and Ma Er vanishes before sunrise. Worse, whoever stands behind Hongrui Real Estate smells trouble and hides before we see the whole game."

Number 34 looked up. "You think this goes higher than Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao."

"A street crew does not line up a station director and a hospital deputy director before lunch unless someone higher is paying," Number 7 said. "Chen Xuan's order is simple. Prove Chen Mingde is innocent with solid original proof, and do not wake the county until we pick the right hour."

Number 14 nodded slowly. "Then we take the original records tonight while they still feel safe."

Number 7 touched two fingers to his earpiece.

"Number 19," he said. "Take Old Street. The bakery across from Mingde Hardware has a sharp camera under its second-floor roof. Pull the real hard drive cleanly, slide an exact copy drive in so the baker notices nothing, and save the proof record."

Two quiet clicks answered.

"Number 27," Number 7 went on. "Take Yunxi People's Hospital. Pull the original X-ray file and server room time record for Ma Er's head scan. Prove the scan shows no injury. Leave zero mess and zero trace."

Another double-click came back.

"Number 42," Number 7 finished. "Ma Er did not stay in a hospital bed. Tower signals put his phone near the old grain depot in the south district, likely a back-alley mahjong parlor. Find him, check the payoff cash and call record from Yan Tiehu, get proof of both, and follow him until morning."

The third double-click snapped back.

Number 7 turned to Number 34. "Watch the hospital and police computers. If Cheng Biao or Deng Yongchang makes one unusual call, tell me at once."

"Already listening," Number 34 said, returning to the keys.

Outside the quiet white van, three dark figures slipped into the wet streets of Yunxi County.
