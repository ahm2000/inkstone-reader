# Chapter 11: The Old Elders' Terror

Deep under the capital, in the National Supreme Military Command Center, a huge hall hummed.

Every theater command, air defense network, fleet and missile brigade in the country had gone to combat readiness. The status boards had turned from green to amber, then red.

At the head of the command deck stood General Qin Zhengguo, the highest serving four-star commander in the nation. He had led millions of troops through border standoffs without his pulse rising once. Tonight sweat gathered at his collar. Ten figures in black masks stood in a ring around his deck, and none of them had said a word.

"Who cleared them onto this deck?" he asked his chief of staff.

"Nobody, General." The chief of staff was pale to the lips. "They passed every checkpoint in the elevator shaft. Our deck guards were still reaching for their pistols when the ten were already standing here."

General Qin Zhengguo walked to the bank of red phones. If his own deck knew nothing, he would ask the men above him.

His first call went to the Minister of State Security.

"Minister, ten masked men are on my deck. The sky over one county town in Lanshui is blind, and a hundred-kilometer air shell sits around it. Is this your operation?"

"Do you think I am enjoying this?" The Minister's voice was ragged. "Eight of them are standing in my living room. Six more have my deputy locked in his office. We have no clearance, no file, no name. I was about to call you and ask if the army was starting a coup."

"A coup? My missiles are loaded, and nobody will tell me who the enemy is."

For a moment neither man spoke.

"Then we did not do this," the Minister said. "Which means someone stands above both of us."

He hung up.

The general punched in the line of the Senior Cabinet Vice Premier.

"Vice Premier, I need an order. The Minister of State Security is blind. The Central Guard Bureau is blind. Who ordered an Absolute Order in Yunxi County?"

"Nobody in this cabinet signed anything." The Vice Premier spoke fast and thin. "Five masks are watching me talk to you right now. Hold your theaters. Fire no shot. You have the only guns in this country that can still move, General. Do not move them."

Click.

General Qin Zhengguo stared at the silent receiver. Every leader in the capital was blind and under guard, and every one of them was giving the same answer. Only one kind of person might know what could silence a capital like this. An old man who had helped build it.

He dialed a private line that ran to no network. It led to a courtyard in the western hills, home to a ninety-year-old retired Supreme Elder. Fifty years ago, this man had helped build the country's deepest security system.

The phone rang four times.

"Zhengguo? A call at this hour means trouble."

"Old Chief, forgive me." The general lowered his voice. "An Absolute Order is active. Masked men who never speak are in the capital compound, in every provincial command center, and on my own deck. The Minister is blind. The cabinet is blind. Tell me who they are."

On the other end, the breathing stopped.

Five seconds passed. Then came the clatter of a porcelain cup on a wooden table.

"Masked," the old man whispered. "Silent. An Absolute Order." For the first time in forty years, his iron voice shook. "No. It cannot be."

"Old Chief, you know them."

"Do not say their name on any line. I knew they existed fifty years ago. Every day since, I prayed that no one would ever wake them."

"Please." Sweat ran down the general's jaw. "Every theater is at full readiness. Washington and Moscow are watching the blackout. One nervous pilot could start a war. Tell me if this country is going to war."

Ten seconds went by.

"I cannot tell you who they are. I do not dare say his name." The old man was terrified to the bone. "They answer to no army, no cabinet and no ministry. If anyone can tell you whether to stand down, it is the people behind the Black Pine numbers."

General Qin Zhengguo's breath caught. As the highest serving military commander in the nation, he knew very well what the Black Pine Guard was. They were an off-books sovereign protection force standing completely outside the military and the cabinet, made up of numbered operatives with no public names and no recorded faces, an Inner Ring of thirty elite protectors backed by an outer shadow network planted quietly across civilian and official life to guard a single supreme figure. Every four-star general knew their legend and knew never to cross their path, yet not even Supreme Military Command held a direct line to reach them.

"The Black Pine Guard?" General Qin Zhengguo said tightly, his heart hammering. "Old Chief, I know what they are and what they guard. Are you telling me the Black Pine numbers locked down the capital and put mute guards on my own deck?!"

"I am telling you I do not know," the old man rasped, his voice shaking. "Write this down, then burn the paper." The old man read out ten digits in a shaking voice. "Call it. Pass their checks one by one. And Zhengguo, if you ever tell anyone I gave you that number, I will say I never heard your name. Pray that none of us has offended him."

The line clicked dead.
