# Chapter 81: Long-Life Noodles

Ten minutes later, Chen Jianguo stepped from the kitchen doorway wiping flour from his hands and caught Chen Xuan's eye with a quiet nod.

"Chen Xuan, take Tang Xiaoyu down the lane to the flour mill at the east end of Hongshi Village," Chen Jianguo said gently. "Buy five jin of freshly hand-pulled long-life noodles before noon. Tell the miller they are for your grandmother's eighty-first birthday."

"Right away, Dad," Chen Xuan replied easily, holding out his hand. "Come on, Tang Xiaoyu. Let us stretch our legs in the sun."

Tang Xiaoyu stood quickly, glad to leave the stifling air near Third Uncle-in-law Liu Guoliang's chair. She slipped her small hand into Chen Xuan's warm palm and walked beside him through the wooden gate.

Outside, the narrow dirt lane wound between low stone walls draped with dried cornstalks and golden chrysanthemums. A gentle autumn breeze stirred the poplar leaves, carrying the clean scent of earth and woodsmoke.

For the first hundred meters, Tang Xiaoyu watched the tips of her sneakers, her brow furrowed lightly.

"Brother Chen Xuan," Tang Xiaoyu whispered softly, looking up with hesitant eyes. "Did I really cause trouble for Uncle Chen Jianguo and Aunt Liu Meifang? That uncle in the leather jacket sounded angry."

Chen Xuan crouched briefly so he could look straight into her clear dark eyes.

"Listen carefully, Tang Xiaoyu," Chen Xuan said warmly, his voice steady and reassuring. "People who judge everything by the price of a jacket never understand what makes a home rich. Since you came to stay with us, Dad laughs every evening, Mom loves cooking extra dishes, and Grandma Zhou Guilan smiled more this morning than all autumn. You are never a burden. You are our family."

Tang Xiaoyu's eyes brightened immediately, the knot between her eyebrows vanishing. She nodded hard, squeezed his hand, and skipped two steps ahead along the sunlit lane.

Forty meters up the terraced hillside above the path, where wild jujube trees cast thin shadows, two featureless matte-black figures paused motionless against a dry stone wall. The permanent Zero Guard team tracked the lane silently, unseen by any villager below, shifting only when Chen Xuan and Tang Xiaoyu continued toward the mill.

At the east end of Hongshi Village, the old miller beamed upon hearing the noodles were for Zhou Guilan, wrapping five generous jin of pale, springy long-life noodles in clean brown paper tied with hemp string. Tang Xiaoyu hugged the warm bundle carefully against her red knitted sweater as they walked back.

* * *

When Chen Xuan and Tang Xiaoyu turned the final bend toward the courtyard, a plain dark-blue compact sedan stood parked neatly outside the brick wall.

Before they reached the gate, the door clicked open and a broad-shouldered middle-aged man stepped out. He wore no uniform today, only a modest gray cotton jacket, dark trousers, and clean cloth shoes. In his right hand, he carried a woven bamboo basket brimming with crisp yellow pears.

It was Director Wei Kun of the Qingyan Public Security Bureau, off duty on his weekend.

Hearing footsteps, Director Wei Kun turned, saw Chen Xuan walking up with Tang Xiaoyu, and straightened his posture immediately with quiet, unmistakable respect.

"Mr. Chen Xuan," Director Wei Kun greeted warmly, inclining his head without spectacle. "I heard your grandmother celebrates her eighty-first birthday today. I happened to be passing through the southern township and brought a small basket of pears to pay my respects."

"Director Wei Kun, you are too kind to come all this way," Chen Xuan replied politely, offering a genuine smile. "Please come inside for a cup of hot tea."

As they entered the courtyard, Chen Jianguo and Second Uncle Chen Mingde paused beside the benches in open surprise. Chen Jianguo had seen Director Wei Kun in Yunxi County and recognized the city's public security director immediately.

"Director Wei Kun!" Chen Jianguo hurried forward, wiping his hands briskly. "What an honor! Please, come sit in the shade!"

Director Wei Kun stepped straight up to the cane chairs and offered a deep, respectful bow to Chen Fugen and Zhou Guilan.

"Old Sir Chen Fugen, Old Madam Zhou Guilan," Director Wei Kun said sincerely, presenting the basket with both hands. "Wishing you health as steady as the southern hills and a life as long as the eastern river."

Zhou Guilan beamed with delight, thanking him warmly while Chen Yuan hurried over with the finest teacup.

Near the wooden table, Liu Guoliang squinted suspiciously at the plainly dressed visitor. He nudged his wife and muttered loud enough to carry, "Director? Sounds like some neighborhood station clerk. Look at that plain gray jacket and a twenty-yuan basket of pears. Anyone can call themselves a director in a little county."

* * *

Before Liu Guoliang finished chuckling at his own remark, the deep, clean rumble of a brand-new commercial engine rolled down the narrow lane.

Everyone turned toward the gate as a gleaming white Haiyue Group contracted delivery van slowed smoothly to a stop outside the wall. Its paint shone like fresh snow in the sunlight, and the crisp green-and-gold Haiyue Logistics emblem gleamed along the side doors.

The driver's door swung open, and Wang Lei hopped down with a wide, beaming smile. Wearing a clean navy work jacket, he opened the rear doors and lifted two heavy wooden crates loaded with glossy orchard apples, golden honey pomelos, and fresh coastal seafood.

"Brother Chen Xuan! Uncle Chen Jianguo! Aunt Liu Meifang!" Wang Lei called cheerfully as he carried the crates straight into the courtyard. "I just finished my morning run and drove straight over to wish Grandma Zhou Guilan a happy eighty-first birthday!"

Setting the crates gently beside the porch, Wang Lei stepped right in front of Chen Xuan and bowed deeply from the waist in front of the whole yard.

"Brother Chen Xuan," Wang Lei said, his voice ringing loud and sincere across the sunlit courtyard. "I never got a proper chance to thank you in front of your elders. Two months ago, when crooked freight bosses tried to crush my family on Old Street, I was days from losing everything. Because you stood up quietly and pointed me straight, I signed a clean direct contract with Haiyue Group. That brand-new van outside your gate is paid in full, and my family sleeps peacefully every night!"

Stunned murmurs swept through the neighbors and relatives around the wooden tables. Second Aunt Wu Lanying paused with a platter of dishes, her eyes shining with proud amazement.

Wang Lei turned toward Zhou Guilan with a grin. "Grandma Zhou Guilan, you raised the most upright grandson in Qingyan City!"

Chen Xuan stepped forward and gently helped Wang Lei straighten up. "You earned that van with your own honest driving, Wang Lei. Sit down and eat a bowl of long-life noodles before you head back."

Across the yard, Liu Guoliang's smug grin cracked visibly. His fingers tightened nervously around his teacup as he stared first at the gleaming Haiyue Group van outside the gate, then at the quietly smiling Director Wei Kun, and finally at Chen Xuan standing calmly beside Tang Xiaoyu.
