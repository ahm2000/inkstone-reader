# Chapter 129: Slippers on the Balcony

After finishing every drop of the midnight shrimp noodle soup, Qin Yuhan felt completely energized. She carried her notebook and the gold-stamped contract draft into the spacious marble bathroom, filling the deep soaking tub with warm water and lavender bath salts so she could outline her five-minute keynote remarks for tomorrow morning's opening forum session.

Out in the living room of Three Willows Suite, Chen Xuan washed the two blue-and-white porcelain noodle bowls in two minutes flat, wiped down the counter with a dry towel, and poured a cup of hot jasmine tea into his green enamel thermos.

Through the frosted glass door of the bathroom, he heard the gentle splash of warm water and his wife's soft voice calling out playfully.

"Chen Xuan, do not fall asleep before I come out! You still owe me a ten-minute shoulder massage for helping me spot that Zurich clause!"

"I am right here keeping your pillow warm, President Qin," Chen Xuan called back with a grin. "Take your time soaking. If the water gets cool, add a little more from the hot tap."

Wearing soft white hotel slippers and a loose gray cotton cardigan over his t-shirt, Chen Xuan slid open the wooden balcony door on the second floor of the suite and stepped out into the cool midnight air.

The artificial lake of Shanshui Villa lay quiet and mirror-smooth under the silver autumn moonlight. Across the water, the distant lights of the main conference building cast long golden reflections across the lotus leaves, while fifty meters down the shoreline, the silent patrol of the Black Order stood unseen beneath the dark willow trees.

Chen Xuan leaned his elbows comfortably against the wooden balcony railing, took a slow sip of fragrant jasmine tea from his thermos lid, and reached into his cardigan pocket to pull out a thin, snow-white smartphone with no brand logo on its casing.

He unlocked the quiet screen with his thumb and typed a simple two-line message to Number 1 of the White Order:

"Alpine Horizon Data Services in Zurich, linked to Li Weiming, Ding Yizhen, and Cai Chenggong's Handong freight contract. Put the server logs and Zurich transfer records on the provincial desks before sunrise."

He tapped send, watched the message vanish in half a second, and slid the white phone back into his pocket before taking another unhurried sip of hot jasmine tea.

Twelve minutes later, three thousand kilometers away in Beijing and down along the quiet avenues of Jingzhou City, the White Order moved in complete silence.

In a high-security communications room in Beijing, Number 1 hung up a direct red line after forwarding the full Zurich banking ledger to Premier Gu Chengshan's midnight duty office and Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming's special task force in Handong Province.

By four-thirty in the morning, while the mist over the lake at Shanshui Villa was still thick and silver, a clean white sedan rolled silently up to the guarded courtyard of the Handong Provincial Committee compound and the Jingzhou Anti-Corruption Bureau.

Number 11 and Number 34 of the White Order stepped out in crisp dark suits, presented central security clearance credentials at the night desk, and delivered three identical sealed white folders directly onto the morning briefing desks of Provincial Secretary Sha Ruijin, Jingzhou Mayor Li Dakang, and Anti-Corruption Bureau Director Hou Liangping.

Inside each white folder sat the complete, notarized Swiss corporate registry and ICBC cross-border clearing trails proving that overseas fixer Li Weiming had set up Alpine Horizon Data Services in Zurich specifically to help Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen and Chairman Cai Chenggong launder 300 million in state logistics loans and steal customs data from Yangtze River Delta shipping groups!

At five-fifteen in the morning, lights snapped on simultaneously in Secretary Sha Ruijin's study, Mayor Li Dakang's municipal office, and Director Hou Liangping's briefing room.

"Look at these Zurich account signatures!" Hou Liangping said sharply, slapping the white dossier on his conference table as two dozen investigators stood at attention before dawn. "Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong actually tried to slip an offshore data siphon into today's Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum! And they even tried to pull Haiyue Group from Shanghai in as their 300 million guarantor! Keep every patrol team in plain clothes outside Shanshui Villa at eight-thirty this morning. Let the forum open on schedule so we do not panic the visiting bank directors from Shanghai, and the moment the morning keynote concludes, seal Dafeng Freight's accounts and bring Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong in!"

While the entire provincial anti-corruption machine quietly locked its net around Shanshui Villa before sunrise, Three Willows Suite beside the willow path was completely peaceful.

Back inside the warm bedroom on the second floor, Chen Xuan had returned from the balcony hours ago. He had given Qin Yuhan her promised ten-minute shoulder massage until she murmured sleepily and drifted off with her head resting squarely on his chest and one arm draped across his waist.

When the soft golden light of seven o'clock morning sunshine filtered through the silk curtains, Qin Yuhan blinked her long eyelashes, stretched lazily like a kitten under the warm quilt, and rubbed her cheek against Chen Xuan's cotton t-shirt.

"Good morning, Clerk Chen," Qin Yuhan murmured with a sleepy smile, tapping his chin with one finger. "What time is it?"

"Seven-fifteen, President Qin," Chen Xuan chuckled, brushing a stray lock of dark hair behind her ear and kissing her forehead. "Your breakfast porridge and steamed dumplings from the lakeside kitchen will be at the door in ten minutes, and I already ironed your white blazer for your morning speech. Ready to go surprise Chairman Cai and Deputy Mayor Ding?"

"More than ready," Qin Yuhan laughed brightly, sitting up in bed with her eyes sparkling.
