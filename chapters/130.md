# Chapter 130: My Wife on Stage

By 9:00 AM the next morning, the Main Convention Hall of Shanshui Villa was filled to capacity. Three hundred corporate executives sat in neat rows beneath the bright auditorium lights, joined by regional directors from China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China.

Across the front leadership row sat Handong Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin, Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang, Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping, and Chief Procurator Ji Changming. Near the far right end of the table, Vice Mayor Ding Yizhen held his chin high, tapping his fingers smoothly on his folder as if the port agreement were already in his pocket.

Way in the back corner at Table 38, reserved for small visiting freight agencies from Shanghai, Chen Xuan sat comfortably in his cushioned chair. His green thermos sat right beside a small plate of crisp walnut cookies.

Next to him, General Manager Sun Haoran adjusted his freshly pressed suit while Hu Dewei leaned over and hissed in a low, exasperated whisper.

"Where on earth were you last night, Chen Xuan?" Hu Dewei demanded, glaring at the walnut cookie in Chen Xuan's hand. "You missed the welcome banquet, you never checked into the East Wing, and now you stroll in ten minutes before the keynote eating snacks! Put that thermos down and take proper notes for our department report. President Qin of Haiyue Group is about to speak!"

"Relax, Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said cheerfully, taking a relaxed bite of his cookie. "Good legroom back here, nobody blocking the screen, and I am paying plenty of attention. Besides, I have a feeling today's speech is going to be very entertaining."

Hu Dewei huffed and shook his head at Sun Haoran, muttering that some young clerks simply had no ambition.

Up at the podium, the opening remarks concluded, and Vice Mayor Ding Yizhen stepped forward to present the Jingzhou River Port Logistics Concession Framework. His voice boomed smoothly through the speakers as he praised the forty-page gold-stamped draft, describing it as a balanced plan designed to guarantee local port stability while welcoming major outside capital.

In the third row, Cai Chenggong leaned back in his chair, flashing a smug grin at his associates.

"Now," Ding Yizhen concluded with a practiced smile, "we invite President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group to share her feedback as our lead Shanghai logistics representative."

Polite applause filled the hall as Qin Yuhan walked onto the bright stage in a crisp tailored ivory suit. Poised and completely in command, she bowed slightly toward the leadership row, thanked Secretary Sha and Secretary Li, and clicked her remote.

Instead of a corporate slide deck, the massive LED screen behind her lit up with a high-resolution scan of page twenty-eight of the bidding draft, with Article 14 highlighted in bold red.

A ripple of surprise passed through the auditorium.

"Haiyue Group came to Jingzhou ready to invest in Handong's river logistics," Qin Yuhan began, her voice calm and resonant. "Before we sign any letter of intent, however, I would like to ask a direct question about Article 14 and Footnote 3 in Appendix B."

Up in the front row, Ding Yizhen's smile stiffened instantly.

Qin Yuhan clicked to the next slide, showing a clean cost breakdown. "Article 14 requires every winning bidder to lock into a 15-year exclusive customs and dispatch contract with one designated local operator, backed by a 200 million yuan non-refundable bond. Footnote 3 adds an automatic surcharge of 300 yuan per container. At projected annual volume, that transfers 150 million yuan every year out of legitimate logistics operations into one private pocket."

Dead silence fell over the three hundred guests. Shocked murmurs broke out across the bank directors' row as executives from China Construction Bank and ICBC rapidly flipped to page twenty-eight of their folders.

"Moreover," Qin Yuhan continued steadily, clicking to her final slide, "the criteria written into Appendix B exclude every major state and private shipping operator in the country. Only one local firm, reorganized less than six months ago under a narrow river-dredging license, meets all three conditions. National maritime pilot standards cost less than forty yuan per container. Charging seven times the national rate is not port safety. It is a private tollbooth."

The convention hall erupted into stunned whispers. In the third row, Cai Chenggong's face drained of color as he shrank into his seat.

At the front table, Ding Yizhen jumped halfway out of his chair, sweat glistening along his hairline. "President Qin, wait! That is a misunderstanding! That document was merely a preliminary technical draft prepared by junior staff for discussion!"

Before Qin Yuhan could answer, a sharp crack echoed from the front leadership table.

Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang had slammed his fountain pen down onto his folder. His eyes blazed with anger as he turned straight toward Ding Yizhen.

"A preliminary draft by junior staff?" Li Dakang's voice cut through the hall like cold steel. "Then why did you bind forty pages in gold-stamped covers and hand them to visiting investors last night with letters of intent attached? The Municipal Party Committee never approved Article 14, and I never saw Appendix B!"

Beside him, Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin did not raise his voice at all, yet the temperature at the front table dropped ten degrees. His gaze rested coldly on the stammering Vice Mayor before he turned calmly to the man on his right.

"Comrade Liangping," Sha Ruijin said quietly, every word carrying immense weight. "It looks like our Anti-Corruption Bureau has urgent work right here in this building."

Hou Liangping closed his leather notebook with a crisp snap. Rising calmly to his feet, the Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief looked straight at Ding Yizhen with sharp, uncompromising eyes and nodded to two investigators stationed near the side doors.

"Secretary Sha, Secretary Li," Hou Liangping stated clearly. "Our team will immediately seal the bidding committee's records and conduct an on-site inquiry into the drafting of Article 14 and the ownership behind that designated operator."

Ding Yizhen's knees nearly buckled as he sank back into his chair, his face gray as ash.

On stage, Qin Yuhan inclined her head gracefully and stepped back from the podium. Thunderous applause broke out across the convention hall, led by Li Dakang himself.

Standing beneath the bright stage lights, Qin Yuhan's gaze swept past the cheering executives and straight to Table 38 in the back corner. Her eyes locked onto Chen Xuan, and a bright, proud smile bloomed across her face.

In the back row, Chen Xuan smiled softly back and raised his green thermos slightly in a quiet toast.

Right beside him, Hu Dewei caught sight of Qin Yuhan smiling in their direction, immediately sat up ramrod straight, and smoothed his tie with a beam of pride. "Did you see that, Manager Sun? President Qin just smiled right at our Hengxin Logistics table! She must have noticed how attentively we were taking notes!"

Sun Haoran blinked, glancing uncertainly from the stage to Chen Xuan, who merely took a calm sip from his green thermos and finished his walnut cookie in quiet amusement.
