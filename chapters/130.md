# Chapter 130: My Wife on Stage

At nine o'clock on Saturday morning, the Main Convention Hall of Shanshui Villa was packed to capacity with three hundred corporate executives, regional bank directors from China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China, and top officials from across Handong Province.

In the very back corner of the hall at Table 38, reserved for small visiting freight agencies from Shanghai, Chen Xuan sat comfortably in a cushioned chair with his scratched green enamel thermos resting beside a plate of complementary walnut cookies.

Next to him, General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics sat stiffly in his freshly pressed suit, still sneaking respectful, bewildered glances at Chen Xuan every thirty seconds.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran whispered, leaning over carefully and pouring a cup of hot water for Chen Xuan before filling his own cup. "Did you sleep well in the East Annex last night? I knocked on your door at nine-thirty to ask you a question about the Pudong route binder, and nobody answered!"

"I went for a long walk around the willow lake and turned in early," Chen Xuan smiled calmly, peeling a walnut cookie in half and popping it into his mouth. "Look up at the main stage, General Manager Sun. The morning keynote session is starting."

Up at the front VIP table below the podium, Provincial Secretary Sha Ruijin and Jingzhou Mayor Li Dakang sat with composed faces. Both had already read the pre-dawn Zurich dossier delivered by the White Order, and both knew that Director Hou Liangping's officers were waiting quietly outside the side exits of the hall.

Three seats down, however, Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen and Dafeng Freight Chairman Cai Chenggong had no idea what was coming. Wearing a shiny burgundy tie, Cai Chenggong beamed confidently as he took the microphone first to introduce his "Handong Provincial Freight Data Alliance" and invite President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group onto the stage to endorse the 300 million framework.

"Once President Qin Yuhan and Haiyue Group join our provincial data alliance today, every container moving between Jingzhou City and Shanghai will share one international cloud network!" Cai Chenggong announced loudly, clapping as he looked down at the front row.

In the center of the front row, Qin Yuhan stood up smoothly in the crisp white blazer that Chen Xuan had ironed for her that morning.

Before walking up the steps to the stage, she turned her head slightly and sent a quick, bright glance all the way back toward Table 38 in the corner.

Sitting at the back table with his green thermos lid in his hand, Chen Xuan grinned and gave his wife a tiny, encouraging nod.

Stepping behind the main mahogany podium under the spotlights, Qin Yuhan looked out across the three hundred executives with calm authority. She placed the gold-stamped contract draft from Cai Chenggong flat on the lectern and tapped the microphone once.

"Thank you for the warm welcome to Jingzhou City, Secretary Sha, Mayor Li, and fellow colleagues," Qin Yuhan's clear voice rang through the hall. "Last night at the welcome dinner, Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen and Chairman Cai Chenggong asked Haiyue Group to sign this forty-page Provincial Freight Data Alliance contract and serve as the lead guarantor for a 300 million credit line with Huaxia Bank and ICBC."

On the side of the stage, Cai Chenggong puffed out his chest and smiled for the cameras.

"However," Qin Yuhan continued, her voice turning crystal sharp as she flipped the folder open to page twenty-eight and projected the page onto the giant LED screen behind her, "while eating midnight noodles in my suite last night, my family member pointed out a four-line sub-clause buried in tiny font at the bottom of Article 14."

The entire hall of three hundred executives leaned forward as the zoomed-in paragraph appeared on the giant screen.

"Could Chairman Cai Chenggong or Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen explain to everyone here," Qin Yuhan asked clearly, her bright eyes sweeping toward the two men, "why an inland freight alliance between Handong Province and Shanghai requires every shipping company and all four national banks to mirror our domestic port manifests and grant pre-authorized debit power to a private shell company called Alpine Horizon Data Services in Zurich, Switzerland?"

A collective gasp echoed across the entire convention hall!

The regional credit directors from China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China stood up from their chairs in shock, staring at the projected Zurich clause on the giant screen.

On the stage, Cai Chenggong's smile froze and turned chalk-white, while down at the front table, Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen knocked over his porcelain teacup, spilling hot tea all over his trousers as he scrambled to his feet in sheer panic!

"That is right!" Mayor Li Dakang stood up at the front VIP table, his voice booming like thunder across the hall as he slammed his palm on the table. "Why does a domestic logistics project in Jingzhou City route 300 million in state bank credit and our customs manifests to an offshore account in Zurich? Security and the Anti-Corruption Bureau, bring Chairman Cai Chenggong and Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen out through the side passage for immediate investigation!"

Right on cue, the side double doors of the hall swung open, and Director Hou Liangping led six officers in neat dark suits straight onto the carpet, escorting the trembling, pale-faced Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong out of the hall before either of them could utter a single excuse.

At the front podium, Secretary Sha Ruijin stood up and led all three hundred executives and bank directors in a thunderous standing ovation for Qin Yuhan!

"Look at President Qin Yuhan up there!" General Manager Sun Haoran clapped until his palms turned red at Table 38, shouting excitedly over the applause to Chen Xuan. "Not only is she brilliant and fearless, she exposed a 300 million offshore fraud trap with a single page! Wait a second, Chen Xuan, did President Qin just say on the microphone that her family member pointed out that Zurich clause while eating midnight noodles with her last night? Who do you think her husband is? He must be some top international Wall Street lawyer or a financial genius from Beijing!"

Sitting lazily in his corner chair, Chen Xuan took a warm sip of Yunnan red tea from his scratched green enamel thermos, watched his wife smiling radiantly under the stage lights as she looked back toward his corner, and chuckled softly.

"Who knows, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said, picking up another walnut cookie from the plate. "Maybe her husband just knows how to cook a really good bowl of river-shrimp noodles."
