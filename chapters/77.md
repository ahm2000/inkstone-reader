# Chapter 77: Tears on the Sixth Floor

The green metal door of the sixth-floor apartment had barely clicked shut before Wu Lanying broke down.

She had held her breath up all six flights of narrow concrete stairs, half afraid a county officer would shout from the courtyard below and say there had been a mistake. The instant the deadbolt snapped behind Chen Xuan, Wu Lanying grabbed Chen Mingde's hands and pushed his faded blue sleeves up to his elbows with shaking fingers.

"Let me look! Do not hide anything from me, Mingde!" Wu Lanying sobbed, turning his forearms toward the window light. "Did Cheng Biao lay a hand on you? Did those animals from Hongrui touch you inside the station?"

Liu Meifang hurried straight into the living room without even pausing to change into slippers. Her eyes filled with tears the moment she spotted a faint red ring circling Chen Mingde's left wrist. "Look at that mark! Putting iron cuffs on an honest man who has never missed a single tax payment in thirty years! Mingde, does your back ache? Did they leave you on cold concrete all night?"

"I am fine, Lanying, really, I am not broken," Chen Mingde said quickly, though his own eyes turned shiny as he gently pulled his sleeves back down. He patted his wife's trembling hand and offered a sheepish smile. "Not a single person hit me. Cheng Biao shouted plenty and banged on the table to scare me into signing that demolition waiver, then tossed me into the holding room. Honestly, the bench was hard, and my stomach is empty, that is all."

Chen Jianguo hurried to the kitchen and came back carrying a steaming kettle and two porcelain mugs. "Sit down first, Mingde. Meifang, get him a warm towel so he can wash his face."

Chen Mingde eased onto the worn fabric sofa, wrapping his fingers around the hot mug of jasmine tea. He took a slow sip, sighed in deep comfort, and shook his head in pure amazement.

"I still cannot wrap my head around what happened this morning," Chen Mingde murmured, setting the red-stamped clearance paper on the low coffee table. "Last night, Cheng Biao swore up and down that Shanshui Group and Hongrui owned half the county. He laughed in my face and said I would rot in a cell for three years if I did not hand over the shop keys."

"That shameless crook!" Chen Jianguo snapped, slapping his knee.

"Then right after sunrise, the whole station turned upside down," Chen Mingde continued, his voice rising with wonder. "Boots ran up and down the hallway. Someone shouted that provincial anti-corruption officers had sealed the front gate. Ten minutes later, a serious young investigator unlocked my door himself, apologized twice, handed back my belt and wallet, and led me straight out past Cheng Biao, who was sitting on the floor in cuffs crying like a child!"

"Serves him right!" Wu Lanying wiped her wet cheeks with a tissue, letting out a watery laugh. "Heaven really does have eyes!"

Just then, the bedroom door swung open and cousin Chen Yuan burst out in a rumpled t-shirt, holding his phone. He had been up half the night calling old classmates trying to find a lawyer, and had only just heard the commotion in the hallway.

"Dad!" Chen Yuan shouted, crossing the tiny living room in two strides to wrap his arms tightly around Chen Mingde's shoulders. "You are actually home!"

"Easy, easy, you are going to spill my tea," Chen Mingde laughed, patting his son's back.

Chen Yuan let go, blinked rapidly to hide the moisture in his eyes, and looked around the crowded room. His parents were tearful, Uncle Jianguo was pacing with excitement, and Aunt Meifang was already bustling toward the kitchen. Then Chen Yuan's gaze landed on Chen Xuan.

Chen Xuan sat quietly on a small wooden stool near the balcony door, pouring himself a fresh cup of tea from the kettle with steady hands, looking as relaxed as if he had spent the morning strolling through a park.

Chen Yuan stared at his cousin for a second, then burst out laughing. "Wait a minute! Everyone in this room was shaking like leaves on a tree, and look at Chen Xuan! You did not even break a sweat! Earlier you told Uncle Jianguo that Dad would be home at 9:30 AM, and the car practically rolled up on the dot!"

Wu Lanying looked over at Chen Xuan and smiled warmly. "That is true. Even on Old Street, when those county trucks pulled up, my heart almost jumped out of my throat, and Xuan just stood there drinking from his thermos like he knew everything would turn out fine."

"He has always been steady like a rock," Chen Jianguo said proudly, sitting beside his brother. "Ever since Xuan started working in the city, he never panics over small storms."

Chen Yuan walked over and nudged Chen Xuan's shoulder playfully. "Come on, cousin, admit it. Do you have a crystal ball hidden inside that green thermos, or did you just guess lucky?"

Chen Xuan took a calm sip of tea and smiled mildly. "No crystal ball. I saw the news about Shanshui Group on my phone early this morning. Once the big tree falls, the little branches in Yunxi County cannot hold on for another hour. It was only a matter of time before they let Second Uncle go."

"Listen to him," Chen Mingde chuckled, shaking his head fondly. "Calm as an old scholar. Still, Xuan, thank you for staying with your Second Aunt all morning. I know she would have worried herself sick without you."

"We are family, Second Uncle," Chen Xuan said softly.

Wu Lanying stood up briskly, tying a faded blue apron around her waist. "Enough talking about those crooks! Today is a good day, and Mingde has not had a hot meal since yesterday afternoon. Meifang, help me clean the fresh carp in the sink. I am making braised fish and stewed pork ribs!"

"I will wash the greens," Liu Meifang replied happily.

Within minutes, the small sixth-floor apartment filled with the rhythmic tap of a cleaver on wood, the sizzle of ginger hitting hot oil, and warm, relieved laughter. Sitting by the sunlit window with his teacup, Chen Xuan listened to his family bickering happily over how much soy sauce to use, and felt a quiet, deep contentment settle in his chest.
