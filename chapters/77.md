# Chapter 77: Corridor Fourteen

Midnight settled deeply over the fourteenth floor of the Lanshui International Convention Hotel.

Plush burgundy carpeting absorbed every faint sound along the long, symmetrical hallway. Warm amber sconces spaced neatly along the walnut walls cast soft pools of light across closed brass-numbered doors, leaving recessed service alcoves and luggage niches bathed in quiet shadow.

Inside Room 1408, Chen Xuan slept soundly beneath a crisp white duvet.

His washed gray jacket hung neatly over the desk chair beside his folded trousers. On the small writing desk sat a half-eaten paper box of walnut pastries from the bakery near the station, a closed notebook, and the small ceramic jar of Grandma Zhou Guilan's pickled eggs he had not yet finished wrapping for Retired Governor Zhao Dehai's driver. His breathing was slow, deep, and completely peaceful.

At the far eastern end of the fourteenth-floor hallway, the brass latch of the fire stairwell turned without a squeak.

A gloved hand eased the heavy steel door open three inches. A slim matte-black signal injector slid cleanly onto the recessed network junction box beside the stairwell frame. A tiny green LED blinked twice.

In the hotel's basement security room, the fourteenth-floor corridor monitors froze seamlessly on a clean thirty-second recording of an empty, silent hallway.

Four men stepped out of the stairwell in fluid, practiced formation.

They wore dark weatherproof jackets, rubber-soled tactical boots, and matte-black knit gloves. Under their left arms hung compact suppressed nine-millimeter pistols, and clipped to their belts were high-voltage contact stunners, polymer zip-restraints, and weighted blackout hoods. All four were seasoned foreign-contract mercenaries, former special-operations veterans who had spent a decade running quiet extractions across Eastern Europe and Southeast Asia.

Their leader, a broad-shouldered veteran with a pale scar across his cheekbone, paused at the stairwell threshold for two seconds, sweeping the silent corridor with cold, professional eyes.

Empty. No guards. No stray guests. Not even a housekeeping cart.

He raised two gloved fingers and sliced forward through the air.

The four mercenaries glided down the burgundy carpet in staggered file, their rubber soles making less noise than dry leaves. As they passed the central elevator bank thirty meters from Room 1408, the veteran point man tapped his ear-piece twice to confirm clear airwaves.

None of them felt anything change.

They had no way of knowing that the exact step that carried them past the elevator bank had placed all four of them inside the fifty-meter proximity perimeter of the permanent Zero Guard team.

* * *

Ten seconds later, the four mercenaries reached Room 1408.

Two took tight cover against the walnut wall on either side of the doorframe, drawing their suppressed pistols cleanly and keeping the muzzles angled low toward the carpet. The third stepped directly behind the point man with a blackout hood and zip-restraints ready in his gloved hands.

The point man crouched lightly and inserted a silent electronic tension decoder into the keycard mortise of Room 1408.

Inside the brass lock mechanism, three microscopic pins shifted softly. A single faint metallic click whispered into the hallway.

The point man pressed the brass lever downward with practiced gentleness. The heavy oak door yielded smoothly, swinging inward three inches into the dark, silent room where Chen Xuan lay sleeping peacefully ten paces away.

Then the temperature in Corridor Fourteen dropped sharply.

There was no warning shout. No creak of leather. No shift of footsteps on the burgundy carpet.

Out of the narrow recessed shadow of the linen alcove directly behind the rear mercenary, and out of the quiet dark niche three paces ahead of the doorway, four figures materialized as effortlessly as smoke condensing into solid iron.

They were clad head to toe in seamless, non-reflective matte-black tactical gear. Across their faces sat smooth, featureless matte-black masks with no visible eye slits, no insignia, and no human expression.

The scarred mercenary leader registered the sudden presence at the edge of his peripheral vision. His combat reflexes fired instantly, his right hand whipping up to bring his suppressed pistol level.

He was already half a second too late.

* * *

What unfolded in Corridor Fourteen lasted exactly twelve surgical, silent seconds.

Second one through three.

A matte-black hand closed around the slide and barrel of the leader's suppressed pistol before his finger even found the trigger guard. With a single crisp rotational twist that made no sound louder than a folded towel, the pistol wrenched cleanly from his fingers. The veteran's wrist bent backward into an unbreakable reverse lock, forcing him onto his toes before his breath could leave his lungs.

Second four through six.

Beside the doorframe, the second mercenary swung his contact stunner blindly toward the featureless shadow stepping inside his guard. The matte-black figure slipped half an inch past the crackling blue prongs, caught the mercenary's elbow cleanly at the nerve cluster, and drove one precise open-palm strike into the side of his neck. The stunner dropped straight into a gloved black hand before it could touch the carpet.

Second seven through nine.

The point man crouching by the cracked door of Room 1408 tried to lunge backward and draw his sidearm. A silent black shadow dropped directly behind him, one knee pinning his shoulder blade while both wrists were swept behind his spine and locked with a single matte-black titanium cuff. A dark mesh containment hood slipped smoothly over his head, cutting off both sight and sound before a gasp could escape his throat.

Second ten through twelve.

The fourth mercenary, holding the extraction bag in the center of the corridor, never even managed to draw his weapon. Two fluid strikes neutralized his right shoulder and left knee simultaneously. As his legs folded beneath him, a matte-black arm caught his weight cleanly so his body never thudded against the floor.

Four armed, veteran foreign-contract mercenaries.

Disarmed. Joints locked. Hooded. Silenced.

Not a single shot had been fired. Not a single elbow had scraped the walnut paneling. Not one neighboring hotel guest on the fourteenth floor stirred in their sleep.

* * *

One matte-black figure gently eased the open door of Room 1408 back into its brass frame. The deadbolt slid softly into place with a whisper barely audible from three feet away.

Two others lifted the four immobilized, hooded mercenaries effortlessly by their harnesses and vanished silently down the dark service stairwell toward the waiting sealed transport two levels below. The fourth stepped to the eastern junction box, plucked the mercenaries' signal injector cleanly off the wall, and restored the hotel's live hallway camera feed without leaving a single dropped frame.

For three heartbeats, the last featureless matte-black mask stood motionless in the center of the softly lit fourteenth-floor corridor, facing the closed door of Room 1408 with silent, absolute vigilance.

Then one flat, neutral, genderless, chilled voice whispered once into the empty corridor air.

*Zero perimeter violated. Hostiles removed.*

Half a second later, the corridor was completely empty. Only the warm amber wall sconces glowed quietly over untouched burgundy carpet.

Inside Room 1408, Chen Xuan rolled gently onto his left side beneath the soft white duvet, pulled the corner of his blanket comfortably over his shoulder, and slept soundly until morning.
