# Chapter 120: Let the Boy Be Lazy

By nine-thirty that night, the banquet guests had gone home, and the red silk lanterns in the Qin family courtyard cast a gentle golden glow across the stone flagstones and old pomegranate trees.

Out in the quiet garden, a cool night breeze blew down from the northern lakes. Chen Xuan stood beneath the lantern light by the stone table, holding a fresh porcelain cup of hot Yunnan red tea. Seeing Qin Yuhan rub her fingers together in her pale blue coat, Chen Xuan reached out and wrapped both of his large, warm hands around her slender fingers and the warm porcelain teacup at the same time.

"Still cold?" Chen Xuan asked softly.

"A little," Qin Yuhan admitted, not pulling her hands away. Under the warm lantern glow, the cool president of Haiyue Group looked relaxed and soft, her eyes reflecting the light. "When Gu Mingzhe dropped his wine cup at dinner tonight, Second Aunt and Qin Haoran did not dare say another word for two whole hours. How did you know his ministry work phone had an automatic voice monitor?"

"I did not know," Chen Xuan grinned shamelessly, rubbing her fingers until they were warm. "I just made up something that sounded like a strict bank rule so he would stop spitting across our roast duck plate. Who knew the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office actually checks its outer staff so closely?"

Qin Yuhan stared at his earnest face for two seconds before bursting into clear, bright laughter under the pomegranate tree. She shook her head, took a sip of the warm red tea from the cup he was holding, and tapped his nose lightly with her finger.

"You really have an excuse for everything, Clerk Chen," Qin Yuhan teased softly. "First a 680k yuan watch from a lucky draw at Plaza 66, then sixteen-move chess from your Second Uncle Chen Mingde's hardware shop on Old Street, and now a lucky guess at the dinner table."

"When a man lives an honest, lazy life and clocks out at four fifty-five, good luck just follows him around," Chen Xuan chuckled, draping his own warm jacket over her shoulders so the night breeze would not chill her neck. "Come on, drink one more sip and let us go pack. If we catch the Sunday morning flight back to Shanghai early, we still have time to stop by the market near Emerald Riverside Villa and buy fresh river perch for lunch."

"Fine," Qin Yuhan smiled, her cheeks warm under the lantern light. "Wait while I get my scarf from the side room."

Up on the raised wooden veranda outside the main hall, Elder Qin Zhengguo stood leaning lightly on his walnut cane in the shadows, watching his granddaughter and Chen Xuan under the courtyard trees. Beside him stood Qin Zhengming and a still-frowning Lin Shuyin.

Even after everything that had happened today, neither Elder Qin Zhengguo, nor Qin Zhengming, nor Lin Shuyin had the faintest clue about Chen Xuan's true identity. To them, the 300 trillion yuan sovereign reserve backing China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China, and the sweeping corruption clean-up led by Sha Ruijin, Li Dakang, Hou Liangping, and Ji Changming in Handong Province belonged to a high, distant world of national statecraft. In their eyes, the young man standing under the pomegranate tree with his scratched green enamel thermos was simply an ordinary, good-natured, twelve-thousand-yuan logistics clerk from Shanghai.

"Dad, look at him," Lin Shuyin sighed quietly on the veranda, still unable to let go of her Beijing pride. "I admit Chen Xuan is polite, plays decent chess, and got lucky when that foolish Gu Mingzhe ruined himself at dinner. But at the end of the day, he has zero ambition! He earns twelve thousand yuan a month at a small shipping firm near Lujiazui, refuses to apply for a promotion, and only thinks about buying river perch and clocking out at four fifty-five! How can an ordinary clerk like him match our Yuhan?"

Qin Zhengming opened his mouth to remind his wife how Chen Xuan had spotted the shipping fraud in Shanghai, when Elder Qin Zhengguo slowly raised one hand.

The seventy-eight-year-old retired general did not look angry. Instead, his weathered face softened into a deep, warm smile as he watched Qin Yuhan walk back out to the courtyard tree, where Chen Xuan immediately tucked her cashmere scarf neatly around her chin and made her laugh again.

"Shuyin," Elder Qin Zhengguo said quietly in his deep, steady voice. "How long has it been since Yuhan took over Haiyue Group in Shanghai after graduating from Fudan University?"

"Five years, Dad," Lin Shuyin replied.

"Look at Yuhan's face under that lantern right now," Elder Qin Zhengguo said, shaking his silver head gently. "She has not laughed like that in five years. Every time she came back to Beijing before, her shoulders were tight and her eyes were tired. Today, she ate two bowls of rice, drank three cups of tea, and laughed like a twenty-year-old girl."

Lin Shuyin fell silent, looking out at her daughter's bright, unguarded smile in the garden.

"Let the boy be lazy," Elder Qin Zhengguo said warmly, tapping his walnut cane once against the wooden floor. "Our Qin family has enough money, enough titles, and enough busy people staring at balance sheets. We do not need another ambitious climber like Gu Mingzhe trying to use our courtyard as a ladder. We need the man who warms her hands and makes her happy."

Qin Zhengming smiled in relief and nodded. "Dad is right, Shuyin. Stop nagging the kids."

Down under the pomegranate tree, fifty meters away from the silent black car parked outside the alley wall, Chen Xuan picked up his green enamel thermos with one hand, took his wife's warm hand with the other, and strolled happily toward the guest wing under the Beijing stars.
