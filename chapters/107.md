# Chapter 107: The White Card at the Watch Counter

As soon as Qin Yuhan stepped out to the terrace corridor to take her call, Chen Xuan strolled over to the glass counter on the east side of the boutique. The salon shared an open gallery with a Swiss watch counter and a heritage tea cabinet.

Inside the lighted case sat a ladies' wristwatch with a clean mother-of-pearl dial and a navy leather strap. It had no gaudy diamonds, just timeless elegance that matched Qin Yuhan's quiet authority. Beside the watch cabinet stood a cedar box of twenty-year aged Wuyi rock tea.

"Excuse me," Chen Xuan said to the consultant behind the glass. "Could you wrap that mother-of-pearl ladies' watch and this box of aged rock tea along with the suit?"

Before the consultant could unlock the cabinet, a sharp laugh echoed from the doorway.

"Hold on! Do not unlock that case yet, or you will be wiping fingerprints off the glass for nothing!"

Two young men in flashy designer jackets walked in. The one in front was Qin Haoran, Qin Yuhan's twenty-five-year-old cousin from a minor branch of the Qin family, wearing a gold chain over his turtleneck. Beside him swaggered Zhao Kai, the son of a Pudong developer, holding sunglasses indoors.

Qin Haoran looked Chen Xuan up and down, his gaze stopping on the scratched green thermos sitting on the marble VIP table. His lip curled into a sneer.

"Well, look who it is," Qin Haoran drawled loudly. "The famous twelve-thousand-yuan logistics clerk my cousin married out of nowhere. Chen Xuan, right? My Aunt Lin Shuyin told the family yesterday that Yuhan lost her mind and picked up a paper-pusher from Hengxin Logistics."

Zhao Kai leaned against the counter and laughed. "Haoran, is this really the guy? Look at that green enamel tea jug on the table. My grandfather's gatekeeper uses the exact same one! Hey, buddy, do you know how much a single silver tie clip in this case costs? Thirty-eight thousand yuan. That is more than three months of your salary!"

Chen Xuan did not look annoyed at all. He leaned one elbow lazily on the counter and smiled at the consultant. "Are the tie clips really thirty-eight thousand? Good thing I do not like clips poking my shirt."

"Still acting calm while your wife is outside?" Qin Haoran sneered, tapping the glass above the mother-of-pearl watch. "That ladies' watch is 680k yuan, and that cedar box of aged rock tea is 120k yuan. Add the 200k yuan suit on your back, and the total is a clean 1 million yuan. Zhao Kai, I bet you a bottle of scotch he cannot even pay for the ribbon on the box with his own card."

"Easiest bet in Shanghai," Zhao Kai grinned, crossing his arms. "Come on, clerk. Swipe your payroll card. If the machine does not beep insufficient funds, I will carry that green thermos down Nanjing Road for you!"

The consultant hesitated, looking awkwardly between the noisy heirs and Chen Xuan.

Chen Xuan reached into the pocket of his folded trousers, pulled out a plain matte-white card with no printed numbers, and set it lightly on the tray. Across the top corner sat two tiny silver emblems belonging to China Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank.

"Swipe the suit, the watch, and the tea together," Chen Xuan told the consultant mildly. "No password needed."

Qin Haoran snorted. "A blank white card? Did you pull out your cafeteria card by mistake?"

The consultant picked up the matte-white card, noticed its heavy metallic core, and tapped it against the wireless terminal.

Ding!

Instead of a normal receipt, the screen flashed bright gold. At the same second, a silent highest-priority sovereign alert fired directly through the dedicated line linking Plaza 66's executive office and the Lujiazui regional headquarters of Huaxia Bank across the street.

Less than forty seconds later, rapid footsteps thundered down the marble hallway outside.

Plaza 66 General Manager Tang Wenhai and Huaxia Bank Lujiazui Regional Director Zhou Mingyuan sprinted through the boutique entrance so fast both men nearly lost their breath. Before Qin Haoran or Zhao Kai could blink, both senior executives stopped three paces in front of Chen Xuan and bowed at a forty-five-degree angle.

"Honored Supreme Cardholder!" Director Zhou Mingyuan said, his voice trembling with respect as he lifted the matte-white card in both hands. "Huaxia Bank and China Construction Bank have cleared your 1 million yuan transaction at zero fee! As today marks our flagship sovereign appreciation day with Plaza 66, the entire 1 million yuan purchase is covered in full by the bank's sovereign reserve!"

General Manager Tang Wenhai bowed even lower. "Sir, please forgive us for not greeting you at the entrance! I have instructed the boutique to include two complimentary silk shirts and silver cufflinks in your bags!"

Qin Haoran's jaw dropped open, while Zhao Kai's sunglasses clattered onto the marble floor.

"Director Zhou?" Zhao Kai stammered, turning pale. "My father waited two weeks just to have tea with you at Huaxia Bank last month! Why are you bowing to a logistics clerk?!"

Director Zhou Mingyuan turned a freezing glare on Zhao Kai and Qin Haoran. "Watch your tongue! This gentleman holds the top-tier joint clearance card recognized directly by President Liang Qingshan in Beijing! If I hear one more rude word out of either of you, Huaxia Bank will review your family's real estate credit lines before Monday morning!"

Zhao Kai's knees went weak. He grabbed Qin Haoran by the sleeve, stammered a terrified apology, and bolted out the back door without picking up his sunglasses.

Chen Xuan took his matte-white card back, slipped it into his pocket, and picked up his green thermos.

"Thank you both," Chen Xuan said quietly, glancing toward the front door where Qin Yuhan was finishing her call. "Head back to your offices now. My wife does not like noisy shops, and as far as she is concerned, I just hit your mall's tenth-anniversary lucky draw."

"Understood at once, Sir!" Tang Wenhai and Zhou Mingyuan whispered, backing out through the side passage in three seconds flat.

When Qin Yuhan walked back inside, Chen Xuan was already in his polo shirt, holding three gift bags in one hand and his green thermos in the other.

"Did you pay already?" Qin Yuhan asked in surprise, reaching for her purse.

"No need, President Qin," Chen Xuan grinned, handing her a small velvet box. "I swiped my card for a pair of socks, hit the mall's tenth-anniversary lucky draw, and won the suit, tea for your dad, and a watch for my wife."
