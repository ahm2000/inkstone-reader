# Chapter 107: The White Card at the Watch Counter

As soon as Qin Yuhan stepped out to the terrace corridor to take her call, Chen Xuan strolled over to the glass counter on the east side of the boutique. The salon shared an open gallery with a quiet Swiss watch counter and a heritage tea cabinet crafted from dark walnut.

Inside the lighted glass case rested a ladies' wristwatch with a clean mother-of-pearl dial and a slim navy leather strap. It had no loud diamonds or flashy gold trim, only a calm, timeless elegance that matched Qin Yuhan's quiet authority. The small brass tag beside it read 280,000 yuan. Next to the watch display stood a sealed cedar box of twenty-year aged Wuyi rock tea, marked at 120,000 yuan.

Chen Xuan smiled to himself. Yuhan's watch had a small scratch on the clasp from her busy weeks at the office, and her father would certainly appreciate a proper pot of aged rock tea tomorrow evening.

"Excuse me," Chen Xuan said politely to the young consultant behind the counter. "Could you wrap that mother-of-pearl ladies' watch and this cedar box of aged rock tea along with the navy suit from the fitting room?"

The consultant glanced briefly at Chen Xuan's plain polo shirt and the edge of a green enamel thermos peeking from his canvas bag, yet his voice stayed courteous. "Certainly, sir. Allow me to fetch the key."

Before the consultant could unlock the cabinet, a sharp, mocking laugh echoed from the doorway.

"Hold on! Do not unlock that case yet, or you will be wiping fingerprints off the glass for nothing!"

Two young men walked in, both dressed in loud designer logos and heavy cologne. The taller one, Zhou Kai, was the spoiled son of a local property developer who liked to flaunt his family's money around Nanjing Road. He swept a disdainful glance over Chen Xuan's white sneakers and canvas bag.

"Look at what is sticking out of his bag," Zhou Kai sneered, nudging his friend. "A green metal thermos from a hardware store! Friend, this is Plaza 66, not a wholesale market. That watch costs 280,000 yuan, and that tea costs 120,000 yuan. Even if you saved your entire salary for five years, you could not buy the leather strap."

Chen Xuan did not frown or raise his voice. He rested one hand in his pocket and looked at Zhou Kai with mild amusement. "I did not know Plaza 66 hired doorkeepers to inspect shoppers' bags. Still, I asked for the watch first."

Zhou Kai's face darkened. He pulled a glossy black private banking card from his wallet and slapped it onto the counter. "Do you see this? My family spends millions in this mall every year. Consultant, ring up that watch on my card right now. My girlfriend has been eyeing it all month, and I am not letting some subway clerk waste your afternoon."

The consultant stiffened, caught between an arrogant regular and a quiet stranger.

"No need to make things difficult for the staff," Chen Xuan said calmly. He reached into his pocket, pulled out a plain matte-white card, and set it on the velvet tray.

The card had no bank logo, no printed numbers, and no magnetic strip. Across its smooth white surface sat only a tiny raised emblem.

Zhou Kai burst out laughing. "What is that? A plastic hotel keycard from a roadside inn? Are you trying to embarrass yourself in front of the whole floor?"

Before the consultant could speak, the oak door of the back office opened. Tang Ming, the regional director of the salon, stepped out carrying a clipboard. His eyes landed on the velvet tray, and every drop of color drained from his face.

Tang Ming dropped his clipboard onto a chair and hurried across the marble floor. He stopped in front of Chen Xuan and bowed deeply at the waist.

"Distinguished Guest," Tang Ming said, his voice tight with awe. "I did not know you were visiting our gallery today. Please forgive our slow service."

Zhou Kai's smirk vanished. "Director Tang, what are you doing? That guy pulled out a blank white card!"

Tang Ming straightened and turned a freezing stare onto Zhou Kai. "Mr. Zhou, lower your voice at once. That matte-white card carries unconditional global clearance reserved for the highest tier of patrons in the world. Your black bank card does not even belong in the same building."

The gallery fell silent. Zhou Kai's friend took a step backward.

"Apologize to our Distinguished Guest immediately," Tang Ming said coldly, "or I will cancel your family's salon privileges across Shanghai this afternoon."

Seeing the genuine terror in the director's eyes, Zhou Kai realized he had provoked someone far beyond his reach. His face burned red as he stiffly lowered his head. "I am sorry. I spoke out of turn."

"Forget it," Chen Xuan said mildly. "Just let people shop in peace next time."

Zhou Kai snatched his black card off the glass and hurried out with his friend trailing behind.

Once they were gone, Chen Xuan slipped the matte-white card back into his pocket. "Director Tang, keep this low-key. My wife is outside on a work call. Put the rock tea in a plain paper bag, and print a simple receipt for the suit and watch showing a seasonal clearance bundle. Do not mention this card to anyone."

"Understood, Distinguished Guest," Tang Ming replied, bowing again. "Not a single word will leave this counter."

Five minutes later, when Qin Yuhan returned from the terrace corridor, the tailored navy suit was packed in a garment bag, and Chen Xuan stood waiting with a small navy velvet box in his hand.

"What is that?" Qin Yuhan asked softly.

"A little gift for you," Chen Xuan said with an easy smile, opening the box to reveal the mother-of-pearl wristwatch. "The store was running an autumn promotion with the suit, so the price was much lower than usual. Try it on."

Qin Yuhan stared at the refined dial, her heart warming instantly. She knew no Swiss watch was truly cheap, even on promotion, and assumed her husband had dipped deeply into his savings just to see her smile. She let him fasten the navy leather strap around her wrist, her fingers lingering gently against his sleeve as they walked out together.
