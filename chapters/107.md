# Chapter 107: The White Card at the Watch Counter

At the east side of the open gallery, the quiet tailoring salon gave way to a softly lit Swiss watch counter and a heritage tea cabinet crafted from dark walnut. Chen Xuan paused beside the polished glass display, his canvas bag resting lightly against his knee while he waited for his trousers to be hemmed.

Inside the lighted glass case rested a ladies' wristwatch with a clean mother-of-pearl dial and a slim navy leather strap. It had no loud diamonds or flashy gold trim, only a calm, timeless elegance that matched Qin Yuhan's quiet poise. The small brass tag beside it read 280,000 yuan. Next to the watch display stood a sealed cedar box of twenty-year aged Wuyi rock tea, marked at 120,000 yuan.

Chen Xuan smiled to himself. He had noticed a faint scratch on the clasp of Qin Yuhan's old wristwatch earlier in the week, left from her long days signing files at Haiyue Group. As for the aged rock tea, her father had spent decades working in Beijing ministries and would certainly appreciate a proper pot of Wuyi tea tomorrow evening.

"Excuse me," Chen Xuan said politely to the young consultant behind the counter. "Could you wrap that mother-of-pearl ladies' watch and this cedar box of aged rock tea along with the navy suit from the fitting room?"

The consultant glanced briefly at Chen Xuan's plain polo shirt and the rim of a green enamel thermos peeking from his half-zipped canvas bag, yet his training kept his voice courteous. "Certainly, sir. Allow me to fetch the key."

Before the consultant could unlock the cabinet, a sharp, mocking laugh echoed from the archway behind them.

"Hold on! Do not unlock that case yet, or you will be wiping fingerprints off the glass for nothing!"

Two young men strolled in, both dressed in loud designer logos and heavy cologne. The taller one, Zhou Kai, was the spoiled son of a local property developer who liked to flaunt his family's money around Nanjing Road. He swept a disdainful glance over Chen Xuan's white sneakers and faded canvas bag.

"Look at what is sticking out of his bag," Zhou Kai sneered, nudging his companion. "A green metal thermos from a hardware store! Friend, this is Plaza 66, not a wholesale market. That watch is 280,000 yuan, and that cedar box of tea is 120,000 yuan. Even if you saved every yuan for five years, you still could not pay for the clasp."

Chen Xuan did not frown or raise his voice. He rested one hand casually in his pocket and looked at Zhou Kai with mild amusement. "I did not know Plaza 66 hired doorkeepers to inspect shoppers' bags. Still, I asked for the watch first."

Zhou Kai's face darkened. He pulled a glossy black private banking card from his wallet and slapped it onto the glass counter. "Do you see this? My family spends millions in this mall every year. Consultant, ring up that watch on my card right now. My girlfriend has been eyeing it all month, and I am not letting some subway clerk waste your afternoon."

The consultant stiffened, his hand hovering near the cabinet lock as he glanced uneasily between an arrogant regular and a quiet stranger.

"No need to make things difficult for the staff," Chen Xuan said calmly. He reached into his trouser pocket, pulled out a plain matte-white card, and set it lightly on the velvet tray.

The card had no bank logo, no printed numbers, and no magnetic strip. Across its smooth white surface sat only a tiny raised emblem.

Zhou Kai burst out laughing. "What is that? A plastic hotel keycard from a roadside inn? Are you trying to embarrass yourself in front of the whole floor?"

Before the consultant could speak, the oak door of the back office swung open. Tang Ming, the regional director of the salon, stepped out carrying a clipboard. His eyes landed on the velvet tray, and every drop of color drained from his face.

Tang Ming dropped his clipboard onto a side chair and hurried across the marble floor. He stopped directly in front of Chen Xuan and bowed deeply at the waist.

"Distinguished Guest," Tang Ming said, his voice tight with awe. "I did not know you were visiting our gallery today. Please forgive our slow service."

Zhou Kai's smirk vanished at once. "Director Tang, what are you doing? That guy pulled out a blank white card!"

Tang Ming straightened and turned a freezing stare onto Zhou Kai. "Mr. Zhou, lower your voice immediately. That matte-white card carries unconditional global clearance reserved for the highest tier of patrons in the world. Your black bank card does not even belong in the same building."

The gallery fell dead silent. Zhou Kai's companion quietly took a step backward, suddenly eager not to be associated with him.

"Apologize to our Distinguished Guest right now," Tang Ming said coldly, "or I will cancel your family's salon privileges across Shanghai this afternoon."

Seeing the genuine dread in the director's eyes, Zhou Kai realized he had provoked someone far beyond his family's reach. His face burned red as he stiffly lowered his head. "I am sorry. I spoke out of turn."

"Forget it," Chen Xuan said mildly. "Just let people shop in peace next time."

Zhou Kai snatched his black card off the glass and hurried out through the archway with his friend trailing close behind.

Once they were gone, Chen Xuan slipped the matte-white card back into his pocket. "Director Tang, keep this low-key. My wife is outside on a work call. Put the rock tea in a plain paper bag, print a simple receipt for the suit and watch showing a seasonal clearance bundle, and do not mention this card to anyone."

"Understood, Distinguished Guest," Tang Ming replied, bowing again. "Not a single word will leave this counter."

Five minutes later, when Qin Yuhan returned from the terrace corridor after finishing her call with Lin Wei, the tailored navy suit was neatly packed in a garment bag. Beside Chen Xuan's canvas bag sat a plain kraft paper bag, and in his hand rested a small navy velvet box.

"You picked up tea too?" Qin Yuhan asked softly, glancing at the paper bag before her eyes fell on the velvet box. "And what is that?"

"The tea is for your father tomorrow," Chen Xuan said with an easy smile, opening the velvet box to reveal the mother-of-pearl wristwatch. "And this is a little gift for you. The store was running an autumn promotion with the suit, so the price was much lower than usual. Try it on."

Qin Yuhan stared at the refined dial, her heart warming instantly. She knew no Swiss watch was truly cheap, even on promotion, and assumed her husband had dipped deeply into his savings just to see her smile. She extended her wrist without arguing, letting him fasten the navy leather strap gently before her fingers lingered against his sleeve as they walked out together.
