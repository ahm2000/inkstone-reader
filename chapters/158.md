# Chapter 158: Business Trip to Nanhai

Tuesday morning at Hengxin Logistics began with the usual clatter of keyboards and the faint smell of instant coffee drifting from the break corner.

Chen Xuan sat at his corner desk near the window in a clean white cotton shirt and dark trousers. Beside his elbow stood his scratched green thermos, filled with hot jasmine tea. Tucked neatly under his chair rested his faded gray canvas travel bag.

He had just stamped his twelfth shipping slip when the glass door of the manager's office flew open with a loud bang.

General Manager Sun Haoran rushed out, his face pale and shining with sweat. His tie hung crookedly, and he clutched a stack of red-stamped cargo notices in one hand while dabbing his forehead with a crumpled handkerchief in the other.

"Everybody stop what you are doing!" Sun Haoran called out breathlessly. "Dispatch team, over to the center table right now!"

Heads snapped up across the room. Hu Dewei slowly set down his teacup with a cautious frown. Two desks away, Zhang Pengfei minimized a stock chart on his screen and strolled over. Chen Xuan capped his blue pen, picked up his green thermos, and walked calmly to the table.

Sun Haoran slapped the red-stamped notices onto the tabletop.

"We have a serious problem down south," Sun Haoran said, wiping his brow. "Haiyue Group's flagship ship, the Haiyue Star, docked at Nanhai Pier Nine yesterday morning. Two hours later, the Nanhai Port Inspection Office locked the pier gate and slapped a safety hold on the whole vessel."

Hu Dewei's eyebrows shot up. "Nanhai Pier Nine? Isn't that the Shen Family's turf?"

"Yes, it is," Sun Haoran groaned, rubbing his temples. "Six of our containers are on that deck, carrying precision parts for Yongcheng Machinery in Nanhai City. If they do not arrive by Thursday afternoon, we face 600k yuan in fines and lose a 2 million yuan yearly contract!"

Silence fell over the table. Everyone knew that when the Shen Family locked a pier gate, ordinary clerks who went down to argue came back humiliated.

Sun Haoran looked around with urgent hope. "Haiyue Group's president is flying down at noon. I cannot leave because the tax auditors arrive at two. I need someone reliable to catch the noon flight, meet Haiyue's team at Pier Nine, and fight for our six containers. Who can go?"

Not a single hand moved.

Sun Haoran turned straight to Hu Dewei, the senior supervisor. "Old Hu, you have twelve years in logistics. You know how southern port clerks talk. Can you take the noon flight?"

Before Sun Haoran finished the last word, Hu Dewei's face twisted in sudden, dramatic agony. He clapped both hands onto his lower back and hissed sharply through his teeth.

"Aiya! Oh, my waist!" Hu Dewei groaned, bending halfway over the table as if his legs might buckle. "General Manager Sun, I would fly down in a heartbeat, truly! It is just my old lower-back slip. It flared up terribly last night. I could barely tie my shoes this morning!"

He gave two weak coughs and squinted painfully at the ceiling. "My doctor warned me that sitting in a cramped plane seat for three hours could lock up my spine. If I collapse at Nanhai Airport, I would only hold up the company's work!"

Sun Haoran stared at Hu Dewei's hunched shoulders, his mouth twitching in frustration. Ten minutes ago, Hu Dewei had climbed three flights of stairs carrying a box of sweet buns without breathing hard. Still, Sun Haoran could not force a man claiming a bad back onto a plane.

Sun Haoran turned sharply to Zhang Pengfei. "Pengfei, you are young and quick. You know Yongcheng's slips. Pack your bag and go."

Zhang Pengfei's eyes widened in alarm. He spun back to his desk, snatched up two thick blue binders, and hurried back waving them in the air.

"General Manager Sun, I wish I could!" Zhang Pengfei said rapidly, putting on an earnest, overworked face. "Look at these files. I have three urgent Qingyan warehouse checks this afternoon, plus Manager Zhou from Dongfang Hardware is coming at three to argue over last month's billing. He will only talk to me! If I fly south today, Dongfang Hardware might pull their account. I am completely swamped!"

Hu Dewei nodded solemnly from his hunched pose. "True, General Manager Sun. Pengfei has barely had time for lunch all week. Maybe we should send someone whose desk is lighter."

Both Hu Dewei and Zhang Pengfei glanced slyly toward Chen Xuan.

In Hu Dewei's mind, Nanhai Pier Nine was a brick wall. Whoever flew south today would get shouted out of the gate, come back with nothing, and take the blame for the 600k yuan fine. Better Chen Xuan than him.

Sun Haoran let out a defeated sigh. "Forget it. Chen Xuan only handles routine filing. Sending him alone into Nanhai Port is too much. I will call the tax office, beg to push the audit to Friday, and go myself."

As Sun Haoran reached for his phone, Chen Xuan calmly raised his right hand. In his left hand, he held his scratched green thermos lightly.

"I haven't seen the ocean in a few years, General Manager Sun. I will go."

The office froze.

Sun Haoran blinked in surprise. "Chen Xuan? Are you sure? The Shen Family people down there are rough, and the port inspectors do not listen to polite words."

"I know," Chen Xuan replied with an easy smile. "I checked the loading sheets for those six containers on Friday. I know every seal number by heart. Since I already know the paperwork, it makes sense for me to go. Besides, Haiyue Group's team will be at the gate too."

Hu Dewei nearly straightened his back in delight, then quickly winced again. "Ah, Chen Xuan, what a fine young man! Always stepping up! General Manager Sun, let him go!"

Zhang Pengfei clapped Chen Xuan on the shoulder, hiding a smug grin. "Brother Chen, that is real team spirit! I will watch your desk while you are away. Just remember to speak softly to those southern inspectors!"

"Thank you, Pengfei," Chen Xuan said mildly. "Take good care of those two blue binders."

Zhang Pengfei's smile stiffened for half a second before he laughed awkwardly and stepped back.

Sun Haoran looked at Chen Xuan with deep relief. "Thank you, Chen Xuan! I will have the front desk book you on the noon flight right now, and draw 5,000 yuan in travel cash. Go home first to pack, then take a cab to the airport."

"No need to go home," Chen Xuan said pleasantly, slipping the six container notices into his cloth folder and lifting his faded gray canvas bag from under his desk. "I happened to bring a spare shirt today."

Sun Haoran laughed gratefully. "Always prepared! Go on, the front desk will text your ticket code in five minutes!"

Chen Xuan slung the bag over his shoulder, nodded politely, and walked out.

The moment the glass door clicked shut, Hu Dewei straightened his spine completely without a single groan and poured himself fresh tea.

"Poor fool," Hu Dewei chuckled quietly. "By five o'clock, Shen Jingzhou's guards will chase him straight back to the airport."

Zhang Pengfei smirked. "Let him go, Brother Hu. At least our backs and desks are safe."

Downstairs in the morning sun, Chen Xuan stepped into a waiting taxi, rested his green thermos on his lap, and headed for Qingyan Airport.
