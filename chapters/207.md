# Chapter 207: Lunch at Haiyue Tower

The noon sun reflected brilliantly off the curved glass curtain wall of Haiyue Financial Center as Chen Xuan stepped through the revolving entrance doors.

He wore his usual light blue dress shirt with the top button comfortably unfastened, dark trousers, and clean black leather shoes. In his left hand he carried his scratched green metal thermos, while his right hand held a red insulated tote bag containing a three-tiered thermal lunchbox he had packed that morning.

In the bustling marble lobby, hundreds of impeccably dressed corporate executives, investment bankers, and legal consultants were hurrying toward the turnstiles for their lunch hour.

Chen Xuan did not join the long queues at the security barriers. He strolled casually toward the executive reception desk on the east side of the atrium.

The head receptionist, a poised young woman named Xiao Tang who had worked at Haiyue for three years, recognized him immediately. Her professional demeanor instantly melted into a warm, respectful smile.

"Good afternoon, Mr. Chen!" Xiao Tang greeted him cheerfully, coming around the side of the marble counter. "You brought lunch for President Qin again today?"

"Good afternoon, Xiao Tang," Chen Xuan smiled, holding up the insulated bag with an easy chuckle. "Braised beef brisket with winter radishes and steamed egg custard. If she eats takeout salad from the executive cafeteria every day, her stomach will start complaining by Thursday."

"President Qin is so fortunate to have you," Xiao Tang beamed, producing an engraved gold-trimmed access card from behind the desk. "She just finished her morning board session and went up to her private office. The private express elevator is waiting for you on the north bank."

"Thank you, Xiao Tang. Here, have an apple," Chen Xuan reached into his tote bag and handed her a crisp red Fuji apple he had washed that morning. "Fresh from the fruit stall by the subway."

"Thank you so much, Mr. Chen!" Xiao Tang giggled, cradling the apple with genuine delight as she tapped the card to open the VIP glass gate.

Chen Xuan nodded politely, stepped into the gleaming private elevator, and pressed the button for the 58th floor.

The elevator climbed silently and rapidly, the digital floor indicator flashing past 20, 30, 40, and 50.

At the 55th floor, the doors chimed softly and slid open to allow a group of senior vice presidents to step off into the executive lounge corridor.

Through the opening, Chen Xuan caught sight of two familiar figures sitting stiffly on the leather sofa in the waiting lounge: General Manager Sun Haoran and Department Manager Hu Dewei.

Sun Haoran was holding a set of laminated presentation charts, his lips moving silently as he rehearsed his opening remarks, while Hu Dewei was anxiously checking his reflection in his dark phone screen, wiping nervous sweat from his forehead.

Chen Xuan blinked in mild surprise, amused by the coincidence. He did not step out or call out to them. The elevator doors closed smoothly after three seconds, continuing its climb toward the 58th floor.

Up on the top floor, the atmosphere was quiet, spacious, and filled with natural light.

Chen Xuan walked past the reception foyer, where Assistant Secretary Lin Wei was reviewing afternoon appointment schedules.

"Hello, Secretary Lin," Chen Xuan said with a gentle nod.

Lin Wei looked up from her computer, her eyes widening with a polite smile. "Mr. Chen! Welcome. President Qin is at her desk reviewing the afternoon files. Please go right in."

"Thank you. Has she had any water today?" Chen Xuan asked.

"Only one cup of coffee this morning," Lin Wei admitted with a guilty shrug. "She was in meetings from nine until eleven-thirty."

Chen Xuan shook his head with fond exasperation, tapped his green thermos, and pushed open the heavy frosted glass door of the president's inner sanctuary.

Inside, the vast corner office offered a breathtaking 270-degree panorama of the Huangpu River and the Shanghai skyline.

Qin Yuhan was seated behind her broad smoked-glass desk, her suit jacket draped over the back of her chair, her slender fingers typing rapidly on her keyboard as she scanned a maritime clearance report.

Hearing the door open, she began without looking up in her crisp executive tone, "Secretary Lin, did the Ningbo port authority confirm the afternoon docking slot, "

She paused, catching the unmistakable, mouthwatering aroma of braised beef and star anise drifting across the office.

Qin Yuhan lifted her head. When she saw Chen Xuan standing by the door holding the red lunch tote and his dented green thermos, all the tension melted from her brow, and her face lit up like morning sunshine.

"Husband!" Qin Yuhan cheered, pushing her chair back and practically running around the desk in her stockinged feet, leaving her high heels behind on the carpet. "You actually cooked braised brisket today?"

Chen Xuan caught her in his arms as she threw herself against him, wrapping her arms tightly around his neck with the happy energy of a schoolgirl.

"Careful, Madam President," Chen Xuan laughed, holding the lunchbox high with one hand while steadying her waist with the other. "If you spill hot radish soup all over that carpet, your cleaning staff will send me the bill."

"Let them send it," Qin Yuhan giggled, standing on tiptoes to plant a warm, lingering kiss on his cheek. "I have been smelling bitter black coffee since eight in the morning. Feed me right now before I collapse!"
