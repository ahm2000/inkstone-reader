# Chapter 104: The Mother-in-Law's Video Call from Beijing

On Sunday evening at Emerald Riverside Villa, the living room lamps cast a warm amber glow across the plush cream sofa. Outside the tall glass doors, a cool autumn wind whispered through the riverside reeds, making the quiet warmth inside feel even cozier.

Chen Xuan leaned back comfortably against the cushions in a plain white cotton T-shirt and soft drawstring house pants. In his lap rested a porcelain bowl of fresh Jiangxi navel oranges. With unhurried, practiced fingers, he peeled the bright rind off a plump orange in one unbroken spiral, plucked away every thin white thread of pith, and separated a juicy wedge.

Curled right beside him with her legs tucked under a light cashmere throw, Qin Yuhan was scrolling through a quarterly shipping report on her tablet. Without even taking her eyes off the screen, she opened her mouth slightly.

Chen Xuan popped the sweet orange wedge straight in.

"Sweet?" he asked, peeling another thin thread of pith off the next slice.

"Very sweet," Qin Yuhan murmured contentedly, chewing the juicy fruit. "I swear oranges taste twice as good when someone else takes all the white strings off."

Before Chen Xuan could feed her another slice, a lively chime rang out from her tablet. A video call banner flashed across the top of the screen: Mom.

Qin Yuhan sat up instantly, smoothing her loose dark hair behind her ears and setting the cashmere blanket neatly over her knees. "It is my mother calling from Beijing," she whispered with a quick grin before tapping the green button.

The screen brightened to reveal the warm study of the Qin family courtyard home in Beijing. Lin Shuyin sat in a dark silk cardigan beside a porcelain vase of autumn chrysanthemums, her posture graceful and alert.

"Yuhan, dear, how are you feeling this week?" Lin Shuyin asked warmly. Her sharp maternal eyes immediately scanned her daughter's face, then widened slightly in pleasant surprise. "Wait a moment. Your complexion looks wonderful. Have you actually been sleeping on time instead of staying at Haiyue Tower until midnight?"

"Every single night, Mom," Qin Yuhan smiled, leaning slightly so Chen Xuan came into view beside her. "My husband refuses to let me bring work to the dinner table, and he makes sure I eat hot soup every evening."

Chen Xuan leaned politely into the camera frame with an easy smile. "Good evening, Mom. The weather in Beijing must be getting chilly. Are you and Dad keeping warm?"

Lin Shuyin looked at Chen Xuan's plain white T-shirt, his relaxed smile, and the bowl of peeled oranges in his lap. In her mind, just like the rest of the Qin family, Chen Xuan was simply a warm-hearted, ordinary 12,000-yuan logistics clerk who had somehow won her daughter's heart.

"We are doing fine, Xiao Xuan," Lin Shuyin said gently. "I am glad to see you taking such good care of Yuhan. She used to live on black coffee and cold sandwiches whenever a big shipping contract came up."

She paused for a beat, folding her hands on the desk. "Still, Xiao Xuan, now that you two are married, have you thought about your own career? Working on the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics is steady, of course, but you are still young. If you want to take an evening management course or move into Haiyue Group, your father and I can help arrange it."

Qin Yuhan opened her mouth to defend him, knowing Chen Xuan hated being pushed into corporate politics, but Chen Xuan merely patted her knee gently and answered with calm, cheerful honesty.

"Thank you for thinking of me, Mom," Chen Xuan said easily. "Honestly, I really like my desk at Hengxin. My manager is fair, my coworkers are friendly, and the best part is that I clock out right at 4:55 PM. If I took a stressful executive job, we would both come home exhausted and end up ordering cold takeout. This way, Yuhan handles the big ships, and I handle the warm kitchen."

Lin Shuyin blinked, half-ready to lecture him about ambition, yet she could not find a single flaw in the serene smile on her daughter's face.

Just then, a broad shoulder appeared behind Lin Shuyin on the screen. Qin Zhengming chuckled softly, followed closely by the hearty, booming voice of Elder Qin Zhengguo, who stepped into view wearing a dark woolen vest over a crisp white shirt.

"Shuyin, stop nagging the boy!" Elder Qin laughed, waving a weathered hand toward the camera. "What is wrong with clocking out at 4:55 PM? When I was stationed at the border, I would have traded half my medals for a quiet evening and a hot pot of braised pork ribs."

Elder Qin leaned closer to the lens, his bright eyes crinkling with mischief. "Xiao Xuan, do not listen to her. You beat me in two out of three chess games last month, and I have been studying new openings ever since. When are you bringing Yuhan back to Beijing so I can win back my honor?"

"Whenever Yuhan has a free weekend, Grandpa," Chen Xuan replied with a warm grin. "And I promise to cook a big pot of pork ribs before we set up the chessboard."

"Now that is what I like to hear!" Elder Qin declared cheerfully.

Qin Zhengming smiled beside his wife, resting a steady hand on her shoulder. "Look at Yuhan's face, Shuyin. Have you seen her smile this freely in the last five years? Let the young ones live at their own pace."

Lin Shuyin looked back at the screen, where Qin Yuhan was leaning lightly against Chen Xuan's arm, looking relaxed and radiant. A soft sigh escaped her lips, followed by a genuine, warm smile.

"All right, all right, three against one," Lin Shuyin admitted gracefully. "Xiao Xuan, keep feeding her well. And both of you dress warmly as the autumn air gets colder."

After a few more minutes of family chatter and warm goodbyes, the call ended. The living room fell peacefully quiet again. Qin Yuhan set her tablet on the coffee table, curled back under the cashmere blanket, and rested her head comfortably against Chen Xuan's shoulder.

"Sorry about my mom," she murmured softly. "She still worries too much."

"She loves you," Chen Xuan said, lifting another peeled orange slice to her lips. "Now open up. One more wedge before the kettle boils for tea."

Qin Yuhan smiled, took the sweet slice, and closed her eyes in complete peace.
