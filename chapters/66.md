# Chapter 66: The Invitation to Lanshui

Mist curled silently through the black pines surrounding the quiet eastern courtyard on the edge of Qingyan City.

Inside the low timber study, Number One sat behind a bare rosewood desk beneath a single brass lamp. The room smelled faintly of cold ink and wet stone. Everything in Qingyan City had seemed calm since Yunxi Old Street reopened and Wang Lei signed his clean freight contract with Haiyue Group.

Then a matte-black terminal recessed into the eastern wall emitted a single low chime.

Number One stood immediately, his spine straightening with automatic reverence. That terminal did not belong to the Black Pine Guard network, and it did not accept replies. It was a one-way automated relay from the permanent Zero Guard team shadowing Chen Xuan's fifty-meter perimeter.

Across the dark glass scrolled four lines of stark crimson text.

*Perimeter Log. 17:04. East Avenue, four hundred meters east of Hengxin Logistics. Inner fifty-meter boundary breached by two armed foreign reconnaissance operatives carrying passive micro-telemetry hardware. Targets neutralized and transferred to sealed holding. Link traced to dual-passport fixer Li Weiming, alias Marcus Vance, Northern Ledger Asia-Pacific Desk.*

Number One stood motionless for five full seconds, his gaze locked on the glowing crimson characters.

"The Northern Ledger," Number One murmured, his voice low and dry. "They lost one hundred trillion yuan and thirty-two ministerial assets in one night, and instead of running back to Zurich, they sent a fixer straight to Qingyan City."

Li Weiming had bypassed every provincial intermediary and pointed two trained trackers directly at Hengxin Logistics. The permanent Zero Guard team had erased the threat in ten seconds before Chen Xuan even reached his bus stop, proving the overseas syndicate had locked onto Qingyan City.

* * *

Before twenty minutes had passed, brisk footsteps crossed the damp stone veranda outside.

Number Fourteen entered the study without knocking, closed the door quietly, and offered a crisp salute. In his gloved hands he carried a cream-colored envelope embossed with gold lettering alongside a folded intelligence brief.

"Number One," Number Fourteen reported cleanly. "A second development just arrived from Hengxin Logistics."

Number One took the folder under the brass lamp. "Speak."

"Forty minutes ago, the Lanshui Provincial Commerce and Logistics Federation issued a formal invitation to Hengxin Logistics," Number Fourteen said. "They requested that Hengxin Logistics send its top Employee of the Quarter, Chen Xuan, to next week's Lanshui Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum in the provincial capital as a regional freight representative."

Number One turned the page slowly. "How did Hengxin Logistics respond?"

"General Manager Sun Haoran and Manager Hu Dewei were thrilled," Number Fourteen replied. "They assumed the invitation came from the Haiyue Group contract and Yunxi Old Street's smooth reopening. Manager Hu Dewei immediately booked a high-speed train ticket and approved a travel allowance. Chen Xuan accepted politely without objection."

Number One flipped to the second page, where the verified VIP guest list for the provincial forum had been printed in clean black columns.

His eyes moved down the roster of provincial heavyweights, banking directors, and foreign capital delegations. Near the top of the European Investment Panel sat three names highlighted in red ink.

Marcus Vance, also known as Li Weiming, Senior Restructuring Partner. Beside him stood two senior regional directors of the Northern Ledger's Asian clearing trust, flying into the provincial capital from Singapore and Zurich.

"A trap dressed up as a provincial honor," Number One said softly. "Every remaining backer of the Northern Ledger intends to inspect the man from Hengxin Logistics up close."

Number Fourteen's jaw tightened slightly. "Number Seven and Number Nineteen are already in the provincial capital securing observation points around the forum hotel. If you give the order, we can quietly have the federation withdraw Hengxin Logistics' slot tonight so Chen Xuan stays in Qingyan City."

Number One closed the cream-colored folder with a soft, decisive tap.

"Never," Number One said calmly. "We do not cancel Chen Xuan's train ticket, and we do not interfere with his job at Hengxin Logistics unless he asks. If the Northern Ledger wants to gather its European directors, its dual-passport fixer, and its remaining provincial protectors inside one banquet hall, it saves us the trouble of hunting them across four continents."

He looked up at Number Fourteen, his eyes cold and steady.

"Have Number Eleven and Number Twelve verify every provincial official attending that forum. Tell Number Fifty-six to keep the outer ring invisible. Next week, the provincial capital is going to learn how heavy an ordinary office worker really is."

"Understood," Number Fourteen replied, saluting sharply before slipping back into the evening mist.

* * *

Three kilometers away, inside Room 601 of the old residential building, warm yellow light glowed above the dining table.

The evening news murmured softly from the living room television, where Chen Jianguo sat peeling a crisp red apple into one unbroken spiral.

At the dining table, Tang Xiaoyu chewed the end of her pencil, frowning at a geometry worksheet. Beside her elbow sat a freshly refilled ceramic bowl of Liu Meifang's lotus-root and pork-rib soup, fragrant with ginger and sweet dates.

Chen Xuan sat calmly beside her in a soft cotton pullover, his washed gray jacket draped over his chair. He tapped his finger gently next to an unmarked triangle on Tang Xiaoyu's page.

"Look at the parallel line at the top, Tang Xiaoyu," Chen Xuan said gently, his voice warm and unhurried. "You already solved the inner angle on the left. If these two lines never cross, what does that tell you about the angle sitting opposite it?"

Tang Xiaoyu blinked, stared at the diagram for three seconds, and brightened. "They are equal! Then I subtract both from one hundred and eighty!"

"That is right," Chen Xuan smiled approvingly. "Write it out step by step so your teacher can see how clean your work is."

Liu Meifang walked out from the kitchen wiping her hands on a floral apron, smiling fondly.

"Chen Xuan, stop staring at math problems for a minute and drink your soup while it is hot," Liu Meifang urged, nudging the bowl closer. "I simmered those pork ribs for two hours. Also, did General Manager Sun Haoran really say you have to travel to the provincial capital next week?"

Chen Xuan lifted his porcelain spoon, blew gently across the golden broth, and took a slow, appreciative sip.

"Yes, Mom," Chen Xuan replied warmly. "Just a three-day logistics forum hosted by the provincial federation. The company covers the train ticket and the hotel, and they said the breakfast buffet is free."

"Look at our son, going to provincial conferences now," Chen Jianguo chuckled from the sofa. "Pack a warmer sweater. The wind in the provincial capital is sharper than here in Qingyan City."

"I will, Dad," Chen Xuan said easily. "And before I come back, I will pick up two boxes of walnut pastries from the old bakery near the station for Grandpa Chen Fugen, Grandma Zhou Guilan, and Tang Xiaoyu."

"Save the sesame ones for me!" Tang Xiaoyu called out happily, finishing her equation with a flourish.

On the corner of the table, Chen Xuan's plain black phone vibrated once with an automated confirmation of his Monday morning high-speed rail seat to the provincial capital.

Beyond the sixth-floor window, heavy autumn clouds rolled across the dark northern sky above the highway leading out of Qingyan City.

Inside Room 601, Chen Xuan set the phone face down, picked up a red pen to draw a neat checkmark beside Tang Xiaoyu's finished homework, and took another warm spoonful of Liu Meifang's pork-rib soup. He smiled peacefully, completely unbothered by the storm gathering in the provincial capital.
