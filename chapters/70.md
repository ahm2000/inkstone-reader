# Chapter 70: The Convoy on the Yunxi Highway

At eight fifteen on Monday morning, the red-and-white barrier arms at the Yunxi County expressway tollgate stood locked straight upright. Two tollbooth attendants leaned from their windows, mouths open, watching the black column sweep through the open lanes without a single vehicle slowing for a ticket.

Eighteen unmarked black provincial CCDI sedans led the formation, followed tightly by four armored counter-terrorism vans and six municipal patrol vehicles from neighboring districts. No sirens sounded. No roof bars flashed. Only the steady roar of twenty-eight engines rolled down the highway toward Yunxi County.

Inside the back seat of the lead sedan, Provincial CCDI Secretary Shen Wenchao and Provincial PSB Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan sat side by side, manila folders spread across their knees. The cabin smelled faintly of stale tobacco and cold green tea. Up front, the silent driver kept his eyes locked on the road.

"We clear the river overpass in five minutes," Han Zhiyuan said, glancing at the dash clock before opening the top binder. "Technical division just confirmed on the encrypted unit. Yunxi County PSB's dispatch repeaters are copied and ready to drop to dead static the moment we cross the county line."

"Good," Shen Wenchao replied, eyes scanning the evidence summary in his lap. "Walk me through the five core pillars of the Yunxi docket one last time before we split columns. I want zero loose nails when we put Sun Weiming and Cheng Biao in front of the cameras."

Han Zhiyuan turned to Tab One and tapped two glossy radiology prints clipped side by side. "Pillar One: Chen Mingde's forged hospital scan. Yesterday afternoon, Yunxi County People's Hospital Deputy Director Peng Shulin altered the digital metadata on a chest X-ray taken three weeks ago from an unrelated traffic accident patient. Peng Shulin stamped Chen Mingde's name and yesterday's date onto two cracked ribs to fabricate an aggravated assault charge against the hardware shopkeeper."

Shen Wenchao's mouth tightened into a thin line. "Do we hold the original radiology server log?"

"Pulled straight from the provincial medical insurance cloud at four o'clock," Han Zhiyuan confirmed crisply. "The raw file hash does not match the print in Cheng Biao's folder. Peng Shulin signed the altered diagnostic report seventeen minutes after Cheng Biao called his private phone. That puts Peng Shulin and Cheng Biao away for fabricating criminal evidence."

"Move to Pillar Two," Shen Wenchao said, holding an unlit cigarette between his fingers.

Han Zhiyuan flipped to Tab Two, revealing a yellow digital audit sheet. "Pillar Two: Yunxi County PSB Director Cheng Biao's twelve-minute-backdated warrant. After Yan Tiehu's thugs failed to force Chen Mingde into selling his storefront on the old commercial street, Cheng Biao sent two patrol vans to haul Chen Mingde from his shop without a valid summons. Twelve minutes after Chen Mingde was already locked inside county intake, Cheng Biao logged into the provincial case terminal with his director key and backdated the criminal detention warrant by exactly twelve minutes."

"Twelve minutes," Shen Wenchao repeated coldly, disgust tightening his jaw. "Did Cheng Biao truly think provincial PSB servers fail to log an immutable timestamp on every keystroke?"

"Eleven years of playing little emperor in Yunxi County rots a man's brain," Han Zhiyuan muttered, jaw flexing. "He figured nobody above county level would audit a hardware shopkeeper's intake timestamp. He never imagined the provincial logs would land on Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's desk before sunrise."

Shen Wenchao shot Han Zhiyuan a razor-sharp sideways look. "Watch the names, Han Zhiyuan. Even inside this car."

Han Zhiyuan exhaled slowly and gave a curt nod. "Understood. Provincial jurisdiction only. Moving to Pillar Three: Yan Tiehu's Pier Four smuggling."

Han Zhiyuan pulled out thermal drone stills and weighbridge receipts. "Gang leader Yan Tiehu runs three unregistered steel barges out of Yunxi County's Pier Four every Tuesday and Friday night. Under Cheng Biao's patrol escort, Yan Tiehu offloads untaxed industrial machinery, contraband copper ingots, and stripped electronics shipped downriver from Qingyan Port's Pier Seven. We have satellite tracking, fuel slips, and signed statements from two deckhands flipped at Pier Seven an hour ago."

Shen Wenchao flicked his lighter, lit his cigarette, and cracked the rear window half an inch. Cold highway wind sliced through the gap, pulling a gray ribbon of smoke outside.

"Pillar Four," Shen Wenchao said, opening his crimson-tabbed banking binder. "Chairman Deng Yongchang's 4.8-billion-yuan shell pipeline. Linking Pier Four in Yunxi County and Pier Seven in Qingyan Port, Deng Yongchang built twenty-seven shell logistics, demolition, and hardware-supply firms. Over five years, those shells routed 4.8 billion yuan through provincial trade accounts, laundering smuggling profits while skimming highway and urban-renewal funds."

Han Zhiyuan let out a low whistle, tapping his knuckle against the armrest. "4.8 billion yuan running through a county hardware-supply shell web. No wonder Deng Yongchang wanted Chen Mingde's old commercial street leveled so badly. That entire block borders the Pier Four access road."

"Not just land," Shen Wenchao said, eyes narrowing through the smoke. "Pillar Five ties the entire chain around County Magistrate Sun Weiming's neck. Read Tab Five."

Han Zhiyuan opened the final tab and scanned the typed transcript. His eyebrows climbed slowly, and a grim, humorless smile tugged at his mouth.

"County Magistrate Sun Weiming's tea-room recordings," Han Zhiyuan murmured, turning the page. "Three high-definition audio files recorded inside the private bamboo tea-room behind Yunxi County Guest House over the last four months. Sun Weiming is on tape laughing with Chairman Deng Yongchang over a twelve-percent kickback split on the old street redevelopment, boasting that Vice Governor Lin Zhengde shields them in Lanshui, and telling Cheng Biao to bury any stubborn shopkeeper who refuses to sign."

Han Zhiyuan snapped the binder shut and leaned back against the headrest as the Yunxi County highway sign flashed past.

"Think about the sheer absurdity, Shen Wenchao," Han Zhiyuan said quietly, rubbing his forehead. "A vice governor in Lanshui, a customs director in Qingyan, a senior colonel in Southern Theater Command, a billionaire kingpin like Deng Yongchang, a county magistrate, and a police chief. Every single one of them falls in broad daylight today because they could not leave one stubborn fifty-four-year-old hardware shopkeeper alone."

Shen Wenchao gazed ahead as Yunxi County's low rooftops rose along the horizon. He took one last calm drag and crushed his cigarette into the door ashtray.

"Do not speculate on who handed the dossier upstairs, Han Zhiyuan," Shen Wenchao said, voice level and sharp. "Our job is not to stare at the sky. Our job is to put every corrupt official in Yunxi County in handcuffs before nine o'clock, and ensure Chen Mingde walks through his own front door clean, unharmed, and untouched."

The encrypted dash radio crackled. Commander Wu Zheng's crisp voice came through. "Lead sedan, Columns One through Four are clear of the tollgate. Yunxi County limits in three minutes. Yunxi PSB dispatch frequencies are offline."

Shen Wenchao lifted the black handset, thumb pressing the switch without hesitation.

"Acknowledged, Commander Wu Zheng," Shen Wenchao ordered coldly. "It is eight twenty. Split into four strike columns at the main intersection. Column Four secures Chen Mingde at the detention center first. Columns One, Two, and Three lock down County Government, Yunxi PSB, and Pier Four simultaneously. Execute every arrest in broad daylight."

Ahead of the lead sedan, the black convoy shifted seamlessly across three lanes, closing over Yunxi County like a steel net.
