# Chapter 47: The Premier Wakes to Silence

At four in the morning, the red telephone on Premier Gu Chengshan's study desk gave a sharp mechanical click. The dead amber indicator flipped to green.

Gu Chengshan had not slept since one. He sat in an unbuttoned jacket beside a half-full ceramic ashtray. The instant the light turned green, his hand snapped onto the receiver. The encrypted bridge chimed twice, linking three lines at once.

"Qin Zhengguo!" Gu Chengshan snapped into the receiver, tight with three hours of bottled fury. "Where in hell have you been? Every red line in the compound has been dead since one fifteen. My own security detail locked the corridor doors and kept my secretary outside. Three theater commands went completely dark. Tell me right now whether we are looking at a coup!"

On the second line, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's breathing came fast and ragged, followed by the metallic snap of a lighter. "It is not just the theater commands, Premier Gu Chengshan," Zhou Zhenhua broke in, voice cracking as he dragged hard on a cigarette. "My chief of staff just hammered on my door. Half the deputy ministers we tried to reach are gone. Their residential guards are standing outside empty bedrooms. Nobody fired a shot, nobody sounded an alarm, and nobody will say who took them!"

For three seconds, the third line held only the dry crackle of burning tobacco and a long exhale.

"Put your pen down, Zhou Zhenhua," General Qin Zhengguo said at last. His voice was hoarse, stripped of courtesy, heavy as cold iron. "There is no coup. If there were a coup, none of us would be holding a live receiver."

Gu Chengshan's jaw tightened. He tapped out a cigarette with his free hand and lit it. Smoke curled past his narrowed eyes. "Do not feed me riddles, Qin Zhengguo. You hold the vault line to Black Pine Guard. Did Black Pine move without State Council authorization?"

"Black Pine did not move," Qin Zhengguo replied flatly. "Black Pine was disarmed and locked inside a basement holding room at one ten."

Across the line, Zhou Zhenhua coughed hard on his smoke, then went completely still. "What did you just say?" Zhou Zhenhua whispered. "Who has the authority to disarm Black Pine?"

Gu Chengshan's fingers froze around his cigarette. A cold weight settled into his stomach. "Zero," Gu Chengshan breathed. "Zero Guard activated."

"Yes," Qin Zhengguo said. Another long inhale carried through the receiver. "I dialed the Black Pine vault line at one forty when the Eastern Theater relay dropped off my board. I got a dead seal. No voice, no clearance prompt. I had to wake Elder Song in the middle of the night just for the founding-era override phrase. Even with Elder Song's phrase, they gave me no command channel. Just a thirty-second status acknowledgment before cutting the line."

"Elder Song gave you the override?" Zhou Zhenhua's voice rose an octave, panic breaking through his composure. "What did they acknowledge? Why did they pull deputy ministers out of their beds at two in the morning?"

"Sit down, both of you," Qin Zhengguo said quietly. "If you are standing, sit down right now."

Gu Chengshan stayed on his feet, gripping the edge of his desk until his knuckles went pale. "Give me the numbers, Qin Zhengguo."

"Fourteen provinces," Qin Zhengguo recited, each phrase landing like a hammer blow. "Forty ministries. Three theater commands. Six major ports. Three hundred and eighteen high-value principals detained. Forty-seven foreign cutouts captured alive before they could cross a border or trigger a burn switch. One hundred and fourteen terabytes of decrypted ledgers, communications, and biometric transfers."

Gu Chengshan's lighter slipped from his fingers and clattered loudly against the desk. He did not pick it up.

"Three hundred and eighteen," Zhou Zhenhua choked out. On his end, a chair scraped sharply backward. "Qin Zhengguo, are you out of your mind? Three hundred and eighteen principals across forty ministries? That is half the senior apparatus!"

"It gets worse," Qin Zhengguo said, his tone dropping lower. "The summary verified through Elder Song's override puts the twenty-year compounded sovereign asset theft above 100 trillion yuan. State procurement, customs channels, energy infrastructure, offshore shells, all stitched into one syndicate. Alongside the money, active defense espionage. Live defense data handed straight to those forty-seven foreign cutouts."

Dead silence fell over the three-way line.

Gu Chengshan stared at the gray ribbon of smoke rising from his ashtray. His chest felt tight, as though the air had been pumped from the study. Over 100 trillion yuan. Active defense espionage inside three theater commands. All of it rotting beneath their noses while they signed circulars and chaired weekly meetings.

"My God," Zhou Zhenhua breathed, his voice trembling openly. "100 trillion. And defense intelligence. How did none of our own inspectors catch a single thread of this?"

"Because the inspectors were inside the thread, Zhou Zhenhua," Qin Zhengguo snapped, raw anger flashing through his calm. "Forty ministries. That includes oversight. That includes ports. That includes people who sat across from us at the last plenary session and smiled while we poured them tea."

Gu Chengshan took a hard pull from his cigarette until his mind sharpened again. His eyes narrowed, cold and calculating. "Qin Zhengguo," Gu Chengshan said slowly. "Zero Guard does not wake on its own. Not in thirty years. Somebody triggered it tonight. Who?"

Silence stretched for four full seconds. Only the faint hiss of the encrypted line filled the gap.

"Tell me, Qin Zhengguo," Gu Chengshan pressed, leaning over his desk with a dangerous edge. "You spoke to Elder Song. You received the vault acknowledgment. Who holds the activation key right now?"

"Stop," Qin Zhengguo said, his voice cold enough to slice straight through Gu Chengshan's authority. "Do not finish that sentence, Premier Gu Chengshan. Do not write a name on a notepad, and do not ask your aides to check an archive."

"I am the Premier of the State Council!" Gu Chengshan shot back, his palm slapping the desk. "Three hundred and eighteen officials vanished under my roof in three hours, and you are telling me not to ask who gave the order?"

"Yes, I am," Qin Zhengguo answered without flinching. "Elder Song said two sentences before he hung up. First, Zero only wakes when the sovereign seal itself authorizes it. Second, if Zero had not moved tonight, we would have woken up next month with our southern grid dark and our naval arrays blind. We do not ask who holds the key. We receive the handoff and pray our own offices are clean."

Gu Chengshan's mouth opened, then slowly closed. Blood drained from his face as the full weight of those words sank in.

On the second line, Zhou Zhenhua let out a shaky, hollow laugh that died almost immediately. "Pray our own offices are clean," Zhou Zhenhua repeated under his breath. "When does the handoff happen?"

"Four thirty," Qin Zhengguo said, checking his watch. "Central Leadership Compound, main briefing hall. Zero is delivering the evidence vaults directly to us. Call Gao Zhengyi at the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection right now. Tell him not to bring a secretary, not to bring a phone, and to walk in alone."

"I will call Gao Zhengyi myself," Gu Chengshan said, his voice steady again, though a fine tremor lingered in his fingers as he crushed out his cigarette. "Thirty minutes. Nobody else enters that hall."

"Thirty minutes," Zhou Zhenhua echoed grimly.

The three receivers clicked down, leaving Premier Gu Chengshan alone in the thickening smoke of his silent study.
