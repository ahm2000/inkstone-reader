# Chapter 44: The Garrison Logistics Officer

At three in the morning on Monday, the electronic lock on the Sub-Level Two audit server room inside Southern Theater Command's logistics wing clicked open under Senior Colonel Ma Zhenyu's palm. He slipped inside, closed the steel door quietly behind him, and pressed his damp forehead against the metal, listening to the steady hum of cooling fans.

Sweat soaked the armpits of his olive-green shirt. He had not buttoned his collar or grabbed his service cap after the duty terminal flashed a theater-wide blackout ten minutes earlier. In his right hand, Ma Zhenyu gripped a heavy portable magnetic degausser; in his left, he held his security keycard and a steel-cased purge drive.

"Just wipe the cross-ledger," Ma Zhenyu muttered, lips dry and cracked as he hurried between the humming racks. "Clear the requisition mirrors back to two thousand seventeen, burn the access logs, and walk upstairs before morning roll call. Nobody audits the archive drives until quarterly review."

He reached Cabinet Four, where the master supply and deployment audit trail sat behind smoked glass. His fingers shook so badly the keycard rattled against the scanner twice before the green light blinked. He pulled the glass open, plugged the purge drive into the maintenance port, and reached for the manual overwrite switch.

The screen did not display a command prompt.

Instead, the ceiling status lights shifted from dull amber to flat, clinical white.

Ma Zhenyu's hand froze on the toggle. A cold prickle raced up his neck, locking his shoulders. Slowly, stiffly, he turned to look down the narrow corridor between the black server racks.

Four motionless figures stood inside the room.

Two stood beside the emergency exit. Two more stood directly between Ma Zhenyu and the steel door he had just entered. They had not triggered a sensor or opened a latch; they had been waiting in the dark before he ever swiped his card.

All four wore featureless matte-black gear with smooth, lightless visors. No branch insignia, no garrison armbands, no rank stars. They stood with an unnatural stillness that made the server fans sound suddenly deafening.

Ma Zhenyu's breath hitched. He backed up until his spine hit Cabinet Four. Military conditioning fought against the dread flooding his gut, forcing his chin up even as his knees trembled.

"Who are you?" Ma Zhenyu barked, his voice tight and overly loud. "Identify yourselves immediately! I am Senior Colonel Ma Zhenyu, Deputy Director of Logistics Coordination for Southern Theater Command! This is a Tier-One classified military facility! How did you clear the checkpoint without a Theater Duty Officer escort? Did General Qin Zhengguo authorize an unannounced inspection?"

None of the four sentries shifted an inch.

The figure nearest the entry door tilted its helmet slightly. Its voice came out flat, neutral, and completely genderless, carrying an even chill that stripped every ounce of authority from Ma Zhenyu's uniform.

"Southern Theater Command Logistics Wing is under Zero lockdown. General Qin Zhengguo does not hold command authority inside this room."

Ma Zhenyu's eyes widened until the whites showed all around his irises. His chest tightened like an iron band. Every senior officer knew the whispered rumors of the unnumbered units above the regular bureaus, units that answered to no theater commander and filed no court-martial paperwork beforehand.

"You have the wrong officer," Ma Zhenyu stammered, his hand twitching away from the purge drive as if the steel had turned red-hot. "I came down here because the main fiber line went dark! I was running a diagnostic check on the fuel-depot backups! Check my duty roster!"

"Ma Zhenyu," the flat, neutral, genderless voice read evenly. "First unauthorized extraction of border-garrison supply rosters: October fourteenth, nine years ago. Thirty-seven subsequent transfers routed through Qingyan Port and the Shanghai relay. Cumulative deposits cleared into Zurich Northern Ledger sub-account CH-41-ZRH-2290 under your maternal cousin, Ma Deliang: forty-one million, six hundred thousand yuan."

Silence crashed down over the server room.

Ma Zhenyu's fingers went numb. His keycard slipped from his left hand and clattered onto the floor tile. A second later, the heavy degausser dropped from his right hand with a dull bang and rolled against his polished shoe.

He did not bend to pick either up.

Violent tremors seized his shoulders and traveled down his legs. His mouth opened and closed before a ragged, choking sound tore from his throat. His military posture collapsed all at once; he slid down Cabinet Four until he was half-crouched on the floor, palms pressed flat against his shaking thighs.

"Nine years," Ma Zhenyu whispered, tears spilling over his eyelids down his grey cheeks. "It was nine years ago. I never meant to betray the garrison. I swear, I never wanted to touch a single classified file!"

He stared up wildly at the blank matte-black visor, words pouring out in a raw, terrified flood as twenty-five years of guarded silence shattered.

"It started in Macau!" Ma Zhenyu gasped, clutching his uniform shirt. "My brother-in-law dragged me into a VIP baccarat room on annual leave! I lost six hundred thousand yuan in one night, borrowed from the house to win it back, and by the third morning I owed four million two hundred thousand yuan! The casino manager smiled and put a photograph of me in my colonel's uniform right beside the debt slip!"

His throat clicked as his voice cracked into a desperate plea. "Two men met me in a private tea room in Zhuhai the next week. They paid off the four million yuan debt that afternoon. They told me all they wanted was a routine fuel-consumption forecast for the southern border depots. Just diesel tonnage and truck maintenance intervals! I told myself nobody gets killed over diesel numbers!"

Ma Zhenyu dragged shaking hands down his face. "Then they opened the Zurich account in Ma Deliang's name and wired two million yuan without asking! When I tried to refuse the second request, they sent a recording of our Zhuhai meeting and a copy of the proxy form with my thumbprint! Every year after that, they asked for more! First convoy schedules! Then border-garrison rotation rosters! Then six months ago they demanded maintenance downtime windows for the coastal naval radar stations near Qingyan Port!"

He let out a broken sob, forehead dropping toward his knees. "I could not stop! Every time I thought about walking into General Qin Zhengguo's office to confess, I thought about my daughter at university and my mother in the hospital! I told myself one more transfer, just one more, and I would retire next spring! Please! Do not send me to a black vault! Hand me to the Southern Theater military tribunal! Let me face a firing squad in uniform so my family knows where my body is buried!"

The four matte-black sentries stood motionless in the white glare, showing neither anger nor pity.

"Confession recorded," the flat, neutral, genderless voice stated calmly. "Extract the purge drive from Cabinet Four and seal the port. Place Senior Colonel Ma Zhenyu under silent restraint and escort him to transport."

Two sentries stepped forward without a sound. Ma Zhenyu did not pull away as they lifted him and bound his wrists. His eyes stared straight ahead, glassy and empty, leaving his fallen keycard lying alone on the server room floor.
