# Chapter 16: The Old Gentleman

At the Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission's inspection team office, Vice Director Gao Zhengyi was zipping up his leather briefcase for a quick Sunday lunch when his private mobile phone rang on his desk. As one of the senior deputy directors overseeing the province's anti-corruption inspection teams, he fielded high-level calls every single day.

Still, the caller ID glowing on his screen was a number that hadn't dialed him in three full years.

Instinctively, Gao Zhengyi straightened his coat and stood up behind his desk before pressing answer, even though he was completely alone in the room.

"Hello, sir!"

"Gao," an old, dry, calm voice came across the line. "I'm down in Yunxi County doing a little fishing by Qingshan Reservoir. Very quiet spot. There's a young man sitting beside me who hasn't caught a single fish in nearly two hours, and he doesn't look the slightest bit impatient. It's been a long time since I sat next to someone like that."

"Sir," Gao Zhengyi answered respectfully, his throat tightening slightly. "It's truly wonderful to hear you're enjoying your rest."

"Your inspection team is scheduled to arrive in Yunxi on Wednesday, isn't it?" The old gentleman paused, the faint sound of lapping water drifting through the phone. "Move it up. Come tomorrow morning instead. I'd like to see what a county looks like when it hasn't had three extra days to clean up its act and hide its account books for the Governor. And Gao, do everything strictly by the book. No fuss."

"Yes, sir! Tomorrow morning, nine sharp, strictly by the rules!"

The line clicked softly dead.

Gao Zhengyi stood motionless behind his desk for a long ten seconds, still gripping the silent phone.

That old gentleman had retired from the very top of Lanshui Province eleven years ago, yet for a solid decade before that, every city and county leader in the province had trembled at a single margin note in his handwriting! If he'd quietly picked a small county reservoir for a three-week fishing stay and suddenly ordered the inspection team in two days early, then Yunxi County's sky was about to fall straight onto somebody's head!

Setting his briefcase aside, Gao Zhengyi reached straight for his office landline and began rearranging his entire team's schedule at top speed! His assistant, sensing the shift in the air before being told anything, had already cancelled her own lunch and was hovering by the door with a fresh pot of tea, the way people do when they want credit for noticing trouble before anyone asks them to.

Ten minutes later, in the Party Secretary's office back in Yunxi, Fan Weiguo hadn't slept a wink in over thirty hours.

Beneath his neatly pressed suit jacket, his white dress shirt clung damply to his back. He sat stiffly behind his wide dark wooden desk with both palms pressed flat against the glass tabletop so nobody walking in could see his fingers tremble.

At seven o'clock this morning, Qingyan's City Party Secretary, Fan Weiguo's direct superior and the top leader of the entire city, had called him personally. The news had hit like three consecutive thunderbolts! Deputy Police Director Cheng Biao was suspended. Every Old Street buyout deadline was frozen, along with the county's 640-million-yuan relocation fund. All eleven previously signed contracts were being pulled for independent legal review. Worse, three investigators from the Municipal Discipline Commission were already sitting in Yunxi County, with a fourth on the highway!

Before hanging up, the City Party Secretary had left Fan Weiguo with one chilling sentence in a voice he'd never heard from his boss before: "Weiguo, I trust you'll cooperate with the investigators. Fully."

Who had triggered this avalanche?! Who had walked straight into Qingyan's Discipline Commission at midnight with the clinic timestamp, video proof, and bank records?! For four agonizing hours, Fan Weiguo had racked his brain. All roads seemed to start at an ordinary hardware shopkeeper and his logistics-clerk nephew, yet that made no sense at all!

Was it linked to that secret complaint letter sent to the Governor's office? Had somebody inside his own county staff leaked the relocation-fund ledgers? Or had County Magistrate Tao Shu, who'd so neatly dodged Friday's dinner with his fake cold, finally opened his drawer?!

Brrring!

His red office landline shattered the silence, making Fan Weiguo's shoulders jerk! Taking a sharp breath to steady his voice, he lifted the receiver. "Fan Weiguo speaking."

"Secretary Fan?" The caller's tone was crisp, polite, and unmistakably official. "I'm calling from the General Office of the Provincial Party Committee. Please do not take notes on this call. Today and tomorrow, a retired senior leader is visiting Qingshan Reservoir in your county privately to fish. There is to be no welcome committee, no security escort, no ID checks, no photographs, and zero questions. Nobody from Yunxi is to approach him, ask his name, or reveal that we called."

Fan Weiguo's breath caught in his chest! The General Office of the Provincial Party Committee was the nerve center of the entire province's highest leadership!

"I understand completely," Fan Weiguo answered, keeping his voice careful and polite. "May I ask which senior leader is visiting?"

"Secretary Fan," the caller cut in smoothly. "When the City Party Secretary walks into your office, how do you stand?"

"I stand up immediately, of course. Straight at attention."

"For this gentleman, the Provincial Party Secretary stands straight." There was one quiet, heavy beat on the line. "If anything spoils the old gentleman's weekend, you won't need to bother coming into work on Monday. That is all."

Click.

Fan Weiguo sat frozen with the dead phone pressed against his ear, feeling all the blood drain from his face!

Someone for whom the top leader of the entire province stood straight at attention! How many living people in Lanshui Province fit that description?! Four? Five at most! All retired, all famous, and one of them, Fan Weiguo remembered with a sickening drop in his stomach, had famously loved quiet reservoir fishing!

Right here at Yunxi's Qingshan Reservoir. This exact weekend. Hours after the Old Street scandal exploded in Qingyan City!

Could that possibly be a coincidence?! Had the old gentleman heard about Old Street? Did he somehow know that hardware shopkeeper?! Wait, did Chen Mingde's family secretly have a huge backer in the provincial capital?!

Fan Weiguo cut off that thought right away! Even finishing that guess felt dangerous!

Bang! His office door burst open without a knock, and his young aide rushed in, white-faced and gasping for breath.

"Secretary Fan! Bad news! The Provincial Inspection Team just phoned the general office! They canceled Wednesday; they're arriving in Yunxi tomorrow morning at nine o'clock sharp!"

Fan Weiguo didn't even blink. Slowly cradling the silent landline, he stared at the open doorway as if waiting for the ceiling itself to collapse next. After thirty years in government, he knew the storm had already hit shore.

Standing up slowly, he straightened his dark tie with fingers that refused to stay steady.

"Notify every member of the County Standing Committee," Fan Weiguo ordered, his voice hoarse yet controlled. "Emergency session in Conference Room Three at two-thirty sharp. Tell them nobody gets a cold today."

Twenty minutes after that, in the quiet eastern courtyard, Chief An Zhiyuan read the message his provincial contacts had just routed to his desk, then read it again.

*The inspection team's just moved its Yunxi arrival up from Wednesday to nine tomorrow morning. And the Provincial Party Committee's General Office has quietly warned every office in the county that a retired senior leader is fishing, in private, at Qingshan Reservoir.*

Very slowly, Chief An set the note flat on the wooden desk.

Right now, at this exact minute, the Boss was sitting by Qingshan Reservoir fishing with his grandfather! Yet Chief An's team hadn't made a single call to the provincial capital! Nobody had touched the inspection schedule! Attorney Bai had simply filed a clean legal complaint at the city level, strictly following Chen Xuan's order!

So who on earth had pushed a provincial anti-corruption team two full days ahead of schedule with one casual phone call?!

Chief An had spent forty years watching the highest levels of government. In this entire province, only a tiny handful of retired elders carried that kind of quiet power, and almost every single one of them preferred plain clothes and an afternoon with a fishing rod!

Reaching swiftly for his encrypted handset, Chief An pressed Nine's channel, and for the first time in years, a faint tremor edged his steady voice.

"Nine! Where is the Boss sitting right now? And who is sitting next to him?"
