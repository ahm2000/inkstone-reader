# Chapter 204: Hot Pot at Emerald Riverside

By five-thirty, the dining room of Emerald Riverside Villa was filled with warmth, laughter, and the rich, mouthwatering aroma of simmering broth.

In the center of the broad rosewood dining table, a heavy copper hot pot bubbled merrily over an electric burner. One side of the divided pot held a clear, golden chicken broth infused with wild Changbai hazel mushrooms and dried wolfberries, while the other held a fragrant, mildly spiced tomato broth that Qin Yuhan had spent an hour perfecting.

Surrounding the pot were porcelain platters piled high with thinly sliced marbled beef brisket, fresh winter bamboo shoots, handmade shrimp paste, sweet corn, and crisp emerald-green spinach.

At the head of the table sat Elder Qin Zhengguo. The retired four-star general wore a comfortable dark wool cardigan, his weathered face glowing with hearty satisfaction as he held a pair of long wooden chopsticks poised over the steaming pot.

To his right sat Qin Zhengming and Lin Shuyin, while Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan sat side by side across from them.

"Look at this beef!" Elder Qin Zhengguo bellowed, lifting a paper-thin slice of marbled brisket that had cooked to tender perfection in the mushroom broth. He dipped it into a small bowl of sesame paste and popped it straight into his mouth, his eyes crinkling with pure delight. "Soft as silk, rich with mushroom flavor! Chen Xuan, my boy, you really brought this back from the north?"

Chen Xuan scooped a spoonful of fresh bamboo shoots into Qin Yuhan's bowl and smiled warmly. "Straight from the Changbai mountain cooperatives, Grandpa. The local forestry workers dry the mushrooms naturally on pine racks. The flavor is ten times richer than anything greenhouse-grown."

"Splendid! Absolutely splendid!" Elder Qin nodded vigorously, tapping his chopsticks on the table rim. "Back during the northern border patrols forty years ago, we used to forage for wild hazel mushrooms beneath the snow to boil with military rations. That was the only thing that kept our blood warm in forty-below weather!"

Lin Shuyin carefully dipped a piece of spinach into the tomato broth, shaking her head with an affectionate sigh. "Dad, must every meal turn into a military history lesson? We are having a peaceful Sunday family dinner."

"What is wrong with a history lesson?" Elder Qin snorted playfully. "It teaches these young corporate executives where real perseverance comes from! Zhengming, look at your daughter. Her cheeks have color in them again. Last month she looked like a walking ghost from all those board meetings at Haiyue Group!"

Qin Yuhan leaned her shoulder against Chen Xuan's arm and chuckled, feeding Chen Xuan a tender slice of beef with her own chopsticks. "That is because my husband takes good care of me, Grandpa. While other managers in Shanghai are chasing promotions until midnight, Chen Xuan clocks out at four-fifty-five and has dinner ready by six."

Lin Shuyin put down her spoon and looked across the table at Chen Xuan with a mixture of amusement and motherly exasperation. "Speaking of promotions, Chen Xuan, I saw Sun Haoran's wife at the tennis club yesterday. She told me Sun Haoran is still hoping you will reconsider the Deputy General Manager position at Hengxin Logistics. She said the company is expanding their coastal routes, and they need a dependable supervisor on the executive floor."

Qin Zhengming cleared his throat and nodded thoughtfully. "Chen Xuan, 50,000 yuan a month with an executive car allowance is not an offer that comes around twice for a logistics supervisor. It would give you a solid footing in the shipping industry."

Before Chen Xuan could answer, Qin Yuhan kicked her father gently under the table. "Dad! We talked about this! Chen Xuan does not want to be a deputy manager. Why should he spend every weekend attending boring banquets and drinking baijiu with port officials when he can stay home with me?"

Elder Qin Zhengguo slammed his palm flat on the table, making the porcelain sauce dishes rattle lightly.

"The girl is right!" the old general boomed, pointing his chopsticks directly at Qin Zhengming. "Zhengming, you have been in corporate management for thirty years, and what has it given you? High blood pressure and an ulcer that flares up every time the quarterly numbers drop! Chen Xuan is twenty-seven years old, his conscience is clean, his wife is happy, and his mushroom broth could win a national culinary medal! What does he need an executive title for?"

Lin Shuyin sighed and threw up her hands. "Here he goes again! Dad, you would defend Chen Xuan even if he decided to quit his job entirely and spend all day fishing in the Huangpu River!"

"And why shouldn't he fish?" Elder Qin grinned mischievously, pouring himself a small cup of hot jasmine tea from Chen Xuan's green thermos that sat neatly on the table. "A man who knows when he has enough is wiser than all the greedy fools in this country put together. Did you read the morning papers? That big shot Victor Leung in Hong Kong, owner of the Seven-Harbor Consortium, worth tens of billions! Look where greed got him, locked up in handcuffs by noon today, all his assets frozen by the state!"

Chen Xuan took a sip of water, his eyes calm and untroubled as he listened to the old general's lecture. "Grandpa speaks the truth. Chasing too much money only invites trouble. A simple desk and a peaceful home are worth more than ten bank vaults."

"Well said, Xiao Xuan!" Elder Qin beamed, pouring a generous serving of shrimp paste into the bubbling broth. "Now, stop talking about corporate nonsense and pass me that platter of pine nuts. After dinner, you owe me two games of chess. Tonight, my red cannons are taking no prisoners!"
