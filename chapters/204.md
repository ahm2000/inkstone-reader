# Chapter 204: Hot Pot at Emerald Riverside

By 5:40 PM, early December dusk had settled over Pudong, and a crisp winter wind tapped lightly against the glass doors of Emerald Riverside Villa. Inside the dining room, however, the air was warm, bright, and filled with the rich aroma of simmering broth.

In the center of the round wooden table sat a gleaming twin-flavor hot pot. One side bubbled with a clear, golden soup made from beef marrow bones, ginger slices, and the wild hazel mushrooms Chen Xuan had brought back from Changbai Mountain. The other side glowed a gentle, inviting red, infused with mild Sichuan peppercorns, sweet winter tomatoes, and fragrant dried chilies.

Around the pot stood neat platters of thinly sliced lamb, marbled beef brisket, hand-cut tofu, winter radishes, chrysanthemum greens, and bowls of freshly mixed sesame dipping sauce.

The doorbell chimed right on schedule. Qin Yuhan hurried to open it, welcoming her parents, Qin Zhengming and Lin Shuyin, along with her grandfather, retired four-star general Elder Qin Zhengguo.

Elder Qin stepped inside wearing a heavy dark wool coat and a knitted scarf. The moment he smelled the kitchen air, the old general's eyes lit up beneath his bushy white eyebrows.

"Aha!" Elder Qin boomed, handing his coat to Chen Xuan with a hearty pat on the shoulder. "Wild mountain mushrooms! I smelled them before we even reached the porch. Good boy, Chen Xuan. At least someone in this family knows how to treat an old man properly in December."

"Grandpa, come sit down before the wind gets in," Chen Xuan smiled, hanging up their coats. "The broth has been simmering for two hours. We can start eating right away."

Lin Shuyin set a box of winter tangerines on the side counter and gave Chen Xuan a warm, appreciative look. "Look at this spread. Yuhan works so late on the 38th floor of Haiyue Tower all week, and if it were not for you cooking proper meals at home, she would live on cold salad and black coffee."

"Mom, I ate lunch today," Qin Yuhan protested playfully, pulling out a chair for her mother.

"Only because I texted you to remind you," Chen Xuan remarked mildly as he carried out a final plate of blanched beef tendon.

Everyone laughed as they gathered around the steaming table. Qin Zhengming poured small cups of warmed Shaoxing yellow wine for himself, Elder Qin, and Chen Xuan, while Qin Yuhan and her mother drank hot pear and ginger tea.

As they dipped lamb slices and crisp greens into the bubbling broth, the conversation drifted naturally to the week's news.

"Did you hear what happened in Hong Kong this morning, Yuhan?" Qin Zhengming asked, setting his wine cup down with a look of mild amazement. "The financial channels have been talking about nothing else all afternoon. That huge shipping group, Seven-Harbor Consortium, completely collapsed overnight. Their chairman, Victor Leung, was arrested by ICAC right in his penthouse, and banks in Singapore and Zurich froze billions."

Qin Yuhan nodded, a relieved smile touching her lips. "Our shipping director at Haiyue Group called me at noon. For the past two months, Seven-Harbor kept tying up coastal container slots and driving up freight insurance. Today, every single one of those bottlenecks disappeared. Our December export schedule is completely clear."

"Serves them right," Elder Qin snorted, picking up a tender piece of beef brisket from the mushroom broth. "Greedy outfits that play dirty games always trip over their own feet sooner or later. Still, it is strange how fast they fell. Someone with serious weight must have handed the regulators a clean case."

Sitting beside Qin Yuhan, Chen Xuan quietly fished two perfectly cooked slices of lamb and a tender hazel mushroom from the broth and placed them into his wife's dipping bowl.

"Big companies probably just get sloppy with their paperwork after too many years," Chen Xuan said calmly, taking a modest sip of warm yellow wine. "Once the tax and customs audits start digging, one loose thread pulls the whole sweater apart."

Qin Zhengming chuckled and shook his head. "Leave it to Chen Xuan to turn a multi-billion financial collapse into a paperwork joke. That is why you stay so relaxed on your 12,000-yuan logistics desk at Hengxin, huh?"

"Steady salary, off work at 4:55 PM, and nobody calls me at midnight," Chen Xuan replied with a relaxed shrug. "What more could a guy ask for?"

Elder Qin laughed loudly and pointed his chopsticks at Chen Xuan. "No corporate ambition at all, and I respect it! Too many young men chase titles until they forget how to enjoy a hot bowl of soup. Besides, as long as you keep taking care of Yuhan and cooking ribs like last weekend, I will back you up."

Not a single person at the table suspected a thing. To Qin Yuhan, her parents, and the retired four-star general, Chen Xuan was simply the warm-hearted, slightly lazy logistics clerk who made the best winter hot pot in Pudong.

After dinner, Lin Shuyin and Qin Yuhan settled onto the living room sofa with a big bowl of the cracked Changbai pine nuts, chatting happily about winter clothes. Meanwhile, Elder Qin marched Chen Xuan straight to the carved wooden chessboard beside the frosted window.

"No excuses tonight," Elder Qin declared, setting his red pieces down with crisp clacks. "Last week I was distracted. Tonight I am taking your general in twenty moves."

Chen Xuan smiled politely and opened with a calm, unhurried defense. For fifteen minutes, he let Elder Qin push his chariots forward with grand military flair, praising the old general's bold center attack. Then, just as Elder Qin leaned forward to claim victory, Chen Xuan slid his black cannon quietly behind his knight.

"Checkmate, Grandpa," Chen Xuan said softly.

Elder Qin froze, stared at the board for five full seconds, and slapped his knee in exasperation. "Again! You have way too much free time at that 12,000-yuan office job, boy! Set the pieces back up. Best two out of three!"

Chen Xuan laughed and began resetting the wooden pieces, listening to his wife's soft laughter from the sofa as the winter night wrapped peacefully around Emerald Riverside Villa.
