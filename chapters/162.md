# Chapter 162: The Quiet Phone Call

Shen Jingzhou crossed his ankles as he leaned against the gleaming hood of his black Mercedes. An assistant hurried over with iced coffee, and Shen Jingzhou took a slow sip while eyeing Qin Yuhan over the rim.

"Twenty-five minutes left, President Qin," Shen Jingzhou called out lazily. "My lawyers already drafted the amended transfer clause so you do not have to read the fine print. One signature, and we can both leave this chilly wind."

Beside Qin Yuhan, Lin Jian tapped his phone screen frantically, calling every shipping broker and municipal contact he knew in Shenzhen. Every call either rang endlessly or ended in a hurried apology. Nobody in the local port circle dared cross the Shen Family on their home turf.

Qin Yuhan kept her back straight, though Chen Xuan felt the tension in her posture. She leaned close and murmured softly, "Chen Xuan, stay behind me and do not let him bait you. The Shen Family plays dirty here. Even if we have to eat the penalty and sue them next week, I will not let him touch you."

Warmth spread through Chen Xuan's chest. Even facing an 80 million loss, his wife's first instinct was still to shield her supposedly helpless husband.

He adjusted her handbag on his wrist and brushed a loose strand of hair behind her ear. "Relax. I am not going to argue with him. Let me step over by the railing for two minutes. I know a friend whose cousin works in maritime logistics. Maybe he knows a senior broker who can untangle the paperwork."

Qin Yuhan gave him a tender, slightly amused smile. She knew a clerk from Hengxin Logistics could not outrank the Shen Family in Shenzhen, yet she appreciated him trying to help. "Alright. Do not wander far."

Chen Xuan handed her a tissue before strolling casually toward the concrete seawall a short distance away. Behind him, Shen Jingzhou snorted loudly. "Look at that. The Shanghai clerk is calling his little buddies to save Haiyue Group. Should I wait for your supervisor to drive down in a delivery van?"

Chen Xuan did not turn around. He stopped by the railing near a small dockside beverage kiosk. Beside the kiosk, a middle-aged woman in a faded knit cardigan was unhurriedly sweeping fallen leaves into a dustpan. As Chen Xuan approached, she paused with a warm, neighborly smile.

"Young man, the sea wind gets sharp in the late afternoon," the auntie remarked comfortably, like an elder chatting with a nephew on a neighborhood street. "Did your wife wear a warm coat?"

"She wore her wool trench coat, Auntie," Chen Xuan replied with a relaxed grin. "We will wrap up soon and get her some hot soup."

"Good, good. Do not keep her out too long," she nodded kindly before resuming her sweeping.

To anyone watching from the gate, she was just a regular port cleaner finishing her shift. Neither Shen Jingzhou's guards nor the dockworkers gave her a second glance. Chen Xuan, however, recognized the effortless ease of the Zero Guard blending seamlessly into everyday life around him.

Facing the shimmering bay, Chen Xuan pulled his single everyday smartphone from his jacket pocket. Its matte black case had a faint scuff on the corner from the time he dropped it while cooking braised pork ribs at Emerald Riverside Villa. It was the same phone he used to text Qin Yuhan grocery lists and check chess puzzles for Elder Qin Zhengguo.

He opened his call log, tapped an unlabelled number, and raised the phone to his ear. It connected before the first ring finished.

"Young Master," came the steady, deeply respectful voice of Number 1.

"Are you busy?" Chen Xuan asked casually, taking a sip of warm jasmine tea from his green thermos.

"Never too busy for you, Young Master. Please give your instructions."

"My wife and I are at Pier Nine in Shenzhen," Chen Xuan said calmly, watching a seagull glide above the moored cargo ship. "Shen Jingzhou from the local Shen Family chained the gates and put a bogus compliance hold on Haiyue Group's 40 containers. He says if she does not sign over her shipping route before 5:00 PM, Haiyue pays an 80 million penalty."

A brief silence settled on the line, calm yet carrying the weight of an approaching storm.

"Shen Jingzhou of Shen Maritime Holdings," Number 1 confirmed smoothly. "I have his file in front of me, Young Master. Would you like the gates opened immediately?"

"Make sure it happens cleanly before 5:00 PM," Chen Xuan said. "No fuss, no drama, and keep my name out of it. I promised Yuhan roast goose at MixC Mall tonight, and I hate being late for dinner."

"Understood, Young Master," Number 1 answered without hesitation. "The White Order will coordinate directly with the General Administration of Customs, the Maritime Safety Administration, and the state banks holding the Shen Family's credit lines. Every irregular hold at Pier Nine will be audited and cleared before 5:00 PM."

"Good. Thanks for the trouble."

"It is my duty, Young Master."

Chen Xuan ended the call, slipped his single smartphone back into his pocket, and screwed the lid onto his thermos.

Ten kilometers away, inside a quiet office overlooking Shenzhen Bay, Number 1 set his phone on the desk. Wearing a simple charcoal wool coat, he looked like a seasoned corporate executive. Number 7 and Number 12 stood across from him in neat business attire.

"Young Master is at Pier Nine," Number 1 said calmly. "Shen Jingzhou has illegally locked Haiyue Group's cargo behind a fabricated port hold. Activate White Order maritime and financial channels now."

Number 7 nodded briskly. "Notifying federal customs and the Shenzhen Port Authority commissioner immediately. We have three documented cases of Shen Maritime Holdings falsifying berth holds. That triggers an emergency license audit on the spot."

"I will forward the compliance alert to Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, and ICBC," Number 12 added. "Their 2.4 billion in revolving credit lines will freeze within five minutes."

"Execute it now," Number 1 instructed. "Everything clears by 5:00 PM sharp."

Back at Pier Nine, Chen Xuan strolled calmly to Qin Yuhan's side. Shen Jingzhou smirked. "Well? Did your friend tell you to take the bus home?"

Chen Xuan smiled faintly. "Actually, he said the lane should clear up right around 5:00 PM."
