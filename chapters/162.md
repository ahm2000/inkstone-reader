# Chapter 162: The White Phone Call

Five paces away from the crowd, the salty wind blew steadily off the harbor.

Chen Xuan stood facing the water with his back to Shen Jingzhou.

His fingers passed over his cracked everyday phone and pulled out a slim, plain matte-white device with no logo.

Chen Xuan tapped the screen and pressed a single white icon.

"Number 88 here, Sir," a calm male voice answered over faint city traffic.

"I am at Shenzhen Pier Nine," Chen Xuan said quietly. "Shenhai Shipping and a port inspector named Lu Guobin have chained the gate to hold up the Haiyue Star. Six of Hengxin Logistics' containers are on board, along with Haiyue Group's cargo."

"Understood, Sir," Number 88 replied sharply. "The Shen Family has treated Pier Nine like their private yard for years. I am twenty minutes from Futian CBD right now. How would you like this handled?"

"I told them five o'clock," Chen Xuan said. "Make sure the Shenzhen Port Authority hears about Chief Inspector Lu's fake inspection stamp. Tell their director to come down to Pier Nine and open the gate before five."

"Yes, Sir. Their office is three blocks from our southern station. I will send someone in five minutes."

"Good. Do not let Shen Jingzhou leave early. I want him to see it open."

"Consider it done, Sir."

Chen Xuan ended the call and dialed a second line.

"Number 11 here, Sir."

"Check Shen Wanshan and Shenhai Shipping in Shenzhen," Chen Xuan said evenly. "Tell me which national banks carry their port loans."

After a brief pause and the soft tap of keys, Number 11 spoke clearly.

"I have their sheet, Sir. Shenhai Shipping holds 12 billion yuan in active port credit lines across three Futian CBD branches: China Construction Bank, ICBC, and Huaxia Bank. That money covers their crane leases, dock fees, and three properties in Shenzhen Bay No. 1. If those lines freeze together, their cash flow stops cold within an hour."

"Freeze all 12 billion yuan for an emergency risk review," Chen Xuan said calmly. "Have all three banks send formal notices to Shen Wanshan and Shenhai Shipping before four fifty."

"Right away, Sir. The notices will hit their desks and phones in twelve minutes."

"Thank you," Chen Xuan said.

He slipped the white phone back into his windbreaker, picked up his folder, and turned back toward the gate. The whole call had taken under two minutes.

Over by the black luxury sedan, Shen Jingzhou saw Chen Xuan put his phone away and snorted loudly.

"Look at that, everyone!" Shen Jingzhou called out from his leather folding chair, waving his cigarette in the air. "Our clerk from Shanghai just finished calling his manager! I bet he is begging for overtime pay!"

Chief Inspector Lu Guobin chuckled dutifully, though he kept glancing nervously at his watch.

Shen Jingzhou smiled smugly, pulled out his own gold-cased smartphone, and dialed his father's private number.

Ten kilometers away in Futian CBD, inside a high-floor private tea room overlooking the glass spire of Ping An Finance Center, Shen Wanshan picked up on the second ring.

"Jingzhou?" Shen Wanshan's deep, confident voice sounded over the line, accompanied by the gentle clink of porcelain teacups. "Did Qin Yuhan show up at Pier Nine?"

"She sure did, Dad," Shen Jingzhou boasted, leaning back in his chair so everyone near the gate could hear him. "She flew all the way down from Shanghai looking like an ice queen, waving some worthless Shanghai safety report in Chief Inspector Lu's face."

Shen Wanshan laughed heartily on the other end. "Good. Did she sign over the 30% route share yet?"

"Not yet. She is acting tough," Shen Jingzhou said, glancing mockingly toward Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan. "She thinks she can wait us out. Oh, and Dad, you will not believe the joke she brought with her."

"Who? A lawyer from Shanghai?"

"Worse!" Shen Jingzhou laughed hard enough to cough a little smoke. "Some ordinary office worker from a tiny shipping firm called Hengxin Logistics. Wearing a fifty-yuan windbreaker and hugging a green thermos! He actually walked right up to me and said I have until five o'clock to open the gate because he does not like working overtime!"

For a moment, there was silence on the line, and then Shen Wanshan burst into booming laughter.

"A shipping clerk gave the Shen Family a five o'clock deadline?" Shen Wanshan chuckled, amused. "Shanghai people really do live in a dream world. Tell you what, Jingzhou. If some nobody in a cheap windbreaker gets Pier Nine open before five o'clock, I will walk down to Shenzhen Bay and jump into the water myself."

"Hear that?" Shen Jingzhou grinned, raising his voice even louder as he eyed Chen Xuan. "My father says if you open that gate by five, he will jump into Shenzhen Bay!"

"Do not waste your breath on small fry," Shen Wanshan added smoothly, his tone turning sharp again. "Hold the gate tight. Let Haiyue Group bleed 8 million yuan a day. By Friday, Qin Yuhan will come to our house in Shenzhen Bay No. 1 and beg us to take 40% instead of 30%. I am having tea with Vice President Ma from China Construction Bank at six, so come join us once you finish playing with them."

"Got it, Dad! I will be right there after five!"

Shen Jingzhou hung up and tossed his phone lazily in his palm.

Four forty-two.

Eighteen minutes left.

"You heard my father, President Qin," Shen Jingzhou drawled, blowing a thin ring of smoke into the humid air. "At six o'clock, he is having tea with the vice president of China Construction Bank in Futian CBD. Meanwhile, your ship is sitting behind a brass padlock. Last chance before five o'clock. Pick up the pen."

Qin Yuhan stood calmly beside Chen Xuan. She did not even look at Shen Jingzhou's folding chair. Instead, she turned her clear dark eyes toward Chen Xuan, noticing how completely relaxed his shoulders were.

"Mr. Chen," Qin Yuhan said softly, just loud enough for him to hear. "The sun is strong out here. Would you like to wait inside my car with the air conditioning on?"

"No need," Chen Xuan replied easily. "The sea breeze is nice, and we only have a few minutes left anyway."

Behind them, Manager Zhou Ming wiped his damp neck with a handkerchief, staring at the locked iron gate in silent misery. He glanced at his watch, then at Chen Xuan's calm face, unable to understand how a regular office clerk could stand so peacefully while 8 million yuan a day hung in the balance.

Across the road, Shen Jingzhou stubbed out his cigarette in a portable ashtray and folded his arms behind his head, grinning at the clock.

Four forty-five.

Four forty-seven.

Four forty-nine.

"One minute to four fifty, tea-boy!" Shen Jingzhou called out cheerfully. "Where is your miracle?"

At that exact second, the gold-cased smartphone in Shen Jingzhou's lap began to vibrate violently against his thigh.
