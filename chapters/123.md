# Chapter 123: Pastries at the Bund Gala

By Wednesday evening, the historic crystal ballroom of the Waldorf Hotel on the Bund glowed with chandeliers and low conversation as the annual Shanghai Chamber of Commerce charity dinner reached full swing. Outside the tall arched French windows, sightseeing boats drifted slowly along the Huangpu River toward the bright towers of Lujiazui.

Earlier that morning at Haiyue Tower, Qin Yuhan had laid Chen Xuan's newspaper clipping on the conference table and dismantled Director Zhao and Qin Haoran's 300 million yuan warehouse trap in front of the entire board. Director Zhao had turned pale and stammered an excuse while senior shareholders praised her sharp judgment. With follow-up meetings running past dusk, she had barely found time to change into a midnight-blue silk gown and drive to the Bund with Chen Xuan, skipping dinner completely.

Now, while several Chamber of Commerce directors greeted Qin Yuhan near the marble fountain, Chen Xuan strolled over to the long white buffet table in his clean dark blue suit.

Ignoring the chilled champagne flutes and tiny caviar spoons, Chen Xuan picked up a warm porcelain plate and a pair of silver tongs. With quiet care, he chose two flaky almond tarts, three miniature vanilla custard puffs, and two freshly baked honey-glazed chestnut cakes. Beside them, he poured a small porcelain cup of hot oolong tea.

"Well, look at this," a sharp, mocking voice spoke up right beside his elbow. "I wondered who was clearing out the dessert table before the charity auction even began. Do you think you are at a neighborhood cafeteria?"

Chen Xuan turned calmly. Standing a step away was a tall young man in a shiny burgundy tuxedo, swirling a crystal champagne glass. A silver lapel pin marked him as Wu Junming, the younger heir of a mid-sized Shanghai coastal shipping family.

Two other dressed-up guests lingered behind Wu Junming, smirking as they looked Chen Xuan up and down. Chen Xuan's dark blue suit fit neatly and looked comfortable, yet it carried no flashy designer tag, and the steel wristwatch on his wrist was plain and understated.

"The chestnut cakes are still warm," Chen Xuan replied mildly, placing one last almond tart onto his plate. "Cold champagne gives you a stomachache when you have not eaten. You should try one."

Wu Junming let out a short, condescending laugh. "I heard President Qin of Haiyue Group brought her husband tonight. Wait a minute. Are you the desk clerk from that tiny freight office in Pudong?"

One of the guests behind him chuckled loudly. "No wonder he is piling up free cakes! A single seat at this banquet costs more than a month of his paycheck."

Wu Junming stepped closer, lowering his voice with smug arrogance. "Take some honest advice, friend. The Waldorf ballroom is for people who sign hundreds of millions of yuan in shipping contracts. President Qin is polite, so she brings you along out of family duty, yet standing here loading plates of pastries only makes Haiyue Group look ridiculous. Why not wait in the lounge near the coat check until she is ready to leave?"

Chen Xuan did not frown, nor did his relaxed expression cool by a single degree. He simply lifted the warm cup of oolong tea in his left hand and looked at Wu Junming with an easy, amused smile.

"Mr. Wu, right?" Chen Xuan asked lightly. "You seem remarkably worried about my wife's reputation. Strange, though. She never once complained about my job, and she definitely prefers warm pastries over listening to empty bragging."

Wu Junming's smile hardened. "You have a bold mouth for a clerk who rides in on his wife's name. Let us see how long President Qin tolerates you embarrassing her in front of real businessmen."

"Embarrassing me?" a clear, cool voice asked from right behind Wu Junming.

The snickering died at once. Wu Junming spun around, his smug sneer twisting into an eager, flattering grin the instant he saw Qin Yuhan approaching in her midnight-blue silk gown.

"President Qin!" Wu Junming stepped forward quickly, raising his glass. "What a pleasure to see you tonight! I was just telling your husband how much I would love to discuss a coastal freight partnership with Haiyue Group over a drink."

Qin Yuhan did not even glance at his raised glass. She walked straight past him, stopped beside Chen Xuan, and her cool executive poise melted immediately into a bright, genuine smile.

"You remembered the honey chestnut cakes," she said warmly, taking the porcelain plate directly from Chen Xuan's hand. She picked up a warm cake, took a small bite, and let out a quiet sigh of relief. "I was starving after those afternoon meetings. Thank you, Chen Xuan."

"Drink the tea slowly," Chen Xuan said, handing her the warm cup. "It is still hot."

Only then did Qin Yuhan turn her gaze back to the frozen Wu Junming. Her tone remained calm and polite, yet every word carried unmistakable steel. "Mr. Wu, my husband is the exact reason Haiyue Group avoided a 300 million yuan mistake this morning. As for a coastal freight partnership with your family, Haiyue Group only works with partners who understand basic manners. Excuse us."

Wu Junming's face flushed dark red as nearby guests whispered behind their hands. Unable to squeeze out a single reply, he stiffly backed away into the crowd while his two friends stared down at their shoes.

Chen Xuan offered Qin Yuhan his arm with a lazy grin. "Shall we escape the noisy crowd, President Qin?"

"Gladly," Qin Yuhan laughed, slipping her hand into the crook of his arm.

They stepped out through the tall French doors onto the quiet stone terrace overlooking the Huangpu River. In the cool autumn breeze, with the golden lights of Lujiazui shimmering across the dark water, they shared the rest of the warm pastries in peaceful silence, looking far happier than anyone left inside the glittering ballroom.
