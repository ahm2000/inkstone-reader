# Chapter 123: Pastries at the Bund Gala

By the following evening, the historic crystal ballroom of the Waldorf Hotel on the Bund was glowing with chandeliers and quiet chatter as the annual Shanghai Chamber of Commerce Charity Gala hit full swing. Outside the tall arched French windows, sightseeing boats drifted slowly along the Huangpu River toward the lit towers of Lujiazui.

Earlier that day on the 38th floor of Haiyue Tower, Qin Yuhan had laid Chen Xuan's newspaper clipping on the conference table and dismantled Director Zhao and Qin Haoran's 300 million yuan warehouse trap in front of the entire board. Director Zhao had turned pale and stammered an apology while senior shareholders praised her foresight. With follow-up meetings running late, she had barely had time to change into a midnight-blue silk gown and drive to the Bund with Chen Xuan, skipping dinner altogether.

Now, while several Chamber of Commerce directors greeted Qin Yuhan near the marble fountain, Chen Xuan strolled over to the long white buffet table in his clean dark blue suit.

Ignoring the tall towers of chilled champagne and fancy caviar spoons, Chen Xuan picked up a warm porcelain plate and a pair of silver tongs. With quiet focus, he selected two flaky almond tarts, three miniature vanilla custard puffs, and two freshly baked honey-glazed chestnut cakes. Beside them, he poured a small porcelain cup of hot oolong tea.

"Well, look at this," a sharp, mocking voice spoke up right beside his elbow. "I wondered who was clearing out the dessert table before the charity auction even started. Do you think you are at a neighborhood buffet?"

Chen Xuan turned calmly. Standing a step away was a tall young man in a shiny burgundy tuxedo, holding a crystal champagne flute. A silver lapel pin marked him as Wu Junming, the younger heir of a mid-sized Shanghai coastal shipping family.

Two other dressed-up guests lingered behind Wu Junming, smirking as they looked Chen Xuan up and down. Chen Xuan's dark blue suit was neat and comfortable, yet it carried no loud designer logo, and his wristwatch was a plain steel model worth a few hundred yuan.

"The chestnut cakes are still warm," Chen Xuan replied mildly, setting one last almond tart onto his plate. "Cold champagne gives you a stomachache on an empty stomach. You should try one."

Wu Junming let out a short, condescending laugh. "I heard President Qin of Haiyue Group brought her husband tonight. Wait a second. Are you the logistics clerk from that tiny Hengxin company on the 12th floor? Earning 12,000 yuan a month?"

One of the guests behind him chuckled loudly. "No wonder he is piling up free pastries! At 12,000 yuan a month, a single charity ticket here costs half his salary."

Wu Junming leaned closer, lowering his voice with smug arrogance. "Listen to some honest advice, friend. The Waldorf ballroom is for people who move hundreds of millions of yuan in shipping contracts. President Qin is polite, so she brings you along out of family duty, but standing here stuffing your face with cakes only makes Haiyue Group look ridiculous. Why not stay in the lounge near the coat check until she is ready to leave?"

Chen Xuan did not frown, nor did his expression cool by a single degree. He simply picked up the warm cup of oolong tea in his left hand and looked at Wu Junming with an easy, amused smile.

"Mr. Wu, right?" Chen Xuan asked lightly. "You seem very worried about my wife's reputation. Strange, though. She never once complained about how much I earn, and she definitely prefers warm pastries over listening to empty bragging."

Wu Junming's smile stiffened. "You have a sharp tongue for a clerk who lives off his wife's name. Let us see how long President Qin tolerates you embarrassing her in front of real businessmen."

"Embarrassing me?" a clear, cool voice asked from right behind Wu Junming.

The laughter died instantly. Wu Junming spun around, his smug sneer melting into an eager, flattering grin the moment he saw Qin Yuhan approaching in her midnight-blue silk gown.

"President Qin!" Wu Junming stepped forward quickly, raising his glass. "What a pleasure! I was just telling your husband that I would love to discuss a coastal freight partnership with Haiyue Group over a drink tonight."

Qin Yuhan did not even glance at his raised glass. She walked straight past him, stopped beside Chen Xuan, and her cool expression softened immediately into a bright, genuine smile.

"You remembered the honey chestnut cakes," she said warmly, taking the porcelain plate directly from Chen Xuan's hand. She picked up a warm cake, took a small bite, and closed her eyes briefly in relief. "I was starving. Thank you, Chen Xuan."

"Drink the tea slowly," Chen Xuan said, handing her the warm cup. "It is hot."

Only then did Qin Yuhan turn her gaze back to the frozen Wu Junming. Her tone remained polite, yet every word carried unmistakable steel. "Mr. Wu, my husband is the reason Haiyue Group avoided a 300 million yuan mistake this morning. As for a coastal freight partnership with your family, Haiyue Group only works with partners who know basic manners. Excuse us."

Wu Junming's face flushed dark red as nearby guests whispered behind their hands. Unable to say a single word, he stiffly stepped back into the crowd.

Chen Xuan offered Qin Yuhan his arm with a lazy grin. "Shall we escape the noisy crowd, President Qin?"

"Gladly," Qin Yuhan laughed, slipping her hand into the crook of his arm.

They walked out through the tall French doors onto the quiet stone terrace overlooking the Huangpu River. In the cool autumn breeze, with the golden lights of Lujiazui shimmering across the dark water, they shared the rest of the warm pastries in peaceful silence, looking far happier than anyone inside the glittering ballroom.
