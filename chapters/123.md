# Chapter 123: Pastries at the Bund Gala

On Wednesday evening, the historic crystal ballroom of the Waldorf Hotel on the Bund glowed with warm golden light. Outside the arched French windows, sightseeing boats drifted past along the Huangpu River toward Lujiazui and Jin Mao Tower. Inside, the annual Shanghai Chamber of Commerce Charity Gala was in full swing.

Because Qin Yuhan had easily crushed Director Zhao and Qin Haoran's 300 million yuan warehouse trap at her morning board meeting with Chen Xuan's newspaper clipping, her afternoon meetings at Haiyue Tower had run late. She had not eaten dinner before changing into a navy silk gown and driving to the Bund with Chen Xuan.

Now, while Chamber of Commerce directors greeted Qin Yuhan near the fountain, Chen Xuan strolled over to the long white buffet table in his dark blue suit.

Ignoring the champagne towers, Chen Xuan picked up a warm plate and silver tongs. With careful focus, he selected three freshly baked roast pork puff pastries, two warm red bean tarts, and a small bowl of warm almond soup.

Twenty feet away, three wealthy heirs from prominent Shanghai real estate families stood holding champagne flutes. Among them was Young Master Zhou Kaiming, whose family owned two plazas near Nanjing Road and who had tried to ask Qin Yuhan to dinner three times last year before finding out she had married an office clerk.

"Look over there by the pastry tray," Zhou Kaiming sneered softly to his two companions, tilting his champagne glass toward Chen Xuan. "That is President Qin Yuhan's husband. Everyone on the Bund heard she turned down half the young executives in Lujiazui, and for what? For a twelve-thousand-yuan logistics clerk who comes to a top business gala just to fill his plate at the free buffet."

"Look at how seriously he is checking which pork pastry is warmer," one of the young heirs laughed behind his hand. "Right now, the four regional presidents of China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China are standing by the VIP doors talking about next quarter's shipping credit lines. We have been waiting twenty minutes just to hand them our business cards, while President Qin's clerk is hunting for red bean tarts!"

As the three young heirs snickered, Qin Yuhan finished excusing herself from the Chamber of Commerce directors and walked straight over to the buffet table.

"Look at you, smiling like a cat that found the fish jar," Qin Yuhan whispered with a bright smile as Chen Xuan handed her the warm porcelain plate. "What did you find for me?"

"Roast pork puff pastries that came out of the oven two minutes ago, plus warm almond soup so your stomach does not ache," Chen Xuan grinned, shielding her slightly from the crowd with his shoulder so she could eat in peace. "Eat two before they cool down. If anyone tries to bother you with business cards while you are chewing, I will tell them to get in line behind the red bean tarts."

Qin Yuhan laughed softly, picked up a warm roast pork pastry with a napkin, and took a grateful bite. The flaky crust and warm filling immediately chased away the hunger from her six-hour afternoon at Haiyue Tower.

Across the carpet, Zhou Kaiming shook his head in disdain. "Eating puff pastries in the corner while the four bank presidents walk across the hall. Let us go greet President Lin of China Construction Bank before someone else steps in front of us."

Zhou Kaiming and his two companions straightened their silk ties, held out their gold-embossed business cards with both hands, and hurried toward the four silver-haired men in dark suits who had just entered the main ballroom.

Those four men were the highest banking authorities in Shanghai: the regional presidents of China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China.

Only thirty minutes earlier, all four bank presidents had received a strict directive from Number 11 of the White Order: the young master of the 300 trillion yuan sovereign reserve was accompanying his wife at the gala tonight as an ordinary clerk. They were to show proper courtesy to President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group, greet her husband politely, and under no circumstances reveal a single word of Chen Xuan's true identity!

"President Lin! President Zhao! I am Zhou Kaiming from Hongda Properties on Nanjing Road," Zhou Kaiming bowed eagerly, stepping into their path. "Could I have thirty seconds to introduce our new commercial plaza?"

President Lin of China Construction Bank barely glanced at the gold card. He nodded half an inch, said, "Excuse us, we see an important guest," and walked right past Zhou Kaiming's frozen hands without breaking stride!

The four regional bank presidents walked straight across the ballroom floor and stopped two paces in front of the pastry table, where Chen Xuan was holding Qin Yuhan's small bowl of almond soup while she finished her second puff pastry.

"President Qin, good evening," President Lin of China Construction Bank smiled warmly, while the regional heads of Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China all bowed their heads with deep respect. "And Mr. Chen, good evening to you as well. We heard from our colleagues in Beijing that the two of you just returned from celebrating Elder Qin Zhengguo's seventy-eighth birthday. Are the pastries and hot tea here tonight to your liking, Mr. Chen?"

Chen Xuan smiled calmly, holding the small porcelain spoon in one hand. "The roast pork pastries are quite good, President Lin, though the hotel's red tea is not as fragrant as what I brew in my green thermos at the office."

"Haha, Mr. Chen has true taste in tea!" all four bank presidents laughed warmly, chatting with Qin Yuhan for three minutes about guaranteeing Haiyue Group's lowest priority interest rates for the entire year before excusing themselves so the young couple could finish supper in peace.

Twenty feet away, Zhou Kaiming and his two companions stood with their mouths wide open.

"Wait, did you hear what President Lin said?" one heir whispered in shock. "They just returned from Elder Qin Zhengguo's seventy-eighth birthday in Beijing! The four bank presidents are giving top respect to Elder Qin Zhengguo and the Beijing Qin family!"

Even Qin Yuhan blinked in pleasant surprise beside the buffet table, turning to Chen Xuan with a soft smile. "Grandfather's old friends in Beijing must have put in a word for us. Even the four Shanghai bank presidents came over to be polite to you!"

"Must be Grandfather's prestige," Chen Xuan agreed with an innocent grin, holding a spoonful of warm almond soup up to his wife's lips. "Come on, President Qin, finish your soup before those red bean tarts get cold."
