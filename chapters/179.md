# Chapter 179: The White Bank Card

For two full seconds, nobody inside Valenti Cashmere moved.

Then Zhou Mei burst into shrill laughter, pointing at the matte-white card. "What is this? A cheap motel keycard? A library card? It does not even have a bank name or a card number!"

Zhao Kai glanced down and laughed loudly. "Unbelievable! He pulled out blank plastic to pretend he is rich! Cashier, swipe it anyway! Let us hear the machine beep red so these three can start crawling!"

The young cashier glanced nervously at Chen Xuan's calm eyes. Seeing him nod, she took the matte-white card with both hands and tapped it against the Huaxia Bank private-client terminal.

There was no red decline beep.

Instead, the screen flashed bright gold. A rare triple chime echoed through the silent boutique, and the terminal locked itself with a bold silver notice: Supreme Sovereign Private Reserve. Manual Director Clearance Required.

The cashier stared at the screen, her mouth open. "Manager Zhou, the terminal locked and sent a priority alert to the branch."

"Nonsense!" Zhou Mei snapped, reaching for the machine. "The network is glitching because of his fake card!"

Before her fingers touched the keypad, rapid footsteps echoed down the marble corridor. Two men in tailored suits rushed through the glass doorway, both breathing hard and pale as paper.

In front was Gu Wenbin, General Manager of Plaza 66. Right behind him hurried Lin Zhiyuan, Private Banking Director of Huaxia Bank Shanghai, clutching a velvet card tray and a handheld scanner, cold sweat glistening on his forehead.

Seeing Gu Wenbin, Zhao Kai puffed out his chest. "Manager Gu! Perfect timing! Three rural freeloaders wandered in waving a fake card and bothering your VIP customers!"

Gu Wenbin brushed straight past Zhao Kai without a glance, his eyes locking onto the matte-white card beside the glowing terminal.

Lin Zhiyuan stepped up with trembling fingers and held his scanner over the card. It emitted a deep tone and displayed an encrypted clearance seal he had only ever seen in confidential headquarters manuals.

There was only one matte-white reserve card like this in the entire country. Its holder's real name was sealed above even the bank's board.

Lin Zhiyuan's face drained of color. He set the scanner down, stepped back, and bowed at a crisp ninety-degree angle toward Chen Xuan.

"Distinguished Guest!" Lin Zhiyuan's voice shook. "I am Lin Zhiyuan, Private Banking Director of Huaxia Bank Shanghai. Forgive our slow arrival and any inconvenience!"

Gu Wenbin bowed deeply beside him, rigid with awe. "Distinguished Guest, I am Gu Wenbin, General Manager of Plaza 66. It is our deepest failure that you were disturbed here. Please tell us what happened!"

The boutique fell dead silent.

Zhao Kai's smug grin cracked completely. His companion stumbled back in terror, and Zhou Mei went stiff as stone, her blood turning ice-cold.

Chen Xuan leaned lightly against the counter. "Nothing big," he said mildly. "I just wanted two cashmere shawls for my elders. Manager Zhou worried my cousin's jacket might shed lint, and this gentleman offered to teach us how to crawl out the door."

Gu Wenbin straightened slowly, his face dark as a storm. He turned to Zhou Mei.

"Take off your badge," he said coldly.

"Manager Gu, please, I did not know!" Zhou Mei gasped, dropping to her knees and clutching his trousers. "Give me one more chance!"

"Security!" Gu Wenbin barked toward four mall guards at the entrance. "Remove Zhou Mei immediately. She is fired on the spot and blacklisted from every luxury retail group in Shanghai."

Two guards lifted the weeping Zhou Mei and escorted her out.

Gu Wenbin turned his cold gaze onto Zhao Kai, whose legs were trembling.

"Manager Gu, there is a misunderstanding!" Zhao Kai stammered. "My father is Zhao Ming of Dingcheng Real Estate! We partner with Huaxia Bank!"

Lin Zhiyuan tapped an authorization code on his phone and spoke quietly. "Risk Control? Suspend Dingcheng Real Estate's 150 million credit line immediately. Freeze all pending project disbursements for a compliance audit."

He lowered his phone and looked coldly at Zhao Kai. "Your family no longer has a partnership with Huaxia Bank, Mr. Zhao."

"No! My father will kill me!" Zhao Kai cried as two security guards gripped his arms.

"Escort Mr. Zhao and his guest out," Gu Wenbin ordered cleanly. "Put him on the permanent non-entry list."

Within thirty seconds, the boutique was quiet again. Chen Hao and Chen Yuan stood frozen behind Chen Xuan, staring wide-eyed at the empty doorway.

Chen Xuan slipped his matte-white card back into his pocket and spoke to Gu Wenbin and Lin Zhiyuan in a quiet, easy voice. "My wife is finishing a phone call across the hall. She does not like big scenes, and she does not need to hear about any of this."

Both men understood instantly. Gu Wenbin exhaled softly, straightened his tie, and composed his face into a warm hospitality smile. Lin Zhiyuan bowed once more, quietly saw to the wrapping of the two shawls in silk ribbon boxes, and slipped discreetly out a side door.

Seconds later, Qin Yuhan walked into Valenti Cashmere, tucking her phone away. She blinked in pleasant surprise when she saw the General Manager of Plaza 66 personally handing her husband two gift boxes with a respectful bow.

"President Qin!" Gu Wenbin greeted her smoothly. "What a pleasure to welcome Haiyue Group to Plaza 66. Please accept these two shawls with our compliments. No charge at all."

Qin Yuhan smiled graciously, slightly touched. "Manager Gu, you are far too polite. We really should pay."

"Please, President Qin, insist no further," Gu Wenbin replied warmly. "Haiyue Group's work in Shanghai has earned everyone's respect. Consider it a small token of goodwill."

Qin Yuhan thanked him sincerely, assuming Plaza 66 was simply extending VIP courtesy to Haiyue Group. As they walked out carrying the wrapped boxes, she smiled at Chen Xuan. "The Haiyue name really carries weight around Jing'an now."

"Of course it does," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, taking her hand. "My wife is a famous president, after all."

Behind them, Chen Hao and Chen Yuan exchanged a completely speechless glance while Chen Xuan glanced back and gave them a tiny wink.
