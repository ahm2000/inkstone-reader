# Chapter 179: The White Bank Card

Chen Xuan drew a plain matte-white card from the inside pocket of his windbreaker and held it toward the counter.

The card had no flashy gold trim and no loud VIP slogans. Its smooth matte-white metal surface carried only a tiny silver serial line on the back and the faint dual seal of China Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank's highest private vault.

Silence hung in the boutique for two seconds.

Then Zhao Kai burst into loud laughter, pointing straight at the matte-white card.

"What on earth is that?" Zhao Kai jeered, leaning against the marble counter. "A budget hotel room key? Did you print a blank plastic card at a copy shop?"

Sun Tingting covered her mouth, her eyes crinkling with scorn. "Kai, look how plain it is! There is not even a bank name on the front! My gym card looks nicer than that!"

Store manager Zhou Mei crossed her arms, raising her chin high. "Sir, I have worked at Plaza 66 for eight years. I have seen gold cards and black cards from every major bank. Whatever white toy you are holding will not pay for 144k yuan of cashmere. Stop embarrassing yourself."

Chen Xuan did not lower his hand. His voice remained steady.

"Swipe it," Chen Xuan said calmly. "If it declines, we walk out right now and I transfer 100k to your shop for your time. If it clears, you apologize to my brother."

Zhou Mei narrowed her eyes, eager to humiliate him in front of Zhao Kai. A smug grin curled her red lips.

"Fine!" Zhou Mei snapped, snatching the matte-white card between two fingers. "Let us watch it decline! Young Master Zhao, you are my witness!"

She turned briskly to the counter, keyed in 144k on the card terminal, and tapped the matte-white card across the reader.

Everyone waited for the dull red buzz of a declined card.

It never came.

Instead, the terminal screen flashed bright white. A crisp double chime rang out through the quiet shop, followed by bold letters scrolling across the display.

LEVEL ONE SOVEREIGN WHITE VAULT. CHINA CONSTRUCTION BANK AND HUAXIA BANK JOINT PRIORITY. DIRECTOR ALERT SENT.

Zhou Mei blinked, her smug smile freezing. "What? Why did it lock up? Did this fake card break my machine?"

Before she could press cancel, the black landline behind the counter rang sharply.

Zhou Mei snatched up the receiver. "Hello, Valenti Cashmere, Manager Zhou speaking."

She barely finished her sentence when a harsh, breathless voice barked from the earpiece loud enough for Lin Xiao to hear two meters away. Every drop of color drained from Zhou Mei's painted cheeks.

"Yes! Yes, General Manager Gu! Nobody is moving! I will wait right here!" Zhou Mei stammered, her hand shaking so badly she nearly dropped the phone.

Zhao Kai frowned, sensing the sudden chill in the air. "Hey, Manager Zhou, what is wrong with your face?"

Zhou Mei could not even answer. She stared down at the matte-white card on the counter as if it had turned into hot coal.

Less than three minutes later, hurried footsteps echoed down the marble hallway.

Two men in dark suits hurried off the escalator at a half-run, out of breath and wiping sweat from their foreheads. The first was Gu Wenbin, General Manager of Plaza 66. Beside him hurried Lin Zhiyuan, Private-Banking Director of Huaxia Bank's Lujiazui Flagship Branch, who had been upstairs in a meeting when the joint Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank alert hit his phone.

Zhao Kai recognized Gu Wenbin immediately and stepped forward with a cocky grin.

"Uncle Gu! Good timing!" Zhao Kai called out. "Your manager was just throwing out a troublemaker with a fake white card!"

Gu Wenbin brushed straight past Zhao Kai without a single glance. Both men rushed to the counter, spotted the matte-white card, and bowed deeply from the waist before Chen Xuan.

"Sir!" Lin Zhiyuan said, lifting the card with both hands, his voice tight with respect. "I am Lin Zhiyuan, Private-Banking Director of Huaxia Bank Lujiazui Branch. Huaxia Bank and China Construction Bank received a priority alert from this terminal three minutes ago. Did someone offend you?"

Gu Wenbin wiped sweat from his temple. "Sir, I am Gu Wenbin, General Manager of Plaza 66. Whatever happened here is my responsibility."

Dead silence fell over the shop. Zhao Kai's grin shattered, his mouth dropping open as he stared at the bowing director, the sweating mall manager, and the calm man in a cheap windbreaker. Sun Tingting gasped and stepped back. Chen Yuan and Chen Hao stood stunned.

Chen Xuan took his matte-white card back calmly.

"The coats are fine," Chen Xuan said evenly. "A customer's bag knocked a hanger loose. My brother caught it before it fell. Your manager slapped his hand away and insulted him. And that young man thinks Plaza 66 belongs to his family."

Gu Wenbin spun around, his face stone-cold.

"Zhou Mei," Gu Wenbin barked. "Put your badge on the counter. You are dismissed on the spot and barred from Plaza 66."

Zhou Mei's knees buckled, tears streaking her cheeks. "General Manager Gu, please! Little brother, I am sorry!"

"Security!" Gu Wenbin snapped. Two mall guards escorted the weeping Zhou Mei out.

Gu Wenbin turned his cold eyes onto Zhao Kai, whose face was pale as chalk.

"Uncle Gu, my father is Zhao Yongsheng of Kaiyuan Real Estate!" Zhao Kai stammered.

"Your father can explain your manners himself," Gu Wenbin replied icily. "You are permanently barred from Plaza 66's VIP lounges."

Director Lin Zhiyuan adjusted his glasses and spoke quietly. "Young Master Zhao, Kaiyuan Real Estate has a 400 million yuan loan renewal on my desk at Huaxia Bank for Monday. After today, our risk committee will put that loan under immediate review."

Zhao Kai's legs went weak. Sun Tingting dropped his arm in panic.

"Sir! Little brother! I am sorry!" Zhao Kai choked out, bowing stiffly with burning shame. "Please tell Director Lin not to blame my father!"

Chen Xuan barely glanced at him. "Leave."

Red-faced and trembling, Zhao Kai fled out the doors, with Sun Tingting hurrying after him.

Gu Wenbin exhaled deeply and turned back to Chen Xuan with a humble smile. "Sir, please let Plaza 66 cover the bill for these coats as a small apology."

"No need," Chen Xuan replied calmly. "I pay for what my family wears. Miss Lin here was polite from the start. Let her ring up the regular price."

Gu Wenbin nodded at once. "Miss Lin, starting today, you are acting shift lead. Pack the coats with care."

"Yes, General Manager Gu! Thank you, Sir!" Lin Xiao beamed, packing the three coats into neat cream garment bags.

Chen Xuan tapped his matte-white card once more. With Director Lin Zhiyuan's thumbprint approval on his tablet, the payment cleared in one second. Declining Gu Wenbin's offer of a private car, Chen Xuan picked up the garment bags and walked calmly out of Plaza 66 beside his stunned brother and sister.
