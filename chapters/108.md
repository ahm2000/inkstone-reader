# Chapter 108: Dinner with the In-Laws by the Bund

At six o'clock on Sunday evening, golden lanterns lit the stone garden of Pine Courtyard, a quiet restaurant tucked one block behind the Bund. Through the second-floor window, the tall skyline of Lujiazui and the glowing spheres of the Oriental Pearl Tower shimmered across the water.

At the round rosewood table sat Qin Yuhan's parents, who had flown in from Beijing three hours earlier.

Her father, Qin Zhengming, wore a dark gray standing-collar jacket. He had the steady, upright posture of a longtime state industry director and sat reading a printed port briefing through wire-rimmed glasses. Beside him sat Qin Yuhan's mother, Lin Shuyin, dressed in an embroidered silk shawl and pearl earrings, her posture stiff with Beijing family pride.

When the sliding door opened, Qin Yuhan walked in alongside Chen Xuan.

Today Chen Xuan had left his green thermos in the Maybach's cup holder. Wearing the dark navy tailored suit from Plaza 66, his broad shoulders and calm, easygoing smile immediately filled the doorway.

For a brief second, even Lin Shuyin paused in surprise. She had pictured a timid clerk looking lost in an expensive restaurant. Instead, the young man walking in looked more composed than the ministers' sons she had met in Beijing.

Then Lin Shuyin reminded herself of his monthly paycheck and set her teacup down with a crisp click.

"Dad, Mom," Qin Yuhan said warmly, pulling out a chair. "This is Chen Xuan."

"Good evening, Uncle Qin, Aunt Lin," Chen Xuan said politely, setting the cedar box of aged Wuyi rock tea by Qin Zhengming's elbow before sitting down beside Qin Yuhan. "I hope the flight from Beijing was smooth. I brought a box of old rock tea for Uncle Qin to try."

Qin Zhengming took off his glasses, looked into Chen Xuan's clear eyes, and nodded. "Sit down, Chen Xuan. You look energetic."

Lin Shuyin did not touch the tea box. She looked at Chen Xuan's suit and spoke in a cool voice. "Yuhan bought you that suit yesterday at Plaza 66, did she not? Chen Xuan, I will speak plainly. In Beijing, our Qin family does not care only about money, yet marriage requires basic balance. Even Yuhan's grandfather, Elder Qin Zhengguo, frowned when he heard she married a twelve-thousand-yuan clerk at Hengxin Logistics."

"Mom," Qin Yuhan frowned slightly, setting her chopsticks down. "We came here for dinner, not an audit."

"It is fine, Yuhan," Chen Xuan smiled easily, picking up the warm porcelain teapot to refill Lin Shuyin's cup first, then Qin Zhengming's, and finally Qin Yuhan's. "Aunt Lin is just looking out for her daughter. Honestly, twelve thousand yuan is plenty for me. Our company canteen has great braised pork at noon, I clock out at four fifty-five every day without bringing stress home, and I have plenty of time in the evening to cook fresh soup for Yuhan."

Lin Shuyin nearly choked on her breath. She had expected him to blush in shame or promise to work eighty hours a week. Hearing him brag about clocking out at four fifty-five and eating canteen braised pork left her speechless for three seconds.

"You hear that, Zhengming?" Lin Shuyin turned to her husband in disbelief. "Yuhan manages billions at Haiyue Group from dawn till night, and this young man is proud of leaving work five minutes before five!"

Just then, the waiter brought in a platter of steamed river perch and crab roe tofu. Before Lin Shuyin could lecture further, Chen Xuan deftly used the serving chopsticks to pick the tenderest piece of fish belly, checked that it had zero small bones, and placed it into Qin Yuhan's bowl.

"Eat while it is hot," Chen Xuan told his wife softly. "You skipped lunch during that port call today."

Qin Yuhan's expression softened immediately. "Thank you. Have some bamboo shoots too."

Qin Zhengming watched the natural care between them and noticed that his daughter's usually tense shoulders were completely relaxed. He tapped the blue shipping folder beside his plate and looked at Chen Xuan.

"You work in freight logistics, Chen Xuan," Qin Zhengming said evenly. "Let me test your eye on something practical. Before I flew from Beijing, a colleague asked our shipping board to take over a discounted bonded warehouse lease in Jingzhou City, Handong Province. It used to belong to a subsidiary of Shanshui Group. The price is forty percent below market, only 150 million yuan for ten years. My assistants think it is a bargain. What do you think?"

Lin Shuyin let out a small huff. "Zhengming, why ask a twelve-thousand-yuan clerk about a 150 million yuan state port lease?"

Chen Xuan took a sip of warm water and wiped his mouth with a napkin.

"If it belongs to an old Shanshui Group subsidiary in Jingzhou City, Uncle Qin, I would not touch it even for ten yuan," Chen Xuan said conversationally.

Qin Zhengming's eyebrows rose slightly. "Oh? Why is that?"

"Because during the last three years at Shanshui Villa, Zhao Ruilong and Qi Tongwei used double-layered equipment liens on almost all their Jingzhou City port warehouses," Chen Xuan explained calmly. "On the surface lease paper, the warehouse looks clean. Underneath, the crane hardware and cold-chain docks were pledged three times over through shell companies tied to Gao Yuliang's old circle. Right now, Secretary Sha Ruijin, Secretary Li Dakang, Director Hou Liangping, and Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming are freezing every hidden second-tier debt linked to Shanshui Group across Handong Province. Anyone who buys that lease today inherits 400 million yuan in defaulted bank liens with ICBC and Bank of China tomorrow morning."

Complete silence fell over the rosewood table.

Qin Zhengming stared at Chen Xuan, his fingers tightening on his teacup. Every detail matched a confidential risk warning Qin Zhengming had received from the Ministry of Commerce two hours before boarding the plane!

"How did you know about the double equipment liens on the Jingzhou City cranes?" Qin Zhengming asked slowly.

Chen Xuan smiled and spooned crab roe tofu into Qin Yuhan's bowl. "Us logistics clerks read all kinds of port bulletins while waiting for container stamps at four-thirty, Uncle Qin. When freight drivers chat at the loading dock, secrets travel fast."

Qin Zhengming let out a long breath, his eyes gleaming with respect. "Dockside bulletins or not, you caught a 400 million yuan trap in ten seconds that three of my senior analysts missed all week."

Lin Shuyin pursed her lips stubbornly. "He just got lucky reading financial news online while slacking off at his desk! Come on, eat your fish before it gets cold."

Chen Xuan chuckled warmly and poured his mother-in-law more hot tea, while Qin Yuhan hid a proud smile behind her cup.
