# Chapter 108: One Text at the Harbor

Outside the clearance hall, the rain had thinned into a damp mist off the harbor.

Chen Xuan walked to Lane Two and handed the stamped release slip up to Zhao Kang in the blue Hengxin Logistics truck.

"You got it stamped already?" Zhao Kang asked, staring at the red seal. "Without paying a single yuan?"

"Everything on our sheet was legal," Chen Xuan said calmly. "Pull onto the Gate Seven scale, Master Zhao. I will walk beside the barrier."

Zhao Kang grinned, started the engine, and eased onto the weighbridge. The green light blinked above the gantry, confirming the weight matched the manifest.

Just as the yellow barrier arm began to lift, a battered white cargo van roared out of the brokerage strip and swerved sideways across the exit lane, stopping three meters in front of Hengxin's bumper and blocking Gate Seven completely.

Zhao Kang hit his air brakes and leaned out. "Hey! Move that van off the exit lane!"

Two burly men climbed out and leaned against its doors with smirks. Behind them walked Zhou Mingcheng, rolling an unlit cigarette between his fingers.

"Sorry, driver," Zhou Mingcheng called mockingly. "Our van just broke down on the exit apron. The repair truck will not arrive until tomorrow."

Zhao Kang flushed with anger. "You drove it right in front of me! That is deliberate blocking!"

"Watch your mouth," Zhou Mingcheng said coolly, fixing his stare on Chen Xuan. "You love quoting port rules, little clerk? See if there is a rule that fixes a broken van."

From the covered walkway, Qin Yuhan stepped out holding Haiyue Group's release slip. She paused with a frown, reaching for her phone to call the port duty office. Then she stopped, because Chen Xuan had not even blinked.

Chen Xuan did not shout or argue. He unscrewed his green thermos, took a slow sip of warm jasmine tea, and screwed the cap shut.

"Manager Zhou," Chen Xuan said evenly, "are you sure you want to leave that van parked across a customs weighbridge?"

"Very sure," Zhou Mingcheng sneered. "My uncle Zhou Desheng has sat in the port building for twelve years. If you want your truck out before noon, bow your head, apologize for embarrassing me inside, and wire the 200k fast-lane fee. Otherwise, your valves sit in the rain until Friday."

Chen Xuan nodded once, as if confirming the weather. He pulled out his plain black phone, opened a silent chat thread linked to Number 14's Shanghai desk, and typed four words.

Gate Seven. Zhou Mingcheng.

He pressed send and pocketed the phone.

Zhou Mingcheng saw the cheap phone case and laughed loudly. "Look at him! Texting his little supervisor at Hengxin Logistics! Go ahead, call your manager, call the police, call whoever you want. Let us see who dares touch Mingcheng Freight Services at Gate Seven!"

The two men by the van laughed along. Zhao Kang paced anxiously, checking his watch. Chen Xuan simply stood by the yellow railing with his hands on his thermos, watching the harbor cranes.

One minute passed. Two minutes. Four minutes.

Zhou Mingcheng tapped his gold watch with a smirk. "Well? Where is your rescue team, little clerk? Still on hold?"

At the five-minute mark, the glass doors of the main Shanghai Port Administration Building swung open. No sirens sounded. No horn blared.

A tall, stern-faced man in his late forties wearing a dark blue customs uniform strode straight down the dock road. Behind him marched six officers from the Customs Anti-Smuggling Bureau and two plain-clothed discipline inspectors carrying sealed folders. Every port guard along the walkway snapped to attention.

Zhou Mingcheng's smirk froze as he recognized the man in front. It was Shen Guohua, the newly appointed chief of the Shanghai Customs Anti-Smuggling Bureau, transferred in two days earlier during the provincial clean-up.

Zhou Mingcheng forced a tight smile and hurried forward with outstretched hands.

"Chief Shen! What brings you down to Gate Seven in this rain?" Zhou Mingcheng called smoothly. "If there is a yard issue, my uncle Zhou Desheng can send someone right over."

Shen Guohua walked straight past Zhou Mingcheng's outstretched hands as if he were a wooden post, stopping between the Hengxin truck and the white van.

"Are you Zhou Mingcheng, head of Mingcheng Freight Services?" Shen Guohua asked, his voice like cold iron.

"Yes, Chief Shen," Zhou Mingcheng said, his smile cracking. "My van just stalled, and we were about to push it aside."

"Save it," Shen Guohua cut him off flatly, nodding to an officer who opened a blue folder. "Effective immediately, Mingcheng Freight Services' port brokerage license is suspended. We have verified complaints of illegal 200k fast-lane extortion, interfering at Counter Three, and deliberately blocking a cleared customs weighbridge."

Zhou Mingcheng's knees went weak. "Wait, Chief Shen, there is a misunderstanding! Let me call my uncle Zhou Desheng right now!"

"You will not reach him," Shen Guohua said coldly. "Deputy Director Zhou Desheng was escorted out of his fourth-floor office four minutes ago by the provincial discipline team, on a list approved days earlier. His phone is already sealed."

Every drop of color drained from Zhou Mingcheng's face. The unlit cigarette slipped from his shaking fingers into a puddle.

"Hand Zhou Mingcheng and his two men over to the public security police," Shen Guohua ordered sharply. "Seal their office behind Counter Five and call the port tow truck to clear this van right now."

"Yes, Chief!"

Within thirty seconds, two officers gripped Zhou Mingcheng by the arms. The two burly men scrambled pale-faced into the white van and drove it off the lane before the tow truck arrived.

As the apron cleared, Shen Guohua stepped aside and raised a gloved hand, personally waving Hengxin Logistics' blue truck through Gate Seven. Zhao Kang leaned out, grinning in stunned relief, and rolled past the barrier.

Before turning back, Shen Guohua paused for a heartbeat. His gaze shifted briefly to the plain man in the faded windbreaker by the railing. He did not salute or speak, giving only a tiny, almost invisible nod of deep respect before turning swiftly away to keep Chen Xuan out of sight.

In the hall doorway, Lin Xiaodong and dozens of tired clerks let out a wave of relief.

On the walkway, Qin Yuhan stood completely still. She had watched Chen Xuan send a short text, counted five exact minutes on her watch, and caught that fleeting glance from the Anti-Smuggling chief.

Down by the gate, Chen Xuan tucked his green thermos into his satchel, watched Zhao Kang's truck merge onto the road, and walked calmly out Gate Seven into the damp street as if nothing had happened.
