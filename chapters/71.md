# Chapter 71: The 8:30 AM Roll Call

At eight twenty-five on Monday morning, the third-floor meeting room of the Yunxi County Government Compound smelled of cheap cigarettes and old tea. Forty county department heads sat stiffly around the tables, eyes jumping between the wall clock and their dark phone screens.

At the front table, County Magistrate Sun Weiming tapped ash with shaking fingers. Beside him, Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong leaned closer, collar tight against his sweaty neck.

"Did your city phone line come back up?" Sun Weiming asked quietly.

Liang Guodong forced a dry laugh. "Still dead. My clerk checked the phone center at seven. Workers swore a main cable snapped near the river bridge during the storm last night. Stop looking like you saw a ghost."

"There was no storm last night, Liang Guodong," Sun Weiming hissed. "The sky was clear at midnight. One minute I was drinking tea with Chairman Deng Yongchang's secretary. The next minute every phone tower in Yunxi County dropped to zero for three hours. Even my brother-in-law in the city office has not called back."

"City officials sleep late on Mondays," Liang Guodong muttered, pressing his silent phone. "By ten o'clock the lines will work. By noon, Old Street will be half cleared."

Sun Weiming nodded. "What about that hardware-store owner, Chen Mingde?"

"Locked tight in Holding Cell Two at Old Street Station," Liang Guodong said with a thin smile. "Cheng Biao handled the papers. Three days on a cold bench, and Chen Mingde will beg to sign over his shop."

Near the back, Land Resources Director Xiong Baolin leaned toward Urban Planning Chief Nie Hongbo. "Did you see the highway overpass at eight?" Xiong Baolin whispered. "Black provincial license plates. Over twenty vans came off the ramp."

Sun Weiming crushed his cigarette and tapped the microphone hard.

"Quiet!" Sun Weiming barked. Forty heads snapped up. "This is the Monday morning county roll call, not a vegetable market! Xiong Baolin, why are the Old Street land permits still unsigned on your desk?"

Xiong Baolin's face turned red. "County Magistrate Sun Weiming, the fire-safety check is not finished, and residents turned in signed complaints yesterday."

"There are no complaints!" Sun Weiming snapped, glaring down the aisle. "The Hongrui Real Estate building plan is a top county project! Anyone who slows it down answers to the Discipline Office personally! Open your binders to page four."

The heavy double doors at the back slammed open, brass handles cracking loud against the plaster wall.

Every voice stopped dead. Forty officials spun around.

A tall man in a dark gray suit marched down the aisle. Behind him came twelve officers in dark windbreakers bearing the gold badges of the Provincial Supervisory Commission and the Procuratorate. Four uniformed court officers blocked both doors.

Sun Weiming's hand froze on his teacup. All color drained from Liang Guodong's face in two heartbeats, leaving his skin gray.

The tall man stopped at the front table without offering a handshake. "I am Shen Wenchao, Deputy Team Leader of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group," he said sharply.

He slapped two red-stamped arrest warrants onto the wood. Sun Weiming's teacup rattled, spilling warm tea across his binder and sleeve.

"Sun Weiming," Shen Wenchao said, eyes locked on the magistrate. "Liang Guodong. Stand up."

For three seconds, neither moved. Xiong Baolin's pen rolled onto the tile floor with a loud clatter. Nobody picked it up.

"Wait a minute!" Sun Weiming blurted, shoving his chair back with a sweaty smile. "Comrade Shen Wenchao, there must be a mistake! Nobody told the Yunxi County Party Committee you were coming today! Even a normal check needs city notice first."

"This is not a normal check," Shen Wenchao cut in coldly. "By detention order of the Provincial Supervisory Commission, with arrest approval from the Provincial Procuratorate, you are both under arrest for taking bribes, abusing power, working with a crime group, and faking evidence."

Liang Guodong jumped up, face twisted with panic and rage. "Faking evidence? That is a lie! I have worn this badge for twenty-two years! You cannot march in here without telling City Secretary Lin Haibo! I demand to call the city office right now!"

"City Secretary Lin Haibo is answering questions of his own," Shen Wenchao replied without blinking. "As for your twenty-two years in uniform, Liang Guodong, perhaps you remember last Thursday night in the private bamboo room at Jade Spring Teahouse."

Liang Guodong's mouth froze open.

Shen Wenchao flipped open the top folder and read flatly. "At nine forty-two in the evening, Sun Weiming told Chairman Deng Yongchang, and I quote: Sign the sixty-percent discount price on the Old Street land tomorrow, and I will push the land stamp through before Monday. Send the 3 million fee to my nephew's Shenzhen bank account by Friday."

A sharp gasp swept through the forty officials.

"That is a lie!" Sun Weiming shouted, voice cracking thin as he gripped the table edge. "Fake recording! Somebody is setting me up! I never stepped foot inside Jade Spring Teahouse!"

Shen Wenchao turned the page and stared coldly at Liang Guodong. "Four minutes later, at nine forty-six, Liang Guodong raised his teacup to Deng Yongchang and said, and I quote: Give Cheng Biao the green light on Old Street. Lock up one stubborn hardware-store owner for seventy-two hours on an assault charge, and the whole street will give up before noon."

Silence crushed the room, broken only by Liang Guodong's wheezing breath.

Sun Weiming's knees gave out. He caught himself with both palms on his wet binder. His forgotten cigarette dropped onto his pants, burning a small black hole he did not feel.

"How did you get that?" Liang Guodong whispered, eyes wide with terror. "There were only three of us in that room. All windows were shut tight."

"Your bank transfers, your nephew's Shenzhen shell company, the fake hospital file, and Hongrui Real Estate's account books are already in provincial hands," Shen Wenchao said, snapping the folder shut. "Take them away."

Four officers stepped behind the front table.

"Do not touch me!" Sun Weiming shrieked, kicking and thrashing as strong hands twisted his arms behind his back. "I am the County Magistrate! Call Deng Yongchang!"

Steel handcuffs clicked shut around Sun Weiming's wrists. Beside him, Liang Guodong slumped without fighting back, his police cap tumbling off the table as a second pair of cuffs locked over his shaking hands.

Officers marched both gray-faced men down the aisle past forty stunned officials. Nobody dared meet their panicked eyes.

Once the doors closed, Shen Wenchao rested both hands on the front table.

"Starting at eight thirty this morning, the Provincial Joint Inspection Group takes full control of Yunxi County," Shen Wenchao said calmly. "Every Hongrui Real Estate demolition order is stopped now. Those with clean hands stay seated and prepare your account books. Those who took cash envelopes from Deng Yongchang have thirty minutes to walk into the side office and confess. After nine o'clock, there will be no mercy."

Nobody in the room dared to breathe.
