# Chapter 23: Three Options

Back on the balcony, Chen Xuan's phone buzzed a second time.

"Boss." Number One sounded pleased and a little wary. "Six of Hongrui's street crew, including Niu Dali and Yao Xiaolong, are sitting in Special Police vans. Two iron pipes are bagged as evidence, and nobody on our side was hurt. Two slipped away on foot: the black-van driver and Xie Boyuan, their bookkeeper."

Chen Xuan rubbed his forehead and let out a slow breath.

"I said keep them safe, Number One. I didn't ask for eighteen Special Police."

"You said they couldn't touch the tailor, Boss. The pipe was already swinging. Yao Xiaolong is eating a hot dumpling in the back of a police van right now."

"Is Yu Changshun hurt? What about the little girl?"

"Both fine, Boss. Shaken up, nothing more."

"Good." Chen Xuan's voice went quiet and flat. "Cheng Biao answers to the law now. He slapped a young officer over a cup of water, and he sent a van to run down an old tailor. Pull together everything he has done, every file. And in the morning, send the recording of Dong Haisheng's call, with the timestamp, to the bank that holds his loan."

Far away in a quiet courtyard, Number One's breath caught for half a second. Then he answered steadily, "Yes, Boss."

Inside the living room, Chen Hao pressed his nose against the glass door. "Who is he even talking to out there?"

Chen Yuan watched her brother's straight back through the pane. "Work, he said. He's carrying more than he lets us see."

* * *

Number One sat in a quiet courtyard in the east of Qingyan City with a clean sheet of paper on his desk. On it, he had written two lines in his neat, old-fashioned hand.

*Option One.*

*Option Two.*

He stared at them for a long moment, then added a third.

The desk speaker crackled. "Number One, this is Twenty-three. I have the full count."

"Money first."

"Yunxi approved six hundred forty million yuan to relocate Old Street and the three blocks behind it, about 1,100 households and shops. At least one hundred ninety million never reached a single family. It flowed into three companies: Hongrui Real Estate, Ankang Private Clinic, and a demolition firm owned by Cheng Biao's brother-in-law."

Number One's pen stopped. Yan Tiehu's full confession, taken by Zero off the ring road on Friday night, had reached his desk that afternoon. It named every rival and every debt, and Hongrui's money was only its first page.

"The same clinic that wrote the injury slip."

"The very one. The slip says the driver was treated at 1:50. Yu Changshun saw him throw himself into the paint shelf at 2:03."

"Treated thirteen minutes before he was hurt." Number One let out a dry breath that was almost a laugh. "Quite a fast doctor."

"One more thing." Number Twenty-three's voice softened. "Last night, Wei Kun brought a cup of hot water to the old man in the cell. Cheng Biao slapped him for it. This morning, Cheng Biao sent poisoned tea to Wei Kun through Lei Bo. Our records clerk spilled it, and we kept a sample."

"Who told Cheng Biao what time Wei Kun wrote on the notice?"

"Zhong Hai, his desk neighbor. His son has a heart condition, and Cheng Biao offered a sixty-thousand-yuan loan. He hasn't eaten a bite since."

"And Cheng Biao himself? Has he looked at the Boss?"

"He ran the Boss's name through the system this afternoon. All he found was an ordinary clerk."

Number One nodded. "His public file is real and completely unremarkable. His sealed file sits where nobody below the very top can touch it. If anyone tries, the screen shows one line, permission denied, and the highest level asks why they were looking."

He added a smaller line under his options: *Wei Kun's wife.*

"Apartment 302 is ready for her," Number Twenty-three said. "Number Ninety-five will run into her at the pharmacy and arrange a proper bed."

Number One turned toward the window. "Number One Hundred and Four."

The Black Pine Guard's lawyer stood by the glass in a gray suit, holding a thin black folder. "Whenever you give the word, Number One."

"Not mine." Number One picked up his phone. "His."

* * *

In Room 601, Chen Hao spoke up from the sofa. "Big Brother, you've been staring at your phone for ten minutes."

"I keep thinking about Second Uncle." Chen Xuan rubbed the bridge of his nose. "It's cold in there."

In the kitchen, Liu Meifang packed a steel thermos with pork-rib soup, blinking back tears. "This is for your uncle. He's fifty-two. He can't stomach holding-cell food." She pointed the ladle at Chen Xuan. "And you, look how thin you've gotten. Eat something."

Chen Xuan's phone buzzed. "Work," he murmured, and stepped onto the balcony.

"Boss. Three options," Number One said. "First, our lawyer files a formal complaint at the city Discipline Commission tonight. It has the clinic slip, the honest detention notice, Yu Changshun's statement, the street footage and the money trail. Your uncle could be home by morning. Every official the Zero Guard visited has kept a black card in a drawer since Friday night. None of them will dare lose a folder this week."

"Home by morning," Chen Xuan repeated quietly.

"Second, we hand everything to the provincial inspection team on Wednesday. That means four more nights in that cell."

"No. Not four more nights."

"Third, I make one call tonight. Someone in the provincial capital would tear Yunxi's police bureau open before breakfast. It is the fastest and the loudest. A dozen shopkeepers and one honest duty officer would be buried in the fallout. I recommend the first."

"Option One," Chen Xuan said. "Through the front door, with a stamped receipt."

"Yes, Boss."

"Number One, I also want a piece of the third. Not the bureau. Just Hongrui's street crew." He spoke in the same even tone he might use to order tea. "Find Xie Boyuan, his ledger and the black-van driver tonight."

"Under what charges, Boss?"

"Every one that fits. Assault, wrecking shops, extortion, threatening a child. They beat an old man in the street, then went to a tailor's door with a steel pipe. That's twice. Whoever carried that pipe faces assault with a weapon and threatening a minor. Not ten days in lockup. Years. Make sure he understands that in the van, before he speaks to anyone."

There was no raised voice in it, and no anger on the surface. That was exactly what made it hard to forget.

"It will be done, Boss. People on the street are calling the spilled tea and the bread truck pure luck. Cheng Biao thinks Wei Kun has a guardian angel."

"Good. Let them think so. Thank Number Thirty-four and the bread-truck driver for me."

"And Zhong Hai, Boss? The man who leaked the timestamp."

"He sold out a colleague for a sixty-thousand-yuan loan, and an honest man almost drank poison for it. He faces charges on Monday."

"And his son?"

"The boy didn't sell anyone out. Hold a bed for Zhong Tiantian on the fourth floor of People's Hospital. Tell his father nothing. If Zhong Hai walks up to Wei Kun's desk before Monday and confesses on his own, I'll reconsider."

"Yes, Boss."

"Also send a hot meal and a blanket to my second uncle through the family window, like any relative would. And nobody touches Wei Kun. Not his wife's ward, not his mortgage, not his desk. He passed a cup of water through the bars and wrote the true hour when lying was easier. Repay that ten times over. And keep someone on Yu Ning's walk to her mother's workshop until she is grown. Her grandfather stood in his doorway facing a steel pipe."

Number One's throat tightened. Three ways to topple a county's leaders lay on his desk, and Chen Xuan had spent half his words on soup, a blanket and a child's walk to the workshop.

"The beef noodles will be piping hot, Boss," he said softly. "With an extra egg."

"Thank you, Number One. Really."

Chen Xuan ended the call and found Chen Yuan standing in the doorway with his green thermos.

"It went cold, so I warmed it up." She pressed it into his hand. "Don't say anything, Big Brother. Just drink it."

She hurried back inside, dabbing her eyes with her sleeve.

Chen Hao leaned out of the kitchen. "Big Brother, why are you smiling?"

Chen Xuan chuckled. "I'm just glad I'm early for Mom's soup."
