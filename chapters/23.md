# Chapter 23: The Bank Vault in Lanshui

Two hundred kilometers west of Yunxi County, rain fell steadily over Lanshui after dusk. On the nineteenth floor of an unmarked building in the financial district, Black Pine Guard's regional Financial Intelligence room sat dark except for the pale glow of forty monitors.

Every analyst wore a plain charcoal jacket with no badge, no nameplate, and no personal phone. Inside Black Pine Guard, nobody knew another member's real name or face. Operatives addressed one another strictly by number, bound together only by discipline and loyalty to Chen Xuan.

At the center console, Number Eleven stood behind Number Seventy-three's chair, watching hexadecimal settlement packets cascade down the screen. Number Seventy-three typed with a steady rhythm, running a decryption pipeline against a forty-terabyte disk image.

Twelve hours earlier, Black Pine field technicians had physically mirrored that image from hidden, water-cooled clearing servers inside a private sub-basement vault beneath a commercial bank branch in downtown Lanshui. On paper, the vault held escrow archives for regional infrastructure bonds. In reality, the servers belonged to Shengyuan Capital, the parent syndicate behind Hongrui Real Estate and the demolition along Yunxi County's Old Street.

"Secondary routing key is holding at layer four," Number Seventy-three said flatly. "They rotate the hardware handshake every six hours using a seed tied to municipal land-auction escrow timestamps."

"Which auction cycle?" Number Eleven asked quietly.

"Yunxi County, last autumn. Brute-force passes keep missing by several minutes."

Number Eleven leaned forward, eyes sharpening. "Use the Yunxi County auction ledger from last November. Hongrui Real Estate won parcel seven at thirty percent below reserve price on a Friday afternoon. Their escrow deposit cleared through the Lanshui branch eleven minutes early. Feed that eleven-minute offset into the derivation."

Number Seventy-three's brow twitched in quiet recognition. He entered the delta and struck execute. For four seconds, white code held frozen on the screen. Then the hash flashed green, and layer four peeled open.

Encrypted SWIFT mirror logs poured across the central display wall, automatically parsed by Black Pine's forensic engine into clean relational graphs.

"We are inside," Number Seventy-three murmured.

At first glance, the surface layer looked like standard provincial corruption. Shell contractors billed inflated concrete and steel prices on highway projects. County land bureaus signed discounted transfers to Hongrui Real Estate. Unrecorded rare-earth ore from the illegal tunnel beneath Yunxi's Old Street was blended into legitimate quarry manifests.

"Routine county-to-province graft," Number Eleven observed coolly. "Expand the deep-clearing partition beneath the domestic escrow tables."

Number Seventy-three opened the partition. Within two seconds, his fingers stopped dead over the keyboard. His shoulders went rigid.

"Number Eleven," Number Seventy-three said, his flat tone dropping half an octave. "This is not construction skimming."

Number Eleven stepped closer to the wall. Transaction vectors bloomed outward from Lanshui, crossing the coastal provinces and piercing straight past the national border.

"Trace the upstream origin of ledger block nine," Number Eleven ordered tightly.

Number Seventy-three isolated the upstream feeds, his hand moving briskly now. "Every inflow into Shengyuan Capital's private clearing core comes from Tier-One state-owned infrastructure vehicles. Long-term energy supply contracts. Strategic rare-earth and lithium quotas. Deep-water port-concession leases."

"How are they pulling funds before treasury audits flag it?" Number Eleven asked.

"Falsified demurrage claims, rigged commodity-hedging derivatives, and phantom equipment-leasing invoices," Number Seventy-three replied, his jaw hardening. "Shengyuan Capital's hidden servers are siphoning a fixed percentage of sovereign resource cash flows before those funds ever reach provincial treasury accounts."

"Follow the conversion layer," Number Eleven murmured. "Where does the yuan go once it leaves Lanshui?"

Number Seventy-three highlighted the glowing amber lines. "Through domestic factoring trusts in Lanshui and three neighboring capitals, then into cross-border trade-settlement mirrors. Within twenty-four hours, the capital reappears offshore as physical gold-backed bearer instruments, stripped of domestic ownership records and held in private clearing vaults in Singapore, Zurich, and Cayman Islands trusts."

He exhaled slowly, staring at the screen. "Regional demolitions and county rare-earth smuggling are just cash-laundering ballast. Hongrui Real Estate in Yunxi County is one tiny valve on the underside of the pipe. The real volume is draining straight out of state energy, mineral, and port concessions."

Number Eleven's hand tightened on the chair back. "Keep drilling. Gold-backed bearer instruments in Zurich and the Caymans do not sit idle for twenty years. Where do the outgoing disbursements land?"

Number Seventy-three cross-correlated the Zurich and Cayman settlement windows against Black Pine Guard's global counter-intelligence registry. The progress bar crawled for eight long seconds.

When results populated in crimson ink on the right-hand monitor, Number Seventy-three froze. Beside him, even Number Eleven's breathing halted for a heartbeat.

Every quarter, on the fifteenth of March, June, September, and December, fixed tranches of bearer-instrument liquidity flowed from the Cayman trusts and Zurich accounts into fourteen seemingly unrelated offshore entities: maritime insurance brokers in Rotterdam, aerospace consultancies in Frankfurt, and satellite-analytics firms in Delaware and Singapore.

Every single one carried a verified Class-A red flag inside Black Pine's global threat archive. They were not luxury holding companies for corrupt officials. They were documented financial cutouts operated by foreign defense-intelligence services.

"Verify the hashes," Number Eleven said, his voice sharp as cold steel. "Are those one-way transfers, or settlement payments against delivered cargo?"

Number Seventy-three opened the metadata headers on the last six quarterly transfers. A faint crease formed between his brows. "Each disbursement carries an encrypted thirty-two-character delivery confirmation tag, paired with a courier archive reference routed back to a local drop node right here in Lanshui."

"Confirm the timing," Number Eleven said quietly.

"They are fee-for-delivery settlements," Number Seventy-three confirmed, crimson lines reflecting off his dark glasses. "And the latest delivery tag cleared four days ago, twenty-four hours before Hongrui Real Estate moved on Old Street in Yunxi County."

Nobody in the dark room spoke. Across neighboring consoles, two analysts had stopped typing, staring silently at the crimson vectors on the wall. They had entered Yunxi County to trace a demolition gang and clear an innocent uncle's name for Chen Xuan. Instead, pulling one loose thread at Old Street had exposed an underground sovereign siphon feeding foreign defense intelligence straight out of the nation's treasury.

Number Eleven reached for the encrypted internal intercom and pressed the direct key to regional command.

"Number One," Number Eleven said evenly. "Financial Intelligence has breached Shengyuan Capital's Lanshui vault servers. We have verified SWIFT mirrors linking state energy, mineral, and port revenues through Singapore, Zurich, and Cayman gold-backed trusts into fourteen foreign defense-intelligence cutouts. Active fee-for-delivery hashes terminate in Lanshui."

A brief, heavy silence answered before Number One's calm voice came through. "Are the tags verified?"

"Double-verified," Number Eleven answered. "The latest clearance ran four days ago."

"Lock down the floor," Number One ordered immediately. "Zero external transmissions. Cross-link the delivery hashes with Signals and Cyber now. Tell Number Thirty-four to decrypt the courier drive we lifted tonight."

"Understood," Number Eleven replied. He ended the link, looked back at the crimson lines burning across the glass wall, and knew the night in Lanshui had barely begun.
