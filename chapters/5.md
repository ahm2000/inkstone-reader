# Chapter 5: Home

Fuxing New Village, Building 2, Room 601. Friday, 7:30 p.m.

By the fourth flight of stairs, Chen Xuan could smell pork-rib soup. Before he could knock, the front door flew open!

"Xuan!" His mother, Liu Meifang, hurried out holding a soup ladle, her eyes red. "You're here! Sit down, sit down! I made pork-rib soup. I didn't touch a bite, waiting for you!"

"Mom, I told you to eat first."

"How could I eat while your Second Uncle is locked in a cell?" Her voice cracked, and she spun back into the kitchen banging pots loudly.

On the edge of the sofa sat Second Aunt Wu Lanying, clutching a cold glass of water. Always neat, tonight her hair hung loose and her cardigan was buttoned wrong.

"Xuan," she said, standing up with trembling lips. "Your uncle has never hit anyone in his life. He practically cries killing a fish!"

"I know, Second Aunt." Chen Xuan gently wrapped her shaking fingers back around the glass. "Please sit. Let's have some soup first, then tell me everything."

It was a simple gesture, yet her shoulders eased enough to sit down.

Soon the whole family crowded around the folding table. His sharp-tongued sister, Chen Yuan, a junior high school teacher, kept filling Second Aunt's bowl, while his younger brother, Chen Hao, hid behind a high-school math book with one earbud in.

"Brother, you look like you just woke up," Chen Hao muttered.

"I did. On the bus."

"Classic!" Chen Yuan snapped. "Second Uncle is in a cell, and our big brother is napping on the bus!"

"Yuan!" their mother scolded.

"I'm just saying, Mom. He's the only person alive who could snore through a flood!"

Chen Xuan drank a long spoonful of hot, salty soup, then set the bowl down and listened closely as Second Aunt laid out the whole trap.

Yunxi County was knocking down Old Street for a new mall built by Hongrui Real Estate. Hongrui offered 3,800 yuan per square meter, coming out to 456,000 yuan for Second Uncle's 120-square-meter corner shop, when real Old Street shops sold for 8,000 a square meter, nearly one million yuan!

They called it relocation pay. To put it plainly, it was robbery at less than half price!

Eleven owners had already buckled. Only three refused, and Second Uncle's corner lot sat right where the mall's main entrance had to go.

"For nine days, thugs sat on his stools smoking," Second Aunt wept. "Today the gold-chain boss warned him to sign before something ugly happened. Then that driver threw himself straight into the paint shelf! Old Yu saw it, and the police walked right in like they were waiting outside."

Clack! Chen Yuan slammed her chopsticks down, her face burning with anger!

"That's daylight bullying! Tomorrow morning I'm marching straight to that police station myself!"

"You're not going anywhere!" Chen Jianguo barked. "You're a schoolteacher. One shout in there and they'll ruin your record!"

"Then what do we do, Dad?" Chen Yuan's eyes turned red. "Just sit here and wait while they break Second Uncle?"

Every eye around the table turned slowly toward Chen Xuan.

He quietly set down his spoon and looked at Second Aunt. "Don't sign anything tonight, no matter who calls. Tomorrow morning at eight-thirty, Dad and I will go down to the police station, take a queue number like everyone else, and ask them to put their reason in writing."

Chen Yuan stared at him in total disbelief. "Take a number at a police station? Brother, did you really bump your head on the bus?"

"No," Chen Xuan replied mildly, picking up his chopsticks again. "Their front hall has service windows and a ticket machine that prints queue numbers, just like a bank. You pull a number, wait until your turn flashes on the screen, and talk at the window. Nobody can accuse us of making trouble."

"Xuan, your father already went today!" Liu Meifang cried anxiously. "They brushed him right off!"

"Then tomorrow we ask for the written detention notice," Chen Xuan said calmly. "By law, they have to give the family a reason in writing. Second Aunt, please promise me one thing. No matter who calls you, don't sign a single sheet of paper. Not for any price."

"But what if they keep him locked up?" Second Aunt's eyes filled with tears again. "What if they ruin our daughter's future?"

"I'll bring him home," Chen Xuan said.

He spoke as simply as a neighbor predicting clear weather tomorrow.

Nobody at the table truly believed he could pull off a miracle. Chen Yuan rolled her eyes, Liu Meifang wiped her cheeks and dropped two more pork ribs into his bowl, and Chen Jianguo cleared his throat with a mumbled proverb about still water running deep. Everyone thought he was just trying to keep Second Aunt from collapsing.

Only Chen Xuan knew he meant every single word.
