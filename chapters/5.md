# Chapter 5: Above the Clouds

Thirty-eight thousand feet above the eastern hills, the matte-slate jet rode steady above the clouds. Inside the cabin, only the hum of turbines and the crisp turn of paper broke the quiet as Chen Xuan finished his warm jasmine tea from the cup lid of his green thermos.

On the surface, the dossier looked like an ordinary county redevelopment dispute. Old Street was a half-mile strip of late-eighties brick shophouses housing hardware dealers, tailors, noodle stalls, and retired mill workers. Four years earlier, Yunxi County named it a heritage-and-commercial renovation zone. Two years later, after three smaller local bidders withdrew under unexplained tax audits, the parcel went at thirty percent below appraised value to Hongrui Real Estate, fronted by Deng Yongchang.

When Chen Xuan reached the back appendix from the Lanshui-Yunxi data desk, his fingers paused on a single margin note.

For a sleepy mountain county whose annual housing demand barely filled two residential towers, Hongrui Real Estate was burning absurd cash to drive out forty-two remaining shopkeepers along the eastern block, right where Mingde Hardware stood. Stranger still, the demolition boundary bent behind the back alleys to swallow an abandoned state-owned geological survey warehouse and drainage culverts leading toward the old Qingfeng mountain ridge.

Chen Xuan tapped the zoning map with one forefinger, eyes narrowing. Developers did not hire quarry thugs like Yan Tiehu to smash counters and frame a fifty-four-year-old hardware merchant over ordinary apartments. A fair buyout would have cost less than a month of legal fees. Someone wanted Old Street cleared fast and completely before outside eyes asked what lay beneath the flagstones.

A soft chime sounded from the armrest console. A green light blinked beside the secure satellite handset.

Chen Xuan closed the folder, picked up the handset, and pressed the side switch. "Speak."

The voice on the encrypted channel was calm, low, and stripped of accent. It belonged to Number 1, the unseen head of the Black Pine Guard. Even after a decade, Chen Xuan had never asked for Number 1's birth name, and Number 1 had never asked for a title beyond his numeral.

"We have live telemetry over Old Street Police Station and Hongrui Real Estate's county office," Number 1 reported steadily. "Our Lanshui relay confirmed the assault complaint against Chen Mingde was filed at one fifty by a site foreman under Yan Tiehu. The medical injury certificate was stamped by a private clinic owned by Deng Yongchang's brother-in-law forty minutes before the alleged altercation occurred."

Chen Xuan's expression remained serene, though his dark eyes chilled another degree. "Forty minutes before."

"Correct," Number 1 said. "Sloppy work by local men who assumed a hardware merchant's family had no reach beyond Yunxi County. Our watcher also confirmed deputy director Zhou Quan signed the temporary detention slip personally after a twelve-minute call from Hongrui Real Estate's downtown office."

"How is Chen Mingde holding up inside?" Chen Xuan asked, tone flat and precise.

"Seated alone in Holding Room Two," Number 1 answered immediately. "Audio confirms he has not been struck, though Zhou Quan's clerk warned him twenty minutes ago that unless his family signs the Mingde Hardware relocation transfer before noon tomorrow, the assault charge escalates to formal criminal arrest."

Chen Xuan's thumb rested still against the handset. He thought of his mother's shaking voice, of Chen Jianguo shouted away from an iron gate holding blood pressure pills, and of Second Uncle Chen Mingde sitting beside a creek in the rain with a bamboo rod.

"Did your desk check the back boundary of the Old Street parcel?" Chen Xuan asked quietly. "The abandoned geological survey warehouse and the drainage culverts toward Qingfeng ridge."

A brief, alert pause followed. "We caught the zoning bend ten minutes ago," Number 1 said, tone sharpening. "Land records list that warehouse as sealed surplus since nineteen ninety-eight, yet municipal power logs show heavy, irregular high-voltage draw on a neighboring line over the past fourteen months. That does not match ordinary construction equipment."

"I thought as much," Chen Xuan murmured. "Deng Yongchang is only the front sign. Someone behind Hongrui Real Estate is using a county demolition as cover, and they were careless enough to put handcuffs on my Second Uncle."

He turned toward the dark window, his voice unhurried, clear, and final. "Listen carefully, Number 1. Nobody in my family steps foot inside a police station. Handle the people who touched my Second Uncle, verify his safety tonight, and pull every root under Old Street. Do not miss a single brick."

A respectful half-second of silence followed as the full scope landed. Not merely a release from detention. Not merely a lesson for Yan Tiehu or Chairman Deng. Every root under Old Street, down to the last buried stone.

"Understood," Number 1 replied, voice hardening to steel. "Chen Mingde's safety and immediate lawful release will be verified before you reach the family courtyard. I am activating three divisions across Yunxi and Lanshui right now."

"One more thing," Chen Xuan added softly. "Do not startle my parents. Keep the noise away from their door."

"Not a whisper will reach their courtyard, Sir," Number 1 promised. "I will report again once the first layer peels back."

Chen Xuan set the handset back into its cradle.

Three hundred miles away, in a windowless operations room beneath a logistics warehouse in the provincial capital, Number 1 entered a nine-digit authorization sequence. Across the wall monitors, three division headers shifted from gray to emerald green: Field Intelligence, Financial Tracing, and Local Containment.

Orders went out without a single spoken name.

In Yunxi County, Number 14 set down his teacup inside a quiet tea house two blocks from Old Street Police Station and glanced at his wrist terminal. Across the table, Number 19 saw the alert and rose immediately.

"Priority clear from Number 1," Number 14 murmured as they stepped outside. "He wants Chen Mingde out clean before midnight and every camera along Old Street backed up."

"I have the station's rear lane and Zhou Quan's call switch," Number 19 replied, splitting toward the opposite corner. "You take the clinic and Yan Tiehu's foreman."

In Lanshui City, Number 23 and Number 27 unlocked three encrypted banking terminals side by side. "Ten years of clearing-house ledgers on Hongrui Real Estate and Deng Yongchang," Number 23 said briskly, fingers flying. "Trace every coastal shell account back to its beneficiary."

"Pulling the first free-trade zone transfer now," Number 27 answered, eyes locked on the cascading figures. "Whoever funds Chairman Deng is about to lose their cover."

Along the darkened highway exits out of Yunxi, four silent sedans from Local Containment eased onto the gravel shoulders and killed their headlights.

Across the command wall, hundreds of green dots flickered awake over Lanshui and Yunxi County. Every road, ledger, switchboard, and back-alley warehouse tied to Old Street had slipped inside a net no county official or property developer could imagine existed.

High above the clouds, the cabin lights dimmed as the jet pitched downward toward Lanshui-Yunxi Regional Airfield.

Chen Xuan capped his green thermos, tucked the uneaten red-bean bun into his canvas bag, and looked calmly down at the dark mountain valley rising to meet him.
