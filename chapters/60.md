# Chapter 60: The First Cross-Department War Room

At eight o'clock sharp on Monday morning, the heavy oak doors of Beijing's main crisis hall locked from the inside. Around the curved horseshoe table sat the leaders of ten top state bodies: the Central Military Commission, Ministry of State Security, Ministry of Public Security, Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, National People's Congress Standing Committee, Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, Central Organization Department, and National Development and Reform Commission.

A low haze of cigarette smoke drifted across the desk microphones. Glass ashtrays overflowed at every seat before a single word was spoken.

Premier Gu Chengshan stood behind his chair, knuckles pressed into the green tabletop, his face carved from gray stone. Beside him, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua lit a cigarette with slow calm, though the tight line of his jaw showed the storm behind his eyes.

"We are not here for polite speeches," Premier Gu Chengshan said, his voice cutting straight through the low murmurs. "Three hundred and eighteen senior suspects are in custody. Over 100 trillion yuan in twenty years of stolen state money has been uncovered and frozen overseas. Nine years of coastal radar gaps and troop schedules walked out of our military vaults. Everyone at this table has fifteen minutes to agree on who handles what before the stock markets open at nine thirty and provinces start asking why their deputies vanished."

National Development and Reform Commission Chairman Qian Wenbo leaned into his microphone, sweat shining on his forehead. "Premier Gu Chengshan, my economists have stared at Gao Zhengyi's 100 trillion yuan ledger since seven thirty. If Discipline Inspection freezes forty-two provincial building funds at nine o'clock, banks will run out of cash before ten! Pull out every dirty brick at once and the whole ceiling drops on our own heads!"

"Leave those bricks in and the ceiling collapses anyway, Qian Wenbo!" Gao Zhengyi fired back across the table, jabbing his cigarette toward him. "Half those funds are empty shells piled fourteen to one with debt using fake county demolition prices! Look at Yunxi County! 480 million yuan in local land turned into 6.2 billion yuan in hidden loans! You want the People's Bank to keep propping up ghosts?"

"Nobody asked you to prop up ghosts, Gao Zhengyi," Organization Department Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei cut in sharply, tapping his pen hard against a stack of files. "I am asking how we replace seventeen provincial department heads, twelve city committee members, and four vice ministers in one morning without bringing fourteen provinces to a dead stop! My office was not even told before the overnight arrests!"

"Nobody was told," General Qin Zhengguo said flatly from the military side, blowing thick smoke across his weathered face. "My own active officers were picked up without a Central Military Commission warrant. State Security lost its Deputy Director before dawn. If you want to argue over rules, Liu Zhengwei, take it up with the vault line still locked under Zero Guard monitoring."

An icy hush rolled around the horseshoe table. Several pens stopped moving mid-word.

Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference Vice Chairman Zhao Yunsheng shifted in his chair, his hand shaking as he set down his teacup. "Premier Gu Chengshan, forgive me, but our senior advisers are terrified. Whispers are already spreading that Black Pine Guard was sidelined and Zero Guard stepped in for the first time in decades. If rumors of a midnight purge reach foreign reporters before noon, we will face major diplomatic trouble."

"Which is why not one single word about Zero Guard leaves this room," Premier Gu Chengshan snapped, his eyes sweeping the table. "National People's Congress Standing Committee Deputy Secretary-General Fan Jianguo, where are the emergency orders?"

Fan Jianguo straightened his collar, pale but steady. "Written and signed, Premier Gu Chengshan. Closed national-security court authority for the three hundred and eighteen big suspects, emergency powers for the Organization Department to name acting provincial heads by noon, and state orders to bring back the frozen money from Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg."

"Good," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said, leaning forward through the smoke and crushing his cigarette out. "Now end the turf fight before it turns into a disaster. State Security Internal-Audit Bureau Director Han Dongsheng, stop hoarding port-broker files. Hand every smuggling shipping list to Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming within twenty minutes."

Han Dongsheng stiffened, his jaw tight. "Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, those files hold active spy code trails. If local Public Security arrests shipping brokers too early, foreign spy stations in Europe will shut down their remaining links."

"Your links were burned for twenty years while the Zurich Northern Ledger sailed right through your ports!" Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming barked back, his knuckles white on the table edge. "Give my officers the local broker names right now, or I will pull the port shipping records myself and arrest your port watch officers right beside them!"

"Enough!" Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's voice cracked like thunder. Both men snapped their mouths shut at once.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua slid a single yellow order sheet to the center of his desk, right beside the court papers signed by Chief Justice Wu Cheng and Procuratorate Director Pei Hong.

"Track One stays sealed inside this room," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua declared, his eyes cold and fierce. "General Qin Zhengguo cleans out the three military zones. Han Dongsheng clears out State Security's moles. Gao Zhengyi and Qian Wenbo steady the provincial banks while bringing the frozen overseas funds home. Wu Cheng and Pei Hong lock the three hundred and eighteen big suspects into airtight closed courts."

He paused, letting heavy silence press down on every chest, then pointed sharply at the yellow order.

"Track Two goes public at eight thirty sharp. Yunxi County."

Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming exhaled through his nose and nodded once. "The provincial joint task force has been waiting forty kilometers outside Yunxi County since seven forty-five. Discipline inspection teams, provincial anti-gang units, counter-terrorism bomb specialists, and prosecutors are sitting in their cars waiting for the green light."

"Confirm the targets," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua ordered.

"County Magistrate Sun Weiming, County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong, developer Deng Yongchang, and demolition thugs Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao," Pei Hong read clearly. "All five face normal, rock-solid provincial charges: bribery, abuse of power, contract fraud, leading an organized gang, and illegal use of explosives against ordinary people. Every piece of proof comes straight from local Yunxi records. Zero mention of the Beijing sweep. Zero mention of Zero Guard."

"And the framed Old Street homeowner?" Premier Gu Chengshan asked quietly.

"Chen Mingde," Chief Justice Wu Cheng answered at once. "His immediate release order and cleared-name paper sit sealed in the lead car. The second provincial officers take over Yunxi County Public Security Bureau, Chen Mingde walks straight out the front gate with a formal apology, and his family is left completely in peace."

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua glanced up at the wall clock. Eight fourteen.

He pressed his heavy red stamp hard onto the bottom of the yellow Yunxi order and slapped his open palm flat onto the paper. The sharp slap echoed off the walls, rattling three glass ashtrays.

"Order signed," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said, his voice ringing with finality. "Guo Zhengming, give the provincial joint task force the green light. Roll into Yunxi County at eight thirty sharp. Let the whole province watch the law come down in broad daylight."

Across the smoke-filled hall, Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming picked up the red secure phone and spoke six crisp words.

The first domino had fallen. Sixty minutes away by highway, Yunxi County had no idea the sky was about to drop.
