# Chapter 60: The First Cross-Department War Room

At 7:15 AM on Monday morning, the heavy oak doors of Beijing's main crisis hall locked from inside. Around the horseshoe table sat the heads of ten top state bodies, from the Central Military Commission and Discipline Inspection to State Security, Public Security, the courts, the National People's Congress, the CPPCC, the Organization Department, and the Reform Commission.

Thick cigarette smoke drifted under the lamps. Glass ashtrays overflowed before a word was spoken.

Premier Gu Chengshan stood behind his chair, knuckles pressed into the wood, his face like gray stone. Beside him, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua lit a cigarette slowly, his tight jaw showing the storm behind his eyes.

"No polite speeches," Premier Gu Chengshan said sharply. "Three hundred and eighteen suspects are in custody. Over 100 trillion yuan in twenty years of stolen wealth is frozen overseas. Nine years of coastal radar gaps and troop schedules leaked from our vaults. Agree on duties before Shanghai and Shenzhen open."

NDRC Chairman Qian Wenbo leaned into his microphone, sweat shining on his brow. "Premier Gu Chengshan, my team stared at Gao Zhengyi's 100 trillion yuan ledger all morning. Freeze forty-two provincial funds at once, and banks run dry before the bell settles! Pull out every dirty brick and the ceiling drops on our heads!"

"Leave them in and it collapses anyway, Qian Wenbo!" Gao Zhengyi snapped, jabbing his cigarette at him. "Half those funds are empty shells piled fourteen to one with fake demolition debt! Look at Yunxi County in Sha Province! 480 million yuan in land became 6.2 billion yuan in hidden loans! You want the People's Bank propping up ghosts?"

"I am not propping up ghosts, Gao Zhengyi!" Qian Wenbo shouted, slapping his sheets. "We do not have enough clean cash ready to plug a hole this big without shaking every bank in the country!"

Right then, the white secure phone beside Premier Gu Chengshan rang once.

Every voice died. Ten heads snapped toward the pale handset.

Premier Gu Chengshan picked it up and hit speaker. "Gu Chengshan speaking."

"Premier Gu Chengshan," a calm voice came through. "This is Number 1 of the White Guard. I am calling for Chen Xuan."

Zhou Zhenhua froze with his cigarette halfway up. General Qin Zhengguo leaned forward, eyes narrowing.

"Speak," Premier Gu Chengshan said tightly.

"Chen Xuan heard the state is cleaning house today," Number 1 said evenly. "He knows money can become an issue when big holes open at once. He is sending 300 trillion yuan right now, with up to 500 trillion yuan available if the country needs it. Every yuan is clean, legal private wealth, completely free to the state. No loans, no interest, no conditions. The transfers are moving directly into China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, Huaxia Bank, and the central treasury now."

The line clicked off.

Dead silence crushed the room. A long gray ash broke off Gao Zhengyi's cigarette onto his jacket. He did not move. Zhao Yunsheng stared at the white phone in shock.

"300 trillion yuan?" Qian Wenbo whispered, voice cracking. "Free? Up to 500 trillion yuan?"

Qian Wenbo's central bank tablet chimed four times. His hands shook as he unlocked the screen. Four green clearing notices glowed side by side: China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank.

"It is real," Qian Wenbo breathed, eyes wide. "All four banks confirmed it. 300 trillion yuan in clean, legal reserves just hit the central pool. Money is no longer an issue."

Premier Gu Chengshan exhaled a long stream of smoke, his face hard as iron. "Then nobody has an excuse left. Organization Department Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei, speak."

Liu Zhengwei tapped his pen against his files. "Seventeen provincial heads, twelve city leaders, and four vice ministers vanished overnight. My office was not told! How do we fill thirty-three seats without stalling fourteen provinces?"

"Nobody was told," General Qin Zhengguo said flatly through thick smoke. "My own officers were taken without a Central Military Commission warrant. State Security lost its Deputy Director before dawn. Argue rules with the vault line under Black Guard monitoring."

An icy hush swept the table. Pens froze.

CPPCC Vice Chairman Zhao Yunsheng shifted nervously. "Premier Gu Chengshan, whispers say White Guard was sidelined and Black Guard stepped in. If foreign reporters hear of a midnight purge, we face major diplomatic trouble."

"Which is why not one word about Black Guard leaves this room," Premier Gu Chengshan snapped. "National People's Congress Deputy Secretary-General Fan Jianguo, the emergency orders?"

Fan Jianguo straightened his collar, pale but steady. "Signed, Premier Gu Chengshan. Closed national-security courts for the suspects, emergency powers for Liu Zhengwei to name acting heads, and orders to bring back frozen funds from Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg."

"Good," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said, crushing his cigarette out. "End the turf fight. State Security Internal-Audit Director Han Dongsheng, give every port-broker smuggling list to Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming now."

Han Dongsheng stiffened. "Those files hold active spy trails. Arrest brokers too early and European stations shut their links."

"Your links were burned for twenty years while stolen wealth sailed through your ports!" Guo Zhengming barked, knuckles white. "Hand over the names, or I arrest your port watch officers beside them!"

"Enough!" Zhou Zhenhua's voice cracked like thunder. Both men shut up instantly.

Zhou Zhenhua slid a yellow order beside the court papers signed by Chief Justice Wu Cheng and Procuratorate Director Pei Hong.

"Track One stays sealed here," Zhou Zhenhua declared coldly. "General Qin Zhengguo cleans the military zones. Han Dongsheng clears State Security's moles. Gao Zhengyi and Qian Wenbo lock down the banks with the new reserve and bring overseas money home. Wu Cheng and Pei Hong put the three hundred and eighteen suspects into closed courts."

He pointed at the yellow sheet. "Track Two goes public now. Yunxi County."

Guo Zhengming nodded. "The provincial joint task force is waiting forty kilometers outside Yunxi County."

"Confirm targets," Zhou Zhenhua ordered.

"County Magistrate Sun Weiming, Police Chief Liang Guodong, developer Deng Yongchang, and thugs Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao," Pei Hong read cleanly. "Solid local charges: bribery, abuse of power, fraud, gang leadership, and illegal explosives. Zero mention of Beijing or Black Guard."

"And the framed homeowner?" Premier Gu Chengshan asked quietly.

"Chen Mingde," Wu Cheng answered at once. "His release order and cleared-name paper sit sealed in the lead car. He walks out the front gate with an apology, and his family is left in peace."

Zhou Zhenhua pressed his red stamp onto the yellow order and slapped his palm down, rattling three ashtrays.

"Order signed," Zhou Zhenhua said firmly. "Guo Zhengming, roll into Yunxi County. Let the whole province watch the law drop in daylight."

Guo Zhengming lifted the red secure phone and spoke six crisp words.

The second he hung up, the heavy oak doors swung wide open.

Every leader at the table shot to their feet, crushing out cigarettes in rigid awe. Because of the massive treason and the 300 trillion yuan gift that could reach 500 trillion yuan, Beijing's highest elders and families had arrived in strict order of rank and seniority.

At the front walked ninety-year-old Founding Elder Song of the Song Family, tapping his dark cane. Half a step behind him came Elder Qin of the Qin Family, retired senior military patriarch; Elder Ye of the Ye Family, retired Discipline and Judicial patriarch; and Elder Lin of the Lin Family, retired State Planning and Economic patriarch.

Strictly behind the first tier stepped the second-tier family heads: Gao Chengde of the Gao Family, Zhou Mingyuan of the Zhou Family, Han Zhengkun of the Han Family, and Pei Shoushan of the Pei Family.

Elder Song took the center seat, tapped his cane sharply, and swept his fierce eyes across the smoky room.

"A citizen just gave this nation 300 trillion yuan for free so the roof would not fall," Elder Song's voice thundered. "Sit down, every last one of you. Today, we clean this house completely."
