# Chapter 60: The First Cross-Department War Room

At 7:15 AM on Monday morning, the heavy oak doors of Beijing's main crisis hall locked from inside.

Around the inner horseshoe table sat the twenty-four core voting members of the Capital Top Council, every one a half-premier leader ranking above provincial party secretaries and cabinet ministers. Behind them in the outer ring sat non-standing cabinet ministers and bureau heads with open notebooks, listening silently. At a side desk, two clerks recorded every word, name, and vote into the sealed formal minutes.

Thick cigarette smoke drifted under the lamps. Premier Gu Chengshan sat at the center, knuckles pressed to the wood, face like gray stone. Beside him, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua lit a cigarette slowly, his hard mouth showing the storm behind his eyes. On his other side sat General Qin Zhengguo, straight as iron.

"No polite remarks," Premier Gu Chengshan said quietly. "Before Shanghai and Shenzhen open, we settle the financial shield and cleanup orders. Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi, deliver the briefing."

At the lower briefing desk, Central Commission for Discipline Inspection Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi rose crisply and opened a dark folder.

"Three hundred and eighteen suspects are in custody," Gao Zhengyi reported evenly. "Over 100 trillion yuan in twenty years of stolen wealth is frozen overseas across Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg. Nine years of coastal radar gaps and troop schedules leaked from our vaults. Forty-two provincial funds are hollow shells piled fourteen to one with fake debt. In Yunxi County of Sha Province, 480 million yuan in land became 6.2 billion yuan in hidden loans. Seventeen provincial heads, twelve city leaders, and four vice ministers were detained overnight under Black Guard vault monitoring, leaving thirty-three seats empty across fourteen provinces."

Gao Zhengyi closed the folder and returned silently to the outer ring. Not one minister spoke. Only the clerks' keyboards clicked as they logged the report into the sealed minutes.

Premier Gu Chengshan looked across the twenty-four core members. "The floor is open."

Across the inner table, Economic and Financial Commission Vice Chairman Qian Wenbo leaned forward. His face looked calm, yet sweat shone on his temples. Half the provincial bank chiefs facing audit had risen under his old coastal circle.

"Premier Gu Chengshan, my concern is stability," Qian Wenbo said smoothly, tapping his pen. "Freeze forty-two provincial funds at once, and banks run dry. Isolate five cases first and give provincial banks six months to adjust."

Several core members exchanged subtle glances. Behind Qian Wenbo's polite words hid a quiet hope: six months gave old banking circles time to bury paper trails.

General Qin Zhengguo blew thin smoke across the table. "Six months?" he said flatly. "My own officers were detained before dawn because our vaults bled secrets for nine years. State Security lost its Deputy Director overnight. Argue for slow mercy with the vault line under Black Order monitoring."

An icy chill dropped over the hall. Pens froze in the outer ring. Qian Wenbo's knuckles turned white around his pen.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua leaned forward, cigarette burning down toward the filter. "We cannot leave rotten debt inside the banks, Qian Wenbo. The only hurdle is clean cash to plug a 100 trillion yuan hole."

Right then, the white secure phone beside Premier Gu Chengshan rang once.

Every breath stopped. Twenty-four half-premier heads snapped toward the pale handset. Even the clerks froze above their keys.

Premier Gu Chengshan lifted it and pressed speaker. "Gu Chengshan speaking."

"Premier Gu Chengshan," a calm voice came through. "This is Number 1 of the White Guard. I am calling for Chen Xuan."

Zhou Zhenhua froze with his cigarette halfway up. General Qin Zhengguo narrowed his eyes.

"Speak," Premier Gu Chengshan said tightly.

"Chen Xuan heard the state is cleaning house today," Number 1 said evenly. "He knows cash flow can become a problem when deep holes open at once. He is sending 300 trillion yuan right now, with up to 500 trillion yuan ready if the country needs it. Every yuan is clean, legal private wealth, gifted completely free to the country. No loans, no interest, no conditions. Transfers are clearing into China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank now."

The line clicked off.

Crushing silence fell over the room. A long gray ash snapped off Zhou Zhenhua's cigarette onto his sleeve; he did not move. A cold chill ran down every spine around the table.

Four chimes sounded on Qian Wenbo's central bank tablet. His hands shook as he unlocked it. Four green clearing notices glowed side by side: China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank.

"It is real," Qian Wenbo whispered, voice cracking. "All four banks confirmed settlement. 300 trillion yuan in clean reserves just hit the central pool."

Deep awe washed over twenty-four seasoned leaders, followed by a heavy, unspoken debt to an unseen citizen. While corrupt officials fought over crumbs and protected factions for twenty years, one private citizen quietly gifted the nation 300 trillion yuan for free, with 500 trillion yuan on standby, so the roof would not fall. Every hidden faction agenda froze dead in their throats.

Premier Gu Chengshan exhaled slowly, face like steel. "Then nobody has an excuse left."

He laid two folders flat. "Track One stays sealed here. General Qin Zhengguo cleans the military zones. State Security clears its moles. Financial leaders stabilize every bank with the new reserve and bring overseas wealth home. Closed courts try the three hundred and eighteen suspects. Not one word about the Black Guard leaves this hall."

He tapped the yellow folder. "Track Two goes public today: Yunxi County in Sha Province. Arrest County Magistrate Sun Weiming, Police Chief Liang Guodong, developer Deng Yongchang, and gang figures Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao on local charges. Release framed shopkeeper Chen Mingde with a cleared name and a quiet apology."

Premier Gu Chengshan swept his gaze across the twenty-four seats. "Meanwhile, the Organization Department launches a personnel sweep: promotions and awards for clean grassroots cadres, prison for traitors, withheld ranks for negligent supervisors, and powerless honorary advisory posts for faction-tainted officials."

He sat straight. "We hold a formal show-of-hands vote of the twenty-four core members. Clerks, record every name and vote in the sealed minutes. All in favor, raise your hands."

Premier Gu Chengshan, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, General Qin Zhengguo, and eighteen other core members raised their right hands cleanly. Twenty-one hands rose into the smoky air.

Three seats stayed still. A suffocating two-second pause stretched across the table. Two senior members with quiet ties to coastal banks kept their palms flat on the wood, choosing hesitant abstentions as sweat broke along their collars.

Beside them, Qian Wenbo's hand hovered an inch above the table. Premier Gu Chengshan fixed him with one ice-cold glare. Qian Wenbo glanced at the four bank notices on his tablet, swallowed hard, and slowly lifted his hand.

"Twenty-two in favor, two abstentions, zero against," Premier Gu Chengshan announced. "Clerks, seal the roll call and preserve every name in the minutes."

The sharp clatter of keyboards sounded like iron bars locking shut. The two abstaining leaders sat pale and stiff, their hesitation carved forever into the state archive.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua pressed his red stamp onto the yellow Yunxi County order and passed it back. From the silent outer ring, Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming rose silently, took the order, lifted the red secure phone, and spoke six crisp words.

The second Guo Zhengming hung up, the heavy oak doors swung wide open.

Every leader and outer-ring minister shot to their feet, crushing out cigarettes in rigid awe. Entering strictly by rank and seniority came ninety-year-old Founding Elder Song of the Song Family tapping his dark cane, followed half a step behind by Elder Qin, Elder Ye, and Elder Lin. Behind them stepped the second-tier family heads: Gao Chengde, Zhou Mingyuan, Han Zhengkun, and Pei Shoushan.

Elder Song took the center seat, tapped his cane sharply, and swept his fierce eyes across the smoky hall.

"A private citizen just gave this country 300 trillion yuan for free so the roof would not fall," Elder Song's voice rang like bronze. "Sit down, every last one of you. Today, we clean this house completely."
