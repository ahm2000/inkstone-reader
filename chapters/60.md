# Chapter 60: The First Cross-Department War Room

At 7:15 AM on Monday morning, the oak doors of Beijing's main crisis hall locked from inside.

Around the inner horseshoe table sat the twenty-four core voting members of the Capital Top Council, each a half-premier leader ranking above provincial secretaries and cabinet ministers. Outer-ring ministers listened silently behind them while two clerks logged every word and vote into sealed minutes.

Cigarette smoke drifted under the lamps. Premier Gu Chengshan sat at the center, face like gray stone, flanked by Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua and General Qin Zhengguo.

"No polite remarks," Gu Chengshan said quietly. "Before Shanghai and Shenzhen open, we settle the financial shield and cleanup orders. Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi, deliver the briefing."

Central Commission for Discipline Inspection Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi rose and opened a dark folder.

"Three hundred and eighteen suspects are in custody," Gao Zhengyi reported evenly. "Over 100 trillion yuan in stolen wealth is frozen overseas across Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg. Nine years of coastal radar gaps and troop schedules leaked from our vaults. Forty-two provincial funds are hollow shells piled fourteen to one with fake debt. In Yunxi County of Sha Province, 480 million yuan in land became 6.2 billion yuan in hidden loans. Seventeen provincial heads, twelve city leaders, and four vice ministers were detained overnight under Black Guard vault monitoring, leaving thirty-three seats empty across fourteen provinces."

Gao Zhengyi closed the folder and sat back down. Only the clerks' keyboards clicked.

Gu Chengshan swept his gaze across the twenty-four members. "The floor is open."

Economic and Financial Commission Vice Chairman Qian Wenbo leaned forward, sweat on his temples. Half the provincial bank chiefs facing audit had risen under his coastal circle.

"Premier Gu Chengshan, my concern is stability," Qian Wenbo said smoothly. "Freeze forty-two provincial funds at once, and banks run dry. Isolate five cases first and give provincial banks six months to adjust."

Several members traded subtle glances. Six months gave old banking circles time to bury paper trails.

Qin Zhengguo blew thin smoke across the table. "Six months?" he said flatly. "My own officers were detained before dawn because our vaults bled secrets for nine years. State Security lost its Deputy Director overnight. Argue for slow mercy with the vault line under Black Order monitoring."

An icy chill dropped over the hall. Qian Wenbo's knuckles turned white around his pen.

Zhou Zhenhua leaned forward. "We cannot leave rotten debt inside the banks, Qian Wenbo. The only hurdle is clean cash to plug a 100 trillion yuan hole."

Right then, the white secure phone beside Gu Chengshan rang once.

Every breath stopped. Twenty-four heads snapped toward the handset.

Gu Chengshan pressed speaker. "Gu Chengshan speaking."

"Premier Gu Chengshan," a calm voice came through. "This is Number 1 of the White Guard. I am calling for Chen Xuan."

Zhou Zhenhua froze with his cigarette halfway up. Qin Zhengguo narrowed his eyes.

"Speak," Gu Chengshan said tightly.

"Chen Xuan heard the state is cleaning house today," Number 1 said evenly. "He knows cash flow can become a problem when deep holes open at once. He is sending 300 trillion yuan right now, with up to 500 trillion yuan ready if needed. Every yuan is clean, legal private wealth, gifted free to the country. No loans, no interest, no conditions. Transfers are clearing into China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank now."

The line clicked off.

Crushing silence fell. Gray ash snapped off Zhou Zhenhua's cigarette onto his sleeve; he did not move.

Four chimes sounded on Qian Wenbo's central bank tablet. His hands shook as he unlocked it. Four green clearing notices glowed side by side: China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank.

"It is real," Qian Wenbo whispered, voice cracking. "All four banks confirmed settlement. 300 trillion yuan in clean reserves just hit the central pool."

Deep awe washed over twenty-four seasoned leaders, followed by an unspoken debt to an unseen citizen. While corrupt officials fought over crumbs for twenty years, one private citizen quietly gifted the nation 300 trillion yuan for free, with 500 trillion yuan on standby, so the roof would not fall. Every hidden faction agenda died instantly.

Gu Chengshan exhaled slowly, face like steel. "Then nobody has an excuse left."

He laid two folders flat. "Track One stays sealed here. Qin Zhengguo cleans the military zones. State Security clears its moles. Financial leaders stabilize every bank and bring overseas wealth home. Closed courts try the three hundred and eighteen suspects. Not one word about the Black Guard leaves this hall."

He tapped the yellow folder. "Track Two goes public today: Yunxi County in Sha Province. Arrest County Magistrate Sun Weiming, Police Chief Liang Guodong, developer Deng Yongchang, and gang figures Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao on local charges. Release framed shopkeeper Chen Mingde with a cleared name and a quiet apology."

Gu Chengshan looked around the table. "The Organization Department launches a personnel sweep: promotions and awards for clean grassroots cadres, prison for traitors, withheld ranks for negligent supervisors, and powerless honorary advisory posts for faction-tainted officials."

He sat straight. "Show-of-hands vote of the twenty-four core members. Clerks, record every name. All in favor, raise your hands."

Gu Chengshan, Zhou Zhenhua, Qin Zhengguo, and eighteen others raised their right hands cleanly. Twenty-one hands rose.

Three seats stayed still. Two senior members tied to coastal banks kept their palms flat on the wood, abstaining as sweat broke along their collars.

Beside them, Qian Wenbo's hand hovered above the wood. Gu Chengshan fixed him with an ice-cold glare. Qian Wenbo glanced at the four bank notices on his tablet, swallowed hard, and slowly lifted his hand.

"Twenty-two in favor, two abstentions, zero against," Gu Chengshan announced. "Clerks, seal the roll call."

Keyboards clattered like iron bars locking shut. The two abstaining leaders sat pale and stiff, their hesitation carved into the state archive.

Zhou Zhenhua pressed his red stamp onto the Yunxi County order. Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming rose, took the order, lifted the red secure phone, and spoke six crisp words.

The second Guo Zhengming hung up, the heavy oak doors swung wide open.

Every leader and minister shot to their feet in rigid awe. Entering by seniority came ninety-year-old Founding Elder Song of the Song Family tapping his dark cane, followed by Elder Qin, Elder Ye, and Elder Lin, then second-tier family heads Gao Chengde, Zhou Mingyuan, Han Zhengkun, and Pei Shoushan.

Elder Song took the center seat, tapped his cane sharply, and swept his fierce eyes across the hall.

"A private citizen just gave this country 300 trillion yuan for free so the roof would not fall," Elder Song's voice rang like bronze. "Sit down, every last one of you. Today, we clean this house completely."
