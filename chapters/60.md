# Chapter 60: The First Cross-Department War Room

At eight o'clock sharp on Monday morning, the heavy oak doors of the capital's main crisis hall locked from inside. Around the horseshoe table sat the leadership of ten organs of state: the Central Military Commission, Ministry of State Security, Ministry of Public Security, Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, National People's Congress Standing Committee, Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, Central Organization Department, and National Development and Reform Commission.

A low haze of cigarette smoke drifted across the microphones. Glass ashtrays overflowed at every seat before a word was spoken.

Premier Gu Chengshan stood behind his chair, knuckles pressed into the green baize, face carved from gray stone. Beside him, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua lit a cigarette with slow calm, though the tight line of his jaw betrayed the storm behind his eyes.

"We are not here for polite briefings," Premier Gu Chengshan said, voice cutting through the murmurs. "Three hundred and eighteen senior principals are in custody. Over 100 trillion yuan in twenty-year compounded sovereign leakage is uncovered and frozen offshore. Nine years of coastal radar and garrison schedules walked out of our military vaults. Everyone at this table has fifteen minutes to align jurisdiction before markets open at nine thirty and provinces ask why their deputies vanished."

National Development and Reform Commission Chairman Qian Wenbo leaned into his microphone, sweat shining on his brow. "Premier Gu Chengshan, my economists have stared at Gao Zhengyi's 100-trillion-yuan ledger since seven thirty. If Discipline Inspection freezes forty-two provincial urban-investment platforms at nine o'clock, interbank liquidity seizes up before ten! Pull every dirty brick at once and the ceiling drops on our own heads!"

"Leave the bricks in and the ceiling collapses anyway, Qian Wenbo!" Gao Zhengyi fired back across the horseshoe, jabbing his cigarette toward him. "Half those platforms are hollow shells leveraged fourteen to one on forged county demolition appraisals! Look at Yunxi County! 480 million in local land turned into 6.2 billion in shadow credit! You want the People's Bank propping up ghosts?"

"Nobody asked you to prop up ghosts, Gao Zhengyi," Organization Department Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei cut in sharply, pen tapping furiously against a stack of dossiers. "I am asking how we replace seventeen provincial department heads, twelve municipal standing committee members, and four ministerial deputies in one morning without paralyzing fourteen provinces! My office was not even notified before the overnight arrests!"

"Nobody was notified," General Qin Zhengguo said flatly from the military wing, exhaling dense smoke across his weathered face. "My own active officers were extracted without a Central Military Commission warrant. State Security lost its Deputy Director before dawn. If you want to argue protocol, Liu Zhengwei, take it up with the vault line still locked under Zero Guard monitoring."

An icy hush rolled around the horseshoe. Several pens stopped mid-stroke.

Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference Vice Chairman Zhao Yunsheng shifted in his chair, hand shaking as he set down his cup. "Premier Gu Chengshan, forgive me, but our senior advisers are terrified. Whispers are spreading that Black Pine Guard was sidelined and Zero Guard activated for the first time in decades. If rumors of a midnight purge hit foreign wires before noon, diplomatic fallout will be severe."

"Which is why not one word of Zero Guard leaves this hall," Premier Gu Chengshan snapped, eyes sweeping the room. "National People's Congress Standing Committee Deputy Secretary-General Fan Jianguo, where are the emergency authorizations?"

Fan Jianguo straightened his collar, pale but steady. "Drafted and countersigned, Premier Gu Chengshan. Closed national-security tribunal authority for the three hundred and eighteen principals, emergency trusteeship powers for the Organization Department to appoint acting provincial heads by noon, and sovereign repatriation decrees for the frozen Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg accounts."

"Good," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said, leaning forward through the smoke and crushing his cigarette dead. "Now end the jurisdiction fight before it becomes a disaster. State Security Internal-Audit Bureau Director Han Dongsheng, stop hoarding port-broker files. Hand every maritime smuggling manifest to Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming within twenty minutes."

Han Dongsheng stiffened, jaw tight. "Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, those files hold active counter-espionage cipher traces. If local Public Security arrests brokers prematurely, foreign stations in Europe burn their remaining cells."

"Your cells were burned for twenty years while the Zurich Northern Ledger sailed through your harbors!" Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming barked back, knuckles white on the table edge. "Give my officers the domestic broker names now, or I pull customs manifests myself and arrest your port liaisons beside them!"

"Enough!" Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's voice cracked like thunder. Both men snapped their mouths shut.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua slid a single yellow dispatch order to the center of his desk beside the judicial docket signed by Chief Justice Wu Cheng and Procuratorate Director Pei Hong.

"Track One stays sealed inside this room," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua declared, eyes cold and fierce. "General Qin Zhengguo cleans the three theaters. Han Dongsheng purges State Security's moles. Gao Zhengyi and Qian Wenbo stabilize provincial banks while repatriating frozen offshore funds. Wu Cheng and Pei Hong lock the three hundred and eighteen principals into ironclad closed tribunals."

He paused, letting heavy silence press on every chest, then pointed sharply at the yellow order.

"Track Two goes public at eight thirty sharp. Yunxi County."

Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming exhaled through his nose, nodding once. "The provincial joint task force has staged forty kilometers outside Yunxi County since seven forty-five. Discipline inspection teams, provincial anti-gang units, counter-terrorism explosive specialists, and prosecutors are in their vehicles waiting for the green light."

"Verify the targets," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua ordered.

"Deputy County Magistrate Sun Weiming, County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong, developer Deng Yongchang, and demolition enforcers Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao," Pei Hong read cleanly. "All five face ordinary, airtight provincial charges: bribery, abuse of authority, contract fraud, organized-crime leadership, and illegal use of explosives against civilians. Every piece of evidence comes from local Yunxi records. Zero mention of the capital sweep. Zero mention of Zero Guard."

"And the framed Old Street homeowner?" Premier Gu Chengshan asked quietly.

"Chen Mingde," Chief Justice Wu Cheng answered immediately. "His wrongful-detention rescission order and full exoneration certificate sit sealed in the lead car. The moment provincial officers take over Yunxi County Public Security Bureau, Chen Mingde walks out the front gate cleanly with a formal apology, and his family stays completely undisturbed."

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua glanced at the wall clock. Eight fourteen.

He pressed his heavy red seal hard onto the bottom of the yellow Yunxi dispatch order and slammed his open palm flat onto the paper. The sharp slap echoed off the walls, rattling three ashtrays.

"Order signed," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said, voice ringing with finality. "Guo Zhengming, give the provincial joint task force the green light. Roll into Yunxi County at eight thirty sharp. Let the whole province watch the law come down in broad daylight."

Across the smoke-filled hall, Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming lifted the red encrypted receiver and spoke six crisp words.

The first domino had fallen, and sixty minutes away by highway, Yunxi County had no idea the sky was about to drop.
