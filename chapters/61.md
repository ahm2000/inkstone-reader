# Chapter 61: National People's Congress: Stripping the Shields

Shortly after the war room broke into action, the doors of the National People's Congress Standing Committee inner hall locked from outside. Ten directors sat around the oval table under thick cigarette smoke.

Deputy Secretary-General Fan Jianguo walked in with a damp collar and a red-sealed folder. Nobody looked at the folder. Every director shot to their feet.

One step behind Fan Jianguo walked Elder Ye, retired Discipline and Judicial patriarch of the Ye Family, in a plain gray Zhongshan suit, carrying an old glass tea jar.

"Sit down," Elder Ye said evenly, taking the head chair and sipping his tea. "Do not stare at an old man. Stare at the folder Fan Jianguo brought."

Ten directors sat stiffly.

Fan Jianguo dropped the folder in the center, rattling three cups. "Nobody leaves until this is done," Fan Jianguo said hoarsely. "Premier Gu Chengshan, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, and the Founding Elders approved Track One secret trials and the Track Two sweep in Yunxi County. We strip thirty deputy protections before courts open."

Credential Review Director Song Wenming opened the folder, breath catching.

"Thirty?" Song Wenming muttered, turning pale. "Seven national deputies and twenty-three provincial deputies. Fan Jianguo, did Discipline Inspection verify every name?"

"Checked against twenty years of overseas bank records from Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg," Fan Jianguo replied coldly, lighting a cigarette. "Shengyuan Capital did not steal over 100 trillion yuan with street thugs alone. Fang Qiming bought lawmakers across fourteen provinces."

Copies slid around the table into heavy silence.

Constitutional and Law Director Zheng Baoguo pulled off his glasses, jaw tight. "Line four. The Southern Coastal Energy Subcommittee Chairman sat beside me three months ago preaching clean rule. Line eleven. That Provincial Deputy Director pushed three mining rule changes last year."

"He pushed them for Vice Governor Lin Zhengde and Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang," Supervisory and Judicial Director Ma Liancheng snapped. "Whenever auditors tried closing rare-earth loopholes, those deputies talked about local stability. Now we know who paid for their speeches."

Provincial Liaison Director Wei Donghai leaned back, sweat on his brow, glancing nervously at Elder Ye. "Hold on. Under the law, a sitting national deputy cannot be arrested without Standing Committee approval, or an emergency Chairmen's Council order confirmed later. Strip thirty deputies in one morning without full checks, and legal scholars will say we broke the rules."

"Broke the rules?" Ma Liancheng barked, slamming his palm down. "Three hundred and eighteen suspects were taken before sunrise! Nine years of coastal radar schedules walked out of military rooms! You lecture us on schedules while traitors hide behind deputy badges?"

"I am not shielding traitors, Ma Liancheng!" Wei Donghai shot back, neck red. "I am protecting the law! Chief Justice Wu Cheng and Procuratorate Director Pei Hong need solid convictions that stand forever, not sloppy papers signed in panic!"

"Wei Donghai is half right," Elder Ye spoke quietly. The room froze.

Elder Ye tapped his tea jar, pinning Wei Donghai with sharp eyes. "The law is not scrap paper. Chief Justice Wu Cheng needs clean orders nobody can overturn twenty years from now."

Wei Donghai exhaled slightly.

"However," Elder Ye continued, voice ice-cold, "do not mistake the people's shield for armor for thieves. A private citizen just gave this country 300 trillion yuan for free so ordinary people will not suffer while we cut out this rot. Any man who hides behind procedure to slow justice is not protecting the law. He is protecting the rot."

Wei Donghai's relief vanished. Sweat rolled down his temple as he lowered his head. "Understood, Elder Ye."

"Then write it clean," Legislative Drafting Director Deng Shuhua cut in, opening her law book. "One bad clause lets defense lawyers stall Track Two for months. We draft the removals cleanly under Article Thirty-Two and the national-security emergency rule, backed by Gao Zhengyi's treason proof."

Across the table, Financial and Economic Director Qiu Jianming sat silent, ash dropping onto his sleeve as he stared at page four.

"Qiu Jianming?" Deputy Audit Director Tang Shufen asked, eyes narrowing. "You have stared at line nineteen for two minutes. Do you know him?"

Qiu Jianming forced a stiff smile, his cup clinking. "Line nineteen is a provincial business vice chairman. We met twice during tax talks. Maybe his signature on Shengyuan Capital's coastal-port paper was routine work. Could we hold his removal forty-eight hours to double-check?"

The table went dead quiet. Elder Ye turned slowly and stared at Qiu Jianming.

Record-Filing Director Rao Liangcai flipped to page four in cold disbelief. "Routine work? Read the bottom note, Qiu Jianming. Three days after he pushed that port-customs break for Customs Director Xu Wenbin, 240 million yuan in Hong Kong fees hit his brother-in-law's Singapore account."

Qiu Jianming's smile died. "I had not read the bottom note yet. If that account is real, strip him at once."

"Forty-eight hours," Elder Ye murmured softly. Fan Jianguo lit a cigarette for him. Elder Ye took a slow drag and blew smoke toward Qiu Jianming. "Do you know how many accounts a guilty man can empty in forty-eight hours, Qiu Jianming? Or how many friends he can call?"

Qiu Jianming hunched his shoulders. "Elder Ye, I meant no harm. I swear it."

"Read every note before asking for forty-eight hours again," Fan Jianguo said coldly. "Anyone trying to buy a friend two days becomes line thirty-one before lunch, and Elder Ye hands that name to Gao Zhengyi himself."

A cold chill swept the hall. Two directors crushed out cigarettes and grabbed their pens.

"Move," Legislative Affairs Director Xu Zhihao ordered briskly, pulling red-header forms over. "Deng Shuhua and I draft the emergency bill for the seven national deputies under sealed authority. Song Wenming and Tang Shufen, verify every ID code."

"Pulling ID codes now," Song Wenming replied. "What about the twenty-three provincial deputies? Half their committees were emptied overnight."

"The Chairmen's Council emergency order overrides local delays," Zheng Baoguo said firmly. "Rao Liangcai and I will order all fourteen provincial committees to confirm the twenty-three removals immediately."

"And Yunxi County in Sha Province?" Wei Donghai asked quietly.

"County Magistrate Sun Weiming and developer Deng Yongchang hold only local honorary seats," Fan Jianguo answered flatly. "Provincial Discipline Inspection and Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's team will strip those on the spot as they roll into Yunxi County. We strip the thirty shields above them so nobody in a provincial capital screams about deputy protection."

Elder Ye exhaled thin smoke and nodded once. "Sign the sheets. Strip every shield clean."

Through the gray smoke, ten pens moved together, tearing apart twenty years of political armor while the old patriarch watched.
