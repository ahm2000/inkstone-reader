# Chapter 148: Dinner at the Qin Villa

Back at Emerald Riverside Villa, the rich aroma of caramelized rock sugar, fresh ginger, and slow-simmered soy sauce drifted from the kitchen into the bright dining room.

Chen Xuan had clocked out of Hengxin Logistics at 4:55 PM sharp, stopped by the neighborhood market on his way home, and tied on his dark blue apron before 5:30 PM. On the stove, a wide clay pot of free-range chicken and lotus root soup bubbled gently beside a glossy wok of sweet and savory braised pork ribs.

In the living room, the front door chimed as Qin Yuhan welcomed her visiting family inside.

Her mother, Lin Shuyin, stepped through the doorway first in an elegant burgundy wool shawl, followed by her father, Qin Zhengming, carrying a wooden box of aged Pu'er tea. Right behind them walked retired four-star general Elder Qin Zhengguo, his silver hair neatly combed and his heavy autumn coat unbuttoned.

Elder Qin stopped three steps into the foyer, lifted his chin, and sniffed the air with pure delight.

"Aha!" Elder Qin boomed, his eyes crinkling with laughter. "I smelled those braised ribs from the garden gate. Tell that lazy grandson-in-law of mine to turn off the stove before I eat straight from the wok."

Chen Xuan stepped out of the kitchen holding a steaming platter of mahogany-glazed ribs, wiping his hands on his apron. "Grandpa, Dad, Mom, welcome. Wash your hands and sit down. The soup is ready too."

Minutes later, five bowls of golden chicken and lotus root soup steamed across the oak dining table, flanked by the glossy braised pork ribs, stir-fried autumn greens with garlic, and crisp pan-seared sea bass.

Elder Qin picked up a rib with his chopsticks before anyone else had even settled their napkins. He took a generous bite, closed his eyes in satisfaction, and let out a hearty sigh.

"Tender right off the bone, not too sweet, and the ginger cuts the richness cleanly," Elder Qin declared. "Even the head chef at the state guesthouse cannot match this plate. Xuan, if you ever open a small noodle and ribs shop, I will be your first customer every noon."

"Do not encourage him, Dad," Lin Shuyin said with a polite smile, though she took a rib herself and chewed appreciatively. "He already has too little ambition as it is."

Qin Yuhan poured warm tea for her mother and laughed softly. "Mom, wait until you hear what happened at his office on Monday. General Manager Sun actually offered him a promotion to Deputy General Manager of Operations."

Qin Zhengming paused with his soup spoon raised, looking pleasantly surprised. "Deputy General Manager? That is wonderful news, Xuan. Hengxin Logistics has been expanding fast since the port forum. What salary did they offer?"

"50,000 yuan a month, plus bonuses," Qin Yuhan answered with a playful glance at her husband. "And he turned it down on the spot."

Silence dropped over the dining table for two full seconds.

Lin Shuyin gently set her chopsticks down on her porcelain rest and turned toward Chen Xuan in open disbelief. "You turned down 50,000 yuan a month?"

"I did, Mom," Chen Xuan said calmly, placing a piece of sea bass into Qin Yuhan's bowl. "The title sounds nice, but it comes with late-night client dinners and weekend calls. I like my 12,000 yuan desk much better."

Lin Shuyin exhaled sharply and shook her head. "Xuan, listen to me. I know Yuhan runs Haiyue Group and our family is not short of money. That is not the point. A man in his twenties needs drive and standing. Going from 12,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan a month shows people you are moving forward. How can you just wave away a promotion like you are turning down an extra bowl of rice?"

Qin Zhengming coughed lightly, trying to soften the mood. "Now, Shuyin, stability is not a bad thing. Still, Xuan, 50,000 yuan a month is a serious step up. Did you think it through carefully?"

Before Chen Xuan could answer, Qin Yuhan set her teacup down and spoke clearly, her voice warm yet firm.

"Mom, Dad, I actually support him completely," Qin Yuhan said, resting her hand lightly on Chen Xuan's wrist. "Every day at Haiyue Tower, I sit across from executives who earn ten times 50,000 yuan a month. Half of them have stomach ulcers, and the other half barely see their spouses for dinner. When I come home tired from meetings, the house is warm, the soup is hot, and Xuan is right here smiling at me. No promotion in Shanghai can buy that."

Elder Qin slapped his open palm against the dining table, making the soup bowls rattle, and burst into thunderous laughter.

"Well said, Yuhan!" Elder Qin cheered, pointing his chopsticks proudly at his granddaughter. "Shuyin, you listen to your daughter. A man who knows when he has enough, keeps his heart steady, and treats his wife like treasure is worth ten stressed executives chasing empty titles. Besides, if he worked until midnight every day, who would cook my braised pork ribs and play chess with me?"

Chen Xuan grinned warmly and ladled another scoop of lotus root soup into Elder Qin's bowl. "Grandpa understands me best. Eat while it is hot."

Lin Shuyin looked from her beaming father-in-law to her smiling daughter, then down at the glossy ribs on the platter. She let out a resigned huff, picked up her chopsticks, and took another rib.

"Fine, fine, three against one," Lin Shuyin muttered, though the corner of her mouth twitched upward as she tasted the tender meat. "I still think 50,000 yuan was a shame to pass up, though I suppose nobody else in Pudong can braise ribs like this."

Warm laughter broke out around the table as the evening breeze rustled the autumn trees outside the bright dining room windows.
