# Chapter 148: Dinner at the Qin Villa

On Wednesday evening in Shanghai, the warm smell of caramelized rock sugar, ginger, and slow simmered soy sauce filled the bright dining room of Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan's villa.

Chen Xuan had clocked out of Hengxin Logistics at 4:55 sharp, stopped by the fresh market on his way home, and tied on his dark blue kitchen apron before five thirty. On the stove, a clay pot of chicken and winter bamboo soup bubbled gently beside a glossy wok of sweet and savory braised pork ribs.

In the living room, the front door chimed as Qin Yuhan welcomed her visiting family inside.

Her mother, Lin Shuyin, stepped in first wearing an elegant burgundy wool shawl, followed by her father, Qin Zhengming, carrying a box of aged Pu'er tea. Right behind them walked retired military commander Elder Qin Zhengguo, his silver hair neatly combed and his heavy wool coat unbuttoned over a crisp gray sweater.

"Grandpa, Dad, Mom, come right in!" Qin Yuhan smiled brightly, taking her mother's handbag. "You arrived just as Chen Xuan finished the ribs."

Elder Qin Zhengguo took one deep breath of the warm kitchen aroma and his stern eyebrows lifted with instant pleasure. "Now that smells like proper home cooking! None of that fancy French hotel nonsense where they put three green peas on a cold plate and call it supper. Where is Chen Xuan?"

"Right here, Grandpa," Chen Xuan called out cheerfully as he stepped out of the kitchen carrying a big white porcelain platter heaped with glossy red braised pork ribs. He set the platter in the center of the round dining table and placed his scratched green thermos neatly beside his own setting. "Wash your hands and sit down while the ribs are hot."

Lin Shuyin looked from the steaming platter to Chen Xuan's dark blue apron and his old green thermos, letting out an exasperated sigh as she took her chair. "Chen Xuan, I just ran into General Manager Sun Haoran's wife at the charity tea this afternoon. Do you have any idea how red my face turned when she told me you rejected a promotion to Deputy General Manager at 50,000 yuan a month?"

Qin Zhengming paused halfway into his chair and blinked at Chen Xuan in surprise. "Fifty thousand a month? Chen Xuan, Sun Haoran offered you the fifteenth floor deputy job and you turned it down?"

"I did, Dad," Chen Xuan said calmly, untying his apron and sitting down beside Qin Yuhan with an easy smile. "Deputy managers have to work weekends and entertain shipping clients until ten at night. I told General Manager Sun I much prefer my 12,000 yuan desk."

Lin Shuyin tapped her manicured fingers on the tablecloth, half lecturing and half helpless. "Look at this young man! Other husbands in Shanghai fight tooth and nail for a corner office so they can match their wives. Yuhan runs the whole Haiyue Group, and her husband tells his general manager he does not want 50,000 yuan because it interferes with his afternoon tea! Do you have even a shred of career ambition inside that head of yours?"

"Mom, leave him alone," Qin Yuhan laughed, putting a glossy pork rib straight into her mother's bowl. "I already make enough money to keep ten offices running. Why do we need two tired corporate executives in one house? Look at my complexion this month. Ever since Chen Xuan started cooking my dinners every night at six, I have not had a single stomach ache!"

Before Lin Shuyin could start round two of her lecture, Elder Qin Zhengguo tapped his chopsticks firmly on the rim of his rice bowl.

"Shuyin, quiet down and eat your ribs!" Elder Qin Zhengguo boomed, chewing a tender piece of pork with pure satisfaction on his weathered face. "What is wrong with a 12,000 yuan clerk who knows how to live? Back in my army barracks, the loud fellows who chased promotions all day could not even boil a pot of noodles without burning the pan!"

Elder Qin Zhengguo pointed his chopsticks approvingly at Chen Xuan. "Look at these ribs! Soft right off the bone, not too sweet, not too greasy! You want him sitting in some stuffy fifteenth floor meeting room listening to windbags argue over shipping forms until nine at night? Who is going to braise my ribs when I visit on Wednesdays?"

Lin Shuyin threw up one hand in defeat. "Dad! You only defend him because he bribes your stomach every week! You used to lecture Zhengming for three hours if he came home five minutes early from the office!"

"Zhengming cannot cook a poached egg without breaking the yolk," Elder Qin Zhengguo snorted without looking up from his bowl, making Qin Zhengming cough awkwardly into his teacup while Qin Yuhan giggled behind her hand. "Besides, a man who keeps his wife smiling and his kitchen warm is doing a better job than half the big shots in Shanghai. Chen Xuan, pass me that spoon for the bamboo soup, and warm up your chess fingers. After dinner, you owe me a rematch on the rosewood board!"

Chen Xuan unscrewed his green thermos, poured himself a warm cup of jasmine tea, and smiled easily at the old general. "Anytime, Grandpa. Though if I win three games in a row again tonight, you cannot blame the lighting in the living room."

"Bah! Last week I slipped up because my glasses were fogged from the soup!" Elder Qin Zhengguo huffed proudly, reaching for a third pork rib. "Tonight I brought my reading glasses. Let us see a 12,000 yuan clerk slip a cannon past my river line tonight!"
