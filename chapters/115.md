# Chapter 115: Asleep on His Shoulder

That evening, after reluctant goodbyes in Hongshi Village and Yunxi County, the silver high-speed train bound for Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station sped smoothly across the darkened plains of Handong Province.

In the quiet second row of the first-class carriage, the overhead reading lamps cast a soft amber glow. On the luggage rack above Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan sat two sturdy cloth bags stuffed by Liu Meifang, Wu Lanying, and Grandmother Zhou Guilan with glass jars of spicy beef sauce, fresh country sweet potatoes, sun-dried red dates, and four paper bags of Auntie Tian's handmade sesame candy.

down the street in the next carriage, the two members of the Black Order sat quietly by the aisle, watching the connecting door while the steady, rhythmic hum of the steel tracks lulled the cabin.

After two full days of climbing six flights of stairs in Fuxing New Village, pinching dumplings with Liu Meifang, strolling down Old Street, and helping host five tables at Grandma's 81st birthday in Hongshi Village, Qin Yuhan was pleasantly tired. She leaned back against the plush blue seat, her fingertips resting lightly on the smooth silver bangle Grandma Zhou Guilan had placed on her wrist.

In the soft amber light of the carriage, the simple silver band looked even warmer than the fine watch beside it.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said quietly, turning her head toward him.

"Hmm?" Chen Xuan replied, unscrewing his dented 15-yuan green metal thermos to take a slow sip of warm water.

"Thank you for bringing me home with you," she murmured, her voice soft and sincere. "This weekend was the warmest, most peaceful two days I have had in years. Nobody asked me about stock prices, quarterly targets, or board meetings. Your mother just worried if I ate enough dumplings, and Grandma held my hand all morning."

Chen Xuan smiled gently and set the thermos back on the folding tray. "That is what home is supposed to feel like. Whenever Lujiazui gets too noisy, we can always buy two train tickets and come back."

"Can we come back together for the Lunar New Year?" Qin Yuhan asked, looking at him with hopeful eyes. "I want to help Auntie make dumplings again, and I want to bring Grandpa Qin some of Auntie Tian's sesame candy tomorrow so he can taste what Yunxi County is like."

"Of course we will," Chen Xuan promised without hesitation. "By New Year, my mom will probably have knitted you three wool sweaters to match."

Qin Yuhan laughed softly, the sound fading into a quiet, contented sigh. She leaned closer against the armrest, watching the distant village lights flicker like warm sparks across the dark autumn fields outside the window. The gentle sway of the high-speed train and the fatigue of a full, joyful weekend soon caught up with her. Her long eyelashes fluttered once, then twice, before closing peacefully. A moment later, her head slid softly down to rest against Chen Xuan's shoulder.

Chen Xuan stayed completely still for a few seconds so he would not wake her. Listening to her calm, steady breathing, he reached across carefully, slipped his gray cotton jacket off his other shoulder, and draped it gently over her arms so the carriage air conditioning would not chill her. He shifted slightly in his seat to give her a more comfortable angle, letting her rest easily against him while the train glided smoothly through the autumn night.

A faint, silent vibration pulsed once inside his trouser pocket.

Using his free hand, Chen Xuan pulled out his phone and glanced at the encrypted message glowing softly on the screen. It was from Number 1 of the White Order.

"Young Master, the autumn cleanup in Handong is complete. Hou Liangping's bureau has sealed the remaining assets from the fallen faction, and the heritage and rural funds from the four national banks are running smoothly. Emerald Riverside Villa has been aired and prepared for your return."

Chen Xuan read the short report with calm, unhurried eyes. A single quiet instruction from him had cleared decades of rot out of an entire province, paved the road to his grandmother's village, and brought fresh life back to Old Street, all without a single official or neighbor ever learning his name.

He tapped a single word in reply.

"Good."

Then he locked the phone, slipped it back into his pocket, and turned his gaze toward the dark train window.

Far off along the flat eastern horizon, the warm golden glow of Shanghai's city lights began to rise against the crisp autumn night sky. In less than an hour, they would step off the platform, take a quiet ride back to Emerald Riverside Villa in Pudong, and unpack Liu Meifang's jars of spicy beef sauce into their kitchen pantry.

Tomorrow was Monday. Chen Xuan would wake up early, boil millet porridge and fry eggs for his wife, pack his dented 15-yuan green metal thermos, and head to his comfortable modest logistics desk on the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics. He would drink tea, handle his shipping forms at a leisurely pace, and clock out right at 4:55 PM to buy fresh ribs from the market on his way home.

Chen Xuan glanced down at Qin Yuhan sleeping soundly against his shoulder, Grandma's silver bangle catching the dim amber glow of the reading lamp.

Let the powerful men of the world fight over thrones, titles, and fortunes. Right here, with his wife resting peacefully beside him and two cloth bags of hometown food overhead, Chen Xuan already had everything he ever wanted.
