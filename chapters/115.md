# Chapter 115: Asleep on His Shoulder

At seven-forty on Sunday evening, the silver high-speed train bound for Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station sped smoothly across the darkened plains of Handong Province.

In the quiet second row of the first-class carriage, the overhead reading lamps were dimmed to a soft amber glow. On the luggage rack above Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan sat two cloth bags stuffed to the brim by Liu Meifang, Wu Lanying, and Grandmother Zhou Guilan: homemade spicy beef sauce in glass jars, fresh country sweet potatoes from Hongshi Village, dried red dates, and four bags of Auntie Tian's handmade sesame candy.

Fifty meters back in the next carriage, the two members of the Black Order sat quietly by the aisle, watching the door while the rhythmic hum of the tracks lulled the cabin.

After two full days of climbing six flights of stairs in Fuxing New Village, pinching dumplings with Liu Meifang, strolling down Old Street, and chatting all afternoon with Grandmother Zhou Guilan and the village aunties in Hongshi Village, the cool and capable president of Haiyue Group was completely worn out.

Still wearing the pale peach silk scarf that old tailor Yu Changshun had given her around her neck, Qin Yuhan leaned her head against Chen Xuan's left shoulder, her breathing soft and even as she slept.

Chen Xuan took off his gray cotton jacket, draped it carefully over her shoulders and knees to keep off the carriage air conditioning, and held her right hand loosely in his warm palm.

Just ten minutes earlier, before her eyelids grew too heavy to stay open, Qin Yuhan had murmured sleepily against his collarbone, "Chen Xuan, my mom Lin Shuyin called from Beijing while you were loading the bags at the station. She asked if I felt out of place sleeping in a sixty-square-meter apartment in Yunxi County."

"And what did President Qin tell Aunt Lin?" Chen Xuan had whispered back with a smile.

"I told her the quilt Aunt Liu Meifang dried in the sun smelled better than any five-star hotel suite in Lujiazui," Qin Yuhan had whispered, a small sleepy smile curving her lips right before she drifted off. "And I told her that next month, when your parents finish their medical checkup in Yunxi County, we are bringing them and Grandma Zhou to Emerald Riverside Villa for a week so I can take them up the Oriental Pearl Tower."

Now, as Qin Yuhan slept soundly on his shoulder, the bright lights of Jingzhou City flashed past the train window in the distance.

Inside the lighted conference room of the Handong Provincial Party Committee compound in Jingzhou City that very Sunday evening, Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin, Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang, Provincial Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming, and Anti-Corruption Bureau Director Hou Liangping were closing the final folders on a massive three-week financial and judicial clean-up.

Over the past seventy-two hours, with the remaining members of the corrupt Handa Faction, including Gao Yuliang and Qi Tongwei, formally transferred to judicial custody alongside fugitive official Ding Yizhen and crooked coal merchant Cai Chenggong, every hidden shell company attached to Shanshui Group and Shanshui Villa had been cleanly dismantled. Because the unseen benefactor's 300 trillion yuan sovereign reserve stood quietly behind the four national banks, China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China had absorbed and restructured all 28 billion yuan of Guangming Peak redevelopment debt without a single ripple in the provincial market or a single day's delay in worker wages.

At the same hour in Beijing, inside the quiet red-walled compound by the lake, Elder Song and Premier Gu Chengshan read the Sunday evening stabilization report delivered by Number 1 of the White Order. Seeing that Handong Province's industrial output and heritage street commerce had actually risen by twelve percent during the sweep while the supreme sovereign was simply riding an evening train back to Shanghai with two bags of country sweet potatoes and homemade beef sauce, Elder Song and Premier Gu Chengshan exchanged an admiring smile and signed the national commendation order for Sha Ruijin's team.

Back inside the quiet first-class carriage, the train crossed the provincial border into the glowing night skyline near Shanghai.

Qin Yuhan stirred slightly in her sleep as the train gently decelerated toward Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station. Her fingers tightened around Chen Xuan's thumb, and she blinked her dark eyes open under the soft amber lamp.

"Are we back in Shanghai already?" Qin Yuhan asked in a soft, husky voice, rubbing her cheek lightly against his shirt before sitting up and noticing his gray jacket tucked around her shoulders. "You gave me your coat again. Are your arms not cold?"

"With a 100 million yuan company president using my shoulder as a pillow for two hours, how could I possibly feel cold?" Chen Xuan grinned, unscrewing his scratched green enamel thermos and pouring half a capful of still-warm honey water for her to moisten her throat. "Drink a sip of warm water. Assistant Su Wan texted twenty minutes ago to say the driver left your Maybach in the VIP parking lot at Hongqiao Station so we can drive straight home."

Qin Yuhan drank the warm honey water from the thermos cap, her eyes clear and affectionate as she folded his gray jacket and helped him put his arms back through the sleeves.

"What time do you have to be at Hengxin Logistics tomorrow morning?" she asked, smoothing the collar of his jacket with her fingers.

"Eight fifty-five, same as always," Chen Xuan chuckled, standing up to lift the two heavy cloth bags of country sweet potatoes and Liu Meifang's beef sauce down from the overhead rack with one effortless hand. "And tomorrow afternoon at four fifty-five, I am clocking out on the dot to come home and boil Grandma Zhou's sweet potatoes in rock-sugar soup for you."

"Do not forget to pack a small jar of Mom's spicy beef sauce for my lunch box at Haiyue Tower tomorrow," Qin Yuhan smiled brightly, linking her arm through his as the train doors glided open onto the familiar Shanghai platform.

"Already packed in the top pocket, Wife," Chen Xuan laughed, walking her hand in hand into the warm spring night of Shanghai.
