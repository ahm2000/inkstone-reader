# Chapter 67: The Provincial Joint Task Force Forms

Ten minutes later, inside the basement war room of the Lanshui Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission and Provincial Public Security Bureau, one hundred and twenty officers sat in four quiet blocks. Anti-corruption investigators filled the left rows. Anti-gang detectives and counter-terrorism officers held the center. Bank auditors with silver cases sat along the right wall.

Only the low hum of wall screens showing maps of Yunxi County broke the silence.

At the front stand, Provincial CCDI Secretary Shen Wenchao stood beside Provincial PSB Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan. Both men had red eyes and smelled of strong tobacco. Han Zhiyuan took one last drag from his cigarette, crushed it into a tin tray, and gripped the mic.

"Phones in the signal-blocking lockers," Han Zhiyuan ordered, his voice cracking like a gunshot. "Every personal phone, smartwatch, and extra SIM card. Team leaders, collect them now and lock the steel boxes. Nobody rolls out with an open signal."

A sharp rustle swept the room as one hundred and twenty officers dropped their devices into cloth bags. Two young detectives near the back traded quick, startled looks. A raid this big without a single word to the local city bureau meant the order came straight from the very top.

Once the steel lids clanked shut, Han Zhiyuan aimed his red pointer at the circle around Yunxi County.

"At eight oh five sharp, our joint task force hits the highway," Han Zhiyuan said. "Eighteen unmarked provincial CCDI sedans, four armored counter-terrorism vans, and six patrol cars from neighboring districts, with zero officers from Yunxi County. We split into four strike teams past the Yunxi County tollgate. Column One hits the Yunxi County Government building. Column Two locks down the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau. Column Three seals Pier Four and arrests gang leader Yan Tiehu's dock crew. Column Four secures the Yunxi County Detention Center."

In the front anti-gang row, Captain Deng Kai raised his hand, his brow furrowed. "Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan, Yunxi County PSB Director Cheng Biao has run that bureau for eleven years. Half his patrol captains are his relatives or old thugs from Yan Tiehu's crew. If Column Two walks in during broad daylight, Cheng Biao might tell his men to stall us while they shred files or tip off County Magistrate Sun Weiming."

Han Zhiyuan's jaw locked, a muscle ticking under his cheek. "Let him try," Han Zhiyuan snapped. "Commander Wu Zheng, your forty counter-terrorism officers go through the gate thirty seconds ahead of our investigators. Any Yunxi officer who touches his gun, locks a computer room, or refuses to drop his radio goes face-down on the concrete in five seconds flat. No talking."

Commander Wu Zheng, a broad-shouldered veteran with a scar on his chin, gave one short nod. "Understood, Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan. Guns stay holstered unless someone draws on us, and full cuffs on anyone who fights back."

"Good," Han Zhiyuan said. "Provincial tech teams are already watching Yunxi County's police radios and office phone lines. The second our lead car clears the tollgate, Yunxi PSB's internal radios drop to dead static. Cheng Biao will not even reach the guard at his own front gate."

On the right, Lead Forensic Auditor Ma Lihua fixed her glasses and tapped the first page of her red-tabbed packet, her forehead creased.

"Secretary Shen Wenchao, I have a question on our first target," Ma Lihua said steadily. "We have a 5.8 billion yuan shell pipeline under Chairman Deng Yongchang, smuggling at Pier Four, and bribes to County Magistrate Sun Weiming. Yet Priority One for Column Four is not a bank vault or a computer room. Priority One is a fifty-four-year-old hardware shopkeeper named Chen Mingde sitting in Cell Nine of the Yunxi County Detention Center."

A quiet murmur rippled through the investigators. Several detectives leaned forward, their eyes sharpening.

Shen Wenchao stepped forward and laid both palms flat on the stand. His face was cold stone, silencing the room right away.

"Priority One is Chen Mingde," Shen Wenchao confirmed, his voice slicing cleanly through the air. "He is a law-abiding hardware shopkeeper who refused to sell his shop on Yunxi County's old commercial street to Deng Yongchang's shell builders. Yesterday, Yan Tiehu's thugs staged a fake fight at his shop, Yunxi County People's Hospital faked an assault X-ray, and Cheng Biao backdated an arrest warrant by twelve minutes to lock him away."

Ma Lihua blinked, her fingers tightening around her pen. "I understand the wrongful arrest, Secretary Shen Wenchao. I am only asking if locking down bank terminals first stops Deng Yongchang's accountants from wiping remote records."

"The bank accounts are already frozen at the provincial bank center," Shen Wenchao replied coldly. "A computer can wait ten minutes. An innocent citizen sitting in a cell with thugs paid to break his ribs cannot wait ten seconds. Listen carefully, every single person in this room."

Shen Wenchao's eyes swept slowly across all one hundred and twenty faces.

"Everything by the book," Shen Wenchao said, every word sharp and firm. "Protect the wrongfully framed hardware shopkeeper Chen Mingde, arrest every corrupt official and gang thug in broad daylight, and never mention anything above provincial authority."

The room went dead still. Captain Deng Kai's eyes narrowed as that last line sank in. A veteran detective knew better than to ask who stood above provincial authority.

"One rule question on Chen Mingde," Captain Deng Kai spoke carefully. "Standard release rules say we call the prisoner's family to the county police bureau to sign the property sheet and pick him up. Should Column Four send a patrol car for Chen Mingde's wife or son once his release is signed?"

"Never," Shen Wenchao cut him off sharply, his eyes narrowing like blades. "Hear this and underline it on your clipboard. Neither Chen Mingde's wife nor his son nor any relative steps foot inside a police station or jail. Not today, not tomorrow, not for a single signature."

Deng Kai stiffened, his pen freezing on the page. "Yes, Secretary Shen Wenchao. How do we handle his release?"

"I will sign the provincial clearance paper myself," Shen Wenchao said. "Our Provincial Joint Inspection Group will check Chen Mingde's health right there, wipe every false charge from the national system before he walks out the gate, and drive him straight to his own front door in an unmarked car. Zero press cameras, zero red tape, zero trouble for his family. Clear?"

"Crystal clear, Secretary Shen Wenchao!" Column Four's lead investigator answered crisply, snapping his folder shut.

Han Zhiyuan checked his steel watch and grabbed his dark jacket. "When we hit Yunxi County at eight twenty, the streets will be full of morning shoppers and store owners. Make every arrest cleanly in broad daylight, read every warrant out loud, lock the cuffs properly, and keep body cameras running. Let Yunxi County see that Sun Weiming, Cheng Biao, and Yan Tiehu are not untouchable."

Han Zhiyuan's eyes hardened. "And obey Secretary Shen Wenchao's rule. If anyone asks who ordered this raid, the answer is the Lanshui Provincial Party Committee and the Provincial Joint Task Force. Not one word higher. Dismissed. Move to the cars!"

One hundred and twenty chairs scraped back in one loud roar as the task force poured out toward the waiting black convoy.
