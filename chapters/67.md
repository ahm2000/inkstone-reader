# Chapter 67: The Provincial Joint Task Force Forms

Ten minutes later, inside the basement joint war room of the Lanshui Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission and Provincial Public Security Bureau, one hundred and twenty officers sat in four silent blocks. Anti-corruption investigators filled the left rows, anti-gang detectives and counter-terrorism tactical officers held the center, and forensic auditors with aluminum cases sat along the right wall.

Only the low hum of wall projectors showing Yunxi County satellite grids broke the silence.

At the front podium, Provincial CCDI Secretary Shen Wenchao stood beside Provincial PSB Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan. Both men had red-rimmed eyes and smelled of strong tobacco. Han Zhiyuan took one last drag from his cigarette, crushed it into a tin tray on the lectern, and gripped the microphone.

"Phones in the Faraday lockers," Han Zhiyuan ordered, his voice cracking like a rifle shot. "Every personal phone, smartwatch, and secondary SIM card. Team leaders, collect them now and lock the steel boxes. Nobody rolls out with an unmonitored signal."

A sharp rustle swept the room as one hundred and twenty officers dropped devices into canvas pouches. Two young detectives near the back traded quick, startled glances. A raid of this scale without a word to the municipal bureau meant the hammer was falling from the very top.

Once the steel lids clanked shut, Han Zhiyuan aimed his laser pointer at the red circle around Yunxi County.

"At eight oh five sharp, our joint task force hits the expressway," Han Zhiyuan said. "Eighteen unmarked provincial CCDI sedans, four armored counter-terrorism vans, and six municipal patrol units from neighboring districts, zero personnel from Yunxi County. We split into four strike columns past the Yunxi County tollgate. Column One hits the Yunxi County Government compound. Column Two locks down the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau. Column Three seals Pier Four and arrests gang leader Yan Tiehu's dock crew. Column Four secures the Yunxi County Detention Center."

In the front anti-gang row, Captain Deng Kai raised his hand, brow furrowed. "Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan, Yunxi County PSB Director Cheng Biao has run that bureau for eleven years. Half his patrol captains are his relatives or former enforcers from Yan Tiehu's crew. If Column Two enters in broad daylight, Cheng Biao might order his duty officers to stall us while shredding records or tipping off County Magistrate Sun Weiming."

Han Zhiyuan's jaw locked, a muscle ticking beneath his cheekbone. "Let him try," Han Zhiyuan snapped. "Commander Wu Zheng, your forty counter-terrorism officers go through the gate thirty seconds ahead of investigators. Any Yunxi officer who touches his holster, locks a server room, or refuses to drop his radio goes face-down on the concrete in five seconds flat. No negotiation."

Commander Wu Zheng, a square-shouldered veteran with a scarred chin, gave one curt nod. "Understood, Deputy Chief Han Zhiyuan. Sidearms stay holstered unless lethal force is drawn, full restraints on anyone who resists."

"Good," Han Zhiyuan said. "Provincial technical teams are already mirroring Yunxi County's police dispatch frequencies and landlines. The second our lead sedan clears the tollgate, Yunxi PSB's internal radios drop to dead static. Cheng Biao will not even reach the guard at his own gate."

On the right, Lead Forensic Auditor Ma Lihua adjusted her glasses and tapped the first page of her red-tabbed packet, forehead creased.

"Secretary Shen Wenchao, I have a question on target priority," Ma Lihua said steadily. "We have 5.8-billion-yuan shell pipeline under Chairman Deng Yongchang, Pier Four smuggling, and bribery of County Magistrate Sun Weiming. Yet Priority One for Column Four is not a bank vault or server rack. Priority One is a fifty-four-year-old hardware shopkeeper named Chen Mingde held in Cell Nine of the Yunxi County Detention Center."

A faint murmur rippled through the investigators. Several detectives leaned forward, eyes sharpening.

Shen Wenchao stepped forward, resting both palms flat on the lectern. His face was cold stone, silencing the room instantly.

"Priority One is Chen Mingde," Shen Wenchao confirmed, voice slicing clean through the air. "He is a law-abiding hardware shopkeeper who refused to sell his storefront on Yunxi County's old commercial street to Deng Yongchang's shell developers. Yesterday, Yan Tiehu's thugs staged an altercation at his shop, Yunxi County People's Hospital forged an assault scan, and Cheng Biao backdated a detention warrant by twelve minutes to lock him away."

Ma Lihua blinked, fingers tightening around her pen. "I understand the miscarriage of justice, Secretary Shen Wenchao. I only ask whether securing bank terminals first prevents Deng Yongchang's accountants from wiping remote ledgers."

"The bank accounts are already frozen at the provincial clearinghouse," Shen Wenchao replied coldly. "A server can wait ten minutes. An innocent citizen sitting in a cell with thugs paid to break his ribs cannot wait ten seconds. Listen carefully, every single person in this room."

Shen Wenchao's gaze swept slowly across all one hundred and twenty faces.

"Everything by the book," Shen Wenchao said, every word crisp and unyielding. "Protect the wrongfully framed hardware shopkeeper Chen Mingde, arrest every corrupt official and gang enforcer in broad daylight, and never mention anything above provincial jurisdiction."

The room went dead still. Captain Deng Kai's pupils tightened as that final line sank in. A veteran detective knew better than to ask who stood above provincial jurisdiction.

"One procedural question on Chen Mingde," Captain Deng Kai spoke carefully. "Standard release protocol requires calling the detainee's family to the county public security bureau to sign the property ledger and escort him out. Should Column Four send a patrol car for Chen Mingde's wife or son once the release is signed?"

"Absolutely not," Shen Wenchao cut him off sharply, eyes narrowing like blades. "Hear this and underline it on your clipboard. Neither Chen Mingde's wife nor his son nor any relative sets foot inside a police station or detention center. Not today, not tomorrow, not for a single signature."

Deng Kai stiffened, pen freezing on the page. "Yes, Secretary Shen Wenchao. How do we execute the release?"

"I will sign the provincial exoneration certificate personally," Shen Wenchao said. "Our Provincial Joint Inspection Group will verify Chen Mingde's medical condition on site, wipe every false charge from the national database before he exits the gate, and drive him directly to his own front door in an unmarked sedan. Zero press cameras, zero bureaucratic harassment, zero blowback on his family. Clear?"

"Crystal clear, Secretary Shen Wenchao!" Column Four's lead investigator answered crisply, snapping his folder shut.

Han Zhiyuan checked his steel watch and grabbed his dark windbreaker. "When we hit Yunxi County at eight twenty, the streets will be full of commuters and shopkeepers. Make every arrest cleanly in broad daylight, read every warrant aloud, lock cuffs properly, and keep body cameras rolling. Let Yunxi County see that Sun Weiming, Cheng Biao, and Yan Tiehu are not untouchable."

Han Zhiyuan's gaze hardened. "And obey Secretary Shen Wenchao's rule. If anyone asks who ordered this raid, the answer is the Lanshui Provincial Party Committee and the Provincial Joint Task Force. Not one syllable higher. Dismissed. Move to the vehicles!"

One hundred and twenty chairs scraped back in one thunderous roar as the task force poured toward the waiting black convoy.
