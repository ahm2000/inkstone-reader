# Chapter 134: Corridor Fourteen

At half past eleven that night, a dark sedan sat parked with its engine off in the shadow behind the Lanshui Grand Hotel.

Inside the back seat, Li Weiming smoked one cigarette after another with shaking fingers. His silver-rimmed glasses sat crooked on his nose, and his shirt collar was damp with cold sweat.

Everything he had built in Lanshui over the past two years had collapsed in one day.

Two hours ago, his contact at the airport had sent a frantic message: Li Weiming's passport was flagged at every border gate and train station in the province. State Security was already closing in. Song Baisheng had stopped answering his phone, and Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang had locked himself in his hotel suite.

The Zurich partners had been furious on the private phone line before cutting contact completely. Their last foothold on the southern coast was gone.

In Li Weiming's panicked mind, the entire ruin came down to one person: that strange, quiet Hengxin Logistics clerk with the scratched green thermos.

"How did a small clerk from Qingyan know who owned Alpine Marine Data Services before the folders were even opened?" Li Weiming muttered hoarsely, crushing his cigarette into the ashtray. "He has a file on us. He must be carrying a phone or a drive from whoever tipped off Haiyue Group. If I catch him tonight and find out who sent him, I might still have something to bargain with."

Beside him sat a broad-shouldered man with a faded scar across his jaw and a black cap pulled low. He was a hired underground enforcer who worked for cash and asked no questions.

"500k yuan in cash," Li Weiming said sharply, tapping a heavy canvas bag stuffed with crisp red bills between them. "Half now and half the minute you bring that clerk to the van in the underground garage. Room 1408 on the fourteenth floor. He went down to the lobby tea counter ten minutes ago alone. Catch him in the fourteenth-floor hallway at midnight, take his phone and bag, and do not make a sound."

The scarred man unzipped the bag, glanced at the thick bundles of cash, and nodded once. "Four of us are already upstairs near the service alcove. We carry short blades and compact pistols with silencers. One plain office clerk will not even have time to shout."

"Good," Li Weiming hissed, his eyes bright with desperate hope. "Bring him down the freight lift. Do not fail."

At twelve o'clock sharp, the fourteenth floor of the Lanshui Grand Hotel was completely silent.

Dim yellow wall lamps glowed over the thick carpet. Every guest door along Corridor Fourteen was shut tight.

At the far end of the hall, the elevator gave a soft chime and slid open.

Chen Xuan stepped out unhurriedly in a dark sweater over his plain white shirt, holding his warm green thermos in one hand and a small paper bag with two late-night steamed buns in the other.

He walked down the quiet hallway at an easy pace, heading toward Room 1408.

Twenty paces from his door, the faintest draft moved near the ice-machine alcove ahead of him. A second later, the shadow behind the linen doorway behind him shifted as well.

Four tough men in dark jackets stepped smoothly into the corridor, two blocking the front and two cutting off the rear. The scarred leader in front pulled a short dark steel knife from his sleeve, while the man beside him drew a compact black pistol fitted with a silencer, keeping it low against his hip.

They had stepped straight into Chen Xuan's fifty-meter perimeter.

"Hold it right there," the scarred leader murmured in a flat, cold voice, taking two steps closer. "Hand over your phone and walk quietly to the service lift. Make one sound and you bleed on this carpet."

Chen Xuan did not drop his paper bag. He did not tighten his grip on his thermos. He did not even look surprised.

He simply stopped in the middle of the corridor, glanced calmly at the two men ahead and then lightly over his shoulder at the two behind, and let out a mild sigh.

"You four picked the wrong hallway," Chen Xuan said softly.

The scarred leader scoffed and lunged forward to grab Chen Xuan's collar.

At that exact split second, the heavy metal fire door of the stairwell ten feet away swung open without a single creak.

Two masked figures in plain black gear stepped silently out of the dark stairwell.

Neither wore a badge, a patch, or any number. Full matte-black masks covered their faces completely, leaving only cold, steady eyes beneath dark hoods. They moved across the carpet with zero sound, like two shadows sliding over still water.

The scarred leader's eyes widened in shock as the first Zero Guard member crossed ten feet in a single heartbeat.

Before the mercenary could swing his knife, the first Zero Guard member caught his forearm cleanly, turned his own weight inward, and drove a silent palm strike straight into the side of his neck. The scarred man's eyes rolled back instantly. The masked figure caught both the falling blade and the collapsing body in one smooth motion, lowering him softly onto the rug without a thump.

Beside him, the gunman tried to swing his silenced pistol up.

The second Zero Guard member was already inside his reach. A crisp chop snapped the gunman's wrist downward, stripping the pistol cleanly out of his fingers before his thumb found the trigger. One swift knee to the ribs folded him in half, followed by a precise strike behind the ear that knocked him out cold.

Behind Chen Xuan, the other two mercenaries froze for half a second, then reached frantically for their knives.

They never cleared their pockets.

The two Zero Guard members moved past Chen Xuan on opposite sides like dark wind, never bumping his shoulder or spilling a drop of his tea. Two muffled thuds, two sharp wrist locks, and two silent neck strikes ended the fight cleanly.

Eight seconds flat.

Not a single shout echoed down Corridor Fourteen. Not a single shot fired. Not even a dropped knife clattered on the floor. All four armed mercenaries lay unconscious across the soft carpet, their weapons lined neatly beside them.

One of the masked figures paused near the stairwell door and spoke three short words into a tiny collar mic, the voice completely flat, neutral, and genderless, cold as winter iron.

"Four down. Clear."

At the far end of the hall, the elevator chimed softly and opened again.

Four stern plainclothes State Security officers stepped out quickly, led by a lean, sharp-eyed team captain wearing black gloves and carrying nylon ties.

Before the officers reached the middle of the corridor, the two Zero Guard members stepped silently back through the fire door. The stairwell was empty once more, leaving the permanent team quietly back on watch within fifty meters of Chen Xuan.

The State Security captain checked the four unconscious men, then stood and gave Chen Xuan a brief, deep nod of quiet respect.

"Sorry to disturb your evening, Mr. Chen," the captain said in a low voice. "We tracked Li Weiming's car to the back alley twenty minutes ago. With these four and the cash downstairs, we have everything we need for tomorrow morning."

"Hard work so late at night, Captain," Chen Xuan replied politely, adjusting his bag of steamed buns. "Have a safe shift."

"Good night, Mr. Chen."

While the officers swiftly bound and lifted the four unconscious mercenaries toward the service lift, Chen Xuan walked calmly past them, unlocked Room 1408, and went to sleep.
