# Chapter 20: Number 1 Calls an Emergency Briefing

Past eleven on Saturday night, three floors under an unmarked cold-storage warehouse on the Sha City ring road, White Guard's regional safehouse sat behind steel doors. Five work booths lined the room behind dark glass.

At 11:18, the speaker chimed. Five lights shifted from yellow to green.

"All five team leads are online," Number 1 said over the private line, calm and sharp. "Money Tracking, Street Watch, Computer Access, River Shipping, and Field Action. Twenty minutes to line up everything from Yunxi County tonight."

"Street Watch, Number 7, online," confirmed Booth Two.

"Computer Access, Number 14, online," answered Booth Three.

"Signals and River Shipping, Number 34, online," came from Booth Four.

"Field Action, Number 56, online," said Booth Five.

"Money Tracking, Number 11, online," replied Booth One. "Files ready."

"Start with the field report," Number 1 ordered.

Number 7 tapped a button. Three screens opened: the tea-room recording where County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong ordered Cheng Biao to hold Chen Mingde two extra days; the matched 1976 air-raid tunnel map under Mingde Hardware; and the heat picture of forty-two lead-lined boxes hidden under gravel on the Pier Four barge.

"What started yesterday as a county frame-up against Chen Mingde has crossed into military smuggling," Number 7 said evenly. "Number 27 confirmed direct teamwork between Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, and Deng Yongchang. Number 42 and Number 68 proved Hongrui Real Estate is digging an underground storage room beneath Old Street with a hidden rail track to Pier Four. Two hours ago, Number 9 and Number 19 caught an unmarked barge leaving Pier Four with rare-earth metals and military casings stolen from the Seventh Inland Military-Industrial Materials Plant, headed for Pier Twelve at Shanghai Port."

Silence held the room for three tense seconds.

"Number 34," Number 1 said calmly. "Confirm the river path and past trips."

"The barge is passing the Sha City south lock under a fake sand-digging permit," Number 34 answered briskly. "Our river watch has eyes on it to Shanghai Port. Five years of Pier Twelve records show the seafood shell company cleared sixty-four identical shipments to Rotterdam and Singapore without a single physical customs check."

"Sixty-four shipments in five years," Number 56 muttered, tension clear. "Zero flags."

"Now show the money," Number 1 ordered. "Number 11."

Across the center wall, the money chart spread from Yunxi County. At the bottom left sat a red dot labeled Hongrui Real Estate Accounts: 4.8 billion yuan. As Number 11 zoomed out, that dot shrank into a tiny stream feeding a massive underground river.

"When we froze Hongrui Real Estate's accounts last night, we expected local panic," Number 11 said coolly. "Instead, we triggered an automatic shift across seventy-four companies over four cities: shell builders, fake farm groups, river-digging outfits, and shipping brokers. Every one is a feeder pipe."

Blue and red lines met at a massive hub in the center.

"Hongrui Real Estate's 4.8 billion yuan is under one percent of the web," Number 11 went on. "Over six years, we traced a hidden provincial network totaling 620 billion yuan. At its center sits Shengyuan Capital, registered in the provincial capital as a private building and trade fund."

Five profiles popped up on the right.

"Who protects Shengyuan Capital inside the province?" Number 1 asked.

"Three high provincial officials across the planning office, transport bureau, and state-property office," Number 11 answered cleanly. "They approve land sales, highway weight-station passes, and inland factory scrap papers. At Shanghai Port, every Pier Twelve bypass carries electronic approval from two customs directors."

"Where does the 620 billion yuan end up?" Number 1 asked quietly.

Number 11 zoomed out again, drawing a gold line to Europe.

"Through three layers of Singapore shipping shells into a numbered secret bank trust in Zurich," Number 11 replied. "Every shipment clearing Pier Twelve triggers a foreign payout into that Zurich trust within two days. The yuan side washes back through Shengyuan Capital to provincial backers and county fronts like Hongrui Real Estate."

Number 56 spoke up immediately, voice urgent. "Number 1, this is way past a county cleanup. Military smuggling, two crooked customs directors, and three provincial officials shielding a 620 billion yuan network. Should we wake Chen Xuan in Yunxi County now and ask to lock down Pier Twelve and arrest the provincial tier before dawn?"

Number 34 added quickly, "If that barge reaches Shanghai Port by Monday morning, another load of military metals leaves our coast."

For five seconds, only computer fans hummed.

Then Number 1 spoke, every word landing like cold iron.

"No."

Nobody cut in.

"Look at the numbers again," Number 1 said, razor-sharp. "620 billion yuan in six years, three provincial officials, two customs directors, and a Zurich trust paying for military materials. Ask yourselves one question. Can three provincial officials rewrite scrap papers inside an inland defense factory, shut down coastal customs checks for five years, and move 620 billion yuan overseas without someone above the province holding the umbrella?"

Heavy silence settled over all five booths.

"No," Number 56 admitted quietly. "Three provincial officials do not have that power alone."

"Yunxi County was only a cracked tile at the yard's edge, and Shengyuan Capital is only the provincial hallway," Number 1 continued evenly. "Hit Pier Twelve before we see who sits above Shengyuan Capital, and the top tier throws the customs directors to the wolves, burns the books, and disappears before sunrise."

"Your orders, Number 1?" Number 11 asked crisply.

"Keep Yunxi under tight watch," Number 1 directed. "Track the Pier Four barge to the dock without touching it. Keep Chen Mingde safe inside lockup through quiet watch, and keep all trouble away from Fuxing New Village while Chen Xuan rests with his family."

"And the deeper search?" Number 14 asked.

"All five teams drop routine watch now," Number 1 ordered. "Number 11 and Number 14, run a twenty-year deep check starting from Shengyuan Capital's founding papers and the Zurich trust record. Trace every linked government bond, defense supplier, customs bypass, and overseas pipe back twenty years. Number 7 and Number 34, map every link between those three provincial officials, the customs directors, and whoever they answer to outside the province."

"Twenty-year deep check confirmed," Number 11 replied. "First full picture in twenty-four to thirty-six hours."

"Take the time you need, and leave nothing buried," Number 1 said, colder than the night outside. "Do not bring him half a story. Find out how high the roof goes."

"Understood," five voices answered in clean number order.

The line clicked off. Across the wall, the map broke into pouring streams of records as White Guard threw its full weight into twenty years of buried secrets.
