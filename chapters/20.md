# Chapter 20: Number One Calls an Emergency Briefing

Past eleven o'clock on Saturday night, rain tapped steadily against the corrugated metal roof of an unmarked cold-storage depot on the industrial ring road of Lanshui. Three floors beneath the silent loading bays, behind blast-rated steel doors and an independent power substation, lay Black Pine Guard's regional safehouse.

Inside the chilled operations chamber, five recessed workstation booths lined the perimeter in a semicircle, separated by smoked acoustic glass angled so no division lead ever saw another's face or silhouette. Inside Black Pine Guard, real names did not exist, faces remained unseen, and not even Number One knew what the others looked like. They were numbers, disciplines, and clean execution.

At 11:18, the overhead speaker gave a single dry chime. Across the six-meter tactical wall, five encrypted status indicators shifted from amber to green.

"All five division leads are logged," Number One said. The voice came through the encrypted audio bridge calm, unhurried, and razor-sharp, carrying quiet authority without ever raising volume. "Financial Intelligence, Field Surveillance, Technical Intrusion, Maritime Logistics, and Containment Operations. We have twenty minutes to align what came out of Yunxi County tonight."

"Field Surveillance, Number Seven, online," confirmed Booth Two.

"Technical Intrusion, Number Fourteen, online," answered Booth Three.

"Maritime Logistics, Number Thirty-four, online," came from Booth Four.

"Containment Operations, Number Fifty-six, online," said Booth Five.

"Financial Intelligence, Number Eleven, online," replied Booth One. "Data package staged."

"Begin with the field summary," Number One ordered.

Number Seven tapped one control. Three windows illuminated on the left third of the wall: the audio waveform from the courtyard tea room where County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong ordered Cheng Biao to hold Chen Mingde two extra days; the overlaid 1976 civil-defense tunnel blueprint beneath Mingde Hardware; and the thermal-density silhouette of forty-two lead-lined crates buried under wet gravel aboard the Pier Four barge.

"What began yesterday as a county framing case against Chen Mingde has crossed into strategic contraband," Number Seven stated evenly. "Number Twenty-seven confirmed direct collusion among Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, and Deng Yongchang. Number Forty-two and Number Sixty-eight proved Hongrui Real Estate is carving a subterranean cargo vault beneath Old Street with a concealed rail spur to Pier Four. Two hours ago, Number Nine and Number Nineteen verified an unmarked barge departing Pier Four with rare-earth mineral concentrates and dual-use precision alloy casings siphoned from the Seventh Inland Military-Industrial Materials Plant, bound for Pier Twelve at Qingyan Port."

Silence held the chamber for three seconds.

"Maritime Logistics," Number One said calmly. "Confirm the downriver track."

"Number Thirty-four reporting," Booth Four answered. "The Pier Four barge is clearing the Lanshui south lock under a forged sand-dredging permit. Passive relays hold full coverage to Qingyan Port. Five years of Pier Twelve transshipment logs show the receiving seafood shell company cleared sixty-four identical outbound consignments to Rotterdam and Singapore without a single physical customs inspection."

"Now show the money," Number One instructed. "Number Eleven."

Across the central four meters of the wall, the financial map expanded outward from Yunxi County like dark ink spreading through water.

At the bottom left sat a red node labeled Hongrui Real Estate Escrow: 4.8 billion yuan. Yesterday, that sum had seemed absurdly large for a fourth-tier county developer. Now, as Number Eleven zoomed back three levels, the 4.8-billion-yuan node shrank into a tiny tributary feeding an underground river.

"When we froze Hongrui Real Estate's county escrow accounts last night, we expected local panic," Number Eleven said coolly. "Instead, we triggered an automated liquidity reroute across seventy-four seemingly unrelated entities spanning four prefectures: shell developers, fake agricultural cooperatives, river-dredging contractors, and bonded freight brokers. Every one of them is a feeder pipe."

Lines of pale blue and crimson light converged on a massive hexagonal hub at the center of the provincial display.

"Hongrui Real Estate's four point eight billion yuan is less than one percent of the structure," Number Eleven continued. "Over the past six years alone, we have traced a provincial shadow clearing network totaling six hundred and twenty billion yuan. At its center sits Shengyuan Capital, registered in the provincial capital as a private infrastructure and commodities fund."

Five encrypted dossiers opened along the upper right edge of the glass wall.

"Shengyuan Capital does not run on private muscle," Number Eleven said. "Its land approvals, highway weigh-station exemptions, and inland scrap reclassifications are shielded by three provincial-level officials across the development commission, transport bureau, and state-assets oversight office. At Qingyan Port, every Pier Twelve inspection override carries the digital counter-signatures of two customs directors."

"And where does the six hundred and twenty billion yuan settle?" Number One asked quietly.

Number Eleven zoomed out once more, drawing a clean golden arc across the continent to Europe.

"Through three layers of Singaporean maritime shells straight into a numbered offshore trust in Zurich," Number Eleven answered. "Every shipment of siphoned rare-earth concentrates and alloy casings cleared through Pier Twelve triggers a foreign-currency settlement into that Zurich trust within forty-eight hours. The yuan side washes back through Shengyuan Capital to provincial patrons and county fronts like Hongrui Real Estate."

In Booth Five, Number Fifty-six spoke immediately. "Number One, this is far beyond a county cleanup. We have defense-material smuggling, two compromised customs directors, and three provincial-level officials protecting a six-hundred-and-twenty-billion-yuan clearing network. Should we wake the Boss in Yunxi County now and request authorization to seal Pier Twelve and detain the provincial tier before dawn?"

For five full seconds, the speaker remained silent save for the low hum of server racks beneath the floor.

Then Number One spoke, each word landing like cold steel.

"No."

No one interrupted.

"Look at the numbers again," Number One said, calm and razor-sharp. "Six hundred and twenty billion yuan in six years, three provincial-level officials, two customs directors, and a Zurich trust paying out for military-industrial materials. Can three provincial officials rewrite scrap classifications inside an inland defense supplier, silence coastal customs audits for five years, and move six hundred and twenty billion yuan overseas without someone above the province holding the umbrella?"

No one answered, because the answer was obvious.

"Yunxi County was only a cracked tile at the courtyard edge, and Shengyuan Capital is only the provincial hallway," Number One continued evenly. "Strike Pier Twelve before we see who sits above Shengyuan Capital, and the top tier will sacrifice the customs directors, burn the ledgers, and vanish before morning."

"Your orders, Number One?" Number Eleven asked.

"Hold Yunxi containment steady," Number One directed. "Track the Pier Four barge onto the dock without interference. Keep Chen Mingde safe inside the county detention block through our passive monitors, and keep all noise away from Fuxing New Village while the Boss rests with his family."

"All five divisions drop non-essential monitoring immediately. Number Eleven and Number Fourteen, run a full twenty-year forensic sweep from Shengyuan Capital's founding charter and the Zurich trust registration. Trace every linked sovereign bond, defense subcontractor, customs override, and offshore corridor back two decades. Number Seven and Number Thirty-four, map every link between the three provincial officials, the customs directors, and whoever they answer to outside the province."

"Twenty-year forensic sweep confirmed," Number Eleven replied. "Initial convergence in twenty-four to thirty-six hours."

"Take the time you need, and leave nothing buried," Number One said, colder than the autumn rain outside. "Do not bring the Boss half a picture. Find out how high the roof goes."

"Acknowledged," five voices answered in clean numerical sequence.

The audio bridge clicked off. Across the dark operations wall, the provincial map fractured into cascading data streams as Black Pine Guard shifted its full weight into twenty years of buried ledgers while Yunxi County slept beneath the mist.
