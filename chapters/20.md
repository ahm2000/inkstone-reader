# Chapter 20: Number 1 Calls an Emergency Briefing

Past eleven o'clock on Saturday night, three floors under an unmarked cold-storage warehouse on the Sha City ring road, White Guard's regional safehouse sat behind heavy steel doors. Five work booths lined the main room in a half-circle behind dark glass.

At 11:18, the ceiling speaker chimed once. Five status lights shifted from yellow to green.

"All five team leads are online," Number 1 said over the private voice line, calm and sharp. "Money Tracking, Street Watch, Computer Access, River Shipping, and Field Action. We have twenty minutes to line up everything that came out of Yunxi County tonight."

"Street Watch, Number 7, online," confirmed Booth Two.

"Computer Access, Number 14, online," answered Booth Three.

"Signals and River Shipping, Number 34, online," came from Booth Four.

"Field Action, Number 56, online," said Booth Five.

"Money Tracking, Number 11, online," replied Booth One. "Files are ready."

"Start with the field report," Number 1 ordered.

Number 7 tapped one button. Three screens opened on the main wall: the tea-room recording where County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong ordered Cheng Biao to hold Chen Mingde two extra days; the matched 1976 air-raid tunnel map under Mingde Hardware; and the heat picture of forty-two lead-lined boxes hidden under gravel on the Pier Four barge.

"What started yesterday as a county frame-up against Chen Mingde has crossed into military smuggling," Number 7 said evenly. "Number 27 confirmed direct teamwork between Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, and Deng Yongchang. Number 42 and Number 68 proved Hongrui Real Estate is digging an underground storage room beneath Old Street with a hidden rail track out to Pier Four. Two hours ago, Number 9 and Number 19 caught an unmarked barge leaving Pier Four with rare-earth metals and precision military metal casings stolen from the Seventh Inland Military-Industrial Materials Plant, headed straight for Pier Twelve at Shanghai Port."

Silence held the room for three tense seconds.

"Number 34," Number 1 said calmly. "Confirm the river path and past trips."

"The barge is passing the Sha City south lock right now under a fake sand-digging permit," Number 34 answered briskly. "Our river watch has full eyes on it all the way to Shanghai Port. Five years of Pier Twelve records show the seafood shell company cleared sixty-four identical overseas shipments to Rotterdam and Singapore without a single physical customs check."

"Sixty-four shipments in five years," Number 56 muttered in Booth Five, tension clear even through the speaker. "Zero flags."

"Now show the money," Number 1 ordered. "Number 11."

Across the center wall, the money chart spread outward from Yunxi County. At the bottom left sat a red dot labeled Hongrui Real Estate Accounts: 4.8 billion yuan. As Number 11 zoomed out, that dot shrank into a tiny stream feeding a huge underground river.

"When we froze Hongrui Real Estate's county bank accounts last night, we expected local panic," Number 11 said coolly. "Instead, we set off an automatic money shift across seventy-four separate companies spread over four cities: shell builders, fake farm groups, river-digging outfits, and shipping brokers. Every single one is a feeder pipe."

Pale blue and bright red lines met at a massive hub in the middle of the screen.

"Hongrui Real Estate's 4.8 billion yuan is less than one percent of the whole web," Number 11 went on. "Over six years alone, we have traced a hidden provincial money network totaling 620 billion yuan. Right at its center sits Shengyuan Capital, registered in the provincial capital as a private building and trade fund."

Five profile files popped up along the top right edge of the wall.

"Who protects Shengyuan Capital inside the province?" Number 1 asked.

"Three high provincial officials across the planning office, transport bureau, and state-property office," Number 11 answered cleanly. "They sign off on land sales, highway weight-station passes, and inland factory scrap papers. At Shanghai Port, every Pier Twelve check bypass carries the electronic approval of two customs directors."

"And where does the 620 billion yuan end up?" Number 1 asked quietly.

Number 11 zoomed out one more time, drawing a gold line across the map to Europe.

"Through three layers of Singapore shipping shell companies straight into a numbered secret bank trust in Zurich," Number 11 replied. "Every shipment that clears Pier Twelve triggers a foreign-money payout into that Zurich trust within two days. The yuan side washes back through Shengyuan Capital to provincial backers and county fronts like Hongrui Real Estate."

In Booth Five, Number 56 spoke up right away, his voice tight with urgency. "Number 1, this is way past a county cleanup. We have military smuggling, two crooked customs directors, and three provincial officials shielding a 620 billion yuan network. Should we wake Chen Xuan in Yunxi County now and ask permission to lock down Pier Twelve and arrest the provincial tier before dawn?"

In Booth Four, Number 34 added quickly, "If that barge reaches Shanghai Port by Monday morning, another load of military-grade metals leaves our coast."

For five full seconds, only the low hum of computer fans filled the speaker.

Then Number 1 spoke, every word landing like cold iron.

"No."

Nobody cut in.

"Look at the numbers again," Number 1 said, calm and razor-sharp. "620 billion yuan in six years, three provincial officials, two customs directors, and a Zurich bank trust paying out for military materials. Ask yourselves one simple question. Can three provincial officials rewrite scrap papers inside an inland defense factory, shut down coastal customs checks for five years, and move 620 billion yuan overseas without someone above the province holding the umbrella?"

Heavy silence settled over all five booths.

"No," Number 56 admitted quietly after a beat. "Three provincial officials do not have that kind of power alone."

"Yunxi County was only a cracked tile at the edge of the yard, and Shengyuan Capital is only the provincial hallway," Number 1 continued evenly. "Hit Pier Twelve before we see who sits above Shengyuan Capital, and the top tier throws the customs directors to the wolves, burns the books, and disappears before sunrise."

"Your orders, Number 1?" Number 11 asked, his voice crisp again.

"Keep Yunxi under tight watch," Number 1 directed. "Track the Pier Four barge all the way to the dock without touching it. Keep Chen Mingde safe inside the county lockup through our quiet watch, and keep all trouble away from Fuxing New Village while Chen Xuan rests with his family."

"And the deeper search?" Number 14 asked from Booth Three.

"All five teams drop routine watch work right now," Number 1 ordered. "Number 11 and Number 14, run a full twenty-year deep check starting from Shengyuan Capital's founding papers and the Zurich trust record. Trace every linked government bond, defense supplier, customs bypass, and overseas money pipe back twenty years. Number 7 and Number 34, map every link between those three provincial officials, the customs directors, and whoever they answer to outside the province."

"Twenty-year deep check confirmed," Number 11 replied. "First full picture in twenty-four to thirty-six hours."

"Take the time you need, and leave nothing buried," Number 1 said, colder than the night outside. "Do not bring him half a story. Find out how high the roof goes."

"Understood," five voices answered in clean number order.

The voice line clicked off. Across the dark wall, the provincial map broke apart into pouring streams of records as White Guard threw its full weight into twenty years of buried secrets.
