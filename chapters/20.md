# Chapter 20: Number 1 Calls an Emergency Briefing

Past eleven o'clock on Saturday night, three floors beneath an unmarked cold-storage depot on the Lanshui ring road, Black Pine Guard's regional safehouse sat behind steel doors and smoked acoustic glass. Five workstation booths lined the operations chamber in a semicircle, angled so no division lead ever saw another's face. Inside Black Pine Guard, real names did not exist, faces remained unseen, and not even Number 1 knew what the others looked like.

At 11:18, the overhead speaker chimed once. Five status indicators shifted from amber to green.

"All five division leads are logged," Number 1 said through the encrypted audio bridge, calm and razor-sharp. "Financial Intelligence, Field Surveillance, Technical Intrusion, Maritime Logistics, and Containment Operations. We have twenty minutes to align what came out of Yunxi County tonight."

"Field Surveillance, Number 7, online," confirmed Booth Two.

"Technical Intrusion, Number 14, online," answered Booth Three.

"Maritime Logistics, Number 34, online," came from Booth Four.

"Containment Operations, Number 56, online," said Booth Five.

"Financial Intelligence, Number 11, online," replied Booth One. "Data package staged."

"Begin with the field summary," Number 1 ordered.

Number 7 tapped one control. Three windows opened on the wall: the tea-room audio where County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong ordered Cheng Biao to hold Chen Mingde two extra days; the overlaid 1976 civil-defense tunnel blueprint beneath Mingde Hardware; and the thermal silhouette of forty-two lead-lined crates under gravel aboard the Pier Four barge.

"What began yesterday as a county framing case against Chen Mingde has crossed into strategic contraband," Number 7 stated evenly. "Number 27 confirmed direct collusion among Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, and Deng Yongchang. Number 42 and Number 68 proved Hongrui Real Estate is carving a subterranean cargo vault beneath Old Street with a rail spur to Pier Four. Two hours ago, Number 9 and Number 19 verified an unmarked barge leaving Pier Four with rare-earth concentrates and dual-use precision alloy casings siphoned from the Seventh Inland Military-Industrial Materials Plant, bound for Pier Twelve at Qingyan Port."

Silence held the chamber for three taut seconds.

"Number 34," Number 1 said calmly. "Confirm the downriver track and historical volume."

"The barge is clearing the Lanshui south lock under a forged sand-dredging permit," Number 34 answered briskly. "Passive relays hold full coverage to Qingyan Port. Five years of Pier Twelve logs show the receiving seafood shell company cleared sixty-four identical outbound consignments to Rotterdam and Singapore without a single physical customs inspection."

"Sixty-four shipments in five years," Number 56 muttered in Booth Five, tension audible even over the filtered bridge. "Zero flags."

"Now show the money," Number 1 instructed. "Number 11."

Across the central wall, the financial map expanded outward from Yunxi County. At the bottom left sat a red node labeled Hongrui Real Estate Escrow: 4.8 billion yuan. As Number 11 zoomed back, that node shrank into a tiny tributary feeding an underground river.

"When we froze Hongrui Real Estate's county escrow accounts last night, we expected local panic," Number 11 said coolly. "Instead, we triggered an automated liquidity reroute across seventy-four seemingly unrelated entities spanning four prefectures: shell developers, fake agricultural cooperatives, river-dredging contractors, and bonded freight brokers. Every one is a feeder pipe."

Pale blue and crimson lines converged on a massive hexagonal hub at the center of the display.

"Hongrui Real Estate's 4.8 billion yuan is less than one percent of the structure," Number 11 continued. "Over six years alone, we have traced a provincial shadow clearing network totaling 620 billion yuan. At its center sits Shengyuan Capital, registered in the provincial capital as a private infrastructure and commodities fund."

Five dossiers opened along the upper right edge of the wall.

"Who shields Shengyuan Capital inside the province?" Number 1 asked.

"Three provincial-level officials across the development commission, transport bureau, and state-assets oversight office," Number 11 answered cleanly. "They sign off on land transfers, weigh-station exemptions, and inland scrap reclassifications. At Qingyan Port, every Pier Twelve inspection override carries the digital counter-signatures of two customs directors."

"And where does the 620 billion yuan settle?" Number 1 asked quietly.

Number 11 zoomed out once more, drawing a golden arc across the continent to Europe.

"Through three layers of Singaporean maritime shells straight into a numbered offshore trust in Zurich," Number 11 replied. "Every shipment cleared through Pier Twelve triggers a foreign-currency settlement into that Zurich trust within forty-eight hours. The yuan side washes back through Shengyuan Capital to provincial patrons and county fronts like Hongrui Real Estate."

In Booth Five, Number 56 spoke immediately, voice tight with urgency. "Number 1, this is far beyond a county cleanup. We have defense-material smuggling, two compromised customs directors, and three provincial-level officials protecting 720-billion-yuan network. Should we wake Chen Xuan in Yunxi County now and request authorization to seal Pier Twelve and detain the provincial tier before dawn?"

In Booth Four, Number 34 added quickly, "If the barge reaches Qingyan Port by Monday morning, another batch of military-grade concentrates leaves coastal waters."

For five full seconds, only the low hum of server racks filled the speaker.

Then Number 1 spoke, each word landing like cold steel.

"No."

No one interrupted.

"Look at the numbers again," Number 1 said, calm and razor-sharp. "620 billion yuan in six years, three provincial-level officials, two customs directors, and a Zurich trust paying out for military-industrial materials. Ask yourselves a simple question. Can three provincial officials rewrite scrap classifications inside an inland defense supplier, silence coastal customs audits for five years, and move 620 billion yuan overseas without someone above the province holding the umbrella?"

Silence settled over all five booths.

"No," Number 56 admitted quietly after a beat. "Three provincial officials do not have that reach alone."

"Yunxi County was only a cracked tile at the courtyard edge, and Shengyuan Capital is only the provincial hallway," Number 1 continued evenly. "Strike Pier Twelve before we see who sits above Shengyuan Capital, and the top tier sacrifices the customs directors, burns the ledgers, and vanishes before morning."

"Your orders, Number 1?" Number 11 asked, tone crisp again.

"Hold Yunxi containment steady," Number 1 directed. "Track the Pier Four barge onto the dock without interference. Keep Chen Mingde safe inside the county detention block through passive monitors, and keep all noise away from Fuxing New Village while Chen Xuan rests with his family."

"And the deeper trace?" Number 14 asked from Booth Three.

"All five divisions drop non-essential monitoring immediately," Number 1 ordered. "Number 11 and Number 14, run a full twenty-year forensic sweep from Shengyuan Capital's founding charter and the Zurich trust registration. Trace every linked sovereign bond, defense subcontractor, customs override, and offshore corridor back two decades. Number 7 and Number 34, map every link between the three provincial officials, the customs directors, and whoever they answer to outside the province."

"Twenty-year forensic sweep confirmed," Number 11 replied. "Initial convergence in twenty-four to thirty-six hours."

"Take the time you need, and leave nothing buried," Number 1 said, colder than the night outside. "Do not bring him half a picture. Find out how high the roof goes."

"Acknowledged," five voices answered in clean numerical sequence.

The audio bridge clicked off. Across the dark wall, the provincial map fractured into cascading data streams as Black Pine Guard shifted its full weight into twenty years of buried ledgers.
