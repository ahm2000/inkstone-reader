# Chapter 20: Number One Calls an Emergency Briefing

Past eleven o'clock on Saturday night, three floors beneath an unmarked cold-storage depot on the Lanshui ring road, Black Pine Guard's regional safehouse sat behind steel doors and smoked acoustic glass. Five workstation booths lined the operations chamber in a semicircle, angled so no division lead ever saw another's face. Inside Black Pine Guard, real names did not exist, faces remained unseen, and not even Number One knew what the others looked like.

At 11:18, the overhead speaker chimed once. Five status indicators shifted from amber to green.

"All five division leads are logged," Number One said through the encrypted audio bridge, calm and razor-sharp. "Financial Intelligence, Field Surveillance, Technical Intrusion, Maritime Logistics, and Containment Operations. We have twenty minutes to align what came out of Yunxi County tonight."

"Field Surveillance, Number Seven, online," confirmed Booth Two.

"Technical Intrusion, Number Fourteen, online," answered Booth Three.

"Maritime Logistics, Number Thirty-four, online," came from Booth Four.

"Containment Operations, Number Fifty-six, online," said Booth Five.

"Financial Intelligence, Number Eleven, online," replied Booth One. "Data package staged."

"Begin with the field summary," Number One ordered.

Number Seven tapped one control. Three windows opened on the wall: the tea-room audio where County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Bureau Chief Liang Guodong ordered Cheng Biao to hold Chen Mingde two extra days; the overlaid 1976 civil-defense tunnel blueprint beneath Mingde Hardware; and the thermal silhouette of forty-two lead-lined crates under gravel aboard the Pier Four barge.

"What began yesterday as a county framing case against Chen Mingde has crossed into strategic contraband," Number Seven stated evenly. "Number Twenty-seven confirmed direct collusion among Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, and Deng Yongchang. Number Forty-two and Number Sixty-eight proved Hongrui Real Estate is carving a subterranean cargo vault beneath Old Street with a rail spur to Pier Four. Two hours ago, Number Nine and Number Nineteen verified an unmarked barge leaving Pier Four with rare-earth concentrates and dual-use precision alloy casings siphoned from the Seventh Inland Military-Industrial Materials Plant, bound for Pier Twelve at Qingyan Port."

Silence held the chamber for three taut seconds.

"Number Thirty-four," Number One said calmly. "Confirm the downriver track and historical volume."

"The barge is clearing the Lanshui south lock under a forged sand-dredging permit," Number Thirty-four answered briskly. "Passive relays hold full coverage to Qingyan Port. Five years of Pier Twelve logs show the receiving seafood shell company cleared sixty-four identical outbound consignments to Rotterdam and Singapore without a single physical customs inspection."

"Sixty-four shipments in five years," Number Fifty-six muttered in Booth Five, tension audible even over the filtered bridge. "Zero flags."

"Now show the money," Number One instructed. "Number Eleven."

Across the central wall, the financial map expanded outward from Yunxi County. At the bottom left sat a red node labeled Hongrui Real Estate Escrow: 4.8 billion yuan. As Number Eleven zoomed back, that node shrank into a tiny tributary feeding an underground river.

"When we froze Hongrui Real Estate's county escrow accounts last night, we expected local panic," Number Eleven said coolly. "Instead, we triggered an automated liquidity reroute across seventy-four seemingly unrelated entities spanning four prefectures: shell developers, fake agricultural cooperatives, river-dredging contractors, and bonded freight brokers. Every one is a feeder pipe."

Pale blue and crimson lines converged on a massive hexagonal hub at the center of the display.

"Hongrui Real Estate's four point eight billion yuan is less than one percent of the structure," Number Eleven continued. "Over six years alone, we have traced a provincial shadow clearing network totaling six hundred and twenty billion yuan. At its center sits Shengyuan Capital, registered in the provincial capital as a private infrastructure and commodities fund."

Five dossiers opened along the upper right edge of the wall.

"Who shields Shengyuan Capital inside the province?" Number One asked.

"Three provincial-level officials across the development commission, transport bureau, and state-assets oversight office," Number Eleven answered cleanly. "They sign off on land transfers, weigh-station exemptions, and inland scrap reclassifications. At Qingyan Port, every Pier Twelve inspection override carries the digital counter-signatures of two customs directors."

"And where does the six hundred and twenty billion yuan settle?" Number One asked quietly.

Number Eleven zoomed out once more, drawing a golden arc across the continent to Europe.

"Through three layers of Singaporean maritime shells straight into a numbered offshore trust in Zurich," Number Eleven replied. "Every shipment cleared through Pier Twelve triggers a foreign-currency settlement into that Zurich trust within forty-eight hours. The yuan side washes back through Shengyuan Capital to provincial patrons and county fronts like Hongrui Real Estate."

In Booth Five, Number Fifty-six spoke immediately, voice tight with urgency. "Number One, this is far beyond a county cleanup. We have defense-material smuggling, two compromised customs directors, and three provincial-level officials protecting a six-hundred-and-twenty-billion-yuan network. Should we wake Chen Xuan in Yunxi County now and request authorization to seal Pier Twelve and detain the provincial tier before dawn?"

In Booth Four, Number Thirty-four added quickly, "If the barge reaches Qingyan Port by Monday morning, another batch of military-grade concentrates leaves coastal waters."

For five full seconds, only the low hum of server racks filled the speaker.

Then Number One spoke, each word landing like cold steel.

"No."

No one interrupted.

"Look at the numbers again," Number One said, calm and razor-sharp. "Six hundred and twenty billion yuan in six years, three provincial-level officials, two customs directors, and a Zurich trust paying out for military-industrial materials. Ask yourselves a simple question. Can three provincial officials rewrite scrap classifications inside an inland defense supplier, silence coastal customs audits for five years, and move six hundred and twenty billion yuan overseas without someone above the province holding the umbrella?"

Silence settled over all five booths.

"No," Number Fifty-six admitted quietly after a beat. "Three provincial officials do not have that reach alone."

"Yunxi County was only a cracked tile at the courtyard edge, and Shengyuan Capital is only the provincial hallway," Number One continued evenly. "Strike Pier Twelve before we see who sits above Shengyuan Capital, and the top tier sacrifices the customs directors, burns the ledgers, and vanishes before morning."

"Your orders, Number One?" Number Eleven asked, tone crisp again.

"Hold Yunxi containment steady," Number One directed. "Track the Pier Four barge onto the dock without interference. Keep Chen Mingde safe inside the county detention block through passive monitors, and keep all noise away from Fuxing New Village while Chen Xuan rests with his family."

"And the deeper trace?" Number Fourteen asked from Booth Three.

"All five divisions drop non-essential monitoring immediately," Number One ordered. "Number Eleven and Number Fourteen, run a full twenty-year forensic sweep from Shengyuan Capital's founding charter and the Zurich trust registration. Trace every linked sovereign bond, defense subcontractor, customs override, and offshore corridor back two decades. Number Seven and Number Thirty-four, map every link between the three provincial officials, the customs directors, and whoever they answer to outside the province."

"Twenty-year forensic sweep confirmed," Number Eleven replied. "Initial convergence in twenty-four to thirty-six hours."

"Take the time you need, and leave nothing buried," Number One said, colder than the night outside. "Do not bring him half a picture. Find out how high the roof goes."

"Acknowledged," five voices answered in clean numerical sequence.

The audio bridge clicked off. Across the dark wall, the provincial map fractured into cascading data streams as Black Pine Guard shifted its full weight into twenty years of buried ledgers.
