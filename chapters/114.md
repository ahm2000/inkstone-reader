# Chapter 114: Grandma's Eighty-First Birthday in Hongshi Village

On Sunday morning at nine-thirty, the red brick courtyard of Grandmother Zhou Guilan's country house in Hongshi Village, twenty minutes outside Yunxi County town, was bustling with laughter and the aroma of stewed pork and longevity noodles.

Today was Grandmother Zhou Guilan's eighty-first birthday. Five round tables had been set up beneath the blossoming pear tree in the courtyard, while fifty meters down the village road by the stone bridge, two members of the Black Order leaned calmly against a parked black sedan.

Inside the main courtyard, eighty-one-year-old Zhou Guilan sat in her cushioned rattan armchair wearing a festive burgundy silk jacket that Qin Yuhan had brought from Shanghai. From the moment Qin Yuhan knelt beside her chair to hand her a box of soft ginseng honey cake and called her "Grandma" in her gentle voice, the old lady had refused to let go of Qin Yuhan's hand for a single minute.

"Look at our Little Xuan's wife!" Zhou Guilan beamed, patting the back of Qin Yuhan's slim hand while showing her off to every village auntie who walked into the yard. "Her skin is fairer than pear blossoms, and she speaks so sweetly! Even my back stopped aching as soon as she walked through the gate!"

"Grandma, Chen Xuan told me you grow the sweetest sweet potatoes in Hongshi Village," Qin Yuhan smiled warmly, peeling a warm roasted chestnut and placing it in the old lady's palm. "Before we take the evening train back to Shanghai tonight, you have to let me pack two sweet potatoes to roast at home."

"Pack a whole basket!" Zhou Guilan laughed happily.

Over by the center table under the pear tree, however, a loud, boastful voice kept rising above the chatter of the relatives.

It was Liu Guoliang, a distant cousin from Liu Meifang's extended clan who ran a small sand-and-gravel truck business near the highway. Wearing a shiny faux-leather jacket with his car keys clipped prominently to his belt, Liu Guoliang was cracking sunflower seeds and waving his hand grandly to the older villagers at his table.

"Let me tell you folks, working a regular salaried job in a big city is nothing compared to having real connections in Handong Province!" Liu Guoliang bragged loudly, casting a sidelong glance toward Chen Xuan, who was calmly pouring hot green tea from his enamel thermos for Chen Jianguo and Chen Mingde. "Just last week, I had dinner with Section Clerk Zhao from the Jingzhou City Municipal Commerce Bureau! Now that Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin and Secretary Li Dakang have cleaned out the corrupt Handa Faction and Shanshui Group in Jingzhou City, someone with connections like mine in the municipal office can get business licenses approved in three days!"

A village uncle asked curiously, "Guoliang, Little Xuan works in Shanghai now, right? His salary must be pretty good too!"

Liu Guoliang let out a dry chuckle and tossed a sunflower seed shell onto the plate. "Uncle, do not be fooled by the word Shanghai! Yesterday Aunt Meifang told me Chen Xuan only makes twelve thousand yuan a month filing customs papers at Hengxin Logistics! Twelve thousand yuan in Shanghai is barely enough to pay rent and buy steamed buns! Look at his wife. She is pretty, sure, but following a twelve-thousand-yuan clerk around while he carries an old green thermos must be tough! Little Xuan, if you ever cannot afford Shanghai rent, come back and I will ask Section Clerk Zhao in Jingzhou City to find you a warehouse guard job!"

Liu Meifang and Chen Mingde frowned slightly at Liu Guoliang's rude bragging, while Qin Yuhan, who signed 500 million yuan checks at Haiyue Group without blinking, nearly laughed out loud at the idea of Chen Xuan needing a warehouse guard job.

Chen Xuan just took a peaceful sip from his green thermos and smiled. "Thank you for the thought, Cousin Guoliang, but the tea at my current desk tastes pretty good."

Before Liu Guoliang could open his mouth to boast again, the sound of engines hummed outside the village lane.

First, a freshly washed white cold-chain delivery van pulled up outside the courtyard gate, and Chen Xuan's childhood best friend Wang Lei jumped out carrying two huge red gift baskets of prime fresh strawberries and river crabs.

"Grandma Zhou! Happy eighty-first birthday!" Wang Lei called out cheerfully, jogging into the yard before grinning at Chen Xuan and bowing politely to Qin Yuhan. "Brother Xuan! Sister-in-Law! Wait until you see who asked me for directions at the village entrance just now!"

Right behind Wang Lei's van, two official black government sedans with Jingzhou City and Handong Provincial license plates glided to a smooth stop outside the red brick gate.

Old Street Police Station Director Wei Kun stepped out of the first car in a crisp uniform, while a middle-aged man in a dark suit wearing a Handong Provincial Committee badge stepped out of the lead sedan carrying a gold-framed rosewood plaque draped in red silk, stamped with the red seal of the Handong Provincial Office.

Liu Guoliang dropped his sunflower seeds onto his lap, his jaw nearly hitting the table as he recognized the provincial license plate code. "A provincial committee car! Why is a secretary from Secretary Sha Ruijin's provincial office coming to our village?"

The provincial secretary and Director Wei Kun walked briskly into the courtyard with warm, respectful smiles, stopping directly in front of Second Uncle Chen Mingde and Grandmother Zhou Guilan.

"Old Madam Zhou, we wish you health and longevity on your eighty-first birthday!" the provincial secretary bowed respectfully before turning to Chen Mingde and presenting the gold-framed rosewood plaque with both hands. "Comrade Chen Mingde, on behalf of Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin's office and the Jingzhou City Commerce Bureau, we are here to personally deliver the first 'Handong Provincial Model Heritage Merchant' plaque to Mingde Hardware for your thirty years of honest business on Old Street!"

Director Wei Kun smiled warmly and added, "Secretary Sha Ruijin specifically instructed our county office to deliver this plaque before noon today so the whole family could celebrate together!"

The entire courtyard erupted into thunderous applause!

Chen Mingde held the shining provincial plaque with tears of pride in his eyes, while Liu Guoliang's face burned bright red from his ears to his neck. Remembering how he had just bragged about knowing a tiny municipal section clerk, Liu Guoliang quietly shrank behind the pear tree and slipped away toward the back washroom without daring to make another sound.

Beside Grandmother Zhou Guilan's chair, Qin Yuhan clapped her hands warmly and looked over at her husband, who was calmly cracking a walnut for her by the tea table, her heart overflowing with quiet joy.
