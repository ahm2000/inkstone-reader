# Chapter 128: Midnight Noodles and Article 14

By ten-thirty at night, the lanterns along the lakeside boardwalk of Shanshui Villa reflected like golden pearls across the dark water, and the quiet courtyard of Three Willows Suite was filled with the savory aroma of simmering shrimp broth.

Inside the warm suite, Qin Yuhan had already washed off her evening makeup and changed into a soft pale-pink silk pajama set, with her long dark hair clipped up loosely at the back of her head. She sat at the wide rosewood dining table with a pair of thin reading glasses perched on the bridge of her nose, flipping through the forty-page gold-stamped contract draft that Cai Chenggong and Deputy Mayor Ding Yizhen had given her assistant at the welcome dinner.

In the open kitchenette five steps away, Chen Xuan wore an apron over his cotton t-shirt and hummed a cheerful tune while stirring a small pot of fresh river-shrimp noodles he had asked the resort kitchen to send over as fresh ingredients. He tossed in two poached eggs, a handful of tender green bok choy, and a sprinkle of chopped scallions before ladling the steaming noodles into two blue-and-white porcelain bowls.

"Stop frowning at that folder, President Qin," Chen Xuan called out playfully, carrying the wooden tray over to the rosewood table and setting a steaming bowl of shrimp noodles right in front of her. "If you keep wrinkling your eyebrows like that, your mother Lin Shuyin is going to accuse me of not feeding you well on our trip to Handong Province."

Qin Yuhan took off her reading glasses, inhaled the rich scent of the fresh river-shrimp broth, and looked up at her husband with a bright, affectionate smile.

"Wait until we get back to Shanghai on Sunday," Qin Yuhan teased, picking up her chopsticks and blowing on a spoonful of warm soup. "I am going to tell my mother that the best part of this entire Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum was the Hami melon you used to rescue me from Ding Yizhen and this bowl of midnight noodles."

"How is the soup?" Chen Xuan asked, sitting down next to her with his own bowl before standing back up halfway with his chopsticks in hand to pass her a small dish of pickled bamboo shoots. "Is it too salty?"

"It is perfect, ten times better than the banquet buffet," Qin Yuhan sighed happily, taking a bite of tender noodles and fresh river shrimp. As she chewed, she nudged the open gold-stamped contract folder on the table with her elbow. "Look at this draft from Cai Chenggong and Ding Yizhen. On the first twenty pages, everything looks like a standard inland freight and cold-chain cooperation agreement backed by Shanshui Group and Jingzhou City. They even copied standard templates from China Construction Bank and ICBC to make the 300 million credit guarantee look routine."

"If the front twenty pages look so neat, why were you frowning at page twenty-eight just now?" Chen Xuan asked lazily, leaning one forearm over the back of her wooden chair with his chopsticks resting casually between his fingers as he looked down over her shoulder.

"Because the fee schedule in the middle feels slightly bloated, though nothing illegal jumps out at first glance," Qin Yuhan murmured, her eyes scanning the dense paragraphs on page twenty-eight. "I was trying to figure out why Ding Yizhen and Cai Chenggong were so desperate for me to initial it tonight instead of waiting for tomorrow."

Chen Xuan chewed a poached egg slowly, reached down over her shoulder with the clean wooden end of his chopsticks, and tapped twice on a tiny four-line sub-clause buried at the very bottom of page twenty-eight under Article 14.

"Wife," Chen Xuan said in a casual, everyday voice, as if he were pointing out a typo on a grocery receipt, "why does a Handong shipping contract send all your cargo records and bank clearing logs to an office in Zurich?"

Qin Yuhan froze with her soup spoon halfway to her mouth.

She leaned forward immediately, put her thin reading glasses back on, and read the tiny eight-point font at the bottom of Article 14 word for word.

Under the title of "Secondary Cloud Backup and International Settlement Technical Support," the clause stated that all participating companies, including the lead guarantor Haiyue Group, agreed to mirror their real-time port manifests, customs declarations, and cross-bank clearing certificates to an offshore server managed by Alpine Horizon Data Services in Zurich, Switzerland, which held pre-authorized debit authority for international clearing fees!

Qin Yuhan's breath caught in her throat. Her fingers tightened on the edge of the page as her sharp Fudan-trained financial mind connected every piece in three seconds.

"This is not a freight alliance at all!" Qin Yuhan whispered, her eyes widening in shock. "Alpine Horizon in Zurich! If Haiyue Group signed as the lead corporate guarantor, Cai Chenggong and Ding Yizhen could use that Zurich server clause to siphon the entire 300 million bank credit line straight out of Huaxia Bank and ICBC under the disguise of offshore data settlement fees, while exposing every container manifest moving through Shanghai!"

She turned her head around in her chair and stared up at Chen Xuan in complete amazement.

"Chen Xuan, how did you spot that in two seconds from behind my chair?" Qin Yuhan asked, her heart thumping as she looked at her lazy husband holding his bowl of noodles. "My two senior assistants read through the first thirty pages after the dinner and missed that footnote completely!"

"I look at waybill appendices and fine-print shipping slips every day at Hengxin Logistics, remember?" Chen Xuan grinned without missing a beat, popping a river shrimp into his mouth and tapping her forehead gently with his knuckle. "Whenever Old Hu tries to sneak an extra twenty-yuan fuel surcharge onto a Pudong delivery form, he always hides it in the smallest paragraph at the bottom of page three. Go on, eat your noodles before they get cold, President Qin. Tomorrow morning, you can ask Chairman Cai why his coal trucks in Jingzhou City need to take a detour through Switzerland."

Qin Yuhan burst out laughing, her eyes shining with both pride and wonder at her seemingly ordinary clerk husband as she clinked her porcelain soup spoon against his bowl.
