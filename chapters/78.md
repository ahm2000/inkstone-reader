# Chapter 78: The Breakfast Arrest

Clear morning sunlight poured through the tall arched windows of the Grand Ballroom, catching the steam rising from silver chafing dishes and porcelain teapots.

Four hundred executives, municipal officials, and reporters gathered around the linen-draped tables for the closing forum breakfast. Though everyone murmured politely over congee and dim sum, an unmistakable tension hung in the bright air. The crimson signing banner had been removed overnight, leaving bare mahogany paneling behind the podium.

At Table Forty-Two near the corner pillar, General Manager Sun Haoran looked ten years younger than the afternoon before. After sharing steamed perch with Retired Governor Zhao Dehai and Chairman Qin Zhengming, he had slept soundly all night.

Beside him, Chen Xuan wore his clean washed gray jacket and calmly spooned warm golden millet porridge, taking neat bites of a steamed vegetable bun and crisp pickled radish.

"Chen Xuan, try the sesame pancakes," Sun Haoran suggested cheerfully, pouring fresh green tea. "Once breakfast finishes at ten, we can check out and catch the afternoon high-speed train back to Qingyan City."

"That sounds very good, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan smiled warmly. "I promised Grandpa Chen Fugen and Tang Xiaoyu two boxes of walnut pastries near the station before we board."

* * *

Thirty paces ahead in the VIP front row, Li Weiming had not touched a bite.

A porcelain cup of espresso sat ice-cold beside his trembling left hand. His tailored charcoal suit looked creased, and dark circles shadowed his eyes.

He had waited at the riverside warehouse from twelve thirty until three in the morning. Nobody arrived. No van. No radio check-in. No trace of four veteran armed contractors who had vanished into thin air on the fourteenth floor of a five-star hotel.

Beneath the white tablecloth, Li Weiming's damp thumb redialed his mercenaries' fourth encrypted phone.

A flat automated tone answered immediately. The subscriber you have dialed is unreachable.

Four phones. Four dead lines.

Cold sweat trickled down Li Weiming's spine. Twenty minutes ago he had watched Chen Xuan walk peacefully into the ballroom beside Sun Haoran, completely unharmed, smiling politely at a waiter as he picked up millet porridge.

*How?* Li Weiming's chest tightened painfully. *Not a single alarm went off. Four armed European veterans went through his door at midnight and vanished without leaving a footprint.*

Two tables away, Song Baisheng looked ash-gray and hollowed out, mechanically stirring untouched black coffee while his eyes darted wildly toward every entrance.

At the head table, Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang sat stiff as wood, staring at his folded napkin to avoid the calm, watchful gaze of Retired Governor Zhao Dehai and Qin Zhengming seated two chairs away.

* * *

At eight forty-five, the heavy mahogany doors at all four entrances of the Grand Ballroom swung open in crisp unison.

The clinking of silverware died instantly across four hundred seats.

More than forty dark-suited officers strode briskly into the hall in disciplined columns, sealing every exit behind them. Half wore the silver-trimmed badges of State Security Counterintelligence. The other half wore the dark credentials of the Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission.

Gasps rippled across the stunned executives. Reporters froze with coffee cups halfway to their lips.

Three stone-faced State Security officers marched directly down the center aisle, stopping beside Li Weiming's chair in the VIP front row.

The lead officer unfolded an official arrest warrant stamped in crimson and held it squarely before Li Weiming's eyes.

"Li Weiming, also operating under the alias Marcus Vance," the officer announced clearly through the dead-silent hall. "You are under arrest by order of the Ministry of State Security for espionage, illegal exfiltration of strategic domestic logistics data to Zurich, and directing four armed foreign operatives inside Chinese territory."

Li Weiming's hand jerked violently. His cold espresso cup slipped off the table and shattered on the marble floor.

"I hold a foreign passport!" Li Weiming snapped hoarsely, scrambling back as two officers stepped behind his shoulders. "I demand consular access!"

"Your passport does not cover directing four armed foreign contractors apprehended on the fourteenth floor last night with suppressed firearms," the officer replied coldly. "They are already in State Security custody along with your encrypted payment logs."

Cold steel handcuffs locked around Li Weiming's wrists.

As officers hauled him to his feet, Li Weiming's terrified eyes swung across the silent ballroom to Table Forty-Two.

There, Chen Xuan sat quietly in his washed gray jacket, dabbing the corner of his mouth with a paper napkin. Their eyes met for half a second. Chen Xuan showed no triumph, no mockery, and no anger, only the serene, unreadable calm of deep autumn water.

A violent shudder tore through Li Weiming's shoulders as State Security marched him out the doors.

* * *

Before the stunned hall could breathe, a second team closed around Song Baisheng's table.

"Song Baisheng, Chairman of the Smart-Logistics Consortium," an officer stated cleanly, seizing the locked briefcase beside his chair. "Your Zurich server contracts and offshore kickback accounts are seized under warrant. You are detained for aiding foreign intelligence collection and commercial bribery."

Song Baisheng's pale face crumpled. He tried to rise, stammering incoherently, only for his legs to buckle completely. He collapsed heavily back into his velvet chair, weeping weakly as officers hoisted him upright and led him away.

Then every head in the ballroom turned slowly toward the head table.

Walking up the carpeted steps with quiet authority came Vice Director Gao Zhengyi of the Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission, flanked by two senior inspectors.

Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang sat frozen, his lips trembling as sweat soaked his navy collar. He glanced desperately toward Retired Governor Zhao Dehai, who merely sipped his jasmine tea in stony silence.

Vice Director Gao Zhengyi stopped beside Wu Zhenbang's seat, presenting a formal detention order in full view of four hundred breathless executives and every camera in the hall.

"Wu Zhenbang," Vice Director Gao Zhengyi said evenly, his voice carrying cleanly across the hushed room. "Under approval of the Provincial Standing Committee and the Central Discipline Inspection Commission, you are placed under immediate detention for severe violations of discipline, abuse of office, and collusion with foreign intelligence assets. Please stand and come with us."

Wu Zhenbang's hands shook violently as he pushed his chair back. Ashen-faced and trembling, he rose on unsteady legs and walked down the steps beside Vice Director Gao Zhengyi, his head hanging low before four hundred stunned witnesses.

* * *

The mahogany doors clicked shut behind the last escorted official, and the ballroom erupted into a stunned, breathless roar.

At Table Forty-Two, Sun Haoran stared blankly at his sesame pancake.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran whispered finally. "That was a Deputy Provincial Governor. And a foreign spy ring. All arrested right in front of our breakfast."

Chen Xuan finished his last warm spoonful of golden millet porridge and smiled mildly.

"It looks like the provincial committee fixed Article 14 after all, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said gently. "Our train back to Qingyan City leaves at two o'clock. If we pack now, we will even have time to stop by the old bakery near the station before the walnut pastries sell out."
