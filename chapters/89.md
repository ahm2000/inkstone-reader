# Chapter 89: The Joint National Clean-Up Order

At eight-thirty on Monday night, thick cigarette smoke filled the crisis room in the Great Hall. The ceiling lamps glowed through the gray haze. Around the long wooden table sat the heads and top deputies of more than forty central offices. Leaders from the Military, State Security, Public Security, the Discipline Office, the Supreme Court, the Supreme Prosecutor's Office, the National People's Congress, the Advisory Conference, the Organization Department, the Development Commission, Finance, and Customs were all present.

Every chair was taken. Red-stamped files lay stacked in front of each nameplate. Cups of cold tea sat untouched.

At the lower end of the table, Ruan Defu from Commerce wiped his glasses with a shaking handkerchief. "Premier Gu Chengshan, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, I do not doubt the forty-two cases," he said. "My worry is the economy. Deng Yongchang's business network touches three port companies, two regional banks, and four public shell companies. If Customs and Finance freeze every account before sunrise, southern markets will crash by ten tomorrow morning."

Beside him, Tong Zhaoxing from the Development Commission nodded fast. His face was gray with exhaustion. "Ruan Defu is right. Spread the freezes over ninety days. That gives us time to protect local government debt and avoid panicking foreign banks."

Before Premier Gu Chengshan could answer, General Qin Zhengguo slammed a red folder flat onto the wood. The loud crack echoed like a gunshot. Ruan Defu flinched so hard his glasses clattered beside his teacup.

"Ninety days?" General Qin Zhengguo barked, eyes blazing under his gray brows. "Give them ninety hours and half those overseas shell companies will move their money to foreign accounts we can never touch! Did you read Case Seven, Tong Zhaoxing? Did you see the military port lease deals Deng Yongchang arranged through those companies? That is not a market dip. That is treason!"

"General Qin Zhengguo is right," Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi of the Discipline Office said coldly. He tapped ash into a crowded brass tray. "We checked every bank transfer number with the Supreme Prosecutor's Office. Kuang Shouyi already has forty-seven arrest warrants ready across six provinces. They go out the second this order carries our joint seal. Every wasted hour gives a guilty official time to swallow a pill or board a private plane."

Kuang Shouyi from the Supreme Prosecutor's Office gave a short nod, his jaw set. "And Mu Delin at the Supreme Court has already set up special judges for closed national security trials. Legally, our proof is rock solid."

"Rock solid," Guo Zhengming of Public Security muttered, rubbing his bloodshot eyes. "Except nobody in this room can explain on paper how those forty-two cases landed on our desks."

A heavy, suffocating silence fell over the room. Several ministers dropped their eyes to their folders. Ding Guoping of State Security shifted stiffly in his chair. His cigarette burned down toward his fingers without him noticing.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua leaned forward and set both arms flat on the table. His gaze moved slowly from Ruan Defu to Tong Zhaoxing, then swept across every tense face in the room.

"Let me remind everyone," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said softly, making the whole room lean in. "The evidence and signal check finished this afternoon. The report is sealed at the highest level. Every single paper inside those forty-two cases has been checked and proven against our own bank records, customs logs, and staff files. Deng Yongchang's network is real. The treason is real. The stolen state money is real."

He held Ruan Defu's gaze until the Commerce official looked down at the table.

"If any office tries to weaken a single line tonight to protect an old ally or save tomorrow's stock numbers," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua added, "the Discipline Office and State Security will open a new case on you before you reach the parking garage."

Jin Rongzhi of State Security did not smile. He simply rested one calm hand on a stack of blank red folders beside his elbow.

"Customs already has port holds ready for all twelve coastal ports," a gray-haired Customs deputy spoke up fast, his damp palms pressed flat on his folder. "Give us the signed order and not one shipping container tied to Deng Yongchang leaves our waters tonight."

"And our two legislative committees have cleared the urgent suspension papers for three sitting provincial lawmakers named in Cases Twelve and Thirty-One," the National People's Congress representative added stiffly, avoiding everyone's eyes. "No special legal protection will slow down Guo Zhengming's arrest teams."

Guo Zhengming let out a short, grim breath through his nose. "Good. My officers have been sitting in unmarked vans outside six homes since seven o'clock waiting for the green light."

At the head of the table, Premier Gu Chengshan lit a cigarette with a worn steel lighter. He took a slow drag of smoke. Deep lines marked the corners of his mouth, yet his eyes were hard as winter steel.

"Enough," Premier Gu Chengshan said.

Every low murmur died at once.

"At eight-thirty tonight, the state stops reacting and starts ruling again," Premier Gu Chengshan said. He set the lighter down with a sharp click. "Before the clerk passes around the Joint National Clean-Up and State Asset Recovery Order, every leader in this room will hear three iron rules. No changes. No private twists."

He raised one finger through the smoke.

"First: full legal action. From the lowest street fixer in Yunxi to the highest backer in the capital, every proven name will be charged, tried, and sentenced strictly under the law. No quiet medical retirements. No light warnings behind closed doors. No powerful protectors."

Ding Guoping let out a slow breath and uncapped his fountain pen.

Premier Gu Chengshan raised a second finger.

"Second: full recovery of stolen state money. Ruan Defu, Tong Zhaoxing, and Customs will freeze every linked local and overseas account tonight. Every yuan stolen from public land, military supply deals, and state projects goes back to the national treasury. If the southern stock market drops three points tomorrow, let it drop. A clean foundation beats a painted crack."

Ruan Defu swallowed hard and nodded once. Color slowly returned to his pale face. "Commerce will start the freeze at nine-oh-five, Premier Gu Chengshan."

"And third," Premier Gu Chengshan said, his voice dropping until the room felt airless. "Complete secrecy about Zero Guard."

Nobody moved. Even a clerk's pencil at the back wall froze in midair.

"There will be zero official mention of unlisted numbers, black cases, or outside guards in any court record, note, or file," Premier Gu Chengshan stated, his eyes cutting like a blade down the table. "No intelligence office opens an inquiry. No military office runs a trace. To the courts, the press, and history, this clean-up started with an internal joint check and ended with the power of the state. Any official who breaks this seal faces a military court as a traitor."

He crushed his half-smoked cigarette into the ashtray and pulled the leather signing book forward.

"Sign it," Premier Gu Chengshan ordered.

His pen scratched firmly across the thick paper.

He slid the book to Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, who signed without hesitation and passed it to General Qin Zhengguo. Around the table, more than forty hands put ink to paper. Gao Zhengyi, Jin Rongzhi, Guo Zhengming, Mu Delin, Kuang Shouyi, Ding Guoping, Ruan Defu, Tong Zhaoxing, and the legislative deputies all signed their names in silence.

When the last signature dried, the brass wall clock stood at two minutes to nine.

Nobody cheered. Tired hands gathered up the heavy red folders, and every leader in the room felt the same cold weight at the back of his neck. Outside the stone walls, the silent watchers still stood in the dark.
