# Chapter 89: Short Squeeze

By two thirty-five on Tuesday afternoon, the air inside the fortieth-floor boardroom of Haiyue Tower felt heavy enough to crush lungs.

On the massive wall-mounted trading screen, Haiyue Hospitality and Property Group bled another nine point two percent below Monday's close, clinging just above the daily limit-down floor. In forty-eight hours, nearly a third of the company's market value had vanished.

Across the mahogany conference table sat Shen Kaiyuan in a charcoal Italian suit, flanked by two stone-faced legal aides. A thin silver earpiece sat tucked into his right ear, linking him live to Aegis Meridian's Asia trading desk in Hong Kong.

With theatrical slowness, Shen Kaiyuan slid a thick navy-bound contract and a gold fountain pen across the wood until they stopped directly in front of Qin Yuhan.

"Fifty-one percent controlling equity at fourteen yuan per share," Shen Kaiyuan announced softly, tapping his wristwatch. "It is two forty, Miss Qin. You have ten minutes before the closing bell triggers automatic default clauses across all three frozen credit lines."

Beside Qin Yuhan, Qin Zhengming gripped his armrests, pale from exhaustion and suppressed fury. Behind them stood Zhou Wenhai, Fang Mei, and Jin Ke, staring grimly at the bleeding crimson ticker.

"You drove our share price down with fabricated rumors and illegal dumping," Qin Zhengming said, his voice trembling. "And you call this a restructuring offer?"

"I call it survival, Chairman Qin Zhengming," Shen Kaiyuan replied with a lazy, mocking smile. "Sign before two fifty, and Aegis Meridian settles your banks tonight. Refuse, and at two fifty-five my Hong Kong desk drops the final three-hundred-million-yuan sell block to lock your stock at limit-down. Tomorrow morning, creditors will carve Haiyue Tower into dust."

Qin Yuhan did not even glance at the gold pen. Her dark eyes met Shen Kaiyuan's gaze without a tremor.

"Haiyue Group was built by two generations of honest work in Qingyan City," Qin Yuhan said clearly, her voice cold as glass. "Take your contract and walk out of my boardroom, Shen Kaiyuan. We will not hand a single share to offshore parasites."

Shen Kaiyuan laughed softly, gesturing toward the massive red wall display. "Eight minutes left, Miss Qin. Watch your empire disappear."

At two forty-four, the boardroom fell silent except for the hum of the vents.

Then the Level-2 order book along the bottom of the screen flickered violently.

Zhou Wenhai gasped aloud, lunging toward the display.

"Look at the bid column!" Zhou Wenhai shouted, his voice cracking with disbelief. "Two billion yuan at market price! Another three billion right behind it! Four national-grade institutional custody seats just entered the market simultaneously with twelve billion yuan in cleared cash!"

Before Shen Kaiyuan could turn fully toward the screen, the crimson waterfall snapped upward like a struck whip and exploded into blinding green.

Every sell wall Aegis Meridian had stacked between seventeen yuan and twenty-one yuan vanished in forty seconds, swallowed whole.

"Impossible!" Shen Kaiyuan snapped, his smug composure shattering as he pressed two fingers against his silver earpiece. "Victor Sterling! What is happening on the tape? Who is buying?"

Through the earpiece, audible even across the quiet boardroom table, came Victor Sterling's frantic scream from Hong Kong.

"They are sweeping the entire order book with unleveraged cash!" Victor Sterling yelled over blaring margin alarms. "Four sovereign-backed domestic institutional trusts! They just absorbed every borrowed share we sold since Monday! Cover now! Cover now!"

"How can we cover when there are no sellers left?" Shen Kaiyuan barked, jumping to his feet as his chair skidded backward.

On the massive wall display, the green price line shot vertically skyward.

Three minutes in, Haiyue Group erased its entire nine-percent morning loss. Seven minutes in, it blasted past plus five percent as panicked offshore short-sellers scrambled to buy back shares at any price.

By two fifty-six, twelve minutes after the first institutional bid hit the tape, Haiyue Hospitality and Property Group slammed solidly into the daily ten-percent limit-up ceiling, locked tight behind a three-billion-yuan wall of unfilled buy orders.

Aegis Meridian's Asia desk had been pulverized live on screen.

"Shen Kaiyuan! We are wiped out!" Victor Sterling shrieked desperately through the earpiece. "The Central Financial Commission just froze all forty-two of our mainland clearing accounts and collateral pools! Our prime brokers in Singapore and Zurich triggered four point six billion dollars in forced liquidation! The Asia desk is dead! Run!"

The blood drained completely from Shen Kaiyuan's face. His gold fountain pen rolled off the table and clattered onto the floor.

"Pack the files!" Shen Kaiyuan hissed hoarsely at his stunned aides, grabbing his briefcase with shaking hands. "We are leaving for the airport now!"

He spun and sprinted three steps toward the tall glass double doors.

Before his fingers could touch the handle, the doors swung wide open from outside.

Standing in the threshold were four stern officers in dark Central Financial Commission and Qingyan Financial Crimes Bureau jackets, led by Inspector Lu Wenbin. Behind them in the elevator lobby, two more officers secured the exits.

Inspector Lu Wenbin stepped squarely into Shen Kaiyuan's path and raised a gold-embossed badge alongside a stamped red detention warrant.

"Shen Kaiyuan, Asia-Pacific Chief Representative of Aegis Meridian Capital," Inspector Lu Wenbin announced crisply. "Under joint order of the Central Financial Commission and the Qingyan Financial Crimes Bureau, you are under immediate detention for illegal cross-border naked shorting, coordinated market manipulation, and fabricating financial rumors. Drop your briefcase."

"You cannot detain me!" Shen Kaiyuan stammered wildly, stumbling backward as his arrogance dissolved into terror. "I hold European corporate credentials! Call the Zurich consulate! Call Henrik Lindqvist!"

"Your Zurich directors can send lawyers after we finish auditing your frozen servers," Inspector Lu Wenbin replied coldly, nodding to his officers.

Within ten seconds, steel handcuffs clicked shut around Shen Kaiyuan's wrists. Pale and sweating, the man who had offered Qin Yuhan ten minutes to surrender was marched quietly out of the boardroom past stunned Haiyue executives.

For three long seconds after the doors closed, nobody spoke.

Then Jin Ke's phone erupted with overlapping calls. He answered the first on speaker, his hands trembling with exhilaration.

"Miss Qin! Chairman Qin Zhengming!" Jin Ke cried out, eyes shining. "It is Donghai Development Bank, Huadong Commercial Bank, and Jincheng Trust! All three presidents are calling personally to apologize, lift the credit freezes immediately, and offer two billion yuan in extra credit lines!"

Qin Zhengming exhaled a long, shaky breath, sinking back into his leather chair as color finally returned to his cheeks. Around the table, Zhou Wenhai and Fang Mei burst into stunned, tearful applause.

Only Qin Yuhan did not cheer.

Quietly, she walked past the celebrating executives toward the tall floor-to-ceiling window overlooking the sunlit avenues of Qingyan City.

In the clear glass, she could see the glowing green reflection of Haiyue Group's locked limit-up board behind her. Far below on the ordinary streets of the city, afternoon buses rolled calmly past everyday office buildings.

Slowly, Qin Yuhan lifted her smartphone and unlocked the screen.

Still open from Monday evening was the mild, unhurried text message from an ordinary freight clerk at Hengxin Logistics.

*No problem at all, Miss Qin. Remember to eat on time. I hope your busy week clears up soon.*

Qin Yuhan stared quietly at those gentle words, her fingers tightening softly around her phone as an amazed, quiet warmth rose inside her chest.
