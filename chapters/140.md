# Chapter 140: Short Squeeze

Fluorescent lights burned bright across Aegis Meridian's trading floor as 2:30 AM approached in Zurich (+1:00 GMT).

Six thousand miles east, it was 9:30 AM on Tuesday morning in Shanghai (+8:00 GMT), and the market was seconds away from opening.

Julian Ash stood behind his lead trader Eric, holding a paper cup of black coffee with an easy, confident smirk.

"Look at those timid retail buyers," Julian said, glancing at the pre-market screen. "Even with Shanghai Customs posting a rushed clearance notice at 8:00 AM, nobody dares catch a falling knife. Hit the button right on the bell, Eric. Drop the entire 4 billion yuan in short orders at once."

"Armed and ready," Eric replied briskly, his fingers poised above the mechanical keyboard.

The digital clock on the wall ticked to 9:30 AM Shanghai time.

"Fire," Julian ordered.

Eric slammed the enter key. Instantly, a massive tidal wave of borrowed Haiyue Group shares crashed onto the Shanghai exchange.

Inside Haiyue Tower's 38th-floor boardroom, Chief Financial Officer Lin Shufen gripped the mahogany table edge until her knuckles turned white.

"That is it! We are finished!" Director Xu Minghui groaned, slumping back in his chair as the opening print plunged straight down. "They dumped 4 billion yuan in one second! Even the customs notice could not save us!"

At the head of the table, Qin Yuhan kept her back straight and her dark eyes locked on the screen, refusing to look away even as her heart hammered against her ribs.

One second passed. Two seconds.

On the third second, the trading screen froze for a heartbeat and then erupted.

Twelve towering buy orders from domestic institutional seats slammed into the order book simultaneously. Like a giant whale swallowing a handful of minnows, the entire 4 billion yuan sell wall vanished in less than twelve seconds.

In Zurich, Eric's coffee cup slipped from his elbow and spilled across the desk.

"Julian! They ate the whole block!" Eric shouted, his voice cracking. "A full 4 billion yuan gone in twelve seconds!"

Julian Ash's smirk vanished instantly. He leaned down over Eric's shoulder, staring at the steep upward surge tearing across the chart. "Check the feed! Is that an exchange glitch?"

"No glitch! Twelve separate domestic accounts just cleared the entire ask side!" Eric yelled, his fingers flying frantically across the keys. "The price just crossed yesterday's close! It is up 4%! Now 7%!"

"Throw our reserve margin at it!" Julian barked, sweat breaking out along his hairline. "Push it back down before the retail crowd piles in!"

Eric dumped their last 1 billion yuan in emergency short capacity. The massive domestic bids swallowed it in four seconds flat without even slowing down.

At 9:32 AM Shanghai time, Haiyue Group's stock price smashed straight into the upper 10% daily limit, erasing Monday's entire drop in two minutes flat.

Over 6 billion yuan in unfilled buy orders stacked up like an iron fortress at the top price. Not a single share was left for sale.

Red warning banners began flashing across every monitor in Zurich as prime broker margin calls screamed through the speakers.

"We are trapped!" Eric gasped, clutching his head. "We are short 13 billion yuan with zero shares available to buy back! Our paper loss just passed 1.3 billion yuan, and with our leverage the prime brokers are forcing automatic liquidation!"

Julian Ash's phone buzzed furiously on the desk. Henrik Lindholm's name flashed on the screen. Julian stared at the burning red numbers, his face ashen, knowing Aegis Meridian's leveraged position had just been wiped out in two minutes flat.

Across the ocean in Haiyue Tower's boardroom, stunned silence held for three seconds before exploding into wild cheers and applause.

"We hit the upper limit!" Lin Wei cried out, tears of relief shining in her eyes. "We erased the whole drop!"

Lin Shufen's desk phone rang, then her mobile phone lit up right beside it. She answered with a beaming smile. "President Qin, that is Shanghai Commercial Bank and Coastal Trust! They are apologizing for yesterday's misunderstanding and offering to expand our credit lines by another 2 billion yuan at lower rates!"

Director Xu Minghui sat frozen in his chair, his face bright red, unable to meet anyone's eyes.

Qin Yuhan exhaled a long, quiet breath, a radiant smile finally breaking across her face. She looked up at the soaring chart and wondered softly which powerful, quiet domestic backers had seen Haiyue's true worth and crushed the foreign raiders so decisively.

That evening, crisp November wind rustled the golden leaves outside Emerald Riverside Villa in Pudong. Inside, the dining room glowed with warm yellow light and the comforting aroma of steamed sea bass, stir-fried greens, and hot clam and tofu soup.

The front door clicked open, and Qin Yuhan practically floated into the kitchen, kicking off her heels and wrapping her arms warmly around Chen Xuan's waist from behind.

"We won, Chen Xuan!" she laughed brightly, resting her cheek against his shoulder. "Haiyue crushed the foreign short sellers in two minutes and locked straight at the upper limit! All four banks even called back to apologize!"

Chen Xuan turned off the stove flame, smiling warmly as he turned around in his faded cotton apron. "I saw the afternoon headline. I told you real ships and hard work always win in the end."

"It was incredible," Qin Yuhan said, her eyes sparkling as she helped carry the bowls to the dining table. "Twelve domestic institutional seats stepped in together right at 9:30 AM, swept over 10 billion yuan onto the board, and wiped those Zurich raiders clean off the screen. Nobody in Shanghai even knows who is behind those accounts. I wish I could thank whoever gave that order."

Chen Xuan ladled a steaming bowl of hot clam and tofu soup and placed it gently in front of her, his eyes soft and amused.

"Maybe whoever gave that order just has good eyes," Chen Xuan said casually, sitting down across from her and picking up his chopsticks. "And maybe they think my wife runs the best company in Shanghai. Eat up before the soup gets cold."

Qin Yuhan laughed joyfully, clinking her porcelain spoon against his bowl, never guessing that the relaxed husband smiling across the table was the very hand that had broken Zurich before breakfast.
