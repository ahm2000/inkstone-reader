# Chapter 12: Beyond Number One

General Qin Zhengguo stared at the ten digits scribbled on his notepad for two seconds, then lifted the isolated red receiver and dialed.

One ring. Two rings. A flat, electronically scrubbed voice answered without a greeting.

"State your clearance and purpose. This line does not exist."

"This is General Qin Zhengguo, Supreme Military Command," the four-star commander said firmly, keeping his voice tight. "I was given this line by a founding elder in the western hills. An Absolute Order has locked down Lanshui Province, and mute, masked operatives are standing inside my own command center. Are these your people? Connect me to whoever is in charge right now!"

"Hold," the cold voice snapped. "Voiceprint verification in progress. Do not speak until cleared."

Ten seconds of dead static ticked by while ten masked Zero Guard operatives watched General Qin Zhengguo in absolute silence from across the deck. His chief of staff stood five paces back, clutching a clipboard so tightly his knuckles were white.

The line clicked twice, transferring through a blind relay node. A harsher, sharper voice barked through the speaker.

"General Qin Zhengguo! Who the hell gave you an unlisted Black Pine relay?!" It was a field commander high up the Black Pine Guard chain, his tone dripping with cold warning and raw tension. "If you hadn't cleared a Tier-One voiceprint, this line would have burned out your entire console! State what you want and make it fast!"

General Qin Zhengguo felt heat rise in his chest. Nobody in the country had barked at a serving four-star general like that in twenty years, yet he swallowed his pride and snapped back urgently. "Tell me straight! Is the Black Pine Guard responsible for the hundred-kilometer lockdown over Yunxi County?! Did your people walk into the capital's red-walled compound and onto my deck?!"

A bitter, sharp laugh cracked through the speaker.

"Us?!" the Black Pine Guard commander barked back, his own voice tight with lingering shock and humiliation. "General, do you think we are crazy enough to lock down the capital?! We didn't do this! We were ordered outside the ten-kilometer line ourselves! That force inside Yunxi is NOT under the Black Pine Guard! They are a level far above every single one of us, and we don't even have clearance to look at them!"

General Qin Zhengguo's breath caught. "Not under the Black Pine Guard?! Then who the hell commands them?!"

"Nobody on my roster!" the voice snapped back. "Wait on the line. I am routing your verification up to the only desk that even knows what lit up tonight. If he refuses your call, you're on your own!"

Three more blind verification clicks echoed down the encrypted circuit, passing node by node through five layers of isolation until the static vanished completely.

The silence on the other end felt older, heavier, and far colder.

"Number One," a calm, weathered voice said from a quiet eastern courtyard in Qingyan City.

General Qin Zhengguo drew a deep breath and steadied his voice, speaking with urgent, careful respect. "Number One. This is General Qin Zhengguo at Supreme Military Command. Forgive the intrusion, but I had no choice. Ten masked operatives are standing mute on my own deck right now. Every high official in the capital, the provincial command centers, and the cities is under a five-to-ten-man silent watch. Every military district in the country is at full combat readiness, fighter wings are holding the hundred-kilometer shell over Lanshui, and foreign intelligence rooms in Washington, Moscow, and London are already reacting to the blackout."

He gripped the receiver until his palm hurt. "Your subordinate just told me the Black Pine Guard did not launch this. Tell me the truth, Number One. What happened in Yunxi County, who are these men, and is this nation going to war?"

In the quiet eastern courtyard, Number One sat beside his cracked porcelain saucer while five fully masked, completely mute Zero Guard operatives stood motionless along the walls of his study, monitoring even this single verified line.

"First, take a breath, General," Number One replied evenly. "This nation is not going to war. Hold every theater command in place, keep your air wings at the outer boundary, and make sure not a single military or police unit crosses into Yunxi County."

"Then why are they standing inside the capital and on my command deck?!" General Qin Zhengguo pressed urgently. "Why is every anti-missile battery and jammer hot over a small county town?! Can you order them to scale back before foreign powers misread this?!"

Number One let out a slow, heavy breath that sounded almost weary.

"General, listen to me very carefully," Number One said quietly, glancing once at the five silent black masks ringing his own study. "The operatives standing on your deck are the Zero Guard. Even I do not hold an activation key for them. In fact, the entire Black Pine Guard has been dismissed from active command, stripped of all authority, and placed under Zero Guard watch just like everyone else. Five of them are standing mute in my own courtyard as I speak to you. I cannot move a single man, I cannot issue a single order, and I have no permission to touch anything while they are active."

General Qin Zhengguo froze, his palm turning ice-cold on the receiver. "Even you, head of the Black Pine Guard, cannot command them?"

"I do not know their exact numbers," Number One admitted plainly. "I do not know every weapon system they have armed inside that zone, and I do not know how long they will maintain the silent watch across the capital and the provinces. When the Zero Guard activates, the entire country is simply expected to be ready for any level of combat until the situation is resolved."

"And what caused it?" General Qin Zhengguo whispered, his throat dry. "What in Yunxi County could possibly make him activate the Zero Guard?"

"Some foolish, blind people in a county town crossed a line they should never have touched," Number One said, his tone dropping to winter iron. "All I can tell you, General, is that I hope this ends quietly and quickly. Because while the Zero Guard is active, nobody on this earth can tell them to stand down except him."

Heavy silence settled over the encrypted line.

General Qin Zhengguo slowly lowered the receiver onto its cradle. Behind him, the ten mute, black-masked figures had not moved a single millimeter.

"General?" his chief of staff whispered, trembling. "What are your orders?"

"Hold every theater at full readiness," General Qin Zhengguo said hoarsely, staring at the tiny crimson dot named Yunxi County on the massive war map. "Keep every outside unit clear of Lanshui. Nobody speaks, nobody moves, and we wait."

* * *

Across the red-walled compound in the national capital, inside provincial command centers across multiple provinces, and inside Qingyan City Hall, the highest leaders of the nation sat frozen over untouched cups of tea under the mute watch of five to ten masked ghosts, realizing that every lever of power in the country meant nothing tonight.

Meanwhile, in Room 601 of Building 2, Chen Xuan turned off the bathroom tap, dried his hands on a clean towel, and walked back to his bed to sleep.
