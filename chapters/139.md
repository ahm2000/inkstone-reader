# Chapter 139: The Trustee's Signature

On the 46th floor of an old stone-and-glass tower in Shanghai's Lujiazui financial district, there was no company signboard beside the elevator doors.

Only a plain brass plate sat quietly on the walnut wall. Inside the spacious study, warm lamplight fell across tall wooden bookshelves and a wide desk where a silver-haired man in a dark wool sweater sat reading a paper ledger.

Gu Shenzhi was sixty-four years old. Most months, he drank aged oolong tea, reviewed quarterly asset reports, and watched loud billionaires argue on financial television without any of them ever knowing his name or the size of the vault he guarded.

At 3:05 PM on Monday afternoon, the plain desk telephone on the corner of his desk rang twice.

That private copper line had not rung once in nearly four months.

Gu Shenzhi set down his fountain pen, straightened his back, and lifted the receiver.

"Gu Shenzhi speaking," he said calmly.

"Good afternoon, Master Gu," Number 1's steady voice came through the line. "Verification code Seven-Nine-Autumn-Pine. I am calling with a direct order from the Young Master."

Gu Shenzhi's posture grew even straighter, his expression instantly focused and respectful. "Code confirmed. Please give me the Young Master's instructions."

"Foreign short sellers from Zurich's Aegis Meridian dumped 8 billion yuan of Haiyue Group shares today using forged customs rumors," Number 1 explained concisely. "They plan to dump another 4 billion yuan right at the 9:30 AM opening bell tomorrow to break the company and force a bank run."

Gu Shenzhi glanced briefly toward the tall window, where late autumn mist softened the glass towers of Lujiazui. "Bold of them to try that in Shanghai."

"The Young Master's order is simple," Number 1 continued. "Authorize 20 billion yuan from the mainland sovereign trust reserve. Route it through twelve clean domestic institutional seats. Tomorrow morning at 9:30 AM, absorb every single share they dump, clear the sell wall completely, and drive the stock straight to the upper limit. Lock the shares tight so Aegis Meridian cannot buy back a single lot cheaply."

A faint, dignified smile touched the corners of Gu Shenzhi's mouth. He did not ask why the Young Master cared about Haiyue Group, nor did he need to know. He had managed the sovereign trust for thirty years, and he knew one rule above all others: when the Young Master moved, the execution had to be clean, silent, and absolute.

"Tell the Young Master that 20 billion yuan will be staged and verified before sunset," Gu Shenzhi replied smoothly. "By 9:31 AM tomorrow, Zurich will regret borrowing a single share."

"Thank you, Master Gu," Number 1 said, and the line clicked gently dead.

Gu Shenzhi placed the receiver back on its cradle and pressed a small brass intercom button beneath his desk lamp.

"Shen Wei, please step inside."

Ten seconds later, the walnut door opened and a calm, sharp-eyed man in his mid-forties entered. Shen Wei had served as Gu Shenzhi's chief execution director for twelve years and had handled multi-billion-yuan treasury shifts without ever making a headline.

Gu Shenzhi stood up, walked to a heavy mahogany cabinet behind his desk, and unlocked the inner drawer with a small iron key. He brought out a thick cream-colored authorization sheet and a plain red-ink seal.

Sitting back down, he filled in the date, wrote the figure 20 billion yuan in neat strokes with his fountain pen, signed his name at the bottom, and pressed the red seal firmly into the paper.

He slid the sheet across the desk to Shen Wei.

Shen Wei picked up the authorization form. His eyes widened for just a fraction of a second as he read the figure and the target ticker.

"Haiyue Group," Shen Wei said quietly. "They hit the 10% floor before lunch on that smuggling rumor. A full 20 billion yuan is more than enough to buy half the floating shares on the entire exchange."

"Then buy them," Gu Shenzhi said, pouring himself a fresh cup of hot oolong tea. "The rumor is garbage, and customs will clear it before 8:00 AM tomorrow. The real target is the foreign fund sitting in Zurich with 12 billion yuan in naked and borrowed short positions."

Shen Wei's eyes sharpened as he understood the trap. "Are we holding the floor to protect Haiyue's banks?"

Gu Shenzhi shook his head gently. "Defense only invites a second attack. Tomorrow morning, do not just catch what they drop. Split the 20 billion yuan evenly across our twelve quietest domestic institutional seats. Wait two seconds after the 9:30 AM opening bell so they dump their full 4 billion yuan block into the open market. The third second, sweep every share off the board and slam the bid straight to the upper 10% limit."

"If we lock it at the upper limit with billions waiting on the buy side, they will not be able to cover," Shen Wei murmured, a rare glint of excitement crossing his composed face. "Their margin losses will double in an hour."

"Let them learn how heavy Shanghai's water can be," Gu Shenzhi said calmly. "Keep our accounts strictly compliant, keep our names completely off the news wires, and call in all twelve lead traders for an evening briefing."

"Right away, Master Gu," Shen Wei replied, bowing slightly before turning swiftly toward the private execution room down the hall.

By the time late autumn dusk settled over the Huangpu River and the office lights of Lujiazui flickered on one by one, twelve quiet institutional accounts across Shanghai sat armed with 20 billion yuan in cleared cash, waiting silently for Tuesday morning.
