# Chapter 75: Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang's Pressure

The lunch recess felt like the quiet eye of a typhoon.

Across the banquet hall, municipal officials and port executives stood with untouched plates, speaking urgently while scrolling through screenshots of Article 14. Song Baisheng's consortium table stood half-empty beneath the windows.

Seeking fresh air away from the stares, Sun Haoran and Chen Xuan stepped through the west double doors into a quiet marble side corridor lined with potted evergreens and walnut paneling.

Sun Haoran leaned briefly against a pillar, loosening his necktie with trembling fingers.

"Chen Xuan, I know you were right," Sun Haoran said hoarsely. "Reading that clause about mineral schedules going to Switzerland made my own skin crawl. Still, that was Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang. A man at his rank can crush Hengxin Logistics with a single phone call."

"Take a slow breath, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said calmly, handing him a paper cup of warm water. "When someone hides a bad clause inside a sixty-page contract, they are far more afraid of sunlight than we are."

Before Sun Haoran could drink, brisk leather footsteps echoed down the marble hallway.

Two stiff secretaries stepped out from the stairwell alcove to block the far end of the corridor. Behind them strode Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang, his public smile completely gone.

His face dark with rage, Wu Zhenbang stopped two paces away, crowding Sun Haoran and Chen Xuan against the walnut wall.

"General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics," Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang hissed coldly. "You have a remarkably bold clerk under your roof."

Sun Haoran straightened, his knuckles pale around the cup. "Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang, Chen Xuan was only asking a technical clarification from the printed booklet."

"Do not play games with me, Sun Haoran!" Wu Zhenbang snapped, his silver-rimmed glasses glinting coldly. "Your clerk just humiliated the provincial committee before four hundred executives and six television stations! Because of his reckless grandstanding, the Smart-Logistics Consolidation Plan has been delayed."

Wu Zhenbang stepped closer, boring into Sun Haoran's terrified eyes.

"I will give you one chance to save Hengxin Logistics," Wu Zhenbang said icily. "Before one thirty, you will fire Chen Xuan immediately. You will sign a written apology drafted by my secretary stating that your clerk misread an outdated draft out of incompetence. Then both of you will leave Lanshui City on the next train."

Sun Haoran's throat constricted painfully. "Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang, firing an innocent employee and signing a false statement, I cannot do that."

"Cannot?" Wu Zhenbang sneered ruthlessly. "If that signed apology is not on my desk by one thirty, I will call the Provincial Transport Bureau, the Qingyan City Tax Administration, and your fire inspectors before five o'clock. By tomorrow morning, Hengxin Logistics will have its operating license revoked. Your fifty drivers will be unemployed by Friday. Understand?"

The crushing weight of provincial authority pressed down on the corridor. Sun Haoran's knees trembled as he pictured his trucks chained in the yard.

Beside him, Chen Xuan remained completely serene. He stepped half a pace forward, steadying Sun Haoran's elbow gently.

"Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang," Chen Xuan said mildly, his voice polite and even. "Everything I read aloud came word for word from pages forty-two and fifty-eight of your committee's printed annex. If a provincial official revokes a private firm's license because a clerk read a printed contract aloud, the Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission might find that very interesting."

Wu Zhenbang flushed purple with fury. "You arrogant little clerk! How dare you mention Discipline Inspection to me! Security! Escort this troublemaker out of the hotel!"

* * *

Before either secretary could reach his earpiece, unhurried footsteps rounded the far corner of the marble corridor.

A warm, weathered voice carried easily through the tense air.

"Now why is somebody shouting for security in such a peaceful hallway?"

Everyone turned.

Walking calmly down the polished floor came an elderly gentleman in a charcoal Zhongshan tunic, his silver hair neatly combed and his hands clasped behind his back. Beside him walked Qin Zhengming, Chairman of Haiyue Group.

Deputy Provincial Governor Wu Zhenbang froze as if struck by lightning. Every drop of color vanished from his face.

Retired Provincial Governor Zhao Dehai.

Even three years after retiring, Zhao Dehai remained the elder statesman of Lanshui Province. Half the standing committee had risen under his mentorship, and his quiet word still carried decisive weight in both Lanshui City and Beijing.

Wu Zhenbang scrambled into a rigid, flattering bow. "Old Governor Zhao Dehai! Chairman Qin Zhengming! What an unexpected honor to see you here!"

Zhao Dehai did not even look at Wu Zhenbang. His weathered face crinkled into a bright, genuine smile as his eyes landed on the young man in the washed gray jacket.

"Well now, look who is here," Zhao Dehai called out warmly, walking straight past Wu Zhenbang to clasp Chen Xuan's forearm. "Chen Xuan! Qin Zhengming told me you came up from Qingyan City, and I hoped I would catch you before lunch ended."

Sun Haoran's jaw dropped open in stunned disbelief.

Chen Xuan bowed slightly with warm ease. "Good afternoon, Grandpa Zhao Dehai. Good afternoon, Chairman Qin Zhengming. I hope your knee feels better in this autumn weather."

"Much better since I started walking after dinner like you suggested," Zhao Dehai chuckled heartily. "Tell me the truth, young man. Did you bring any more of Grandma Zhou Guilan's spiced pickled eggs from Qingyan City? My wife has talked about that last jar for two months."

Chen Xuan smiled gently. "Grandma Zhou Guilan packed a small ceramic jar wrapped in newspaper before I boarded the train. It is upstairs in my travel bag. I will bring it over before the afternoon session."

"Wonderful! That makes this stuffy conference completely worthwhile," Zhao Dehai laughed.

* * *

Then the laughter faded from Zhao Dehai's eyes.

He turned slowly, his posture relaxed yet carrying the crushing gravity of thirty years at the top of the province, and fixed his calm gaze on Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang.

"By the way, Wu Zhenbang," Zhao Dehai asked mildly. "Why is a sitting Deputy Provincial Governor intimidating an ordinary logistics clerk and his general manager in a side hallway during a forum recess?"

Cold sweat sprang instantly across Wu Zhenbang's temples.

"Old Governor Zhao Dehai, it is a misunderstanding," Wu Zhenbang stammered, forcing a terrified smile. "I was merely discussing industry feedback with General Manager Sun Haoran."

"Industry feedback," Zhao Dehai repeated gently. "From twenty paces away, it sounded a great deal like threatening to revoke a private company's operating license by five o'clock."

Qin Zhengming stepped beside Zhao Dehai, his expression razor-sharp. "I heard Chen Xuan's two questions in the ballroom, Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang. Haiyue Group's legal team is suddenly very interested in Article 14 and those Zurich mirror servers. Hengxin Logistics just saved this province from signing a national security disaster."

Wu Zhenbang turned paper-white. His legs felt hollow inside his trousers.

"I completely agree with Chairman Qin Zhengming," Wu Zhenbang choked out, wiping his damp upper lip. "The technical committee will review Article 14 immediately. Please excuse me, Old Governor Zhao Dehai. I must oversee the revision right away!"

Without daring to glance at Chen Xuan again, Wu Zhenbang bowed stiffly twice, spun around, and practically fled down the corridor in sheer panic, his secretaries hurrying at his heels.

Sun Haoran stared at the empty hallway, his heart pounding. "Old Governor Zhao Dehai, thank you so much."

"Thank your clerk, General Manager Sun Haoran," Zhao Dehai smiled warmly, gesturing toward the dining hall. "Now come along, both of you. Qin Zhengming reserved a quiet table with steamed perch, and Chen Xuan owes me Grandma Zhou Guilan's recipe for those eggs."
