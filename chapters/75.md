# Chapter 75: Old Street's Red Paint Washed Away

By late morning, Old Street no longer felt like a quiet lane waiting for bulldozers. It sounded like a dam had broken.

First came the buzz of mobile phones reconnecting after the overnight signal outage. Then shouting rolled down from the breakfast stall at the east archway. Within minutes, metal shutters rattled open up and down the block, and neighbors hurried out onto the narrow pavement in slippers and aprons.

"Hongrui Real Estate is finished!" a bicycle repairman yelled as he pedaled past Mingde Hardware, waving his phone above the handlebars. "Provincial anti-corruption vans just sealed Hongrui Plaza! Shanshui Group head Zhao Ruilong and Hongrui's whole board were marched out in handcuffs!"

"What did you say?" old tailor Yu Changshun called out. He pushed through the beaded curtain of his shop across the lane with a yellow measuring tape still draped around his neck. "Are you sure? Yesterday their foreman swore Old Street would be flattened by Friday!"

"Look at the video yourself!" the repairman shouted, braking beside the noodle stall. A dozen shopkeepers crowded around his cracked screen, gasping as they saw blue and white seals slapped across the glass doors of Hongrui Plaza.

Across the lane, Chen Xuan stood quietly beside Aunt Wu Lanying near the curb. He held his old green thermos under one arm and wore a plain gray cotton shirt. While everyone around them buzzed with disbelief, his face stayed calm and unbothered, as if he were merely watching rain clear after a summer storm.

Wu Lanying gripped the hem of her cardigan with both hands. Her eyes were red from a sleepless night, and her gaze kept darting back to the rolled metal shutter of Mingde Hardware. Yesterday afternoon, Hongrui's thugs had splashed a huge red demolition character across the center of the steel slats, leaving thick crimson drips down to the concrete step.

"Xuan, did you hear them?" Wu Lanying whispered, her voice trembling. "Hongrui is sealed. Does that mean your Second Uncle will really come home?"

"He will, Second Aunt," Chen Xuan said gently. "The storm has already passed."

Before Wu Lanying could ask another question, the low rumble of engines echoed from the west end of Old Street. The crowd went stiff at once. Several older shopkeepers stepped back toward their doorways, afraid that Hongrui's demolition crews had come for one last round of trouble.

Instead of rusted dump trucks, two clean blue municipal service trucks rolled slowly down the lane and parked neatly along the curb. A sedan from the Yunxi County government followed behind them. The doors opened, and a middle-aged man in a crisp white shirt hurried out, clutching a clipboard against his chest. He was Director Zhou from the county urban management office, a man who had never once set foot on Old Street without a scowl.

Today, his forehead shone with sweat, and his expression was all apology.

"Neighbors, please do not be alarmed!" Director Zhou called out, raising both hands so everyone could see he held nothing but paper. "On direct orders from Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang and the provincial inspection team, the forced redevelopment permit for Old Street has been revoked immediately! No shop here will be demolished. Every single property right on this street is protected by law!"

For two heartbeats, Old Street went completely silent. Then the noodle shop auntie clapped her hands together and let out a loud laugh. Old tailor Yu Changshun wiped his eyes with the back of his sleeve, and cheers broke out from one end of the lane to the other.

Director Zhou did not linger in the applause. He waved sharply at the municipal workers climbing out of the blue trucks. "Unload the pressure washers and solvent! Start right here at Mingde Hardware! I want every drop of that illegal red paint scrubbed clean before noon, and touch up any scratched shutters with fresh gray primer!"

Six workers in orange vests moved quickly, uncoiling thick black hoses and carrying heavy plastic buckets to the front of Mingde Hardware. One worker brushed clear solvent across the ugly red character. Another aimed a pressure wand and squeezed the trigger.

A steady hiss filled the lane. Bright red streaks dissolved into pale pink foam, sliding down the grooves of the metal shutter and washing straight into the roadside drain. Within minutes, the harsh warning mark that had hung over the shop like a curse faded away, leaving clean, wet steel shining in the morning sunlight.

Wu Lanying watched the pink water disappear down the grate. All the tension that had knotted her shoulders since last night seemed to melt away at once. She let out a long, shaky breath and pressed a hand over her mouth.

"Look at that, Xuan," she murmured, tears welling up again, though this time they were warm. "It is gone. Not a stain left."

"It never belonged on Second Uncle's door," Chen Xuan replied softly.

Director Zhou hurried over, bowing slightly to Wu Lanying without daring to meet Chen Xuan's eyes for more than a second. "Madam Wu, on behalf of the county office, I apologize for the distress your family suffered. We will also repair the dented hinge on your shutter free of charge."

"Thank you, Director," Wu Lanying said, nodding quickly, still unused to such courtesy from a county official.

Old tailor Yu Changshun shuffled over once Director Zhou moved down the street to inspect the next shop. "What a miracle!" Yu Changshun marveled, shaking his head. "First Zhao Ruilong's Shanshui Group gets raided, then Hongrui collapses overnight, and now Jingzhou City sends orders straight to our tiny lane. Someone high up must have taken pity on honest folks! What do you think, Chen Xuan?"

Chen Xuan took a quiet sip from his green thermos and offered a mild smile. "Good people get lucky sometimes, Uncle Yu. Crooks always trip over their own feet in the end."

Yu Changshun laughed heartily and went back to watch the workers rinse the next shutter.

Chen Xuan glanced toward the sunlight climbing over the tiled roofs. "Second Aunt, the crew can finish here on their own. Let us head back to Fuxing New Village. Second Uncle will be arriving soon."

Wu Lanying nodded eagerly, taking one last look at the clean metal shutter of Mingde Hardware before walking beside her nephew out of Old Street, her steps lighter than they had been in months.
