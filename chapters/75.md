# Chapter 75: Old Street's Red Paint Washed Away

By five minutes to nine, Old Street no longer sounded like a quiet lane waiting for bulldozers. It sounded like a dam had broken.

First came the frantic buzz of phones springing back to life after the overnight signal outage. Then came shouting from the breakfast stall at the east archway, rolling door to door until shutters rattled open and neighbors spilled onto the pavement in slippers and aprons.

"Hongrui Real Estate is finished!" a bicycle repairman yelled as he pedaled past Mingde Hardware, waving his phone above his handlebars. "Chairman Deng Yongchang was just marched out of Hongrui Plaza in handcuffs! Live video! Provincial economic-crime vans sealed the whole tower!"

"What did you say?" old tailor Yu Changshun called out, bursting through the beaded curtain of his shop across the lane with a yellow measuring tape still looped around his neck. His hands shook as he gripped his younger neighbor Wang Lei's arm. "Wang Lei, show me your phone! Is that really Deng Yongchang?"

Wang Lei held the screen under Yu Changshun's nose, grinning wide. "Look right there, Uncle Yu Changshun! Gray suit, cuffed behind his back, yelling for lawyers while provincial officers wheel servers out of his basement! And my cousin inside the county compound just texted: County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Chief Liang Guodong were both cuffed right in the eight-thirty roll call!"

A sharp gasp rippled through the shopkeepers crowding around Wang Lei's phone.

"What about Yan Tiehu?" the soybean-milk vendor asked, fingers clutching her apron. "Last night Yan Tiehu swore he would bring excavators down Old Street at nine sharp!"

"Gone!" Wang Lei laughed loudly, slapping his knee. "Provincial counter-terrorism officers raided Yan Tiehu's riverfront yard before sunrise! Arrested Yan Tiehu and all twenty of his thugs on organized-crime, smuggling, and counter-terrorism charges! They found illegal blasting caps in his warehouse! And Station Director Cheng Biao and Deputy Director Zhou Quan were hauled out of Old Street Station in handcuffs ten minutes ago!"

Yu Changshun stared at the phone screen for two stunned seconds. Then the seventy-year-old tailor threw his head back and burst into loud, shaking laughter, hot tears spilling down the deep creases beside his eyes.

"Heaven finally opened its eyes!" Yu Changshun laughed, wiping his wet cheeks with his sleeve while his voice cracked with joy. "Twelve years Chairman Deng Yongchang squeezed this county dry! Yesterday Yan Tiehu stood outside my tailor shop spitting on my doorstep and telling me to pack my sewing machine into a coffin! Now he is in chains!"

Outside the closed green shutters of Mingde Hardware, Wu Lanying stepped onto the threshold, one hand pressed over her racing heart while Liu Meifang held her elbow steady. Behind them stood Chen Jianguo, face tight with hopeful disbelief, and beside him stood Chen Xuan, hands tucked calmly into his jacket pockets, expression quiet and unruffled.

"Auntie Wu Lanying! Uncle Chen Jianguo!" Yu Changshun hurried across the lane, tears still shining as he gripped Chen Jianguo's arm. "Did you hear? Hongrui Real Estate is sealed! Cheng Biao is in cuffs! That crooked director cannot hold Chen Mingde for one more minute!"

Wu Lanying's lips trembled, tears springing immediately to her eyes. "Really, Uncle Yu Changshun? Cheng Biao is truly arrested? What about Chen Mingde? Nobody has called us from the station yet!"

"If Cheng Biao is in cuffs, the province will let Chen Mingde out right away!" Chen Jianguo said, though his own knuckles were white on the doorframe. "Everyone on Old Street saw those thugs fall down on their own!"

Before Wu Lanying could answer, two clean provincial vans rolled smoothly through the east archway.

The crowd parted with hushed excitement. Six uniformed provincial officers stepped out before Mingde Hardware, carrying an official crimson-stamped notice, metal scrapers, and two buckets of clear solvent with stiff brushes.

A square-jawed provincial captain halted in front of the hardware shop and spoke in a clear, carrying voice.

"Residents of Old Street, attention please," the captain announced. "By direct order of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group, every demolition notice and relocation seal posted by Hongrui Real Estate across Old Street is declared illegal and void. Hongrui Real Estate is under criminal receivership, and Yan Tiehu's organized-crime group has been dismantled."

A massive cheer exploded down the lane. Neighbors clapped, hugged each other, and shouted with relief echoing off the tile roofs.

The captain turned toward Mingde Hardware's doorstep and nodded politely to Wu Lanying and Chen Jianguo. "Are you the family of Comrade Chen Mingde?"

"Yes, I am his wife Wu Lanying!" Wu Lanying stepped forward quickly, voice shaking. "Officer, is my husband safe? When can we go pick him up?"

"You do not need to go anywhere, Auntie Wu Lanying," the captain replied with a warm smile. "Comrade Chen Mingde has already been fully cleared and released with an official exoneration certificate. Deputy Division Chief Han Zhiyuan is personally escorting him home right now. He will be at your doorstep in less than three minutes."

Wu Lanying's knees nearly buckled with overwhelming joy. She buried her face against Liu Meifang's shoulder, sobbing happily while Liu Meifang patted her back with wet eyes of her own.

"Thank you, Officer! Thank the provincial government!" Chen Jianguo said hoarsely, bowing deeply.

"We are only doing our duty," the captain replied briskly, gesturing toward the corner brick wall of Mingde Hardware where Yan Tiehu's crew had slapped a yellow demolition seal and splashed a huge red demolition character in glossy paint the previous afternoon. "Clean it off completely. Leave not a single mark."

Two provincial officers stepped up to the wall. One peeled the illegal Hongrui Real Estate seal cleanly off the plaster and sealed it into an evidence bag. The second dipped a wide brush into clear solvent and scrubbed steadily across the bold red paint.

Thirty neighbors watched in smiling silence as the red pigment dissolved into pale pink foam and washed down the bricks into the stone gutter.

"Look at that!" Yu Changshun cheered, clapping hard while tears of laughter ran down his chin. "Wash every drop of their filth away! Old Street belongs to us again!"

"Auntie Wu Lanying, open the shutters!" Wang Lei called out happily. "Uncle Chen Mingde is coming home! Let him see Mingde Hardware wide open and shining!"

Chen Jianguo slid the iron bolt back with shaking fingers and rolled the green metal shutter upward with a loud, triumphant clatter.

Beside the doorway, Chen Xuan stood quietly in the morning light, watching the last faint streak of red paint rinse off the brick until the gray stone looked clean and whole again. His eyes lifted calmly toward the east archway.

At the far end of the lane, a clean black provincial sedan turned off the main road, rolling slowly through the cheering neighbors toward the open doorway of Mingde Hardware.
