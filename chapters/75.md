# Chapter 75: Old Street's Red Paint Washed Away

By five minutes to nine, Old Street no longer sounded like a quiet lane waiting for bulldozers. It sounded like a dam had broken.

First came the loud buzz of phones turning back on after the overnight signal outage. Then came shouting from the breakfast stall at the east archway. The news rolled from door to door until metal shutters rattled open and neighbors ran out onto the street in slippers and aprons.

"Hongrui Real Estate is finished!" a bicycle repairman yelled as he pedaled past Mingde Hardware, waving his phone above his handlebars. "Chairman Deng Yongchang was just marched out of Hongrui Plaza in handcuffs! Live video! Provincial economic-crime vans sealed the whole building!"

"What did you say?" old tailor Yu Changshun called out. He hurried through the beaded curtain of his shop across the lane with a yellow measuring tape still hanging around his neck. His hands shook as he grabbed his younger neighbor Wang Lei's arm. "Wang Lei, show me your phone! Is that really Deng Yongchang?"

Wang Lei held the screen right under Yu Changshun's nose, grinning wide. "Look right there, Uncle Yu Changshun! Gray suit, cuffed behind his back, yelling for lawyers while provincial officers wheel computers out of his basement! And my cousin inside the county compound just texted me: County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Police Chief Liang Guodong were both cuffed right in the eight-thirty roll call!"

A sharp gasp swept through the shopkeepers crowding around Wang Lei's phone.

"What about Yan Tiehu?" the soybean-milk seller asked, her fingers clutching her apron. "Last night Yan Tiehu swore he would bring bulldozers down Old Street at nine sharp!"

"Gone!" Wang Lei laughed loudly and slapped his knee. "Provincial special police raided Yan Tiehu's riverfront yard before sunrise! They arrested Yan Tiehu and all twenty of his thugs on organized-crime and smuggling charges! They even found illegal blasting caps in his warehouse! And Station Director Cheng Biao and Deputy Director Zhou Quan were hauled out of Old Street Station in handcuffs ten minutes ago!"

Yu Changshun stared at the phone screen for two stunned seconds. Then the seventy-year-old tailor threw his head back and burst into loud, shaking laughter, hot tears running down the deep lines beside his eyes.

"Heaven finally opened its eyes!" Yu Changshun laughed. He wiped his wet cheeks with his sleeve while his voice cracked with joy. "For twelve years, Chairman Deng Yongchang squeezed this county dry! Yesterday Yan Tiehu stood outside my tailor shop, spat on my doorstep, and told me to pack my sewing machine into a coffin! Now he is in chains!"

Outside the closed green shutters of Mingde Hardware, Wu Lanying stepped onto the front step. She pressed one hand over her racing heart while Liu Meifang held her arm steady. Behind them stood Chen Jianguo, his face tight with hopeful disbelief. Beside him stood Chen Xuan, his hands tucked calmly into his jacket pockets, his expression quiet and relaxed.

"Auntie Wu Lanying! Uncle Chen Jianguo!" Yu Changshun hurried across the lane, tears still shining as he gripped Chen Jianguo's arm. "Did you hear? Hongrui Real Estate is sealed! Cheng Biao is in cuffs! That crooked director cannot hold Chen Mingde for one more minute!"

Wu Lanying's lips shook, and tears jumped straight to her eyes. "Really, Uncle Yu Changshun? Cheng Biao is truly arrested? What about Chen Mingde? Nobody has called us from the police station yet!"

"If Cheng Biao is in cuffs, the province will let Chen Mingde out right away!" Chen Jianguo said, though his own knuckles were white on the doorframe. "Everyone on Old Street saw those thugs fall down on their own!"

Before Wu Lanying could answer, two clean provincial vans rolled smoothly through the east archway.

The crowd stepped aside with quiet excitement. Six uniformed provincial officers stepped out in front of Mingde Hardware. They carried an official red-stamped notice, metal scrapers, and two buckets of paint remover with stiff brushes.

A square-jawed provincial captain stopped in front of the hardware shop and spoke in a clear, strong voice.

"Residents of Old Street, attention please," the captain announced. "By direct order of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group, every demolition notice and paper seal posted by Hongrui Real Estate across Old Street is illegal and void. Hongrui Real Estate is under provincial control, and Yan Tiehu's crime group has been wiped out."

A huge cheer exploded down the lane. Neighbors clapped, hugged each other, and shouted with relief that echoed off the old tile roofs.

The captain turned toward Mingde Hardware's doorway and nodded politely to Wu Lanying and Chen Jianguo. "Are you the family of Comrade Chen Mingde?"

"Yes, I am his wife Wu Lanying!" Wu Lanying stepped forward quickly, her voice shaking. "Officer, is my husband safe? When can we go pick him up?"

"You do not need to go anywhere, Auntie Wu Lanying," the captain replied with a warm smile. "Comrade Chen Mingde has already been fully cleared and released with an official cleared name paper. Deputy Division Chief Han Zhiyuan is personally driving him home right now. He will be at your doorstep in less than three minutes."

Wu Lanying's knees nearly gave out from pure joy. She buried her face against Liu Meifang's shoulder and cried happily while Liu Meifang patted her back with wet eyes of her own.

"Thank you, Officer! Thank the provincial government!" Chen Jianguo said hoarsely, bowing deeply.

"We are only doing our job," the captain replied briskly. He pointed toward the corner brick wall of Mingde Hardware, where Yan Tiehu's crew had slapped a yellow demolition seal and painted a huge red demolition sign in thick paint the afternoon before. "Clean it off completely. Do not leave a single mark."

Two provincial officers stepped up to the wall. One peeled the illegal Hongrui Real Estate paper seal cleanly off the brick and put it into an evidence bag. The second dipped a wide brush into the bucket of paint remover and scrubbed steadily across the bright red paint.

Thirty neighbors watched in smiling silence as the red paint melted into pale pink foam and washed down the bricks into the stone drain.

"Look at that!" Yu Changshun cheered, clapping hard while tears of laughter ran down his chin. "Wash every drop of their filth away! Old Street belongs to us again!"

"Auntie Wu Lanying, open the shutters!" Wang Lei called out happily. "Uncle Chen Mingde is coming home! Let him see Mingde Hardware wide open and shining!"

Chen Jianguo slid the iron bolt back with shaking fingers and rolled the green metal shutter upward with a loud, joyful clatter.

Beside the doorway, Chen Xuan stood quietly in the morning sun. He watched the last faint streak of red paint rinse off the brick until the gray stone looked clean and whole again. Then his eyes lifted calmly toward the east archway.

At the far end of the lane, a clean black provincial car turned off the main road. It rolled slowly through the cheering neighbors toward the open doorway of Mingde Hardware.
