# Chapter 150: Leave on Time

At 4:47 on a bright Friday afternoon, the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics in Shanghai had stopped pretending to work. Nearly every clerk in the room was watching the wall clock.

Chen Xuan sat comfortably at desk fourteen in his gray jacket and dark trousers. Beside his mouse pad rested his scratched green thermos of jasmine tea and a paper bag of pumpkin seeds. On the company payroll, he was still a junior operations supervisor earning 12,000 yuan a month before tax. Just like three years ago, he had arrived at 8:55 sharp, finished every port spreadsheet before noon, and kept his desk spotless.

Two desks over, Pan Xiulan snapped her notebook shut with a satisfied smile. "Eight minutes left," she said cheerfully. "If I catch the elevator before five, I can beat the Friday rush on Binjiang Avenue and pick up fresh pork dumplings for my family."

Across the aisle, Zheng Qian leaned back in his chair and stretched both arms overhead. "Sister Pan, you do not even have to whisper about clocking out anymore! Ever since Monday morning when Brother Chen turned down 50,000 yuan in front of the whole floor, Friday afternoons on the twelfth floor feel like a holiday."

Pan Xiulan laughed softly and looked warmly toward desk fourteen. "That is because Manager Hu Dewei does not dare dump overtime on our row now. Look at Chen Xuan. His computer screen has been clean since eleven thirty this morning."

Zheng Qian rolled his chair closer to the aisle and grinned at Chen Xuan. "Brother Chen, tell us the truth. Did your wife really not scold you at home for turning down 50,000 yuan a month and a corner office on the fifteenth floor?"

Chen Xuan unscrewed his green thermos, blew gently on the jasmine steam, and took a slow sip. "Why would she scold me?" he asked in a mild, easy voice. "She was glad I stayed at desk fourteen. Tonight is Friday, and she asked me to make shrimp and chive dumplings from scratch. If I were sitting upstairs in a deputy manager's office right now, I would still be signing port budgets at seven o'clock."

"Wait a minute," Zheng Qian blinked, leaning forward curiously. "Yesterday at lunch you let it slip that your wife works over at Haiyue Tower. Does she work in their accounting office too?"

"Something like that," Chen Xuan smiled lightly, screwing the lid back onto his green thermos. "She manages a lot of paperwork over there, and she gets picky if her dinner is late."

Just then, the glass door of the corner manager's office clicked open.

Unlike a few months ago, not a single clerk froze or scrambled to fake typing. Department Manager Hu Dewei stepped out with a polite smile on his plump face, holding a blue folder. He glanced straight toward desk fourteen first and gave Chen Xuan a respectful nod before addressing the room.

"Everyone, listen up for ten seconds," Hu Dewei said in a gentle tone. "General Manager Sun Haoran just signed off on the southeast dock container sheets. Thanks to Chen Xuan checking the port numbers before lunch, there are zero errors this week. There is no overtime tonight, so tidy up your desks and go home on time."

"Thank you, Manager Hu!" Zheng Qian called out happily.

"Have a wonderful weekend, Manager Hu," Pan Xiulan added, slipping her notebook into her handbag.

Hu Dewei beamed with relief, gave Chen Xuan one more careful nod, and strolled back into his office to pack his briefcase.

Across the twelfth floor, chairs rolled back and winter coats came off their hooks amid light, unhurried laughter.

Chen Xuan tucked his green thermos into his canvas bag, clicked his monitor off cleanly, and watched the wall clock on the far pillar tick quietly to 4:55.

He stood up at once and slung his canvas bag over one shoulder. "Have a good weekend, Sister Pan. Have a good weekend, Zheng Qian. Do not eat too much roasted duck tonight."

"Have fun making dumplings with your wife, Brother Chen!" Zheng Qian waved cheerfully.

Chen Xuan walked down the aisle and stepped into the elevator hall. At five minutes to five on a Friday, the middle brass doors chimed softly and slid open to reveal an empty, spotless elevator cab waiting on the twelfth floor.

He stepped inside, rode peacefully down to the lobby, and walked out through the glass doors onto Binjiang Avenue.

The late afternoon sun was sinking over the river, painting the sky in warm shades of gold and soft rose. Along the clean sidewalk, the crisp breeze carried the sweet scent of roasted chestnuts and river air.

Far beyond this Shanghai street, the entire nation was breathing easier as the weekend began. Across forty national departments and every province from the capital down to the county roads, the greedy syndicates and crooked parasites that had troubled ordinary workers were gone. More than 800,000,000,000 yuan in recovered funds now sat safely inside public accounts, building clean highways, bright schools, and safe streets like Old Street in Yunxi County.

High up on the terrace of a riverside teahouse three blocks away, two men in crisp white gear stood beside the railing looking down over the golden avenue.

"Every last ledger across the provinces is closed and balanced," Number 7 said quietly, setting two cups of tea on the table. "And our teams have returned to quiet watch."

Number 1 picked up his teacup and watched the figure in the gray jacket walking calmly along the river sidewalk below. "Good," Number 1 replied evenly. "Keep every team out of sight. He only wants an ordinary life with his family, so nobody disturbs his peace."

Down by the curb in front of Hengxin Tower, a sleek black sedan had just pulled up under the plane trees. The back window rolled down halfway, revealing Qin Yuhan in a cream wool coat, her dark hair loose over her shoulders and a bright, playful smile in her eyes.

"Hey, handsome clerk," Qin Yuhan called out softly as Chen Xuan reached the curb. "I finished my Friday board meeting ten minutes early. Do you need a ride to the dumpling market, or are you taking the number twelve bus without me?"

Chen Xuan smiled warmly, opened the back door, and slid into the seat beside his wife with his canvas bag and green thermos resting on his lap.

"I always have room in my schedule for a rich lady offering a free ride," Chen Xuan teased gently, taking her warm hand in his. "Let us go buy fresh shrimp before the market closes."

Qin Yuhan laughed happily and leaned her head against his shoulder as the car merged into the golden traffic, carrying the quiet sovereign and his wife home for the weekend.
