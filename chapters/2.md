# Chapter 2: The Shaking Voice

Chen Xuan stepped out of Hengxin Tower onto Binjiang Avenue as evening settled over Qingyan. Office workers hurried toward the subway stairs in the cool air.

He adjusted his canvas bag beside a stone planter. His plan for the night was simple. He would buy half a pound of lean pork ribs, cook lotus root soup, and read two chapters of a paperback book about sea trade before ten o'clock. Three years of this quiet routine had taught him to enjoy ordinary nights.

The phone in his left pocket buzzed twice.

Expecting a store message, Chen Xuan pulled it out and froze. The screen showed three voice messages in a row from his mother, Liu Meifang, sent forty seconds apart. The last one was forty-eight seconds long.

Liu Meifang almost never sent voice messages on workdays. Even on weekends, she only sent short texts asking if he had eaten, because she did not want to bother him at work. Chen Xuan paused for half a second, then raised the phone to his ear.

The first message started with a ragged breath. "Chen Xuan, are you off work yet?" Liu Meifang's voice shook so hard her words broke apart. "Something terrible happened to your Second Uncle today. At two o'clock, men from Old Street Police Station walked into Mingde Hardware and put handcuffs on Chen Mingde in front of the whole street!"

Chen Xuan's fingers tightened around the phone. He did not move a muscle, yet all warmth faded from his eyes.

He tapped the second message.

"They claimed your Second Uncle beat a site foreman from Hongrui Real Estate with a steel pipe," Liu Meifang sobbed, full of fear and disbelief. "Chen Xuan, you know Chen Mingde! He has never even killed a chicken in his whole life! Two thugs walked into his shop at noon, smashed his glass counter, and yelled at him to sign the move-out paper. When he pulled your Second Aunt Wu Lanying away from the broken glass, one of them threw himself onto the floor screaming that his ribs were broken!"

Chen Xuan's breathing stayed slow and steady. His eyes dropped to the pavement, going completely still.

The third message played right away, carrying the sharp clatter of a teacup.

"Your Dad heard and ran straight to Old Street Station at three," Liu Meifang choked out. "Chen Jianguo stood outside the iron gate begging them to pass Chen Mingde his blood pressure pills. The officer on duty yelled at him to get lost before they locked him up for getting in the way of police work too. Your Second Aunt Wu Lanying fainted outside the shop. Chen Xuan, we do not know anyone in Yunxi County with power. What are we going to do?"

The message ended. Taxi horns honked and young clerks walked past laughing with milk tea, paying no mind to the still man beside the planter.

Chen Xuan stared down at the dark screen.

Second Uncle Chen Mingde did not belong near steel pipes or handcuffs. He was fifty-four years old, thin in the shoulders, with fingers stained from sorting brass screws and bike chains at Mingde Hardware. When he bumped his knee against a wooden stool in his own shop, he muttered sorry to the stool out of habit.

When Chen Xuan was seven and had a bad summer cough, Chen Mingde had closed his shop for a whole Saturday. He borrowed a bamboo fishing rod and sat beside Yunxi Creek for six straight hours without catching a single fish, just so the boy could watch the red float bob on the water. When rain poured down at dusk, he put his only dry denim jacket over Chen Xuan's head and walked two miles home soaked to the skin, smiling so the boy would not feel bad.

Today, someone in Yunxi County had put iron handcuffs on that man in front of his neighbors and shouted Chen Jianguo away from a police station gate.

Something shifted deep inside Chen Xuan's chest. There was no loud anger, no shaking hands, no heat in his face. Instead, a deep, cold stillness settled over him, hard and unmoving as winter stone. For three years, he had set aside his old weight and lived as a quiet clerk. Now a careless hand had reached straight into his own home.

Chen Xuan let out a slow breath and called his mother.

She answered before the first ring ended. "Chen Xuan!" Liu Meifang gasped, her breath wet and shaky. "Did you hear my messages?"

"Mom, I heard every word," Chen Xuan said. His voice was warm, steady, and calm, slowing her frantic breathing right away. "Sit down and drink some warm water. Do not cry."

"How can I not cry?" Liu Meifang whispered, sniffling hard. "Chen Mingde has high blood pressure and has never spent a night away from home in thirty years. Your Dad is pacing in the yard saying he will go back to Old Street Station tonight and kneel at the gate if he has to."

In the background came the scrape of a chair and Chen Jianguo's hoarse voice talking about finding the deputy station chief.

"Listen to me carefully, Mom," Chen Xuan said. His tone stayed gentle, yet carried unmistakable steel. "Do not let Dad go back to the police station. Tell Chen Jianguo to lock the front door, boil hot ginger soup for Second Aunt Wu Lanying, and stay inside. Neither Dad nor anyone in our family is to go near Old Street Station tonight or tomorrow. Promise me."

Liu Meifang hesitated, her voice dropping to a frightened whisper. "Your Dad says if nobody from our family begs them, Hongrui Real Estate will frame your Second Uncle and send him to prison before morning. Who else in Yunxi will speak up for him?"

"I will handle it," Chen Xuan replied simply. "I left work and I am coming home right now. Tell Dad that going to that gate only gives them another chance to bully him. I will be in Yunxi before midnight. Tell him to wait at home and not worry."

"Before midnight?" Liu Meifang sounded startled through her tears. "Do not lie just to make me feel better! The last highway bus leaves at 6:10, and the mountain road takes four hours in the dark! Do not rush and get hurt!"

"I am not taking the bus, Mom," Chen Xuan said calmly. "A friend from work has a fast ride heading toward Lanshui tonight, and I am catching it now. How is Second Aunt Wu Lanying?"

"She woke up twenty minutes ago," Liu Meifang murmured, growing calmer from his steady voice. "She is lying on our bed shaking and blaming herself for arguing about the counter. Your Dad is pouring her hot water."

"Good. Stay beside her, keep Dad inside, and do not open the gate for strangers," Chen Xuan said. "If anyone from Hongrui Real Estate knocks, do not argue and do not step outside. Wait for me."

"Alright," Liu Meifang breathed, the panic easing a little. "I will hold your Dad back. Be careful on the road."

He waited until her breathing turned steady before ending the call.

Chen Xuan put his ordinary phone in his pocket, then pulled a second phone from inside his jacket lining. It was flat black, heavier than a normal phone, with no brand name and no icons.

His thumb pressed the side sensor. A plain green text line blinked open.

Chen Xuan typed three simple words, hit send, and slipped the black phone back into his jacket.

*Cloud Gate. Yunxi.*

Turning away from the subway crowd, he walked calmly toward the side lane behind Qingyan South Park.
