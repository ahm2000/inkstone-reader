# Chapter 149: The Quiet Sovereign

Fifteen minutes after dinner, the carved rosewood Chinese chess board sat open on the low tea table in the villa living room.

Elder Qin Zhengguo perched his reading glasses on his nose, rolled up his gray sweater sleeves like a battlefield commander, and slammed his red chariot down with a sharp clack.

"Watch closely, Zhengming!" Elder Qin Zhengguo declared loudly to his son, who stood behind the sofa holding a cup of hot Pu'er tea. "Last week this lazy boy caught me sleeping after two bowls of soup. Tonight I have locked down both sides of the river. Not even a mosquito can fly past my central elephant!"

Across the low table, Chen Xuan leaned lazily against a sofa cushion with his scratched green thermos resting by his knee. He stifled a wide, relaxed yawn behind one hand, blinked his sleepy eyes, and casually nudged a wooden black horse two squares forward with one finger.

"Check, Grandpa," Chen Xuan said mildly, reaching for his thermos lid.

Elder Qin Zhengguo's smile froze right on his face.

He leaned down until his nose was four inches above the board, staring in disbelief at the quiet black horse sitting beside his double cannons. "Wait a minute! When did that little horse cross the river? Zhengming, did you see him move that horse?"

Qin Zhengming coughed into his fist to hide a laugh. "Dad, he moved it three turns ago while you were bragging about your central elephant. And if you move your general left, his hidden cannon at the corner takes your chariot too."

"No offering advice from the sidelines!" Elder Qin Zhengguo barked, his ears turning red as he glared at the wooden pieces. "Let me look! A forty year military veteran does not lose in twelve moves to a guy who yawns into a green thermos!"

Seated on the side sofa peeling an apple for her daughter, Lin Shuyin shook her head with a hopeless sigh. "Look at the two of them. One is a retired general shouting at wooden blocks, and the other is a 12,000 yuan clerk who could fall asleep sitting upright. Chen Xuan, if you put half of that chess brain into working at Haiyue Group or taking that 50,000 yuan deputy manager post at Hengxin Logistics, I would not have to make excuses when my friends ask what Yuhan's husband does all day!"

"Mom, what excuses do you need to make?" Qin Yuhan laughed, popping a crisp slice of apple into her mouth. "Just tell your friends that my husband clocks out at 4:55 every day to take care of me, cooks better than a five star chef, and keeps Grandpa busy for two hours every Wednesday night!"

"He is a sneaky chess player, that is what he is!" Elder Qin Zhengguo grumbled, finally pushing his red general back in surrender before grinning warmly at Chen Xuan. "All right, you win again, boy! Come Saturday, pack two jars of that spicy pickled radish you made last month and bring them over to the old courtyard. Even if you only earn 12,000 yuan a month, your cooking and your chess beat every stuffy millionaire your mother tries to brag about!"

"Dad!" Lin Shuyin protested, though a reluctant smile tugged at her own mouth as she watched Chen Xuan politely refill the old man's teacup. "Fine, fine! As long as he keeps Yuhan happy and does not forget to simmer pear soup for her when the weather turns dry, I will stop nagging him about the promotion."

"I already bought a basket of snow pears this afternoon, Mom," Chen Xuan smiled warmly. "They are sitting in the kitchen pantry right now."

Even Lin Shuyin had nothing left to complain about at that, patting Qin Yuhan's knee with a soft chuckle before calling Qin Zhengming and Elder Qin Zhengguo to wrap up their coats for the ride home.

After waving goodbye to her parents and grandfather as their black sedan rolled down the driveway, Qin Yuhan slipped her wool coat over her shoulders and linked her arm through Chen Xuan's elbow.

"Walk with me to the corner gate before we go inside," Qin Yuhan said softly. "I ate three more ribs than I planned to."

They strolled slowly side by side beneath the quiet avenue of winter plane trees outside the villa courtyard. Yellow streetlamps cast a gentle golden glow across the clean pavement, and the cool night breeze smelled faintly of river mist and winter plum blossoms.

"You really do not mind my mother nagging you about that 50,000 yuan job?" Qin Yuhan asked playfully, tilting her head to look up at his calm profile. "Any other man in Shanghai would be itching to prove himself."

"Why would I mind?" Chen Xuan chuckled softly, tucking her hand together with his inside his warm jacket pocket. "She only wants to make sure you are well looked after. And Grandpa defended me so fiercely tonight that I barely had to open my mouth."

Qin Yuhan let out a bright, musical laugh, leaning her cheek briefly against his shoulder as they walked. "Grandpa thinks you are the most carefree twelve thousand yuan clerk in the country. When you yawned right in the middle of trapping his red chariot, I thought his eyeglasses were going to fall right into his teacup."

"He plays a very aggressive game," Chen Xuan smiled easily. "If I did not wrap it up in twelve moves, we would still be sitting at the tea table at midnight, and you have an early board meeting tomorrow."

Qin Yuhan stopped under a warm streetlamp near the courtyard gate and turned to face him, her dark eyes crinkling with affection.

"Everything has been so peaceful lately," she murmured softly, smoothing the collar of his plain gray jacket. "Those terrifying stock market predators who attacked Haiyue Group vanished overnight, the port contracts are running smoothly, and every evening at six o'clock I get to come home to a house that smells like hot soup. Sometimes I feel like the luckiest woman in Shanghai just having a lazy clerk waiting for me at home."

"You work hard enough for both of us, President Qin," Chen Xuan teased gently, tapping the tip of her nose with a warm finger. "My job is simple. Clock out at 4:55, keep my green thermos full, and make sure my rich wife eats hot dinner on time."

Qin Yuhan grinned happily and pulled him back toward the glowing porch lights of their villa. "Then let us go inside, Clerk Chen. You promised me warm snow pear soup before bed!"
