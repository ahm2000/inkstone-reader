# Chapter 145: The Secret Kept by the Lake

Half an hour later, the small purple clay teapot sat empty beside the cooling charcoal stove.

Chen Xuan stood up from the stone stool, screwed the lid onto his scratched green thermos, and tucked it under his arm.

"Please stay seated, Elder Song," Chen Xuan said warmly. "The lake breeze is picking up, and the stone steps are cool. I should head back to the airfield now if I want to make it home to Shanghai before three o'clock."

Elder Song smiled fondly from his stool, his bright ninety year old eyes crinkling. "Right, right! You still have to finish your Saturday warehouse check so your wife does not scold your manager! Go on, young friend. I already rinsed the second teacup and tucked it away in my basket so nobody who comes up here later will even know I had a guest."

"Take good care of your health, Elder Song," Chen Xuan said, bowing slightly from the waist. "Thank you for a wonderful cup of tea."

"Safe flight home, young man," Elder Song replied softly. "Go back to your quiet kitchen and your peaceful weekends. This old man will guard your secret until the end of time."

Chen Xuan nodded once with an easy smile, turned, and walked unhurriedly down the stone steps of the pavilion.

The lakeside trail remained completely empty. Not a single sentry, aide, or camera watched the shaded flagstones as Chen Xuan strolled down to the cedar courtyard at the foot of the slope.

Beside the mossy wall, Number 7 stood in crisp white gear next to the waiting black sedan. Seeing Chen Xuan emerge from the cypress trees, Number 7 immediately opened the rear door and bowed his head.

"Everything went well, sir?" Number 7 asked quietly as Chen Xuan climbed inside.

"Very well," Chen Xuan smiled, setting his green thermos beside him on the seat. "Elder Song makes a great pot of Wuyi rock tea. Let us head back to the runway so I have time to pick up two bags of roasted chestnuts in Shanghai before I go home."

"Right away, sir," Number 7 answered with a grin, closing the door and taking the front passenger seat.

The black sedan glided smoothly out of the cedar courtyard and rolled down the quiet north garden road. Fifty meters ahead through the pine shadows, the unmarked dark van carrying the masked Black Guard members pulled silently onto the pavement without a horn or a signal, leading the way back toward the executive airfield.

An hour after Chen Xuan's pearl white jet had lifted back into the southern clouds toward Shanghai, the outer security barriers of the Western Hills were finally reopened.

Footsteps crunched briskly up the stone path toward the lake, and retired military grandfather Elder Qin Zhengguo walked into the stone pavilion wearing his plain olive coat, carrying a wooden chessboard case under one arm.

Elder Song sat alone at the round stone table with only a single porcelain teacup in front of him, calmly watching the ducks paddle across the water.

"Old leadership!" Elder Qin Zhengguo called out cheerfully, setting the chessboard on the stone table and taking the opposite stool. "Your guards down at the south valley told me you locked the entire north ridge all morning to drink tea by yourself! Did that mysterious holder of the Black Order actually send a message, or did you secretly meet him here?"

Elder Qin Zhengguo leaned forward with intense curiosity, his weathered eyebrows lifting.

"Come on, Elder Song, tell me!" Elder Qin Zhengguo pressed eagerly. "Who is the legendary dragon who gave the country 300,000,000,000,000 yuan for free and swept forty ministries clean in a week? What did he look like? Was he an old overseas patriarch in his seventies, or some stern titan with eyes like lightning?"

Elder Song lifted his single porcelain teacup, took a slow sip, and chuckled softly into the rim. Not a flicker of giveaway showed on his ninety year old face.

"Just an ordinary citizen who likes peace, Zhengguo. Do not ever look for him," Elder Song said calmly, setting his teacup down with a gentle tap.

Elder Qin Zhengguo blinked and let out a theatrical sigh, slapping his thigh. "You will not even give me a tiny hint? Not even whether he has a white beard or a gray beard?"

"Not a single hair of a beard," Elder Song laughed, opening the wooden chessboard case and setting up the red and black pieces. "If a man hands the country 300,000,000,000,000 yuan without asking for his name in the history books, the only proper way we can repay him is to leave him completely in peace. Set up your chariots, Zhengguo. You owe me three games from Tuesday!"

"Speaking of chess, do not even remind me!" Elder Qin Zhengguo grumbled cheerfully, arranging his black pieces on the board with a half proud, half exasperated grin. "I am still annoyed about last week!"

"Oh?" Elder Song chuckled, his eyes twinkling as he lined up his red cannons. "Who managed to beat an old battlefield commander like you last week? Was it old General Liu?"

"If it were General Liu, I would not be complaining!" Elder Qin Zhengguo huffed playfully, waving both hands. "It was my granddaughter Qin Yuhan's lazy Shanghai clerk husband, Chen Xuan!"

Elder Song paused with a wooden knight between his fingers and smiled brightly. "Your granddaughter Yuhan's young husband? The 12,000 yuan logistics clerk you always brag makes good braised ribs?"

"That very one!" Elder Qin Zhengguo laughed, shaking his head in fond disbelief. "Yuhan called me on video while that lazy rascal was drinking jasmine tea out of a dented fifteen yuan green thermos on their sofa! I challenged him to a game over the phone just to teach the young fellow some discipline. That carefree clerk did not even set down his green thermos! Eating roasted peanuts with one hand, he trapped both of my chariots in eighteen moves and beat me three games in a row while telling me how to season sweet and sour fish!"

Across the stone table, Elder Song laughed warmly, pushing his first pawn forward without the slightest suspicion in the world.

"Well, well!" Elder Song chuckled over his teacup, completely unaware that the young man he had just bowed to an hour ago was the exact same grandson in law Elder Qin was complaining about. "It sounds like your granddaughter Yuhan found herself a clever little clerk in Shanghai! Next time he visits Beijing, you had better practice your openings first, Zhengguo, or that lazy Shanghai clerk husband of hers will sweep your board clean again!"
