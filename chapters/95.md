# Chapter 95: Hengxin's New Chapter

Monday morning sunlight streamed brightly across the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics, warming the rows of gray office desks and the potted pothos along the window ledges.

At twenty-five minutes past eight, Chen Xuan arrived right on his usual schedule. He set his scratched green metal thermos beside his monitor, placed a small paper bag holding a warm pork-and-cabbage steamed bun in the sunny corner of his desk, and began sorting through the morning's coastal freight manifests.

Two desks over, Wang Lei was stirring instant coffee with a plastic spoon while Lin Dong squinted at a stack of customs receipts.

"Did you hear?" Wang Lei whispered, leaning over the partition with wide eyes. "Manager Hu Dewei has been smiling since eight o'clock. He did not even scold me when I dropped my stapler in the hallway."

"Something big must have come through over the weekend," Lin Dong muttered, glancing toward the glass-walled offices. "Even Pan Xiulan from accounting put on her best blue blazer today."

At nine o'clock sharp, the double glass doors at the front of the twelfth floor swung open.

General Manager Sun Haoran strode out onto the main floor, his face glowing with a joy nobody in the building had ever seen before. Right behind him walked Manager Hu Dewei, grinning from ear to ear, followed by the human resources director carrying a thick crimson presentation folder.

General Manager Sun Haoran clapped his hands twice, his voice ringing brightly across the open office.

"Everyone, please pause your keyboards for five minutes! Today marks a brand new chapter for Hengxin Logistics!"

Every head on the twelfth floor popped up over the cubicle dividers.

"Over the weekend," General Manager Sun Haoran announced proudly, holding up two stamped contract binders, "Hengxin Logistics formally signed five-year strategic master partnerships with both Haiyue Hospitality Group and Provincial Coastal Freight!"

A collective gasp rippled across the office, instantly followed by a burst of excited chatter.

"Haiyue Hospitality and Provincial Coastal Freight?" Pan Xiulan exclaimed, pressing a hand to her chest. "Those two contracts alone will more than double our annual freight volume!"

"Not just double," General Manager Sun Haoran laughed heartily. "Both boards approved advance settlement terms, priority port lanes, and full cold-chain expansion. Starting next month, every single employee on this floor gets a twenty percent bonus pool increase, and our year-end bonuses are officially guaranteed!"

Cheers and applause erupted across the twelfth floor. Wang Lei pumped his fist in the air, Lin Dong slapped his desk in delight, and even the quietest junior clerks grinned from ear to ear.

General Manager Sun Haoran waited for the cheers to settle, then raised one hand with a warm, deeply appreciative smile. His eyes turned directly toward the quiet corner desk by the sunlit window.

"We all worked hard to keep our routes steady," General Manager Sun Haoran said, his voice softening with genuine gratitude. "Yet everyone in management knows why both Miss Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Hospitality and the board of Provincial Coastal Freight trusted Hengxin over three larger national carriers. Both partners specifically praised the precision, calm reliability, and zero-error dispatch coordination of one person on this floor."

General Manager Sun Haoran took the thick crimson presentation folder from the human resources director and walked straight down the aisle to Chen Xuan's corner desk.

Every pair of eyes in the office followed him in breathless silence.

"Chen Xuan," General Manager Sun Haoran declared clearly, holding out the crimson appointment folder with both hands. "By unanimous vote of the company board, I am formally offering you promotion to Deputy General Manager of Hengxin Logistics! Triple your current salary, a private corner office on the fourteenth floor, a dedicated company sedan, and a permanent seat on the executive committee!"

For half a second, the twelfth floor forgot how to breathe.

Then a wave of stunned gasps swept the room.

"Deputy General Manager!" Wang Lei blurted out, nearly spilling his coffee across his keyboard. "Triple salary and a company car! Chen Xuan, you are jumping three whole ranks in one morning!"

"A private office on the fourteenth floor!" Lin Dong whispered in sheer awe.

Manager Hu Dewei beamed proudly from the aisle, nodding rapidly as if urging Chen Xuan to take the folder before anyone changed their mind.

Chen Xuan set his blue ballpoint pen neatly beside his freight ledger and stood up with unhurried politeness.

He looked at the crimson folder, then looked up at General Manager Sun Haoran with a mild, completely sincere smile.

"Thank you, General Manager Sun, and thank you to the board for thinking so highly of me," Chen Xuan said calmly. "I am genuinely happy to see Hengxin Logistics growing. Everyone on this floor deserves the good news."

Then, gently and politely, he lifted both hands and declined the crimson folder.

"As for the Deputy General Manager title, I had better pass," Chen Xuan added mildly. "Executive positions mean too many afternoon meetings, client banquets, and weekend inspections. If I get tied up in meetings past six o'clock, I will miss the 6:10 bus home."

Dead silence dropped over the twelfth floor.

Wang Lei's jaw hung completely open. Pan Xiulan blinked three times behind her glasses, convinced her ears had malfunctioned. Even Manager Hu Dewei froze mid-nod, staring at Chen Xuan as though he had just declined a winning lottery ticket because the line at the bank was too long.

General Manager Sun Haoran stared at Chen Xuan for two full seconds.

Then, remembering who Chen Xuan really was behind that mild, ordinary smile, General Manager Sun Haoran let out a helpless, deeply affectionate laugh.

"Only you, Chen Xuan," General Manager Sun Haoran said, shaking his head with genuine admiration. "Half the managers in Lanshui City would fight tooth and nail for a fourteenth-floor office and a company sedan, and you are worried about missing the 6:10 bus."

"The 6:10 bus has an open seat by the back window if you reach the stop on time," Chen Xuan explained pleasantly. "If the company really wants to reward me, a quiet pay raise is more than enough. And I would rather keep this corner desk by the window. The afternoon sun comes straight through the glass here and keeps my steamed bun warm."

Warm laughter broke out across the entire twelfth floor, dissolving the shock into cheerful disbelief.

"Done!" General Manager Sun Haoran laughed, closing the crimson folder with a crisp snap. "Effective today, Chen Xuan receives a senior specialist salary adjustment with zero mandatory overtime, zero evening banquets, and permanent ownership of his sunlit corner desk!"

Applause rang out again, louder and warmer than before.

"Chen Xuan, you really are a living sage," Wang Lei laughed, rushing over to pat Chen Xuan's shoulder once General Manager Sun Haoran headed back toward the elevators. "Triple pay and a sedan, and you chose your steamed bun and the 6:10 bus!"

"A warm bun and an on-time bus are harder to find than people think," Chen Xuan replied with a mild smile, sitting back down in his worn swivel chair.

Within ten minutes, the twelfth floor settled back into its comfortable Monday rhythm of clicking keyboards, ringing dispatch lines, and cheerful banter.

Twelve floors below on the sunlit avenue, completely unseen amid the ordinary flow of morning commuters and delivery vans, the permanent Zero Guard team held its silent fifty-meter perimeter in absolute stillness.

Up in his quiet corner by the window, Chen Xuan uncapped his scratched green thermos, took a slow sip of warm tea, and glanced fondly at the bright patch of autumn sunlight resting across his paper-wrapped steamed bun.

The world outside was vast, noisy, and full of ambition.

Right here by the window, everything was peaceful, fair, and right on time.
