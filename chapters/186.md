# Chapter 186: Back at Hengxin Logistics

Monday morning on the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics began with the hum of printers and the smell of steamed buns from the break room. Outside the windows, winter sun caught Jin Mao Tower and Shanghai Tower across the river.

Chen Xuan arrived at eight forty-five in his faded dark-blue windbreaker and canvas sneakers, hung his jacket on his chair, and poured hot water over green tea leaves in his chipped porcelain mug.

Beside him, Zheng Qian popped up over the low partition with a cup of soy milk.

"Morning, Brother Chen!" Zheng Qian grinned. "Did your brother and sister enjoy Shanghai?"

"They loved it," Chen Xuan smiled warmly. "We walked the Bund on Saturday night, watched the Oriental Pearl Tower, and ate roasted chestnuts. My little brother swore he is going to study hard and get into Fudan University next year."

"Fudan University! Now that is real ambition," Pan Xiulan chimed in across the aisle. "Wait until he gets in. You will be bragging to the whole floor."

"I will buy everyone milk tea that day," Chen Xuan laughed softly.

Before Zheng Qian could tease him further, the executive office doors swung open.

General Manager Sun Haoran strode out beaming from ear to ear, holding a crisp blue contract folder stamped with the red seal of Haiyue Group.

"Everyone, pause for two minutes!" Sun Haoran clapped twice, his voice booming across the floor. "Big news to start the week!"

Chairs squeaked as twenty clerks gathered in the center aisle.

Sun Haoran held up the folder. "Five minutes ago, Haiyue Group's Shanghai headquarters sent over their renewed annual freight contract. They doubled our yearly shipping volume! From 60 million yuan last year to 120 million yuan this year, with priority settlement directly through China Construction Bank!"

For one stunned second, the twelfth floor went dead quiet. Then the whole office erupted in cheers.

"120 million yuan!" Zheng Qian gasped, grabbing Chen Xuan's arm. "Brother Chen, our year-end bonuses are going to double!"

"No more worrying about slow winter months!" Pan Xiulan clapped happily.

Sun Haoran beamed. "This is the biggest contract Hengxin Logistics has signed in five years!"

As the applause began to settle, a loud, theatrical cough echoed from the front row.

Hu Dewei stepped smoothly out from beside the copy machine, tugging down his shiny gray suit jacket and pushing his gold-rimmed glasses up his nose. A proud smile sat on his plump face as he raised both hands.

"General Manager Sun, you are being too modest about our management efforts," Hu Dewei said smoothly, puffing out his chest. "Securing a giant like Haiyue Group takes months of careful groundwork. For the past three months, I personally stayed late almost every Friday revising our pitch decks and keeping warm contact with Haiyue's Shanghai managers. Seeing it pay off with a 120 million yuan deal makes all those sleepless nights worthwhile."

He nodded graciously toward the clerks like a senior executive accepting praise. "Keep up the effort, everyone. If our department keeps delivering like this, maybe next spring General Manager Sun will finally have someone reliable to share his workload as Deputy General Manager."

Behind Hu Dewei's back, Pan Xiulan nearly choked on her tea. She quickly hid her mouth behind a file folder, her shoulders shaking with silent laughter. Beside her, Zheng Qian bit the inside of his cheek so hard his face turned pink, staring at his shoes so he would not burst out laughing.

Everyone on the twelfth floor knew Hu Dewei spent his Friday nights playing cards at a tea house near Nanjing Road.

Pan Xiulan lowered her folder, wearing her most innocent smile. "Oh, Manager Hu, you really are amazing! Since you worked so closely with Haiyue Group, maybe you can teach us. Which berth at Shenzhen Pier Nine was their urgent cargo stuck at last week? And what was the surname of their Shenzhen senior director who signed the emergency release?"

The aisle dropped into awkward silence.

Hu Dewei's smug smile froze stiff. He blinked rapidly behind his gold-rimmed glasses, sweat breaking out across his forehead.

"Well, Sister Pan, you know how high-level business works," Hu Dewei stammered, waving a hand vaguely. "I focus on big-picture strategy here in Shanghai. Minor dock numbers and branch names in Shenzhen are small details for ground clerks."

"Is that so?" Pan Xiulan murmured sweetly, hiding another smirk.

Sun Haoran let out a hearty, amused laugh, patting the blue folder. "We all appreciate your enthusiasm, Old Hu. Still, I should be fair and read the exact note Haiyue Group's Shenzhen senior office attached to this contract."

Sun Haoran flipped open the cover and read aloud clearly. "They wrote that they doubled Hengxin's contract specifically because of our calm on-site handling during last week's cargo jam at Shenzhen Pier Nine. And they named one person directly for saving their shipment."

Sun Haoran closed the folder, walked straight past Hu Dewei, and stopped in front of Chen Xuan's wooden desk.

He clapped a warm, heavy hand onto Chen Xuan's shoulder. "Chen Xuan! Haiyue's Shenzhen director praised your paperwork and calm coordination to the skies. You barely made a sound when you got back, and yet you brought home a 120 million yuan contract!"

Hu Dewei's face turned dull purple in half a second. His mouth opened, closed, and twisted into a stiff, painful smile as twenty pairs of eyes swung toward Chen Xuan.

"I did not do much, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said calmly, standing up with a modest smile. "I just checked the shipping lists, ran the forms to the right office at Shenzhen Pier Nine, and kept the trucks moving."

"Not a drop of arrogance!" Sun Haoran laughed proudly. "Hengxin Logistics rewards real work, not loud talk. Effective today, Chen Xuan gets a twenty thousand yuan special bonus, and lunch for the entire twelfth floor is on the company!"

Fresh cheers broke out across the floor. Zheng Qian gave Chen Xuan two enthusiastic thumbs-up, while Pan Xiulan shot Hu Dewei a sidelong glance that sent the plump man muttering about an urgent call and retreating straight into his glass cubicle.

Once Sun Haoran strolled back into his office, Zheng Qian leaned across the partition grinning wide.

"Brother Chen, did you see Hu Dewei's face?" Zheng Qian whispered gleefully. "He looked like he swallowed a whole raw lemon!"

"Honestly, Chen Xuan," Pan Xiulan said warmly, setting a stack of folders on his desk. "You are too quiet for your own good. Most people would have marched straight into Sun Haoran's office bragging on Friday."

"Bragging takes too much energy," Chen Xuan replied lightly, sitting down to sip his hot tea. "Getting the work done cleanly is enough."

"Well, twenty thousand yuan is a nice bonus," Zheng Qian teased. "Remember your promise about milk tea?"

"After lunch, my treat," Chen Xuan smiled. "Order whatever toppings you want."

As the office settled back into the clatter of keyboards, Chen Xuan's personal phone buzzed softly on his desk.

He picked it up and saw a short message from Qin Yuhan.

*Are you free at noon? There is a small braised beef noodle shop tucked right behind Jin Mao Tower. My treat, to celebrate your big morning at Hengxin Logistics.*

Chen Xuan smiled faintly and tapped out a quick reply.

*Free at twelve. Save me a seat by the window.*

He set the phone down beside his mug and opened the first folder on his desk. Monday in Shanghai had started nicely enough.
