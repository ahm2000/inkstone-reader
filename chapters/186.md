# Chapter 186: Back at Hengxin Logistics

The next morning, the wall radiators on the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics hissed softly, keeping the operations room warm and drowsy against the river wind outside. Clerks hunched over their monitors with steaming mugs, easing back into the start of the workweek.

Chen Xuan stepped out of the elevator at 8:25 AM, wearing a plain dark wool jacket over a gray knit sweater. In his left hand swung his dented 15-yuan green metal thermos filled with hot jasmine tea, while around his neck sat a soft charcoal scarf that Qin Yuhan had insisted on wrapping around him at the front door of Emerald Riverside Villa. She had even stood on her tiptoes to straighten his collar, reminding him that winter wind near the river was no joke.

"Look who finally came back from the warm south," Sister Pan Xiulan called out cheerfully from her desk near the printer. She held up a ceramic mug of chrysanthemum tea and squinted at him with amused warmth. "Shenzhen treated you well, Xiao Chen. You even look like you gained half a kilo."

"Good dim sum down there, Sister Pan," Chen Xuan said with an easy smile, hanging his jacket on the back of his chair and setting the green thermos next to his keyboard. "I brought back two boxes of almond biscuits for the office. They are in the pantry."

A murmur of happy thanks rippled across the nearby cubicles. Two rows over, Hu Dewei leaned back in his swivel chair, sipping coffee with a smug expression. He wore a thick knitted vest and looked completely relaxed.

"Well, someone had to stay behind and keep the Shanghai dispatch lines from collapsing while you were strolling around Shenzhen," Hu Dewei said loudly, tapping his pen against a stack of folders. "You young guys think business trips are all work, when really the pressure is right here at headquarters."

Sister Pan Xiulan rolled her eyes and opened her mouth to reply, but the frosted glass door of the corner office swung open first.

General Manager Sun Haoran walked out beaming from ear to ear, holding a thick blue contract folder against his chest. He clapped his hands twice to draw everyone's attention.

"Everyone, pause for two minutes," Sun Haoran announced, his voice bright with genuine pride. "I have some great news to start the week. Haiyue Group just signed our new annual logistics agreement this morning. Not only did they renew early, they doubled our freight allocation from 60 million yuan to 120 million yuan."

A loud cheer broke out across the 12th floor. Several clerks sat up straight, exchanging excited glances. For a mid-sized logistics firm like Hengxin, a 120 million yuan anchor contract meant fat year-end bonuses and steady business all through the coming year.

Sun Haoran held up a hand to quiet the applause and looked straight toward the corner desk.

"Haiyue Group's southern operations director specifically praised how smoothly our Shenzhen customs and warehouse handover went last month," Sun Haoran said, smiling warmly at Chen Xuan. "Zero delays, zero damaged pallets, and clean paperwork from start to finish. Chen Xuan, you did solid work down there. The company is awarding you a 20,000-yuan special performance bonus, straight into this month's payroll. And this Friday evening, dinner for the whole 12th floor is on the company."

"Thank you, Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said calmly, nodding with polite modesty. "It was mostly standard coordination. The Shenzhen warehouse crew did the heavy lifting."

"Too humble as always," Sun Haoran laughed. "Keep that steady pace."

Before Sun Haoran could step back into his office, Sister Pan Xiulan leaned sideways in her chair, resting her chin on her hand as she looked straight at Hu Dewei with a sparkling, mischievous grin.

"Aiya, Old Hu, what a pity," Sister Pan said in a loud, theatrical sigh that carried across the entire floor. "Originally that Shenzhen assignment had your name on the top line, did it not?"

Hu Dewei's smug smile froze instantly. "Well, Sister Pan, my lower back had an acute flare-up the night before the flight. You know how serious lumbar strain can be."

"Oh, I know how serious it was," Sister Pan replied smoothly, sipping her tea. "You walked out of this office on Friday afternoon clutching your waist and groaning like an eighty-year-old man who could barely reach the elevator. Yet somehow, on Saturday morning, my nephew saw you jumping around the badminton court at the community sports center, smashing shuttlecocks like a provincial champion."

Dead silence lasted for half a second, and then the entire 12th floor erupted into laughter.

Even General Manager Sun Haoran paused at his doorway, his shoulders shaking as he tried to keep a polite straight face. Hu Dewei's ears turned bright crimson.

"That was, that was prescribed stretching!" Hu Dewei stammered, furiously shuffling blank papers on his desk. "A traditional massage therapist told me gentle arm movement loosens the spine!"

"Gentle arm movement for two straight hours, plus winning three doubles matches," Sister Pan teased, waving her hand. "Next time you want to skip a tiring trip, just say so. At least Chen Xuan walked away with a 20,000-yuan bonus for his trouble."

More chuckles echoed across the cubicles as Hu Dewei ducked behind his computer monitor, muttering about people meddling in medical recovery. Sun Haoran shook his head with a fond grin, told everyone to get back to their morning manifests, and closed his office door.

Chen Xuan sat back comfortably in his chair, unscrewed the lid of his dented green thermos, and took a slow sip of hot jasmine tea. His single regular smartphone vibrated softly on the desk beside his mouse.

He picked it up and glanced at the screen. A text message from his wife, Qin Yuhan, had just arrived.

"Heard a certain 12,000-yuan logistics clerk just earned a big bonus this morning. Free for noodles behind Jin Mao Tower at noon?"

Chen Xuan smiled quietly to himself and typed back with one thumb.

"Always free for my wife. I will save the table by the window."

He set the phone down, opened the first batch of December freight schedules on his screen, and settled happily into his quiet morning routine.
