# Chapter 42: Shengyuan Capital's Midnight Collapse

At twenty minutes past two on Monday morning, the twin turbines of the private helicopter on Lanshui's hilltop heliport whined down to a hollow cough and died. The green instrument panel inside the cockpit went completely dark.

"Restart it! Restart it right now, Zhao Kun!" Shengyuan Capital Chairman Fang Qiming hammered his palm against the open cabin door, a crumpled cigarette whipping sparks between his left fingers. "I pay you two million yuan a year to keep this bird flying! Why is the battery bus dead?"

Inside the cockpit, Pilot Zhao Kun stared at the blank avionics screens, knuckles pale on the stick. "Chairman Fang Qiming, both main batteries and the starter read zero voltage at the exact same second. Even the transponder is fried. Something hit the pad with a remote kill switch."

"Move! Get out of the way!" Provincial Vice Governor Lin Zhengde shoved past Fang Qiming, spectacles fogging from his ragged breath. He gripped a leather briefcase against his hip and spun straight into Fang Qiming's face. "You told me this heliport was off the provincial aviation grid! You told me we would cross the coast before three!"

"Do not point your finger at my nose, Lin Zhengde!" Fang Qiming slapped the hand away, eyes wide and furious. He took a violent drag from his cigarette and blew smoke past Lin Zhengde's ear. "My helicopter did not die by accident! Somebody locked down Lanshui's airspace five minutes after your office secretary stopped answering his phone!"

"My secretary?" Lin Zhengde's face flushed mottled purple under the tarmac beacons. "You dare blame my office? Every alarm tonight traced straight back to your greedy little empire in Yunxi County!"

Fang Qiming let out a sharp, brittle laugh. "Yunxi County? You think a provincial aviation freeze happened because of a county development zone?"

"I warned you six months ago!" Lin Zhengde shouted, stepping so close spittle flecked his lips. "I told you to keep Shengyuan Capital clean on the surface! I told you not to let local dogs like Deng Yongchang tear up farmland, rig county bids, and beat citizens in broad daylight! You got greedy, Fang Qiming! You wanted the rare-earth tailings, you wanted the industrial park bonds, and you let Deng Yongchang run Yunxi like a bandit stronghold until somebody above Beijing finally looked down!"

"Listen to the saintly Vice Governor!" Fang Qiming sneered, jaw clenched so tight his teeth ground. He jabbed his burning cigarette toward Lin Zhengde's chest. "Who took forty percent of every land-transfer fee out of Yunxi County? Who demanded six hundred million yuan in provincial treasury bonds routed through Shengyuan Capital's offshore clearing desk before signing the Qingyan Port export waivers? You, Lin Zhengde!"

Lin Zhengde's eyes widened in panic. He glanced toward the cockpit where Pilot Zhao Kun sat frozen with lifted hands. "Shut your mouth! Are you insane, shouting that in the open?"

"Why whisper now?" Fang Qiming yelled, his composure cracking as the perimeter floodlights snapped off one by one, leaving only low red edge lamps along the pad. "Your brother-in-law's shell trust in Zurich, Helvetia Meridian Four, sits on three billion yuan of my clearing money! You signed the customs exemptions with your own seal! You took twenty-two million yuan in gold bars after the Lanshui highway tender! If I drown tonight, Lin Zhengde, I am tying your ankles to my neck!"

"You bastard!" Lin Zhengde lunged forward and seized the lapels of Fang Qiming's overcoat, shaking him hard. "I can bury Shengyuan Capital with one emergency decree before dawn! I will tell the provincial committee you forged my signature on the Qingyan waivers! Who will they believe, a Vice Governor or a profiteer caught fleeing on a private tarmac?"

"Try it!" Fang Qiming grabbed Lin Zhengde's wrists, his face twisted in rage. "I kept every voice recording from our dinners at Lakeview Villa! I kept every handwritten slip you passed across the mahjong table! You are not walking out of this alive!"

Fang Qiming's voice cut off abruptly as his eyes flicked past Lin Zhengde's shoulder and his throat locked shut.

From the hangar shadows, eight figures stepped onto the tarmac in a smooth half-circle. They wore featureless matte-black gear with blank, light-absorbing visors. No rifles were raised, no sirens wailed from the road below. They sealed the heliport in suffocating silence.

Pilot Zhao Kun unbuckled his harness, climbed down with both hands raised, and knelt without being told.

Lin Zhengde's fingers slipped off Fang Qiming's coat. His briefcase slapped onto the concrete. He swallowed hard, forcing a stiff, trembling posture as he stared at the nearest matte-black figure.

"Who authorized this?" Lin Zhengde demanded, his voice cracking on the second word. "I am Lin Zhengde, Vice Governor of this province! Identify your commanding officer immediately! You have no legal right to detain a sitting vice-ministerial cadre on an unannounced sweep!"

Fang Qiming dropped his cigarette and crushed it under his heel, breathing shallow and fast. He forced a smooth, trembling smile as sweat ran down his neck. "Gentlemen, whatever misunderstanding brought you here, Shengyuan Capital cooperates fully with all inquiries. Let us step inside the lounge and call Secretary Zhou Zhenhua directly to clear this up."

The lead figure at the center did not turn toward the lounge or acknowledge Zhou Zhenhua's name.

When it spoke, the voice was flat, neutral, and utterly genderless, carrying an icy stillness that cut straight through the hilltop wind.

"Fang Qiming. Shengyuan Capital offshore clearing ledger code SY-09-ZRH. Unaccounted capital outflow routed to Zurich Northern Ledger: sixty-one billion, four hundred million yuan."

Fang Qiming's forced smile vanished as if wiped off by a cold cloth. His knees went weak, and he staggered half a step back against the helicopter's fuselage.

The flat, neutral, genderless voice shifted without pausing. "Lin Zhengde. Secondary signatory hash on Helvetia Meridian Four and Qingyan Port rare-earth customs waiver batch forty-seven. Both files are verified against the Shanghai relay mirror."

Lin Zhengde's spectacles slid down his nose. He did not lift a hand to fix them. His face turned ash-grey as the shock hit him: the Shanghai relay was supposed to be invisible to every agency in the country.

"Wait," Lin Zhengde choked out, his legs buckling until he braced one hand on his knee. "I was coerced! Fang Qiming threatened my family if I did not sign those waivers! I have records in my briefcase that prove it!"

"You lying dog!" Fang Qiming hissed, eyes bloodshot and wild, his voice shrunk to a terrified croak. "He asked for thirty percent! He called me every Friday asking when the Zurich wire would settle!"

"Silence," the flat, neutral, genderless voice said.

It was neither loud nor angry, yet both men snapped their jaws shut instantly.

"Neither of you holds anything left to trade," the voice stated evenly. "Secure Fang Qiming and Lin Zhengde. Confiscate the briefcase and the flight-data recorder. Transfer both detainees to central holding."

Four matte-black operatives stepped forward in unison. Lin Zhengde let out a broken whimper as cuffs locked around his wrists. Fang Qiming stared straight ahead, shoulders slumped and eyes hollow, as the silent figures marched them off the darkened hilltop into waiting black vans.
