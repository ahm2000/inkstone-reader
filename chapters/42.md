# Chapter 42: Shengyuan Capital's Midnight Collapse

At twenty minutes past two on Monday morning, the two engines of the private helicopter on Lanshui's hilltop pad slowed down with a hollow cough and died. The green lights on the dashboard went completely dark.

"Restart it! Restart it right now, Zhao Kun!" Shengyuan Capital Chairman Fang Qiming slapped his palm against the open cabin door. A bent cigarette threw sparks between his left fingers. "I pay you 2 million yuan a year to keep this bird flying! Why is the main battery dead?"

Inside the cockpit, Pilot Zhao Kun stared at the dark flight screens, his knuckles white on the stick. "Chairman Fang Qiming, both main batteries and the starter dropped to zero power at the exact same second. Even the radio signal is dead. Something hit the pad with a remote kill switch."

"Move! Get out of the way!" Provincial Vice Governor Lin Zhengde shoved past Fang Qiming, his glasses fogging from his fast, ragged breath. He held a leather briefcase against his hip and spun right into Fang Qiming's face. "You told me this landing pad was off the province's flight map! You told me we would cross the coast before three!"

"Do not point your finger at my nose, Lin Zhengde!" Fang Qiming slapped his hand away, eyes wide and furious. He took a hard drag from his cigarette and blew smoke past Lin Zhengde's ear. "My helicopter did not die by accident! Somebody locked down Lanshui's skies five minutes after your secretary stopped answering his phone!"

"My secretary?" Lin Zhengde's face turned blotchy purple under the pad lights. "You dare blame my office? Every alarm tonight traced straight back to your greedy little empire in Yunxi County!"

Fang Qiming let out a sharp, bitter laugh. "Yunxi County? You think a province-wide flight ban happened because of a county building zone?"

"I warned you six months ago!" Lin Zhengde shouted, stepping so close spit hit the air. "I told you to keep Shengyuan Capital clean on the surface! I told you not to let local dogs like Deng Yongchang tear up farmland, rig county bids, and beat regular people in broad daylight! You got greedy, Fang Qiming! You wanted the rare-earth mine waste, you wanted the factory park loans, and you let Deng Yongchang run Yunxi like a gang camp until somebody above Beijing finally looked down!"

"Listen to the saintly Vice Governor!" Fang Qiming sneered, jaw clenched so tight his teeth ground together. He pointed his burning cigarette at Lin Zhengde's chest. "Who took forty percent of every land fee out of Yunxi County? Who demanded 600 million yuan in province money sent through Shengyuan Capital's overseas bank desk before signing the Qingyan Port export permits? You, Lin Zhengde!"

Lin Zhengde's eyes widened in panic. He glanced toward the cockpit where Pilot Zhao Kun sat frozen with raised hands. "Shut your mouth! Are you crazy, shouting that out in the open?"

"Why whisper now?" Fang Qiming yelled. His calm cracked as the big lights around the pad snapped off one by one, leaving only low red edge lamps. "Your brother-in-law's shell company in Zurich, Helvetia Meridian Four, sits on 3 billion yuan of my bank money! You signed the customs passes with your own seal! You took 22 million yuan in gold bars after the Lanshui highway bid! If I drown tonight, Lin Zhengde, I am tying your ankles to my neck!"

"You bastard!" Lin Zhengde lunged forward and grabbed the front of Fang Qiming's coat, shaking him hard. "I can bury Shengyuan Capital with one emergency order before dawn! I will tell the provincial leaders you forged my name on the Qingyan passes! Who will they believe, a Vice Governor or a crook caught running on a private pad?"

"Try it!" Fang Qiming grabbed Lin Zhengde's wrists, his face twisted with rage. "I kept every voice recording from our dinners at Lakeview Villa! I kept every note you passed across the mahjong table! You are not walking out of this alive!"

Fang Qiming's voice cut off all at once. His eyes flicked past Lin Zhengde's shoulder and his throat locked shut.

From the hangar shadows, eight figures stepped onto the concrete in a smooth half-circle. They wore plain black gear with blank visors that gave back no light. No rifles were raised, and no sirens sounded on the road below. They sealed the hilltop pad in heavy silence.

Pilot Zhao Kun unbuckled his belt, climbed down with both hands up, and knelt without being told.

Lin Zhengde's fingers slipped off Fang Qiming's coat. His briefcase slapped onto the ground. He swallowed hard and forced his trembling body straight as he stared at the nearest black-clad figure.

"Who ordered this?" Lin Zhengde demanded, his voice cracking on the second word. "I am Lin Zhengde, Vice Governor of this province! Name your commanding officer right now! You have no legal right to hold a sitting vice-ministerial official in a surprise raid!"

Fang Qiming dropped his cigarette and crushed it under his heel, breathing fast and shallow. He forced a smooth, shaking smile as sweat ran down his neck. "Gentlemen, whatever mistake brought you here, Shengyuan Capital will work with you fully. Let us step inside the lounge and call Secretary Zhou Zhenhua directly to clear this up."

The lead figure at the center did not turn toward the lounge or react to Zhou Zhenhua's name.

The guard spoke once, cutting straight through the hilltop wind.

"Fang Qiming. Shengyuan Capital overseas account code SY-09-ZRH. Missing money sent to the Zurich Northern Ledger: 61.4 billion yuan."

Fang Qiming's forced smile vanished as if wiped off by a cold rag. His knees went weak, and he staggered half a step back against the side of the helicopter.

The guard went on without a pause. "Lin Zhengde. Second signature record on Helvetia Meridian Four and Qingyan Port rare-earth customs pass batch forty-seven. Both files match the Shanghai relay copy."

Lin Zhengde's glasses slid down his nose. He did not lift a hand to fix them. His face turned ash-grey as the shock hit him. The Shanghai relay was supposed to be invisible to every agency in the country.

"Wait," Lin Zhengde choked out, his legs giving way until he braced one hand on his knee. "I was forced! Fang Qiming threatened my family if I did not sign those passes! I have papers in my briefcase that prove it!"

"You lying dog!" Fang Qiming hissed, eyes bloodshot and wild, his voice down to a terrified croak. "He asked for thirty percent! He called me every Friday asking when the Zurich money would land!"

"Silence," the guard said.

It was neither loud nor angry, yet both men snapped their mouths shut at once.

"Neither of you has anything left to trade," the voice said evenly. "Take Fang Qiming and Lin Zhengde. Seize the briefcase and the flight recorder. Move both men to central holding."

Four black-clad operatives stepped forward together. Lin Zhengde let out a broken whimper as cuffs locked around his wrists. Fang Qiming stared straight ahead, shoulders slumped and eyes hollow, as the silent figures marched them off the dark hilltop into waiting black vans.
