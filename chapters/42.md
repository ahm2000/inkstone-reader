# Chapter 42: Shanshui Group's Midnight Collapse

At twenty minutes past two on Monday morning, the two engines of the private helicopter on Jingzhou City's hilltop pad slowed down with a hollow cough and died. The green lights on the dashboard went completely dark.

"Restart it! Restart it right now, Zhao Kun!" Shanshui Group Zhao Ruilong slapped his palm against the open cabin door. A bent cigarette threw sparks between his left fingers. "I pay you 200,000 yuan a year to keep this bird flying! Why is the main battery dead?"

Inside the cockpit, Pilot Zhao Kun stared at the dark flight screens, his knuckles white on the stick. "Zhao Ruilong, both main batteries and the starter dropped to zero power in the same second. Even the radio signal is dead. Something hit the pad with a remote kill switch."

"Move! Get out of the way!" Provincial Deputy Secretary Gao Yuliang shoved past Zhao Ruilong, his glasses fogging from his fast, ragged breath. He held a leather briefcase against his hip and spun right into Zhao Ruilong's face. "You told me this landing pad was off the province's flight map! You told me we would cross the coast before three!"

"Do not point your finger at my nose, Gao Yuliang!" Zhao Ruilong slapped his hand away, eyes wide and furious. He took a hard drag from his cigarette and blew smoke past Gao Yuliang's ear. "My helicopter did not die by accident! Somebody locked down Jingzhou City's skies five minutes after your secretary stopped answering his phone!"

"My secretary?" Gao Yuliang's face turned blotchy purple under the pad lights. "You dare blame my office? Every alarm tonight traced straight back to your greedy little empire in Yunxi County!"

Zhao Ruilong let out a sharp, bitter laugh. "Yunxi County? You think a province-wide flight ban happened because of a county building zone?"

"I warned you six months ago!" Gao Yuliang shouted, stepping so close spit hit the air. "I told you to keep Shanshui Group clean on the surface! I told you not to let local dogs like Deng Yongchang tear up farmland, rig county bids, and beat regular people in broad daylight! You got greedy, Zhao Ruilong! You wanted the rare-earth mine waste, you wanted the factory park loans, and you let Deng Yongchang run Yunxi like a gang camp until somebody above Beijing finally looked down!"

"Listen to the saintly Vice Governor!" Zhao Ruilong sneered, jaw clenched so tight his teeth ground together. He pointed his burning cigarette at Gao Yuliang's chest. "Who took forty percent of every land fee out of Yunxi County? Who demanded 600 million yuan in province money sent through Shanshui Group's overseas bank desk before signing the Shanghai Port export permits? You, Gao Yuliang!"

Gao Yuliang's eyes widened in panic. He glanced toward the cockpit where Pilot Zhao Kun sat frozen with raised hands. "Shut your mouth! Are you crazy, shouting that out in the open?"

"Why whisper now?" Zhao Ruilong yelled. His calm cracked as the big lights around the pad snapped off one by one, leaving only low red edge lamps. "Your brother-in-law's shell company in Zurich, Helvetia Meridian Four, sits on 3 billion yuan of my bank money! You signed the customs passes with your own seal! You took 22 million yuan in gold bars after the Jingzhou City highway bid! If I drown tonight, Gao Yuliang, I am tying your ankles to my neck!"

"You bastard!" Gao Yuliang lunged forward and grabbed the front of Zhao Ruilong's coat, shaking him hard. "I can bury Shanshui Group with one emergency order before dawn! I will tell the provincial leaders you forged my name on the Shanghai passes! Who will they believe, a Vice Governor or a crook caught running on a private pad?"

"Try it!" Zhao Ruilong grabbed Gao Yuliang's wrists, his face twisted with rage. "I kept every voice recording from our dinners at Lakeview Villa! I kept every note you passed across the mahjong table! You are not walking out of this alive!"

Zhao Ruilong's voice cut off all at once. His eyes flicked past Gao Yuliang's shoulder and his throat locked shut.

From the hangar shadows, eight figures stepped onto the concrete in a smooth half-circle. They wore plain black gear with blank visors that gave back no light. No rifles were raised, and no sirens sounded on the road below. They sealed the hilltop pad in heavy silence.

Pilot Zhao Kun unbuckled his belt, climbed down with both hands up, and knelt without being told.

Gao Yuliang's fingers slipped off Zhao Ruilong's coat. His briefcase slapped onto the ground. He swallowed hard and stared at the nearest black-clad figure.

"Who ordered this?" Gao Yuliang demanded, his voice cracking on the second word. "I am Gao Yuliang, Vice Governor of this province! Name your commanding officer right now! You have no legal right to hold a sitting vice-ministerial official in a surprise raid!"

Zhao Ruilong dropped his cigarette and crushed it under his heel. He forced a smooth, shaking smile as sweat ran down his neck. "Gentlemen, whatever mistake brought you here, Shanshui Group will work with you fully. Let us step inside the lounge and call Secretary Zhou Zhenhua directly to clear this up."

The lead figure at the center did not turn toward the lounge or react to Zhou Zhenhua's name.

The guard spoke once, cutting straight through the hilltop wind.

"Zhao Ruilong. Shanshui Group overseas account code SY-09-ZRH. Missing money sent to the Zurich Northern Ledger: 61.4 billion yuan."

Zhao Ruilong's forced smile vanished. His knees went weak, and he staggered half a step back against the side of the helicopter.

The guard went on without a pause. "Gao Yuliang. Second signature record on Helvetia Meridian Four and Shanghai Port rare-earth customs pass batch forty-seven. Both files match the Shanghai relay copy."

Gao Yuliang's glasses slid down his nose. He did not lift a hand to fix them. His face turned ash-grey as the shock hit him. The Shanghai relay was supposed to be invisible to every agency in the country.

"Wait," Gao Yuliang choked out, his legs giving way until he braced one hand on his knee. "I was forced! Zhao Ruilong threatened my family if I did not sign those passes! I have papers in my briefcase that prove it!"

"You lying dog!" Zhao Ruilong hissed, eyes bloodshot and wild, his voice down to a terrified croak. "He asked for thirty percent! He called me every Friday asking when the Zurich money would land!"

"Silence," the guard said.

It was neither loud nor angry, yet both men snapped their mouths shut at once.

"Neither of you has anything left to trade," the voice said evenly. "Take Zhao Ruilong and Gao Yuliang. Seize the briefcase and the flight recorder. Move both men to central holding."

Four black-clad operatives stepped forward together. Gao Yuliang let out a broken whimper as cuffs locked around his wrists. Zhao Ruilong stared straight ahead, shoulders slumped and eyes hollow, as the silent figures marched them off the dark hilltop into waiting black vans.
