# Chapter 132: Corridor Fourteen

While half of Jingzhou's officialdom braced for a sleepless night, Suite 1408 remained calm and warm.

Qin Yuhan sat cross-legged on the bed in blue cotton pajamas while Chen Xuan stood behind her, blotting her damp hair with a towel before switching on the hair dryer.

"Tilt your chin up a little, President Qin," he said lightly, combing smoothly through her hair. "If your neck stays damp, you will be sneezing through breakfast."

She obeyed like a sleepy cat, closing her eyes. "My father praised you three times at dinner. When I told him how Ding Yizhen tried to corner us in the east hallway, he said you kept a cooler head than half the board directors."

"Your father has sharp judgment," Chen Xuan chuckled, switching off the dryer. "Tell him I will cook a proper meal next time he visits Pudong."

Qin Yuhan laughed, then spotted the black leather folder on the desk. "Oh, my father asked for the revised harbor blueprint before his morning meeting. His suite is just down the hall in 1402."

"I will walk over with you," Chen Xuan said, slipping into his sneakers.

They stepped out into quiet Corridor Fourteen, lined with warm wall lamps and burgundy carpet.

They had walked barely ten paces when the far elevator chimed. The polished doors slid open, and four broad-shouldered men in dark windbreakers stepped out empty-handed. The leader wore a thick gold chain beneath his collar and carried a jagged scar above his left brow. He spotted the couple at once and smirked, waving his three companions forward to block the width of the corridor.

Qin Yuhan halted, her grip tightening around the leather folder. Chen Xuan stepped smoothly half a pace ahead of her, his hands tucked loosely in his hoodie pockets, his expression completely calm.

"Evening, President Qin," the scarred man drawled, rolling his shoulders and cracking his knuckles. "A friend in Jingzhou thinks you and your husband are a little too stubborn. He asked us to have a quiet chat so you remember to pack your bags early tomorrow."

"This is a provincial guest villa," Qin Yuhan said sharply, keeping her voice steady. "Take one more step and I am calling hotel security and the police."

"Security is enjoying a long coffee break downstairs," the scarred man chuckled, reaching a heavy hand out toward Chen Xuan's shoulder. "Step aside, little clerk. This will not take long."

Before his thick fingers even brushed Chen Xuan's sleeve, two wooden suite doors on opposite sides of Corridor Fourteen swung open at the exact same second.

From Suite 1406 stepped a cheerful, round-bellied middle-aged uncle in a faded knit vest and plaid pajama pants, clutching a warm paper bag of roasted chestnuts in one hand and a chipped ceramic mug in the other.

"Hey there, neighbors!" the uncle beamed brightly, looking every bit like a friendly tourist out for a late snack. "The little stall by the lake gate still had hot chestnuts! Want a handful?"

From Suite 1405 stepped a smiling young woman in oversized fleece loungewear and fuzzy bunny slippers, carrying a stack of folded towels and a small open box of bright tangerines.

"Excuse me, coming through!" she chimed warmly. "My cousin spilled hot tea by the doorway, and these tangerines keep rolling everywhere!"

Neither of them looked tense, guarded, or remotely trained. They looked completely like two relaxed, cheerful hotel guests wandering the hallway before bed.

Yet the chubby uncle took one casual step forward, seemed to catch his slipper edge on the carpet seam, and lurched smoothly sideways. His round shoulder bumped the scarred leader's hip at a tiny, effortless angle.

The burly man grunted in shock as both boots flew right out from under him, sending him spinning sideways into the paneled wall with a heavy thud.

"Oh dear, watch your footing, young man!" the uncle cried warmly, stepping forward as if to steady him while planting his heel neatly across the second tough's ankle, tipping him straight over his leader's tangled legs.

At the exact same heartbeat, four round tangerines tumbled from the young woman's box and rolled directly beneath the soles of the third and fourth thugs. Both men windmilled wildly for balance. Bending down with a bright, apologetic smile to scoop up the fruit, her elbow nudged the fourth man's knee just enough to fold him straight onto the burgundy carpet.

"Goodness, I am so sorry!" she exclaimed cheerfully. "Are your shoes slippery? This hotel carpet really needs a good brushing!"

In four seconds flat, all four hired toughs were tangled in a groaning, bewildered heap amid scattered tangerines and the sweet scent of roasted chestnuts.

The chubby uncle grinned warmly at Chen Xuan and pressed a generous handful of hot chestnuts into his palm.

"Take your wife back inside, young man," the uncle said kindly. "The hall gets chilly this late, and these fellows clearly had way too much to drink."

Chen Xuan smiled easily, feeling the quiet, natural warmth of the Zero Guard around him. "Thank you, Uncle. Good night."

Just then, the far elevator chimed again. Hou Liangping strode out briskly at the head of six uniformed provincial officers and local police.

Hou Liangping blinked in brief surprise at the four burly men tangled among tangerines and chestnuts, then flashed his badge crisply.

"Anti-Corruption Bureau and Municipal Police," Hou Liangping announced. "We tracked one of Ding Yizhen's aides to the lobby twenty minutes ago and got word of hired men heading up to this floor. Cuff all four of them."

The officers hauled the stunned thugs upright and snapped steel cuffs onto their wrists. Hou Liangping walked over and gave Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan a respectful nod.

"President Qin, and sir," Hou Liangping said politely, having zero clue who Chen Xuan really was. "I apologize for the disturbance. We have secured a full arrest warrant for the people behind this. Please rest easy tonight."

"Thank you, Chief Hou," Qin Yuhan breathed in relief as the police marched the four groaning men back toward the elevator.

After dropping off the blueprint with her father and returning to Suite 1408, Qin Yuhan locked the door and let out a delighted laugh.

"Did you see that?" she marveled, taking a warm chestnut Chen Xuan peeled for her. "That nice uncle and that girl in bunny slippers had the best accidental timing I have ever seen!"

"Good neighbors make all the difference," Chen Xuan smiled quietly, tossing the shell into the bin. "Now eat up and get some sleep. Tomorrow will be a much nicer morning."
