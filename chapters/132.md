# Chapter 132: Corridor Fourteen

By ten o'clock that evening, a cool autumn breeze blew across Jingzhou Lake and tapped softly against the fourteenth-floor windows at Shanshui Villa.

Inside Suite 1408, the mood was warm and relaxed.

Qin Yuhan sat cross-legged in the middle of the big bed wearing soft blue cotton pajamas, her dark hair still damp. Chen Xuan stood behind her with a warm towel, gently drying her long hair while an old comedy played on the muted television screen.

"Tilt your chin up a bit, President Qin," Chen Xuan said lightly, draping the towel around her shoulders and reaching for the hair dryer. "Otherwise your neck stays damp, and tomorrow morning you will sneeze all the way to breakfast."

Qin Yuhan obeyed like a sleepy cat, lifting her chin and closing her eyes as the warm air hummed softly over her hair.

"My father praised you three times at dinner tonight," Qin Yuhan murmured with a happy smile. "He even told my mother on the phone that marrying an unbothered clerk was good for my temper. If you get any prouder, your tail will knock the lamp off the table."

"Father-in-law has sharp judgment," Chen Xuan chuckled, running his fingers gently through her dark locks until they were dry. "Look at me. I pour your tea, dry your hair, guard your coat from grumpy deputy mayors, and only cost you a bowl of beef noodles a day. Where else in Shanghai could Haiyue Group hire an assistant with that kind of value?"

Qin Yuhan switched off the dryer when he finished, turned around on her knees, and pinched his cheek playfully.

"You ate three plates of braised lake fish at my father's table tonight," she teased, laughing softly. "That is a whole week of beef noodles right there."

"Growing boys need protein," Chen Xuan replied solemnly, kissing her knuckles. "Come on, under the blanket. You stood in high heels for four hours today and talked down half a hall of shipping bosses. Time to sleep."

Qin Yuhan smiled, slid down under the fluffy white duvet, and rested her cheek against Chen Xuan's shoulder.

"My black briefcase is on the desk by the door," she mumbled sleepily, her eyelids fluttering shut. "It has my annotated copy of the alliance contract and the notes Father gave me for the morning review. Did you double-lock the latch?"

"Double-locked and chained," Chen Xuan promised softly, pulling the quilt up to her chin and patting her shoulder. "Even a mosquito would need an appointment to get through that door. Close your eyes."

Within three minutes, Qin Yuhan's breathing turned deep and steady against his chest.

Chen Xuan clicked off the bedside lamp and closed his eyes with one arm around his wife's waist.

Three floors below, inside a suite on the eleventh floor, Li Weiming paced across the carpet with his glasses clutched tightly in one fist.

After Sha Ruijin shut down the signing session that afternoon, two unmarked sedans had parked near the gate. Li Weiming knew an investigation was closing in. If Qin Yuhan handed her annotated research notes and witness list to the provincial team tomorrow, his partners in Zurich would cut him loose before noon.

"Suite 1408," Li Weiming hissed to four thick-necked men standing by the bar in maintenance jackets. Each man carried a rubber baton and a steel lock wedge. "Her father moved to the government guesthouse across the street an hour ago. Only the woman and her useless clerk husband are inside."

The tallest thug, a scarred former dock enforcer named Scar Liu, tapped his baton against his palm. "What about the cameras, Mr. Li?"

"Cai Chenggong's security supervisor looped the fourteenth-floor camera feed for fifteen minutes," Li Weiming said harshly. "Go up the west stairs, pop the lock with the wedge, grab the black briefcase off the desk, and smash their phones. If the clerk wakes up, knock him out. Just get me that folder before midnight!"

"Ten minutes, Mr. Li," Scar Liu grinned darkly.

At 11:42 PM, the fire door of the fourteenth-floor stairwell clicked open.

Scar Liu and his three men slipped out onto the thick carpet in rubber-soled boots. The corridor was quiet, lit only by amber wall lamps. Suite 1408 sat forty-five meters down the hall.

Scar Liu raised two fingers and pointed toward Suite 1408.

His three men crept forward in a line, pulling black masks over their noses while Scar Liu drew the lock wedge.

They took five silent steps past the ice machine alcove.

Then two figures stepped quietly out from the shadow of the service doorway right behind them.

Both men wore plain black gear and plain black masks, with no numbers or markings anywhere. As part of the permanent Black Guard team stationed within fifty meters of Chen Xuan around the clock, they had tracked the stairwell sensors since ten o'clock.

Neither Black Guard member spoke a word.

The thug at the rear barely heard a rustle before a black-gloved hand clamped over his mouth and a press against the nerve on his neck dropped him limp onto the carpet without a sound.

The second thug spun around with his baton raised.

The second Black Guard member stepped inside the swing, twisted the baton free, and drove a palm strike into the man's solar plexus. The thug folded unconscious into waiting arms and was laid gently on the floor.

Up ahead, Scar Liu and the third thug froze thirty meters from Suite 1408 and whirled around.

Before Scar Liu could shout, the two Black Guard operatives glided forward like shadows.

A low kick took the third thug's ankles out from under him, and a chop to the collarbone put him to sleep before his head touched the rug. Scar Liu lunged with his steel wedge, only to have his wrist pinned, his knees swept, and his face pressed smoothly into the carpet.

One quiet pinch behind Scar Liu's ear ended the struggle.

From the first step out of the alcove to Scar Liu's eyes rolling back, eight silent seconds had passed.

Forty meters down the corridor, inside Suite 1408, Qin Yuhan let out a soft sigh in her dream and tucked her hand warmer under Chen Xuan's chin.

Out in the hallway, the two Black Guard members bound all four thugs neatly with black nylon ties, gagged them, stacked their batons beside the elevator, and sent one silent signal to the night patrol line of Anti-Corruption Bureau Director Hou Liangping outside the villa.

Before the elevator bell chimed on the ground floor, the two figures in plain black vanished into the shadows, fifty meters from Chen Xuan's pillow.
