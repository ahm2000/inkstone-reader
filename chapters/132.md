# Chapter 132: Corridor Fourteen

While half of Jingzhou's officialdom braced for a sleepless night, Suite 1408 on the fourteenth floor of Shanshui Villa remained completely unhurried.

Outside the tall windows, dark ripples stirred across Jingzhou Lake, but inside the warm bedroom there was not a trace of tension.

Qin Yuhan sat cross-legged on the bed in soft blue cotton pajamas, her dark hair damp from a hot shower. Chen Xuan stood behind her with a thick towel, gently drying her hair while an old comedy played on the muted television.

"Tilt your chin up a little, President Qin," Chen Xuan said lightly, switching on the hair dryer to a low, warm hum. "If your neck stays damp, you will be sneezing through breakfast tomorrow."

Qin Yuhan obeyed like a sleepy cat, closing her eyes as his fingers combed through her hair.

"My father praised you three times at dinner tonight," she murmured with a soft smile. "He said you kept a cooler head than half the board directors when Ding Yizhen tried to intimidate us. He told me I picked a steady husband."

"Your father has great judgment," Chen Xuan chuckled, turning off the dryer. "Though tell him I am not going easy on him at chess tomorrow."

Qin Yuhan laughed, then spotted the black leather folder on the desk.

"Oh, I almost forgot," she said, sliding off the bed and pulling a knit cardigan over her pajamas. "My father needs the revised harbor blueprint before his morning meeting. His suite is just down the hall in 1402."

"I will walk with you," Chen Xuan said, slipping into his sneakers and grabbing the key card. "The hallway is drafty."

They stepped out into Corridor Fourteen. Plush burgundy carpet and soft brass lamps lined the quiet passage.

They had walked ten paces when the far elevator chimed. The doors slid open, and four broad-shouldered men in dark windbreakers stepped out without luggage. The leader had a thick gold chain under his collar and a jagged scar above his left brow. He spotted the couple and smirked, waving his three companions forward to block the corridor.

Qin Yuhan halted, her grip tightening on the leather folder. Chen Xuan stepped half a pace ahead of her, his hands tucked loosely in his hoodie pockets, his expression completely calm.

"Evening, President Qin," the scarred man drawled, cracking his knuckles. "A friend in Jingzhou thinks you and your husband are too stubborn. He asked us to have a quiet chat so you remember to pack your bags early tomorrow."

"This is a provincial guest villa," Qin Yuhan said sharply. "Take one more step and I am calling hotel security and the police."

"Security is on a long coffee break downstairs," the scarred man chuckled, reaching a heavy hand toward Chen Xuan's shoulder. "Step aside, little clerk."

Before his fingers touched Chen Xuan's sleeve, two wooden doors on opposite sides of Corridor Fourteen swung open at the exact same moment.

From Suite 1406 stepped a cheerful, round-bellied middle-aged uncle in a faded knit vest and plaid pajama pants, holding a warm paper bag of roasted chestnuts and a chipped mug.

"Hey there, neighbors!" the uncle beamed brightly, looking every bit like a friendly tourist. "The stall by the lake still had hot chestnuts! Want a handful?"

From Suite 1405 stepped a smiling young woman in oversized fleece loungewear and fuzzy bunny slippers, carrying folded towels and a small box of tangerines.

"Excuse me, coming through!" she chimed warmly. "My cousin spilled hot tea by the door, and these tangerines are rolling everywhere!"

Neither of them looked tense, guarded, or trained. They looked completely like two relaxed, cheerful hotel guests wandering the hall before bed.

Yet the uncle took one casual step forward, seemed to catch his slipper on the carpet edge, and lurched smoothly sideways. His round shoulder bumped the scarred leader's hip at a tiny, effortless angle.

The burly man grunted in shock as his boots flew right out from under him, sending him spinning into the wall with a loud thud.

"Oh dear, watch your footing, young man!" the uncle cried warmly, stepping forward to help while planting his heel across the second tough's ankle, tipping him neatly over his leader's legs.

At the exact same moment, four round tangerines tumbled from the young woman's box and rolled straight beneath the soles of the third and fourth thugs. Both men windmilled wildly. Bending down with a bright, apologetic smile to catch the fruit, her elbow nudged the fourth man's knee just enough to fold him straight onto the carpet.

"Goodness, I am so sorry!" she exclaimed cheerfully. "Are your shoes slippery? This carpet really needs a brushing!"

In four seconds flat, all four hired toughs were tangled in a groaning heap amid scattered tangerines and the sweet scent of roasted chestnuts.

The chubby uncle grinned warmly at Chen Xuan and pressed a handful of hot chestnuts into his palm.

"Take your wife back inside, young man," the uncle said kindly. "The hall gets chilly at night, and these fellows clearly had too much to drink."

Chen Xuan smiled easily, feeling the quiet, natural warmth of the Zero Guard around him. "Thank you, Uncle. Good night."

Just then, the far elevator chimed again. Hou Liangping strode out at the head of six uniformed provincial officers and local police.

Hou Liangping blinked in surprise at the four burly men tangled among tangerines and chestnuts, then flashed his badge crisply.

"Anti-Corruption Bureau and Municipal Police," Hou Liangping announced. "We received a report of witness intimidation on this floor. Cuff all four of them."

The officers hauled the stunned thugs up and snapped steel cuffs onto their wrists. Hou Liangping walked over and gave Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan a respectful nod.

"President Qin, and sir," Hou Liangping said politely, having zero clue who Chen Xuan really was. "I apologize for the disturbance. We have secured a full arrest warrant for the people behind this. Please rest easy tonight."

"Thank you, Chief Hou," Qin Yuhan breathed as the police marched the four men away.

Back inside Suite 1408, Qin Yuhan locked the door and let out a delighted laugh.

"Did you see that?" she marveled, taking a warm chestnut Chen Xuan peeled for her. "That nice uncle and that girl in bunny slippers had the best accidental timing I have ever seen!"

"Good neighbors make all the difference," Chen Xuan smiled quietly. "Now eat up and sleep. Tomorrow will be a much nicer morning."
