# Chapter 15: The Cross-Department War Room

At five in the morning, the Senior Vice Premier convened the emergency cross-department session inside the main dark-wood hall of the State Council compound.

Around the long, polished table sat the Minister of State Security, the Minister of Defense, the Chief Auditor of the Central Financial Commission, the Minister of Public Security, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, and the Deputy Secretary of the Central Discipline Commission.

Heavy ashtrays sat at every second seat, already filling fast. Nobody touched the hot tea. Every face around the table was gray with sleepless tension, fear, and barely contained anger. Behind both oak doors stood silent, matte-black Zero Guard sentries, reminding every minister in the room that not a single whisper was leaving this hall.

"Gentlemen," the Senior Vice Premier began, lighting a cigarette and setting the lighter down with a sharp click. "The Zero Guard has already handed the captured traitors and seized servers over to your agencies for formal cleanup and prosecution. Every department has finished its internal meeting. Now put everything on the table together. Hold nothing back."

Before the Vice Premier even finished exhaling, the room erupted.

"I want an explanation from Finance!" the Minister of Defense barked, slapping a thick stack of intercepts onto the wood. "How did nineteen regional bank desks inside Huaxin Bank and four state-owned trusts move illegal clearing payments for twenty years without a single red flag from your auditors?!"

"Don't point your finger at Finance, General!" the Chief Auditor snapped back, his face flushing red as smoke curled from his fingers. "We are staring at over one hundred trillion yuan in layered sovereign-asset theft across fourteen provinces! Every single shell trust was shielded behind fake municipal redevelopment projects and signed off by local Party and government officials! Where was the Discipline Commission?! Where was Public Security?!"

"You think we enjoyed watching masked ghosts unload six analysts from our own Third Bureau onto our basement floor?!" the Minister of State Security shot back, his voice low and vibrating with cold fury. "And Foreign Affairs has been fielding frantic back-channel calls from Washington, Moscow, and London since midnight because their satellites went blind over Lanshui!"

"My desk has received seven urgent ambassador inquiries in three hours," the Minister of Foreign Affairs said tightly, rubbing his temples as his cigarette burned down toward his knuckles. "They think we are staging a strategic strike in eastern China. If we don't put out a clean, airtight explanation once the lockdown lifts, international markets and foreign defense ministries will lose their minds."

"Enough!" the Senior Vice Premier slammed his palm flat on the dark wood. Every voice cut off at once.

The Chief Auditor swallowed hard and pushed his ledger forward. "Vice Premier, on the financial side alone, one hundred trillion yuan is the largest economic crime in modern history. If we arrest everyone publicly at once, markets will panic."

"Stop staring at the hundred trillion yuan!" the Minister of State Security cut in coldly, leaning forward through the haze of cigarette smoke. "Turn to page twelve of the combined annex."

Papers flipped around the long table. One by one, the bickering died, and the color drained from every minister's face.

"National power-grid override keys," the Minister of State Security read evenly. "Next-generation naval propulsion telemetry. Missile-guidance parameters. Strategic port-logistics schedules across six coastal hubs. Core agricultural seed-bank genetics. And the real identities of nineteen active overseas intelligence officers, three of whom are already buried because of leaks paid through these exact same underground banks. A coordinated external sabotage trigger was being staged to hit our ports, our power grid, and our banking clearing system at the exact same time."

Dead, crushing silence fell over the hall. Only the thin wisps of smoke rising from six ashtrays moved in the air.

The Senior Vice Premier closed his eyes for three long seconds, exhaling slowly. When he opened them, every trace of political calculation was gone, replaced by stark, chilling clarity.

"One hundred trillion yuan is a massive wound," the Senior Vice Premier said softly, "yet a country of our size could rebuild one hundred trillion yuan in twenty years. What sits on page twelve would have broken the spine of this nation before we even knew we were bleeding. Compared to that, even a hundred trillion yuan is the smallest thing in this room."

He looked around the silent table, his voice turning crisp and decisive.

"Here is the joint cleanup plan I am presenting upstairs. One: every national traitor, spy, and compromised bureau chief handed over by the Zero Guard goes straight to sealed military and state-security tribunals. Zero public leaks. Two: the Central Financial Commission freezes every offshore trust and claws back every recoverable yuan quietly. Three: in Lanshui Province and Yunxi County, where the thread began, Public Security and Discipline will close the local case publicly as a clean joint anti-corruption, anti-gang, and counter-terrorism sweep so the public, the markets, and foreign watchrooms see only a domestic security drill and a routine local crackdown."

He stood up slowly, gathering the matte-black binder with both hands.

"Nobody leaves this building. I am taking this straight up to the Red-Walled Hall right now."
