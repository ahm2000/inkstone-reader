# Chapter 15: The First Crack in the Concrete

Inside the Qinghe Fresh Florals van on Riverside Road, the rain outside sounded suddenly very far away.

Number Seven and Number Fourteen stepped behind the center console, leaning over Number Eleven's shoulders as the financial analyst cleared the local evidence folders from the widest central display and projected a single, sprawling network diagram in their place.

Pale blue lines, crimson arrows, and clusters of numbered corporate nodes bloomed across the dark glass. It looked less like a county builder's accounting sheet and more like an arterial scan of something hidden deep beneath the ground.

"Start from the baseline," Number Seven said quietly.

"Hongrui Real Estate Development Limited," Number Eleven began, his tone clipped and precise. "Registered in Yunxi County six years ago under Deng Yongchang. Registered capital: thirty million yuan. Active payroll: forty-two full-time employees, including site foremen and reception staff. Over the past four years, Hongrui Real Estate has completed exactly three residential complexes in the county seat. Mid-tier six-story walk-ups selling at four thousand eight hundred yuan per square meter. Even at one hundred percent occupancy and generous construction margins, their legitimate four-year gross revenue cannot exceed three hundred and twenty million yuan."

Number Fourteen folded his arms slowly. "A standard county developer. Big enough to bully Old Street shopkeepers and buy off a station director like Cheng Biao, small enough to stay beneath provincial notice."

"That is what their public tax filings say," Number Eleven replied.

He tapped two keys. A second translucent overlay slid across the diagram in bright, unmistakable amber.

"This is the four-year capital flow reconstructed from the encrypted basement ledger and cross-matched against the interbank clearing mirror we froze twenty minutes ago," Number Eleven said. "Between January four years ago and last Tuesday, exactly four point eight billion yuan has cycled through Hongrui Real Estate's Yunxi demolition escrow accounts."

Silence fell inside the soundproofed van. Even Number Thirty-four paused at the signals keyboard, turning his head toward the main display.

"Four point eight billion?" Number Fourteen repeated softly. "In Yunxi County? The entire county government's annual fiscal revenue is barely a fraction of that."

"Look at the direction of the arrows," Number Eleven said, zooming in on the central cluster of escrow accounts. "Normally, when a local developer runs a shady demolition project, money flows inward from municipal banks and out to local contractors, kickback accounts, and land-transfer fees. Here, the demolition escrow accounts for Old Street and the south riverside parcel are not acting as construction pools at all. They are acting as a wash basin."

Number Eleven traced a luminous crimson thread from Yunxi County outward along three branching tiers.

"Large tranches of capital arrive inside Yunxi County disguised as fragmented municipal infrastructure compensation, rural land-reserve reclamation subsidies, and distressed asset buyouts," Number Eleven explained. "Within seventy-two hours of landing in Hongrui Real Estate's escrow accounts, the balances are split into clean batches under ten million yuan each and pushed outward. First hop: seven logistics and building-materials trading companies registered in Lanshui City, none of which own a single warehouse or freight truck. Second hop: four equity investment partnerships in Qingyan with nominee legal representatives. Third hop: three offshore trade-settlement shells registered in Hong Kong."

Number Thirty-four whistled softly under his breath. "Layered smurfing on a sovereign-scale clearing rhythm. Deng Yongchang does not have the brains or the banking reach to build an architecture like that."

"He did not build it," Number Eleven confirmed, pulling up the shareholder cross-reference beside the flow chart. "Look at the settlement dates. Every major outbound pulse through Hongrui Real Estate's escrow accounts coincides within forty-eight hours with an uncontested land-reserve auction or a rushed Old Street demolition push. Deng Yongchang is not the owner of this pipeline. He is the county janitor keeping the floor swept so nobody walks into the basement."

Number Seven stood completely still, his gaze locked onto the glowing web of offshore routing numbers stretching out of a quiet riverside county toward Lanshui, Qingyan, and Hong Kong.

In the corner of the auxiliary monitor, the green confirmation icons for Number Nineteen, Number Twenty-seven, and Number Forty-two blinked peacefully. Chen Mingde's wrongful detention case was already solved. The bakery hard drive, the blank DICOM cranial scan, the pre-typed hospital diagnosis, the backdated detention warrant, and Ma Er's recorded payoff call from Yan Tiehu were all sealed and hash-verified. By sunrise, the Black Pine Guard could hand that neat little file to provincial inspectors, walk Chen Mingde straight out of Holding Cell Two, and send Cheng Biao, Deputy Director Fang Zhihua, and Yan Tiehu into handcuffs before lunch.

If they had stopped there, they would have cleaned up a dirty county bully and walked away blind to the sinkhole opening beneath their feet.

"A county land-grab does not move four point eight billion yuan," Number Seven said at last, his voice low and iron-hard in the quiet van. "And nobody freezes in sheer terror over a two-room hardware shop on Old Street unless the ground underneath Old Street is hiding something worth killing for."

Number Seven turned to Number Eleven and Number Thirty-four.

"Do not close the Hongrui file," Number Seven ordered. "Trace every single account upstream and downstream. Find out where the four point eight billion actually originated before it ever touched Yunxi County, and find out which beneficial owners sit behind those Hong Kong shells. Pull the county land-reserve archives going back twenty years, every mineral and geological survey filed for the riverside belt, and every official who signed off on Yunxi's demolition approvals."

"Should I wake the Boss tonight?" Number Fourteen asked quietly.

Number Seven glanced briefly at the quiet green dot resting over Fuxing New Village on the left monitor. It was half past midnight. Inside that modest third-floor apartment, the Boss was sitting quietly with his parents after a long flight, letting them believe their son was just an ordinary office clerk who had come home to help his uncle.

"Not yet," Number Seven said. "We do not bring half a picture to the Boss. Notify Number One on the encrypted relay to place the regional financial and archival teams on silent standby. By the time the Boss finishes breakfast with Chen Jianguo and Liu Meifang tomorrow morning, I want to know whether this four point eight billion is the whole hole or merely the first crack in the concrete."

"Understood," Number Eleven said, his fingers already flying back across the matte keyboard as new query windows opened across the dark glass. Beside him, Number Thirty-four began pulling archived county land-reserve deeds and riverfront geological logs from the provincial registry mirror, line after line of forgotten seals and signatures scrolling silently upward.

Outside on Riverside Road, the rain continued to fall over the sleeping roofs of Yunxi County, washing down the stone gutters of Old Street toward the black river, while inside the flower-delivery van, the screens burned brighter than ever.
