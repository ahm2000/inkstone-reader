# Chapter 76: The Car at Building 2

At nine o'clock on Monday morning, Chen Jianguo paced outside Building 2 of Fuxing New Village, his knuckles white around his bag strap. Every ten seconds he checked his watch and squinted toward the gate.

"You told your mother and second aunt that Mingde would be home at nine," Chen Jianguo muttered, stopping beside Chen Xuan. "It is nine oh two, Xuan! If we do not go to Old Street Station now, what if Cheng Biao moves him out of the county?"

"He will not be moved, Dad," Chen Xuan said calmly, leaning against the doorframe with his green thermos under his arm. "Wait three more minutes."

Beside them, Wu Lanying clutched a folded jacket to her chest, her eyes red from crying all night. Liu Meifang held her arm steady, glancing anxiously at Chen Xuan.

"Jianguo, stop pacing, you are making Lanying dizzy," Liu Meifang said softly. "Xuan, tell me the truth. Over boiled eggs this morning you sounded so sure. Did someone at the city office call you, or are you just keeping your second aunt calm?"

"Nobody called me, Mom," Chen Xuan replied, watching the lane. "Cheng Biao broke the law in broad daylight. The people above Yunxi County do not need my phone call to see a fire that big."

"The people above Yunxi County!" Chen Jianguo laughed bitterly, pushing his glasses up with a shaking thumb. "When has the province ever dropped out of the sky at nine in the morning for a hardware shopkeeper? Deng Yongchang dines with County Magistrate Sun Weiming every week! Liang Guodong runs the police bureau like his own backyard! You stand here drinking hot water like we are waiting for a bus!"

Wu Lanying gasped and grabbed Liu Meifang's wrist. "Look. At the gate."

A black sedan with provincial government plates rolled silently past the iron gate with no sirens or flashing lights. Two neighbors on the second-floor railing froze, staring down at the rare plates.

Color drained from Chen Jianguo's face. "Provincial plates," he stammered, backing half a step toward Chen Xuan. "Why is a provincial car stopping here? Did Cheng Biao push the case higher? Xuan, stand behind me, do not say a word!"

The car braked smoothly ten feet from the steps. A tall, lean man in a dark gray jacket stepped out from the front passenger seat, carrying the clear weight of a senior provincial officer. It was Han Zhiyuan, lead inspector of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group.

Han Zhiyuan walked briskly around the car, opened the back door with both hands, and bowed slightly in clear respect.

"Mr. Chen Mingde," Han Zhiyuan said clearly. "We are here. Watch your step."

Chen Mingde stepped out in his wrinkled blue work jacket, hair messy, blinking in the morning sun as if he still could not believe he was outside his brother's building.

"Mingde!" Wu Lanying cried out. She stumbled forward two steps, then froze in sudden fear of the tall official.

All stiffness melted from Chen Mingde's shoulders. "Lanying! Jianguo! I am fine, completely fine!"

Han Zhiyuan stepped aside so Wu Lanying could reach her husband, then held out a red-stamped folder with both hands.

"Mr. Chen Mingde, on behalf of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group and the provincial court oversight team, here is your Cleared Name Paper and Wrongful Arrest Dismissal," Han Zhiyuan said gravely. "Every fake charge filed by Cheng Biao at Old Street Station has been wiped from provincial records. You have zero record, zero open orders, and zero need to ever set foot in a police station."

Chen Jianguo stared at the bright red stamp through fogged glasses, mouth open. "Wait, comrade leader," he blurted in shock. "You did not bring him on bail? What about Cheng Biao? What about Yan Tiehu and Hongrui Real Estate?"

"Cheng Biao, Yan Tiehu, County Police Chief Liang Guodong, County Magistrate Sun Weiming, and Hongrui Chairman Deng Yongchang have all been arrested by provincial officers this morning," Han Zhiyuan answered cleanly. "An official team is checking the damage to Mingde Hardware. Full payout papers will be brought straight to your door."

Wu Lanying pressed both hands over her mouth, tears spilling down her cheeks as she clutched Chen Mingde's sleeve.

Chen Mingde stared at the red-stamped paper, fingers trembling. "Inspector Han Zhiyuan," he said hoarsely in honest wonder. "I just sell hinges and bolts on Old Street. All night I thought my family was finished. How did a provincial team move so fast?"

"When rot reaches the bone, the province does not wait for a complaint," Han Zhiyuan replied smoothly, hiding every trace of the capital's thunder. "You suffered a great wrong, Mr. Chen Mingde. Go upstairs, rest, and eat with your family."

Turning to nod to the family, Han Zhiyuan's eyes passed over Liu Meifang and Chen Jianguo and landed squarely on the young man by the doorframe.

Han Zhiyuan paused.

Chen Xuan had not rushed forward, gasped at the provincial plates, or flinched at the names of Sun Weiming and Deng Yongchang. He simply stood in an ordinary dark jacket holding a green thermos, watching with a deep steadiness that made the hair on Han Zhiyuan's neck prickle.

"And this young man is?" Han Zhiyuan asked, stepping half a pace closer.

"My son, Chen Xuan," Chen Jianguo answered quickly, pride breaking through his shock. "Home for a few days. He works an ordinary office job in the city."

"Good morning, Inspector Han Zhiyuan," Chen Xuan said politely with a slight nod, voice mild and free of nerves or flattery. "Thank you for bringing my second uncle home cleanly and on time."

On time.

Two simple words, yet Han Zhiyuan felt a sudden jolt in his chest as his eyes met Chen Xuan's. There was no pride or boasting in the young man's gaze, only the calm weight of someone who had watched storms far bigger than Yunxi County break without raising his voice. For one strange second, the seasoned inspector felt like a junior officer reporting a finished order.

"It is our duty, Mr. Chen Xuan," Han Zhiyuan said slowly, choosing his words with care. "If anyone in Yunxi County causes your family even a tiny bit of trouble, my direct number is on the back of that paper."

"I doubt anyone will," Chen Xuan replied with a faint smile. "Have a safe drive back, Inspector."

Han Zhiyuan studied him one heartbeat longer, shook off the chill, and returned to the car. As the black sedan backed out of the lane, he glanced into the side mirror and muttered to his driver.

"An ordinary office worker. Strange. I have never seen an ordinary office worker with eyes that still."

By the stairwell, Chen Jianguo checked his watch in disbelief.

Nine oh five sharp.
