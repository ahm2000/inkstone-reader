# Chapter 76: Li Weiming's Desperate Gambit

Night fell heavily over Lanshui City, smearing amber streetlights across the rain-slicked avenues below the Lanshui International Convention Hotel.

Inside Suite 2801 on the twenty-eighth floor, the lamps remained off. Only the cold blue glow of two encrypted laptops illuminated the dark mahogany desk and the taut profile of Li Weiming standing before the floor-to-ceiling windows.

His tailored jacket lay tossed over an armchair, his silk tie pulled loose for the first time since landing in China.

On the desk, a black satellite handset vibrated twice with a red-priority alert from Zurich. Li Weiming scanned four lines of compressed German cipher text, and his jaw locked hard.

Server cluster Helvetia-Nine in Zurich had been probed and flagged by Chinese State Security Counterintelligence cyber units ninety minutes after the morning session adjourned. Two clearing accounts in Singapore were already frozen under emergency financial holds. Worse, the two European clearing directors from his VIP row had abandoned the forum at four o'clock and booked an emergency charter flight out of Lanshui Airport.

They had left him holding the wreckage.

A frantic knock hammered against the door of Suite 2801. Li Weiming pressed the electronic release without turning from the window.

Song Baisheng stumbled inside, his hair disheveled and his eyes bloodshot with terror, clutching his phone like a drowning man.

"Li Weiming! We are finished!" Song Baisheng gasped, slamming the door. "Provincial Discipline Inspection officers just visited my headquarters and took my chief technical director for questioning! They demanded the original contracts for Helvetia-Nine! And Deputy Governor Wu Zhenbang will not answer my calls!"

Li Weiming turned slowly, his eyes cold and hollow. "Calm down, Song Baisheng. Screaming in a hotel corridor will get you arrested before breakfast."

"Calm down?!" Song Baisheng's voice cracked wildly. "That clerk from Qingyan City ruined everything! Who is he? Why did Retired Governor Zhao Dehai eat lunch with him like family? You told me the Zurich clauses were untouchable!"

"Get out of my suite," Li Weiming said softly, his voice flat and lethal. "Go back to your room, take a sedative, and do not call me again tonight. If you keep panicking in the hallways, you will lead State Security straight to this floor."

Song Baisheng recoiled from the icy glint in Li Weiming's eyes and backed out of Suite 2801 without another word.

* * *

Alone in the blue shadows, Li Weiming stared at a printed photograph pinned beside his laptop screen: Chen Xuan standing calmly at Table Forty-Two in his washed gray jacket, holding a cordless microphone with ordinary composure.

Li Weiming's mind raced with the cold clarity of a cornered predator hearing hounds closing in.

He mapped every failure of the past month onto one straight line.

First, the Northern Ledger's entire Qingyan City network had imploded overnight, taking thirty-two ministerial assets and one hundred trillion yuan in leveraged channels down with it. Second, the two elite armed reconnaissance operatives he dispatched to Hengxin Logistics had vanished without a trace four hundred meters from Chen Xuan's office. Third, the same unassuming clerk had walked into Lanshui City, lunched with Retired Governor Zhao Dehai and Qin Zhengming, and dismantled the Northern Ledger's three-year Zurich intelligence rebuild in thirty seconds.

*There are no three coincidences like that,* Li Weiming thought grimly.

He reached the only conclusion his training could construct: Chen Xuan was not a logistics clerk at all, but the hidden linchpin who had broken the Northern Ledger's entire Lanshui apparatus.

State Security was already closing around the Zurich servers. By tomorrow afternoon, the border exits would be sealed. If Li Weiming fled back to Switzerland tonight empty-handed, without knowing who destroyed the eastern China network, the Zurich board would liquidate him before the week ended.

He needed answers tonight. And the only man holding them was sleeping fourteen floors below in Room 1408.

* * *

Li Weiming unzipped a hidden lining inside his leather briefcase and pulled out a matte-gray encrypted shortwave handset with no SIM card.

He dialed a twelve-digit relay sequence bouncing off an industrial repeater in the eastern docks of Lanshui City.

A raspy, accented voice answered on the second ring. "Harbor Maintenance. Go ahead."

"This is Marcus Vance," Li Weiming said coldly, using his Northern Ledger alias. "Activate Blackout Protocol. Tonight at midnight."

A brief silence crackled. "Confirm target and extraction parameters."

"Lanshui International Convention Hotel, fourteenth floor, Room 1408," Li Weiming instructed cleanly. "Single adult male, civilian build, no visible security detail on his floor. Loop the fourteenth-floor corridor cameras from the service stairwell. Enter silently at zero hundred hours. Do not kill him. Subdue him, bag him, and move him through the freight elevator to the riverside warehouse in East Industrial Zone."

"Understood," the gravelly voice replied smoothly. "Four operators, full suppression kit. Window after interrogation?"

"Two hours," Li Weiming said without warmth. "I will meet you at the riverside warehouse at twelve thirty. Once I extract who stands behind him and which agency broke our Qingyan City ledger, we dispose of the package and move to the coastal pier before dawn. Double hazard bonuses are already wired to your Zurich escrow accounts."

"Four operators moving now. Midnight on Floor Fourteen."

The line clicked dead. Li Weiming snapped the handset in half over the desk edge and pocketed the shards. For the first time since morning, he felt back in control of the board.

* * *

Two blocks south of the Lanshui International Convention Hotel, a plain dark sedan sat parked quietly beneath wet sycamore trees.

Inside, Number Seven watched a palm-sized passive spectrum monitor flash once with an amber spike.

"Shortwave burst from Suite 2801," Number Seven reported calmly. "Directed toward an unregistered warehouse near the east riverfront. Four forged European engineering passports just moved off standby there."

Behind the steering wheel, Number Nineteen watched the hotel's glass facade. "Li Weiming is bringing in armed foreign contractors. Likely targeting Room 1408."

Number Seven tapped his encrypted ear-relay. "Number One, this is Number Seven. Li Weiming has activated a four-man armed sleeper cell in Lanshui City. Estimated arrival at midnight. Should Number Nineteen and I intercept them in the underground garage?"

A quiet pause answered, followed by Number One's dry, unhurried voice from Qingyan City.

"Negative, Number Seven," Number One replied calmly. "Do not intercept them in the garage, and do not step onto the fourteenth floor. State Security Counterintelligence already has its arrest warrants signed for tomorrow morning. Let Li Weiming's mercenaries walk straight upstairs."

Number Seven glanced up toward the dimly lit fourteenth floor, a faint, respectful understanding settling across his face.

"The permanent Zero Guard team is already inside fifty meters of Room 1408," Number Seven murmured.

"Correct," Number One said softly. "Hold the outer ring two blocks back. Tell Number Fifty-six to notify State Security where to collect four gift-wrapped foreign contractors before sunrise."
