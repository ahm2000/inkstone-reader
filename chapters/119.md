# Chapter 119: The Second Month Review

Thin blue layers of cigarette smoke drifted beneath the lamps of the walnut conference hall inside Beijing's Great Hall. Outside, the weekend sky hung gray and calm over the capital. Inside, heavy doors stayed locked, lidded teacups steamed softly, and plain gray review binders lay neatly before every seat.

Around the inner ring sat all 24 core voting members of the Capital Top Council, every one at half-premier or sub-national rank, standing above provincial party secretaries and cabinet ministers. At the head sat Premier Gu Chengshan, tapping ash into a glass tray. To his left sat Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua with a brass lighter beside his cup. To his right sat General Qin Zhengguo in a plain olive uniform without medals, straight as steel.

Behind the head of the table, in four senior mahogany chairs, sat the white-haired Founding Elders: Elder Song resting thin hands on his dark cane, Elder Qin sitting upright, Elder Ye watching calmly, and Elder Lin exhaling a thin curl of smoke.

In the outer ring sat the non-standing agency chiefs and ministry heads: State Security Minister Ding Guoping, Discipline Office Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi, and the four presidents of the Four National Banks, Fang Zhengming of China Construction Bank, Han Guoliang of ICBC, Tang Wenhao of Bank of China, and Liang Qingshan of Huaxia Bank. Every outer-ring official kept his back straight, notebook open, and pen moving quietly without interrupting.

At the side desk, two archive clerks in gray uniforms recorded every figure and vote into the linen book of sealed state minutes.

Premier Gu Chengshan nodded toward the lectern. "Deliver the sixty-day review report."

Ma Jiansheng, deputy director of the Joint National Clean-Up Office, stepped forward with a gray folder and spoke crisply.

"Premier Gu Chengshan, Founding Elders, and 24 core voting members," Ma Jiansheng reported. "Two full months have passed since the clean-up began. First, asset recovery: 46 trillion yuan in traitors' stolen state funds, hidden shares, overseas property, gold certificates, and shell holdings has been recovered outright or locked inside state trusts."

He turned one page smoothly.

"Second, financial stability: private citizen Chen Xuan's 300 trillion yuan clean, legal gift across China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank, with 500 trillion yuan still on standby, has kept Beijing and Shanghai markets rock-steady. Not one county bank ran short of cash. Every factory payroll and port shipment cleared on schedule. Grain and vegetable prices stayed flat. Twice last month, foreign traders tried shorting the yuan overseas and broke their teeth against Bank of China's desk."

Ma Jiansheng turned to the final sheet.

"Third, the exact personnel tally. One hundred and twenty-eight clean frontline investigators, honest county cadres, and bank auditors have been rapidly promoted into key posts, with sixty-four awarded national commendations. Three hundred and forty-two senior traitors have been removed and charged, with one hundred and eighty-six already sentenced to prison and open verdicts read aloud across forty-one prefectures and counties, including Yunxi County and Sha City. One hundred and twelve negligent officials are under withheld ranks and multi-year promotion bans. Fifty-eight senior faction figures nationwide have been quietly moved into higher-sounding, completely powerless honorary and advisory titles with zero budget and zero staff, peacefully dismantling old patronage webs. Across sixty days, domestic and overseas monitors recorded zero leaks regarding the Black Guard or the benefactor behind Number 1. Report complete."

Ma Jiansheng closed his folder, bowed once, and returned silently to the outer ring.

Deep silence settled over the hall.

A cigarette burned slowly in an ashtray near the middle of the ring, sending up a thin thread of smoke. Even among the half-premier members who had gripped their folders with nervous hands two months earlier, old dread had given way to quiet awe. The storm had swept forty provinces and central bureaus without cracking a single bank window or frightening a single ordinary family.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua spoke first, his voice low and reflective as he set his lighter down.

"Two months ago, half the senior advisors in Beijing swore that pulling 46 trillion yuan of dirty roots out of the soil would snap the financial roof," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said quietly. "Instead, shopkeepers in Sha City, Yunxi County, and Shanghai opened earlier this week than last year. Ordinary people only saw crooked bullies going to prison and honest cadres stepping up."

He looked down at his closed folder, rare humility softening his eyes.

"Every leader here knows why ordinary families barely felt a bump," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua added softly. "That 300 trillion yuan free legal shield held the floor solid as bedrock. No strings, no interest, no favors asked. In forty years of service, I have never seen a debt like the one this nation owes an unseen citizen."

Silence lingered. In the outer ring, the four bank presidents lowered their eyes respectfully toward their notebooks.

General Qin Zhengguo spoke next, his voice steady as a soldier standing watch.

"All three theater commands are completely clean," General Qin Zhengguo reported directly to the inner ring. "Every crooked procurement officer and contractor paying kickbacks for military land is facing military court. All three commands finished live readiness drills this week without delay. Troop morale stands at a ten-year high."

He paused, broad hands resting flat beside his binder, stern eyes growing quiet.

"A soldier understands loyalty better than politics," General Qin Zhengguo said softly. "That unseen citizen handed our country clean truth when moles sat inside our vaults, then placed 300 trillion yuan behind our banks so ordinary people would not suffer during the surgery. He did not ask for a single title, a seat in this hall, or even a public thank-you. That leaves every man in uniform with quiet awe and an unspoken debt we can only repay by keeping this country clean."

Several core voting members nodded slowly. Behind the head of the table, Elder Song rested his chin over his hands on his cane, eyes warm and steady.

Premier Gu Chengshan stubbed out his cigarette and looked around all 24 seats.

"We honor that unspoken debt in two ways," Premier Gu Chengshan declared calmly. "First, we never pry, never push, and never break our word of secrecy over the Black Guard and the citizen behind Number 1. Second, we carry this work cleanly into Phase Three. Over the coming year, state trusts will return every recovered factory, land deed, and stolen fund to public benefit. Courts will finish every pending trial by law. Our personnel standards stay iron-tight: clean cadres rise fast, negligent officials keep their ranks frozen, and no faction leader exiled to an honorary title ever touches a real seal again."

He slid the Phase Three resolution forward across the walnut wood.

"We now call the show-of-hands vote of the 24 core voting members to approve Phase Three of the national recovery and governance plan," Premier Gu Chengshan announced evenly. "All members in favor, raise your right hand."

Without a single hesitation, without one nervous sideways glance, all 24 right hands rose cleanly around the walnut ring. Premier Gu Chengshan, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, General Qin Zhengguo, and every remaining half-premier member held their hands high in solemn unity.

"Abstentions?" Premier Gu Chengshan asked. Zero hands moved.

"Against?" Zero hands moved.

At the archive desk, the chief clerk logged the line in neat black ink and read the permanent entry aloud.

"Formal vote recorded in the sealed state minutes: twenty-four in favor, zero abstentions, zero against. Phase Three approved unanimously by all 24 core voting members and sealed permanently in the state archives."

The heavy red wax seal pressed cleanly onto the linen ledger. Behind the head of the table, Elder Song, Elder Qin, Elder Ye, and Elder Lin gave a single quiet nod.

In the outer ring, the agency chiefs and bank presidents closed their notebooks in silent order. Around the walnut table, the nation's highest leaders sat in fading blue smoke, knowing the country was breathing clean air again, and carrying in their chests a quiet, permanent awe for the unseen hand that had steadied the wheel.
