# Chapter 119: The Capital Suitor's Boast

By six in the evening, the main banquet hall of the Qin courtyard glowed under warm brass chandeliers. Two large round rosewood tables were set with steaming Beijing roast duck, poached crystal shrimp, and aged rice wine.

Just as the dishes arrived, a thirty-year-old man in a stiff dark ministry suit and gold-rimmed glasses walked into the hall carrying a red velvet gift box. It was Gu Mingzhe, a deputy section chief in a capital economic bureau and the exact Beijing suitor Lin Shuyin had tried for two years to match with Qin Yuhan.

"Happy seventy-eighth birthday, Elder Qin!" Gu Mingzhe bowed respectfully before straightening his suit jacket with an air of deep self-importance. "Please forgive me for arriving ten minutes late. Our outer coordinating group at the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office had an afternoon briefing that ran over schedule."

At the mention of the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office, Lin Shuyin, Second Aunt Zhou Meilin, and Qin Haoran all looked up with starry eyes.

"Mingzhe, sit right here at the main table!" Lin Shuyin beamed, waving him to the chair directly across from Qin Yuhan and Chen Xuan. "Everyone in Beijing knows that task force handles the biggest national financial cases this year. For a thirty-year-old deputy chief to sit in those meetings, your future is limitless!"

Gu Mingzhe sat down, adjusted his gold-rimmed glasses, and cast a condescending glance across the tablecloth at Chen Xuan, who was wearing his plain shirt sleeves rolled up and calmly peeling poached crystal shrimp into a small porcelain bowl for Qin Yuhan.

"Aunt Lin praises me too much," Gu Mingzhe said loudly, making sure Qin Yuhan heard every syllable. "Of course, the work we handle at the capital level is completely beyond what ordinary people in private shipping offices can imagine. Just this afternoon, our outer ring reviewed a confidential briefing on the special sovereign liquidity reserve anchoring China Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank, along with the recovered asset transfers coming up from Jingzhou City and Shanshui Group in Handong Province."

Qin Haoran leaned forward eagerly. "Brother Mingzhe, I heard ICBC and Bank of China are also part of that massive reserve framework! Is it true that a mysterious central trust backed the whole clean-up?"

"That is classified level-three information, Haoran," Gu Mingzhe said with a smug chuckle, tapping his wine glass as if he practically owned the vault himself. "All I can say at this table is that our director trusted me to proofread the outer summary index for the China Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank sovereign trust line before Monday's audit. Without top-tier capital clearance like mine, an ordinary twelve-thousand-yuan logistics clerk could live ten lifetimes and never even hear the code number of that trust."

He looked straight at Chen Xuan with a mocking smile. "Brother Chen, Aunt Lin mentioned you file freight receipts at a small firm near Lujiazui in Shanghai. Do you even know what a sovereign trust clearing code looks like, or does your office only use paper stamps?"

Qin Yuhan frowned coldly and set her chopsticks down, ready to put Gu Mingzhe in his place.

Under the table, Chen Xuan gently tapped the back of Qin Yuhan's hand to tell her not to bother. He finished pulling the tail shell off a plump shrimp, placed it neatly into Qin Yuhan's bowl, wiped his fingers on a warm towel, and looked up at Gu Mingzhe with an innocent, curious expression.

"Our twelfth-floor shipping desk only stamps customs forms and port receipts, Deputy Chief Gu," Chen Xuan said mildly. "Still, even when we sign a basic clerk confidentiality sheet at China Construction Bank or Huaxia Bank to pay freight fees, rule one on the back of the receipt says that anyone who speaks the name of an active central trust audit at a private dinner table gets flagged by the automatic compliance monitor on their work phone. Does the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office let outer-ring proofreaders leave their work phones turned on while bragging at birthday banquets?"

Gu Mingzhe let out a loud, dismissive laugh. "An automatic compliance monitor on a work phone? Please, Chen Xuan, do not use a small shipping clerk's imagination to measure capital ministry officers! Who would dare monitor my phone at Elder Qin's dinner table?"

Less than fifty meters outside the courtyard wall, inside the quiet black sedan of the Black Order, the team leader glanced at the standard encrypted compliance log on his console and sent a single three-line administrative flag straight to Number 1's automated audit desk at the central task force.

Barely four minutes passed.

Gu Mingzhe was lifting his wine cup to toast Elder Qin Zhengguo when the encrypted ministry work phone in his breast pocket suddenly blared with an urgent, piercing ringtone.

Gu Mingzhe blinked, pulled out the phone, and turned pale when he saw the caller ID of his bureau director. "Excuse me for one moment, Elder Qin. My director is calling me personally, probably about Monday's China Construction Bank file!"

Looking smug, Gu Mingzhe tapped answer, accidentally hitting the speaker button with his sweaty thumb in the quiet hall.

"Gu Mingzhe, have you lost your mind?!" a thunderous voice roared out of the speaker so loudly that every wine glass on the table rattled. "Did you just sit at a private banquet table and brag out loud about the China Construction Bank and Huaxia Bank sovereign trust index while carrying an active task-force phone?! The central compliance desk just flagged your voice timestamp two minutes ago!"

Gu Mingzhe froze like a block of ice, the wine cup slipping from his fingers and splashing across his suit trousers. "Director, I only mentioned the title of the outer index, I did not give any numbers!"

"You are fired from the outer coordinating group effective this second!" the director bellowed. "Get down to the ministry inspection office within twenty minutes and turn in your badge and security pass before the discipline officers come to fetch you!"

The call slammed shut.

The entire banquet hall was so quiet that everyone could hear the roast duck chef slicing meat in the side kitchen. Gu Mingzhe stood trembling like a wet leaf, his face gray as ash, before stumbling backward out of the hall without even picking up his coat.

Around the main table, Lin Shuyin, Second Aunt Zhou Meilin, and Qin Haoran sat with their mouths wide open in total shock.

Chen Xuan did not even glance toward the door. He picked up a fresh peeled shrimp with his clean chopsticks and held it gently toward Qin Yuhan's lips.

"Try this one, Wife," Chen Xuan smiled warmly. "The ginger vinegar sauce in Beijing is pretty good."
