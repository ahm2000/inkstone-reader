# Chapter 119: The Second Month Review

Thin cigarette smoke drifted beneath the lamps of the walnut conference hall inside Beijing's Great Hall. Heavy doors stayed locked, lidded teacups steamed, and gray review binders lay before every seat.

Around the inner ring sat all 24 core voting members of the Capital Top Council, every one at half premier or sub national rank, above provincial party secretaries and cabinet ministers. At the head sat Premier Gu Chengshan, flanked by Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua and General Qin Zhengguo in a plain olive uniform without medals. Behind them in four mahogany chairs sat the white haired Founding Elders: Elder Song, Elder Qin, Elder Ye, and Elder Lin.

In the outer ring sat the non standing agency chiefs and bank presidents: State Security Minister Ding Guoping, Discipline Office Deputy Secretary Gao Zhengyi, Fang Zhengming of China Construction Bank, Han Guoliang of ICBC, Tang Wenhao of Bank of China, and Liang Qingshan of Huaxia Bank. Each kept his notebook open without interrupting, while two archive clerks at the side desk recorded every word into the linen book of sealed state minutes.

Premier Gu Chengshan nodded toward the lectern. "Deliver the sixty day review report."

Ma Jiansheng, deputy director of the Joint National Clean Up Office, stepped forward with a gray folder.

"Premier Gu Chengshan, Founding Elders, and 24 core voting members," Ma Jiansheng reported. "Two months have passed since the clean up began. First, asset recovery: 46,000,000,000,000 yuan in traitors' stolen state funds, hidden shares, overseas property, gold certificates, and shell holdings has been recovered outright or locked inside state trusts."

He turned one page smoothly.

"Second, financial stability: private citizen Chen Xuan's 300,000,000,000,000 yuan clean, legal gift across China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank, with 500,000,000,000,000 yuan still on standby, has kept Beijing and Shanghai markets steady. Not one county bank ran short of cash. Every factory payroll and port shipment cleared on schedule, and grain prices stayed flat. Twice last month, foreign traders tried shorting the yuan overseas and broke their teeth against Bank of China's desk."

Ma Jiansheng turned to the final sheet.

"Third, the personnel tally. One hundred and twenty eight clean investigators, county cadres, and bank auditors have been rapidly promoted, with sixty four awarded national commendations. Three hundred and forty two senior traitors have been removed and charged, with one hundred and eighty six already sentenced to prison and verdicts read aloud across forty one prefectures and counties, including Yunxi County and Sha City. One hundred and twelve negligent officials are under withheld ranks and promotion bans. Fifty eight senior faction figures nationwide have been moved into higher sounding, powerless honorary and advisory titles with zero budget and zero staff. Across sixty days, monitors recorded zero leaks regarding the Black Guard or the benefactor behind Number 1. Report complete."

Ma Jiansheng closed his folder, bowed, and returned to the outer ring.

Silence settled over the hall. Even among the half premier members who had gripped their folders nervously two months earlier, old dread had become quiet awe. The storm had swept forty provinces and central bureaus without cracking a single bank window or frightening an ordinary family.

Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua spoke first, setting his lighter down.

"Two months ago, half the senior advisors in Beijing swore that pulling 46,000,000,000,000 yuan of dirty roots from the soil would snap the financial roof," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said quietly. "Instead, shopkeepers in Sha City, Yunxi County, and Shanghai opened earlier this week than last year. Ordinary people only saw crooked bullies going to prison and honest cadres stepping up."

Humility softened his eyes.

"Every leader here knows why ordinary families barely felt a bump," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua added softly. "That 300,000,000,000,000 yuan free legal shield held the floor solid as bedrock. No strings, no interest, no favors asked. In forty years of service, I have never seen a debt like the one this nation owes an unseen citizen."

In the outer ring, the four bank presidents lowered their eyes respectfully.

General Qin Zhengguo spoke next, his voice steady.

"All three theater commands are completely clean," General Qin Zhengguo reported. "Every crooked procurement officer and contractor paying kickbacks for military land is facing military court. All three commands finished live readiness drills this week without delay, and troop morale stands at a ten year high."

He rested his broad hands beside his binder.

"A soldier understands loyalty better than politics," General Qin Zhengguo said softly. "That unseen citizen handed our country clean truth when moles sat inside our vaults, then placed 300,000,000,000,000 yuan behind our banks so ordinary people would not suffer during the surgery. He did not ask for a title, a seat in this hall, or even a public thank you. That leaves every man in uniform with quiet awe and an unspoken debt we can only repay by keeping this country clean."

Elder Song rested his chin over his hands on his cane, eyes warm and steady.

Premier Gu Chengshan stubbed out his cigarette and looked around all 24 seats.

"We honor that unspoken debt in two ways," Premier Gu Chengshan declared calmly. "First, we never pry and never break our secrecy over the Black Guard and the citizen behind Number 1. Second, we carry this work cleanly into Phase Three. Over the coming year, state trusts will return every recovered factory, land deed, and stolen fund to public benefit, and courts will finish every trial by law. Clean cadres rise fast, negligent officials keep their ranks frozen, and no faction leader exiled to an honorary title ever touches a real seal again."

He slid the Phase Three resolution forward. "We now call the show of hands vote of the 24 core voting members to approve Phase Three. All in favor, raise your right hand."

All 24 right hands rose cleanly around the walnut ring in solemn unity, with zero abstentions and zero against.

"Formal vote recorded in the sealed state minutes: twenty four in favor, zero abstentions, zero against," the chief clerk read aloud. "Phase Three approved unanimously by all 24 core voting members and sealed permanently in the state archives."

The red wax seal pressed onto the linen ledger as the four Founding Elders nodded and the outer ring closed their notebooks, carrying a quiet, permanent awe for the unseen hand that had steadied the wheel.
