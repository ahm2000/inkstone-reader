# Chapter 119: The Capital Suitor's Boast

By evening, Elder Qin's high spirits had carried straight into the birthday banquet. Under the warm brass chandeliers of the main hall, two large round rosewood tables were laid out with steaming Beijing roast duck, poached crystal shrimp, braised sea cucumber, and warm pitchers of aged rice wine.

Just as the hot dishes reached the table, a thirty-year-old man in a stiff dark ministry suit and gold-rimmed glasses strode into the hall carrying a red velvet gift box. It was Gu Mingzhe, a deputy section chief in a capital economic bureau and the very Beijing suitor Lin Shuyin had spent two years trying to match with Qin Yuhan.

"Happy seventy-eighth birthday, Elder Qin!" Gu Mingzhe bowed with practiced formality before straightening his suit jacket with an air of deep self-importance. "Please forgive me for arriving a little late. Our outer coordinating group at the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office had an afternoon briefing that ran over schedule."

At the mention of the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office, Second Aunt's eyes lit up at once. "Oh my goodness, Mingzhe! Is that the special office handling the huge Handong Province asset sweep we saw on the evening news?"

Gu Mingzhe adjusted his gold-rimmed glasses and offered a rehearsed smile that fooled nobody. "I cannot share classified details, Second Aunt, so I will keep it brief. Over 300 billion yuan in frozen assets passed through our desk this month. High-level directives move fast, and my Section Director relies heavily on my reports."

Lin Shuyin beamed with pride and quickly gestured for Gu Mingzhe to sit at the main table. "Sit down, Mingzhe. You work so hard for the country. Not like some people who have all the free time in the world."

Gu Mingzhe sat down smoothly, casting a brief, condescending glance across the table at Chen Xuan, who was calmly peeling a crystal shrimp and placing it into Qin Yuhan's bowl.

"Ah, you must be Yuhan's husband from Shanghai," Gu Mingzhe said, lifting his porcelain wine cup with a superior smile. "Aunt Lin mentioned you work in logistics. That is honest work, of course. Every big city needs clerks to check shipping forms."

Qin Wenbo snickered into his napkin, while Second Aunt chimed in eagerly, "Chen Xuan makes 12,000 yuan a month on the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics, Mingzhe. He leaves work at 4:55 PM sharp every day."

Gu Mingzhe chuckled softly and shook his head as if speaking to a child. "Well, Chen Xuan, if you ever grow tired of stamping forms in Shanghai, feel free to send me your resume. A friend of mine runs a state warehouse outside Beijing's fifth ring road. I could put in a word and get you a supervisor post."

Qin Yuhan set her chopsticks down with a crisp click, her expression turning cold as autumn frost. Before she could speak a sharp word, Chen Xuan gently patted her hand under the tablecloth and smiled easily.

"Thank you for the kind offer, Section Chief Gu," Chen Xuan said in a mild, unhurried voice. "I actually enjoy my 12th floor desk. Still, I am curious about one small thing. If your Asset Recovery Office handles sensitive national cases, is it really allowed to talk about afternoon briefings and frozen asset figures at a private birthday dinner?"

Gu Mingzhe froze for half a second, then let out a loud, dismissive laugh. "Haha, Chen Xuan, you are a private company clerk, so you do not understand how capital ministries operate. Someone at my level knows exactly where the boundaries are. Besides, my Section Director trusts my judgment completely."

Two minutes earlier, Chen Xuan had excused himself briefly to wash his hands in the outer corridor. While drying his fingers, he had sent a single plain text message to Number 1 noting that an outer-ring clerk was using the Capital Cross-Department Asset Recovery Office's name to boast at a private dinner. Number 1 had replied within five seconds: "Handled quietly through discipline channels, Young Master."

Back at the banquet table, Gu Mingzhe puffed out his chest and lifted his wine cup to toast Elder Qin. "To Elder Qin's long life, and to people who actually contribute to the nation!"

Before his cup could even leave the table, his mobile phone began buzzing violently beside his plate. Gu Mingzhe glanced at the screen and smiled smugly at the whole table. "Speak of the devil. It is my Section Director calling. Probably another urgent briefing for tomorrow."

Wanting to show off his importance in front of Qin Yuhan and Lin Shuyin, Gu Mingzhe pressed answer and accidentally tapped the speaker icon instead of holding it to his ear. "Hello, Director! I am at Elder Qin's birthday dinner right now, ready to receive your instructions!"

A furious roar exploded from the phone speaker, echoing loudly across the suddenly silent banquet hall.

"Gu Mingzhe, have you lost your mind?!" the Section Director bellowed. "The central discipline inspection desk just flagged our office for an unauthorized leak! Who gave you permission to drop the Asset Recovery Office's name at a private dinner and brag about confidential briefings?!"

Gu Mingzhe's smug smile vanished. His face turned white as paper. "Director, Director, I did not leak anything! I only mentioned our briefing!"

"What briefing?!" the Director roared. "You are a junior outer-ring clerk! You were never allowed inside the briefing room! Your entire job was stapling press clippings and fetching coffee! Effective immediately, you are suspended from the coordinating group! Report to the discipline office on Monday morning for a full review!"

The line clicked dead with a sharp beep.

In the dead silence of the banquet hall, Gu Mingzhe's fingers went completely numb. His porcelain wine cup slipped straight out of his trembling hand and shattered loudly against the stone floor, splashing warm rice wine across his polished leather shoes.

Lin Shuyin stared at him with her mouth slightly open. Second Aunt and Qin Wenbo shrank back into their chairs, too embarrassed to look anyone in the eye.

Chen Xuan did not even blink. He calmly signaled a servant to sweep up the broken porcelain, placed a slice of crisp Beijing roast duck onto Qin Yuhan's plate, and smiled warmly.

"Eat up, Yuhan. Roast duck tastes best while it is still hot."

At the head of the table, Elder Qin Zhengguo watched Gu Mingzhe scramble up in panic and stammer an apology before fleeing the hall. The old general stroked his chin, his eyes twinkling with quiet amusement, and raised his own cup to Chen Xuan.
