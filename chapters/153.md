# Chapter 153: The Unannounced Guests

"Excuse me, is this Grandma Zhou Guilan's home?"

The clear, gentle voice made every head in the courtyard turn at once.

Qin Yuhan stood just inside the wooden gate in a simple cream wool coat and a light scarf.

"Yuhan?" Chen Xuan stood up, genuine surprise lighting his eyes. "What brings you all the way out to Hongshi Village?"

"I heard today was your grandmother's eighty-first birthday," Qin Yuhan said with a bright, natural smile. She stepped forward and gave Grandma Zhou Guilan a respectful bow. "Happy birthday, Grandma Zhou. I am Qin Yuhan, a friend of Chen Xuan. I brought two tins of mild mountain tea that is gentle on the stomach and some sweet pears."

Liu Meifang hurried out of the kitchen wiping her hands on her apron, beaming the second she saw Qin Yuhan. "Yuhan! Come in! You should have called Xuan to meet you at the bridge."

"It was a lovely walk, Aunt Liu," Qin Yuhan replied warmly, handing over the pears.

Zhou Guilan held Qin Yuhan's cool hand in both of hers, looking at her with pure delight. "So polite, so pretty, and bringing mild tea for an old woman's stomach. Xuan, why did you not tell me you had such a thoughtful friend?"

"I did not know she was coming," Chen Xuan smiled lightly, pulling out a clean bamboo stool for her.

Before anyone else could speak, Liu Guoliang let out a loud snort from across the table.

"Well, well," Liu Guoliang said, tapping his gold watch against the wood. "Two plain bamboo tins and a basket of pears. Let me guess, young lady, you work at that same little shipping office with Xuan, right? Another clerk?"

Qin Yuhan turned her calm gaze toward him. Her smile stayed polite, though it cooled slightly. "I work in shipping too, yes."

"Ha! I knew it!" Liu Guoliang laughed loudly, pointing at Chen Jianguo and Chen Mingde. "Look at your son's circle, Jianguo. Birds of a feather flock together. A 12,000-yuan clerk brings home another clerk with cheap bamboo tins, and everybody acts like it is a grand occasion. Down in Shenzhen, the Shen Family port managers drink old-tree tea that costs twenty thousand yuan a single box!"

"Uncle Guoliang, that is enough!" Chen Yuan snapped, her cheeks flushed. "Yuhan is our guest!"

"I am just telling the truth," Liu Guoliang puffed, lifting his teacup smugly. "Out in the real world, connections and power decide everything."

Chen Jianguo's jaw clenched hard.

Outside the courtyard, however, the village lane grew suddenly quiet.

There was no loud horn. Ten minutes after Qin Yuhan's arrival, two plain black sedans with provincial plates stopped respectfully fifty meters down near the stone bridge. Soon, steady footsteps crunched along the gravel path outside the gate.

Three men appeared in the wooden doorway.

The man in front was in his late fifties, dressed in an ordinary dark winter jacket and plain cloth shoes. Behind him walked a younger secretary holding a simple cloth parcel, while the driver stayed quietly outside the gate.

The moment the older man stepped into the courtyard, Chen Jianguo froze. His teacup tilted, spilling hot water across the table.

Beside him, Chen Mingde shot up so fast his bamboo stool tipped onto the bricks. "Wait, that is the Provincial Party Secretary!"

Two old neighbors gasped and scrambled to their feet.

It was Provincial Party Secretary Zhao Dehai. Every household in Hongshi Village saw his face on the evening news three times a week.

Zhao Dehai walked straight into the courtyard with a warm, easy smile and held out both hands to Grandma Zhou Guilan.

"Old Aunt Zhou," Zhao Dehai said kindly, his voice rich and unhurried. "I hope we are not interrupting your lunch. I was passing through the county on a rural inspection trip and heard today is your eighty-first birthday. Since we were ten minutes away, I stopped by to wish you good health and a long life."

Zhou Guilan blinked in happy surprise, wiping her hands before shaking his. "Oh, good heavens! Leader Zhao, you walked all the way up our little lane just to see an old woman like me? Please sit! Meifang, bring fresh hot tea!"

"No need to fuss, Aunt Zhou," Zhao Dehai laughed gently. He took the cloth-wrapped box from his secretary and set it respectfully beside Chen Xuan's boxes of walnut pastries, sliding Liu Guoliang's flashy red box aside without even glancing at it. "Just dried mountain jujubes and herbal tea from the county farm."

Then Zhao Dehai turned to Chen Jianguo and Chen Mingde, shaking each man's hand firmly. "Comrade Chen Jianguo, Comrade Chen Mingde, good to see you. Honest working families like yours are the backbone of this province. You have raised your children well."

"Thank you, Secretary Zhao," Chen Jianguo replied, his voice unsteady with pride. "We are truly honored."

Zhao Dehai's eyes moved to Qin Yuhan, and he gave her a warm, familiar nod. "Miss Qin, you beat me here. How is your uncle doing this week?"

"He is very well, Secretary Zhao," Qin Yuhan answered gracefully. "Thank you for asking."

Finally, Zhao Dehai stopped in front of Chen Xuan. His relaxed smile deepened into something quieter and far more respectful. He reached out and shook Chen Xuan's hand with both of his own, holding it for a brief, steady moment.

"Young man," Zhao Dehai said softly. "Take good care of your grandmother today."

"I will, Secretary Zhao," Chen Xuan replied calmly with a polite nod. "Thank you for making the trip. Please watch the cold wind on the road back."

Across the yard, Liu Guoliang stood frozen like stone.

The smug grin had vanished so completely it looked slapped off his face. Every drop of blood drained from his cheeks, leaving his skin pale as wet ash. His eyes jumped between the Provincial Party Secretary, the familiar nod Zhao Dehai gave Qin Yuhan, and the two-handed handshake Zhao Dehai had just given the "tiny 12,000-yuan clerk."

Liu Guoliang's throat went bone dry. Every loud boast about Shenzhen, 2 million yuan orders, and the Shen Family died inside his chest. His knees felt weak, and the teacup in his trembling hand rattled softly against its saucer.

Zhao Dehai checked his watch and waved warmly to the stunned neighbors.

"I will not delay your lunch hour," Zhao Dehai smiled at Zhou Guilan. "Enjoy your long-life noodles, Aunt Zhou. I wish you peace and health every year."

"Thank you, Secretary Zhao! Safe travels!" Zhou Guilan called happily, walking him halfway to the gate with Chen Jianguo and Chen Xuan beside her.

Ten minutes after arriving, Zhao Dehai walked calmly back down the lane and drove away without a single honk.

Inside the silent courtyard, every pair of eyes slowly turned toward the pale, sweating man shrinking back against the brick wall.
