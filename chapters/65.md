# Chapter 65: Ministry of Public Security: Ten Bureaus on Red Alert

At eight twenty-two on Monday morning, red alert lamps pulsed above the Ministry of Public Security national command table. Cigarette smoke hung over the glowing map of fourteen provinces as ten bureau directors stood rigid around the console, waiting for Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming to hang up the red encrypted receiver.

Guo Zhengming set the phone down with a sharp clack, lit a fresh cigarette, and swept his cold gaze across all ten faces.

"Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua just signed the yellow dispatch order," Guo Zhengming said, voice slicing through the hum of the room. "Eight minutes until eight thirty. Ten bureaus are on red alert. We execute two tracks simultaneously, and anyone who mixes Track One into Track Two loses his badge on the spot."

Command and Operations Bureau Director Fu Zhigang tapped his console, splitting the wall screen. Left glowed fourteen coastal ports and provincial capitals; right pulsed the county grid of Yunxi.

"Track One first," Guo Zhengming ordered sharply. "Border and Port Security Director Ren Changhe, State Security Director Han Dongsheng handed over the maritime smuggling manifests. Seal every coastal container terminal, private marina, and international airport tarmac exit now. Nobody tied to Fang Qiming's Shengyuan Capital or Customs Director Xu Wenbin leaves the mainland."

Ren Changhe straightened, jaw tight as he spoke into his shoulder mic. "Transmitting red-flag border holds across fourteen coastal provinces now, Minister Guo Zhengming. Every passport flagged by the National People's Congress and Consultative Conference is locked in the gate system. Even a private charter jet gets zero runway clearance."

"Economic Crime Director Zhu Haoran," Guo Zhengming snapped, turning left. "At nine o'clock sharp, twenty minutes before the Reform Commission severs the shadow-clearing pipes, your teams hit Shengyuan Capital's onshore brokerages and all forty-eight rigged mineral headquarters. Grab the cold-storage servers before Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang's managers touch a shredder."

Zhu Haoran took a sharp drag, eyes gleaming coldly. "Six hundred economic-crime officers are staged in unmarked vans three blocks from every target, Minister Guo Zhengming. Nine o'clock sharp, we kick the doors and bag every drive."

"Cyber Security Director Teng Xiaoming," Guo Zhengming continued briskly. "Keep domestic networks clean. Not one rumor of overnight capital arrests, not one word about sealed tribunals, and zero photographs of military movements reach foreign wires before noon."

"Backbone filters are live," Teng Xiaoming replied, fingers flying across his tablet. "Anything tied to Track One drops into quarantine in two seconds."

Guo Zhengming paused, his stare turning icy as he looked down the table at Internal Discipline and Inspection Bureau Director Ke Zhenhua. "Before we touch Yunxi County, tell me our own house is clean. Did anyone leak the staging order to Yunxi County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong?"

The air around the console chilled instantly.

Ke Zhenhua stepped forward, pale with anger, dropping a printed call log beside the ashtray. "One rat tried, Minister Guo Zhengming. At seven fifty-eight, a duty officer at the provincial relay desk attempted to dial Liang Guodong's private burner from a hallway landline. Criminal Intelligence Director Yin Baoshan flagged the number in real time, and my Internal Discipline team tackled him before the second ring. The call never connected."

Guo Zhengming's jaw locked until a white ridge stood along his cheek. Ash broke off his cigarette onto the steel console.

"Put that officer in solitary custody and pull every name in his contacts," Guo Zhengming said softly, the quiet tone making two directors stiffen in dread. "Yin Baoshan, confirm now: is Liang Guodong still sitting comfortably in his Yunxi office?"

Criminal Intelligence Bureau Director Yin Baoshan tapped his earpiece and nodded. "Confirmed, Minister Guo Zhengming. Street cameras show Liang Guodong entered Yunxi County Public Security Bureau at eight fifteen. Deputy County Magistrate Sun Weiming is inside the county compound. Developer Deng Yongchang is at his construction headquarters, and enforcers Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao are eating breakfast at a tea house near Old Street. None of them suspect a thing."

"Good," Guo Zhengming said, exhaling thick smoke and pointing at the right screen. "Now execute Track Two in broad daylight. Yunxi County is an ordinary, airtight provincial law-enforcement sweep. Every charge is local, every piece of evidence is local, and the whole province watches it happen cleanly."

Transport and Traffic Control Bureau Director Dong Mingyuan tapped three red checkpoints onto the county map. "All three expressway exits and the old national road out of Yunxi lock down at eight twenty-nine under routine heavy-truck inspection. Not even a motorcycle slips past once our convoy rolls in."

"Criminal Investigation Director Xing Weidong and Anti-Gang Director Qiao Jianjun," Guo Zhengming ordered. "Your provincial joint units take Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, Deng Yongchang, Yan Tiehu, and Cheng Biao simultaneously at eight forty-five. Zero warning calls to the local station. Seize the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau command room first so Liang Guodong cannot burn a single detention log or forged confession."

Xing Weidong crushed his cigarette dead. "Provincial investigators hold arrest warrants signed by Procuratorate Director Pei Hong. Every bribery ledger and forced-demolition contract is already secured."

"And my Anti-Gang teams hold Yan Tiehu's and Cheng Biao's organized-crime files ready for instant arraignment," Qiao Jianjun added fiercely. "Once those thugs are in irons, every Old Street shopkeeper will testify."

"Counter-Terrorism Director Lei Zhendong," Guo Zhengming said, locking eyes with the tall veteran at the table's end. "The military-grade explosives planted against Old Street civilians fall squarely under your counter-terrorism statute. Your ordnance specialists seize Deng Yongchang's blasting warehouse before nine and trace every detonator serial number. Make sure neither Deng Yongchang nor Yan Tiehu sees daylight outside maximum security."

"Briefed and rolling," Lei Zhendong answered flatly. "Two counter-terrorism bomb squads ride in the lead armored vans. If Deng Yongchang hid one gram of blasting gel inside Yunxi County, we pull it out before lunch."

Guo Zhengming turned last to Fu Zhigang, voice dropping with the full weight of Premier Gu Chengshan's and Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's direct orders.

"Repeat the primary civilian directive for the lead car, Fu Zhigang."

Fu Zhigang stood straight and recited cleanly. "The moment provincial officers control Yunxi County Public Security Bureau, the Provincial Joint Inspection Group rescinds the wrongful detention of Old Street homeowner Chen Mingde, hands him his signed official exoneration certificate with a formal apology, and escorts him safely home. Under no circumstances is any member of Chen Mingde's family summoned, questioned, or asked to step foot inside a police station. Zero disturbance, zero media intrusion, zero blowback."

"Make sure every commander in the convoy hears that twice," Guo Zhengming commanded.

On the wall display, the red clock clicked to eight thirty sharp.

A green confirmation tone chimed through the smoke. Fu Zhigang pressed his headset to his ear, listened three seconds, and looked up with fierce satisfaction.

"Minister Guo Zhengming, the provincial joint task force has crossed the Yunxi County line. Forty vehicles, sirens off, warrants hot. Fifteen minutes to target."

Guo Zhengming crushed his cigarette dead in the steel tray. Across the pulsing red lights of the command hall, ten bureaus locked into motion as the iron net dropped over Yunxi County.
