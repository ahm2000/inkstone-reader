# Chapter 65: Ministry of Public Security: Ten Bureaus on Red Alert

At eight twenty-two on Monday morning, red alert lights pulsed above the Ministry of Public Security national command table. Cigarette smoke hung over the glowing map of fourteen provinces. Ten bureau directors stood straight around the table, waiting for Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming to hang up the red secure phone.

Guo Zhengming put the receiver down with a sharp click, lit a fresh cigarette, and swept his cold eyes across all ten faces.

"Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua just signed the yellow dispatch order," Guo Zhengming said, his voice cutting through the quiet room. "Eight minutes until eight thirty. Ten bureaus are on red alert. We run two tracks at the same time, and anyone who mixes Track One into Track Two loses his badge on the spot."

Command and Operations Bureau Director Fu Zhigang tapped his screen, splitting the wall map in two. The left side showed fourteen coastal ports and provincial capitals. The right side pulsed with the street grid of Yunxi County.

"Track One first," Guo Zhengming ordered sharply. "Border and Port Security Director Ren Changhe, State Security Director Han Dongsheng handed over the smuggling lists. Lock down every coastal container port, private boat dock, and international airport runway exit right now. Nobody tied to Fang Qiming's Shengyuan Capital or Customs Director Xu Wenbin leaves the mainland."

Ren Changhe straightened, his jaw tight as he spoke into his shoulder radio. "Sending red-flag border blocks across fourteen coastal provinces now, Minister Guo Zhengming. Every passport flagged by the National People's Congress and Consultative Conference is locked in the gate system. Even a private jet gets zero runway clearance."

"Economic Crime Director Zhu Haoran," Guo Zhengming snapped, turning left. "At nine o'clock sharp, twenty minutes before the Reform Commission cuts the hidden money channels, your teams hit Shengyuan Capital's local offices and all forty-eight rigged mining headquarters. Grab the offline computer servers before Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang's managers touch a shredder."

Zhu Haoran took a sharp drag, his eyes gleaming coldly. "Six hundred economic-crime officers are waiting in unmarked vans three blocks from every target, Minister Guo Zhengming. At nine o'clock sharp, we kick the doors in and bag every hard drive."

"Cyber Security Director Teng Xiaoming," Guo Zhengming went on briskly. "Keep domestic networks clean. Not one rumor of overnight capital arrests, not one word about secret trials, and zero photos of troop movements reach foreign news before noon."

"Network filters are live," Teng Xiaoming replied, his fingers flying across his tablet. "Anything tied to Track One gets pulled down in two seconds."

Guo Zhengming paused. His gaze turned icy as he looked down the table at Internal Discipline and Inspection Bureau Director Ke Zhenhua. "Before we touch Yunxi County, tell me our own house is clean. Did anyone tip off Yunxi County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong?"

The air around the table turned freezing cold.

Ke Zhenhua stepped forward, pale with rage, and dropped a printed call log beside the ashtray. "One rat tried, Minister Guo Zhengming. At seven fifty-eight, a duty officer at the provincial relay desk tried to call Liang Guodong's secret burner phone from a hallway landline. Criminal Intelligence Director Yin Baoshan spotted the number right away, and my Internal Discipline team tackled him before the second ring. The call never went through."

Guo Zhengming's jaw locked until a white ridge stood out along his cheek. Ash broke off his cigarette and fell onto the steel console.

"Put that officer in a solo cell and pull every name from his contacts," Guo Zhengming said softly. His quiet voice made two directors stiffen in fear. "Yin Baoshan, confirm right now: is Liang Guodong still sitting comfortably in his Yunxi office?"

Criminal Intelligence Bureau Director Yin Baoshan pressed his earpiece and nodded. "Confirmed, Minister Guo Zhengming. Street cameras show Liang Guodong walked into the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau at eight fifteen. County Magistrate Sun Weiming is inside the county office building. Developer Deng Yongchang is at his construction headquarters, and thugs Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao are eating breakfast at a tea house near Old Street. None of them suspect a thing."

"Good," Guo Zhengming said, blowing out thick smoke and pointing at the right screen. "Now run Track Two in broad daylight. Yunxi County is an ordinary, airtight provincial police sweep. Every charge is local, every piece of proof is local, and the whole province watches it happen cleanly."

Transport and Traffic Control Bureau Director Dong Mingyuan tapped three red checkpoints onto the county map. "All three highway exits and the old national road out of Yunxi lock down at eight twenty-nine under routine heavy-truck checks. Not even a motorbike slips out once our convoy rolls in."

"Criminal Investigation Director Xing Weidong and Anti-Gang Director Qiao Jianjun," Guo Zhengming ordered. "Your provincial joint teams grab Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, Deng Yongchang, Yan Tiehu, and Cheng Biao all at the same time at eight forty-five. Zero warning calls to the local police station. Take the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau command room first so Liang Guodong cannot burn a single jail log or fake confession."

Xing Weidong crushed his cigarette dead. "Provincial investigators hold arrest warrants signed by Procuratorate Director Pei Hong. Every bribe record and forced-demolition contract is already locked in."

"And my Anti-Gang teams have Yan Tiehu's and Cheng Biao's gang files ready for instant charges," Qiao Jianjun added fiercely. "Once those thugs are in cuffs, every Old Street shopkeeper will speak up."

"Counter-Terrorism Director Lei Zhendong," Guo Zhengming said, locking eyes with the tall veteran at the end of the table. "The military-grade explosives planted against Old Street families fall straight under your counter-terrorism law. Your bomb experts seize Deng Yongchang's blasting warehouse before nine and trace every detonator number. Make sure neither Deng Yongchang nor Yan Tiehu ever sees daylight outside a high-security prison."

"Briefed and rolling," Lei Zhendong answered flatly. "Two counter-terrorism bomb squads are riding in the lead armored vans. If Deng Yongchang hid one gram of blasting gel inside Yunxi County, we pull it out before lunch."

Guo Zhengming turned last to Fu Zhigang. His voice dropped with the full weight of Premier Gu Chengshan's and Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's direct orders.

"Repeat the main civilian order for the lead car, Fu Zhigang."

Fu Zhigang stood straight and spoke cleanly. "The second provincial officers take control of the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau, the Provincial Joint Inspection Group cancels the wrongful arrest of Old Street homeowner Chen Mingde, hands him his signed official clearance paper with a formal apology, and drives him safely home. Under no circumstances is anyone in Chen Mingde's family called in, questioned, or asked to step inside a police station. Zero trouble, zero press crowding them, zero fallout."

"Make sure every commander in the convoy hears that twice," Guo Zhengming commanded.

On the wall display, the red clock clicked to eight thirty sharp.

A green chime sounded through the smoke. Fu Zhigang pressed his headset to his ear, listened for three seconds, and looked up with fierce satisfaction.

"Minister Guo Zhengming, the provincial joint task force has crossed the Yunxi County line. Forty vehicles, sirens off, warrants live. Fifteen minutes to target."

Guo Zhengming crushed his cigarette dead in the steel tray. Under the pulsing red lights of the command hall, ten bureaus snapped into motion as the iron net dropped over Yunxi County.
