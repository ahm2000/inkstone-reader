# Chapter 65: Ministry of Public Security: Ten Bureaus on Red Alert

At eight sixteen on Monday morning, red alert lights pulsed above the Ministry of Public Security national command table. Smoke hung over the glowing map of fourteen provinces. Ten bureau directors stood straight around the table, waiting for Minister of Public Security Guo Zhengming to hang up the red phone.

Guo Zhengming set the receiver down with a sharp click, lit a cigarette, and swept his cold eyes across all ten faces.

"Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua just signed the yellow dispatch order," Guo Zhengming said, voice cutting through the quiet room. "Four minutes until the convoy crosses the county line at eight twenty. Ten bureaus are on red alert. We run two tracks at once, and anyone who mixes Track One into Track Two loses his badge on the spot."

Command and Operations Bureau Director Fu Zhigang split the wall map in two. The left showed fourteen coastal ports and provincial capitals. The right pulsed with the street grid of Yunxi County.

"Track One first," Guo Zhengming ordered. "Border and Port Security Director Ren Changhe, State Security Director Han Dongsheng handed over the smuggling lists. Lock down every coastal container port, private boat dock, and international airport runway exit now. Nobody tied to Zhao Ruilong's Shanshui Group or Public Security Chief Qi Tongwei leaves the mainland."

Ren Changhe straightened and spoke into his shoulder radio. "Sending red-flag border blocks across fourteen coastal provinces now, Minister Guo Zhengming. Every passport flagged by the National People's Congress and Consultative Conference is locked in the gate system. Even a private jet gets zero runway clearance."

"Economic Crime Director Zhu Haoran," Guo Zhengming snapped. "At nine o'clock sharp, twenty minutes before the Reform Commission cuts the hidden channels, your teams hit Shanshui Group's local offices and all forty-eight rigged mining headquarters. Grab the offline servers before Ministry Division Chief Zhao Dehan's managers touch a shredder."

Zhu Haoran took a sharp drag, eyes gleaming coldly. "Six hundred economic-crime officers are waiting in unmarked vans three blocks from every target. At nine o'clock sharp, we kick the doors in and bag every hard drive."

"Cyber Security Director Teng Xiaoming," Guo Zhengming went on briskly. "Keep domestic networks clean. Not one rumor of overnight capital arrests, not one word about secret trials, and zero photos of troop movements reach foreign news before noon."

"Network filters are live," Teng Xiaoming replied, fingers flying across his tablet. "Anything tied to Track One gets pulled down in two seconds."

Guo Zhengming paused, his gaze turning icy on Internal Discipline and Inspection Bureau Director Ke Zhenhua. "Before we touch Yunxi County, tell me our own house is clean. Did anyone tip off Yunxi County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong?"

The air around the table turned freezing cold.

Ke Zhenhua stepped forward, pale with rage, and dropped a call log beside the ashtray. "One rat tried, Minister Guo Zhengming. At seven fifty-eight, a duty officer at the provincial relay desk tried to call Liang Guodong's secret burner phone from a hallway landline. Criminal Intelligence Director Yin Baoshan spotted the number right away, and my team tackled him before the second ring. The call never went through."

Guo Zhengming's jaw locked until a white ridge stood out along his cheek. Ash broke off his cigarette onto the console.

"Put that officer in a solo cell and pull every name from his contacts," Guo Zhengming said softly, making two directors stiffen in fear. "Yin Baoshan, confirm right now: is Liang Guodong still sitting comfortably in his Yunxi office?"

Criminal Intelligence Bureau Director Yin Baoshan pressed his earpiece and nodded. "Confirmed. Street cameras show Liang Guodong walked into the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau at eight fifteen. County Magistrate Sun Weiming is in the county office building. Developer Deng Yongchang is at his construction headquarters, and Cheng Biao is at Old Street Station. Thug Yan Tiehu is eating breakfast at a tea house near Old Street. None of them suspect a thing."

"Good," Guo Zhengming said, pointing at the right screen. "Now run Track Two in broad daylight. Yunxi County is an ordinary, airtight provincial police sweep. Every charge is local, every piece of proof is local, and the whole province watches it happen cleanly."

Transport and Traffic Control Bureau Director Dong Mingyuan tapped three checkpoints onto the map. "All three highway exits and the old national road out of Yunxi lock down at eight twenty under routine heavy-truck checks. Not even a motorbike slips out once our convoy rolls in."

"Criminal Investigation Director Xing Weidong and Anti-Gang Director Qiao Jianjun," Guo Zhengming ordered. "Your provincial joint teams grab Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, Deng Yongchang, Yan Tiehu, and Cheng Biao at the same time at eight twenty-five. Zero warning calls to the local station. Take the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau command room first so Liang Guodong cannot burn a single jail log or fake confession."

Xing Weidong crushed his cigarette. "Provincial investigators hold arrest warrants signed by Procuratorate Director Pei Hong. Every bribe record and forced-demolition contract is locked in."

"And my Anti-Gang teams have Yan Tiehu's and Cheng Biao's gang files ready for instant charges," Qiao Jianjun added fiercely. "Once those thugs are in cuffs, every Old Street shopkeeper will speak up."

"Counter-Terrorism Director Lei Zhendong," Guo Zhengming said, locking eyes with the tall veteran. "The military-grade explosives planted against Old Street families fall under your counter-terrorism law. Your bomb experts seize Deng Yongchang's blasting warehouse before nine and trace every detonator number. Make sure neither Deng Yongchang nor Yan Tiehu ever sees daylight outside a high-security prison."

"Briefed and rolling," Lei Zhendong answered flatly. "Two bomb squads are riding in the lead armored vans. If Deng Yongchang hid one gram of blasting gel inside Yunxi County, we pull it out before lunch."

Guo Zhengming turned last to Fu Zhigang, voice carrying the full weight of Premier Gu Chengshan's and Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua's direct orders.

"Repeat the main civilian order for the lead car, Fu Zhigang."

Fu Zhigang stood straight and spoke cleanly. "The second provincial officers take control of the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau, the Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau cancels the wrongful arrest of Old Street homeowner Chen Mingde, hands him his signed official clearance paper with a formal apology, and drives him safely home. Under no circumstances is anyone in Chen Mingde's family called in, questioned, or asked to step inside a police station. Zero trouble, zero press, zero fallout."

"Make sure every commander in the convoy hears that twice," Guo Zhengming commanded.

On the wall display, the red clock clicked to eight twenty.

A green chime sounded. Fu Zhigang listened to his headset for three seconds, then looked up with fierce satisfaction.

"Minister Guo Zhengming, the Anti-Corruption Bureau joint task force has crossed the Yunxi County line. Twenty-eight vehicles, sirens off, warrants live. Five minutes to target."

Guo Zhengming crushed his cigarette in the steel tray. Under the pulsing red lights, ten bureaus snapped into motion as the iron net dropped over Yunxi County.
