# Chapter 58: CCDI and National Supervision: The 100-Trillion Shock

At seven twenty on Monday morning, the dark wooden table inside the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection meeting room vanished under bound bank printouts and low cigarette smoke. Ten bureau chiefs sat shoulder to shoulder, faces gray under the ceiling lights.

Gao Zhengyi sat at the center with his sleeves rolled up, silently watching the veteran Chief Financial Auditor across from him.

Lu Jianmin was sixty-one. He had audited three banking crises and forty ministerial crackdowns without blinking. Now his hands shook so hard the pages rattled like dry leaves as his eyes checked the twenty-year national loss sheet a fourth time.

Snap.

The sharp crack echoed across the quiet room. Blue ink spilled from the broken fountain pen in Lu Jianmin's fist, dripping across his pale knuckles onto the grand total.

"104 trillion yuan," Lu Jianmin whispered, voice hollow. "Stacked-up losses, stolen state guarantees, fake land prices, and hidden overseas deals over twenty years. Gao Zhengyi, this cannot be real. A country does not lose 100 trillion yuan without every bank in Beijing noticing."

"They did not steal it from central vaults, Lu Jianmin," Gao Zhengyi said coldly, tapping ash into a brass dish. "They drained it through ten thousand tiny pipes, one county street at a time. Open Section Nine."

First Inspection Bureau Chief Peng Dehua flipped angrily to the tab. "Section Nine is county demolition contracts! Gao Zhengyi, what does clearing an old street in a small county have to do with a 100 trillion yuan overseas drain?"

"Look at page six," Asset-Recovery Bureau Chief Cai Rong snapped, jabbing a finger into the paper. "Yunxi County. Old Street rebuilding project. Official land value: 480 million yuan. Actual bank loans pulled using those same deeds through three provincial shell companies: 6.2 billion yuan."

Silence hit the room like a slap. Second Inspection Bureau Chief Xu Mingfang squinted through her smoke. "6.2 billion yuan borrowed on a 500 million yuan block? How did a county land office clear a fourteen-to-one loan gap?"

"Because they did not borrow against it just once," Banking-Compliance Liaison Chief Ren Xiaolan cut in, voice tight with dread. "Look at the Yunxi developer, Deng Yongchang. His company handed those Old Street land papers to a provincial building fund, bundled them with forty-two other county demolition jobs across seven provinces, then packaged and sold the loans through coastal shell companies."

"And where did the cash go once the shell companies cleared the loans?" Case-Review Bureau Chief Shao Weimin demanded.

"Straight into the Zurich Northern Ledger," Cross-Border Tracing Bureau Chief Ding Yanhua answered grimly, slapping a second binder open. "Every county demolition gave a noisy local payout to the developer, county officials, and street thugs forcing families to sign. In Yunxi, County Magistrate Sun Weiming and County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong pocketed a few million each to bury complaints and lock up stubborn homeowners. Meanwhile, the fake land papers went up to provincial middlemen, then out through fake shipping bills to Zurich."

County-Governance Oversight Chief Pan Zhiming went pale. "Wait a minute. In Yunxi County, a demolition gang run by Yan Tiehu and Cheng Biao bullies ordinary families off Old Street. Sun Weiming stamps a fake land price report. And that tiny county file pulls out a 1 billion dollar loan drain bleeding national funds?"

"Multiply Yunxi County by two thousand counties over twenty years," Lu Jianmin said hoarsely, wiping blue ink off his hand. "Add interest, state bond gaps, rigged buying, and stolen mining limits. Every county thought it was running a small local scam. Nobody at the bottom saw the top of the pipe, and nobody at the top ever got dust on his shoes walking down a county street."

State-Enterprise Supervision Chief Ge Hongwei crushed out his cigarette with shaking hands. "Our inspection teams visited Yunxi's province twice in five years! How did our own auditors miss fourteen-to-one fake loan deals sitting right in the open?"

"Because the provincial audit office was on the same payroll," Evidence-Verification Bureau Chief Wei Guoping snapped, sliding a red folder down the table. "Three deputy directors of the provincial finance office are already on the overnight list of three hundred and eighteen arrested suspects. They buried every complaint from Yunxi and forty other counties as ordinary local demolition fights."

Peng Dehua cursed and lit a new cigarette. "This is betraying the country's economy. If we announce a 100 trillion yuan drain tomorrow, the bond market crashes before lunch and provincial banks face bank runs by three o'clock!"

"Which is why we are not announcing a panic, Peng Dehua," Gao Zhengyi said, eyes hard through the haze. "Read the recovery pages in the back. Whoever cracked this ring already froze the overseas accounts in Zurich, Singapore, and Luxembourg before dawn using top-level state codes we did not even know existed."

Lu Jianmin's eyes went wide. "The overseas money is locked? Not drained away?"

"Locked and marked to be brought back," Ding Yanhua confirmed, voice shaking with awe and fear. "My team checked ten minutes ago. Every shell account tied to the three hundred and eighteen suspects froze solid at two forty-five. No court order on earth moves that fast across four continents. Gao Zhengyi, who did this?"

Gao Zhengyi took a slow drag. "You do not write their name in reports or notes. Your job is to verify every domestic yuan, trace every provincial middleman, and lock down the paper trail before eight o'clock."

"What about the local Yunxi players?" Shao Weimin asked sharply, tapping the names Deng Yongchang, Sun Weiming, Liang Guodong, Cheng Biao, and Yan Tiehu. "Why were none of them grabbed overnight? Every provincial backer above them is locked up, yet Yunxi's crew is still eating breakfast."

"Because they were left for us," Gao Zhengyi replied evenly, standing and buttoning his jacket. "The night sweep cut out the giant cancer in the dark. Yunxi's local rot gets handled in broad daylight under ordinary discipline, anti-gang, and criminal laws. No mystery. No panic. Every framed homeowner walks out completely clean."

He looked around the smoke-filled table at the ten shaken chiefs.

"Forty minutes until Premier Gu Chengshan and Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua open the War Room. Wipe the ink off your hands, Lu Jianmin. Bring me the verified recovery totals, the bank safety fund plan, and arrest warrants for every provincial trust director on that page. Move."

Lu Jianmin stared at his broken pen, jaw tight, then grabbed a pencil and pulled the 100 trillion yuan ledger back under the lamp.
