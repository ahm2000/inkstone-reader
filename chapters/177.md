# Chapter 177: Saturday at IFC Mall and Nanjing Road

Saturday morning arrived crisp and bright, with clear skies over the Pudong skyline. Sunshine slanted through the dining room windows of Emerald Riverside Villa, catching the steam rising from a pot of golden millet porridge and a plate of freshly pan-fried pork buns.

Chen Xuan had woken up early to make breakfast, flipping the buns until their bottoms turned crisp and golden. By the time Chen Hao and Chen Yuan trotted downstairs in their socks, the whole first floor smelled like a neighborhood breakfast stall at its finest.

"I could live here forever," Chen Hao mumbled with half a bun already in his mouth. "Brother Xuan, if your logistics company ever fires you, open a bun shop outside my university gate."

"I have a very stable desk on the 12th floor, thank you," Chen Xuan said, sliding a saucer of dark vinegar across the table. "Finish your porridge. Your sister-in-law already mapped out our morning."

Qin Yuhan came downstairs in an ivory cashmere coat, a soft gray scarf, and leather boots, looking rested and bright. Chen Xuan threw on his dark gray quilted jacket and sneakers, tucking his single smartphone into one pocket and sliding his dented green thermos into his canvas tote bag.

Their first stop was Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street across the river. Even in late November, the wide stone avenue bustled with energy. Golden leaves from the old plane trees drifted past historic department stores, neon signs, and slow-moving sightseeing trams ringing their brass bells.

Chen Yuan walked between Qin Yuhan and Chen Hao, her eyes darting happily from bakery windows to glowing storefronts. Halfway down the block, the sweet, smoky scent of roasted chestnuts drifted across the walkway.

"Wait here," Chen Xuan said, stepping over to a wooden cart under a plane tree.

The chestnut vendor was a cheerful uncle in his late fifties wearing a knitted cap and a dark canvas apron. He looked up, greeted Chen Xuan with a completely natural, neighborly grin, and scooped a generous mound of hot, glossy chestnuts into a paper bag.

"Bringing the young ones out for a walk today?" the vendor asked warmly, adding an extra handful before folding the bag shut. "Keep warm, young man. The wind picks up near the river."

"Thanks, Uncle," Chen Xuan replied easily, tapping his phone to pay. "Business looks good this morning."

"Always busy on a sunny Saturday," the uncle chuckled, turning back to stir the dark pebbles in his iron roaster.

Chen Xuan handed the warm bag to Chen Yuan. As they strolled along cracking hot chestnuts, Chen Hao stopped in front of the old clock tower and struck a stiff, dramatic pose in his puffy navy county jacket.

"Yuan, take a photo! Get the whole tower behind me so I can send it to my roommates!"

Chen Yuan raised her phone, waited until Chen Hao puffed out his chest, and then snapped the picture right as a passing pigeon swooped across his face.

"Look, Hao, you made a new friend," she giggled, showing him the screen.

"Hey! Delete that!" Chen Hao yelped, chasing her ten steps down the walkway while Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan laughed behind them. Qin Yuhan raised her phone and caught a candid photo of the cousins bickering under the autumn sun, then snapped Chen Xuan peeling a chestnut with total focus.

By late morning, they crossed back to Lujiazui and stepped inside IFC Mall. The soaring marble atrium and gleaming luxury storefronts felt like another world. Soft music floated from hidden speakers, and the air smelled faintly of fresh lilies and espresso.

Chen Hao slowed down, pulling his navy jacket tighter. He whispered to Chen Xuan, "Brother Xuan, even the floor tiles look cleaner than my school desk. If I drop a chestnut shell, will they charge me a thousand yuan?"

"Probably two thousand," Chen Xuan whispered back deadpan, making Chen Hao jump before realizing he was being teased.

Qin Yuhan turned with a warm smile and took Chen Yuan by the hand. "Since you two came all this way in late November, your sister-in-law is buying you winter coats."

"Oh, no, Sister-in-law, we already brought jackets!" Chen Yuan said quickly. "Grandma said we must not make you spend money on us."

"Grandma also told me to take good care of you," Qin Yuhan replied lightly, steering Chen Yuan into a bright women's boutique.

Within fifteen minutes, Qin Yuhan picked out an oatmeal wool coat, a soft cream turtleneck sweater, and brown leather ankle boots. When Chen Yuan stepped out of the fitting room, the tailored wool coat suited her beautifully, making Chen Hao whistle softly.

Then Chen Yuan peeked at the white price tag inside the sleeve and nearly choked. 6,800 yuan.

"Sister-in-law, this is way too expensive!" Chen Yuan stammered, starting to unbutton the coat. "A regular coat back home is three hundred yuan!"

Qin Yuhan gently fastened the button back up and smoothed the collar. "Yuan, you are Xuan's little sister, which makes you my little sister too. Winter wind on campus is freezing. Keep it on, and let me feel like a proper older sister."

Seeing the genuine warmth in Qin Yuhan's eyes, Chen Yuan felt her nose tingle. She nodded softly. "Thank you, Sister-in-law."

Next, Qin Yuhan marched Chen Hao into a menswear store across the hall and selected a sharp charcoal wool overcoat, a navy knit sweater, and clean leather sneakers.

"If I let Xuan pick clothes for you," Qin Yuhan teased, "you will walk out wearing a 99-yuan warehouse hoodie."

Chen Xuan raised both hands in surrender, three paper bags already hooked over his forearm. "Warehouse hoodies have deep pockets. Very practical for snacks."

Even with his new charcoal coat packed in a garment bag, Chen Hao insisted on wearing his old navy jacket for the afternoon so the new coat would stay spotless until he showed his mom back home.

They stopped for lunch at a cozy Shanghainese restaurant upstairs, sharing steaming crab-meat soup dumplings, scallion oil noodles, and stir-fried bok choy. Over hot tea afterward, Qin Yuhan smiled at Chen Xuan.

"Why do we not drive over to Plaza 66 in Jing'an for coffee? I also want to pick out two good cashmere shawls for Mom and Grandma so Hao and Yuan can carry them back tomorrow."

Chen Xuan nodded easily. "Sounds like a plan. Grandma always says her shoulders get chilly once December starts."

None of them expected that a quiet errand for two winter shawls would stir up a storm inside Plaza 66.
