# Chapter 177: Saturday at IFC Mall and Nanjing Road

Saturday morning in Shanghai dawned crisp and clear, with pale gold sunlight warming the wide avenues.

At nine o'clock sharp, Chen Xuan stood outside the arrival gate of Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station, wearing his faded dark-blue windbreaker and holding three cups of hot soy milk.

"Ge! Over here!"

Chen Yuan hurried out first in a neat cream knit sweater and jeans, carrying a heavy cloth tote bag.

Chen Xuan stepped forward with a wide smile and took the heavy tote from his sister. "Careful, this feels like a bag of bricks."

"Mom packed two jars of her chili sauce, dried bamboo shoots from Yunxi County, and sweet potato strips," Chen Yuan said, immediately scanning his face with sisterly worry. "Ge, you look thinner again. Are you eating proper meals, or living on instant noodles?"

"Three meals a day, I swear," Chen Xuan chuckled, handing them the hot soy milk. He tapped his younger brother's shoulder lightly. "Look at you, Chen Hao. Six more months and you will be taller than me."

Chen Hao grinned shyly, taking a big sip while his eyes darted across the huge station hall. "Ge, this station is bigger than our whole county town!"

"Save your neck," Chen Xuan laughed. "Let us drop your bags at my place first, then we head straight to Nanjing Road."

An hour later, after leaving their bags and Mom's jars of chili sauce at Chen Xuan's modest rented apartment, the three siblings stepped out of People's Square subway station onto Nanjing Road Pedestrian Street.

The weekend crowd was lively and bright.

Chen Xuan bought two paper boxes of hot pan-fried buns and three cups of milk tea from a busy corner shop.

"Careful when you bite," Chen Xuan warned his brother. "Hot soup inside."

Chen Hao nodded eagerly, bit straight in anyway, and jumped back fanning his mouth while Chen Yuan burst out laughing.

"I told you!" Chen Yuan teased, handing him a napkin. "Slow down, nobody is stealing your buns. And stop taking photos of every lamppost. You already took forty pictures since the subway!"

"My classmates back home made me promise to send pictures of Shanghai," Chen Hao protested between chews, holding up his phone proudly. "Look at these old buildings! They look just like our textbook!"

Chen Xuan walked beside them at an easy pace, watching his sister and brother bicker happily.

By early afternoon, they rode Metro Line 2 under the Huangpu River to Lujiazui.

The moment they stepped up the escalator onto the open circular walkway of Lujiazui, Chen Hao stopped dead in his tracks.

Right ahead, the towering skyline of Pudong rose into the blue sky.

Chen Hao tilted his head all the way back, craning his neck so far that his school collar bunched up and his mouth fell open. He spun slowly in a half-circle, staring straight up at the Oriental Pearl Tower in sheer amazement.

Chen Xuan laughed softly and steadied his brother's shoulder before a passing runner bumped him. "Careful, or you will trip over your shoes. What do you think?"

"It is huge!" Chen Hao breathed, his eyes shining with wonder. "On television it looks big, Ge, but standing right under it makes you feel tiny. How do people even build something that tall?"

"One steel beam at a time," Chen Xuan said warmly. "Stand by the railing. I will take a photo of you and Chen Yuan with the tower behind you."

Chen Yuan pulled Chen Hao close and smiled brightly as Chen Xuan snapped three photos. Afterward, Chen Hao gazed back up at the gleaming skyscrapers, his playful grin settling into something quieter and far more earnest.

"Ge," Chen Hao said, lowering his voice a little in the breeze. "Next June is my college entrance exam. My practice scores last month put me in the top five of the grade. I want to apply to Fudan University or Shanghai Jiao Tong University."

Chen Yuan turned to him with a proud smile. "Our little brother has big dreams! If you get into Fudan or Shanghai Jiao Tong, Mom will cook a feast for the whole village."

"I am serious," Chen Hao said, straightening his back. "I want to study here in Shanghai and work in a building like those someday so Mom and Dad never have to worry about money again."

Chen Xuan looked at his seventeen-year-old brother and felt a quiet swell of pride. He rested a warm hand on Chen Hao's shoulder. "Fudan University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University are both great schools. Keep studying steady like you are now, get enough sleep, and you will make it. Shanghai will have a place waiting for you."

A chilly gust swept off the river, making Chen Yuan shiver and tuck her hands into her pockets.

"The wind is picking up," Chen Xuan noticed right away. "Let us step inside IFC Mall to warm up."

They walked into the bright glass atrium of IFC Mall in Lujiazui. Warm air greeted them immediately, carrying the scent of coffee and flowers. Gleaming marble floors reflected bright chandeliers overhead, and spotless luxury storefronts lined every level.

Chen Hao kept his hands tucked neatly behind his back as they strolled past the ground-floor shops, whispering in shock at a display window.

"Ge, look at that leather belt," Chen Hao hissed quietly. "It says 6,800 yuan! For a belt! Does it hold your pants up by itself?"

Chen Xuan chuckled quietly. "No, you still have to buckle it yourself."

Chen Yuan tugged gently on Chen Xuan's windbreaker sleeve. "Ge, this mall is way too fancy. Let us warm up for ten minutes and go find a normal lunch spot. Do not waste your hard-earned office salary here."

"I am not buying a six-thousand-yuan belt," Chen Xuan assured her calmly. "Still, winter is coming fast in Yunxi County. Mom's old padded jacket has been patched twice, and your coat from freshman year is thin. I have been saving for two years. I want to buy Mom a real warm wool coat and get you a clean jacket before you go home tomorrow."

"Mom will scold you if you spend too much!" Chen Yuan protested at once.

"She will scold me for five minutes, then wear it happily all winter," Chen Xuan replied easily. "IFC Mall is crowded today anyway. Let us grab a quick lunch of xiaolongbao, then head across to Plaza 66 on West Nanjing Road. There is a quiet coat shop there with thick wool that lasts for years."

Chen Yuan sighed in defeat, a soft smile touching her lips. "Fine, Ge. Only if we check the prices first."

"Deal," Chen Xuan smiled, leading them toward the escalator. None of them expected that a simple afternoon coat errand at Plaza 66 was about to turn into a scene none of them would forget.
