# Chapter 21: Three Options

Back on the balcony, Chen Xuan's phone buzzed a second time.

"Boss." Number One sounded both pleased and slightly wary. "Six of Hongrui's street crew, including Niu Dali and Yao Xiaolong, are sitting in Special Police vans. Two iron pipes are bagged as evidence, and nobody on our side was hurt. Two slipped away on foot: the black-van driver and Xie Boyuan, their bookkeeper."

Chen Xuan rubbed his forehead and let out a slow breath.

"I said keep them safe, Number One. I didn't ask for eighteen Special Police."

"You said they couldn't touch the tailor, Boss. The pipe was already swinging. Yao Xiaolong is eating a hot dumpling in the back of a police van as we speak."

"Good work, Number One. Is Yu Changshun hurt? What about the little girl?"

"Both fine, Boss. Shaken up, nothing more."

"Good." Chen Xuan's voice went quiet and flat. "Number One, Cheng Biao answers to the law now."

"Yes, Boss."

"He slapped a young officer over a cup of water and sent a van to run down an old tailor. Pull together everything he's done, every last file. And in the morning, send the recording of Dong Haisheng's call, with the timestamp, straight to the bank holding his loan."

Far away in a quiet courtyard, Number One's breath caught for half a second. Then he answered steadily, "Yes, Boss."

Inside the living room, Chen Hao pressed his nose against the glass door and whispered, "Who is he even talking to out there?"

Chen Yuan watched her brother's quiet, straight back through the pane. "Work, he said. He's carrying a lot more than he's letting us see."

* * *

Number One, head of the Black Pine Guard, sat in a quiet courtyard in the east of Qingyan City with a clean sheet of paper on his desk. On it, he had written two lines in his neat, old-fashioned hand.

*Option One.*

*Option Two.*

He stared at them for a long moment, then added a third.

The desk speaker crackled. "Number One, this is Twenty-three." It was Number Twenty-three, stationed on Old Street. "I have the full count."

"Money first."

"Yunxi approved six hundred forty million yuan to relocate Old Street and the three blocks behind it, roughly 1,100 households and shops. At least one hundred ninety million never reached a single family. It flowed into three companies: Hongrui Real Estate, Ankang Private Clinic, and a demolition firm owned by Cheng Biao's brother-in-law."

Number One's pen stopped.

"The same clinic that wrote the injury slip."

"The very one. The slip states the driver was treated at 1:50. Yu Changshun saw him throw himself into the paint shelf at 2:03, and the arrest happened at 2:05."

"Treated thirteen minutes before he was hurt." Number One let out a dry breath that was almost a laugh. "Quite a fast doctor."

"One more thing." Number Twenty-three's voice softened. "Last night, Wei Kun brought a cup of hot water to the old man in the holding cell. Cheng Biao slapped him for it and threatened his wife in the hospital. This morning, Cheng Biao sent a cup of poisoned tea to Wei Kun through Lei Bo. Our records clerk spilled it, and we saved a sample."

"Who told Cheng Biao what time Wei Kun wrote on the notice?"

"Zhong Hai, Wei Kun's desk neighbor. His son has a heart condition, and Cheng Biao offered him a sixty-thousand-yuan loan. He hasn't eaten a bite since."

"And Yu Changshun?"

"A van tried to run him down near the river arch at 4:20. Our bread truck blocked it, and the hired driver is already talking to the police."

Number Twenty-three paused before adding, "Cheng Biao ran the Boss's name through the system this afternoon. All he found was an ordinary clerk."

Number One nodded. "His public file is real and completely unremarkable. Honest, quiet, no debts. Cheng Biao looked, and others will too."

"And his sealed file, Number One?"

"It sits where nobody below the very top can touch it. If anyone tries, the screen returns a single line: permission denied. Then the highest level asks them why they were looking."

Number One looked back at his three options and added a smaller line beneath them: *Wei Kun's wife.*

Number Twenty-three spoke up without waiting to be asked. "Apartment 302 is ready for her. Number Ninety-five will run into her at the pharmacy and arrange a proper bed."

Number One turned toward the window. "Number Eleven."

Standing by the glass, Number Eleven, Number Eleven and the Black Pine Guard's lawyer, stepped forward with a slight bow. He held a thin black folder.

"Whenever you give the word, Number One."

"Not mine." Number One picked up his phone. "His."

* * *

In Room 601 back in Yunxi, Chen Hao spoke up from the sofa.

"Big Brother, you've been staring at your phone for ten minutes."

"I keep thinking about Second Uncle." Chen Xuan rubbed the bridge of his nose. "It's cold in there."

In the kitchen, Liu Meifang packed a steel thermos with pork-rib soup, blinking back tears as she ladled it in.

"This is for your uncle," she muttered to no one in particular. "He's fifty-two. He can't stomach holding-cell food." She pointed the ladle at Chen Xuan. "And you, look how thin you've gotten. Eat something."

Chen Xuan's phone buzzed. "Work," he murmured, stepping out onto the balcony.

Number One's voice came through low and steady. "Boss. Three options."

"Go ahead, Number One."

"First. Our lawyer files a formal complaint at the city Discipline Commission tonight. It includes the clinic slip, the honest detention notice, Yu Changshun's statement, the street footage, and the money trail. Your uncle could be home by morning."

Chen Xuan rested his free hand on the railing. "Home by morning," he repeated quietly.

"Second. We wait and hand everything to the provincial inspection team on Wednesday, when they're scheduled to arrive. That means four more nights in that cell."

"No. Not four more nights."

"Third. I make one call tonight. Someone in the provincial capital would tear Yunxi's entire police bureau wide open before breakfast. It's the fastest route, and the loudest. A dozen shopkeepers and one honest duty officer would get buried in the fallout."

"I recommend the first, Boss." Number One paused. "One more thing. Two of Hongrui's crew slipped away tonight: their black-van driver and a bookkeeper named Xie Boyuan. Xie Boyuan keeps the crew's debt ledger."

"Option One," Chen Xuan said calmly. "Through the front door, with a stamped receipt."

"Yes, Boss."

"Number One, I also want a piece of the third. Not the whole bureau. Just Hongrui's street crew." Chen Xuan spoke in the same even tone he might use to order tea. "Find Xie Boyuan, his ledger, and the black-van driver tonight."

"Under what charges, Boss?"

"Every single one that fits. Assault, wrecking shops, extortion, threatening a child. They beat an old man in the street, then went to a tailor's door with a steel pipe. That's twice. Charge the whole crew tonight, every last man, strictly by the law. Whoever carried that pipe faces assault with a weapon and threatening a minor. Not ten days in lockup. Years. Make sure he understands that in the van before he speaks to anyone."

There was no raised voice in it, no anger on the surface. That was precisely what made it impossible to forget.

"It will be done, Boss. One more thing about today: people on the street are calling the spilled tea and the bread truck pure luck. Cheng Biao thinks Wei Kun has a guardian angel."

Chen Xuan rubbed his forehead lightly.

"Luck and a guardian angel. Good. Let them keep thinking that. Thank Number Thirty-four and the bread-truck driver for me."

"I'll pass that along, Boss."

"And Officer Zhong Hai, Boss? The man who leaked the timestamp."

Chen Xuan lowered his hand.

"He sold out a colleague for a sixty-thousand-yuan loan, and an honest man almost drank poison for it. He faces charges on Monday."

"And his son, Zhong Tiantian?"

"The boy didn't sell anyone out. Hold a bed for him on the fourth floor of People's Hospital. Tell his father nothing. If Zhong Hai walks up to Wei Kun's desk before Monday and confesses on his own, I'll reconsider."

"Yes, Boss."

"And Number One, please send a hot meal and a blanket for my second uncle through the family window, just like any relative would. My mother is bringing soup in the morning. Also, make sure nobody touches Officer Wei Kun. Not his wife's ward, not his mortgage, not his desk. He passed a cup of water through the bars and wrote the truth on a form when lying was easier. Repay that ten times over."

"And Yu Changshun, Boss?"

"Keep someone watching Yu Ning's walk to school until she's grown. He stood in his doorway facing a steel pipe. That's no small thing."

Number One's throat tightened.

Three ways to topple a county's leadership sat on his desk, and Chen Xuan had spent half his instructions on soup, a blanket, and a child's walk to school.

"The beef noodles will be piping hot, Boss," Number One said softly. "With an extra egg."

"Thank you, Number One," Chen Xuan said with quiet sincerity. "Really."

He ended the call and turned to find Chen Yuan standing in the doorway, holding his green thermos.

She held it out. "Here. It went cold, so I warmed it up."

"Thanks, Chen Yuan."

"Don't say anything, Big Brother. Just drink it."

She pressed it into his hand and hurried back inside, dabbing her eyes with her sleeve.

Chen Hao leaned out of the kitchen. "Big Brother, why are you smiling?"

Chen Xuan chuckled softly. "Just glad I'm early for Mom's soup."
