# Chapter 63: Organization Department: The Empty Cadre Files

At eight thirty-two on Monday morning, the third-floor war room of the Central Organization Department was thick with cigarette smoke. Ten bureau chiefs sat elbow to elbow along the narrow table, with stacks of personnel files piled in front of them.

Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei kicked the door shut, his face dark with anger. Without a greeting, he threw three yellow file folders down the table, rattling two glass ashtrays.

"Explain these!" Liu Zhengwei barked, his knuckles white against the wood. "Vice Governor Lin Zhengde! Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang! Customs Director Xu Wenbin! Twenty years of yearly background checks, and not one warning in all three files!"

Nobody touched the folders at first. Through the smoke, Cadre Background Audit Chief Yao Minghui swallowed hard and opened the top folder.

"Look at the review sheets, Liu Zhengwei," Yao Minghui said tightly, flipping through stamped pages. "Every review from party school to ministerial promotion has straight 'Excellent' marks. Zero warnings. Zero strange income. Zero overseas family members listed."

"Zero overseas family!" Cadre Supervision Bureau Chief Hou Jianjun laughed harshly, pointing his cigarette at Second Cadre Bureau Chief Peng Zhiqiang. "Lin Zhengde's son has owned three villas in Zurich and a shipping company in Singapore for nine years! How did your coastal office push Lin Zhengde's vice-governor promotion through in six days without spotting his son?"

Peng Zhiqiang shot to his feet, his face turning purple. "Do not dump your mess on me, Hou Jianjun! My office checks job records and provincial recommendations! Checking overseas passports and bank accounts is your Supervision Bureau's job! You stamped 'Verified Clean' on Lin Zhengde's file four times in twelve years!"

"Because the provincial memos on my desk had real seals from four provincial personnel heads!" Hou Jianjun roared, slapping his palm down so hard his teacup jumped. "Half of whom were dragged out of bed at four o'clock this morning by Discipline Inspection!"

"Stop shouting!" Fourth Cadre Bureau Chief Shi Lianying snapped, shoving He Guoliang's folder across the table. "Look at Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang's file! Four mining-deal complaints were filed against him eight to twelve years ago. Where are they? Gone! Someone pulled the original complaint papers out of the fourth-floor archive and swapped them for glowing praise reports!"

Yao Minghui's face turned gray. His cigarette burned down to his fingers before he jerked and dropped it into the tray. "That is impossible, Shi Lianying. Nobody gets into the fourth-floor archive without two keys, one from my deputy and one from Personnel Discipline!"

"Ask your deputy where he is right now," Personnel Discipline Inspection Liaison Bai Yunfeng said softly from the corner.

Every head snapped toward him.

Bai Yunfeng tapped a thin blue slip with one finger, his eyes cold as ice. "At five fifteen, Gao Zhengyi's team arrested your archive deputy and two senior clerks. For fourteen years, whenever our audit teams planned a surprise check on Customs Director Xu Wenbin or Vice Governor Lin Zhengde, your deputy sent a coded weather report to Fang Qiming's contact at Shengyuan Capital seventy-two hours early. That gave them plenty of time to hide overseas shares in shell companies and wipe local records clean."

Heavy dread crushed the room. Yao Minghui sank back into his chair, gripping the armrests as his chest heaved. "Fourteen years. Right under my nose."

"Not just under your nose, Yao Minghui," First Cadre Bureau Chief Tan Guohua said grimly, blowing out gray smoke. "They turned our review system into their shield. Seventeen provincial department heads, twelve city leaders, and four vice ministers vanished overnight across fourteen provinces. Leave those seats empty past noon, and provincial budgets freeze while customs ports stop moving."

"Premier Gu Chengshan got us emergency fill-in authority from the National People's Congress," Liu Zhengwei said coldly. He lit a fresh cigarette with a shaking thumb before steadying his wrist. "Stop blaming each other for yesterday. Discipline Inspection will check every clerk in this building by sunset. Right now, we have three hours to fill thirty-three key posts across fourteen provinces with people Shengyuan Capital never touched."

Third Cadre Bureau Chief Cao Weidong pulled a red backup list toward him, sweat beading on his temples. "How do we know the backup picks are clean if the fourth-floor archive was compromised, Liu Zhengwei? If we promote another secret friend of Lin Zhengde, Premier Gu Chengshan will skin us alive!"

"Use the rejection pile," Grassroots and County Cadre Review Chief Meng Changqing said bluntly.

The whole table went quiet. Tan Guohua blinked through the smoke. "Say that again, Meng Changqing."

"Use the sidelined officials," Meng Changqing repeated, slapping a worn gray binder onto the wood. "For fifteen years, whenever an honest provincial auditor, customs officer, or county deputy refused to sign Shengyuan Capital's fake demolition loans or crooked mining deals, Lin Zhengde and He Guoliang blocked their promotions. They called them 'stubborn' or 'hard to work with' and buried them in research rooms and remote county archives."

Shi Lianying's eyes lit up with sudden clarity. "He is right! Three years ago, Senior Customs Inspector Zhou Ming refused to wave through fourteen unmarked ore barges for Xu Wenbin. Xu Wenbin sent him away to a quiet post in the northern hills. If Shengyuan Capital buried a man for five years, he never took Fang Qiming's money!"

"Same in the eastern provinces!" Emergency Deployment and Trusteeship Chief Zou Shuanglin added, opening the sidelined-officials list on his laptop. "Forty-eight veteran provincial directors and deputy chiefs were passed over three times in a row despite spotless records! Every single one of them stood up to Lin Zhengde's circle!"

Liu Zhengwei took a slow drag, his tight jaw relaxing a little as he looked at Meng Changqing's binder. "Run every sidelined name through Bai Yunfeng's Discipline Inspection line right now. No favors, and no polite recommendations from sitting provincial leaders."

"What about Yunxi County?" Cao Weidong asked, looking up. "Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's task force rolled into Yunxi two minutes ago. Once Deputy County Magistrate Sun Weiming and County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong are arrested, the county needs an acting magistrate and an acting police chief before dark."

"Already picked," Meng Changqing replied right away, sliding two crisp folders to Liu Zhengwei. "Two veteran provincial inspection officers with zero family or business ties in the province. Clean records, tough backbones."

"Check their spouses, children, and in-laws through Bai Yunfeng's line anyway," Liu Zhengwei snapped, his eyes still burning over Yao Minghui's ruined archive. "I will not sign a single acting order based on reputation alone. Not after today."

"I already ran both family trees through Discipline Inspection twenty minutes ago," Bai Yunfeng confirmed flatly, nodding across the haze. "Zero overseas accounts, zero private company shares, and zero contact with Shengyuan Capital or Deng Yongchang's construction group."

"Approve both," Liu Zhengwei ordered, pressing his pen to the first emergency appointment paper. "Tan Guohua, Peng Zhiqiang, Cao Weidong, and Shi Lianying, split up the fourteen provinces now. Write every acting order before eleven thirty. Today we clear out the dirty files and hand the keys back to the people who refused to bend."

Through the swirling smoke, ten bureau chiefs leaned over the sidelined lists, racing noon to rebuild the backbone of fourteen provinces.
