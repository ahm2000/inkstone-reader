# Chapter 63: Organization Department: The Empty Cadre Files

At eight thirty-two on Monday morning, the third-floor war room of the Central Organization Department was thick with cigarette smoke. Ten bureau chiefs sat elbow to elbow along the narrow table, stacks of cadre dossiers piled before them.

Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei kicked the door shut, face dark with fury. Without a greeting, he hurled three yellow archival jackets down the table, rattling two glass ashtrays.

"Explain these!" Liu Zhengwei barked, knuckles white on the varnish. "Vice Governor Lin Zhengde! Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang! Customs Director Xu Wenbin! Twenty years of annual cadre vetting, and not one red flag in all three jackets!"

Nobody touched the folders at first. Through the haze, Cadre Background Audit Chief Yao Minghui swallowed hard and flipped the top jacket open.

"Look at the evaluation sheets, Liu Zhengwei," Yao Minghui said tightly, turning stamped pages. "Every review from party school to ministerial promotion carries unanimous 'Excellent' ratings. Zero warnings. Zero unexplained income. Zero declared overseas dependents."

"Zero overseas dependents!" Cadre Supervision Bureau Chief Hou Jianjun laughed harshly, jabbing his cigarette toward Second Cadre Bureau Chief Peng Zhiqiang. "Lin Zhengde's son has owned three Zurich villas and a Singapore shipping brokerage for nine years! How did your coastal appointment bureau push Lin Zhengde's vice-governor nomination through in six days without flagging his son?"

Peng Zhiqiang bolted upright, face flushing purple. "Do not dump your failure on me, Hou Jianjun! My bureau evaluates governance and provincial standing committee recommendations! Checking overseas passports and bank accounts is your Supervision Bureau's job! You stamped 'Verified Clean' on Lin Zhengde's ledger four times in twelve years!"

"Because the provincial memos on my desk carried authentic seals from four provincial organization heads!" Hou Jianjun roared, slapping his palm down so hard his teacup jumped. "Half of whom were dragged from bed at four o'clock this morning by Discipline Inspection!"

"Stop shouting!" Fourth Cadre Bureau Chief Shi Lianying snapped, shoving He Guoliang's folder across the table. "Look at Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang's file! Four mineral-concession audit complaints were filed against him eight to twelve years ago. Where are they? Gone! Somebody pulled the original complaint slips out of the fourth-floor archive and replaced them with clean commendation summaries!"

Yao Minghui's face turned ash-gray. His cigarette burned down to his fingers before he jolted and dropped it into the tray. "Impossible, Shi Lianying. Nobody enters the fourth-floor archive without dual-key sign-off from my deputy and Personnel Discipline!"

"Ask your deputy where he is now," Personnel Discipline Inspection Liaison Bai Yunfeng said softly from the corner.

Every head snapped toward him.

Bai Yunfeng tapped a thin blue slip with one finger, eyes cold as frost. "At five fifteen, Gao Zhengyi's commission detained your archive deputy and two senior clerks. For fourteen years, whenever our supervision teams scheduled a random audit on Customs Director Xu Wenbin or Vice Governor Lin Zhengde, your deputy sent a coded weather forecast to Fang Qiming's Shengyuan Capital liaison seventy-two hours early. Plenty of time to park offshore equity under shell trusts and scrub local registries."

Suffocating dread crushed the room. Yao Minghui sagged back, gripping his armrests as his chest heaved. "Fourteen years. Right under my nose."

"Not just under your nose, Yao Minghui," First Cadre Bureau Chief Tan Guohua said grimly, exhaling gray smoke. "They turned our vetting ledger into armor. Seventeen provincial department heads, twelve municipal standing committee members, and four ministerial deputies vanished overnight across fourteen provinces. Leave those chairs empty past noon, and provincial treasuries freeze while customs ports lock up."

"Premier Gu Chengshan gave us emergency trusteeship authority from the National People's Congress," Liu Zhengwei said coldly, lighting a fresh cigarette with a trembling thumb before steadying his wrist. "Stop pointing fingers about yesterday. Discipline Inspection audits every clerk in this building by sunset. Right now, we have three hours to fill thirty-three critical seats across fourteen provinces with people Shengyuan Capital never touched."

Third Cadre Bureau Chief Cao Weidong pulled a red reserve ledger toward him, sweat beading on his temples. "How do we guarantee reserve candidates are clean if the fourth-floor archive was compromised, Liu Zhengwei? Promote another hidden protégé of Lin Zhengde, and Premier Gu Chengshan skins us alive!"

"Use the rejection pile," Grassroots and County Cadre Review Chief Meng Changqing said bluntly.

The table hushed. Tan Guohua blinked through the smoke. "Say that again, Meng Changqing."

"Use the sidelined cadres," Meng Changqing repeated, slapping a battered gray binder onto the varnish. "For fifteen years, whenever an honest provincial auditor, customs inspector, or county deputy refused to sign Shengyuan Capital's rigged demolition bonds or rare-earth concessions, Lin Zhengde and He Guoliang blocked their promotions. They labeled them 'inflexible' or 'politically stubborn' and buried them in research offices and remote county archives."

Shi Lianying's eyes sharpened with sudden clarity. "He is right! Three years ago, Senior Customs Inspector Zhou Ming refused to clear fourteen unmarked mineral barges for Xu Wenbin. Xu Wenbin banished him to a dry-dock post in the northern hills. If Shengyuan Capital buried a man for five years, he never took Fang Qiming's money!"

"Same in the eastern provinces!" Emergency Deployment and Trusteeship Chief Zou Shuanglin added, opening the sidelined-cadre registry on his laptop. "Forty-eight veteran provincial directors and deputy commissioners passed over three cycles in a row despite spotless records! Every single one crossed Lin Zhengde's urban-investment clique!"

Liu Zhengwei took a slow drag, his tight jaw easing slightly over Meng Changqing's binder. "Cross-check every sidelined candidate against Bai Yunfeng's Discipline Inspection clearance line right now. No favors, no courtesy recommendations from sitting provincial chairs."

"What about Yunxi County?" Cao Weidong asked, looking up. "Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's task force rolled into Yunxi two minutes ago. Once Deputy County Magistrate Sun Weiming and County Public Security Chief Liang Guodong are arrested, the county needs an acting magistrate and interim bureau chief before nightfall."

"Already chosen," Meng Changqing replied without hesitation, sliding two crisp dossiers to Liu Zhengwei. "Two veteran provincial inspection cadres with zero family or business ties in the province. Clean records, hard spines."

"Verify their spouses, children, and in-laws through Bai Yunfeng's line anyway," Liu Zhengwei snapped, his eyes still burning with the memory of Yao Minghui's compromised archive. "I will not sign a single acting appointment on reputation alone. Not after today."

"Already ran both family trees through Discipline Inspection twenty minutes ago," Bai Yunfeng confirmed flatly, nodding across the haze. "Zero offshore accounts, zero private-equity holdings, and zero contact with Shengyuan Capital or Deng Yongchang's construction group."

"Approve both," Liu Zhengwei ordered, pressing his pen to the first trusteeship order. "Tan Guohua, Peng Zhiqiang, Cao Weidong, and Shi Lianying, divide the fourteen provinces now. Draft every acting appointment before eleven thirty. Today we empty the dirty files and hand the keys back to the people who refused to bend."

Through swirling smoke, ten bureau chiefs bent over the sidelined rosters, racing noon to rebuild the spine of fourteen provinces.
