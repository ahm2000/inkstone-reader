# Chapter 63: Organization Department: The Empty Cadre Files

The third-floor war room of the Central Organization Department hung thick with blue smoke. Ten bureau chiefs sat elbow to elbow along the narrow table behind stacks of personnel files.

Executive Deputy Minister Liu Zhengwei kicked the door shut, dark with anger. Before he spoke, the whole room stiffened and rose as one.

Walking calmly behind Liu Zhengwei were two men whose names made every personnel chief in Beijing sweat: Elder Qin, the retired senior military elder of the Qin Family, and Elder Ye, the retired former Discipline and Judicial patriarch of the Ye Family.

Elder Qin wore a plain dark jacket, back straight as a rifle barrel. He took the head chair, set down a white teacup, and glared down the row. Elder Ye sat beside him, lighting a cigarette with slow menace.

"Sit," Elder Qin barked, rattling the ashtrays. "Nobody stands around looking polite today."

Liu Zhengwei threw three yellow folders down the center. "Explain these!" Liu Zhengwei roared, knuckles white. "Vice Governor Lin Zhengde! Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang! Customs Director Xu Wenbin! Twenty years of yearly checks, and not one warning in all three files!"

Nobody touched them at first. Cadre Background Audit Chief Yao Minghui swallowed hard and opened the top folder with shaking fingers.

"Look at the review sheets, Liu Zhengwei," Yao Minghui said tightly. "Every review from party school to ministerial promotion has straight 'Excellent' marks. Zero warnings. Zero strange income. Zero overseas family listed."

"Zero overseas family!" Cadre Supervision Chief Hou Jianjun laughed harshly, pointing his cigarette at Second Cadre Bureau Chief Peng Zhiqiang. "Lin Zhengde's son has owned three villas in Zurich and a shipping firm in Singapore for nine years! How did your coastal office push Lin Zhengde's promotion in six days without spotting him?"

Peng Zhiqiang shot up, face purple. "Do not dump your mess on me, Hou Jianjun! I check job records and provincial memos! Checking overseas passports and accounts is your Supervision Bureau's job! You stamped 'Verified Clean' on Lin Zhengde four times in twelve years!"

"Because the memos carried real seals from four provincial personnel heads!" Hou Jianjun roared, slapping his palm down until his cup jumped. "Half of whom Discipline Inspection dragged out of bed before dawn!"

Bang!

Elder Qin slammed his porcelain teacup onto the table. Hot tea splashed the cloth. Hou Jianjun and Peng Zhiqiang shut their mouths instantly, blood draining from their faces.

"Listen to you two barking like street dogs," Elder Qin said coldly, eyes slicing from Peng Zhiqiang to Hou Jianjun. "Six days for a vice governor? Four clean stamps in twelve years? Do not blame seals. Tell me how many calls came from coastal family factions, old classmates, and retired uncles asking you to look away!"

Peng Zhiqiang's throat bobbed. He stared at the floor, unable to meet Elder Qin's gaze.

"Family factions," Elder Ye spoke softly through gray smoke, tapping ash into his tray. "That is the real rot here, Liu Zhengwei. For twenty years, men in these chairs traded favors. You pass my coastal nephew today, and I pass your mining brother-in-law tomorrow. This department stopped picking servants of the people and became a private club for circles and clans."

"Look at He Guoliang's file too!" Fourth Cadre Bureau Chief Shi Lianying cut in grimly, shoving the second folder over. "Four mining complaints were filed against He Guoliang eight to twelve years ago. Gone! Someone pulled the real complaints from our fourth-floor archive and swapped in glowing praise!"

Yao Minghui turned gray. His cigarette burned his fingers before he dropped it. "Impossible, Shi Lianying! Nobody enters the fourth-floor archive without two keys, one from my deputy and one from Personnel Discipline!"

"Ask your deputy where he is right now," Personnel Discipline Liaison Bai Yunfeng said softly from the corner.

Every head snapped toward him.

Bai Yunfeng tapped a thin blue slip, eyes like ice. "Before sunrise, Gao Zhengyi's team arrested your archive deputy and two clerks. For fourteen years, whenever auditors planned a surprise check on Xu Wenbin or Lin Zhengde, your deputy sent a coded weather note to Fang Qiming's contact at Shengyuan Capital seventy-two hours early. They hid overseas shares in shell firms and wiped local records."

Heavy dread crushed the room. Yao Minghui gripped his armrests, chest heaving. "Fourteen years. Right under my nose."

"Because nobody asked why complaints vanished," Elder Ye murmured sharply, eyes locked on Yao Minghui. "Wait until Gao Zhengyi finishes questioning your deputy. We will see who else shared his tea."

Yao Minghui shivered and bowed his head.

"First things first," First Cadre Bureau Chief Tan Guohua said grimly through the smoke. "Seventeen provincial heads, twelve city leaders, and four vice ministers vanished across fourteen provinces. Leave thirty-three seats empty past noon, and provincial budgets freeze while ports stop."

"Premier Gu Chengshan got us emergency authority from the National People's Congress," Liu Zhengwei said coldly, lighting a fresh cigarette. "Discipline Inspection checks every clerk here by sunset. Right now, we fill thirty-three posts with people Shengyuan Capital never touched."

Third Cadre Bureau Chief Cao Weidong pulled a red backup list closer, sweat on his temples. "How do we know backup picks are clean if the archive was rotten? Promote one secret friend of Lin Zhengde, and Premier Gu Chengshan and the Elders will skin us alive!"

"Use the rejection pile," Grassroots Cadre Chief Meng Changqing said bluntly.

The table went quiet. Elder Qin raised an eyebrow. "Say that again, young man."

"Use the sidelined officials, Elder Qin," Meng Changqing repeated clearly, slapping a worn gray binder down. "For fifteen years, whenever an honest auditor, customs officer, or county deputy refused to sign Shengyuan Capital's fake demolition loans or crooked mining deals, Lin Zhengde and He Guoliang blocked them. They called them 'stubborn' and buried them in research rooms and remote archives."

Shi Lianying's eyes lit up. "He is right! Three years ago, Senior Customs Inspector Zhou Ming refused to wave fourteen unmarked ore barges through for Xu Wenbin. Xu Wenbin buried him in the northern hills. If Shengyuan Capital buried a man five years, he never took Fang Qiming's money!"

"Same in the eastern provinces!" Emergency Deployment Chief Zou Shuanglin added, opening his laptop. "Forty-eight veteran provincial directors were passed over three times despite spotless records! Every one stood up to Lin Zhengde's circle!"

Elder Qin let out a short, fierce laugh and nodded hard. "Now that sounds like real work. The men the thieves hated are the men with iron backbones. Pull them out of the dust and put them in charge."

"What about Yunxi County in Sha Province?" Cao Weidong asked. "Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming's team is rolling into Yunxi County now. Once Sun Weiming and Liang Guodong fall, the county needs acting leaders before dark."

"Already picked," Meng Changqing replied, sliding two folders to Liu Zhengwei. "Two veteran provincial inspection officers with zero ties in Sha Province. Clean records, tough backbones."

"Check their spouses, children, and in-laws through Bai Yunfeng anyway," Elder Ye warned sharply. "That rule applies to every Beijing family too. Nobody gets a free pass today on a surname or a polite memo."

"I already ran both family trees through Discipline Inspection," Bai Yunfeng confirmed flatly. "Zero overseas accounts, zero private shares, and zero contact with Shengyuan Capital or Deng Yongchang."

"Approve both," Liu Zhengwei ordered, signing the first paper. "Tan Guohua, Peng Zhiqiang, Cao Weidong, and Shi Lianying, split the fourteen provinces now. Write every acting order before noon. Today we burn old favors and hand the keys back to the people who refused to bend."

Through the swirling smoke, ten bureau chiefs leaned over the sidelined lists while Elder Qin and Elder Ye watched in stony silence.
