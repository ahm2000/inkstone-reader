# Chapter 98: The Stuck Containers at Shanghai Port

Silence hung over the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics for two seconds. Then Sun Haoran exploded.

"Do you know what a three-day port hold means for those three containers?!" Sun Haoran shouted, slapping the blue folder against his palm. "Hongda Foods has a strict evening delivery clause! If those frozen seafood containers miss tonight's gate release, late penalties and cold-storage fees will cost Hengxin Logistics 2 million yuan! Where am I supposed to pull 2 million yuan from?!"

Hu Dewei stepped back, sweating hard. "General Manager Sun, please calm down! We filed all the customs forms on Friday afternoon. Even if Xu Wenbin is under investigation, our cargo is legal! The port office cannot lock down legal goods!"

"They did not lock it down for smuggling, you fool!" Sun Haoran yelled, waving his phone in Hu Dewei's face. "The duty clerk at Shanghai Port just hung up on me! Because Xu Wenbin's special desk is shut down, all pending containers went straight into the regular review queue! And our three containers were kicked out five minutes ago!"

"Kicked out?" Hu Dewei stammered, grabbing the folder with shaking hands. "Why would the regular queue kick them out?"

"Because the filing packet is incomplete!" Sun Haoran roared. "The regular office will not review a new submission until Friday afternoon unless we prove the original filing only missed a minor stamp! That is a three-day hold! Hu Dewei, you swore on Friday afternoon that your team checked every single sheet!"

Across the floor, every clerk stayed frozen behind their desks.

Two desks from Chen Xuan, Zhang Pengfei's smirk had vanished. He slid lower in his chair, hunching behind his tall monitor and clicking aimlessly through a blank spreadsheet, hoping nobody looked his way.

Hu Dewei flipped through the crumpled shipping sheets in a panic. On Friday afternoon, he had left early for a sauna after Zhang Pengfei promised to handle the Hongda Foods packet personally.

Desperate to shift the blame, Hu Dewei whipped his head toward the corner desk.

"Chen Xuan!" Hu Dewei barked shrilly. "Get over here right now! Hongda Foods is on your client list! You rushed out on Friday afternoon and left a mess behind! Did you drop a form before leaving?!"

Pan Xiulan gasped softly. Zheng Qian frowned hard. Everyone nearby knew Zhang Pengfei had bragged all Friday afternoon about taking over the Hongda Foods filing to show off his ties to Xu Wenbin's office.

Chen Xuan did not raise his voice or look annoyed.

He took one slow sip of hot tea from his green thermos, screwed the cap shut with a soft click, and walked calmly down the aisle.

"General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said evenly. "May I look at page two of that packet?"

Sun Haoran glared for a second, then shoved the folder into Chen Xuan's hands. "Look at it! If you messed up this account, you are paying for it!"

Chen Xuan held the printed shipping form under the ceiling light. His eyes scanned the container numbers, weights, and temperature codes in less than five seconds.

He turned the page toward Sun Haoran and tapped one small box near the bottom right corner.

"Nothing is missing from the Friday morning list," Chen Xuan said calmly. "All three container numbers, the bill of lading, and the cold-storage certificates match. I entered those before noon on Friday."

Hu Dewei snapped, "Then why did the port reject it?!"

"Because of Box 14," Chen Xuan replied, pointing at the empty square. "Cold-chain imports going through the regular port queue need the local quarantine pre-check stamp from the city inspection window. It only takes ten minutes to stamp online before four o'clock on Friday."

Sun Haoran leaned in, staring at the blank box. "It is completely empty."

"Yes," Chen Xuan said plainly. "Whoever took over the packet on Friday afternoon skipped the city inspection upload. Instead, look at the top margin."

Chen Xuan tilted the page. In the upper right corner, written in blue ink, was a small handwritten initials mark and a five-digit routing code.

"That routing code belongs to Xu Wenbin's private clearance desk," Chen Xuan explained calmly. "Whoever filed this on Friday afternoon did not bother getting the real quarantine stamp because they planned to push the containers through Xu Wenbin's desk this morning. Now that Xu Wenbin's desk is shut down, the regular system sees a blank Box 14 and blocks the containers automatically."

Dead silence fell over the twelfth floor.

Sun Haoran slowly raised his eyes from the blue ink. His chest heaved once, twice. Then he turned his pale, furious face toward Hu Dewei.

"Hu Dewei," Sun Haoran said in a dangerously low voice. "Who handled the Friday afternoon upload?"

Hu Dewei's mouth opened and closed. Sweat rolled down his temple as his eyes darted toward the second row of desks.

"I did not write that code, General Manager Sun!" Hu Dewei stammered, his face turning an ugly purple. "On Friday afternoon, Zhang Pengfei told me he knew Director Xu's cousin! He swore he would handle the Hongda Foods filing personally and clear it two hours early! He even made me sign his overtime slip yesterday!"

Sun Haoran slowly turned toward the second row. Every pair of eyes on the twelfth floor followed his gaze straight to the desk behind the tall monitor.

Zhang Pengfei froze like a trapped rabbit. His pen slipped from his fingers and clattered onto the floor.

"Zhang Pengfei!" Sun Haoran's roar shook the glass walls. "Stand up! Get your face out from behind that monitor right now!"

Zhang Pengfei scrambled up so fast his chair slammed into the filing cabinet. His slick confidence was gone, his knees visibly shaking.

"General Manager Sun! Manager Hu! Listen, I can explain!" Zhang Pengfei stammered, waving both hands. "Xu Wenbin's secretary told me on Friday that Box 14 was just extra paperwork! He said if I paid the fast-track fee, I did not need to upload the quarantine stamp! How could I know Director Xu was getting arrested before sunrise?!"

Hu Dewei's face turned even darker purple, veins bulging on his neck. "You idiot! You told me every stamp was complete! You stood in my office yesterday bad-mouthing Chen Xuan while bragging about your overtime! If Hengxin Logistics loses 2 million yuan today, I am dragging you down with me!"

"Shut up, both of you!" Sun Haoran snapped, pressing his fingers to his forehead. He glared at Zhang Pengfei with cold fury, then turned back to Chen Xuan. "Chen Xuan! You know the regular system. Can we get Box 14 stamped before tonight, or are we really stuck paying 2 million yuan?"

Chen Xuan closed the blue folder neatly and handed it back.

"The city inspection office opens its urgent re-check window at ten o'clock," Chen Xuan said calmly. "The original cold-chain test reports are in my Friday morning archive. Upload them now and walk a printed copy to Window 3 before ten-thirty, and the regular port queue will release all three containers by four this afternoon."

Sun Haoran stared at Chen Xuan in stunned silence for two seconds, then barked at Hu Dewei, "What are you waiting for?! Print those reports from Chen Xuan's desk and drive to Window 3 yourself! And Zhang Pengfei, stay in your seat until I decide whether to dock your whole year's bonus or fire you before lunch!"

Chen Xuan nodded politely, walked back to his corner desk, and took another quiet sip from his green thermos. Behind him, Pan Xiulan hid a wide smile behind her hand, and Zheng Qian quietly gave Chen Xuan a thumbs-up under his desk.
