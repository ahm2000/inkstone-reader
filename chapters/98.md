# Chapter 98: The Stuck Containers at Shanghai Port

Silence hung over the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics for two seconds.

"Do you know what a three-day port hold means for those three containers?!" Sun Haoran shouted, slapping the blue folder against his palm. "Hongda Foods has a strict evening delivery clause! If those frozen seafood containers miss tonight's gate release, late penalties and cold-storage fees will cost Hengxin Logistics 200,000 yuan! Where am I supposed to pull 200,000 yuan from?!"

Hu Dewei stepped back, sweating hard. "General Manager Sun Haoran, please calm down! We filed all the customs forms Friday afternoon. Even if Qi Tongwei is under investigation, our cargo is legal! The port office cannot lock down legal goods!"

"They did not lock it down for smuggling, you fool!" Sun Haoran yelled, waving his phone in Hu Dewei's face. "The duty clerk at Shanghai Port just hung up on me! Because Qi Tongwei's special desk is shut down, all pending containers went into the regular review queue! And our three containers were kicked out five minutes ago!"

"Kicked out?" Hu Dewei stammered, grabbing the folder with shaking hands. "Why would the regular queue kick them out?"

"Because the filing packet is incomplete!" Sun Haoran roared. "The regular office will not review a new submission until Friday afternoon unless we prove the original filing only missed a minor stamp! That is a three-day hold! Hu Dewei, you swore Friday afternoon that your team checked every sheet!"

Across the floor, every clerk froze behind their desks.

Two desks from Chen Xuan, Zhang Pengfei's smirk had vanished.

Hu Dewei flipped through the shipping sheets in a panic.

Desperate to shift blame, Hu Dewei whipped his head toward the corner desk.

"Chen Xuan!" Hu Dewei barked shrilly. "Get over here right now! Hongda Foods is on your client list! You took leave Friday morning and left a mess behind! Did you drop a form before leaving?!"

Pan Xiulan gasped softly. Everyone nearby knew Zhang Pengfei had bragged all Friday afternoon about taking over the Hongda Foods filing to show off his ties to Qi Tongwei's office.

Chen Xuan did not raise his voice or look annoyed.

"General Manager Sun Haoran," Chen Xuan said evenly. "May I look at page two of that packet?"

Sun Haoran glared for a second, then shoved the folder into Chen Xuan's hands. "Look at it! If you messed up this account, you are paying for it!"

Chen Xuan held the printed shipping form under the light, scanning container numbers, weights, and temperature codes in under five seconds. He turned the page toward Sun Haoran and tapped one small box near the bottom right.

"Nothing is missing from the Friday morning list," Chen Xuan said calmly. "All three container numbers, the bill of lading, and the cold-storage certificates match. I entered those before noon on Friday."

Hu Dewei snapped, "Then why did the port reject it?!"

"Because of Box 14," Chen Xuan replied, pointing at the empty square. "Cold-chain imports going through the regular port queue need the local quarantine pre-check stamp from the city inspection window. It only takes ten minutes to stamp online before four o'clock on Friday."

Sun Haoran leaned in, staring at the blank box. "It is completely empty."

"Yes," Chen Xuan said plainly. "Whoever took over the packet Friday afternoon skipped the city inspection upload. Instead, look at the top margin."

Chen Xuan tilted the page. In the upper right corner, written in blue ink, sat small handwritten initials and a five-digit routing code.

"That routing code belongs to Qi Tongwei's private clearance desk. It is on the desk list in the port bulletin," Chen Xuan explained calmly. "Whoever filed this Friday afternoon did not bother getting the real quarantine stamp because they planned to push the containers through Qi Tongwei's desk this morning. Now that Qi Tongwei's desk is shut down, the regular system sees a blank Box 14 and blocks the containers automatically."

Dead silence fell over the twelfth floor.

Sun Haoran raised his eyes slowly from the blue ink. His chest heaved twice before he turned his pale, furious face toward Hu Dewei.

"Hu Dewei," Sun Haoran said in a dangerously low voice. "Who handled the Friday afternoon upload?"

Hu Dewei's mouth opened and closed. Sweat rolled down his temple as his eyes darted to the second row.

"I did not write that code, General Manager Sun Haoran!" Hu Dewei stammered, his face turning purple. "On Friday afternoon, Zhang Pengfei told me he knew Chief Qi Tongwei's cousin! He swore he would handle the Hongda Foods filing personally and clear it two hours early! He even made me sign his overtime slip yesterday!"

Sun Haoran turned slowly toward the second row. Every pair of eyes followed his gaze straight to the desk behind the tall monitor.

Zhang Pengfei froze like a trapped rabbit. His pen clattered onto the floor.

"Zhang Pengfei!" Sun Haoran's roar shook the glass walls. "Stand up! Get your face out from behind that monitor right now!"

Zhang Pengfei scrambled up so fast his chair slammed the filing cabinet. His slick confidence was gone, his knees visibly shaking.

"General Manager Sun Haoran! Manager Hu Dewei! Listen, I can explain!" Zhang Pengfei stammered, waving both hands. "Qi Tongwei's secretary told me Friday that Box 14 was extra paperwork! He said if I paid the fast-track fee, I did not need the quarantine stamp! How could I know Chief Qi Tongwei was getting arrested that Monday?!"

Hu Dewei's face turned darker purple, veins bulging on his neck. "You idiot! You told me every stamp was complete! You stood in my office yesterday bad-mouthing Chen Xuan while bragging about your overtime! If Hengxin Logistics loses 200,000 yuan today, I am dragging you down with me!"

"Shut up, both of you!" Sun Haoran snapped, pressing his fingers to his forehead. He glared at Zhang Pengfei with cold fury, then turned back to Chen Xuan. "Chen Xuan! You know the regular system. Can we get Box 14 stamped before tonight, or are we really stuck paying 200,000 yuan?"

Chen Xuan closed the blue folder neatly and handed it back.

"The city inspection office opens its urgent re-check window at ten o'clock," Chen Xuan said calmly. "The original cold-chain test reports are in my Friday morning archive. Upload them now and walk a printed copy to Window 3 before ten-thirty, and the regular port queue will release all three containers by four this afternoon."

Sun Haoran stared at Chen Xuan in stunned silence for two seconds, then barked at Hu Dewei, "What are you waiting for?! Print those reports from Chen Xuan's desk and drive to Window 3 yourself! And Zhang Pengfei, stay in your seat until I decide whether to dock your whole year's bonus or fire you before lunch!"

Chen Xuan nodded politely, walked back to his corner desk, and took another quiet sip from his green thermos. Behind him, Pan Xiulan hid a wide smile behind her hand, and Zheng Qian quietly gave Chen Xuan a thumbs-up under his desk.
