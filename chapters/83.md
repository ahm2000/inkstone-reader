# Chapter 83: The Second Cross-Department War Room: Financial Firebreak

Velvet curtains stayed drawn over the tall windows of Beijing's second cross-department war room. Warm tungsten lamps cast steady pools of light across the long mahogany table, reflecting off white porcelain teacups and neat stacks of red-bordered financial briefs. Along the north wall, three wide digital displays traced real-time clearing flows across the country's major banks.

Around the inner ring sat the 24 core voting members of the Capital Top Council, every one holding half-premier or sub-national rank above provincial party secretaries and cabinet ministers. At the head sat Premier Gu Chengshan with his sleeves rolled neatly to his forearms, beside Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua, who tapped a silver fountain pen against his open ledger.

Flanking the head in high-backed chairs sat three white-haired Founding Elders: Elder Lin, the longtime architect of the state's financial backbone; Elder Chen Yanshi, whose sharp eyes carried thirty years of border command; and ninety-year-old Elder Song, resting both palms calmly on the curved handle of his wooden cane.

In the outer ring sat the non-voting observers with open notebooks: Fang Zhengming of China Construction Bank, Han Guoliang of ICBC, and senior directors from the central bank and national treasury.

Premier Gu Chengshan looked up from the Handong arrest cables and swept his gaze across the room.

"We have two financial battles to finish before 5:00 PM," Premier Gu Chengshan said clearly. "First, we lock down every yuan of dirty capital tied to the Handong and energy ministry rings before it slips offshore. Second, we finalize the legal and technical firebreak around the 300 trillion yuan sovereign reserve. Director Fang, give us the freeze count."

Fang Zhengming stood and gestured toward the left wall screen, where red network lines branched out from Jingzhou City toward Hong Kong, Macau, and Zurich.

"With the ledgers recovered from Ministry Division Chief Zhao Dehan and the confessions from Shanshui Group head Zhao Ruilong and Deputy Secretary Gao Yuliang, we have mapped forty-two linked shell companies," Fang Zhengming reported. "The combined dirty assets stand at 1.2 trillion yuan across real estate trusts, energy equity, and offshore clearing accounts. If we trigger a synchronized freeze right now, we can trap ninety-six percent of it before European markets open."

Han Guoliang of ICBC leaned forward. "One caution, Premier. Freezing 1.2 trillion yuan in a single afternoon could cause short-term jitters in provincial credit markets if rumors leak to retail investors."

"We do not let rumors leak," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua cut in firmly. "Every state bank absorbs the legitimate payroll obligations of Shanshui Group's simple workers overnight. The factories keep running, the salaries clear on time, and only the corrupt principals lose access to their accounts."

Premier Gu Chengshan nodded. "All 24 core members, cast your votes on the 1.2 trillion yuan firebreak order."

Around the inner ring, twenty-four hands rose without a single hesitation. Premier Gu Chengshan signed the red warrant and handed it to a waiting courier.

As the doors clicked shut behind the courier, the temperature in the room seemed to drop a degree. Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua closed the Handong binder and placed a single black-bound file in the center of the mahogany table.

"Now for the second matter," Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua said quietly. "The 300 trillion yuan sovereign reserve gift transferred by the anonymous benefactor."

Even the seasoned bank presidents in the outer ring sat straighter. A sum of 300 trillion yuan was a figure capable of reshaping national infrastructure, scientific research, and strategic security for half a century.

Elder Lin adjusted his spectacles and spoke in a steady, measured tone. "The technical firebreak is complete. We have sealed the entire 300 trillion yuan inside a dedicated sovereign stabilization vault under triple-key central control. It sits completely outside simple provincial budgets and commercial lending pools. No local clique, no ministry office, and no future Zhao Ruilong can ever siphon a single cent from it."

A younger treasury deputy in the outer ring hesitated, then raised his hand slightly. "Premier, Elders, forgive the question. Now that the transfer has cleared and the vault is secure, should the State Council prepare a quiet, highest-level delegation to visit the anonymous benefactor and express the nation's formal gratitude?"

Silence fell instantly across the mahogany table.

Tap.

Elder Song struck the tip of his wooden cane once against the thick carpet. The ninety-year-old elder did not raise his voice, yet every person in the room turned toward him at once.

"Listen carefully, young man," Elder Song said, his eyes clear and unblinking. "Nobody in this room knows the name of the holder of the Black Order. Nobody in this building has the clearance to ask for it, and nobody is permitted to send a delegation, an inquiry, or even a greeting card toward the untouchable Yunxi household."

The treasury deputy's face paled. He bowed his head quickly. "I understand, Elder Song. I spoke out of turn."

Elder Chen Yanshi spoke next, his voice rough as gravel. "The anonymous benefactor gave us 300 trillion yuan and handed us the knives to cut out a decade of rot in Handong. He asked for nothing in return except silence and clean governance. If we repay that by knocking on his door like nosy clerks, we deserve whatever storm follows."

Premier Gu Chengshan uncapped his pen and wrote a final line across the black-bound directive.

"The order is sealed," Premier Gu Chengshan declared. "No inquiries. No contact. By 5:00 PM, the 1.2 trillion yuan dirty network is frozen, and the 300 trillion yuan vault locks behind the central firebreak. Do your jobs, and keep the state clean."
