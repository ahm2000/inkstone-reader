# Chapter 83: Tang Xiaoyu's First Exam

Friday afternoon sunlight slanted softly across the linoleum floor of Room 601 in Yunxi County, painting warm golden squares beside the dining table.

At half past four, the front door clicked open and Tang Xiaoyu stepped inside, placing her white sneakers neatly beside Chen Jianguo's work shoes.

From the kitchen came the comforting sizzle of garlic in hot peanut oil as Liu Meifang stirred fresh green beans. Chen Jianguo was downstairs chatting by the bicycle shed, and Chen Yuan sat on the sofa folding freshly dried laundry while humming softly.

"Welcome home, Tang Xiaoyu!" Liu Meifang called warmly over the wok. "Wash your hands and grab an apple. Dinner is at six sharp, right after Chen Xuan gets off the bus."

"Okay, Aunt Liu Meifang!" Tang Xiaoyu answered brightly.

Instead of reaching for an apple, Tang Xiaoyu walked quietly to the dining table, set down her blue canvas schoolbag, and unzipped the front pocket. From a clear plastic sleeve, she slid out a crisp white exam sheet from Yunxi No. 2 Middle School.

It was her first monthly mathematics exam.

In the upper right corner stood a bold red *99*, circled cleanly twice. Beneath the score, her mathematics teacher had written a neat crimson note: *First in Class. Outstanding logical clarity.*

Tang Xiaoyu stood still for a few seconds, tracing the red ink lightly with her thumb. For a girl who had spent two years studying beside dim lamps and worrying about exercise books, that single page felt heavier than gold.

She did not run into the kitchen to brag or wave the paper around.

Instead, she walked softly to the white refrigerator humming in the corner. Right at eye level hung Chen Yuan's bright red rectangular fridge magnet, printed in bold black letters: *HOUSE RULE 1: DINNER IS AT SIX*.

Tang Xiaoyu lifted the bottom edge of the red magnet gently, slid the top margin of her exam neatly underneath it, and pressed the magnet back down until the paper hung completely straight.

Chen Yuan glanced over from the sofa, saw the bold red *99* gleaming against the white door, and broke into a wide, proud grin.

"Ninety-nine! First in the entire class!" Chen Yuan exclaimed, jumping up to pat Tang Xiaoyu's head affectionately. "Wait until Mom and Dad see that! You are putting me and Chen Xuan both to shame!"

Tang Xiaoyu blushed slightly, smiling shyly as she smoothed her red knitted sweater. "I just followed the parallel-line steps Brother Chen Xuan showed me last week."

She picked up her Chinese textbook, pulled a wooden stool close to the sixth-floor window overlooking the bus stop on Yunxi Avenue, and read quietly while glancing down at the street every few minutes, waiting for Chen Xuan's familiar washed gray jacket.

* * *

At twenty minutes past five, steady footsteps echoed up from the fifth-floor landing.

Tang Xiaoyu closed her textbook immediately, her dark eyes brightening. A moment later, the brass key turned in the lock, and Chen Xuan stepped inside carrying his worn cloth bag and the crisp evening breeze.

"I am home, Mom," Chen Xuan called warmly as he changed into his slippers.

"Just in time," Liu Meifang laughed from the kitchen doorway, wiping her hands on her floral apron. "Go wash your face, and look at the refrigerator door before you sit down!"

Chen Xuan smiled curiously and walked across the dining area toward the white refrigerator.

There, pinned neatly beneath Chen Yuan's red *HOUSE RULE 1: DINNER IS AT SIX* magnet, hung Tang Xiaoyu's crisp white mathematics exam.

Chen Xuan did not just glance at the score. He leaned in close, reading down every geometry proof and algebraic equation line by line, his expression softening with genuine, unhurried pride.

"Look at question twelve," Chen Xuan murmured approvingly, turning toward Tang Xiaoyu, who stood beside the dining chair twisting the hem of her sweater. "You drew the auxiliary line neatly with a ruler, labeled every angle cleanly, and skipped not a single step. Ninety-nine points and first in your class on your first monthly exam at Yunxi No. 2 Middle School."

He stepped over and rested a warm hand lightly on the top of her head.

"You worked hard, Tang Xiaoyu. I am very proud of you."

Tang Xiaoyu's chest swelled with quiet happiness, a bright smile blooming across her face. "Thank you, Brother Chen Xuan. Next month I will fix the one careless arithmetic mark and get a full hundred."

"Ninety-nine is already wonderful," Chen Xuan chuckled softly. "Now sit down for a second. I brought you something from the old craft street in Qingyan City."

* * *

Chen Xuan reached into his cloth bag and lifted out a small cardboard box wrapped neatly in pale blue paper and tied with white cotton string.

He placed the box gently on the wooden table in front of Tang Xiaoyu.

Tang Xiaoyu blinked in surprise, looked up for permission, and carefully untied the string without tearing the blue paper. When she lifted the lid and parted the white tissue inside, her small fingers froze completely.

Resting inside was a delicate, hand-painted porcelain doll about six inches tall.

The doll had glossy dark hair braided with tiny red ribbons, bright painted eyes, and a miniature quilted red winter coat with tiny brass buttons stitched down the front.

For three full seconds, not a sound escaped Tang Xiaoyu's lips.

Chen Xuan remembered the quiet evening weeks ago when Tang Xiaoyu had spoken softly to Liu Meifang about her late father. Whenever her father worked on distant construction sites before his accident, he used to call her every Sunday night from a payphone and promise that when his contract finished, he would bring her a little porcelain doll in a red coat from the big city.

He had never made it home to keep that promise.

Slowly, with trembling fingers, Tang Xiaoyu lifted the porcelain doll out of the box and cradled it tightly against her chest. Her lower lip quivered once, and silent, warm tears slipped down her cheeks onto her red knitted sweater.

They were not the frightened tears of a child hiding from the world. They were the quiet, long-held tears of a girl who finally knew she did not have to wait alone by the doorway anymore.

"Thank you, Brother Chen Xuan," Tang Xiaoyu whispered, her voice soft and unsteady as she pressed her cheek lightly against the doll's red sleeve. "It looks just like the one Dad described on the phone."

Chen Xuan pulled out the chair right beside her, sat down quietly, and handed her a clean white tissue without rushing her.

"Your father would be very proud to see that exam hanging on the refrigerator today, Tang Xiaoyu," Chen Xuan said gently, his voice warm and steady. "From now on, whenever you work hard and earn something to celebrate, you will always have a family right here waiting for you."

Tang Xiaoyu wiped her cheeks, hugged the porcelain doll once more before setting it upright beside her pencil case, and looked up at Chen Xuan with wet, shining eyes and the truest smile she had worn all year.

Right on cue, the wall clock struck six, and Liu Meifang stepped out carrying a steaming clay pot of lotus-root and pork-rib soup.

"House Rule Number One!" Chen Yuan announced cheerfully, setting down the chopsticks. "Dinner is at six! Nobody is letting Mom's soup get cold!"

Warm laughter filled Room 601 as Chen Jianguo walked through the door and everyone gathered beneath the bright yellow lamp.
