# Chapter 86: Black Monday at Haiyue Tower

Monday morning arrived in Qingyan City under a pale, cloudless sky, yet the fortieth floor of Haiyue Tower felt like the eye of a hurricane.

On the massive glass display mounted along the eastern wall of the executive war room, the stock ticker for Haiyue Hospitality and Property Group bled bright crimson.

At nine thirty, the share price had opened comfortably at twenty-eight yuan and forty fen. By ten o'clock, an avalanche of massive sell blocks had hammered it down seven percent. By eleven thirty, less than two hours into trading, Haiyue Group had plunged a staggering eighteen percent, wiping six billion yuan off its market value before lunch.

Inside the war room, Chief Financial Officer Zhou Wenhai stood beside the screen, his tie loosened and his forehead glistening with cold sweat as he scrolled through live order books.

"This is not retail panic, Miss Qin," Zhou Wenhai said hoarsely. "Every time domestic funds try to catch the drop, another fifty-million-yuan sell wall slams down from offshore northbound channels. They are selling to break our floor."

At the long walnut table, Qin Yuhan kept her spine straight in a dark navy blazer. Her dark eyes moved rapidly across three open laptops, tracking order flow and incoming notices without a flicker of panic.

"Trace the brokerage seats," Qin Yuhan ordered cleanly. "And pull the source of those articles that went live at ten fifteen."

Legal Director Fang Mei stepped forward, placing freshly printed bulletins onto the wood.

"Sixteen overseas wires and four domestic portals published the exact same report within ten minutes," Fang Mei said tightly. "They claim Haiyue Group concealed four point two billion yuan in off-book property guarantees tied to Wanfeng Freight's collapse in Lanshui. It is complete fabrication."

"Issue an exchange clarification immediately with our audited third-quarter balance sheet," Qin Yuhan replied without hesitation. "File emergency defamation notices against every portal carrying that rumor before afternoon trading."

Before Fang Mei could turn, the glass doors burst open.

Jin Ke rushed into the war room, his face pale as paper, clutching three stamped bank faxes in his trembling hand.

"Chairman Qin! Miss Qin!" Jin Ke called out, breathing hard. "Huadong Commercial Bank just suspended our one-point-five-billion-yuan revolving credit line. Five minutes later, Jincheng Trust and Donghai Development Bank sent identical freeze notices. All three partner banks froze our credit lines at once, citing unverified offshore default risk!"

A suffocating silence dropped over the room.

Three point six billion yuan in operational credit lines, frozen in a single morning. With two coastal resort projects entering peak settlement this week, losing all three lines at once could choke Haiyue Group's liquidity in forty-eight hours.

At the head of the table, Qin Zhengming slowly pushed himself up, his face flushing dark red before draining to an alarming ash gray.

"Donghai Development Bank has partnered with Haiyue for eighteen years," Qin Zhengming rasped, his hand rising to clutch the left side of his chest. "Their president would never freeze our line over an unsigned Hong Kong rumor unless someone forced his hand."

Qin Zhengming's knees buckled against the walnut table, his breathing turning shallow and sharp.

"Dad!"

Qin Yuhan was out of her chair in an instant, catching Qin Zhengming firmly by the arm before he could slump onto the carpet.

"Jin Ke, bring Dad's blood pressure tablets and warm water right now!" Qin Yuhan commanded, easing Qin Zhengming back into his chair and loosening his collar. "Everyone else, keep working."

Jin Ke returned within seconds with warm water and two white tablets. Qin Zhengming swallowed them slowly, leaning back against the leather headrest with his chest rising heavily.

"Qin Yuhan," Qin Zhengming whispered weakly, gripping her wrist with cold fingers. "This is not a normal market drop. Someone wants to swallow Haiyue whole."

"I know, Dad," Qin Yuhan said softly, covering his trembling hand with her warm palm. "Rest in the inner lounge. Let me handle the afternoon session. I will not let anyone take what you built."

* * *

Before Jin Ke could escort Qin Zhengming to the rest room, a crisp chime sounded from the encrypted video console.

Zhou Wenhai glanced at the caller tag and stiffened.

"Miss Qin," Zhou Wenhai said quietly. "It is Aegis Meridian Capital's Shanghai office. Their Asia-Pacific chief envoy, Shen Kaiyuan, is already at Qingyan Airport and requests five minutes."

Qin Yuhan's gaze turned arctic.

"Put him on the main screen," Qin Yuhan said calmly.

The wide display beside the bleeding stock chart flickered on. Appearing in high definition was a tall, impeccably groomed man wearing a silver silk tie inside a sunlit private lounge.

Shen Kaiyuan offered a practiced, condescending smile that never reached his cold eyes.

"Good morning, Chairman Qin Zhengming, Miss Qin Yuhan," Shen Kaiyuan greeted smoothly. "I apologize for the abrupt call, though I imagine you have been watching the exchange. Eighteen percent in two hours is a terrible wound for a family-run hospitality group."

"Cut the theater, Shen Kaiyuan," Qin Yuhan said coldly, stepping squarely into the camera frame in front of her father. "Aegis Meridian is behind the offshore short blocks and the fabricated debt reports. What do you want?"

Shen Kaiyuan chuckled softly, lifting both hands in mock innocence.

"Careful with accusations, Miss Qin," Shen Kaiyuan murmured smoothly. "Aegis Meridian Capital simply spots distressed assets early. Right now, three partner banks have frozen your liquidity, your share price is heading for a margin collapse, and by Wednesday your contractors will start filing liens."

He leaned closer to the lens, his smile sharpening like a blade.

"I am flying into Qingyan City today to offer Haiyue Group a lifeline," Shen Kaiyuan continued. "Aegis Meridian will inject four billion yuan in immediate bridge capital and calm the market before tomorrow's close. In exchange, the Qin family transfers fifty-one percent controlling equity in Haiyue Group to our Zurich holding vehicle at a forty-percent discount to Friday's close."

Behind Qin Yuhan, Qin Zhengming gripped his armrests until his knuckles turned white.

"Forty percent below Friday's close for control?" Qin Zhengming breathed, shaking with fury. "You manufacture a crash in the morning and try to rob us at knifepoint before lunch! You are offshore vultures!"

"Business is never personal, Chairman Qin Zhengming," Shen Kaiyuan replied lazily, glancing at his gold watch. "Review the term sheet I emailed your legal director. Tomorrow afternoon, I will visit Haiyue Tower in person with the final contract. Sign it, and your family keeps a comfortable minority stake. Refuse, and we will see how much of Haiyue Group survives Tuesday's closing bell."

Qin Yuhan did not raise her voice. Her face was pale from tension, yet her eyes burned with unbreakable resolve.

"Save yourself the drive to Haiyue Tower, Shen Kaiyuan," Qin Yuhan said clearly, every word crisp as glass. "Haiyue Group does not deal with extortionists, and we will not sell a single controlling share to Aegis Meridian."

Without waiting for his reply, Qin Yuhan pressed the red disconnect switch.

The screen went black.

Qin Yuhan turned sharply back to Zhou Wenhai, Fang Mei, and Jin Ke.

"Zhou Wenhai, tally every yuan of liquid cash across all fourteen hotel subsidiaries and prepare the deeds for my personal commercial properties in Qingyan City as collateral," Qin Yuhan instructed swiftly. "Fang Mei, file our formal complaint with the provincial securities bureau within the hour. Jin Ke, tell the presidents of all three partner banks I am coming to their offices with our audited ledgers."

She drew a steady breath, her gaze returning to the glowing crimson ticker.

"They think eighteen percent will break us," Qin Yuhan said quietly. "Let them watch us hold the line."
