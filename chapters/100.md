# Chapter 100: Moving into Emerald Riverside Villa

An hour later, two fresh red marriage booklets rested neatly inside Qin Yuhan's leather handbag. The official photo against the red backdrop had turned out surprisingly natural, capturing Chen Xuan's relaxed, cheerful smile beside a slightly blushing Qin Yuhan.

Their stop at Chen Xuan's small rented flat afterward had taken scarcely ten minutes. Everything he cared to bring fit effortlessly into his worn dark blue canvas bag, a small cardboard box of spices and kitchen knives, and his dented green metal thermos.

Now, Qin Yuhan guided her sleek black sedan smoothly through the tree-lined gates of Emerald Riverside Villa in Pudong. Through the tall windshield, the glowing spheres of the Oriental Pearl Tower and the glass towers of Tomson Riviera right next door shimmered under the deepening autumn sky.

As Qin Yuhan parked in her private stone courtyard, she turned to Chen Xuan in the passenger seat with a slightly nervous smile.

"This is Emerald Riverside Villa," she explained gently. "I bought it two years ago when Haiyue Group expanded its Lujiazui headquarters. It is a bit large for one person, and it sits right next to Tomson Riviera, so the view of the Huangpu River is quite peaceful. Please do not feel pressured or uncomfortable living here. From today on, it is your home too."

Chen Xuan stepped out into the crisp autumn evening air and looked around the quiet courtyard framed by golden ginkgo trees and manicured hedges. He chuckled warmly to himself.

He certainly had no intention of telling his new wife that the land trust behind half of this Pudong riverfront district, including several top floors over in Tomson Riviera, sat quietly inside an old White Order asset folder he had not bothered to open in three years. To Qin Yuhan, he was simply her warm-hearted, junior logistics supervisor husband, and he liked it that way more than anything in the world.

"Not uncomfortable at all," Chen Xuan said cheerfully, hoisting his canvas bag over one shoulder and lifting his cardboard box of kitchen tools. "The courtyard gets great autumn sun for drying tangerine peels, and I bet the kitchen has a strong gas stove."

Qin Yuhan smiled, visibly relieved, and led him inside.

The interior of Emerald Riverside Villa was spacious, bright, and spotless, with tall windows overlooking the glittering lights of the river. Yet as Chen Xuan walked through the marble living room, he noticed how quiet and untouched it felt, more like a five-star hotel suite than a lived-in home.

Qin Yuhan showed him up the wide staircase to a bright guest suite facing the garden. "This room has its own bathroom and balcony. Feel free to arrange it however you like. My room is across the hall."

"Very nice," Chen Xuan said, setting his blue canvas bag by the wardrobe and placing his dented green thermos proudly on the sleek oak desk. "Now show me the kitchen. You promised I get full command of dinner."

Downstairs, the wide marble kitchen was gleaming and practically brand new. Chen Xuan opened the tall silver refrigerator and laughed out loud. Inside sat six bottles of sparkling water, half a carton of milk, and two plastic boxes of cold lettuce salad.

"Just as I suspected," Chen Xuan teased, shaking his head. "President Qin, how have you survived two years in this house?"

Qin Yuhan folded her arms, looking mildly sheepish. "Usually I work late at the office and eat whatever my assistant orders. I never really learned to cook."

"Then it is a good thing you married a logistics clerk," Chen Xuan said with a grin.

He rolled up the sleeves of his white shirt, washed his hands, and unpacked the fresh groceries he had picked up from a lively neighborhood market right before they entered the villa gates: prime pork ribs, fresh ginger, green scallions, rock sugar, and crisp autumn greens.

Within minutes, the quiet marble kitchen came alive. The steady tap of Chen Xuan's chef knife against the wooden board sounded crisp and rhythmic. Soon, ginger and scallions sizzled in hot oil, and the rich, sweet-savory aroma of caramelized rock sugar and braised pork ribs drifted through the entire first floor.

Qin Yuhan leaned against the kitchen doorway, her cream blazer draped over a dining chair, watching him work with quiet fascination. Under the warm yellow kitchen lights, the huge, chilly villa suddenly felt soft and alive.

Half an hour later, three steaming dishes sat on the wide dining table overlooking the shimmering Huangpu River: glossy braised pork ribs, garlic-sautéed autumn greens, and a clear pot of golden radish soup, alongside two bowls of fluffy white rice.

"Try a piece," Chen Xuan urged, placing a tender rib into her bowl.

Qin Yuhan took a bite. The meat melted off the bone, savory and lightly sweet with a clean finish of ginger. Her eyes widened in genuine delight.

"Chen Xuan," she murmured softly, "this is better than any five-star restaurant in Lujiazui."

"Eat up," Chen Xuan laughed, pouring her a warm cup of jasmine tea. "From tomorrow on, I clock out at 4:55 PM, swing by the market, and have dinner waiting when you get off work."

For the first time in years, Qin Yuhan felt a deep, quiet warmth settle in her chest as she smiled across the table at her new husband.

Outside the villa, a short distance beyond the garden wall in the quiet autumn shadows, silent matte-black silhouettes of the Black Guard settled effortlessly into their permanent perimeter watch, completely unseen by anyone inside as the warm lights of Emerald Riverside Villa glowed peacefully beside the river.
