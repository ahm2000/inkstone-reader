# Chapter 50: The Banyan Club Falls

Outside the Riverside Inn, cold drizzle turned the empty parking lot black under the sodium lamps.

Lian Cheng stepped out of the lobby in his neat gray cardigan, carrying the steel tea flask under his left arm. He unlocked his gray Passat, slid behind the steering wheel, and started the engine.

He adjusted his thin glasses, reached for his phone to message Huang Boren, and froze as the passenger door clicked open.

Number Seven slid into the front passenger seat without a sound. Before Lian Cheng could draw breath, the rear lock clicked and Number Eleven shifted quietly behind his headrest.

"Hands on the wheel, Lian Cheng," Number Seven said flatly, his tone as calm as a closing vault door.

Panic flashed behind Lian Cheng's mild mask. His right hand twitched toward his coat pocket, where a spare glass vial lay beside his keys.

Number Seven moved faster than sight, locking Lian Cheng's wrist cleanly against the console until his fingers opened. With his free hand, Number Seven took the steel flask from the seat and lifted the glass vial from Lian Cheng's pocket.

"The tea in Room 1208 was swapped before Dong Haisheng drank," Number Seven said calmly. "The test strip is bagged, and Dong Haisheng is already on the phone with the Provincial Discipline Commission."

Lian Cheng's face turned gray. "Who are you?"

"People who need you alive to testify," Number Seven replied. "Intentional poisoning carries a capital sentence. Handing over every order Huang Boren gave you is the only way you avoid an execution yard."

Number Eleven reached forward from the backseat, switched off the Passat's engine, and pocketed the keys.

Rain tapped softly on the windshield for five long seconds. Lian Cheng stared at the steel flask. After nine years working for Huang Boren, he knew his boss never saved a failed errand boy.

His shoulders slumped against the seat.

"Take me to the Commission," Lian Cheng whispered shakily. "I have Huang Boren's voice recordings from the Banyan Club on my backup phone. I will give you everything."

* * *

Three hours later, inside a brightly lit room at the Provincial Discipline Commission in Qingyan, Dong Haisheng sat with a wool blanket over his trembling shoulders.

His eyes were red from sleeplessness, yet his hand moved steadily as he signed and thumbprinted the final page of his full confession. Beside him, a clerk sealed the witness statement of his driver, Hua Qiang, together with the forensic report confirming cardiac poison in Lian Cheng's flask.

At the head of the table, Vice Director Gao Zhengyi laid Dong Haisheng's thick blue ledger next to the confession. Secretary Fan Weiguo leaned over, scanning columns of shell companies and wire codes.

"Look at the third tab, Vice Director Gao," Fan Weiguo said tightly. "This reaches far past Yunxi County."

Gao Zhengyi adjusted his spectacles and traced the numbers.

For six years, Shen Jinbao's Tiancheng Demolition had skimmed resettlement funds and forced unlawful land transfers across three counties. Forty percent of the black cash flowed directly into Huang Boren's Banyan Club, washed through inflated memberships and private auctions.

From the Banyan Club, the funds split into three corrupt regional shipping accounts at Qingyan Port. Every single account cleared customs under Zhou Guodong's Shenghua Maritime Group, routed straight through Berth Seven.

"Huang Boren built a pipeline from county demolition sites straight to the docks," Gao Zhengyi said coldly. "And Zhou Guodong's Shenghua Maritime Group took a twelve percent cut to wash the manifests."

"Lian Cheng's backup phone just finished extraction," Fan Weiguo added, tapping his tablet. "Two voice messages from Huang Boren ordering the poisoning, plus logs showing Shen Jinbao met Huang Boren at the Banyan Club at six this evening."

Gao Zhengyi closed the ledger sharply and reached for his stamp.

"Notify Director Mai Zhenhua immediately," Gao Zhengyi ordered. "Coordinate with Qingyan Public Security. Seal the Banyan Club tonight, arrest Shen Jinbao on the spot, freeze Huang Boren's accounts, and open a special audit into Shenghua Maritime Group."

* * *

Beside Qingyan's western lake, the Banyan Club sat behind high brick walls and weeping willows, its courtyard lanterns glowing gold in the rain.

Inside the second-floor mahogany lounge, Shen Jinbao paced the carpet, a crystal glass of liquor shaking in his thick fingers. Sweat darkened his open silk collar.

For the sixth time in twenty minutes, he redialed Huang Boren's private line and got only a dead automated message.

"Pick up, Huang Boren!" Shen Jinbao cursed, swallowing half his glass in one gulp.

His bodyguard shifted uneasily by the curtains. "Boss Shen, maybe we should drive south tonight. Tiancheng Demolition was already sealed yesterday."

"Shut up!" Shen Jinbao snapped, his eyes bloodshot. "Half the bureau chiefs in Qingyan eat at Chairman Huang Boren's table! Lian Cheng went to Riverside Inn tonight. Once Dong Haisheng's heart stops in his sleep, there is no witness left. By tomorrow noon, Huang Boren makes two calls and this whole mess disappears!"

Before the last word faded, the heavy iron courtyard gates outside clanged open.

Red and blue flashing lights flooded through the frosted windows, slicing across the mahogany lounge in harsh streaks. Heavy tires crunched over wet gravel, followed by the rapid tramp of dozens of boots climbing the marble stairs.

The crystal glass slipped from Shen Jinbao's numb fingers and hit the carpet, soaking his shoes in amber liquor.

The lounge doors swung wide. Four armed public security officers stepped inside to block the exits. Behind them walked Vice Director Gao Zhengyi in a dark wool overcoat, holding up a stamped red-header warrant.

"Shen Jinbao," Gao Zhengyi announced clearly. "Under joint order of the Provincial Discipline Commission and Qingyan Public Security, you are under arrest for embezzlement, unlawful demolition, and conspiracy. The Banyan Club is hereby sealed."

Shen Jinbao stumbled back against the mahogany bar as his last shred of hope died.

"This is a mistake!" Shen Jinbao yelled, his voice cracking high. "Call Chairman Huang Boren! Call Zhou Guodong at Shenghua Maritime Group! You cannot touch me!"

"Huang Boren's domestic accounts were frozen forty minutes ago, and a border watch is active on his name," Gao Zhengyi replied evenly. "Dong Haisheng has confessed in full, and Lian Cheng is in custody with the poisoned flask. As for Zhou Guodong's Shenghua Maritime Group, our auditors will be at his door before morning."

Two officers stepped forward, locked Shen Jinbao's arms behind his back, and snapped steel handcuffs shut around his wrists. His knees buckled, and they hauled him upright toward the stairs.

Down at the front gate, investigators smoothed white paper seals across the Banyan Club's gilded brass plaque and stamped them with red ink.

Across town, in a quiet courtyard, Number One read a three-line report on his encrypted handset and set it gently beside his teacup.

"Shen Jinbao is taken," Number One said softly. "The Banyan Club is closed. Let us see how long Huang Boren and Zhou Guodong can hold their breath."
