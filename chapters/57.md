# Chapter 57: Ministry of State Security: The Mole Hunt

At five past seven on Monday morning, the third-floor briefing room of the Ministry of State Security sat under blue cigarette smoke. Ten bureau directors sat around a long steel table, eyes drifting again and again toward the empty leather chair at the head.

Twelve hours earlier, their Deputy Director had sat there signing off on the quarterly port review. Two hours before dawn, his badge was cancelled, his home sealed, and his name wiped from the active roster.

Internal-Audit Bureau Director Han Dongsheng tapped a folder against the steel, lighting his fourth cigarette in thirty minutes. "Stop staring at the empty seat. He is not coming back. The State Council order is blunt. We deliver our breach report before the eight o'clock War Room, or this ministry goes under outside control."

Counter-Espionage First Bureau Director Tang Bowen let out a dry laugh that turned into a cough. "Outside control? Look at the seals on those boxes, Han Dongsheng. We are already under outside control. Someone dropped twenty years of Zurich Northern Ledger money charts onto our vault computer at four fifteen, and our alarms never blinked."

"Because they did not come through our network," Cyber-Defense Bureau Director Song Yiming snapped, pushing up his glasses. "Three full checks since five o'clock. Zero outside logins. Zero warnings. Those files appeared straight on an offline computer three floors underground. Either someone walked past twelve locked doors with a drive, or we face deep access we lack the rank to track."

"Do not hide behind computer talk, Song Yiming," Foreign-Station European Desk Director Mei Guohua said sharply. "Zurich Northern Ledger has been a ghost file on my desk since 2006. Every time Geneva got close to a fake middleman moving shipping insurance through Zurich, headquarters froze my budget. Who signed those freeze orders?"

Every head turned slowly toward Personnel Security Bureau Director Yuan Wenkai.

Yuan Wenkai stiffened, jaw tight. "If you claim I covered for the Deputy Director, say it into the recorder, Mei Guohua. Every freeze order carried his stamp and a second signature from Domestic Port-Watch."

"Keep my bureau out of your suicide pact!" Domestic Port-Watch Bureau Director Shi Kang barked, slamming his ashtray onto the steel so ash scattered across the maps. "My inspectors flagged strange container weights in Ningbo, Tianjin, and Xiamen fourteen times in six years! Every report vanished into Counter-Espionage Second Bureau for joint review!"

Counter-Espionage Second Bureau Director Fu Zhen did not flinch, though a vein pulsed along his neck. "Your reports were trash, Shi Kang. Half had no middleman names, and half pointed at normal state-backed shipping companies. You wanted me to raid three major ports on your wild guess?"

"My wild guess just turned out to be a twenty-year national money drain!" Shi Kang shouted, face bright red. "Read line forty-two of the Zurich Northern Ledger file! Those normal shipping companies smuggled restricted rare-earth minerals and stolen navy blueprints inside farm-goods containers right under our noses!"

"Sit down, Shi Kang," Signals Interception Bureau Director Qiao Lian said softly, knuckles white around her mug. "Yelling will not erase the dates. Look at what Technical Forensics pulled off the overnight drop."

Every eye turned to Technical Forensics Bureau Director Luo Pei, hunched over a tablet with red-rimmed eyes and a quiet dread that silenced the room.

"The Zurich Northern Ledger did not just bribe port clerks," Luo Pei whispered hoarsely. "Two secret code sets hiding their coastal messages match our own Ministry of State Security code books from 2019 and 2022. They were not cracked from outside. They were copied right off an internal code-making computer."

A suffocating silence fell over the table.

"Say that again," Tang Bowen demanded, cigarette frozen near his lips. "The Deputy Director was not the only mole here?"

"The Deputy Director never had physical access to the code-making room," Luo Pei replied, lifting bloodshot eyes. "Only three bureau directors in this room hold the two-key passes for that room."

Chairs scraped back as men pulled their elbows off the steel table.

Foreign-Station Maritime Desk Director Cheng Jiakai crushed his cigarette out, eyes darting between Song Yiming, Qiao Lian, and Fu Zhen. "Hold on. Before we tear each other apart, how did whoever sent these files crack a twenty-year Zurich ring, trace every port link, and unlock our 2022 secret codes in one weekend?"

"We know who cracked it," Han Dongsheng muttered, staring at the blank cover page. "Nobody here is allowed to write the name down, and none of us holds the rank to ask. White Guard went silent on every line at midnight. When the regular watchdogs lock down and a foreign money book drops on our desk by sunrise, you do not need a memo to know what moved."

"I do not care what ghost moved in the dark," Tang Bowen snapped, sweat darkening his collar. "I care that we chased shadows in Europe for twenty years while a criminal ring ran a smuggling highway through our ports. At eight o'clock, Vice Premier Zhou Zhenhua will ask why State Security needed an unrecorded ghost unit to tell us our own Deputy Director was on a Zurich payroll."

"Then we do not walk in empty-handed," Qiao Lian said sharply, sliding a printed sheet across the steel. "Every sign-in record for the code-making room from 2018 to last night is right there. Three deputy section chiefs under Counter-Espionage Second Bureau and two computer repair workers under Song Yiming signed in after hours on the exact nights the codes leaked."

Song Yiming went pale. "My repair workers were cleared by Personnel Security!"

"And Personnel Security answers to Yuan Wenkai," Fu Zhen shot back. "Every dirty clearance went across his desk!"

"Enough!" Han Dongsheng slammed both hands flat onto the table. "Internal Audit has locked all five exits. Every director hands over his phone, room pass, and staff list right now. Mei Guohua, Cheng Jiakai, send emergency stop warnings to Europe and our sea desks before Zurich realizes their book is exposed. Shi Kang, freeze every flagged shipping broker from Dalian to Shenzhen."

"And the three directors who hold passes to the code room?" Tang Bowen asked softly.

"They stay under armed guard until Internal Audit checks their fingerprints against the door records," Han Dongsheng replied, lighting a fresh cigarette. "If another mole sits at this table, he is not making it to the eight o'clock War Room."

Nobody moved for five long seconds. Then ten pairs of eyes slid sideways through the smoke, staring coldly at coworkers they had trusted for twenty years.
