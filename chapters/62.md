# Chapter 62: CPPCC: The Quiet Reckoning

The meeting hall of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference smelled of stale smoke and cold sweat. Ten committee chairs sat around the round table, not one face calm.

Vice Chairman Zhao Yunsheng stood at the head of the table, thin and drained from the war room. His hand no longer shook, and a cold hardness sat in his eyes.

Yet nobody looked at Zhao Yunsheng. Every eye stayed locked in fear on the four men seated behind him.

In the center chair sat ninety-year-old Elder Song of the Song Family, his hands resting calmly on his dark wood cane. Flanking him in order of seniority sat three family heads: Gao Chengde of the Gao Family, Zhou Mingyuan of the Zhou Family, and Pei Shoushan of the Pei Family. Not one smiled.

"Turn off every phone and drop it in the tray by the door," Zhao Yunsheng ordered, exhaling gray smoke. "Nobody calls a secretary. Nobody calls Zurich."

At the word Zurich, Overseas Chinese and Foreign Affairs Chair Kong Weiguo froze with his teacup halfway up. Across from him, billionaire Commerce and Industry Chair Hu Zhenbang set his gold pen down with a sharp click.

"Zhao Yunsheng," Retired Provincial Chair and Senior Adviser Pei Yongnian said, forcing a polite smile and glancing hopefully at Pei Shoushan. "Rumors have flown since sunrise. Three hundred officials taken overnight? The Standing Committee deserves a calm briefing instead of secrecy. Our elders know stability matters."

Pei Shoushan looked straight at Pei Yongnian with cold disgust, cutting off any hope of family shelter. "Do not drag the Pei Family into your excuses, Pei Yongnian. Open the binder first."

Pei Yongnian's smile froze.

Zhao Yunsheng pushed ten gray binders across the wood. "Open page one. Read Shengyuan Capital's donor records."

Ten binders flipped open, and every calm mask shattered at once.

Culture and Philanthropy Chair Rong Shichang turned chalk-white, gripping page two. "This cannot be real. Fang Qiming donated 300 million yuan to our southern museum fund four years ago through official charity channels!"

"Turn to page six, Rong Shichang," Social and Legal Affairs Chair Du Qingshan said coldly. "Look where the money really went. 40 million yuan fixed roofs. 260 million yuan bounced through a fake art firm in Luxembourg into Zurich accounts owned by the sons of Vice Governor Lin Zhengde and Energy Vice Minister He Guoliang."

Rong Shichang slumped back as if struck. "I knew nothing about Luxembourg! Fang Qiming shook hands with provincial leaders and signed a display check! You cannot blame an adviser for money laundering!"

"Nobody says you wired Zurich, Rong Shichang," Proposals and Advisory Review Chair Wen Ruolan cut in sharply. "Discipline Inspection wants to know why your office gave Fang Qiming's deputy a VIP travel pass on three European trips with zero customs checks."

Rong Shichang's mouth opened, then shut. He wiped his damp brow with a shaking hand.

Beside him, Hu Zhenbang loosened his silk tie, jaw tight over page nine. "Why is the Private Business Roundtable here? Yes, Shengyuan Capital sponsored our coastal summit, and Fang Qiming sat on my committee. Half the richest men in the country sit there! Treat every donor like a traitor, and private money will panic before Shanghai and Shenzhen open!"

"Do not hide behind business, Hu Zhenbang!" Economic Affairs Chair Cai Boxiang snapped, jabbing his cigarette until hot ash hit the cloth. "Read line fourteen of your guest list! Twelve provincial investment chiefs met Fang Qiming's Swiss bankers in your hotel's back suites! While you toasted businessmen in the ballroom, they packaged fake demolition loans from Yunxi County at fourteen times real value!"

"I am an unpaid adviser, not a policeman!" Hu Zhenbang shouted, face turning bright red. "How could I know what businessmen said in back rooms? I barely spoke ten sentences to Fang Qiming all year!"

"Ten sentences?" United Front Liaison Chair Jiang Chengzhi spoke softly, sliding one photo across the wood. "Here you are on Fang Qiming's yacht off Hong Kong last October, drinking Moutai with Customs Director Xu Wenbin."

Color drained from Hu Zhenbang's cheeks. "That was a business reception! Forty people stood on that deck!"

"And thirty-two of them are sitting in Discipline Inspection questioning rooms right now," Du Qingshan said flatly.

Silence crushed the hall. Resource and Environment Chair Xie Guangming took a shaky drag of his cigarette, fingers trembling as he shut his binder.

"I have served twelve years since retiring," Pei Yongnian said quickly, raising both hands and stealing another frantic glance at Pei Shoushan. "I went to two ribbon-cuttings for Fang Qiming's green-mining charity three years ago. I never took a fee, never pushed a deal, never made a call. Throw the dirty ones out right now."

"How fast you remember exact visits, Pei Yongnian," Wen Ruolan murmured without looking up. "Your grandson's shipping firm got a 13 million yuan fee from Shengyuan Capital two weeks after the second ribbon-cutting."

Pei Yongnian's hands dropped onto the wood. Behind Zhao Yunsheng, Pei Shoushan closed his eyes in cold shame, then nodded once to Gao Chengde and Zhou Mingyuan. The Pei Family had cut Pei Yongnian loose.

Kong Weiguo crushed his cigarette hard, sweat beading on his forehead. "Zhao Yunsheng, stop this! Kick out half our wealthy advisers in one morning, and business groups in London, Singapore, and Zurich will scream Beijing is attacking private wealth! Let tainted members step down quietly next month for health reasons! Save face for this house, and keep capital calm!"

Thump.

Elder Song's dark wood cane struck the floor like a gunshot. Every man flinched.

Elder Song leaned forward, ninety-year-old eyes blazing until billionaire advisers shrank in their suits.

"Keep capital calm?" Elder Song's voice rumbled across the smoke. "Kong Weiguo, Hu Zhenbang, you dare threaten this house with private money? Hear what real patriotism looks like. Earlier today, one private citizen gave this country 300 trillion yuan completely free, with up to 500 trillion yuan ready if needed, straight into China Construction Bank, ICBC, Bank of China, and Huaxia Bank. He did not ask for a committee badge, a seat here, or a photo on a yacht."

Hu Zhenbang's jaw dropped. Kong Weiguo stared in speechless shock, turning gray.

"Meanwhile, your wealthy advisers hid behind polite titles while Fang Qiming drained over 100 trillion yuan from the state," Elder Song said like winter iron. "Gao Chengde, Zhou Mingyuan, and Pei Shoushan sit right here so every Beijing family hears one rule: nobody saves face for traitors. Not one family will shield a dirty relative."

"Not one," Gao Chengde said flatly. Zhou Mingyuan nodded beside him with cold eyes.

Zhao Yunsheng jammed his cigarette into the ashtray. "No quiet resignations. No medical retirements. Du Qingshan and Wen Ruolan run the emergency review now. Every national and provincial adviser whose name, family fund, or charity touches Fang Qiming's donor records is suspended now and stripped of titles before noon. Passport blocks go to Public Security Minister Guo Zhengming within thirty minutes. Anyone here who wants to confess before Gao Zhengyi's team knocks has thirty minutes left."

Nobody argued. Wealthy tycoons and retired elders stared at their shaking hands as the shelter of honorary titles crashed down around them.
