# Chapter 31: Card Zero-One

That night in the quiet courtyard, Number One, head of the Black Pine Guard, sat beside a locked drawer that held two ledgers.

The first listed everyone who had ever shown kindness to Chen Xuan. Beside each name sat a note recording what was owed, a debt of gratitude to be repaid many times over.

The second listed what others owed Chen Xuan. Every page was blank. He never kept score of favors.

Number Seven, commander of the mobile unit, set down a cup of tea. "Number One, People's Hospital just reported in. Zhong Tiantian, Zhong Hai's young son, has been moved to a fourth-floor bed. His surgery is set for Thursday. A shell charity fund is covering the bill, so nothing can be traced back to the Boss."

"And Zhong Hai?"

"On Sunday he walked up to Wei Kun's desk and confessed everything on his own, before anyone even questioned him. Wei Kun didn't forgive him. He only told him to go sit with his son."

Number One wrote a single line in the ledger, then added a second beneath it: *The law will weigh the cooperation of a witness. The hospital bed stays.*

"One more thing," said Number Nineteen, the mobile unit's second. "Number Fourteen oiled his chair in exchange for a tea egg. And Hengxin Logistics has booked a Friday dinner for twenty people at the Yunhai Grand Hotel."

Number One's pen paused above the page.

Then, for the first time all week, he smiled. It was small and dry, like a man watching an old wager come due.

"The Yunhai, of all the hotels in the city." He closed the ledger. "Pull Friday's guest list. And make sure nobody tells him what that hotel really is."

"Number One?"

"Hengxin is just an ordinary logistics firm. The Boss works there like any other clerk and owns none of it. The Yunhai, however, belongs to a holding company, and three years ago its trustee gave the hotel manager one strict instruction: one day a young man will lay a black card on your counter. Serve him, ask nothing, tell nobody. The Boss thinks it's just a dining card a friend gave him."

Number Seven frowned slightly. "And the manager?"

"He's about to have the worst and best Friday of his life." Number One slid the drawer shut.

* * *

On Friday evening, thirty-eight stories of illuminated glass rose above the riverfront. Beneath them stood twenty clerks from Hengxin Logistics, snapping selfies and trying not to look out of place.

A sleek black Audi glided up to the curb. The tinted window rolled down to reveal Zhang Pengfei in a crisp red tie, still wearing sunglasses after dark.

"Manager Hu! Pan Xiulan! Hold on, I'll go park!" He waved a metallic silver card out the window. "Young Master Qi Zihao lent me his Silver VIP card and his car! Ten percent off and a complimentary fruit platter!"

The clerks let out an impressed murmur. Zhang Pengfei's chest puffed out, the humiliation of the week finally fading.

Across the street, a city bus hissed to a stop. Chen Xuan stepped off with his worn canvas bag slung over one shoulder.

"You took the bus?" Zhang Pengfei laughed so loudly that a doorman glanced over. "You could have squeezed into my back seat!"

Chen Xuan nodded easily to the group. "Evening, Manager Hu. Evening, Pan Xiulan. The stop is right outside. No traffic, and I even got a seat."

Inside the lobby, Hu Dewei handed their reservation slip to the front-desk clerk, a young woman with her hair pinned in a neat bun. Her expression shifted at once.

"I'm terribly sorry, Manager Hu. Your Peony Room on the third floor was released ten minutes ago. A Diamond guest needed it to entertain clients. We can offer you two side tables in the second-floor buffet hall instead."

"Leave this to me!" Zhang Pengfei slapped the silver card onto the marble counter. "Miss, do you know whose card this is? Young Master Qi Zihao's! Give us our room back!"

The clerk glanced down at the card, her polite smile never wavering.

"Sir, Silver is our entry-level membership. The guest in the Peony Room holds a Diamond card and has spent three million yuan with us this year. A Silver card cannot bump a Diamond guest."

At the bottom of the ladder again. Zhang Pengfei's face turned deep purple under the stares of the entire department.

"It's alright, Manager Hu." Chen Xuan stepped up calmly. "The buffet hall is fine. Or I can try the card a friend gave me for dinner."

"What card?" Zhang Pengfei snapped, desperate to vent his embarrassment on someone. "A bus pass?"

Chen Xuan reached into his canvas bag. Something slipped from the side pocket and rolled across the polished marble.

It was a small float carved from dark yellow wood, with a tiny square seal cut into its base.

Then came the card. It was plain matte black with no text on either side, only a single number engraved in one corner: 01. It was the hotel's highest pass.

It touched the marble counter with a soft click.

The young clerk stared down at it, and her entire body went rigid.

Her training binder contained one page she had never expected to need. *If a black card engraved 01 is ever placed on this desk, press the red button beneath the counter before speaking to anyone.*

Her hand trembling slightly, she pressed the button.
