# Chapter 96: Early Flight Back to Shanghai

At five-fifty on Tuesday morning, the small alarm clock beside Chen Xuan's bed let out a soft buzz. He pressed the button down and sat up before the sound could wake the apartment.

In the kitchen, a warm light was already glowing. By the time Chen Xuan washed his face and stepped into the living room, the smell of millet porridge and boiled eggs filled the air.

Liu Meifang stood by the stove with a wooden ladle. She turned and frowned gently when she saw him already in his plain gray jacket. "Sit down and eat while the porridge is hot. Do not rush out on an empty stomach."

"I have plenty of time, Mom," Chen Xuan smiled, pulling out a chair.

Before he picked up his spoon, a soft knock sounded at the door. Chen Jianguo pulled it open, and Chen Mingde and Wu Lanying stepped inside. Chen Mingde held a paper bag that still steamed slightly at the top.

"Look who beat the rooster out of bed," Chen Jianguo grinned. "Mingde, you just got home yesterday. Why are you running over here before six?"

"I could not sleep past five anyway," Chen Mingde replied, setting the bag on the table. "Old Zhou's corner steamer just opened its first batch of scallion buns. I bought four for Chen Xuan to eat on the road. The bus ride to Shanghai takes hours."

Wu Lanying placed a small cloth bundle beside the bag. "And I roasted pumpkin seeds last night. Keep them in your coat pocket, Chen Xuan."

Chen Xuan stood up and took the warm bag. "Second Uncle, Second Aunt, you did not need to walk over so early. You have to open Mingde Hardware in a few hours."

"Opening a shop is easy," Chen Mingde said, patting Chen Xuan firmly on the shoulder. His eyes softened. "You came all the way back from Shanghai to sit with your parents while I was locked up. If I did not see you off this morning, I would not feel right all day."

"Sit down, both of you, and have some porridge," Liu Meifang urged, grabbing two extra bowls. "Chen Xuan, eat your eggs first. I filled your green thermos with fresh jasmine tea. Careful when you open it, the water just boiled."

The five of them sat around the wooden table for twenty minutes. Nobody mentioned Hongrui Real Estate. Chen Jianguo asked Chen Mingde which supplier gave fair prices on steel hinges, while Liu Meifang reminded Chen Xuan twice to put the jars of pickled chilies in the fridge as soon as he reached his apartment.

At six-fifteen, Chen Xuan finished his porridge, slung his dark blue canvas travel bag over one shoulder, and picked up his scratched green thermos and the bag of scallion buns.

"Let me walk you to the bus station," Chen Jianguo offered.

"No need, Dad," Chen Xuan said easily. "It is only a ten-minute walk, and the morning wind is cold. Stay home and help Second Uncle get his shelves ready."

Liu Meifang followed him to the door and smoothed his collar. "Call me tonight after dinner. Do not stay up late staring at your phone."

"I will, Mom," Chen Xuan promised. He nodded warmly to Chen Mingde and Wu Lanying. "Take care of yourselves. I will come back on the next holiday."

"Have a safe trip, Chen Xuan," Chen Mingde called out as he headed down the stairs.

Outside Fuxing New Village, the sky was turning pale gray. Chen Xuan walked two quiet blocks, turned past a closed fruit stand, and stopped beside a plain dark sedan parked under a willow tree.

Number 7 stepped out of the front seat, opened the rear door, and took the heavy canvas bag without a word.

"Morning, Number 7," Chen Xuan said, climbing into the back seat.

"Good morning," Number 7 replied quietly. He set the travel bag upright on the floor so the glass jars inside would not knock together, closed the door, and got back in front.

Twenty minutes later, the sedan passed through a quiet side gate of a small private airfield outside Yunxi County. A silver business jet sat waiting on the empty tarmac, its engines humming softly.

Chen Xuan stepped out with his green thermos and his paper bag of buns and walked up the boarding stairs. Number 7 followed right behind with the travel bag. Two minutes later, the cabin door closed and the jet rolled onto the runway.

Once the plane leveled out above the thin morning clouds, Chen Xuan unscrewed his green thermos, poured a cup of hot jasmine tea, and took a slow sip.

Number 7 sat across the narrow table with a thin folder on his knees.

"The flight to Shanghai takes forty-two minutes," Number 7 said calmly. "A plain car is waiting at the side exit of Shanghai Hongqiao Airport. You will reach your apartment by seven-forty, drop off your extra jars, and still walk into Hengxin Logistics before nine."

"Good," Chen Xuan said, breaking a warm scallion bun in half. "How is the clean-up going across the provinces?"

"Moving faster than expected," Number 7 answered. "Deng Yongchang gave up a full list of names last night without holding anything back. Three provincial departments started checking their books before sunrise. In Shanghai, the anti-smuggling team moved at four this morning. Port Customs Director Xu Wenbin was taken in from his house an hour ago."

Chen Xuan chewed slowly and nodded. "Keep everything moving through the proper offices. Do not let our people step in front of the cameras."

"Understood," Number 7 said. He paused for a second and glanced toward the oval window. "There is one more thing."

"Go ahead," Chen Xuan said, pouring a little more tea.

"The Black Guard team is still following you," Number 7 reported evenly. "Before takeoff, I spotted two of them standing outside the east fence of the airfield, fifty meters from the tarmac. They did not cross the gate or interfere with our crew. My people in Shanghai also saw two more in plain dark jackets waiting near the outer road of Shanghai Hongqiao Airport."

Chen Xuan held his warm cup and smiled faintly. "Always within fifty meters."

"Yes," Number 7 said. "They do not answer our radio calls, they do not check in with Number 1, and they never leave a trace on cameras. Should I have our perimeter team ask them to pull back?"

Chen Xuan shook his head gently and took another sip of tea. "Leave them be. They stood outside Fuxing New Village all weekend in the cold wind without waking a single neighbor. If they want to follow me around, let them."

Number 7 gave a small nod. "Very well. I will tell our drivers not to bother them."

"Good," Chen Xuan said, leaning back comfortably and picking up the second half of his uncle's scallion bun. "Let me finish breakfast. I have a regular workday waiting on the twelfth floor."
