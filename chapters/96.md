# Chapter 96: Back to the Lazy Life

At seven-thirty on Tuesday morning, a pearl-white private jet cruised quietly through the soft morning clouds toward Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport.

Inside the warm cabin, Chen Xuan sat back in a wide leather chair with a worn dark blue canvas bag resting near his sneakers. On the low wooden table in front of him sat his scratched green thermos, its metal cap unscrewed to let the clean scent of hot jasmine tea drift into the air.

Across the aisle, Number 7 sat upright in a crisp white suit, holding a slim white tablet in both hands.

"Sir," Number 7 reported in a quiet, respectful voice, "Number 1 sent the morning summary from Beijing and Handong Province ten minutes ago. In Jingzhou City and Yunxi County, Secretary Sha Ruijin and Director Hou Liangping have taken full control of the provincial review. Chief Prosecutor Ji Changming has frozen the remaining shell accounts tied to Shanshui Group and Shanshui Villa. Gao Yuliang, Qi Tongwei, and Zhao Ruilong are all under strict guard, and Secretary Li Dakang has already begun clearing out the bad loans across the city banks."

Chen Xuan lifted his green thermos, blew gently across the floating tea leaves, and took a slow, warm sip. He let out a long, lazy yawn and rubbed the back of his neck.

"Good," Chen Xuan said easily. "Premier Gu Chengshan and Elder Song have plenty of capable people in Beijing, and Sha Ruijin and Hou Liangping know how to clean a house without breaking the foundation. Let Number 1 keep an eye on the big accounts from afar. As long as Second Uncle Chen Mingde can open his hardware shop on Old Street in peace, I do not want to hear another word about provincial committees or bank audits."

Number 7 bowed his head slightly, a faint smile touching the corner of his mouth. "Understood, Sir. The four national banks, China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China, have already returned to normal service under Number 1's quiet oversight. What are your plans once we land in Shanghai?"

"My plans?" Chen Xuan leaned back against the soft headrest and closed his eyes with a contented sigh. "My plan is to swipe my attendance card at Hengxin Logistics before nine o'clock, drink three thermoses of hot tea, eat my mother's pickled chilies with white rice at noon, and walk out the front door at four fifty-five on the dot. Anything that requires more than five minutes of thinking is officially none of my business."

"A very wise plan, Sir," Number 7 replied smoothly.

Twenty minutes later, the pearl-white jet touched down gently on the private runway at Shanghai Hongqiao.

Chen Xuan declined the pearl-white sedan waiting by the hangar. Instead, he walked out through a quiet side gate carrying his canvas bag and his green thermos, blending right into the morning crowd heading toward the subway entrance. Less than fifty meters behind him, two men in plain black jackets walked at an unhurried pace, keeping their quiet watch without drawing a single glance.

Just as Chen Xuan stepped onto the platform, the ordinary phone in his pocket began to buzz.

He pulled it out, saw the caller name on the screen, and smiled as he pressed answer.

"Morning, Mom," Chen Xuan said cheerfully. "I just got off the express bus in Shanghai. The jars of pickled chilies did not leak a single drop."

"Forget the pickled chilies for a second!" Liu Meifang's brisk voice rang loudly through the receiver. "Chen Xuan, listen to me carefully. Do you remember Auntie Wang from the third floor of our old building in Yunxi County?"

"Auntie Wang who always tries to feed everyone dried sweet potatoes?" Chen Xuan asked, shifting his green thermos to his other hand. "What about her?"

"Her niece works in Shanghai, and her niece's manager has a distant cousin whose friend knows a very nice girl working in Lujiazui!" Liu Meifang said in one breath, her voice full of excitement. "The girl is twenty-six, works in a white-collar office near Jin Mao Tower, and has a steady job. Auntie Wang already gave her your phone number and booked a table for two at twelve-fifteen today at a quiet coffee shop right below Jin Mao Tower."

Chen Xuan blinked slowly. "Mom, it is Tuesday. I just got back to Shanghai ten minutes ago, and I have to be at Hengxin Logistics by nine. How did I suddenly get signed up for a noon blind date?"

"Because you are twenty-seven and your apartment has nothing in it except a green thermos and three pairs of work shoes!" Liu Meifang scolded warmly. "Every time I ask you about finding a wife, you tell me you are too busy resting. What kind of young man is too busy resting to get married?"

"Resting takes real effort, Mom," Chen Xuan teased gently. "If you do not take a proper nap after lunch, the whole afternoon feels twice as long."

"Do not give me your lazy excuses!" Liu Meifang warned, though anyone could hear the fondness in her voice. "The coffee shop is called Cloud Corner, right across the street from Jin Mao Tower and five minutes from the Oriental Pearl Tower. Your office is right there in Lujiazui anyway. She will be sitting at Table Six by the window wearing a cream-colored blazer."

"Mom, what if we have nothing to talk about?" Chen Xuan laughed. "I only make twelve thousand yuan a month as a logistics clerk. Most girls working around Lujiazui want a guy who talks about stock markets and big houses."

"Then you sit there nicely, buy her a cup of coffee, and talk about the weather for half an hour!" Liu Meifang ordered firmly. "Auntie Wang promised me the girl is polite and sensible. If you dare skip this date to sleep on your office desk at noon, I am going to call your father and have him lecture you for two hours tonight. Do you hear me, Chen Xuan?"

"I hear you, Mom. Table Six at Cloud Corner, twelve-fifteen sharp. I promise I will go," Chen Xuan chuckled.

After hanging up, Chen Xuan looked down at his scratched green thermos and shook his head with an easy grin.

"Well," he murmured to himself as the subway doors slid open, "at least the coffee shop is close to the office. Maybe they will let me refill my hot water for free."
