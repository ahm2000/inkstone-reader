# Chapter 146: Hengxin's Promotion Offer

At 8:55 on Monday morning, the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics in Shanghai was buzzing with phone calls and clerks carrying port folders. Ever since the Shanghai Port Forum ended with Hengxin Logistics standing tall beside Shanghai's biggest trade names, new clients had been calling nonstop.

Chen Xuan hung his gray jacket on his chair at desk fourteen, set his scratched green thermos beside his mouse pad, and unscrewed the lid to let the jasmine steam rise.

Two desks over, Zheng Qian spun his chair around eagerly.

"Chen Xuan, did you see the group chat?" Zheng Qian asked, eyes wide. "Three importers called at eight sharp asking for Hengxin Logistics by name! Everyone says the Shanghai Port Forum made us the hottest office on Binjiang Avenue."

"I saw the notice downstairs," Chen Xuan said calmly, opening his spreadsheet. "More containers mean a better bonus for all of us."

Pan Xiulan leaned over the partition with a warm smile, holding customs receipts. "Brother Chen, you are too modest. General Manager Sun Haoran has been grinning since eight o'clock. Half the fifteenth floor is talking about how you answered every port question at the forum without looking at a notebook."

"I only read the numbers off the sheet," Chen Xuan replied mildly. "The dock crew and our clerks did the counting."

"Tell that to my wife," Zheng Qian laughed. "If our bonus goes up, I am treating our row to roasted duck on Friday!"

"Hold onto your wallet until Friday, Zheng Qian," Pan Xiulan teased gently. "You still have four days of dock sheets to finish."

Just then, the elevator hall doors swung open.

General Manager Sun Haoran strode onto the twelfth floor in a dark suit, his face shining with pride. Right behind him walked Department Manager Hu Dewei, clutching a notebook with an uneasy look.

Every keyboard fell silent as thirty clerks straightened in their seats.

Sun Haoran walked down the aisle and stopped at desk fourteen.

"Good morning, everyone," Sun Haoran called out cheerfully. "Keep your seats. I came down today for one announcement."

He placed a hand on Chen Xuan's desk.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran said warmly. "At the Shanghai Port Forum, you saved our company's face and brought Hengxin Logistics more respect in three days than our sales team earned in three years. Even President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group and the port commissioners praised our operations desk."

Beside him, Hu Dewei's jaw tightened, a flush creeping up his cheeks.

"I do not forget hard work," Sun Haoran continued loudly so every desk could hear. "I spoke with the board over the weekend, and we agreed. Starting today, I am promoting you to Deputy General Manager of Hengxin Logistics."

A gasp rolled across the twelfth floor.

Zheng Qian dropped his pen onto his keyboard. Pan Xiulan covered her mouth, her eyes shining with joy. Hu Dewei's head snapped up and his face turned pale green. Deputy General Manager meant jumping over Hu Dewei's head onto the fifteenth floor.

"The pay is 50,000 yuan a month before bonuses," Sun Haoran added proudly. "You get a corner office on the fifteenth floor, a car allowance, and full authority over operations and port teams, reporting to me. What do you say?"

The floor held its breath. Earning 50,000 yuan a month was more than four times Chen Xuan's pay.

Chen Xuan picked up his green thermos, took a slow sip of jasmine tea, set it down, and looked up at Sun Haoran with an easy smile.

"Thank you for thinking of me, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said calmly. "I appreciate your kindness, but I have to turn it down."

For three seconds, nobody on the twelfth floor moved.

Sun Haoran blinked twice, his smile freezing. "Turn it down? Chen Xuan, did you hear me? I said Deputy General Manager at 50,000 yuan a month."

"I heard you clearly, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan replied gently. "Deputy managers work weekends and go to late banquets. I like my 12,000 yuan desk right here."

Zheng Qian choked and coughed into his sleeve. Pan Xiulan stared in disbelief, while Hu Dewei's mouth fell open in shock.

Sun Haoran let out a short laugh and leaned closer. "Chen Xuan, no need to be shy. If 50,000 yuan feels low after the port forum, come upstairs and we can add a year end share."

"It is not about the money," Chen Xuan said honestly, patting his green thermos. "An executive upstairs answers Sunday calls, sits in long meetings, and drinks wine with clients at night. My desk here gets afternoon sunlight. I finish my spreadsheets before lunch, drink my tea, and clock out at 4:55 every afternoon to buy groceries and cook dinner for my wife. If I take that promotion, my wife will eat takeout, and neither of us wants that."

Sun Haoran searched Chen Xuan's calm face, finding only contentment.

"You really want to stay a junior supervisor at 12,000 yuan a month just to leave at 4:55 and cook dinner for your wife?" Sun Haoran asked, half amused and half baffled.

"Yes, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan nodded happily. "My wife works hard running her office all day. If I am not home by six to put hot soup on the table, she skips dinner and drinks coffee instead. Keeping her well fed is more important to me than a corner office."

Sun Haoran stared at him, then slapped his thigh and laughed heartily. "Well! In twenty years of running companies, you are the first man I have met who turned down an executive title for desk fourteen and his wife's supper pot!"

He turned a sharp gaze toward Hu Dewei. "The offer stays open, Chen Xuan. And Manager Hu."

Hu Dewei jumped. "Yes, General Manager Sun!"

"Since Chen Xuan prefers desk fourteen, make sure his desk stays peaceful," Sun Haoran ordered firmly. "No pointless meetings, no weekend calls, and no dumping five o'clock paperwork on his row. If he wants to walk out at 4:55 to cook for his wife, nobody had better block his way. Clear?"

"Crystal clear, General Manager Sun!" Hu Dewei bobbed his head. "Nobody will delay him!"

Once Sun Haoran and Hu Dewei took the elevator upstairs, the floor burst into whispers.

Zheng Qian rolled his chair over. "Brother Chen! Are you running a fever? That was 50,000 yuan a month! You could hire three chefs to cook for your wife!"

"Hired chefs use too much oil, and my wife likes the way I braise ribs," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, opening the dock schedule. "Besides, Zheng Qian, look at line nine on your Tuesday sheet. You typed forty tons instead of four tons. Fix it before noon so you do not stay late."

Pan Xiulan laughed warmly as Zheng Qian scrambled back to his keyboard, while Chen Xuan sipped his tea in the sunlight of desk fourteen.
