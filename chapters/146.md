# Chapter 146: Hengxin's Promotion Offer

Phones rang almost nonstop across the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics, and clerks hurried between desks with thick shipping folders tucked under their arms. Tall glass windows overlooked Binjiang Avenue, where morning traffic crawled past the bustling office tower.

Ever since the Shanghai Port Forum ended with the company standing comfortably beside the city's largest trade names, new clients had been lining up at the front desk before the doors even opened.

Chen Xuan hung his gray jacket on the back of his chair at desk fourteen. He set his dented fifteen-yuan green metal thermos beside his mouse pad, unscrewed the lid, and watched a thin ribbon of jasmine steam curl into the cool morning air.

Two desks over, Zheng Qian spun his chair around with bright eyes.

"Chen Xuan, did you see the company group chat?" Zheng Qian asked, lowering his voice with excitement. "Three major importers called at 8:00 AM sharp asking for Hengxin Logistics by name. Everyone downstairs says the Shanghai Port Forum turned us into the hottest shipping office on the whole avenue."

"I saw the welcome screen in the lobby," Chen Xuan said calmly, opening his port schedule spreadsheet. "More containers mean the warehouse crew will stay busy through November."

Across the aisle, Pan Xiulan laughed as she sorted customs forms. "Listen to him, Zheng Qian. The whole floor is buzzing about year-end bonuses, and Chen Xuan only cares whether his tea is steep enough."

"Hot tea keeps the mind steady," Chen Xuan replied with a mild smile. "A steady mind makes fewer typos."

He typed quietly for the next hour, clearing his morning freight logs well ahead of everyone else.

Shortly before midmorning, General Manager Sun Haoran's assistant walked over to desk fourteen.

"Chen Xuan, General Manager Sun would like to see you in his office," she said politely.

Zheng Qian immediately gave Chen Xuan an excited thumbs-up. "I knew it," he whispered. "You are getting promoted."

Chen Xuan capped his green thermos and strolled toward the glass corner office.

Inside, General Manager Sun Haoran stood beside a wide window overlooking the Huangpu River. A blue folder sat open on his desk beside two porcelain teacups. When Chen Xuan knocked and stepped inside, Sun Haoran turned with a broad smile.

"Come in, Chen Xuan, sit down," Sun Haoran said warmly, gesturing to the chair across from him. "I poured fresh Longjing tea."

"Thank you, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said, sitting down comfortably.

Sun Haoran tapped the blue folder with pride. "Our freight volume has nearly tripled since the forum. Several major partners praised how smooth and error-free our twelfth-floor operations desk has been. I reviewed three years of attendance and error logs over the weekend, and your name sits at the very top."

"I just check the numbers twice before hitting send," Chen Xuan said modestly.

"Do not sell yourself short," Sun Haoran laughed, sliding a crisp printed contract across the desk. "The board approved a new senior role this morning. I want you as Deputy General Manager of Operations. Your base salary will jump from 12,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan a month, plus quarterly bonuses, a private office, and a car allowance."

For most young clerks in Shanghai, jumping from 12,000 yuan to 50,000 yuan a month in a single morning was the break people chased for a decade.

Chen Xuan glanced at the contract briefly, then gently slid it back across the desk.

"Thank you for trusting me, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said politely. "I appreciate the offer, but I have to decline."

Sun Haoran blinked, his smile freezing. "Wait a moment. Did I misread the numbers? That is 50,000 yuan a month before bonuses, Chen Xuan. More than four times what you earn now."

"I heard you clearly," Chen Xuan replied with an easy smile. "It is a generous package. Still, Deputy General Manager means strategy meetings every afternoon, client dinners three nights a week, and urgent phone calls on Sundays."

"That is simply what leadership takes," Sun Haoran said earnestly. "With your calm temper, you could run this whole branch in five years."

"I like my current desk much better," Chen Xuan said honestly. "My 12,000 yuan salary covers groceries and bills just fine. I finish every spreadsheet cleanly, nobody calls me after work, and I clock out at 4:55 PM sharp. That gives me time to stop by the fresh market and cook dinner before my wife gets home."

Sun Haoran stared at him in genuine disbelief. In twenty years of Shanghai logistics, he had never watched someone turn down 50,000 yuan a month to buy fresh vegetables on time.

"Are you sure?" Sun Haoran asked softly. "Take the contract home and ask your wife tonight."

"I am sure," Chen Xuan said gently. "If you need someone eager and reliable to help manage the new accounts, Zheng Qian and Pan Xiulan have both put in extra hours all autumn. Either of them would treasure the chance."

Sun Haoran exhaled a long breath and shook his head with a wry laugh. "You really are one of a kind, Chen Xuan. All right. Desk fourteen is yours for as long as you want it."

"Thank you, General Manager Sun."

When Chen Xuan returned to his seat, Zheng Qian leaned over eagerly. "Well? Are we calling you Deputy Manager Chen now?"

"I turned it down," Chen Xuan said, unscrewing his green thermos for a sip of jasmine tea. "Too many late meetings. I prefer leaving at 4:55 PM."

Zheng Qian stared with his mouth open before bursting into a helpless laugh. "You are unbelievable, Chen Xuan."

Beside Chen Xuan's mouse pad, his single smartphone lit up briefly with a short message from Number 1.

"Young Master, the autumn shipping schedules for Haiyue Group cleared customs ahead of time. Everything across Shanghai remains quiet."

Chen Xuan typed two words in reply: "Well done."

He locked his phone, placed it face down beside his thermos, and returned peacefully to his spreadsheet.
