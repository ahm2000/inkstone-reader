# Chapter 146: Hengxin's Promotion Offer

At 8:55 on Monday morning, the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics was buzzing with phone calls and busy clerks carrying thick port folders. Ever since the Shanghai Port Forum ended with Hengxin Logistics standing tall beside Shanghai's biggest trade names, new clients had been calling nonstop.

Chen Xuan hung his clean gray jacket on his chair at desk fourteen, set his scratched green thermos beside his mouse pad, and unscrewed the lid to let the jasmine steam rise.

Two desks over, Zheng Qian spun his chair around eagerly.

"Chen Xuan, did you see the company group chat?" Zheng Qian asked, eyes wide. "Three cold chain importers from the south called asking for Hengxin by name! Everyone says the Shanghai Port Forum made us the hottest logistics firm in Shanghai."

"I saw the notice downstairs," Chen Xuan said calmly, opening his spreadsheet. "It is good news for the year end bonus."

Pan Xiulan leaned over the partition with a warm smile, holding customs receipts. "Brother Chen, you are too modest. General Manager Sun Haoran has been grinning since eight o'clock. Half the fifteenth floor is talking about how you handled the port figures at the forum without breaking a sweat."

"I only read the numbers off the sheet," Chen Xuan replied mildly. "The dock team did the heavy lifting."

Just then, the elevator hall glass doors swung open.

General Manager Sun Haoran strode onto the twelfth floor in a dark suit, his face shining with pride. Right behind him walked Department Manager Hu Dewei, clutching a notebook with an uneasy look.

Every keyboard paused as clerks straightened in their seats.

Sun Haoran walked straight down the center aisle and stopped at desk fourteen.

"Good morning, everyone," Sun Haoran called out cheerfully. "Keep your seats. I came down here today for one special reason."

He placed a friendly hand on Chen Xuan's desk.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran said warmly. "At the Shanghai Port Forum, you saved this company's face and brought us more respect in three days than our sales team earned in three years. Big partners from Haiyue and the port authority asked me who runs our operations desk."

Hu Dewei's jaw tightened beside him, a sour shade creeping into his cheeks.

"I do not forget hard work," Sun Haoran went on loudly so every desk could hear. "I spoke with the board over the weekend, and we agreed. Starting today, I am promoting you to Deputy General Manager of Hengxin Logistics."

A collective gasp rolled across the twelfth floor.

Zheng Qian dropped his pen onto his desk. Pan Xiulan covered her mouth, her eyes shining with joy. Hu Dewei's head snapped up and his face turned green. Deputy General Manager meant jumping clean over his head onto the fifteenth floor.

"The pay is 50,000 yuan a month before bonuses," Sun Haoran added proudly. "You get a private corner office on the fifteenth floor, a car allowance, and full say over operations and port teams, reporting straight to me. What do you say, Chen Xuan?"

The entire floor held its breath. Earning 50,000 yuan a month was more than four times a junior supervisor's pay. Hu Dewei gripped his notebook until his knuckles turned white, forcing a stiff smile while wondering how he would survive under Chen Xuan.

Chen Xuan took a slow sip of jasmine tea from his green thermos, set it down, and looked up at Sun Haoran with an easy smile.

"Thank you for thinking of me, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said calmly. "I have to turn it down."

For three seconds, nobody on the twelfth floor moved.

Sun Haoran blinked twice, his smile freezing. "Turn it down? Did you hear me right? I said Deputy General Manager and 50,000 yuan a month."

"I heard you clearly," Chen Xuan replied gently. "Deputy managers have to work weekends, General Manager Sun. I like my 12,000 yuan desk."

Zheng Qian choked and coughed loudly. Pan Xiulan stared in disbelief, while Hu Dewei's mouth fell open in blank shock.

Sun Haoran gave a short laugh and leaned closer. "Chen Xuan, no need to be shy. If 50,000 yuan is low after the forum, we can talk in my office and add a year end share."

"It is not about the money," Chen Xuan said politely. "A deputy manager has to attend evening dinners, answer Sunday calls, and sit through long meetings. My desk here gets nice afternoon sunlight. I finish my spreadsheets before lunch, drink my tea, and leave at 4:55. That suits me much better."

Sun Haoran searched his face for a joke, finding only calm contentment.

"You really prefer staying a junior operations supervisor at 12,000 yuan over a private office upstairs?" Sun Haoran asked, half amused and half baffled.

"Yes, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan nodded. "I am an ordinary worker. I like quiet days."

A murmur rippled through the desks. Who in Shanghai turned down 50,000 yuan a month just to keep their weekends? Hu Dewei closed his mouth, torn between relief and unease.

Sun Haoran slapped his knee and laughed genuinely. "Well, in twenty years of running companies, you are the first man to turn down a corner office for the sunlight at desk fourteen."

He turned a sharp look toward Hu Dewei. "The offer stays open, Chen Xuan. And Manager Hu."

Hu Dewei jumped. "Yes, General Manager Sun!"

"Since Chen Xuan wants to stay at desk fourteen, make sure his desk stays quiet," Sun Haoran ordered firmly. "No pointless overtime and no dumping sloppy mistakes on him at five o'clock. Clear?"

"Crystal clear, General Manager Sun!" Hu Dewei bobbed his head. "Nobody will bother him."

After Sun Haoran and Hu Dewei left in the elevator, the floor burst into noisy whispers.

Zheng Qian rolled his chair over. "Brother Chen! Are you feverish? That was 50,000 yuan a month!"

"Chen Xuan, are you sure?" Pan Xiulan asked in amazement. "You could have tried it for half a year and saved a fortune."

"If I sat upstairs, I would miss the five o'clock bus and my tea would get cold in meetings," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, opening the port schedule. "Besides, Zheng Qian, your Tuesday dock sheet has a weight mismatch on line nine. Fix it before noon so you do not stay late."

Zheng Qian groaned at his screen while Chen Xuan sipped his tea in the quiet warmth of desk fourteen.
