# Chapter 157: Number 1's Southern Dossier

Half an hour later, the upstairs hallway of Emerald Riverside Villa fell completely quiet. Qin Yuhan had taken a warm shower and drifted into a deep sleep in the master bedroom, her breathing even and peaceful after the mug of ginger tea.

Chen Xuan pulled a dark wool jacket over his cotton shirt, stepped quietly through the sliding door onto the second-floor terrace, and closed the glass behind him. The late November breeze off the river was sharp and clean. Across the water, the distant skyline of Pudong shimmered in quiet rows of gold and white.

The single everyday smartphone in his jacket pocket vibrated twice. Chen Xuan pulled it out, glanced at the glowing screen that still held a reminder from Qin Yuhan to buy fresh scallions on Friday, and swiped to accept an encrypted voice line.

"Speak," Chen Xuan said softly.

"Young Master," Number 1's steady, respectful voice came through the speaker. "Routine maritime monitoring flagged an irregular cargo detention in Guangdong two hours ago. Madam's chartered vessel, the Haiyue Star, has been blocked inside the bonded yard at Shenzhen Pier Nine."

"I know," Chen Xuan replied, resting his forearms lightly on the stone railing. "Yuhan just got the call. Tell me what you found on the Shen Family."

"We compiled a full southern dossier before calling, Young Master," Number 1 reported clearly. "The Shen Family is headed by Shen Wanli, with his nephew Shen Chao overseeing daily operations at Pier Nine. Over the past decade, they secured long-term leases on three bonded container yards and a private stevedoring company. Publicly, they present themselves as a local shipping clan. Behind the books, they run a grey customs-evasion and smuggling corridor."

Chen Xuan listened without interrupting, his gaze calm on the dark surface of the river.

"Their standard method is simple," Number 1 continued. "Whenever an outside enterprise ships high-value cargo with a tight European or Southeast Asian transfer window, Shen Chao invents a quarantine or berth compliance dispute, locks the yard gate, and demands a private fee between 5 million and 15 million yuan. Most companies pay quietly rather than suffer breach penalties."

"Is that all?" Chen Xuan asked lightly.

"No, Young Master. There is a second layer," Number 1 said. "Financial tracing shows that two shell logistics companies linked to Shen Wanli's younger brother recently moved 180 million yuan north into Handong Province. They submitted indirect subcontracting bids for a feeder port upgrade in Jingzhou and the cargo apron expansion at Luzhou Airport."

Chen Xuan raised an eyebrow slightly. "Handong again."

"Yes, Young Master. After the arrests of Gao Yuliang and Zhao Ruilong, several remaining local interests in Handong lost their usual funding channels. They appear to be pulling southern grey capital into provincial infrastructure bids to stall the new leadership's reforms."

Chen Xuan tapped the stone railing once with his fingertip. "Keep the southern dossier clean, verified, and strictly through legal channels. Do not send anyone to Pier Nine to make a scene tomorrow. I am flying to Shenzhen with Yuhan as a regular Hengxin clerk."

"Understood, Young Master," Number 1 answered immediately. "Shall I place the customs and maritime evidence on standby?"

"Hold it ready," Chen Xuan said calmly. "If the Shen Family has any sense tomorrow, they will open the gate and apologize to my wife. If they insist on acting like local kings, then the proper departments can inspect every ledger they have kept for the last ten years."

"Understood, Young Master. Everything will be ready at your word."

Chen Xuan ended the call, slipped his single phone back into his pocket, and stood for one more quiet minute in the cool river air before heading back inside.

Hundreds of kilometers away in Handong Province, the lights on the sixth floor of the Provincial Discipline Inspection Commission building were still burning bright.

Inside a plain, tidy office stacked with blue audit binders, Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping stood by the window with his phone pressed to his ear. Across the desk sat Tian Guofu, the Provincial Discipline Secretary who had been parachuted into Handong alongside Secretary Sha Ruijin to clean up the province's entrenched factions.

"Got it, Xiaoai," Hou Liangping said into the phone, jotting two company names onto a yellow notepad. "Send the customs cross-check to our encrypted mailbox before eight tomorrow morning. And get some sleep. Beijing is freezing this week."

On the other end of the line, his wife Zhong Xiaoai, Deputy Director at the Central Discipline Commission in Beijing, gave a brief, crisp reminder to watch his own health before hanging up.

Hou Liangping set the phone down and walked over to Tian Guofu's desk, sliding the notepad across the polished wood. "Secretary Tian, Xiaoai just pulled a national procurement and customs alert from Beijing. Those two stalled bids in Jingzhou and Luzhou share the exact same southern guarantee source."

Tian Guofu adjusted his glasses and read the two names carefully. "Shen Family Logistics and Nanwan Bonded Storage, both operating out of Shenzhen Pier Nine."

"Exactly," Hou Liangping said, his expression sharp. "Secretary Yi Xuexi over in Luzhou has been fighting for months to clear out old faction bottlenecks on the Luzhou Airport expansion and the Crescent Lake corridor. Just when he thought the bidding was clean, these two shell firms showed up with suspiciously low quotes and massive cash guarantees to drag the review process into the mud."

Tian Guofu tapped the desk slowly, a knowing glint in his eyes. "Gao Yuliang and Zhao Ruilong are already in custody, so the old Handong networks cannot move money openly anymore. Borrowing grey capital from a southern port clan is clever, but it leaves a wide trail."

"Should we notify Secretary Sha tonight?" Hou Liangping asked.

"First thing tomorrow morning," Tian Guofu replied decisively. "Secretary Sha has a Standing Committee meeting in the afternoon, and the Governor's camp has already been hinting that Luzhou's airport project is moving too slowly. Once we put hard evidence of Shenzhen smuggling money inside those rival bids on the table, let us see who still dares to defend them."

Hou Liangping grinned slightly, closing his folder. "I will coordinate with Guangdong customs and discipline inspection at dawn. If this Shen Family has been running dirty games at Pier Nine, their good days are about to run out."
