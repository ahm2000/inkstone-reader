# Chapter 163: Four Fifty-Nine

Shen Jingzhou frowned as he picked up his vibrating gold phone.

"What now, Old Lin?" Shen Jingzhou answered lazily, leaning back in his folding chair. "I am busy teaching a Shanghai woman some manners at Pier Nine. Make it quick."

On the other end, Lin Jianming's voice sounded like a man watching his house burn down.

"Young Master! Something terrible just happened!" Lin Jianming shouted, his breath ragged. "At four fifty sharp, our company inbox and phone lines exploded! China Construction Bank in Futian CBD just froze our 5 billion yuan port credit line for an emergency review!"

Shen Jingzhou's lazy smirk vanished.

"What kind of joke is that?" Shen Jingzhou snapped. "My grandfather is having tea with Vice President Ma from China Construction Bank at six o'clock! Call their loan manager and fix the mistake!"

"It is not a mistake, Young Master! Wait, hold on!" Lin Jianming cried out in pure panic, papers rustling wildly in the background. "ICBC just sent a notice too! They froze our 4 billion yuan crane and dock facility line! And Huaxia Bank just called my second phone! They froze our remaining 3 billion yuan operating line!"

Shen Jingzhou shot to his feet so fast that the sleek leather folding chair tipped over backward and clattered onto the concrete.

"All three banks?" Shen Jingzhou yelled, his voice cracking across the dock road. "China Construction Bank, ICBC, and Huaxia Bank? All 12 billion yuan frozen at the exact same minute?!"

"All 12 billion yuan!" Lin Jianming wailed. "Every single penny is locked! Our afternoon transfer to Yantian Port bounced two minutes ago! The crane leasing company is already calling to pull our crews! Young Master, if those lines stay frozen overnight, Shenhai Shipping will default tomorrow morning!"

Before Shen Jingzhou could even scream a reply, his phone beeped sharply with an incoming call.

The caller ID flashed: Grandfather.

Shen Jingzhou's thumb shook as he switched lines. "Grandfather! Did Old Lin tell you? What is going on with Vice President Ma?"

"Jingzhou!" Shen Wanshan's voice roared out of the speaker, so loud and frantic that even Chief Inspector Lu Guobin standing three meters away heard every word. "Vice President Ma canceled our meeting and hung up on me! The branch head at ICBC would not even let my secretary past the lobby! And Huaxia Bank just flagged our Shenzhen Bay No. 1 mortgages for review!"

The blood drained completely from Shen Jingzhou's face. "Grandfather, why? We never missed a payment!"

"Because an order came straight down from Beijing headquarters to all three national banks at once!" Shen Wanshan shouted, his voice shaking with pure terror. "A friend at the Futian banking office risked his job to whisper one sentence to me before hanging up. He asked me who my idiot grandson offended at Shenzhen Pier Nine!"

Silence slammed down on Shen Jingzhou like a falling shipping container.

His hand froze beside his ear.

Chen Xuan stood right where he had been ten minutes ago, his cheap dark-blue windbreaker fluttering slightly in the sea breeze, holding his green thermos in both hands as if nothing in the world could bother him.

"Jingzhou! Answer me!" Shen Wanshan screamed through the phone. "Who is standing at Pier Nine with Qin Yuhan?! Tell me right now!"

"Nobody!" Shen Jingzhou stammered, his throat dry as sand. "Just President Qin, her local manager, and that shipping clerk from Hengxin Logistics! The one in the cheap windbreaker who made a phone call ten minutes ago!"

"A phone call ten minutes ago?!" Shen Wanshan gasped, his voice dropping into a horrified whisper. "You fool! You blind, arrogant fool! Open the gate! Drop to your knees and open Pier Nine before five o'clock, or the Shen Family is finished tonight!"

The call clicked dead.

Shen Jingzhou stood rooted to the spot, his gold phone dangling loosely from trembling fingers.

"Young Master Shen?" Lu Guobin asked nervously, stepping closer. "Is something wrong with your grandfather's banks?"

Before Shen Jingzhou could force a word past his pale lips, the sharp screech of tires tore through the afternoon air.

A black official sedan with Shenzhen municipal plates sped down the concrete dock road, swerving hard to a halt just ten meters from the iron gate.

The rear door flew open before the car had even finished rocking on its springs.

Out jumped a stout, middle-aged man in a soaked white dress shirt, his dark tie yanked loose around his neck and his forehead dripping with sweat.

Chief Inspector Lu Guobin's eyes nearly popped out of his head.

"Director Gao!" Lu Guobin gasped, scrambling forward with a forced smile. "Director Gao Zhenhua! Why did you come all the way down to Pier Nine in person? I was just finishing up a routine safety check on the Haiyue Star."

"Shut your mouth!" Director Gao Zhenhua roared, skidding to a halt in front of Lu Guobin, gasping for air with his chest heaving.

With one furious sweep of his arm, Gao Zhenhua knocked the stamped inspection clipboard straight out of Lu Guobin's hands.

"A routine check?!" Gao Zhenhua barked, his face red with fury and fear as sweat poured down his temples. "Do you have any idea what kind of phone call just hit the Shenzhen Port Authority office?! The municipal deputy head called me personally and told me that if Pier Nine is not open by five o'clock, I can pack my desk before dinner!"

Lu Guobin's knees buckled slightly. "Director Gao, please listen. Young Master Shen asked me to hold the gate."

"You are suspended on the spot!" Gao Zhenhua shouted, spinning toward the two shocked dock guards standing beside the gatehouse. "What are you idiots staring at?! Check the clock! It is four fifty-eight! Get the keys! Unlock that chain and roll this gate wide open right this second!"

"Yes, Director! Right away!"

One of the dock guards scrambled forward with a jingling ring of keys, his hands shaking so badly he fumbled the brass padlock twice before snapping it open.

With a loud metallic groan, the heavy iron gate of Shenzhen Pier Nine began to roll back smoothly along its steel track.

Above the dock, the silent red port cranes suddenly hummed to life, their warning bells ringing brightly in the salty afternoon air.

On the water, the massive horn of the Haiyue Star let out a long, deep blast of triumph that echoed all the way across Shenzhen Bay.

Director Gao Zhenhua pulled a handkerchief from his pocket and mopped his dripping face, looking at his watch in breathless relief.

Four fifty-nine.

He had made it with sixty seconds to spare.

Behind Qin Yuhan, Manager Zhou Ming let out a shaky, disbelieving laugh, staring at the wide-open gate and the moving cranes as if he had just woken up from a wild dream.

Beside the black luxury sedan, Shen Jingzhou stood frozen in place, his face as white as chalk. His gold phone slipped from his numb fingers and hit the concrete with a sharp crack.
