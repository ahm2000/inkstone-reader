# Chapter 163: 4:59 PM

The late autumn sun dipped lower over the western horizon, turning the harbor waters of Shenzhen into sheets of molten copper. A chilly breeze swept across Pier Nine, rattling the iron chains wrapped around the locked gates.

At 4:48 PM, Shen Jingzhou pushed himself off the hood of his Mercedes and tossed his empty coffee cup to an assistant. He smoothed his charcoal suit, walked up to Qin Yuhan, and clicked open a heavy gold fountain pen.

"Twelve minutes left, President Qin," Shen Jingzhou said with smug triumph. "Your manager's phone stopped ringing because everyone in Shenzhen knows better than to cross my family. Sign the transfer agreement now, take your 1 million, and save Haiyue Group from an 80 million penalty."

Lin Jian clenched his fists, pale with frustration. Qin Yuhan looked straight into Shen Jingzhou's eyes without blinking.

"Put your pen away, Shen Jingzhou," she said evenly. "Haiyue Group would rather pay 80 million and fight you in court for five years than hand our shipping lane to a thug."

"Suit yourself," Shen Jingzhou shrugged, irritation flickering in his eyes. "In twelve minutes, your vessel loses its berth, your clients cancel, and you can explain to Shanghai why you threw away a year's profit just to act proud."

Beside Qin Yuhan, Chen Xuan shifted her beige handbag comfortably to his left arm and looked toward the port access road. "The evening rush around MixC Mall starts right after 5:00 PM," he remarked mildly. "If we leave on time, we can still grab a window table."

Shen Jingzhou stared at him in disbelief and let out a sharp laugh. "Is your husband always this delusional, President Qin? We are standing at a locked port gate, and he is planning his seating chart!"

Before Qin Yuhan could answer, the phone in Shen Jingzhou's pocket rang urgently.

Seeing the caller ID, his smug grin returned. "Look at that. Deputy Commissioner Wu of the Shenzhen Port Authority. Probably calling to make sure your cargo stays locked up tight."

He tapped answer and put the phone to his ear. "Uncle Wu, good afternoon. Everything at Pier Nine is going smoothly, just like we discussed."

He froze. Even from two paces away, the shrill, terrified shouting pouring out of the earpiece cut sharply through the quiet air.

"Smoothly? Are you out of your mind, Shen Jingzhou!" Deputy Commissioner Wu's voice cracked with panic. "An emergency directive just came straight from the Maritime Safety Administration in Beijing! A federal inspection team flagged Shen Maritime Holdings for illegal berth obstruction and falsified compliance holds! They are revoking your Pier Nine operating license within the hour! Open those gates right now!"

Shen Jingzhou's smile vanished. "Uncle Wu, wait, what are you talking about? That hold was signed by your own local office this morning!"

"The clerk who signed it was suspended five minutes ago!" Wu roared before hanging up.

The clock on the port tower ticked to 4:53 PM. Shen Jingzhou lowered the phone slowly, his brow furrowing. Before he could process what had happened, his chief legal advisor's phone rang, followed instantly by Shen Jingzhou's own phone vibrating furiously in his palm.

"Mr. Shen!" the legal advisor gasped, turning chalk-white as he listened. "It is Shenzhen Customs! A joint federal customs audit team just overrode the local hold on Haiyue Group's 40 containers! They issued priority clearance for the Haiyue Star and placed Pier Nine under direct supervision!"

"What?" Shen Jingzhou snapped, answering his own ringing phone. "Who is this?"

"Mr. Shen, this is Vice President Zheng from Construction Bank's Shenzhen branch," a cold, clipped voice answered. "We just received a formal regulatory risk alert regarding Shen Maritime Holdings' port licenses. Effective immediately, your family's 1.1 billion credit facility is frozen pending full collateral review."

"Wait! Vice President Zheng, listen to me!" Shen Jingzhou stammered, his voice cracking. "There is a mistake!"

The line went dead. At 4:55 PM, another call flashed across his screen from Huaxia Bank. Seconds later, an urgent alert popped up from ICBC. Within three minutes, all three major state banks had slammed the brakes on 2.4 billion in revolving credit lines tied to the Shen Family's shipping empire.

Sweat broke out across Shen Jingzhou's forehead. His arrogant posture collapsed completely. Around him, his lawyers stared at their buzzing phones in silent horror, while Lin Jian watched with his jaw hanging open.

Qin Yuhan's eyes widened slightly. She glanced at Shen Jingzhou's trembling hands, then slowly turned to look at Chen Xuan. Chen Xuan simply stood beside her, calmly capping his green metal thermos as if watching a brief afternoon shower pass by.

At 4:57 PM, Shen Jingzhou's phone rang one final time. Seeing the name on the screen drained every trace of color from his face. It was his father, Shen Wanchang, head of the Shen Family.

Shen Jingzhou's thumb shook so badly he accidentally hit the speaker button.

"Jingzhou, you useless fool!" Shen Wanchang's terrified roar blasted across the concrete apron, echoing off the containers. "Who in heaven's name did you provoke at Pier Nine? The Maritime Administration, General Customs, and three national banks just descended on our headquarters at the same minute! Our entire 2.4 billion credit line is locked! They told me if Haiyue Group's cargo is delayed by one single minute past 5:00 PM, the Shen Family is finished in Shenzhen!"

Shen Jingzhou stumbled backward against his Mercedes, his gold fountain pen slipping from his numb fingers and clattering onto the concrete.

"Dad, I did not know!" Shen Jingzhou choked out, his eyes darting wildly between Qin Yuhan and the quiet man holding her handbag. "I only locked Haiyue's containers! They are just a company from Shanghai!"

"I do not care who they are!" Shen Wanchang shrieked. "Open that gate before 5:00 PM! Cut the chains with your bare teeth if you have to! Apologize to President Qin and pray she lets this go!"

The call cut off.

The digital display above the port security booth clicked to 4:59 PM.

Shen Jingzhou stood frozen for half a second, his collar soaked with sweat, his chest heaving. Then he whipped around toward his stunned security guards and screamed at the top of his lungs.

"Move! Move those SUVs right now! Unlock the gates! Open Pier Nine before 5:00 PM!"
