# Chapter 4: Watching and Waiting

In a quiet walled courtyard on the east side of Qingyan City, a phone buzzed.

"Chief, this is Nine."

The low, steady voice came from a small coded speaker on a dark wooden desk. Beside it sat a white-haired elder with a soldier's straight back, and a cup of tea went cold by his elbow.

His name was An Zhiyuan, and everyone in the hidden protection force called him Chief An. That force was the Detail, a hidden team of numbered officers, and its members called Chen Xuan "the Boss."

His serial number was Two, which meant that in the whole elite network guarding Chen Xuan from the shadows, only one person ranked above him.

"Go ahead, Nine."

"The Boss is on the ten past six express bus to Yunxi County," Nine reported. He was the plain man in the gray jacket, sitting three rows behind Chen Xuan. "At 5:52 p.m., his mother sent him a voice message. His second uncle, Chen Mingde, was taken from his hardware shop in handcuffs at five past two. The Boss listened twice and said one word. 'Hmm.'"

"Only 'hmm'?" Chief An raised an eyebrow.

"Yes, Chief. Then he texted his mother to eat dinner first. He is completely calm. He has sent no signal. He ate a red-bean bun."

Silence.

Chief An looked down at his tea, and his eyes softened with warm pride and helpless worry at the same time.

To put it simply, nobody at Hengxin Logistics knew who Chen Xuan really was, and neither did his own parents.

They thought he was an easygoing operations supervisor riding a county bus home for soup.

They did not know that a quiet team of veteran security officers, analysts and lawyers watched over every city he lived in.

And they did not know that one real emergency request from him could start a top-level review across the whole province.

But even this force lived by strict rules, and it never broke the law or skipped a proper step without real cause.

Now his uncle had been dragged out of his shop in front of a whole street, and what had the Boss done? He had told his mother to eat and chewed a red-bean bun!

Did he not know that one word from him could put investigators on the road to Yunxi before his bus even stopped?

Of course he knew!

He simply refused to pull strings over what might still be an ordinary problem. That was Chen Xuan's iron rule: handle things the normal, lawful way first, like any citizen, and step in only when someone truly crosses the line.

That was why an old veteran like Chief An respected him down to his bones, and also why Chief An kept losing sleep!

"Nine, here are my orders," Chief An said crisply. "Stay on the bus and keep out of his sight. Then contact Number Twenty-three in Yunxi, Gong Yi's unit. Tell them to keep quiet eyes on the family apartment and the Old Street shop tonight."

Wherever Chen Xuan's family lived, Chief An kept trusted people blended into the neighborhood, just in case.

"And hear me clearly," he added firmly. "Nobody touches the police, nobody calls any official and nobody pulls any internal file. We do not lift a finger unless the Boss gives the signal, and until then we read public records only. Is that clear?"

"Clear, Chief."

"One more thing."

Chief An tapped a thin folder that his aide had laid on the desk.

On the top page, a title was circled in red ink: Yunxi County, Old Street Rebuilding Project. A note clipped to it said that the provincial inspection team arrives next Wednesday.

An old street, a hardware shop, a sudden arrest, and provincial inspectors due in five days. All in the same week?

Was that really an accident?

Chief An had spent forty years reading men and power, and he did not believe in accidents.

"Tell Twenty-three I want the public filings on that project," he said quietly. "The approved relocation budget, the developer's loans, and which county office runs the buyouts. Put it on my desk by morning."

"Understood, Chief."

The speaker clicked off.

Somewhere down the highway, a hardware shop stood dark, and the Boss was riding a bus home for soup. Chief An hoped it was a small matter, but after forty years on the job, he was already bracing for a storm.

---

At Yunxi County Bus Station, the bus hissed to a stop, and the smell of home rushed in, fried dough and damp earth and sweet woodsmoke from the fields.

"Xuan! Over here!"

At the edge of the lot, his father, Chen Jianguo, waved both arms beside a battered electric scooter.

He still wore the faded gray work jacket from his foreman days.

"Dad, it is a ten-minute walk," Chen Xuan said. "You did not have to come."

"Nonsense! Your mother kicked me out at six sharp. Get on!"

He shoved a tiny plastic helmet into Chen Xuan's hands.

"I will tell you on the way."

The scooter hummed into the evening traffic.

"Dad, what happened?" Chen Xuan asked.

All the cheer drained out of Chen Jianguo's voice, and raw anger took its place.

"You heard your mother's message. Here is the rest! I ran to the police station at three, and a clerk told me, 'The case is under review. Go home and wait.' I said I am his older brother, and the smug man behind the glass sneered, 'Who do you think you are?' Who do I think I am?! I am his brother!"

The handlebars shook with his anger!

"And that real-estate boss with the thick gold chain stood outside the shop, smiling. Smiling, Xuan!"

Even Old Lady Fang from the vegetable stall had leaned toward their window as they passed, and she had not come to help.

She had come to say her piece.

"Your brother should have signed, Jianguo. No shop is worth going to jail over."

As if poverty were a character flaw, and being arrested were simply bad manners!

"I hear you, Dad," Chen Xuan said steadily, holding the chin strap of the too-small helmet. "Did anyone see the driver fall?"

"Old Yu the tailor saw everything! He swears your uncle never left his counter. But who is going to listen to an old tailor?"

"We will listen," Chen Xuan said. "Let's eat first, Dad. Then we will know what to ask."

Chen Jianguo let out half a laugh and half a sigh.

"You sound just like your grandfather."

He steered into Fuxing New Village, and then he asked the question that had been burning in him all evening.

"But Xuan, will asking nicely be enough?"
