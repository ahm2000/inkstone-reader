# Chapter 116: Public Verdict in Yunxi County

By nine-thirty on Saturday morning, the sun had warmed the old stone pavement along Old Street.

Yu Changshun leaned forward with his hands on his knees and turned the volume knob up another notch.

"This is a special public broadcast from the Yunxi County Intermediate Court," the announcer said clearly. "At nine o'clock this morning, the court issued its formal public ruling on the joint corruption, gang crime, smuggling, and national security case involving five primary defendants in Yunxi County and Shanghai."

A hush dropped over the whole group. Chen Mingde stopped fanning himself with his folded newspaper. Beside him, Wu Lanying clasped her hands tightly in her apron. Even the noodle seller from two doors down set his empty basket on the ground and held his breath.

"First defendant, Deng Yongchang, former head of Yongchang Development," the radio continued. "Found guilty of leading an organized criminal gang, extortion, illegal demolition, bribery of public officials, cross-border smuggling, and aiding threats to public safety. All personal and company assets totaling 420 million yuan are seized and turned over to the state treasury. Sentence: life imprisonment with no chance of reduction."

"Life!" Yu Changshun slapped his thigh so hard the teacups rattled on the wooden bench. "Not ten years, not fifteen, life behind bars until his hair turns white!"

"Shh, keep quiet, Yu Changshun, let us hear the rest!" Old Auntie Tian from the dried-goods stall waved her hand at him, grinning so wide her eyes disappeared into wrinkles.

"Second defendant, Yan Tiehu," the announcer read on. "Found guilty of leading violent gang assaults, illegal detention, weapons possession, and cross-border smuggling. Personal assets of 68 million yuan seized. Sentence: death with a two-year reprieve, permanent loss of political rights."

A low ripple of relief moved through the crowd. Everyone on Old Street remembered Yan Tiehu's bald head, his thick gold chain, and the way his hired thugs kicked over vegetable baskets whenever a shopkeeper was one day late paying protection fees.

"Third defendant, Cheng Biao, former Director of Old Street Police Station," the voice said evenly. "Found guilty of taking bribes, shielding gang members, framing innocent citizens, and abusing his office. Illegal assets of 14 million yuan seized. Sentence: eighteen years in prison."

"Eighteen years!" the hardware delivery boy shouted from the back, pumping his fist into the air. "Cheng Biao used to take cartons of cigarettes off our counter without paying a single fen! Let him smoke the prison wall for eighteen years!"

"Fourth defendant, Liang Guodong, former Chief of the Yunxi County Public Security Bureau," the announcer stated. "Found guilty of accepting large bribes, selling official posts, covering up major crimes, and obstructing justice. Assets of 85 million yuan seized. Sentence: life imprisonment."

"Fifth defendant, Sun Weiming, former County Magistrate of Yunxi County," the radio finished. "Found guilty of massive corruption, abuse of public power, colluding with criminal gangs and smuggling rings, and endangering public security. Personal and hidden family assets totaling 260 million yuan seized. Sentence: life imprisonment."

For two full seconds after the announcer paused, Old Street was silent. Then the whole block erupted.

"They got all of them!" Yu Changshun jumped straight off the bench, grinning from ear to ear and waving both arms. "Deng Yongchang, Yan Tiehu, Cheng Biao, Liang Guodong, and even County Magistrate Sun Weiming! Not one slippery fish escaped the net!"

"All that stolen money taken back, too!" the tailor from across the lane called out, clapping Chen Mingde on the shoulder. "Hundreds of millions of yuan, and read straight over the county radio so every family in Yunxi can hear it!"

"Listen, they are reading the shop repayment notice next!" Wu Lanying pointed excitedly at the radio. "Every shop on Old Street that suffered broken windows or forced fees from Deng Yongchang's men can register at the county hall on Monday for full repayment!"

Cheers broke out all over again. Someone from the grocery store ran inside and came out holding a red string of firecrackers, though Yu Changshun quickly waved him down with a loud laugh.

"Hold on, do not light firecrackers right next to the hardware shop's paint tins," Yu Changshun joked, wiping the corner of his eye with his sleeve. "If we burn down Chen Mingde's awning today, Wu Lanying will chase us to the river with a broom."

"You know me well, Yu Changshun," Wu Lanying laughed, her face glowing with relief. She turned to her husband and softly touched his arm. "Chen Mingde, it is really over. Nobody is ever coming back waving fake demolition papers again."

Chen Mingde looked down the sunlit length of Old Street, where neighbors were shaking hands and laughing from doorway to doorway. For two years, a heavy gray cloud had hung over this street. Now the air felt light enough to breathe deep.

"It is really over," Chen Mingde murmured, his voice thick with emotion. He switched off the radio and glanced toward the shaded doorway of his shop. "Chen Jianguo, did you hear that? Even Sun Weiming got life."

Inside the doorway, Chen Jianguo stood holding a porcelain teacup, watching the cheering neighbors with a satisfied smile. Beside him, Chen Xuan sat on a low wooden stool, calmly peeling a tangerine from the counter bowl. He had taken the early morning bus home from Shanghai for Saturday.

"I heard every word, big brother," Chen Jianguo said, taking a slow sip of hot tea. "When the sky wants to clear, no amount of mud can hold the sun back. Those men thought Yunxi County was their private backyard. They forgot the law still has eyes."

"You tell them, Chen Jianguo!" Yu Changshun strode up the two stone steps into the shade, still grinning so wide his cheeks looked sore. He spotted Chen Xuan on the stool and clapped him warmly on the back. "Hey, Chen Xuan! Good thing you came home for Saturday! Your uncle can finally sleep without keeping a wooden club behind the shop door!"

"I heard it, Uncle Yu," Chen Xuan said with a mild, easy smile, handing half of his peeled tangerine to Yu Changshun. "It is good news for the whole street."

"More than good news, it is a fresh start!" Yu Changshun popped a slice into his mouth and chewed happily. "Chen Mingde, your brother told me you are lighting the charcoal stove in the back courtyard tonight. I am bringing two jugs of my best yellow rice wine, and nobody leaves the table before ten!"

"You bring the wine, and I will buy four extra pounds of pork ribs," Chen Mingde laughed heartily. "Tonight, we celebrate properly."

Chen Xuan looked down at the last slice of tangerine in his palm, his expression completely calm and ordinary. Out on the sunlit street, laughter rolled from doorway to doorway, and thirty meters away near the bus stop, a quiet man in a plain black jacket stood still in the shade, watching over the peaceful street without making a sound.
