# Chapter 141: Number 1's Question

At 4:55 on Friday afternoon, Chen Xuan closed the shipping ledger on his desk, wiped his pen, and tucked his scratched green thermos under his arm.

Across the Hengxin Logistics office, Sun Haoran shrugged into his suit jacket with a bright grin. "Have a good weekend, Chen Xuan! Do not let me catch you thinking about freight forms on your rest days."

"You do not need to worry about that, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan said easily. "Once I step out the front door, my brain shuts off until Monday morning at nine."

"That is why you live better than the rest of us!" Sun Haoran laughed, waving from the hallway. "Say hello to Yuhan for me!"

"I will. See you Monday."

Chen Xuan walked downstairs onto the cool Shanghai sidewalk. Fifty paces down the curb, a plain dark sedan sat parked beside a plane tree. Standing beside the rear door in a crisp white coat was Number 1, hands clasped and face calm.

Chen Xuan strolled over at an unhurried pace. "You came in person today," he said mildly.

"Good afternoon, sir," Number 1 replied, opening the rear door with a bow. "May we speak somewhere quiet for ten minutes?"

"Of course. There is an old teahouse two blocks over."

Ten minutes later, they sat in a private room at the back of the neighborhood teahouse with a pot of roasted barley tea. Once the waiter closed the wooden door, Number 1 sat upright in his white gear, pulled an unaddressed white envelope from his pocket, and laid it on the table.

"Word arrived from the capital two hours ago," Number 1 said quietly. "It came directly from the private desk of Elder Song, the ninety year old elder in the Western Hills."

Chen Xuan poured two cups of barley tea, pushing one across. "Did something happen with the ministry audits?"

"No, sir. All forty departments are quiet," Number 1 said, his dark eyes steady. "Elder Song sent a personal invitation. He hopes to share one quiet cup of tea with the holder of the Black Order tomorrow morning by the Western Hills lake."

Chen Xuan set the teapot down with a soft clink. "He is ready for that cup of tea."

"Yes, sir. Elder Song arranged a sealed landing slot at the capital's executive airfield himself," Number 1 said. "No flight list will carry your name, no camera along the Western Hills road will be turned on, and he dismissed every single guard, aide, and general from the lakeside. Neither Premier Gu Chengshan nor Elder Qin Zhengguo knows who is flying in. Only Elder Song will sit in the stone pavilion."

Number 1 folded his hands, his calm voice carrying genuine worry. "Sir, forgive me for asking this. Do you truly want to meet Elder Song face to face?"

Chen Xuan picked up his cup and blew gently across the tea. "Why do you ask?"

"Because until today, you have been an absolute ghost to everyone in Beijing," Number 1 answered plainly. "Gao Zhengyi, Zhou Zhenhua, Premier Gu Chengshan, and even Elder Qin Zhengguo only know the weight of the Black Order and the 300,000,000,000,000 yuan gift that saved the national banks. Not one soul in the capital knows your face or your name. Even Madam Qin Yuhan and her whole family believe you are just a carefree clerk earning 12,000 yuan a month."

Number 1 looked across the table earnestly. "Elder Song is ninety, yet his eyes are sharp as a blade. Once you sit across from him, one person in Beijing will know your face. If you prefer, I can reply tonight that you never meet anyone in person."

Chen Xuan sipped his warm barley tea and looked at Number 1 with an easy smile. "You always think three steps ahead. That is why I leave the whole White Guard in your hands. Still, you do not need to worry about tomorrow. Elder Song spent his life holding up the roof for ordinary people. If he cleared the whole lake just to pour me a cup of tea in secret, refusing him would be poor manners."

Number 1 stared at him for two seconds before the tight line between his eyebrows softened. "Yes, sir. I was thinking like a guard, not like a guest."

"What time should I be at the hangar tomorrow?" Chen Xuan asked with a chuckle.

"Seven thirty in the morning," Number 1 replied. "Number 7 will fly up with you in white gear on your pearl white jet and drive you to the Western Hills himself. You will land by ten, have tea by eleven, and be back in Shanghai before midafternoon."

"Good. I will tell Yuhan tonight that I have a quick Saturday morning warehouse check in the suburbs," Chen Xuan smiled.

Number 1 glanced toward the frosted window. "There is one small matter, sir. Several Black Guard members are sitting in a dark van fifty meters down the lane. If masked figures in plain black gear shadow your car into the Western Hills tomorrow, the outer perimeter sentries might get nervous."

"Have the Black Guard ever stepped closer than fifty meters?" Chen Xuan asked mildly.

"Never, sir."

"Then leave them be," Chen Xuan said simply, standing up with his thermos.

Half an hour later, Chen Xuan walked into his warm apartment carrying fresh pork ribs and greens. Qin Yuhan came out of the living room in her soft sweater, her eyes lighting up as she took his windbreaker.

"You are back early!" Qin Yuhan smiled, kissing his cheek. "The market ribs look great today."

"Picked the best ones in the stall," Chen Xuan grinned, heading into the kitchen. "By the way, honey, Sun Haoran asked me to do a quick Saturday morning inventory check at the outer suburban warehouse tomorrow. I have to leave around seven, check the shipping crates, and I will be back before three in the afternoon."

Qin Yuhan pouted slightly, leaning against the doorframe. "A warehouse check on a Saturday? Why is Sun Haoran making a 12,000 yuan clerk work on the weekend?"

"He is giving me an extra 200 yuan meal allowance and Monday afternoon off," Chen Xuan laughed, rinsing the ribs. "Plus, the warehouse watchman makes great roasted peanuts."

Qin Yuhan giggled, shaking her head fondly at her easygoing husband. "Only you would be happy about a Saturday warehouse check for 200 yuan and free peanuts. Come on, Clerk Chen, I will peel the garlic for you!"
