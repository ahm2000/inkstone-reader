# Chapter 125: Business Class Cakes

The next morning at 9:10 AM, high-speed train G7128 glided smoothly out of Shanghai Hongqiao Station, accelerating west toward Jingzhou City in Handong Province at 300 kilometers per hour.

In Car 5, Chen Xuan settled into the window seat of Row 8 with his dented green metal thermos resting on the fold-down tray table and Supervisor Hu Dewei's three thick brown route binders tucked neatly into the overhead rack. With two quiet hours ahead of him, he leaned his cheek against his palm, sipping hot Yunnan red tea while flat fields and swaying reed marshes flashed past the glass.

Directly across the aisle sat General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics, alongside Supervisor Hu Dewei and Manager Lu, a talkative branch manager from another small Pudong freight firm who was also traveling to the Shanshui Villa forum.

Sun Haoran had tried to buy first-class tickets the day before, only to discover that every business-class and first-class seat on G7128 had already been booked by provincial officials and senior corporate executives. Sitting in a standard second-class carriage clearly bruised his pride, and seeing Chen Xuan lounging peacefully with a chipped green thermos only irritated him more.

"Chen Xuan," Sun Haoran said in a loud, lecturing voice meant for Manager Lu to hear. "Sit up straight. Do you have any idea what kind of event we are attending at Shanshui Villa today?"

Chen Xuan turned his head calmly. "The Handong Regional Shipping Forum, General Manager Sun."

"Right, hosted by Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang's office," Sun Haoran snapped, adjusting his shiny silk tie. "Every major logistics group in the Yangtze River Delta will be there. I heard through a friend that even President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group is riding in Business Class Car 1 on this very train."

Manager Lu whistled in admiration. "President Qin of Haiyue Group? She just crushed a 300 million yuan bad deal in Shanghai two days ago! If our little companies could even shake her hand at Shanshui Villa, we would be set for the next five years."

"Precisely," Supervisor Hu Dewei chimed in, wagging a finger at Chen Xuan. "If General Manager Sun manages to catch even three minutes with Haiyue Group's team during the tea break, Hengxin Logistics could land a 500k yuan regional subcontract. So listen carefully, Chen Xuan. Once we get off the train, hide that ugly green thermos inside your bag. Walk behind us, carry the binders quietly, and whatever you do, do not wander near the VIP lounge or try to speak to important people like President Qin. One careless word from a junior clerk could ruin our company's image."

Manager Lu glanced at Chen Xuan's simple autumn jacket and chuckled softly. "Relax, Hu Dewei. Your clerk probably does not even know what a business-class carriage looks like. He will stay right where he belongs."

Chen Xuan did not argue or frown. He simply nodded politely, picked up his dented green thermos, and took another unhurried sip of warm red tea. "Understood. I will stay out of the way."

Sun Haoran grunted with satisfaction and leaned back, already rehearsing how he would introduce himself to Haiyue Group's executives at Shanshui Villa.

Ten minutes later, the automatic sliding glass door at the front of Car 5 hummed open.

A tall, graceful woman in a tailored camel autumn coat and soft cream scarf walked calmly down the center aisle. In her left hand, she carried a neat white bakery box tied with a thin gold ribbon. In her right hand, she held a warm paper cup of sweet osmanthus milk tea.

Conversations across the carriage faded as passengers turned to look. Sun Haoran froze mid-sentence, his eyes widening in shock as he recognized the poised woman immediately.

"That is President Qin Yuhan!" Sun Haoran whispered urgently, scrambling to button his suit jacket and rising halfway from his seat. "President Qin! What an honor! I am Sun Haoran, General Manager of Hengxin Logistics in Pudong!"

Hu Dewei and Manager Lu practically leaped to their feet as well, stretching out their hands with eager, nervous smiles.

Qin Yuhan did not even slow her steps for Sun Haoran's outstretched hand. She walked cleanly past Row 8 on the aisle side, turned directly toward the window seat, and her cool executive composure melted into a warm, affectionate smile.

"I told you the business-class lounge had fresh osmanthus walnut cakes," Qin Yuhan said softly, setting the white bakery box and the warm milk tea right beside Chen Xuan's dented green metal thermos. "They just came out of the oven, so I brought them over while they are still warm."

Silence dropped over Row 8 like a heavy stone.

Sun Haoran stood frozen halfway out of his seat, his hand hanging awkwardly in midair. Hu Dewei's jaw fell open so wide he forgot to blink, and Manager Lu stared at the white bakery box as if the high-speed train had suddenly started flying.

Chen Xuan untied the gold ribbon with easy familiarity, opened the box, and picked up a warm walnut cake. Instead of eating it himself, he held it up lightly toward Qin Yuhan.

"You have the first bite, Wife," Chen Xuan said with a lazy grin. "You skipped your second cup of porridge this morning."

Qin Yuhan laughed softly, took a neat bite of the sweet cake, and gently brushed a tiny crumb from his sleeve. "Come sit with me in Car 1 once you finish your tea," she said warmly, casting a single calm, polite glance across the aisle at the stunned trio. "Or are your colleagues still busy giving you lectures?"

"Not at all," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, closing his thermos lid. "They were just finished."

Behind them, Sun Haoran's face turned as pale as paper as he watched the junior supervisor he had just scolded stroll effortlessly toward Business Class Car 1 beside the President of Haiyue Group.
