# Chapter 125: Business Class Cakes

At nine-ten on Friday morning, the silver high-speed train G7128 glided smoothly out of Shanghai Hongqiao Station, heading west toward Jingzhou City in Handong Province at three hundred kilometers per hour.

In Car 5, a clean second-class carriage, Chen Xuan sat in the window seat of Row 8 with his scratched green enamel thermos resting on the tray table. Because the three brown route binders from Hu Dewei were tucked neatly into the overhead luggage rack, Chen Xuan had nothing to do except watch the green autumn fields outside the window and sip his hot Yunnan red tea.

Two seats away across the aisle sat General Manager Sun Haoran of Hengxin Logistics, alongside two branch managers from other small Pudong freight companies who were also traveling to the forum at Shanshui Villa.

Even though Sun Haoran had tried to book a first-class ticket yesterday, all the first-class and business-class seats on Train G7128 had already been booked out by top executives from Lujiazui and major banks. Forced to sit in Car 5 with Chen Xuan, Sun Haoran still crossed his legs in his gray suit and talked loudly so everyone within three rows could hear him.

"Let me tell you, Old Wang," Sun Haoran boasted to the freight manager beside him, swirling his plastic travel cup of green tea. "This Handong Provincial Economic and Logistics Forum at Shanshui Villa is no small meeting! Not only are the regional credit directors from China Construction Bank, Huaxia Bank, ICBC, and Bank of China attending, but I saw President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group boarding Car 1 in the VIP lounge at Hongqiao Station twenty minutes ago!"

Old Wang gasped in admiration. "President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group is on our train right now? Ever since she graduated from Fudan University and took over Haiyue Group, every shipping company in Shanghai has dreamed of getting a subcontract from her! Did you get a chance to say hello to her on the platform, General Manager Sun?"

"Well, President Qin was surrounded by two assistants and walking toward the business-class car, so I merely nodded to her from a distance," Sun Haoran said grandly, smoothing his silk tie before glancing across the aisle at Chen Xuan.

Seeing Chen Xuan sitting lazily by the window with his worn green enamel thermos and eating roasted pumpkin seeds, Sun Haoran shook his head in condescension.

"Chen Xuan, put away those pumpkin seeds and sit up straight," Sun Haoran lectured across the aisle. "When we arrive at Shanshui Villa in Jingzhou City this afternoon, figures like President Qin Yuhan and Mayor Li Dakang will be walking through the main hall. A twelve-thousand-yuan clerk like you has probably only seen President Qin on financial television. Do not wander around with that old green thermos and embarrass Hengxin Logistics!"

"Do not worry, General Manager Sun," Chen Xuan chuckled good-naturedly, brushing a pumpkin seed shell into a paper napkin. "I see President Qin often enough that I promise not to stare at her."

Sun Haoran snorted and rolled his eyes toward Old Wang. "Listen to this boy bragging! He says he sees President Qin often enough! Where, on the giant LED screen outside IFC Mall in Lujiazui?"

Old Wang and the other freight manager burst out laughing, while the empty middle seat right beside Chen Xuan remained unoccupied.

Just then, the automatic glass door at the front of Car 5 slid open with a soft chime.

A tall, breathtakingly beautiful young woman in a tailored pale-blue autumn coat and beige silk blouse walked down the center aisle from the direction of the Car 1 business-class cabin. Her dark hair was tied back lightly with a pearl clip, and in her slender hands she carried a warm, gold-ribboned pastry box from a famous bakery on Nanjing Road, along with a pair of white headphones.

Every conversation in the front half of Car 5 stopped instantly.

General Manager Sun Haoran's jaw dropped open so fast that the plastic lid of his travel teacup slipped right out of his hand and bounced onto the carpet.

It was President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group!

"President Qin!" Sun Haoran scrambled to his feet in the aisle, his face turning red with excitement. "What brings you all the way back from Car 1 business class to our second-class carriage?"

Qin Yuhan paused politely by Row 8, gave Sun Haoran a brief, courteous nod as a fellow forum guest from Shanghai, and then turned her bright eyes straight toward the window seat.

The cool aura of the billionaire corporate president vanished into a warm, playful smile.

"Is this middle seat empty, Clerk Chen?" Qin Yuhan asked softly, tapping the seat cushion right next to Chen Xuan. "Car 1 is full of bank directors talking about bond yields, and I could not get a single minute of quiet. The conductor checked the manifest and said the seat next to you was unbooked all the way to Jingzhou City."

"Sit down, the view is better back here anyway," Chen Xuan grinned, lifting the center armrest for her and pulling a folded cashmere travel shawl out of his canvas bag. He draped it neatly over her knees so the air conditioning would not chill her legs. "What is in the gold box?"

"Freshly steamed osmanthus rice cakes I had the driver pick up on the way to the station," Qin Yuhan smiled happily, sitting down shoulder-to-shoulder with him and untying the gold ribbon. She picked up a warm, fragrant white cake dotted with golden osmanthus blossoms and held it right up to Chen Xuan's mouth. "Try one while it is still warm. You sent me a text asking for osmanthus cake yesterday afternoon, remember?"

"Delicious," Chen Xuan said, taking a bite of the soft, sweet cake before unscrewing his green enamel thermos and pouring half a cup of hot Yunnan red tea for her. "Drink a little hot tea with it so it is not too sweet."

Qin Yuhan took the scratched green thermos lid with both hands, took a happy sip of his hot red tea, and leaned her shoulder comfortably against his arm while putting one side of her white headphones over his left ear to share a soft song.

Two feet away across the aisle, General Manager Sun Haoran, Old Wang, and the entire row of freight managers stood frozen like wooden statues, staring at the billionaire president of Haiyue Group sharing osmanthus cakes, headphones, and a scratched green thermos lid with the twelve-thousand-yuan clerk from Hengxin Logistics!
