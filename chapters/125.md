# Chapter 125: Business Class Cakes

The next morning at 9:10 AM, high-speed train G7128 glided smoothly out of Shanghai Hongqiao Station, accelerating west toward Jingzhou City in Handong Province at 300 kilometers per hour.

Because Hengxin Logistics had booked their trip at the last minute, their seats in Car 5 were split apart. Chen Xuan ended up in the quiet window seat of Row 2, right near the front sliding glass door leading toward the forward carriages. His green thermos rested on the fold-down tray table, and Supervisor Hu Dewei's three thick brown route binders sat neatly in the overhead rack.

General Manager Sun Haoran and Supervisor Hu Dewei had been assigned seats way at the back of Car 5 in Row 16. Sun Haoran had tried to buy business-class or first-class tickets the day before, only to find every seat already taken by provincial officials and senior corporate executives. Riding in standard second class clearly bruised his pride, and seeing Chen Xuan lounging peacefully with a chipped green thermos on their way down the aisle only irritated him further.

Before heading to the back, Sun Haoran paused beside Row 2 and spoke in a loud, lecturing voice. "Chen Xuan, sit up straight and stay alert. Do you even realize what kind of event we are attending at Shanshui Villa today?"

Chen Xuan turned his head calmly. "The Handong Regional Shipping Forum, General Manager Sun."

"Hosted by Jingzhou Municipal Party Secretary Li Dakang's office," Sun Haoran snapped, adjusting his shiny silk tie. "Every major logistics group in the Yangtze River Delta will be there. I heard through a friend that even President Qin Yuhan of Haiyue Group is riding in Business Class Car 1 on this very train."

"Precisely," Supervisor Hu Dewei chimed in, wagging a finger at Chen Xuan. "If General Manager Sun catches even three minutes with Haiyue Group's team during the tea break, Hengxin Logistics could land a 500k yuan regional subcontract. Once we get off the train, hide that ugly green thermos inside your bag. Walk behind us, carry the binders quietly, and whatever you do, do not wander near the VIP lounge or try to speak to important people like President Qin. One careless word from a freight clerk could ruin our company's image."

Chen Xuan did not argue or frown. He simply nodded politely and lifted his thermos for an unhurried sip of warm Yunnan red tea. "Understood. I will stay out of the way."

Satisfied, Sun Haoran grunted and led Hu Dewei all the way down the crowded carriage to Row 16 at the very back. Once seated fourteen rows behind Chen Xuan, Sun Haoran slipped on his reading glasses to rehearse his pitch, and Hu Dewei buried his face in a stack of company brochures, completely blocked from the front rows by tall seatbacks and overhead luggage.

Across the aisle from Chen Xuan in Row 2 sat Manager Lu, a talkative branch manager from a different Pudong freight firm who was also bound for Shanshui Villa. Having overheard Hu Dewei's lecture, Manager Lu glanced at Chen Xuan's simple autumn jacket and chuckled softly.

"Relax, young man," Manager Lu said with a smug smirk. "President Qin of Haiyue Group just crushed a 300 million yuan bad deal in Shanghai two days ago. People like us would be lucky just to shake her hand at the forum, let alone a small clerk sitting in second class. You probably do not even know what a business-class carriage looks like. Best to listen to your manager and stay right where you belong."

Chen Xuan merely smiled faintly and turned back toward the window, watching golden autumn fields and swaying reed marshes flash past the glass.

Ten minutes later, the automatic sliding glass door at the very front of Car 5 hummed open, just two steps from Row 2.

A tall, graceful woman in a tailored camel autumn coat and soft cream scarf stepped calmly into the carriage. In her left hand, she carried a neat white bakery box tied with a thin gold ribbon. In her right hand, she held a warm paper cup of sweet osmanthus milk tea.

Voices across the front rows faded immediately as passengers turned to look. Manager Lu froze mid-smirk, his eyes widening in shock as he recognized the poised woman at once.

"President Qin Yuhan!" Manager Lu whispered eagerly, scrambling halfway out of his seat and fumbling to button his suit jacket as he thrust out a hand. "What an honor! I am Manager Lu from Donghai Freight in Pudong!"

Qin Yuhan did not even slow her steps for Manager Lu's outstretched hand. She walked cleanly past him, stopped beside Chen Xuan's window seat in Row 2, and her cool executive composure melted into a warm, affectionate smile.

"I told you the business-class lounge had fresh osmanthus walnut cakes," Qin Yuhan said softly, setting the white bakery box and the warm milk tea right beside Chen Xuan's green thermos. "They just came out of the oven, so I brought them over while they are still warm."

Silence fell over Row 2 like a dropped stone.

Manager Lu stood frozen halfway out of his seat, his hand hanging awkwardly in midair and his jaw dropping open so wide he forgot to blink. He stared at the white bakery box as if the high-speed train had suddenly lifted off the tracks.

Chen Xuan untied the gold ribbon with easy familiarity, opened the box, and picked up a warm walnut cake. Instead of eating it himself, he held it up lightly toward Qin Yuhan.

"You have the first bite, Wife," Chen Xuan said with a lazy grin. "You skipped your second cup of porridge this morning."

Qin Yuhan laughed softly, took a neat bite of the sweet cake, and gently brushed a tiny crumb from his sleeve. "Come sit with me in Car 1 once you finish your tea," she said warmly, casting a single calm, polite glance across the aisle at the speechless Manager Lu. "Unless your neighbor here still has more advice to share."

"Not at all," Chen Xuan replied smoothly, capping his thermos and lifting his canvas satchel. "He was just finished."

Manager Lu's face turned as pale as paper as he watched the quiet freight clerk stroll effortlessly through the front sliding door beside the President of Haiyue Group.

Way back in Row 16 at the rear of Car 5, Hu Dewei finally looked up from his brochures a few minutes later. Seeing nothing past the tall rows of seats, he muttered to Sun Haoran that Chen Xuan must have wandered off to refill his thermos, completely unaware that his clerk was sitting in Business Class Car 1 sharing warm walnut cakes with the President of Haiyue Group.
