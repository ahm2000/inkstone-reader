# Chapter 110: Gossip on the Twelfth Floor

At eight-forty on Monday morning, the streets around Lujiazui were freshly washed after Sunday night's spring rain.

Because the morning air was still damp and Qin Yuhan was heading toward Hongqiao Airport to see her parents, Qin Zhengming and Lin Shuyin, off on their flight back to Beijing, she had refused to let Chen Xuan take Metro Line 2 today. Instead, her sleek black Maybach pulled up at the street corner one block south of Hengxin Logistics.

Inside the cabin, Chen Xuan picked up his canvas bag, his scratched green enamel thermos, and a warm paper bag containing two steamed red-bean buns he had bought near Emerald Riverside Villa.

"Why do you always make me stop a block away?" Qin Yuhan asked from behind the wheel, wearing a charcoal blazer and the mother-of-pearl wristwatch he had given her at Plaza 66. "If I pull up right in front of your lobby, will your green thermos lose its reputation?"

"If you pull a 3 million yuan Maybach up to the front doors of Hengxin Logistics at eight forty-five," Chen Xuan chuckled, tapping her nose lightly, "half the twelfth floor will choke on their soy milk, and my manager Hu Dewei will spend all morning asking me for car rides instead of letting me read the newspaper."

Qin Yuhan brushed his finger away with a small smile, though her eyes were warm. "I am driving myself back from the airport today."

"Drive slow on the elevated road," Chen Xuan smiled, opening the passenger door. "After you drop Uncle Qin and Aunt Lin off at the terminal, do not skip lunch at Haiyue Tower. Come home by six tonight. I promised you braised spare ribs with lotus root."

"Do not make the sauce too sweet," Qin Yuhan called softly as he stepped onto the sidewalk.

"Just one spoon of rock sugar, President Qin!" Chen Xuan waved his green thermos cheerfully as the black Maybach glided away.

Fifty meters down the sidewalk behind a newspaper kiosk, two plainclothes members of the Black Order bought morning papers and kept their quiet watch as Chen Xuan strolled toward the lobby.

Twenty meters ahead by the crosswalk, senior clerk Pan Xiulan and young intern Zheng Qian stood outside the convenience store holding hot soy milk, frozen like statues.

Zheng Qian rubbed his eyes hard. "Sister Pan, did I sleep too little last night, or did Brother Chen Xuan just step out of a Maybach S680?"

Pan Xiulan stared after the luxury sedan, her jaw dropping. "Not only did he step out of a Maybach, Little Zheng, did you see the woman driving? She looked like a magazine cover model! And she smiled when he waved that green thermos!"

Five minutes later, on the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics, Pan Xiulan and Zheng Qian hurried through the glass doors before Chen Xuan finished taking the slow elevator.

Department Manager Hu Dewei stood by the water cooler bragging about his weekend, while Zhang Pengfei sat at the second-row desk still looking sour from having his bonus docked by General Manager Sun Haoran the previous week.

"You will never believe what Little Zheng and I just saw downstairs at the south corner!" Pan Xiulan burst out. "A 3 million yuan Maybach pulled up by the curb, and Chen Xuan stepped out of the passenger seat! The woman driving was young, gorgeous, and wearing a custom suit!"

"That is the truth!" Zheng Qian nodded excitedly. "I saw the silver emblem on the hood and the watch on her wrist! Brother Chen Xuan talked to her like they live together! Did Brother Chen secretly marry a rich beauty?"

Silence lasted two seconds before Zhang Pengfei let out a loud, scoffing laugh.

"Chen Xuan? Married a rich beauty who drives a 3 million yuan Maybach?" Zhang Pengfei slapped his knee and pointed toward Chen Xuan's empty corner desk. "Zheng Qian, are you crazy? Look at his desk! He uses a ten-yuan mouse pad, drinks loose tea from a chipped green jug, and earns twelve thousand yuan a month! What rich woman in Lujiazui would look twice at a lazy clerk who runs out the door at four fifty-five?"

Hu Dewei cleared his throat pompously, hooking his thumbs into his belt. "Pan Xiulan, use your head. Do you know why a guy like Chen Xuan would step out of a Maybach a block away from the office?"

"Why, Manager Hu?" Zheng Qian asked.

"Because he works a part-time morning job as a car-wash runner in Pudong!" Hu Dewei declared confidently. "Maybe some distant cousin from his hometown in Yunxi County works as a housekeeper at a rich villa and asked the owner to give poor Chen Xuan a lift when it rained! That is why they dropped him at the corner! They were embarrassed to be seen with his green thermos!"

Zhang Pengfei grinned widely. "Manager Hu has sharp eyes! No wonder he never stays past four fifty-five. He probably has to go walk the rich owner's dog!"

Just then, the glass door swung open.

Chen Xuan strolled in at eight fifty-six, wearing his gray polo shirt and carrying his green thermos and his paper bag of red-bean buns. He swiped his card and walked calmly to his corner desk.

Zhang Pengfei leaned over his monitor with a smug grin. "Hey, Chen Xuan! Pan Xiulan says you got dropped off in a Maybach at the corner. Tell the truth, did some rich auntie let you ride along after you washed her windshield, or is she a distant relative taking pity on you?"

Chen Xuan sat down, unscrewed his green thermos, and took a happy bite of his warm red-bean bun.

"You caught me, Pengfei," Chen Xuan said easily. "The driver felt bad that I had to walk in the damp air with my buns, so she gave me a free lift from the river. Have you guys finished filing the morning customs sheets yet? Manager Hu promised General Manager Sun Haoran they would be on his desk by nine-fifteen."

Hu Dewei's smug smile vanished as he checked the clock. "Nine-fifteen! Zhang Pengfei, stop chatting and print those sheets right now!"

While Hu Dewei and Zhang Pengfei scrambled around the printer, Chen Xuan sipped his hot tea, picked up his phone, and sent a quick text to the president of Haiyue Group:

*Picked up fresh lotus root before the ride. Do not be late tonight, Wife.*

Ten seconds later, his phone buzzed with Qin Yuhan's reply:

*Five forty-five sharp. Save me one red-bean bun if you did not eat both.*

Chen Xuan grinned at the second bun in his bag, tucked it into his drawer, and leaned back to enjoy another peaceful Monday morning.
