# Chapter 97: The 4:55 Slacker on the Twelfth Floor

At eight fifty-five on Tuesday morning, the elevator doors chimed open on the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics in Lujiazui.

Chen Xuan strolled out at his usual unhurried pace, carrying his scratched green thermos in his right hand and a red plastic bag with a glass jar of Liu Meifang's pickled chilies in his left. He pressed his thumb against the wall scanner by the glass door.

"Attendance recorded," the machine chirped at eight fifty-six.

"Look who finally came back from Handong Province!" Pan Xiulan called out from her desk near the pantry with a big grin. "Chen Xuan, if you had arrived four minutes later, Supervisor Hu Dewei would have fined you fifty yuan."

"Four minutes is plenty of time to walk from the elevator and pour a cup of hot water, Sister Pan," Chen Xuan replied cheerfully. He stopped by her desk and set the heavy glass jar of bright red pickled chilies next to her keyboard. "Here. My mother made three jars on Monday night in Yunxi County. She told me to bring one for the office so everyone can have something spicy with their lunch boxes."

Pan Xiulan's eyes lit up as she twisted the metal lid open a crack and sniffed the rich aroma of garlic, ginger, and chopped red chilies. "Oh, smell that! Your mother's hands are pure gold, Chen Xuan. The cafeteria cabbage has tasted like cardboard all week."

From the desk right next to Chen Xuan's corner, twenty-three-year-old Zheng Qian spun around in her chair with dark circles under her eyes and a stack of shipping sheets in her lap.

"Brother Xuan, you are finally back!" Zheng Qian groaned, resting her chin on her wrist. "While you were gone on Monday, Zhang Pengfei insisted on handling the Shanghai Port container sheets by himself to show off in front of Supervisor Hu Dewei. Now three cold-chain containers bound for Ningbo are stuck at the terminal check, and Hu Dewei has been pacing around his office since eight o'clock!"

"Let them pace," Chen Xuan said calmly, hanging his jacket on the back of his chair. "Walking is good for digestion. I am going to the pantry to rinse my thermos and steep some fresh jasmine tea."

"How can you be so relaxed every single day?" Zheng Qian asked, half envious and half amused. "Everyone else in Lujiazui drinks three cups of iced coffee just to survive the morning, and you walk in here looking like you just came back from a walk in the park."

"Because coffee makes your heart beat too fast, and working too hard makes your hair fall out," Chen Xuan told her seriously, tapping the side of his green thermos. "Twelve thousand yuan a month only buys eight hours of sitting at this desk. It does not buy my blood pressure."

Pan Xiulan burst out laughing. "Zheng Qian, do not listen to him. Nobody in this building has mastered the art of slacking off quite like Chen Xuan. At four fifty-four every afternoon, his computer is already off and his hand is on his thermos."

Before Chen Xuan could take a step toward the water dispenser, the glass door of the supervisor's office flew open.

Hu Dewei marched out holding a thick folder, his hair sticking up on one side and sweat shining on his forehead. Right behind him came Zhang Pengfei, whose tie was crooked and whose face was pale.

"What do you mean the Shanghai Port customs computer rejected the bill of lading three times?" Hu Dewei barked, waving the folder in Zhang Pengfei's face. "If that fresh fruit shipment sits on the dock past twelve-thirty today, the client will fine Hengxin Logistics 200k for late clearance! Where am I going to get 200k? Out of your bonus?"

"Supervisor Hu, I checked the container weight and the tax numbers five times!" Zhang Pengfei stammered, his hands shaking as he flipped through the pages. "The port system keeps flagging an error on Line Fourteen, and the port clerk on the phone refused to override it."

Hu Dewei spotted Chen Xuan standing by his desk with his empty green thermos and hurried right over.

"Chen Xuan! Good, you are back!" Hu Dewei said breathlessly, shoving the thick folder toward Chen Xuan's chest. "Quick, cancel your lunch break today and go down to the port customs hall with Zhang Pengfei. Help him figure out why their system is blocking the release!"

Chen Xuan took half a step back so the folder did not bump his green thermos.

"Supervisor Hu, I cannot cancel my lunch break today," Chen Xuan said in a mild, unbothered voice. "My mother set up a blind date for me at twelve-fifteen right across from Jin Mao Tower. If I do not show up, she will call me ten times tonight."

Zhang Pengfei let out an awkward laugh to cover his panic. "Chen Xuan, the company is about to lose 200k on an urgent port shipment and you are worried about a blind date? Do you have any team spirit at all?"

Chen Xuan did not even get angry. He set his green thermos down on his desk and flipped Zhang Pengfei's folder open to the third page.

"I do not need to go to the port, and it does not take a whole lunch break," Chen Xuan said lazily, tapping the bottom right box of the yellow sheet. "It takes twenty seconds. Look right here, Zhang Pengfei. For cold-chain containers transferring through Pier Three, you used the old domestic warehouse stamp instead of the bonded quarantine seal. And on Line Fourteen, you typed the Bank of China guarantee serial number backward: it ends in seventy-eight, not eighty-seven."

The entire open office went quiet.

Hu Dewei snatched the folder back and squinted hard at the yellow sheet through his glasses. His mouth dropped open. "He is right! That is the old domestic stamp! And the serial number on the Bank of China slip really ends in seventy-eight!"

Hu Dewei whirled around and glared at Zhang Pengfei, whose face had turned bright red. "You spent three hours yelling at the port computer when you put the wrong stamp on the paper yourself? Go fix the scan and resend it right now!"

"Yes, Supervisor Hu! Right away!" Zhang Pengfei mumbled, grabbing the folder and scurrying back toward his desk with his head down.

Chen Xuan picked up his green thermos again and strolled toward the pantry water dispenser with an easy smile.

"See, Zheng Qian?" Chen Xuan called lightly over his shoulder. "Solve it in twenty seconds now, and nobody bothers your lunch break at noon."
