# Chapter 97: Back on the Twelfth Floor

At eight fifty-five on Tuesday morning, the elevator doors on the twelfth floor of Hengxin Logistics slid open with a soft chime.

Chen Xuan stepped out wearing a plain white shirt and dark gray slacks. In his left hand, he held his scratched green thermos. In his right hand, he carried a glass jar of homemade pickled chilies inside a clean cloth bag.

He walked past the glass doors, tapped his badge on the time clock, and heard the crisp green beep.

"Chen Xuan, you are back!"

Pan Xiulan looked up from her cubicle with a warm smile. "How is your uncle? Did everything back in your hometown get sorted out?"

"All sorted out, Sister Pan," Chen Xuan said warmly, setting his green thermos beside his monitor. "Just a small misunderstanding with the local office. He is back home and opening his shop today."

"Thank goodness," Pan Xiulan said with relief. Her eyes dropped to the cloth bag on his desk. "Wait. Is that the pickled chilies your mother makes?"

Chen Xuan smiled and lifted out the glass jar packed with bright red chilies, garlic cloves, and ginger. "She packed three jars. I brought one to share at lunch. Spoon some out whenever you want."

"You are too thoughtful," Pan Xiulan laughed, grabbing a clean plastic cup. "The cafeteria cabbage tastes like boiled water without your mother's chilies."

From the desk behind him, Zheng Qian leaned back and gave a quiet nod. "Welcome back, Chen Xuan. Glad your family is okay."

Zheng Qian lowered his voice and glanced toward the glass-walled manager's office. "Watch your step this morning, though. Hu Dewei has been snapping at everyone since Monday morning. And Zhang Pengfei spent half of yesterday inside his office whispering."

"Let him whisper," Chen Xuan replied calmly, waking up his computer screen. "My shipping sheets were balanced before I left on Friday."

"Oh, listen to that," a mocking voice drifted over from two desks away.

Zhang Pengfei leaned back in his swivel chair, spinning a black pen between his fingers with a smug smirk. He wore a tight navy suit jacket and smelled strongly of cologne.

"Look who finally decided to show up on the twelfth floor," Zhang Pengfei said loudly enough for the nearby rows to hear. "Walking in at eight fifty-five with a jar of village pickles like he is strolling into a tea house. Some of us stayed behind on Friday afternoon to carry the department while others ran off without approval."

Pan Xiulan frowned, ready to speak up, but Zheng Qian gently shook his head to keep her out of trouble.

Chen Xuan did not look away from his monitor. He twisted open his green thermos, took a quiet sip of hot tea, and opened his Tuesday delivery table. "I handed in my leave slip on Friday morning, Zhang Pengfei. And I finished all twelve of my shipping lists before noon."

"How noble," Zhang Pengfei scoffed with a sly grin. "Tell that to Manager Hu. He has been waiting to talk to you."

Right on cue, the glass door of the manager's office snapped open.

Department Manager Hu Dewei marched out holding a rolled stack of papers and a heavy scowl. His eyes locked straight onto Chen Xuan's desk.

"Chen Xuan!" Hu Dewei barked, striding down the aisle as nearby clerks ducked behind their monitors. He stopped beside Chen Xuan's desk and smacked the rolled papers against the partition. "Do you know what time it is? And are you bringing pickled vegetables into a logistics office now?"

"Good morning, Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said evenly. "It is eight fifty-eight. The jar lid is sealed tight."

"Do not talk back to me about the clock!" Hu Dewei snapped, raising his voice for the whole floor to hear. "I am talking about your attitude on Friday! You walked out early without waiting for my final signature, left your desk empty all Monday, and now you stroll back in drinking tea like nothing happened!"

Zhang Pengfei folded his arms and watched with a satisfied smirk.

"Manager Hu," Chen Xuan said calmly, "I handed you my leave slip at ten-thirty on Friday. You waved me away and told me to finish my shipping sheets first. I finished every sheet and sent them to your inbox before clocking out."

"I do not care about your excuses!" Hu Dewei shouted, his round face turning red. "Hengxin Logistics runs on discipline! Because you left early on Friday, Zhang Pengfei had to work overtime covering the port paperwork! Look at how responsible he is, and look at you!"

Hu Dewei jabbed a thick finger at Chen Xuan's desk. "Before noon, I want a two-thousand-word self-criticism report on my desk. And tonight, you are staying late to sort last quarter's warehouse receipts. If you do not like it, pack up your green thermos and your jar of chilies and find another job!"

The twelfth floor fell painfully silent. Pan Xiulan bit her lip tightly. Zheng Qian stared at his screen with a tight jaw.

Chen Xuan looked up at Hu Dewei with steady, calm eyes. Before he could say a word, a loud bang echoed from the far corner of the floor.

The wooden door of the General Manager's office flew wide open and slammed against the wall.

General Manager Sun Haoran burst out into the main hallway. His tie was crooked, his shirt collar was soaked with sweat, and his face was pale as chalk. He gripped his ringing phone in one hand and a crumpled blue shipping folder in the other.

"Hu Dewei!" Sun Haoran shouted, his voice cracking with panic. "Get over here right now! Where is Hu Dewei?!"

Hu Dewei jumped so hard he nearly dropped his papers. His stern scowl vanished in a heartbeat, replaced by a nervous smile as he hurried down the aisle toward Sun Haoran.

"General Manager Sun! I am right here! What happened?"

"What happened?!" Sun Haoran roared, shoving the blue folder straight into Hu Dewei's chest. "Turn on the news! Qingyan Port Customs Director Xu Wenbin just vanished! The anti-smuggling team dragged him out of his house before dawn in a massive sweep! Every fast-track channel at Qingyan Port is frozen solid!"

Hu Dewei's face drained of color. "Wait, Xu Wenbin was taken in?!"

"Yes!" Sun Haoran yelled, wiping cold sweat from his forehead while the whole twelfth floor froze in silence. "And every container tied to his office is under full lockdown! Our three urgent cold-chain containers for Hongda Foods are stuck at Qingyan Port right this second!"
