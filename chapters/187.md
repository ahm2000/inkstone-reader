# Chapter 187: Lunch Behind Jin Mao Tower

By 12:05 PM, the pale winter sun hung high over Lujiazui, bright against the steel and glass towers yet doing little to soften the crisp December wind. White clouds of breath drifted from the collars of office workers hurrying out of the financial plazas.

Tucked down a narrow brick lane behind Jin Mao Tower stood a small, steaming noodle shop with a faded red awning and a heavy clear plastic curtain hanging across the doorway. Inside, the air smelled wonderfully of slow-simmered beef bones, star anise, and toasted scallions. Every table was simple worn wood, and the front window had fogged over from the boiling stockpots.

Chen Xuan pushed through the curtain a few minutes early and took his favorite two-seat table near the foggy glass. Behind the steaming counter, the shop owner, a stout middle-aged uncle with flour dusted across his apron, looked up and grinned.

"Xiao Chen, right on time," the uncle called out warmly over the hiss of boiling noodles. "Same usual order, or is your wife joining you today?"

"She is on her way, Uncle Zhao," Chen Xuan replied easily, pouring two cups of hot barley tea from the aluminum kettle on the table. "Two bowls of braised beef noodles. Extra cilantro in mine, zero cilantro and an extra marinated egg in hers. And a small plate of pickled radish."

"Got it. I will slice the tender brisket for her bowl," Uncle Zhao laughed, tossing two fresh bundles of hand-pulled noodles into the rolling water.

At the neighboring table, an auntie in a padded floral vest was wiping down chopsticks and chatting casually with a young delivery rider about how sharp the wind had turned overnight. Everything inside the tiny shop felt warm, noisy, and completely unhurried.

Three minutes later, the plastic curtain parted again. A brief rush of cold air followed Qin Yuhan inside. She wore a tailored camel wool coat over her charcoal business suit, her dark hair tucked neatly behind her ears, cheeks faintly pink from the brisk walk down from the 38th floor of Haiyue Tower.

She spotted Chen Xuan immediately, and the cool, composed poise she carried in corporate boardrooms melted into a soft, bright smile.

"Cold out there today," she murmured as she slid onto the wooden bench opposite him and unwrapped her scarf.

"Give me your hands," Chen Xuan said softly.

Qin Yuhan chuckled, resting her slender fingers across the small table. Chen Xuan cupped her chilled hands between his warm palms for a few seconds, rubbing them gently before nudging a steaming cup of barley tea toward her.

"Better?" he asked.

"Much better," she said, taking a grateful sip while looking at him with playful amusement. "So, Mr. Model Employee, how does it feel to be the star of the 12th floor?"

"Word travels fast to the 38th floor," Chen Xuan said, leaning back with a grin. "You barely waited three hours after the contract renewal to spy on my bonus."

"I did not spy," Qin Yuhan protested lightly, her eyes curving. "Logistics sent the 120 million yuan contract summary to my desk at 10:00 AM, and the Shenzhen audit sign-off had your neat handwriting right at the bottom. My vice president actually asked if Hengxin was hiding a senior operations veteran on their floor."

"Tell him I am just a lazy clerk who likes clocking out at 4:55 PM," Chen Xuan teased. "Besides, you are the big president who doubled our company's contract. Technically, I am just living off my wife's generosity."

Qin Yuhan laughed softly and tapped his shin gently under the table with the toe of her boot. "Stop selling yourself short. The Shenzhen warehouse directors wrote a real commendation for how calmly you cleared that customs bottleneck. You always act like you do nothing, yet everything around you runs smoothly."

Just then, Uncle Zhao carried over two generous bowls of steaming beef noodles, the amber broth glistening with fragrant chili oil, along with a crisp dish of pickled radish.

"Eat while it is hot, young lady," Uncle Zhao said with a cheerful nod. "You work too hard in those tall glass buildings."

"Thank you, Uncle Zhao," Qin Yuhan replied politely, her smile genuine.

She picked up her chopsticks, glanced at Chen Xuan's bowl, and neatly plucked a thick slice of braised beef from his side before he could blink.

"Hey," Chen Xuan said in mock outrage, though he immediately used his own chopsticks to lift the extra marinated egg from his saucer and drop it gently into her broth. "You always claim you are watching your appetite, and then you raid my bowl first."

"Your bowl always tastes better," she replied smoothly, taking a happy bite. "Oh, Grandpa called me at 11:00 AM. He asked when you are coming over to the courtyard again. He bought a tin of aged Pu'er tea and swears he figured out a new opening to beat you at Chinese chess."

Chen Xuan smiled warmly at the mention of Elder Qin Zhengguo. "Let us go over this weekend. Tell Grandpa I will bring the braised pork ribs he likes, and I promise not to trap his chariot in the first fifteen moves."

"If you let him win too obviously, he will scold you for going easy on him," Qin Yuhan warned with a giggle. "You have to make it look close."

"Leave it to me," Chen Xuan said, slurping a mouthful of hot noodles. "What about tonight at home? It is freezing out. How about lamb hotpot with fresh Napa cabbage and handmade tofu?"

Qin Yuhan's eyes lit up immediately. "Yes, please. I have a long budget review at 3:00 PM, so knowing there is hotpot waiting at Emerald Riverside Villa will keep me alive."

They finished their bowls in comfortable, easy rhythm, talking about small household plans and laughing over Sister Pan Xiulan's morning roast of Hu Dewei's miraculous badminton recovery. To Qin Yuhan, sitting in this steamy little lane shop with her warm, witty husband felt like the safest place in the entire city.

When they stepped back outside into the biting wind, Chen Xuan paused at the corner of the lane to tuck her scarf snugly under her chin.

"Do not skip your afternoon water," he reminded her lightly.

"Yes, yes, I hear you," she said, smiling up at him before turning back toward Haiyue Tower. "See you at home tonight."

Chen Xuan stood with his hands in his coat pockets, watching her slim figure cross the plaza until she vanished through the revolving glass doors. Then he turned around with a quiet smile and strolled back toward Hengxin Logistics for the afternoon shift.
