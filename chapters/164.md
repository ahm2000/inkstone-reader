# Chapter 164: Leaving on Time

At five o'clock sharp, the heavy iron gate of Shenzhen Pier Nine locked into its fully open stop with a solid clang.

Behind the wide opening, the dock exploded into busy motion.

Inside the gatehouse, a port clerk printed out a fresh green clearance sheet, stamped it twice with trembling hands, and ran outside to hand it to Director Gao Zhenhua.

Gao Zhenhua hurried straight over to Qin Yuhan, holding the stamped clearance sheet out with both hands.

"President Qin, I offer my sincerest apologies on behalf of the Shenzhen Port Authority," Gao Zhenhua said breathlessly, wiping his brow. "This entire hold-up was caused by Lu Guobin abusing his position without approval. The Haiyue Star is now fully cleared at Pier Nine. All dock delay fees for the last two days are waived, and I have assigned three extra crane crews so your six hundred containers unload ahead of schedule."

Behind Qin Yuhan, Manager Zhou Ming let out a long breath he felt like he had been holding since yesterday morning.

"Thank you, Director Gao!" Zhou Ming said quickly, flipping through the stamp. "I will get our cold-chain trucks into lane one right now!"

Qin Yuhan nodded coolly, her posture poised and gracious. "Thank you for handling this promptly, Director Gao. Haiyue Group only asks for fair rules when we do business in Shenzhen."

"Of course! Fair rules, always!" Gao Zhenhua promised hastily.

Then, cautiously, Gao Zhenhua shifted his eyes one step to the right.

He looked at Chen Xuan.

Just ten minutes ago, the municipal deputy head had screamed at him over the phone to open Pier Nine before five o'clock and make sure the gentleman from Hengxin Logistics was not kept waiting a single extra minute.

"Excuse me, Sir," Gao Zhenhua said respectfully, lowering his voice and nodding politely to Chen Xuan. "The six containers for Hengxin Logistics are flagged for priority transfer. Our dispatchers will see them onto your company trucks within the hour. Is there anything else our port office can assist you with today?"

Chen Xuan gave a small, easy nod. "That is plenty. Thank you for running all the way down here, Director Gao."

"Not at all, not at all! It is my duty!" Gao Zhenhua replied, secretly letting out a huge sigh of relief when he saw no anger on Chen Xuan's face.

Ten meters away, beside the black luxury sedan, Shen Jingzhou had not moved an inch.

His tailored light-gray linen suit was wrinkled from knocking over his chair, and his hair hung damp over his pale forehead. He stared at the wide-open iron gate, at the towering red cranes lifting Haiyue's containers one after another, and at Chief Inspector Lu Guobin standing slumped in the corner like a beaten dog.

On the hot concrete beside Shen Jingzhou's leather shoe, his cracked gold phone began to ring again. The screen lit up with his grandfather's name, vibrating angrily against the cement.

Shen Jingzhou did not even reach down to pick it up. His fingers were shaking too hard.

Slowly, he took two unsteady steps forward toward Chen Xuan. His six broad-shouldered bodyguards did not dare follow him; they stood stiffly by the car, avoiding Chen Xuan's gaze altogether.

"Wait," Shen Jingzhou croaked, his voice hoarse and thin. All the loud arrogance from thirty minutes ago had vanished without a trace. "Wait a second! Who are you?"

Chen Xuan paused. He lifted his green thermos, unscrewed the dented lid, and took one slow, unhurried sip of warm jasmine tea. Then he screwed the cap back on with a crisp metallic click.

"I already told you," Chen Xuan said calmly. "I work in the shipping office at Hengxin Logistics."

"Stop lying to me!" Shen Jingzhou shouted, though his voice cracked with fear rather than anger. "An office clerk from Shanghai cannot make China Construction Bank, ICBC, and Huaxia Bank freeze 12 billion yuan in credit lines at the exact same minute! An office clerk cannot make Director Gao Zhenhua sprint across Pier Nine in a sweat-soaked shirt! Just tell me who you really are! If my family offended someone from Beijing, tell me whose door my grandfather needs to kneel at!"

Chen Xuan looked at Shen Jingzhou's trembling hands and pale face with mild, quiet eyes.

"I did not ask your grandfather to kneel anywhere," Chen Xuan said lightly. "I only asked you to open the gate before five o'clock. Now the gate is open, and your cranes are working. Oh, and remind Shen Wanshan about his promise earlier. Though the water in Shenzhen Bay looks a little chilly this evening, so maybe he should bring a towel."

Shen Jingzhou's mouth opened and closed without a sound. A cold shiver ran straight down his spine as he remembered his grandfather's laughing boast twenty minutes ago.

At that moment, the cracked gold phone on the concrete stopped ringing, then immediately started buzzing again. Shen Jingzhou's assistant rushed over, picked it up with pale hands, and whispered, "Young Master, it is Chairman Shen again. He says the Shenzhen Bay No. 1 loan office just called him."

Shen Jingzhou stumbled back a step, snatching the phone as if it were a burning coal. He did not even look up again as he pressed it to his ear and began stammering apologies to his furious grandfather.

Qin Yuhan watched the scene for two seconds, then turned smoothly to Manager Zhou Ming.

"Manager Zhou, stay here and oversee the full unloading of the Haiyue Star," Qin Yuhan instructed crisply. "Make sure Hengxin Logistics' six containers clear customs first and send me the final manifest tonight."

"Yes, President Qin! Leave it to me!" Zhou Ming answered with fresh energy. He glanced sideways at Chen Xuan with deep, quiet awe. He still did not know what kind of connections this plain clerk held, only that standing behind him felt safer than standing behind an army.

Chen Xuan checked his simple black wristwatch. The minute hand sat straight up on the twelve.

Five o'clock sharp.

He tucked his cloth folder neatly under his arm, shifted his green thermos into his left hand, and looked over at Qin Yuhan with an easy, relaxed smile.

"It is five o'clock," Chen Xuan said lightly. "Let us go find some Cantonese roast goose."

For a second, Qin Yuhan blinked at the sudden shift from a 12 billion yuan financial storm to dinner plans. Then a soft, genuine laugh escaped her lips, brightening her cool face completely.

"All right, Mr. Chen," Qin Yuhan smiled, gesturing toward the waiting silver sedan. "I know a great place near MixC Mall in Nanshan. My treat tonight."

Together, they walked calmly to the silver sedan and climbed inside, leaving Shenzhen Pier Nine bustling with noise behind them and the proud heir of the Shen Family staring helplessly at his ringing phone.
