# Chapter 101: The Husband Who Rides the Subway

At seven-thirty on Wednesday morning, the kitchen at Emerald Riverside Villa smelled of toasted sesame and hot jasmine tea.

Chen Xuan stood by the stove in a soft gray cotton sweater, flipping two golden scallion pancakes in a flat pan while his battered green enamel thermos sat open on the marble counter, catching steam from the kettle.

Behind him, crisp high heels clicked down the staircase.

Qin Yuhan walked into the kitchen wearing a sharp ivory blazer and tailored navy trousers. Her long dark hair was pinned up neatly, making her look every bit the cool, decisive president of Haiyue Group. In her right hand, however, she held two leather key fobs that jingled softly.

She stopped beside the kitchen island, set the two keys on the polished marble, and slid them across toward Chen Xuan.

"Pick one," Qin Yuhan said, trying to sound strictly businesslike even as her eyes drifted toward the sizzling skillet.

Chen Xuan flipped a pancake onto a porcelain plate and glanced down at the keys. One bore the silver three-pointed emblem of a Maybach sedan. The other had the gold crest of a Porsche coupe.

"Are you opening a car rental desk in the kitchen, President Qin?" Chen Xuan asked, picking up the teapot to fill his green thermos.

"The Maybach and the white Porsche in the east garage have been sitting idle all month," Qin Yuhan said, crossing her arms lightly. "Yesterday afternoon I saw you walking up the driveway from the bus stop carrying two bags of groceries. You are my husband now, Chen Xuan. You do not need to squeeze onto Metro Line 2 every morning like an intern."

Chen Xuan screwed the lid onto his green thermos and gave it an affectionate pat. "Squeeze? Yuhan, if you catch the seven-fifty train at the rear door of the third carriage, there is always an empty corner seat right next to the window. The air conditioning hits just right, and it takes twenty-two minutes to reach my stop. That is my prime morning nap."

Qin Yuhan blinked at him, half amused and half exasperated. "You are turning down a Maybach worth 3 million yuan and a Porsche worth 2 million yuan for a subway corner seat?"

"Have you seen the Century Avenue roundabout in Lujiazui at eight in the morning?" Chen Xuan asked, sliding the hot scallion pancakes, two poached eggs, and a few slices of sweet glazed beef into a warm glass breakfast box. "Every driver in those luxury cars looks like they are about to chew through their steering wheel. If I drive, I have to watch the traffic lights, dodge delivery scooters, and pay 1,200 yuan a month for company parking. On the subway, the driver does all the work for four yuan."

"Haiyue Group can reimburse four yuan," Qin Yuhan muttered, though the corner of her mouth twitched upward.

"Save the company budget," Chen Xuan smiled, snapping the lid onto the glass breakfast box and slipping it into a clean cloth bag. He walked around the counter and handed the bag to her. "Here. Two scallion pancakes, two eggs, and the spiced beef you liked last night. I wrapped a warm towel around the box so it stays hot until you reach Haiyue Tower."

Qin Yuhan took the cloth bag. The gentle warmth seeping through the fabric made her fingers curl softly around the handles. Since taking over Haiyue Group three years ago, her mornings usually consisted of cold black coffee in the back seat of her car while reviewing shipping spreadsheets. Having someone hand her a hot homemade breakfast before work felt strangely comforting.

"Did you eat already?" she asked, looking up at him.

"I ate three pancakes while the kettle was boiling," Chen Xuan chuckled, picking up his canvas shoulder bag and tucking his green thermos into the side pocket. "Do not skip the eggs today. Last night your stomach was growling on the sofa by six o'clock."

A faint pink rose on Qin Yuhan's cheeks. "That was not my stomach. That was the floor heating pipe."

"Sure it was," Chen Xuan grinned, picking up the Maybach and Porsche keys and placing them gently back into her palm. "Keep your fancy toys warm in the garage. If you really want to spoil your 12,000-yuan logistics clerk, come home before six tonight. I bought fresh river shrimp yesterday."

Qin Yuhan closed her fingers over the keys and watched him stroll toward the front door with his canvas bag slung casually over one shoulder. "I have a port finance meeting at four, so I will be back by five-forty. And do not put too much ginger in the shrimp!"

"Just two slices, President Qin!" Chen Xuan called back with a lazy wave as he stepped out into the bright morning sun.

Ten meters down the quiet tree-lined lane outside the villa gates, a plain black sedan sat parked beside a flower bed. Inside, two members of the Black Order in dark civilian jackets checked the road ahead through their mirrors, keeping their permanent fifty-meter silent perimeter around Chen Xuan as he strolled toward the subway station whistling a tune.

Nine hundred kilometers to the north in Beijing, the morning atmosphere inside a quiet conference room at the State Council was considerably more solemn.

Premier Gu Chengshan and Elder Song sat across from each other with two thick blue folders open on the table. The reports detailed the first closed pre-trial hearings for former ministry division director Zhao Dehan and former Shanshui Group chairman Zhao Ruilong, along with the full accounting of the 300 trillion yuan legal defense fund that had already cleared the backlog of over four thousand wrongfully detained small shopkeepers and citizens across twelve provinces.

At the end of the table, Number 1 stood in his crisp white suit, checking a short message on his white phone before slipping it back into his pocket.

"The four national banks confirmed that every local compensation payout was completed before dawn," Elder Song said quietly, adjusting his glasses as he looked at Number 1. "And how is our young benefactor in Shanghai this morning? Did the Handong transfers disturb his schedule?"

"Not at all, Elder Song," Number 1 replied with a faint, respectful smile. "He just turned down two luxury cars so he would not miss his morning nap on Metro Line 2."

Gu Chengshan let out a soft, genuine laugh and closed his folder. "Good. Let him enjoy his twenty-two minutes on the subway. Keeping the country clean enough for a good man to nap in peace is our job."
