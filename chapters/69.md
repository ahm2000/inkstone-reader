# Chapter 69: Qingyan Municipal Emergency Session

At eight o'clock sharp on Monday morning, the third-floor emergency briefing room of the Qingyan Municipal Government felt like a boiler room about to burst. Thick gray cigarette smoke hung across the long table, veiling the pale faces of twelve department heads hauled out of their morning commutes on ten minutes' notice.

At the head of the table, Qingyan Municipal Party Secretary Lin Haibo crushed his third cigarette in fifteen minutes and tapped a fourth from a rumpled pack. His collar button was undone, his jaw clenched until his teeth ground audibly.

Beside him, Qingyan Mayor Wu Yongqiang wore a stiff, forced smile that never touched his eyes, his thumb rubbing nervously along his porcelain mug.

"Where is Customs Director Xu Wenbin?" Lin Haibo snapped, voice raw from smoke. "His deputy told my secretary at seven thirty that Xu Wenbin never reached the office and his home phone is dead. Did nobody in Qingyan check on a bureau director before eight?"

Two seats down, Qingyan Municipal Public Security Bureau Deputy Chief Tang Weiming cleared his throat uneasily and pushed a folded note across the table.

"Secretary Lin Haibo, Mayor Wu Yongqiang," Tang Weiming said tightly. "I sent two plainclothes officers past Xu Wenbin's compound at seven forty. The gate guard reported three black sedans with Beijing central plates pulled in just past midnight. Men in dark suits showed central discipline credentials, escorted Xu Wenbin out in his coat, and drove off without notifying our bureau or Lanshui."

Wu Yongqiang's forced smile cracked clean off his face. His hand froze on his mug. "Midnight? Central discipline officers took Qingyan's Customs Director from his home at midnight without a single courtesy call to the Municipal Party Committee?"

"That is not the worst of it, Mayor Wu Yongqiang!" Port Authority Director Qian Zhigang blurted, half-rising from his seat. His hands shook so hard his elbow clipped his teacup, spilling hot tea straight across his briefing folder. He did not even wipe the soaking sheets. "At seven thirty, forty central customs inspectors backed by armed naval security rolled through Qingyan Port's main freight gate! No municipal permits, no warning call to my office!"

Lin Haibo's cigarette stopped halfway to his lips. "Armed naval security inside a municipal port? What are they seizing, Qian Zhigang?"

"Pier Seven!" Qian Zhigang gasped, sweat trickling down his nose. "They sealed every crane and bonded warehouse on Pier Seven, ordered dockworkers off the apron at gunpoint, and kicked my IT supervisor out of the Port Authority data center! They have taken physical control of Qingyan Port's entire digital manifest server room!"

Raw panic ripped around the table like a live wire.

Municipal Commerce Director Feng Jianhua jerked upright, scraping his chair back. "The digital manifest server room? Five years of bonded cargo logs, customs stamps, and tax rebate records sit on those servers! If central customs audits Pier Seven's manifests, half the maritime logistics firms in Qingyan will freeze before noon!"

"Forget the logistics firms, Feng Jianhua!" Lin Haibo barked, slamming his lighter onto the wood so hard it skittered across the table. "Xu Wenbin vanishes at midnight. Armed naval security seizes Pier Seven at seven thirty. Why Pier Seven? Who holds the main bonded warehouse lease on Pier Seven?"

Suffocating silence dropped over the room. Every official stared down at the tabletop.

Wu Yongqiang's face tightened, sweat catching the light along his temples. He turned slowly toward Qian Zhigang. "Answer Secretary Lin Haibo, Qian Zhigang. Which shipping group leases Pier Seven?"

Qian Zhigang swallowed dryly, his throat clicking in the quiet room. "Haiyuan Coastal Logistics, Mayor Wu Yongqiang. Along with three affiliated freight forwarding firms registered in Yunxi County."

"Haiyuan Coastal Logistics is a shell front for Chairman Deng Yongchang!" Municipal Finance Director Zhou Liqiang burst out, eyes wide with dread. "Everyone in the trade bureau knows Deng Yongchang runs Pier Seven's midnight container transfers! Last year when my auditors flagged forty million yuan in missing tariff receipts from Pier Seven, Xu Wenbin told us to drop it because the exemption was cleared from upstairs!"

Wu Yongqiang slapped his palm flat against the table. "Watch your mouth, Zhou Liqiang! Who told you I touched tariff exemptions for Deng Yongchang? Customs answers vertically to Beijing, not my mayor's office!"

Qian Zhigang's face went chalk-white as he stared at his tea-soaked folder, lower lip trembling. "Mayor Wu Yongqiang, I am not accusing anyone. It is only that central customs inspectors asked my deputy who signed the municipal green-channel fast-track permits for Pier Seven's night barges over the past two years."

"And what did your deputy tell them?" Wu Yongqiang demanded, voice rising half an octave while his knuckles turned white on the table edge.

"He told them the truth, Mayor Wu Yongqiang," Qian Zhigang whispered miserably. "Customs Director Xu Wenbin cleared the customs slips, and the municipal fast-track approvals bore the red stamp of the Mayor's Deputy General Office."

Silence hit like a dropped safe.

Lin Haibo turned slowly to stare at Wu Yongqiang through thick blue smoke, his eyes cold and narrow. "Wu Yongqiang, you told the Standing Committee in March that Pier Seven's night channel carried perishable seafood exports. Did you read the sub-manifests before your deputy office stamped them?"

"I sign two hundred approvals a week, Lin Haibo!" Wu Yongqiang shot back, composure shattering as he threw his fountain pen onto his blotter. "You think I pry open shipping crates at two in the morning? Xu Wenbin vouched for Haiyuan Coastal Logistics! Vice Governor Lin Zhengde's office in Lanshui called my secretary twice telling us not to delay coastal trade throughput! If anyone set this fire, it was Lanshui and Customs, not me!"

"Do not use Vice Governor Lin Zhengde as a shield," Tang Weiming muttered darkly, glancing toward the closed doors. "I called Lanshui's Provincial General Office ten minutes ago to ask about the naval units at Pier Seven. The line rang twenty times and nobody answered."

Before Lin Haibo could snap back, the double doors swung open without a knock.

A lean man in a dark windbreaker walked in, flanked by two uniformed officers wearing provincial discipline badges. Every voice died mid-sentence.

The liaison officer strode to the head of the table, placed a single white notice in front of Lin Haibo, and spoke flatly.

"By order of Lanshui Provincial Party Secretary Zhao Dehai and the Central Joint Inspection Group," the officer stated. "Effective immediately, all Qingyan municipal officials at or above deputy bureau level are barred from leaving Qingyan City limits pending central audit interviews. Passports, travel permits, and official vehicles are suspended. Remain in this building until your names are logged."

The officer turned and walked out, shutting the doors with a heavy click.

Nobody moved. Wu Yongqiang stared at the white slip as if it were a death warrant, breathing fast and shallow.

Across the table, Qian Zhigang's forgotten cigarette burned straight down to the filter between his shaking fingers. A sharp sting pierced his skin. He flinched violently, yelped once, and dropped the glowing stub into his lap, slapping frantically at his trousers while eleven ash-pale municipal officials watched him through the choking smoke.
