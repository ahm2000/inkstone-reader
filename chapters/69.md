# Chapter 69: Qingyan Municipal Emergency Session

At eight o'clock sharp on Monday morning, the third-floor emergency meeting room of the Qingyan City Government felt like a boiler about to blow. Thick gray cigarette smoke hung over the long table, hiding the pale faces of twelve department heads pulled off their morning drive with ten minutes' warning.

At the head of the table, Qingyan Municipal Party Secretary Lin Haibo crushed his third cigarette in fifteen minutes and tapped a fourth out of a crumpled pack. His top collar button was undone, and his jaw clenched so tight his teeth ground together.

Beside him, Qingyan Mayor Wu Yongqiang wore a stiff, fake smile that never reached his eyes. His thumb rubbed nervously up and down his porcelain mug.

"Where is Customs Director Xu Wenbin?" Lin Haibo snapped, his voice rough from smoke. "His deputy told my secretary at seven thirty that Xu Wenbin never made it to the office and his home phone is dead. Did nobody in Qingyan check on a bureau director before eight?"

Two seats down, Qingyan Municipal Public Security Bureau Deputy Chief Tang Weiming cleared his throat uneasily and slid a folded note across the table.

"Secretary Lin Haibo, Mayor Wu Yongqiang," Tang Weiming said tightly. "I sent two plainclothes officers past Xu Wenbin's compound at seven forty. The gate guard said three black sedans with Beijing central plates pulled in just after midnight. Men in dark suits showed central discipline badges, walked Xu Wenbin out in his coat, and drove off without telling our bureau or Lanshui."

Wu Yongqiang's fake smile vanished right off his face. His hand froze on his mug. "Midnight? Central discipline officers took Qingyan's Customs Director from his home at midnight without a single warning call to the City Party Committee?"

"That is not the worst of it, Mayor Wu Yongqiang!" Port Authority Director Qian Zhigang blurted, half-standing from his chair. His hands shook so badly his elbow knocked his teacup, spilling hot tea straight across his folder. He did not even wipe the wet pages. "At seven thirty, forty central customs inspectors backed by armed naval guards rolled through Qingyan Port's main truck gate! No city permits, and no call to my office!"

Lin Haibo's cigarette stopped halfway to his lips. "Armed naval guards inside a city port? What are they seizing, Qian Zhigang?"

"Pier Seven!" Qian Zhigang gasped, sweat running down his nose. "They locked down every crane and customs warehouse on Pier Seven, ordered dockworkers off the deck at gunpoint, and kicked my computer manager out of the Port Authority server room! They have taken total control of Qingyan Port's entire shipping computer room!"

Pure panic shot around the table like a live wire.

Municipal Commerce Director Feng Jianhua jerked upright, scraping his chair back. "The shipping computer room? Five years of cargo lists, customs stamps, and tax refund records sit on those machines! If central customs checks Pier Seven's records, half the shipping companies in Qingyan will freeze before noon!"

"Forget the shipping companies, Feng Jianhua!" Lin Haibo barked, slamming his lighter onto the wood so hard it slid across the table. "Xu Wenbin vanishes at midnight. Armed naval guards seize Pier Seven at seven thirty. Why Pier Seven? Who holds the main customs warehouse lease on Pier Seven?"

A heavy, choking silence fell over the room. Every official stared down at the table.

Wu Yongqiang's face tightened, sweat shining along his temples. He turned slowly toward Qian Zhigang. "Answer Secretary Lin Haibo, Qian Zhigang. Which shipping group rents Pier Seven?"

Qian Zhigang swallowed hard, his throat clicking in the quiet room. "Haiyuan Coastal Logistics, Mayor Wu Yongqiang. Along with three partner freight companies registered in Yunxi County."

"Haiyuan Coastal Logistics is a shell front for Chairman Deng Yongchang!" Municipal Finance Director Zhou Liqiang burst out, his eyes wide with fear. "Everyone in the trade bureau knows Deng Yongchang runs Pier Seven's midnight container swaps! Last year when my auditors found 40 million yuan in missing customs taxes from Pier Seven, Xu Wenbin told us to drop it because the break was approved from upstairs!"

Wu Yongqiang slapped his palm flat against the table. "Watch your mouth, Zhou Liqiang! Who told you I touched tax breaks for Deng Yongchang? Customs answers straight to Beijing, not to my mayor's office!"

Qian Zhigang's face turned chalk-white as he stared at his tea-soaked folder, his lower lip shaking. "Mayor Wu Yongqiang, I am not blaming anyone. It is just that central customs inspectors asked my deputy who signed the city fast-track passes for Pier Seven's night barges over the past two years."

"And what did your deputy tell them?" Wu Yongqiang demanded, his voice rising sharp as his knuckles turned white on the table edge.

"He told them the truth, Mayor Wu Yongqiang," Qian Zhigang whispered miserably. "Customs Director Xu Wenbin cleared the customs papers, and the city fast-track passes carried the red stamp of the Mayor's Deputy General Office."

Silence hit like a dropped safe.

Lin Haibo turned slowly to stare at Wu Yongqiang through the thick blue smoke, his eyes cold and narrow. "Wu Yongqiang, you told the Standing Committee in March that Pier Seven's night lane carried fresh seafood exports. Did you read the cargo lists before your deputy office stamped them?"

"I sign two hundred approvals a week, Lin Haibo!" Wu Yongqiang shot back, losing his cool as he threw his fountain pen onto his desk pad. "You think I pry open shipping crates at two in the morning? Xu Wenbin vouched for Haiyuan Coastal Logistics! Vice Governor Lin Zhengde's office in Lanshui called my secretary twice telling us not to slow down coastal shipping! If anyone started this fire, it was Lanshui and Customs, not me!"

"Do not hide behind Vice Governor Lin Zhengde," Tang Weiming muttered darkly, glancing toward the shut doors. "I called Lanshui's Provincial General Office ten minutes ago to ask about the naval guards at Pier Seven. The phone rang twenty times and nobody picked up."

Before Lin Haibo could snap back, the double doors swung open without a knock.

A lean man in a dark jacket walked in, with two uniformed officers wearing provincial discipline badges beside him. Every voice died mid-sentence.

The officer walked straight to the head of the table, set a single white notice in front of Lin Haibo, and spoke flatly.

"By order of Lanshui Provincial Party Secretary Zhao Dehai and the Central Joint Inspection Group," the officer said. "Starting right now, all Qingyan city officials at or above deputy bureau level are barred from leaving Qingyan City limits until central audit interviews are done. Passports, travel passes, and official cars are suspended. Stay in this building until your names are logged."

The officer turned and walked out, shutting the doors with a heavy click.

Nobody moved. Wu Yongqiang stared at the white slip as if it were a death sentence, breathing fast and shallow.

Across the table, Qian Zhigang's forgotten cigarette burned all the way down to the filter between his shaking fingers. A sharp burn stung his skin. He flinched hard, let out a sharp yelp, and dropped the glowing butt into his lap, slapping wildly at his trousers while eleven pale city officials watched him through the choking smoke.
