# Chapter 112: Building 2 Welcomes the Daughter-in-Law

Late that Saturday morning, a clean blue taxi pulled up beneath the golden plane trees outside Building 2 of Fuxing New Village in Yunxi County. a short distance behind the brick community gate, a plain black sedan parked quietly beside a small corner newsstand as two members of the Black Order took their posts without drawing a single glance.

Before Chen Xuan even finished paying the cab fare, a cheerful voice rang out from the sixth-floor balcony above.

"Old Chen! Mingde! Come look! Little Xuan and Yuhan are right downstairs!"

Up on the sixth-floor balcony, Liu Meifang was leaning over the railing and waving a bright cotton kitchen towel with a huge smile on her face. Seconds later, the heavy metal security door at the bottom of the stairwell banged open as Chen Jianguo and Second Uncle Chen Mingde hurried down the concrete steps four at a time to help carry the bags.

By the time Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan reached the third-floor landing, the two middle-aged men met them with beaming faces.

"Uncle Chen, Second Uncle!" Qin Yuhan greeted them warmly, bowing slightly with a bright, sincere smile.

"Aiya, Yuhan! Look how polite you are!" Chen Jianguo laughed heartily, wiping his hands on his trousers before reaching for the wooden gift boxes in Chen Xuan's arms. "Give those to me, you lazy boy. Why are you making Yuhan climb six floors right after getting off the train?"

"I offered to carry her up, Dad," Chen Xuan said calmly. "She said the neighbors would laugh."

"Do not listen to him, Uncle," Qin Yuhan said, her cheeks turning a soft pink as she laughed. "The stairs are not high at all."

Second Uncle Chen Mingde chuckled as he grabbed the remaining bags. "Little Xuan has always had a smooth tongue. Come on up, your mother and aunt have been cooking since early morning."

When they stepped into the sixth-floor apartment, the warm, rich aroma of braised pork belly, lotus root rib soup, and freshly pan-fried dumplings wrapped around them immediately. The living room was modest and spotless, with crisp autumn sunlight streaming across the polished terrazzo floor and a fresh vase of yellow chrysanthemums sitting on the television cabinet.

Liu Meifang and Second Aunt Wu Lanying hurried out of the kitchen, wiping their hands on their floral aprons. The moment Liu Meifang saw Qin Yuhan standing in the doorway in her soft cream sweater, her eyes lit up with pure delight.

"Yuhan, you are finally here!" Liu Meifang stepped forward and clasped both of Qin Yuhan's hands in her warm palms. "Look at you, even prettier than in the photos Little Xuan sent us. Were you tired on the train? Come sit on the sofa right now."

"Auntie, Second Aunt, I am not tired at all," Qin Yuhan said gently. "Thank you for going to so much trouble to cook for us."

From the doorway of the small study room, two teenage boys peeked out shyly. Chen Yuan and Chen Hao were both wearing neat autumn tracksuits, their math workbooks still open on the desk behind them.

"Sister-in-law!" the two boys called out together in loud, eager voices.

Qin Yuhan smiled warmly and opened the gift bags she had brought from Shanghai. She handed the West Lake Longjing tea to Chen Jianguo, placed the soft silk scarves into Liu Meifang's and Wu Lanying's hands, and passed the bags of Shanghai fruit candies and sleek fountain pens to Chen Yuan and Chen Hao.

"Aiya, why did you spend so much money?" Liu Meifang protested, though she could not stop touching the smooth silk scarf with a proud smile. "Just coming home to eat a meal with us is enough!"

"They are not expensive, Auntie," Chen Xuan chimed in smoothly, setting his dented green thermos on the coffee table. "Yuhan's company gave out autumn holiday vouchers, so she picked these up at a staff discount."

Qin Yuhan glanced at her husband with an amused flicker in her eyes and played along without missing a beat. "Yes, Auntie. Please keep it. The autumn wind in Yunxi County is getting chilly in the mornings, and this will keep your neck warm."

Wu Lanying patted Liu Meifang's shoulder and laughed. "Listen to your daughter-in-law, sister. She is so thoughtful. Let us put the dishes on the table before the soup cools down."

Everyone crowded around the large folding wooden dining table in the living room. There were twelve home-cooked dishes arranged tightly across the surface, from golden braised pork ribs and steamed river fish to crisp stir-fried greens and two steaming plates of plump pork-and-chive dumplings.

Chen Jianguo poured small cups of warm local rice wine for himself, Chen Mingde, and Chen Xuan, while Liu Meifang poured hot jujube tea for Qin Yuhan. Before Qin Yuhan had even finished her first dumpling, Liu Meifang had already placed three pieces of braised ribs, two peeled river shrimp, and a spoonful of tender lotus root into her bowl.

"Eat more, Yuhan!" Liu Meifang urged happily. "You are far too thin. Shanghai food is too light, so eat plenty while you are home."

Qin Yuhan did not act delicate or distant for a single second. She tasted the braised lotus root, her eyes widening with genuine pleasure. "Auntie, this is delicious! The broth is so rich and sweet. Could you teach me how you season it before we leave?"

Liu Meifang beamed from ear to ear. "Of course I will! It is easy. By the way, Yuhan, tell me honestly. Does Little Xuan behave himself in Shanghai? Does he work hard at his logistics company, or is he still lazy?"

Chen Xuan paused with his chopsticks mid-air and looked at his wife with a lazy grin.

Qin Yuhan set her bowl down and smiled softly. "Auntie, do not worry. He works very steadily on the 12th floor of Hengxin Logistics, comes home right on time every evening, and makes the best braised pork ribs in all of Shanghai. Even my grandfather praises his cooking and plays chess with him every week."

"Really?" Chen Jianguo laughed loudly, raising his wine cup. "Our lazy boy actually learned how to take care of a wife! Come, let us toast to Little Xuan and Yuhan!"

As the glasses clinked and laughter filled the sixth-floor apartment, Chen Xuan quietly peeled another shrimp and dropped it into Qin Yuhan's bowl. Watching his wife chat effortlessly with his mother and aunt, he felt a warmth deeper than any wealth in the world could buy.
