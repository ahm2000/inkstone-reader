# Chapter 112: Building 2 Welcomes the Daughter-in-Law

At ten-twenty on Saturday morning, a clean blue taxi pulled up beneath the plane trees outside Building 2 of Fuxing New Village in Yunxi County. Fifty meters behind the community gate, a plain black sedan parked quietly by the newsstand as two members of the Black Order took their posts.

Before Chen Xuan even finished paying the cab fare, a cheerful voice rang out from the sixth-floor balcony above.

"Old Chen! Mingde! Come look! Little Xuan and Yuhan are right downstairs!"

Up on the sixth-floor balcony, Liu Meifang was waving a bright kitchen towel with a huge smile on her face, while Chen Jianguo and Second Uncle Chen Mingde hurried down the stairwell four steps at a time to help carry the bags.

By the time Chen Xuan and Qin Yuhan reached the third-floor landing, Chen Jianguo and Chen Mingde met them with beaming faces.

"Uncle Chen, Second Uncle!" Qin Yuhan greeted them warmly, bowing slightly with a bright, sweet smile as she held out two red-ribbon pastry boxes with both hands. "Chen Xuan wanted to carry everything himself, so I only managed to steal these two light pastry boxes from him."

"Aiya, Yuhan! Look how polite and pretty our new daughter-in-law is!" Chen Mingde laughed heartily, taking the larger gift bags from Chen Xuan's hands. "Little Xuan, let your dad and me carry the boxes. Do not let Yuhan get tired climbing six floors!"

Up at the sixth-floor doorway, Liu Meifang and Second Aunt Wu Lanying were already waiting with warm indoor slippers laid out neatly on the mat. Behind them stood the eighteen-year-old twins, Chen Yuan and Chen Hao, both craning their necks curiously in their blue Yunxi First High School track jackets.

"Mom, Second Aunt, I brought your daughter-in-law home," Chen Xuan announced cheerfully, setting his green thermos on the shoe cabinet.

"Mom! Second Aunt!" Qin Yuhan called out clearly, without the slightest hint of big-city distance. Her voice was warm and sweet as she handed the pastry boxes to Liu Meifang and Wu Lanying. "I am sorry Chen Xuan and I were busy with work in Shanghai after getting our certificate and could not visit you right away. Chen Xuan told me Mom loves red-date walnut pastries and Dad likes Tieguanyin tea, so I picked these out on Nanjing Road yesterday."

Liu Meifang was so happy when she heard Qin Yuhan call her "Mom" right at the front door that her eyes crinkled up. She immediately caught Qin Yuhan's slim hands and pulled a thick red envelope from her apron pocket.

"Good child! What a sensible girl!" Liu Meifang beamed, pressing the plump red envelope into Qin Yuhan's hands before leading her to the sofa. "Come sit down! When Little Xuan sent us your marriage photo last month, his dad and I looked at it for three hours and wondered how our lazy boy managed to marry a girl who looks like a fairy!"

"Mom, Chen Xuan takes wonderful care of me every day in Shanghai," Qin Yuhan smiled, accepting the red envelope respectfully before looking over at the two twins standing by the bookshelf. "You must be Chen Yuan and Chen Hao! Chen Xuan told me on the train that both of you are preparing for the June entrance exam and want to come to Fudan University."

Chen Yuan's cheeks turned pink with excitement. "Sister-in-Law! Auntie Liu said you graduated at the top of your class from Fudan University! Is the library on Handan Road really open until ten-thirty every night?"

"Not only is the main library open until ten-thirty," Qin Yuhan laughed, pulling two gold-plated Fudan University fountain pens and two campus notebooks from her bag and handing them to Chen Yuan and Chen Hao, "the third-floor quiet room has the best morning sunlight. I picked up these campus pens and notebooks for you from the Fudan University alumni store this week. When both of you pass the exam and come to Shanghai in September, your brother and I will pick you up at the station and treat you to a big dinner on the Bund!"

"Thank you, Sister-in-Law!" Chen Hao and Chen Yuan cheered in unison, holding the Fudan University pens like priceless treasures.

In the kitchen, the fragrant steam of braised pork ribs and freshly boiled chicken soup floated out into the living room. Liu Meifang patted Qin Yuhan's shoulder warmly. "Yuhan, you sit here and eat fruit with Chen Yuan and Chen Hao while your Second Aunt and I roll out the dumpling wrappers!"

Before Liu Meifang took two steps, Qin Yuhan had already taken off her cream cardigan, rolled up her sleeves, and followed her right to the kitchen doorway.

"Mom, let me help roll the dough or pinch the dumplings," Qin Yuhan said brightly, tying a spare blue apron around her waist. "Chen Xuan taught me how to pinch cabbage-and-pork dumplings at home last month. I want to learn how Mom makes the filling so fragrant!"

Wu Lanying looked at Liu Meifang in pure admiration. "Sister-in-Law, look at Yuhan! Not a single bit of rich-girl pride, and look how fast her hands pinch those pleats!"

"I learned from a strict teacher at home," Qin Yuhan giggled, winking at Chen Xuan, who was leaning against the kitchen counter peeling garlic cloves.

Ten minutes later, eight steaming dishes and two big platters of white dumplings were set out on the round dining table. Chen Jianguo poured small cups of plum wine and fruit juice for everyone, cleared his throat like a retired schoolteacher giving an important lecture, and raised his glass.

"Today is the happiest day our sixth-floor home has seen in twenty years!" Chen Jianguo declared proudly, looking around the crowded table. "Wait, as the old folk proverb says: 'When a gentle spring breeze blows from Shanghai into Building 2, even the cabbages in the dumplings taste twice as sweet, and a lazy son finally looks pleasing to his father's eyes!'"

The entire table froze for one second before bursting into loud laughter.

"Dad!" Chen Xuan protested around a laugh, picking up a dumpling for Qin Yuhan's bowl. "You taught math for thirty-five years! Since when is there an old folk proverb that specifically mentions Building 2 and cabbages in dumplings? You clearly just made that up five seconds ago!"

"A proverb has to be invented by someone before it becomes an old proverb!" Chen Jianguo chuckled behind his teacup, completely unbothered as Liu Meifang heaped three prime braised ribs into Qin Yuhan's bowl.

Qin Yuhan laughed until her eyes curved into bright crescents, feeling a warm, joyful liveliness in this cozy sixty-square-meter apartment that she had never felt in any grand banquet hall in Shanghai or Beijing.
