# Chapter 215: The Green Channel Opens

At 8:15 PM, the fog at Pudong Port Terminal Six was thick enough to swallow the tops of the gantry cranes. Inside Captain Lu's heated dockside office, however, the atmosphere was warm and cheerful.

Qian Weidong checked his watch and raised his paper cup of tea. "Forty-five minutes left until the 9:00 PM tide window closes, Captain Lu. Even if Haiyue Group somehow found a miracle right now, they could never clear harbor dispatch in time. To a very profitable winter."

Captain Lu laughed, raising his own cup to clink against Qian Weidong's. "To smooth cooperation, Director Qian. My ballast hold is airtight. Nobody at this port dares question my stamp."

Before the paper cups even touched, heavy, rapid footsteps echoed down the steel corridor outside.

Captain Lu frowned, lowering his cup. "Who is stomping around out there? I told Haiyue's shipping manager to stay off the stairs."

He never finished his sentence. The office door swung open with a sharp metallic bang against the wall.

Three men stepped inside, bringing a rush of freezing December air with them. In front stood Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau Chief Hou Liangping, his eyes razor-sharp beneath the bright fluorescent lights. Directly beside him were Maritime Commissioner Shen Bo of the Shanghai Maritime Safety Administration and Port Enforcement Captain Zhou Ming, flanked by uniformed maritime officers and discipline investigators.

Captain Lu froze. The paper cup slipped from his fingers, splashing hot tea across his trousers. All color drained from his face the moment he recognized Commissioner Shen Bo.

"Commissioner Shen!" Captain Lu stammered, his chair crashing backward against the cabinet. "What brings you to Terminal Six in this fog? I was just reviewing a routine ballast file with a logistics representative!"

Qian Weidong recovered faster, forcing a tight smile. "Gentlemen, there is a misunderstanding. I am Qian Weidong of Tianhai International. I only stopped by to ask about winter berth schedules."

He slid his hand toward the phone on the desk, hoping to text his contacts.

Hou Liangping stepped forward and pressed two laminated sheets directly over Qian Weidong's hand, pinning the phone down.

"Hands off the screen, Director Qian," Hou Liangping said coolly. "I am Hou Liangping from the Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau. Here is our joint detention warrant, and underneath it is the verified bank wire showing 500k yuan moving from Yongshun Marine Supplies into Captain Lu's brother-in-law's account forty-eight hours ago. Care to explain how a fake valve invoice buys a seventy-two-hour ballast hold on your rival's ship?"

Qian Weidong's confident mask shattered. His lips parted without a sound as his eyes darted toward the blocked doorway.

Maritime Commissioner Shen Bo picked up the red detention stamp and looked at Captain Lu with icy disgust.

"A fake ballast violation to choke a compliant export vessel?" Shen Bo's voice shook with quiet fury. "You took 500k yuan to drag Shanghai Port's name through the mud. Hand over your badge right now. You are suspended pending criminal prosecution."

Captain Lu's knees gave out as investigators locked steel handcuffs around his wrists. A second pair clicked shut on Qian Weidong, who began shouting frantic excuses nobody bothered to answer.

"Captain Zhou Ming," Commissioner Shen Bo ordered sharply. "Take the signed release order down to Berth Four yourself. Clear the Haiyue Star immediately, assign priority tugboats, and hand the sealed National VIP Green Channel certificate to Haiyue Group's port director on the pier."

"Right away, Commissioner!" Captain Zhou Ming saluted and jogged out into the mist.

Ten minutes later, as Qian Weidong and Captain Lu were led into enforcement vans, Captain Zhou Ming tore up the fake ballast notice at the gangway and waved a bright green clearance lamp.

At 8:40 PM, the deep foghorn of the Haiyue Star boomed across the harbor. Twin tugboats leaned into the winter tide, guiding the massive hull smoothly into the outbound channel with twenty minutes to spare.

Back at Emerald Riverside Villa, Qin Yuhan was curled up on the living room sofa in her cream sweater, anxiously peeling an orange wedge while glancing at the wall clock. It was 8:50 PM.

Her phone suddenly rang, vibrating loudly against the glass coffee table.

She snatched it up, her heart hammering. "Director Lin? Did the tugboats stand down?"

Instead of bad news, her port operations director's voice burst through the speaker, shaking with pure disbelief and joy. "President Qin! The Haiyue Star just cleared the outer channel! It is sailing on schedule!"

Qin Yuhan sat bolt upright, her eyes widening. "What? How?"

"You would not believe what just happened down here!" Director Lin nearly shouted over the harbor wind. "Maritime Commissioner Shen Bo and an anti-corruption chief from Handong kicked open Captain Lu's office twenty minutes ago! They arrested both Captain Lu and Qian Weidong on the spot for a 500k yuan bribe! And President Qin, that is not even the craziest part. Captain Zhou Ming just handed me an official sealed approval granting Haiyue Group a permanent National VIP Green Channel across every coastal port! Priority berthing, instant customs clearance, everything!"

Qin Yuhan stared blankly at the warm lamp across the room for two full seconds. When she finally hung up, a bright, breathless laugh broke from her lips.

Before Chen Xuan could even hand her a tissue for the orange juice on her fingers, she threw her arms around his neck and hugged him tightly against the sofa cushions.

"Xuan! The ship sailed! They arrested Qian Weidong and Captain Lu, and the Maritime Administration just gave Haiyue a permanent National VIP Green Channel!" she laughed, her eyes shining with relief and happiness. "Every single time I feel backed into a corner, I come home to your hot soup and somehow the whole sky clears before 9:00 PM!"

She pulled back just enough to pinch his cheek playfully, beaming at him. "Forget your twelfth-floor desk at Hengxin Logistics. My lazy husband is officially the greatest lucky charm alive."

Chen Xuan chuckled warmly, gently brushing a stray strand of hair behind her ear and handing her a peeled orange wedge. "Does that mean the lucky charm gets to sleep in until 7:30 AM tomorrow?"

"Deal," Qin Yuhan laughed, resting her head happily against his shoulder.

Outside the tall windows, the cold December fog drifted quietly over the river. Inside the warm villa, the tea was still hot, the soup pot was empty, and life remained sweet, peaceful, and completely unhurried.
