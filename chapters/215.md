# Chapter 215: The Green Channel Opens

Down at Pudong Port Terminal Six, the dockside operations office was filled with the pungent smoke of expensive Cuban cigars.

Qian Weidong poured another glass of scotch for Captain Lu, clinking his tumbler with an arrogant smirk. "Captain Lu, when Qin Yuhan calls back in an hour, tell her the environmental re-inspection fee is 5 million yuan. We will split it seventy-thirty."

Captain Lu grinned greedily, adjusting his gold shoulder epaulets. "Director Qian, working with you is always a pleasure. In Terminal Six, what I say is law! Not even the Mayor of Shanghai could force me to clear that ship without my official stamp, "

*BAM!*

The reinforced timber door of the office was kicked violently off its hinges, crashing against the interior drywall with a deafening thud!

Cold sea mist and the flashing strobe of red and blue emergency beacons flooded into the smoky room.

Qian Weidong jumped from the sofa, spilling his scotch across his expensive trousers. "What the hell is this? Who authorized you to kick my door, "

His voice died in his throat as a dozen armed federal officers in dark blue maritime tactical gear swept into the room, their assault carbines held at the low ready.

Behind them strode three figures whose appearance made Captain Lu's soul leave his body:
Director Hou Liangping of the Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau,
Commissioner Shen Bo of the Shanghai Maritime Safety Administration,
and Captain Zhou Ming of Shanghai State Security!

Commissioner Shen Bo's face was as dark as thunder as he marched directly up to Captain Lu's desk.

"Captain Lu!" Commissioner Shen boomed, his voice shaking the glass windows. "By joint emergency order of the State Council, the Supreme People's Procuratorate, and the Ministry of Transport, you are stripped of your inspector credentials with immediate effect!"

Before Captain Lu could stammer a single syllable, Captain Zhou Ming stepped forward, tore the gold epaulets straight off Lu's uniform shoulders, and slapped heavy steel handcuffs onto his wrists!

"Commissioner Shen! Director Hou!" Qian Weidong yelled, raising his hands in panic. "There is a misunderstanding! I am Qian Weidong of Tianhai International! We have legal container contracts with, "

"Save your explanations for the procuratorial tribunal, Qian Weidong!" Hou Liangping interrupted sharply, stepping forward with a red-stamped warrant. "We have seized the Changbai financial hard drives and traced your 180 million yuan extortion fund. You are under arrest for bribery, economic sabotage, and obstruction of national strategic maritime lanes. Take him!"

Two federal tactical officers seized Qian Weidong's arms, twisting them behind his back and forcing him face-first onto the carpet before hauling him out into the rain.

Commissioner Shen Bo turned to the harbor control technician who was trembling behind the console. "Clear the *Haiyue Star* immediately! Sound the departure horn, open the sea gates, and transmit National Green Channel Clearance Code Alpha-Zero across all coastal stations!"

Within two minutes, the deep, thunderous horn of the *Haiyue Star* blasted across the Pudong harbor basin, echoing through the night mist like a triumphant roar. The massive container ship cast off its mooring lines and began gliding smoothly into the deep-water channel, bound for Qingdao.

Fifteen kilometers away at Emerald Riverside Villa, Qin Yuhan answered her vibrating phone with a tense breath. "Director Zhang, what happened?"

On the other end of the line, her operations director was screaming with tears of sheer, ecstatic disbelief.

"President Qin! A miracle! A complete miracle! The Shanghai Maritime Commissioner personally arrived at Terminal Six with twenty armed federal officers! Captain Lu has been arrested on the spot! Qian Weidong was dragged away in handcuffs! And the Ministry of Transport just issued Haiyue Group a permanent National VIP Green Channel Permit! Our ship is underway, and all future Haiyue vessels are exempt from local berth inspections nationwide!"

Qin Yuhan's phone nearly slipped from her trembling fingers.

She sat frozen in her dining chair, staring into space with her mouth parted in utter astonishment.

"A permanent national green channel? Captain Lu arrested?" she whispered, her voice trembling with sheer joy.

Chen Xuan sat beside her, serenely eating a piece of winter melon, looking at her with mild curiosity. "Good news from the docks, Yuhan?"

Qin Yuhan snapped out of her daze, dropped her phone onto the table, and threw her arms around Chen Xuan's neck with a shriek of delight, smothering his cheeks in joyous kisses.

"Good news? It is an absolute miracle!" she cheered, her eyes shining like diamonds. "The bad inspector was arrested, the competitor was hauled away by federal agents, and our ship set sail on time! Xiao Xuan, you really are my lucky charm! Every time you tell me not to worry, all my problems magically disappear!"

Chen Xuan laughed softly, wrapping his arms around her waist and lifting her slightly off the chair, his green thermos resting peacefully on the table behind them.

"I told you," Chen Xuan smiled, his eyes warm and tender. "When a good wife eats her soup on time, good fortune always follows."
