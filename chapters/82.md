# Chapter 82: Fang Qiming and Lin Zhengde Turn on Each Other

Two floors below the Sha Provincial Disciplinary Annex, Room Three and Room Four shared a thick concrete wall, matching steel tables, and the sour smell of stale smoke.

In Room Three, Shengyuan Capital Chairman Fang Qiming sat with his fine wool jacket folded across his knees.

Guo Zhengming, Minister of Public Security, sat across from him.

"You told my officers forty minutes ago that Vice Governor Lin Zhengde was just a distant work contact," Guo Zhengming said. He lit a cigarette and blew smoke toward the vent. "You said you only saw him at business meetings."

Fang Qiming adjusted his shirt cuffs with a calm flick of his wrists. "That is the truth, Guo Zhengming. A private builder does not walk into a vice governor's office uninvited. If Lin Zhengde broke the rules in Sha City, blame his own greed, not Shengyuan Capital."

Guo Zhengming smiled without warmth and pressed play.

Static hissed for two seconds.

"You told me the Sha City wrecking job was clean, Fang Qiming! You swore the county land stamp would bury the tunnel entrance under an apartment block before the provincial survey team showed up! If they drill through the concrete floor on Parcel Nine, my head is on the block for your smuggling tunnel!"

Fang Qiming's hand froze halfway to his water glass. His eyes went wide as his own shouting voice answered from the speaker.

"My tunnel, Lin Zhengde? Whose signature cleared the rush city-rebuild permits across six provinces? You took twelve percent on every ton of pure rare-earth ore we moved out through those county wrecking sites! You think you can wash your hands at a helicopter pad?"

Guo Zhengming hit stop. The silence in Room Three felt heavy enough to crush them.

Fang Qiming's chest heaved. A vein bulged at his temple, and his neat fingernails dug deep marks into his palms. "He wore a wire?" Fang Qiming hissed, pure poison in his voice. "That spineless coward taped me at the landing pad to buy himself a lighter deal?"

"No," Guo Zhengming said calmly, tapping ash into the tray. "Neither of you wore a wire. That sound was picked up by long-range listening gear you did not know was there. Right now, next door, Lin Zhengde is listening to the exact same tape."

Fang Qiming stared at the blank wall between the two rooms, his lips pulling back in raw hatred. "Lin Zhengde will tell you I forced him. That is what officials always do when the roof caves in. They cry that the businessman led them astray."

"Then tell me the truth before he signs his statement," Guo Zhengming said, leaning back. "Parcel Nine. And the other five provinces."

Fang Qiming let out a sharp, bitter laugh. The smooth rich businessman vanished, replaced by a cornered beast. "Fine. If Lin Zhengde wants to burn, I will pour the fuel myself. Those county real-estate wrecking jobs were never about building homes, Guo Zhengming. Nobody puts up forty-story towers in half-empty mountain counties to make money."

"Go on," Guo Zhengming said, his eyes narrowing through the smoke.

"You need digging machines, blast permits, nonstop dump trucks, and tall metal fences that keep locals and satellites from asking questions," Fang Qiming said fast, leaning forward. "Every time Shengyuan Capital won a county rebuild bid, Lin Zhengde and his friends in five nearby provinces stamped a fast wrecking permit. Under the rubble of old cloth mills and grain sheds, we built heavy concrete underground storage rooms."

"Storage rooms for what?" Guo Zhengming pressed.

"Pure rare metals and top-grade aircraft metal stolen from state factories," Fang Qiming spat, his voice shaking with spite. "Road checkpoints scan metal trucks. Nobody scans twenty thousand tons of broken brick hauled out in sealed dump trucks registered to city trash offices. Lin Zhengde personally signed the cross-province trash permits that let our trucks roll through three toll roads without a single customs check."

Guo Zhengming stood up without another word, took the recorder, and stepped into Room Four thirty seconds later.

Vice Governor Lin Zhengde looked ten years older than he had that morning. Damp hair stuck to his forehead, and his hands gripped the steel table edge as if bracing for an earthquake. The young officer by the door had already played the helicopter tape twice.

"He is lying!" Lin Zhengde shouted the second Guo Zhengming sat down, his voice shrill and cracking. "Whatever Fang Qiming told you in Room Three, he is pinning his gang on me! I am a provincial official, Guo Zhengming! I approved city-rebuild plans to raise county tax money!"

Guo Zhengming lit a fresh cigarette, watching Lin Zhengde's shaking jaw over the flame. "Fang Qiming named six provinces, Lin Zhengde. He gave us the trash permit numbers you signed in your own hand. He also gave us the twelve-percent payout book under your brother-in-law's building firm."

Lin Zhengde's face turned dark purple. He slammed his flat palm onto the steel table with a loud crack.

"Twelve percent!" Lin Zhengde roared, spittle flying from his chin. "That bastard Fang Qiming kept sixty percent of the foreign payout and threw me scraps while making me sign every risky paper! You want to know how deep Shengyuan Capital goes? Ask him why his wrecking sites in Jiangnan, Yunhai, and Beichuan all sit within eight kilometers of army metal plants and state rare-earth stores!"

Guo Zhengming's pen stopped moving. "Say that again."

Lin Zhengde breathed hard, eyes bloodshot, rage pushing away all caution. "He did not just smuggle raw ore, Guo Zhengming! Fang Qiming hid foreign experts inside the site offices dressed as building engineers! They tested army-grade metal loads stolen straight from state defense plants. Then they hid the metal inside hollow concrete bridge pillars and shipped them downriver to tax-free export docks!"

"Who gave Shengyuan Capital the factory shipping schedules?" Guo Zhengming asked, his voice ice-cold.

Lin Zhengde swallowed hard. He realized a second too late how far he had stepped over the cliff, yet he could not stop the flood of hatred.

"He Guoliang's office in the capital hid the power usage records so the metal plants could run secret night shifts without showing power spikes," Lin Zhengde whispered. His shoulders slumped as rage turned into cold dread. "And Fang Qiming's contact in Shanghai set up the coastal cargo boats. Fang Qiming bragged once, after drinking in Sha City, that even if inspectors found one county pit, the wrecking permits would bury the trail under six layers of local shell firms."

Guo Zhengming blew out a thin stream of smoke, staring at the shaking vice governor.

"He was wrong," Guo Zhengming said quietly. "Because you just drew us the map to all six provinces, and Fang Qiming just handed us your signature on every truck leaving them."

Lin Zhengde dropped his forehead onto the cold steel table with a dull thud. His fingers clawed weakly at the metal edge as Guo Zhengming signaled the clerk to bring in the confession sheets.
