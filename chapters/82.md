# Chapter 82: The Unannounced Guest

The warm chatter in the courtyard swelled as Chen Jianguo invited Wang Lei and Director Wei Kun to sit beneath the persimmon tree, while Liu Meifang carried out plates of spiced peanuts and sliced pears.

At the second wooden table, Third Uncle-in-law Liu Guoliang felt his face burning hot. Minutes ago he had been holding court as the most prosperous relative in Hongshi Village, and now half the neighbors were whispering about how a grateful van owner and a city bureau director had driven out for Chen Xuan's family.

Refusing to lose face, Liu Guoliang cleared his throat loudly, smoothed his glossy leather jacket, and waved a dismissive hand.

"Well, look at that," Liu Guoliang said with a forced chuckle. "A delivery driver bringing fruit and a plainclothes county officer bringing pears. Very sweet for a rural village, Chen Jianguo."

He leaned back on his red stool and raised his voice so everyone near the gate could hear.

"Still, let us not confuse village favors with real standing," Liu Guoliang boasted, puffing out his chest. "My son's general manager in the city plays cards every weekend with a deputy section chief in Lanshui! Do you know what Lanshui is? The provincial capital! Real influence lives up in Lanshui, not in delivery vans or baskets of pears."

Director Wei Kun took a calm sip of jasmine tea, a faint gleam of amusement crossing his eyes without a single word spoken. Wang Lei frowned and opened his mouth, but Chen Xuan merely shook his head gently, smiling as he handed Tang Xiaoyu a clean napkin.

* * *

Before Liu Guoliang could savor the sound of his own voice, the quiet, refined purr of an unmarked black sedan rolled smoothly down the dirt lane and stopped outside the courtyard gate.

Even the chatter outside the wall dropped to a hushed murmur.

The rear door opened cleanly.

Qin Yuhan stepped out onto the packed earth wearing a tailored cream wool trench coat and simple flat shoes, her dark hair pinned neatly back. She carried no entourage, only a tasteful red-lacquered box cradled respectfully in both hands.

She walked lightly through the open wooden gate, her poised warmth immediately brightening the modest brick yard.

"Grandfather Chen Fugen, Grandmother Zhou Guilan," Qin Yuhan greeted warmly, offering a graceful bow. "Please forgive me for arriving unannounced. I heard today is Grandmother Zhou Guilan's eighty-first birthday, and I brought a box of freshly steamed longevity peaches from Qingyan's old pastry house to wish you many healthy years."

She turned smoothly toward Chen Xuan, her eyes softening with genuine respect.

"Chen Xuan," Qin Yuhan said politely. "Thank you again for all your steady help to Haiyue Group. My father asked me to convey his warmest wishes to your elders as well."

"You are very thoughtful, Miss Qin Yuhan," Chen Xuan replied calmly, pulling out a clean chair beside Zhou Guilan. "Please stay for a bowl of long-life noodles."

Behind the second table, Liu Guoliang's jaw dropped open so wide he forgot to breathe.

His wife grabbed his sleeve with trembling fingers, whispering frantically, "Old Liu! Is that not the chairwoman of Haiyue Group from the evening business news? The multi-billion-yuan group?"

Liu Guoliang's throat went bone dry. His son's trading company spent half the year begging just to meet a junior clerk at Haiyue Group, and here stood Qin Yuhan herself, handing longevity peaches to Zhou Guilan with both hands and thanking Chen Xuan like an honored equal.

"Miss Qin Yuhan, come sit down!" Liu Meifang hurried over, beaming warmly.

Qin Yuhan smiled and took a modest seat beside Tang Xiaoyu, complimenting the girl's neat red knitted sweater with effortless kindness.

* * *

Five minutes later, before Liu Guoliang could recover enough breath to speak, the crunch of steady footsteps sounded along the sunlit dirt path outside the gate.

There was no sedan horn and no driver trailing behind.

A silver-haired elder strolled unhurriedly through the wooden gate on foot. He wore a faded olive fishing vest over a cotton shirt and comfortable walking shoes dusted with country road dirt. Under his arm rested a small, dark-polished wooden box tied with silk cord.

"I told my driver to park out by the village archway so I would not block the lane," the silver-haired man called cheerfully, his deep voice warm and familiar. "Did I arrive in time for long-life noodles?"

At the second table, Third Uncle-in-law Liu Guoliang froze as if struck by lightning.

His teacup rattled violently against the wood, spilling hot jasmine tea across his leather cuff. He did not even feel the heat.

Every person over thirty in the province knew that face from twenty years of evening news broadcasts.

It was Retired Governor Zhao Dehai.

Director Wei Kun stood immediately with a crisp, quiet nod of deep respect. Qin Yuhan rose gracefully as well, greeting the elder warmly. Even Chen Jianguo and Chen Mingde stood straight in stunned silence.

Yet before anyone could stiffen into formal ceremony, Grandmother Zhou Guilan rose from her cane chair, placed both hands on her hips, and looked straight at Zhao Dehai with a delighted, scolding smile.

"Zhao Dehai, you stubborn old man!" Zhou Guilan scolded warmly, just like lecturing a forgetful neighbor. "The autumn wind on the village road is sharp enough to chill your bones, and you walk all the way from the archway without wrapping a scarf around your neck! Where is your common sense?"

Zhao Dehai burst into a hearty, unguarded laugh, bowing playfully to the eighty-one-year-old elder.

"Old Sister Zhou Guilan, I knew you would catch me the second I stepped through the gate," Zhao Dehai chuckled, setting the small wooden box of tea gently on the table. "Happy eighty-first birthday. I brought a tin of mild aged tea for you and Old Brother Chen Fugen."

His warm eyes shifted naturally to Chen Xuan, and the two exchanged a quiet, deeply respectful nod.

"Thank you for walking all this way, Grandpa Zhao Dehai," Chen Xuan said softly, pouring him hot tea.

"The air in Hongshi Village is ten times sweeter than the city," Zhao Dehai laughed gladly.

Just then, Liu Meifang and Wu Lanying carried out two massive steaming platters of golden long-life noodles topped with braised pork and green scallions.

Zhou Guilan smiled brightly, took Chen Xuan by her left hand and Tang Xiaoyu by her right hand, and seated them right beside her at the heart of the main birthday table.

"Sit right here beside Grandma," Zhou Guilan said happily, gesturing for Zhao Dehai, Qin Yuhan, Director Wei Kun, and Wang Lei to join them. "Today is a good day, and everyone who loves this family eats together!"

Far back on the narrowest corner bench, Liu Guoliang shrank silently over his empty bowl, unable to utter another word for the rest of the banquet.
