# Chapter 82: Zhao Ruilong and Gao Yuliang Turn on Each Other

Two floors beneath the Handong Provincial Disciplinary Annex in Jingzhou City, Room Three and Room Four sat side by side behind gray soundproof panels. The hallway smelled faintly of pine floor cleaner, and the chilled air conditioning kept both rooms crisp and quiet.

In Room Three, Zhao Ruilong, the arrested head of Shanshui Group, sat with his fine linen shirt unbuttoned at the throat. Even without his tailored jacket or gold watch, he leaned back in the steel chair with the practiced ease of a man used to buying his way out of every room.

Across from him sat Guo Zhengming, Minister of Public Security, dispatched directly from Beijing to oversee the Handong crackdown.

"Forty minutes ago, you told my officers that Deputy Provincial Secretary Gao Yuliang was barely a distant acquaintance," Guo Zhengming said calmly, tapping a blue dossier with two fingers. "You claimed you only shook his hand at public investment dinners."

Zhao Ruilong adjusted his shirt cuffs with a smooth flick of his wrists. "That is the honest truth, Minister Guo. I run a private enterprise. I do not stroll into the Deputy Provincial Secretary's office whenever I please. If Gao Yuliang bent the rules in Jingzhou City, or if former Vice Mayor Ding Yizhen took kickbacks, hold them responsible. Shanshui Group simply followed approved bidding procedures."

Guo Zhengming smiled faintly. He reached over and slid a freshly printed fax across the table.

"Ministry Division Chief Zhao Dehan broke down in Beijing an hour ago," Guo Zhengming said. "He handed over his private ledger. Every fifty-kilogram crate of cash delivered to his suburban villa came from Shanshui Group couriers. Every expedited energy permit went straight into your pocket. Do you still want to pretend you are an innocent businessman?"

Zhao Ruilong's jaw tightened by a fraction of an inch. Before he could speak, Guo Zhengming pressed a button on a small black recorder. Zhao Dehan's weeping voice filled Room Three, naming Zhao Ruilong and Ding Yizhen as the architects of the kickback pipeline.

Just beyond the thick dividing wall, in Room Four, Gao Yuliang sat with his spine straight and his glasses perched neatly on his nose. As the arrested Deputy Provincial Secretary of Handong and longtime political mentor of the Handa Faction, he still wore the composed mask of a former law professor.

Sitting across from him was Hou Liangping, Chief of the Handong Anti-Corruption Bureau and once one of Gao Yuliang's brightest university students.

"Liangping," Gao Yuliang said softly, his tone carrying mild disappointment rather than fear. "I taught you criminal law myself. You know better than anyone that guilt requires hard proof, not political hysteria. Yes, I trusted Qi Tongwei too much as Public Security Director, and yes, I failed to watch Ding Yizhen closely enough. That is a mistake of oversight, not a crime."

Behind the one-way glass in the darkened observation corridor, Handong Provincial Party Secretary Sha Ruijin stood beside Chief Procurator Ji Changming, watching both rooms on twin monitors. Sha Ruijin's expression remained stone-cold.

"Still lecturing his student," Sha Ruijin murmured. "He thinks his old faction can still shield him."

Inside Room Four, Hou Liangping did not flinch at the familiar lecture. He opened his laptop and turned the screen toward his former teacher.

"Teacher Gao, you taught me to follow the money, not the speeches," Hou Liangping said evenly. "Ten minutes ago, in the room right next to yours, Zhao Ruilong realized Beijing already has Zhao Dehan's ledger. Would you like to hear what your distant acquaintance just decided to tell Minister Guo?"

Hou Liangping tapped the spacebar. Live audio from Room Three poured cleanly through the speaker.

"Fine!" Zhao Ruilong's voice snapped, all calm elegance gone. "You want the truth about Shanshui Group's land approvals in Jingzhou? Ask Gao Yuliang! Not a single acre of that lakeside development moved without his private nod. I spent 30 million yuan on antique calligraphy and a Hong Kong shell account just to keep him happy. He used Qi Tongwei to clear our rivals and pocketed half the profits through his relatives!"

In Room Four, Gao Yuliang's scholarly composure shattered in a heartbeat. His hand slammed down onto the steel table, rattling his glasses.

"That ungrateful dog!" Gao Yuliang hissed, his face flushing dark red. "He is lying through his teeth to save his own skin!"

"Then set the record straight, Teacher Gao," Hou Liangping said, leaning forward. "Who really built the Shanshui Resort slush fund?"

"Zhao Ruilong did!" Gao Yuliang barked, his voice rising out of control. "He ran that resort like a private hunting ground to trap provincial cadres on hidden cameras! Whenever a city official refused to sign off on his illegal land swaps, Zhao Ruilong sent Qi Tongwei to dig up dirt or frame them! You want proof? Check the offshore trust registered under his sister's maiden name in Macau. 400 million yuan moved through that account last autumn alone!"

In the observation corridor, Ji Changming signaled the technician. Two seconds later, Hou Liangping's audio feed played directly into Room Three.

When Zhao Ruilong heard Gao Yuliang expose the Macau trust and the hidden resort cameras, his face twisted in fury. He lunged forward until the steel chair groaned against its bolts.

"You old hypocrite!" Zhao Ruilong shouted at the concrete wall as if Gao Yuliang could hear him through the foam. "You were the one who told me to install those cameras! Minister Guo, write this down! I have the backup drives in a safe deposit box in Shenzhen, and Gao Yuliang is on three of those recordings himself!"

Behind the glass, Sha Ruijin finally turned away from the monitors and looked at Ji Changming.

"Freeze the Shenzhen vault and the Macau trust immediately," Sha Ruijin ordered. "The Handa Faction just finished burying itself."
