# Chapter 82: Fang Qiming and Lin Zhengde Turn on Each Other

Two floors below the Lanshui Provincial Disciplinary Annex, Room Three and Room Four shared a concrete wall, identical steel tables, and the sour smell of stale cigarette smoke.

In Room Three, Shengyuan Capital Chairman Fang Qiming sat with his tailored wool jacket across his knees, his silk tie confiscated at intake. For three hours he had insisted that every land acquisition in Lanshui passed municipal bidding review.

Guo Zhengming, head of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group, sat across from him and set a matte-black recorder beside an overflowing ashtray.

"You told my deputies forty minutes ago that Vice Governor Lin Zhengde was a distant policy contact," Guo Zhengming said, lighting a cigarette and blowing smoke toward the vent. "That you met him only at investment summits."

Fang Qiming adjusted his cuffs with an unhurried flick of his wrists. "That is the truth, Guo Zhengming. A private developer does not wander into a vice governor's office uninvited. If Lin Zhengde overstepped his authority in Lanshui, blame his ambition, not Shengyuan Capital."

Guo Zhengming smiled without warmth and pressed play.

Static hissed for two seconds, followed by idling turbine blades and Lin Zhengde's panicked voice crackling through the speaker.

"You told me the Lanshui demolition clearance was clean, Fang Qiming! You swore the county land-use seal would bury the shaft entrance under a residential foundation before the provincial survey team arrived! If they drill through the concrete slab on Parcel Nine, my neck goes on the block for your smuggling tunnel!"

Fang Qiming's hand froze halfway to his water glass. His composure cracked down the middle, his eyes widening as his own shouting voice answered from the recorder.

"My tunnel, Lin Zhengde? Whose signature approved the emergency urban-renewal reclassification across six provinces? You took twelve percent on every metric ton of refined rare-earth oxide we moved out through those county demolition sites! You think you can wash your hands at a heliport?"

Guo Zhengming hit stop. The silence over Room Three felt suffocating.

Fang Qiming's chest heaved. A vein bulged at his temple, and his manicured fingernails dug crescent marks into his palms. "He wore a wire?" Fang Qiming hissed, dripping venom. "That spineless coward recorded me at the heliport to buy himself a lighter sentence?"

"No," Guo Zhengming said calmly, tapping ash into the tray. "Neither of you wore a wire. That audio was captured by directional surveillance you did not know existed. Right now, next door, Lin Zhengde is listening to the exact same recording."

Fang Qiming stared at the blank wall separating them, his lips curling back in unfiltered hatred. "Lin Zhengde will tell you I coerced him. That is what bureaucrats do when the roof caves in. They cry that the businessman corrupted them."

"Then tell me the truth before he signs his statement," Guo Zhengming said, leaning back. "Parcel Nine. And the other five provinces."

Fang Qiming let out a sharp, bitter laugh, the polished financier vanishing to reveal a cornered predator. "Fine. If Lin Zhengde wants to burn, I will pour the kerosene myself. County-level real-estate demolition was never about housing, Guo Zhengming. Nobody builds forty-story towers in half-empty mountain counties for profit."

"Go on," Guo Zhengming said, eyes narrowing through the smoke.

"You need excavation equipment, blast permits, twenty-four-hour dump-truck convoys, and three-meter corrugated iron fences that keep residents and satellite surveyors from asking questions," Fang Qiming said rapidly, leaning forward. "Every time Shengyuan Capital won a county redevelopment bid, Lin Zhengde and his peers in five neighboring provinces stamped an expedited demolition waiver. Underneath the rubble of old textile mills and grain depots, we sank reinforced concrete transfer bunkers."

"Transfer bunkers for what?" Guo Zhengming pressed.

"High-purity dysprosium, terbium, and aviation-grade titanium-scandium alloys diverted from state smelters," Fang Qiming spat, his voice shaking with spite. "Freight checkpoints scan ore shipments. Nobody scans twenty thousand tons of demolition debris hauled out in sealed tipper trucks registered to municipal sanitation bureaus. Lin Zhengde personally signed the inter-provincial waste-disposal permits that let our trucks cross three toll corridors without a single customs inspection."

Guo Zhengming stood up without another word, took the recorder, and stepped into Room Four thirty seconds later.

Vice Governor Lin Zhengde looked ten years older than he had that morning. Damp hair clung to his forehead, and his hands gripped the steel table edge as if bracing against an earthquake. The junior investigator beside the door had already played the heliport tape twice.

"He is lying!" Lin Zhengde shouted the instant Guo Zhengming sat down, his voice cracking shrill. "Whatever Fang Qiming told you in Room Three, he is pinning his syndicate on me! I am a provincial official, Guo Zhengming! I approved urban-renewal quotas to boost county tax revenue!"

Guo Zhengming lit a fresh cigarette, watching Lin Zhengde's trembling jaw over the flame. "Fang Qiming named six provinces, Lin Zhengde. He gave us the sanitation permit numbers you signed in your own hand, and the twelve-percent commission ledger under your brother-in-law's construction firm."

Lin Zhengde's face turned mottled purple. He slammed his flat palm onto the steel table with a deafening crack.

"Twelve percent!" Lin Zhengde roared, spittle flecking his chin. "That bastard Fang Qiming kept sixty percent of the offshore settlement and threw me scraps while making me sign every high-risk clearance! You want to know how deep Shengyuan Capital goes? Ask him why his demolition sites in Jiangnan, Yunhai, and Beichuan all sit within eight kilometers of military-alloy foundries and state rare-earth stockpiles!"

Guo Zhengming's pen stopped moving. "Say that again."

Lin Zhengde breathed hard, eyes bloodshot, fury overriding caution. "He did not just smuggle raw ore, Guo Zhengming! Fang Qiming staged overseas technicians inside the demolition site offices disguised as structural-engineering consultants! They assayed military-grade alloy batches diverted straight out of state defense contractors, repacked them inside hollow precast concrete bridge pilings, and shipped them downriver to bonded export wharves!"

"Who gave Shengyuan Capital the foundry dispatch schedules?" Guo Zhengming asked, his voice ice-cold.

Lin Zhengde swallowed hard, realizing a second too late how far he had stepped over the cliff, yet unable to stop the avalanche of mutual hatred.

"He Guoliang's office in the capital cleared the energy-consumption logs so the smelters could run off-book night shifts without showing power spikes," Lin Zhengde whispered, his shoulders sagging as rage collapsed into dread. "And Fang Qiming's liaison in Shanghai arranged the coastal barges. Fang Qiming boasted once, after drinking in Lanshui, that even if inspectors uncovered one county pit, the demolition permits would bury the trail under six layers of county shell contractors."

Guo Zhengming exhaled a thin stream of smoke, staring at the trembling vice governor.

"He was wrong," Guo Zhengming said quietly. "Because you just drew us the map to all six provinces, and Fang Qiming just handed us your signature on every truck leaving them."

Lin Zhengde dropped his forehead onto the cold steel table with a hollow thud, his fingers clawing uselessly at the edge as Guo Zhengming signaled the clerk for the formal confession sheets.
