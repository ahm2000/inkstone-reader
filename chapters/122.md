# Chapter 122: The Husband's Lucky Map

At seven-thirty on Tuesday evening, the front door of Emerald Riverside Villa clicked open, and Qin Yuhan walked into the foyer looking exhausted.

After a warm Monday dinner of braised spare ribs and lotus root soup, her Tuesday at Haiyue Tower had been a long headache. She slipped off her high heels by the shoe cabinet, rubbed her temples, and walked into the warm living room carrying a thick blue folder of real estate documents.

On the plush fabric sofa facing the glass balcony and the glowing lights of Lujiazui, Chen Xuan was lounging against two soft cushions in his gray cotton pajamas. In his left hand, he held a round red Fuji apple; in his right hand, a small fruit knife glided smoothly around the fruit, peeling off a single unbroken red ribbon of skin.

On the low glass coffee table in front of him sat his scratched green enamel thermos and the Tuesday evening issue of the Shanghai Municipal Daily.

"Welcome home, President Qin," Chen Xuan smiled, finishing the apple peel in one continuous spiral and slicing off a crisp, sweet wedge with the clean blade. "Dinner is keeping warm in the kitchen steamer, and I saved you the sweetest apple in the basket. Here, have a bite first."

Qin Yuhan walked over and dropped onto the sofa right beside him. She leaned her head against his shoulder with a soft sigh and opened her mouth to take the crisp apple slice from his fingers.

"Why does my brilliant Fudan University graduate look like someone stole her lunch box?" Chen Xuan asked playfully, cutting another wedge of apple for her while keeping the core for himself. "Who dared to make the president of Haiyue Group frown until seven-thirty at night?"

"Who else could it be?" Qin Yuhan sighed after chewing the sweet apple. She tossed the thick blue folder onto the coffee table beside the evening newspaper. "My cousin Qin Haoran flew down from Beijing this morning with an investment partner named Director Zhao. Even though Gu Mingzhe got arrested in Beijing over the weekend, Second Aunt still pushed Qin Haoran to bring this project to our Haiyue Group board."

"What project is Qin Haoran selling now?" Chen Xuan asked casually, feeding her a third slice of apple.

"A large bonded warehouse compound near the outer Pudong port," Qin Yuhan explained, frowning at the blue folder. "Director Zhao and Qin Haoran want Haiyue Group to buy the entire compound tomorrow morning for 300 million yuan. Three of our older board directors think the warehouse sits right next to the main freight highway, so they are pressuring me to sign the purchase contract before another shipping company takes it."

"Three hundred million yuan is not pocket change," Chen Xuan remarked, popping a small piece of apple near the core into his own mouth. "Do you want to buy it?"

"No," Qin Yuhan shook her head firmly. "The asking price of 300 million yuan is at least forty percent higher than the normal value in that district, and Director Zhao kept rushing me to wire the 100 million yuan deposit to his Huaxia Bank account before noon tomorrow. Whenever someone rushes a contract that hard, something is wrong. But the freight traffic data looks clean on paper, so if I reject it tomorrow morning without solid proof, Qin Haoran and those three old directors will complain to my father and Grandfather in Beijing that I am blocking company expansion."

Chen Xuan looked down at the blue folder on the coffee table, where a satellite map of the outer Pudong port compound was clipped to the cover page. The address read: No. 418 Donggang Freight Avenue, Pudong New Area.

Three days ago, Number 11 of the White Order had sent a brief summary of Shanghai municipal loans funded by China Construction Bank and ICBC to Chen Xuan's white phone. Chen Xuan already knew that Donggang Freight Avenue was scheduled for municipal road widening next month. Director Zhao had caught wind of the upcoming freeze and was trying to dump the property onto Haiyue Group through the clueless Qin Haoran.

Of course, an ordinary twelve-thousand-yuan clerk could not pull out a White Order briefing. Luckily, Chen Xuan had read the public notices in the evening paper twenty minutes ago.

"No. 418 Donggang Freight Avenue?" Chen Xuan blinked, pointing the tip of his small fruit knife toward the folded Shanghai Municipal Daily on the coffee table. "Wait a second, Yuhan. Is that not the same street printed in the tiny municipal notice at the bottom of page fourteen today?"

"Page fourteen?" Qin Yuhan sat up straight on the sofa, her dark eyes widening as she reached for the evening newspaper.

"Look right there in the bottom left corner, under the subway construction notice," Chen Xuan said, reaching around her shoulder to tap a small grey box that most business executives in Lujiazui never bothered to read. "At Hengxin Logistics, Old Hu makes me check road closure notices every Tuesday so our delivery vans do not get fined. I saw it while reading the paper just now."

Qin Yuhan lowered her head and read the small black print line by line.

It was a public municipal road-widening announcement issued at four o'clock that afternoon by the Shanghai Municipal Transport Commission. Starting the first of next month, numbers 400 through 460 on Donggang Freight Avenue were designated for a three-year freight highway expansion and underground drainage corridor, during which all commercial warehouse leases and heavy container truck access on that block would be suspended!

"Director Zhao knew the road widening notice was coming out this week!" Qin Yuhan gasped, her cheeks flushing with joy as she clutched the newspaper page. "With this official newspaper notice, those three old directors will drop the project in five seconds tomorrow morning, and Qin Haoran will not dare open his mouth!"

Turning on the sofa, the usually cool president of Haiyue Group threw both arms around Chen Xuan's neck and gave him a tight hug.

"Chen Xuan, you just saved Haiyue Group 300 million yuan!" Qin Yuhan laughed happily against his shoulder, her eyes shining like stars. "You really are my lazy lucky star!"

"Careful with the fruit knife, President Qin," Chen Xuan chuckled warmly, setting the knife on the coffee table before wrapping his arm around his wife's waist. "Since your lazy lucky star just saved you 300 million yuan, do I get an extra scoop of rice with our braised chicken tonight?"

"You can have two extra scoops, and I will wash all the dishes myself!" Qin Yuhan beamed, pulling him up toward the dining room while the lights of the Oriental Pearl Tower twinkled outside their window.
