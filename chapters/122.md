# Chapter 122: The Husband's Lucky Map

Monday passed in peaceful routine, and by Tuesday evening the autumn wind along the Huangpu River carried a crisp chill. At 7:30 PM, the front door of Emerald Riverside Villa clicked open, and Qin Yuhan stepped into the foyer looking thoroughly drained.

Her day at Haiyue Tower had been an endless string of arguments. She slipped off her black heels beside the shoe cabinet, rubbed her aching temples, and walked into the warm living room carrying a thick blue folder stuffed with real estate maps and port shipping reports.

On the plush fabric sofa facing the glass balcony and the glowing skyline of Lujiazui, Chen Xuan was lounging comfortably against two soft cushions in gray cotton pajamas. In his left hand, he held a crisp red Fuji apple. In his right hand, a small paring knife glided smoothly around the fruit, peeling off a single unbroken red ribbon of skin.

On the low glass coffee table in front of him sat his green thermos and the Tuesday evening edition of the Shanghai Municipal Economic Daily.

"You look like you fought three boardrooms before sunset," Chen Xuan said, sitting up and offering her the freshly peeled apple. "Sit down and rest your feet. I simmered ginger pear soup on the stove. Give me one minute."

Before Qin Yuhan could protest, he padded into the kitchen and returned with a steaming porcelain bowl of pale golden soup fragrant with rock sugar, ginger slices, and soft autumn pear. She took a careful spoonful, feeling the gentle warmth soothe her dry throat.

"Director Zhao and Qin Haoran are impossible," Qin Yuhan sighed, setting her blue folder onto the coffee table and spreading out a large coastal shipping and warehouse map. "They brought a full valuation team into the boardroom today. They want Haiyue Group to spend 300 million yuan buying an old riverside warehouse complex in the northern dock zone. They claim the city is expanding freight capacity there and that the land will double in value within two years."

Chen Xuan leaned forward with a lazy stretch, resting his elbows on his knees as he studied the colorful map. "300 million yuan buys a lot of steamed sea bass and autumn pears. Do you trust Director Zhao's numbers?"

"Not for a second," Qin Yuhan said, tapping the northern dock zone with the cap of her pen. "The lease records look messy, and the seller is an offshore shell company that refuses to name its real owners. Worse yet, my own logistics team is split over which eastern terminal route connects best to the new Handong provincial freight corridor. If I cannot counter Director Zhao's pitch with hard facts at tomorrow morning's board meeting, the older shareholders might side with him just to keep the peace."

Chen Xuan yawned lightly and reached over to pick up the Tuesday evening newspaper from the coffee table. He flipped smoothly to page four and tapped a small rectangular notice tucked into the bottom corner beneath the shipping index.

"Wife, you work too hard to read the boring pages," Chen Xuan said with an easy smile. "Look at this little bulletin right here. It came out this afternoon."

Qin Yuhan leaned closer, her eyes scanning the dense black print. Within three seconds, her breath caught.

It was an official public announcement from the Shanghai Municipal Planning and Environmental Bureau. Starting next quarter, the entire northern dock strip was being rezoned into an ecological riverside park and public greenbelt. All commercial warehouse expansion permits in that zone were frozen immediately, and existing freight yards would be required to relocate within eighteen months.

Qin Yuhan stared at the newspaper in disbelief. "If Haiyue Group spent 300 million yuan buying those warehouses tomorrow, we would be stuck with dead land before the ink dried on the contract. Director Zhao and Qin Haoran either failed to check the municipal bulletin, or they are deliberately trying to dump a worthless asset onto the company."

"My guess is the second one," Chen Xuan remarked casually, taking a sip of warm tea from his green thermos. "Anyone who rushes you to sign a 300 million yuan check before lunch usually has something to hide."

Qin Yuhan folded the page carefully and tucked it straight into her blue folder, her eyes bright with sudden relief. That single clipping was more than enough to flatten Director Zhao's scheme in five minutes flat.

Then she glanced back down at the shipping map. "What about the eastern corridor? My analysts handed me three transfer terminals, and every report contradicts the others."

Chen Xuan picked up her red ballpoint pen, twirled it once between his fingers, and drew a neat circle around a quieter deep-water terminal southeast of the main docks. From there, he drew a clean line heading west toward Jingzhou in Handong Province.

"If you ask a desk clerk who stares at freight charts all day, pick this one," Chen Xuan said lightly. "The main northern bridges always jam up with container trucks in autumn, and the toll fees eat straight into the margin. This southeastern terminal connects directly to the newly upgraded rail spur. Fewer delays, lower fuel costs, and clean customs clearance. Call it your husband's lucky map."

Qin Yuhan studied the red line for a long moment. The more she traced the highway junctions and rail links he had marked so casually, the clearer the logic became. Her expensive consulting team had overlooked the cleanest, most practical path on the entire page.

"How does a clerk who naps by the window notice things my directors miss?" she asked softly, leaning her head briefly against his shoulder.

"Simple," Chen Xuan grinned, tapping the side of his thermos. "I drink good tea, I clock out at 4:55 PM, and my brain is not fried by back-to-back meetings."

Qin Yuhan laughed, feeling lighter than she had all week. She took his hand and gave his fingers a warm squeeze. "Since my husband just saved Haiyue Group 300 million yuan, you are coming with me to the charity dinner at the Waldorf on the Bund tomorrow evening. Otherwise, I will be trapped listening to boring heirs pitch business deals all night."

"Deal," Chen Xuan agreed at once. "Just make sure the hotel buffet has decent pastries."
