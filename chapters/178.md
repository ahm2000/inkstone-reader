# Chapter 178: Trouble at Plaza 66

By 2:15 PM, the silver sedan pulled into the underground garage of Plaza 66 on West Nanjing Road. Upstairs, the luxury mall gleamed under high glass ceilings, decorated with gold ribbons and early winter pine displays. Soft piano music drifted through the corridors past flagship boutiques from Milan and Paris.

Chen Xuan carried the shopping bags from IFC Mall in his left hand, his canvas tote with the dented green thermos slung over his right shoulder. Chen Hao walked beside him, still wearing his bulky navy county jacket while guarding his new garment bag like a treasure.

As they reached the second-floor atrium, Qin Yuhan's smartphone vibrated in her coat pocket. She glanced at the screen and gave Chen Xuan an apologetic smile.

"It is Assistant Lin from Haiyue Group," Qin Yuhan said softly. "The Ningbo port shipment needs my verbal sign-off before Monday morning. Give me five minutes?"

"Take your time," Chen Xuan said easily. "We will look around that cashmere shop right over there and pick out colors for Mom and Grandma."

Qin Yuhan nodded and stepped into a quiet corner near a scarf display across the walkway to take the call. Chen Xuan led Chen Hao and Chen Yuan into Valenti Cashmere, a spacious Italian boutique lined with pale oak shelves and soft spotlighting.

The shop was quiet except for two people near the velvet counter. One was Zhao Kai, the spoiled son of Dingcheng Real Estate's chairman, wearing a tight crocodile-print leather jacket, designer sunglasses in his gelled hair, and a heavy gold watch. Beside him clung a young woman in a sequined dress holding a tiny white handbag.

Standing before them was the store manager, Zhou Mei, a sharp-featured woman in her late thirties with a practiced smile reserved strictly for big spenders.

Chen Xuan strolled over to the shawl rack with his cousins. Near the center hung two exquisite pieces: one in deep burgundy cashmere and another in warm camel.

"Brother Xuan, look at this red one," Chen Hao said quietly. "Grandma loves dark red. It looks super soft."

Chen Hao reached out to check the care tag hanging from the corner. His fingers had barely come near the fabric when a sharp voice sliced across the boutique.

"Stop! Do not touch that!"

Zhou Mei strode over in her high heels, her polite smile turning into a pinched scowl. She planted herself between Chen Hao and the rack, eyeing his puffy navy jacket and scuffed sneakers with open distaste.

"Are you blind?" Zhou Mei snapped, smoothing the untouched shawl. "This is imported Italian baby cashmere. 28,000 yuan a piece. Look at that cheap county-market down jacket! If duck feathers or lint stick to this wool, can you even pay for dry cleaning?"

Chen Hao froze, pulling his hand back stiffly as blood rushed up his neck. "I did not even touch it! I was only looking at the tag!"

"We are customers too!" Chen Yuan spoke up immediately, stepping beside her brother with her chin raised. "How can you talk to people like that?"

Before Zhou Mei could reply, a loud, lazy chuckle echoed from the counter behind them. Zhao Kai sauntered over with both hands in his pockets, looking Chen Hao, Chen Yuan, and Chen Xuan up and down with exaggerated amusement. His gaze lingered on Chen Xuan's plain dark gray jacket and the scratched green metal thermos poking out of his canvas tote.

"Well, well," Zhao Kai drawled, shaking his head. "Manager Zhou, when did Plaza 66 start running free sightseeing tours for rural folks straight off the long-distance bus? Look at that green thermos. My grandfather threw away a cup like that twenty years ago."

The woman beside Zhao Kai giggled behind her manicured fingers. "Kai, maybe they thought this was the wholesale market near the train station."

Zhou Mei turned immediately to Zhao Kai with a fawning grin. "Young Master Zhao, please do not let them ruin your afternoon! Security downstairs must be slacking off. You spend 200,000 yuan here without blinking, while people like this just wander in for the free air conditioning."

She spun back toward Chen Xuan and his cousins, waving a dismissive hand toward the glass entrance. "Out, all three of you. Do not stand around lowering the grade of my boutique. If you want a fifty-yuan scarf, take the subway back to the suburbs."

Chen Hao's fists clenched tightly at his sides, his face bright red with humiliation and anger. Chen Yuan's eyes glistened with hurt as she gripped her brother's sleeve to hold him back.

Chen Xuan did not raise his voice at all. He placed a calm, steady hand on Chen Hao's shoulder, his expression completely mild and unbothered.

"Manager Zhou, right?" Chen Xuan asked softly, pointing at the brass rack. "Are that burgundy shawl and the camel shawl beside it for sale?"

Zhou Mei let out a harsh, mocking laugh. "Of course they are for sale! Together they cost 58,000 yuan. Do you know how many months of moving boxes it takes to earn 58,000 yuan? If a guy carrying a 15-yuan thermos can swipe a real card for those two shawls, I will chew up the receipt right here on the floor!"

Zhao Kai stepped closer, sneering as he tapped his gold watch. "I will make it even better, country bumpkin. If you can pay for those two shawls right now without running away, I will drop to my knees and apologize to your little cousin. If your card declines, you three crawl out that glass door on your hands and knees. Deal?"

"Brother Xuan, let us just go," Chen Hao whispered tightly, his voice shaking. "We do not need to buy from people like this."

"No need to leave," Chen Xuan said gently, patting Chen Hao's shoulder. "Grandma likes dark red, and Mom likes camel. Why let two noisy strangers spoil our shopping?"

He stepped calmly up to the marble counter, slipped two fingers into his jacket pocket, and set a single card flat onto the dark leather tray.

It was completely plain and matte-white, with no bank logos and no printed digits. Only in the upper right corner sat a tiny, faintly raised silver emblem shaped like a knot of clouds.

Chen Xuan looked up at Zhou Mei with an easy, quiet smile.

"Wrap both shawls nicely, please. You can swipe this one."
