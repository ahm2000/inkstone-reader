# Chapter 178: Trouble at Plaza 66

By three o'clock, soft afternoon light filtered through the tall glass roof of Plaza 66, known across Shanghai as Henglong Plaza.

On the second floor, the marble hallway was quiet and spotless. Chen Xuan led Chen Yuan and Chen Hao into a bright coat shop called Valenti Cashmere, where rows of thick wool and cashmere coats hung along tall walnut shelves.

A young saleswoman named Lin Xiao stepped forward with a polite smile. "Good afternoon! Welcome to Valenti Cashmere. How can I help you today?"

"Good afternoon," Chen Xuan said easily. "I want a thick winter wool coat for my mother, and a clean wool jacket for my sister."

"Please come this way," Lin Xiao said kindly, guiding them to the winter racks.

Chen Xuan stepped over to a deep burgundy double-faced wool coat and felt the cuff. "This one is good. Mom's shoulders always get cold in January back in Yunxi County. This will hold the warmth."

He lifted a clean oatmeal-colored wool jacket from the next rack for Chen Yuan. "Try this on. The cut fits you."

Chen Yuan hesitated and reached for the sleeve. As she lifted it, the small cream price tag swung out from the cuff.

Her breath caught at once.

"Ge!" Chen Yuan whispered urgently, tugging his windbreaker sleeve. "Look at the tag! This jacket is 28,000 yuan! And Mom's burgundy coat is 48,000 yuan! We cannot buy this! Let us put it back and go to a normal department store!"

Chen Xuan chuckled softly. "Try it on first. If it fits your shoulders, we take it. Do not worry about the price."

"Ge, that is half a year of your wages!" Chen Yuan protested quietly, her cheeks red with worry.

Before Chen Xuan could reply, the sharp click of leather shoes sounded from the doorway.

A flashy young man in a glossy black designer bomber jacket strolled inside laughing, his arm slung around a young woman in a fur-trimmed vest carrying a bright orange handbag.

It was Zhao Kai, the spoiled only son of Shanghai real estate developer Zhao Yongsheng of Kaiyuan Real Estate. His girlfriend, Sun Tingting, glanced around the shop with a bored pout until her eyes landed on the oatmeal wool jacket in Chen Yuan's hands.

"Kai, look at that oatmeal jacket!" Sun Tingting said immediately, pointing straight at Chen Yuan. "That is the exact cut I saw in the magazine last week! I want that one!"

From behind the marble counter, the store manager, Zhou Mei, hurried out at once. She was a sharp-faced woman in a tight black blazer and heavy red lipstick. Her stiff smile vanished the instant she looked over Chen Xuan's faded two-hundred-yuan windbreaker and canvas sneakers.

Zhou Mei hurried past Chen Xuan's family and bowed eagerly to Zhao Kai.

"Young Master Zhao! Miss Sun! Welcome back to Valenti Cashmere!" Zhou Mei gushed warmly. "We just unpacked our new Milan winter line!"

Zhao Kai tossed his car keys in one hand and smirked, turning his gaze straight onto Chen Xuan. He looked slowly over the cheap dark-blue windbreaker, ordinary trousers, and Chen Hao's blue-and-white school jacket.

A loud, mocking laugh burst from Zhao Kai's throat.

"Manager Zhou," Zhao Kai sneered loudly so everyone in the shop could hear. "When did Plaza 66 turn into a train station waiting room? Look at that windbreaker. Did you buy it from a street stall outside the subway for ninety-nine yuan?"

Sun Tingting covered her mouth and giggled sharply. "Kai, stop teasing! Maybe they just came in to dodge the wind and touch the coats for free."

Chen Yuan's face went pale, then flushed red with anger. Her fingers tightened around the hanger.

Right beside her, seventeen-year-old Chen Hao stood quietly near the men's display rack with his hands behind his back. As Sun Tingting swung her big orange handbag carelessly past the rack, her strap caught a heavy navy cashmere coat, tilting its wooden hanger off the brass bar.

Without thinking, Chen Hao reached out quickly to catch the slipping hanger so the coat would not hit the floor.

"Hey! Stop right there!"

Store manager Zhou Mei's voice cracked through the quiet shop like a whip. She rushed over in her high heels and slapped Chen Hao's wrist away from the hanger with a disgusted scowl.

"Kid, take your hands off that rack right now!" Zhou Mei snapped coldly. "Do you know what that coat costs? It is 68,000 yuan! Look at your cheap school jacket and your hands! If your dirty fingers leave a mark on that cashmere, your brother's whole year of wages cannot even pay for the dry cleaning!"

Chen Hao froze on the spot. He pulled his hand back slowly, his ears burning bright red with humiliation and hurt.

"I did not dirty it," Chen Hao said quietly, his voice tight. "Her bag bumped it and it was falling."

"Do not talk back to me!" Zhou Mei scoffed, straightening the hanger with exaggerated fuss. "Miss Sun is a valued VIP! If you cannot afford anything here, keep your hands in your pockets and stop blocking real buyers!"

"That is enough!" Chen Yuan stepped directly in front of her brother, her eyes bright with fury. "He was trying to help! Why are you talking to a kid like that?"

Zhao Kai laughed louder, pulling a shiny gold credit card from his pocket and waving it lazily in the air.

"Manager Zhou, stop wasting breath on window shoppers," Zhao Kai drawled arrogantly. "I drop 200k in this mall without blinking. Take that oatmeal jacket from the girl and wrap it for Tingting. Clear these three out of my sight. Looking at that cheap windbreaker ruins my mood."

Zhou Mei beamed eagerly at Zhao Kai, then turned back to Chen Xuan with an icy wave of her hand.

"You heard Young Master Zhao," Zhou Mei said smugly, folding her arms. "Hand back the oatmeal jacket and leave immediately. Valenti Cashmere is for real customers, not sightseers from out of town."

Lin Xiao bit her lip nervously, looking deeply uncomfortable, yet she did not dare challenge her manager.

Chen Yuan's hands shook slightly, ready to hand over the jacket and pull her brothers away.

Then a calm, steady hand rested gently on Chen Hao's shoulder.

Chen Xuan had not raised his voice. He had not frowned or clenched his fists. Yet the warmth in his eyes was gone, replaced by a quiet stillness as cold as winter water.

"You did nothing wrong, Chen Hao," Chen Xuan said softly. "Never feel small because someone else forgets their manners."

He looked gently at Chen Yuan. "Keep holding that jacket. It looks good on you."

Then Chen Xuan turned his calm gaze to Lin Xiao, ignoring Zhou Mei and Zhao Kai completely.

"Miss Lin," Chen Xuan said evenly. "Please pack three items for me. The burgundy wool coat in medium for my mother, this oatmeal jacket for my sister, and that navy cashmere coat my brother saved from the floor. Size large for him."

Lin Xiao blinked in shock.

Zhou Mei stared for a second, then let out a shrill, mocking laugh. Zhao Kai laughed even harder, slapping his knee.

"Three coats?!" Zhou Mei jeered, pointing straight at Chen Xuan. "Can you even count? Forty-eight thousand, twenty-eight thousand, and sixty-eight thousand! That is 144k yuan! Stop making a scene in Plaza 66 and get out before I call mall security!"

"Do not bother security," Chen Xuan said calmly.

He slid one hand slowly into the inside pocket of his faded dark-blue windbreaker.
