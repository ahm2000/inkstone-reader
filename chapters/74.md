# Chapter 74: Hongrui Plaza Sealed in Broad Daylight

At forty-five minutes past eight on Monday morning, traffic along Yunxi County's central avenue ground to a halt outside Hongrui Plaza.

Six dark provincial economic-crime and counter-terrorism vans angled sharply across the marble driveway, blocking both underground garage ramps and the main glass entrance. Within sixty seconds, uniformed officers unspooled bright yellow cordon tape between the brass bollards while two dozen stunned Hongrui Real Estate clerks and receptionists spilled out onto the front steps, clutching handbags and staring in panic.

Across the sidewalk, a crowd of commuters, delivery riders, and nearby shopkeepers swelled from thirty people to over two hundred. Phones rose above heads, lenses aimed straight at the towering glass lobby that had dominated Yunxi County for twelve years.

"Look at those license plates," a noodle-shop owner muttered, his cigarette burning forgotten between his fingers. "Provincial plates. Not county police. And those vests say Counter-Terrorism and Economic Crime!"

"Is Hongrui Real Estate really getting raided?" a young office clerk whispered beside him, eyes wide. "Chairman Deng Yongchang had dinner with County Magistrate Sun Weiming just last week! Nobody in Yunxi County dares touch Hongrui Plaza!"

Before anyone could answer, the heavy brass-framed glass doors swung open from inside.

A wave of gasps rippled across the plaza.

Two tall provincial economic-crime officers marched Chairman Deng Yongchang straight across his own polished marble lobby. His bespoke charcoal suit hung crooked off one shoulder, his silk tie was gone, and his wrists were locked tightly behind his back in gleaming steel handcuffs. Behind him walked Provincial Economic Crime Investigation Division Deputy Director Fang Qiming, flanked by four armed counter-terrorism officers.

Deng Yongchang's face was purple with rage and panic, veins bulging sharply along his thick neck. Every few steps he twisted violently against the officers' grip, shouting hoarsely toward his terrified employees huddled behind the yellow tape.

"Get my lawyers!" Deng Yongchang roared, his voice cracking across the marble steps. "Call Lin Zhengde at the provincial law firm! Tell him to file an emergency injunction right now! Fang Qiming, do you have any idea what you are doing? Hongrui Real Estate pays forty percent of Yunxi County's commercial tax! You march me out in handcuffs in broad daylight and every bank in the city will panic!"

Fang Qiming paused cleanly at the top of the front steps, adjusting his cuff without a flicker of impatience. "Your banks were notified at eight thirty, Chairman Deng Yongchang. Every corporate account, offshore shell transfer, and personal trust under your name has already been frozen by joint provincial order."

Deng Yongchang froze mid-stride, his eyes widening in disbelief. "Frozen? On whose signature? County Magistrate Sun Weiming signed our municipal development guarantee! Chief Liang Guodong approved our site security! Let me make one phone call to Sun Weiming and he will have your entire squad recalled before nine!"

A cold, humorless smile touched the corner of Fang Qiming's mouth. "County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Public Security Chief Liang Guodong were placed in handcuffs fifteen minutes ago inside the county government conference room. As for your chief legal counsel, he handed over his security keycard five minutes ago in exchange for a cooperation statement."

"He what?" Deng Yongchang's voice dropped an octave, raw shock punching the arrogance straight out of his chest. "No! That is impossible! You are bluffing! Without hard ledgers, you have nothing on Hongrui Real Estate! Every contract on the fourteenth floor is clean!"

"We did not bother with the fourteenth floor," Fang Qiming replied calmly, stepping half a pace aside and gesturing back toward the lobby doors.

The rumble of heavy steel casters rolled across the marble floor.

Deng Yongchang's head whipped around. Every drop of color drained from his face, leaving his jowls trembling and gray.

Six provincial forensic auditors in dark blue jackets wheeled three tall matte-black server racks straight out of the sub-basement freight elevator, cables neatly tagged with yellow evidence barcodes. Behind them came two flatbed carts stacked high with twelve sealed archival boxes stamped in red ink: Confidential Dual Ledgers, Old Street Land Appraisals, and Cash Disbursement Logs.

"The basement vault," Deng Yongchang choked out, his knees going suddenly weak so the two officers had to haul him upright by the elbows. "How did you know the concrete wall behind the boiler room was hollow? Even my own vice president did not know those servers existed!"

"Your encrypted off-book servers, your forged land-valuation stamps, your bribery sheets for fourteen county officials, and your cash payments to Yan Tiehu's demolition crew are all logged and sealed," Fang Qiming said evenly. "Even the illegal industrial detonators stored in Yan Tiehu's riverfront warehouse have been matched directly to Hongrui Real Estate's quarry invoices."

Deng Yongchang stared blankly at the wheeled server racks rolling past him into the waiting evidence van. His mouth opened twice, no sound coming out except a dry, wheezing rasp. For the first time in a decade, the man who had boasted he owned half of Yunxi County looked small, old, and utterly broken.

"Take him to the van," Fang Qiming ordered.

As the officers pushed Deng Yongchang's head down into the rear door of the dark transport van, a roar of disbelief broke out across the sidewalk crowd.

"They actually cuffed Deng Yongchang!" someone shouted from the front row. "Look at those boxes! Those are his double books!"

"What about our houses on Old Street?" an elderly man in a faded blue jacket yelled across the yellow tape, his voice trembling with desperate hope. "Hongrui Real Estate slapped red demolition notices on our doors yesterday! Are they still tearing down Old Street?"

Fang Qiming walked to the edge of the marble steps, accepted a compact megaphone from a junior officer, and faced the dense crowd of Yunxi County residents.

"Citizens of Yunxi County, please listen carefully," Fang Qiming announced, his crisp voice carrying clearly down the avenue. "I am Fang Qiming of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group. Hongrui Real Estate Chairman Deng Yongchang and his core associates have been criminally detained on charges of organized crime, bribery, contract fraud, and illegal forced demolition."

A sudden hush fell over the two hundred spectators.

"By immediate decree of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group," Fang Qiming continued loudly, "all assets of Hongrui Real Estate are frozen and placed under provincial receivership. Every single demolition notice, eviction warning, and forced-relocation order issued by Hongrui Real Estate across Old Street and Yunxi County is hereby declared illegal, void, and canceled effective immediately!"

For one stunned heartbeat, nobody moved.

Then the sidewalk exploded.

Cheers erupted from one end of the avenue to the other. Shopkeepers clapped until their palms stung; a middle-aged woman pressed both hands over her mouth, sobbing with sudden relief; delivery riders leaned on their scooter horns in a wild, celebratory chorus.

Inside the sealed glass entrance behind Fang Qiming, two provincial court clerks smoothed crisp white paper seals stamped with bold crimson provincial emblems directly across the double doors of Hongrui Plaza.

The van carrying Chairman Deng Yongchang pulled away into the morning glare, sirens silent, leaving the towering glass headquarters dark, empty, and dead in broad daylight.
