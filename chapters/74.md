# Chapter 74: Hongrui Plaza Sealed in Broad Daylight

At forty-five minutes past eight on Monday morning, traffic along Yunxi County's main street stopped dead outside Hongrui Plaza.

Six dark provincial vans swung sharply across the stone driveway. They blocked both underground garage ramps and the main glass entrance. Within sixty seconds, uniformed officers stretched bright yellow police tape between the metal posts. Two dozen stunned Hongrui Real Estate clerks and front-desk staff rushed out onto the front steps, clutching their bags and staring in panic.

Across the sidewalk, a crowd of workers, delivery riders, and nearby shopkeepers grew from thirty people to over two hundred. Phones rose above heads, cameras pointed straight at the tall glass tower that had ruled Yunxi County for twelve years.

"Look at those license plates," a noodle-shop owner muttered, his cigarette forgotten between his fingers. "Provincial plates. Not county police. And those vests say Special Police and Economic Crime!"

"Is Hongrui Real Estate really getting raided?" a young office worker whispered beside him, eyes wide. "Chairman Deng Yongchang had dinner with County Magistrate Sun Weiming just last week! Nobody in Yunxi County dares to touch Hongrui Plaza!"

Before anyone could answer, the heavy glass doors swung open from inside.

A wave of gasps rolled across the plaza.

Two tall provincial economic-crime officers marched Chairman Deng Yongchang straight across his own shiny marble lobby. His expensive gray suit hung crooked off one shoulder. His silk tie was gone, and his wrists were locked tightly behind his back in gleaming steel handcuffs. Behind him walked Provincial Economic Crime Deputy Director Hua Jingbo, flanked by four armed special police officers.

Deng Yongchang's face was purple with rage and panic, thick veins bulging on his neck. Every few steps he jerked violently against the officers' hands, shouting at his terrified staff huddled behind the yellow tape.

"Get my lawyers!" Deng Yongchang roared, his voice cracking across the front steps. "Call Bi Yaozu at the provincial law firm! Tell him to file an emergency court order right now! Hua Jingbo, do you have any idea what you are doing? Hongrui Real Estate pays about one-fifth of Yunxi County's business taxes! If you march me out in handcuffs in broad daylight, every bank in the city will panic!"

Hua Jingbo paused at the top of the front steps. He adjusted his sleeve without a hint of rush. "Your banks were told at eight thirty, Chairman Deng Yongchang. Every company account, overseas shell transfer, and private bank account under your name has already been frozen on a court order."

Deng Yongchang froze mid-step, his eyes wide with disbelief. "Frozen? On whose order? County Magistrate Sun Weiming signed our city building guarantee! Chief Liang Guodong approved our site security! Let me make one phone call to Sun Weiming, and he will pull your whole team back before nine!"

A cold, humorless smile touched the corner of Hua Jingbo's mouth. "County Magistrate Sun Weiming and Police Chief Liang Guodong were put in handcuffs fifteen minutes ago inside the county government meeting room. As for your head lawyer, he handed over his security keycard five minutes ago in exchange for a deal."

"He what?" Deng Yongchang's voice dropped low as raw shock knocked the pride right out of his chest. "No! That is impossible! You are bluffing! Without account books, you have nothing on Hongrui Real Estate! Every contract on the fourteenth floor is clean!"

"We did not bother with the fourteenth floor," Hua Jingbo replied calmly. He stepped half a pace aside and waved toward the lobby doors.

The heavy rumble of steel wheels rolled across the marble floor.

Deng Yongchang's head whipped around. Every drop of color drained from his face, leaving his cheeks trembling and gray.

Six provincial financial investigators in dark blue jackets wheeled three tall matte-black computer racks straight out of the basement cargo lift. Every cable was neatly marked with a yellow evidence tag. Behind them came two rolling carts stacked high with twelve sealed boxes stamped in red ink: Secret Double Account Books, Old Street Land Prices, and Cash Payout Lists.

"The basement room," Deng Yongchang choked out. His knees went weak so suddenly that the two officers had to hold him up by the elbows. "How did you know the concrete wall behind the boiler room was hollow? Even my own vice president did not know those computers were down there!"

"Your locked secret computers, your fake land-price stamps, your bribe lists for fourteen county officials, and your cash payouts to Yan Tiehu's crew are all logged and sealed," Hua Jingbo said evenly. "Even the illegal blasting caps hidden in Yan Tiehu's riverfront warehouse have been matched straight to Hongrui Real Estate's quarry bills."

Deng Yongchang stared blankly at the rolling computer racks as they passed him into the waiting evidence van. His mouth opened twice, but no sound came out except a dry, wheezing breath. For the first time in ten years, the man who had bragged that he owned half of Yunxi County looked small, old, and completely broken.

"Put him in the van," Hua Jingbo ordered.

As the officers pushed Deng Yongchang's head down into the back door of the dark prisoner van, a roar of disbelief broke out across the sidewalk crowd.

"They actually cuffed Deng Yongchang!" someone shouted from the front row. "Look at those boxes! Those are his double books!"

"What about our houses on Old Street?" an old man in a faded blue jacket yelled across the yellow tape. His voice shook with desperate hope. "Hongrui Real Estate slapped red demolition signs on our doors yesterday! Are they still tearing down Old Street?"

Hua Jingbo walked to the edge of the stone steps. He took a small bullhorn from a young officer and faced the thick crowd of Yunxi County residents.

"Citizens of Yunxi County, please listen carefully," Hua Jingbo announced. His clear voice carried all the way down the street. "I am Hua Jingbo of the Provincial Joint Inspection Group. Hongrui Real Estate Chairman Deng Yongchang and his top people have been arrested on charges of organized crime, bribery, fraud, and illegal forced demolition."

A sudden hush fell over the two hundred watchers.

"By Provincial Court warrant, signed with the Provincial Procuratorate and Public Security Department," Hua Jingbo continued loudly, "all property of Hongrui Real Estate is frozen and placed under provincial control. Every single demolition notice, eviction paper, and forced move-out order put out by Hongrui Real Estate across Old Street and Yunxi County is illegal, void, and canceled right now!"

For one stunned heartbeat, nobody moved.

Then the sidewalk exploded.

Cheers broke out from one end of the street to the other. Shopkeepers clapped until their hands stung. A middle-aged woman pressed both hands over her mouth, crying with sudden relief. Delivery riders leaned on their scooter horns in a joyful chorus.

Inside the sealed glass doorway behind Hua Jingbo, two provincial court clerks pressed crisp white paper seals stamped with bold red provincial marks straight across the double doors of Hongrui Plaza.

The van carrying Chairman Deng Yongchang pulled away into the bright morning traffic, sirens silent, leaving the tall glass tower dark and empty.
